# Quality bar — composite 2020s space games (for Fable + builders)

Not a clone list. Use as **player-experience bar** for polish and depth.

## Composite pillars (what “Steam-ready space game” feels like)

| Pillar | Reference composite | SpaceFace implication |
|---|---|---|
| **World keeps moving for reasons** | Starsector fleets, Freelancer traffic, Rebel Galaxy radio | Ecology, traffic, factions, Band, encounters must be *motivated* and readable sitting still |
| **Reading the world is power** | Freelancer wreck rumors, Starsector intel, Endless Sky story | Depth thesis already: rumor→bearing→wreck→ledger. Finish the natural loop |
| **Combat is readable mastery** | Starsector doctrine, modern twin-stick readability | Doctrines, telegraphs, recovery; not death fog |
| **Economy has causes** | Starsector/Endless Sky markets | Prices/news/contracts show why; no abstract numbers |
| **Identity of place** | NMS wonder *as aspiration*, Freelancer sector memory | Sectors/landmarks/stations must not feel copy-paste (art lane separate; systems must still *place* identity) |
| **First hour teaches without walls of text** | Modern onboarding | One objective, one action, one threat; mining/flight drills feel good |
| **Professional chrome** | Any 2020s Steam hit | No broken previews, black frames, mute audio, stuck recovery, evidence lies |

## Known SpaceFace strengths (do not rebuild)

- Persistent galaxy architecture, fixed-timestep sim, single-writer economy
- Career origins scaffolding, doctrine systems, unique wreck *data*, Band *tuner*, set-piece compiler
- Depth content corpus partially built

## Likely weakness zones (research-backed candidates for Fable ranking)

1. **Natural discovery friction** — content exists; ordinary players may never see it (compressed captures only)
2. **Information hierarchy** — repeated objective spam, cluttered HUD (M1/M3 rows)
3. **Recovery / death** — Game Over proven; natural respawn settlement flaky
4. **Visual presentation** — 0 accepted classifications; glare/materials (graphics lane owns much of this)
5. **Performance** — crowded flight p95 historically red
6. **Story embodiment** — B0–B7 ordinary routes incomplete
7. **Living opposition density** — doctrines audit-green but Helix/carriers may not appear naturally
8. **Mining mastery** — systems present; may lack “juicy” feedback loop polish
9. **Map as truth** — dual authority historically fixed; keep one map truthful
10. **Save/Continue trust** — continuity for bearings, titles, Band, claims

## Orchestrator rule

Fable ranks and prunes this list against live play. Builders only take **admitted** slices with acceptance.
