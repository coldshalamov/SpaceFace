# SPACEFACE — SIDE STORIES (the collision engine)

*Thread A happens to everyone. Thread B happens to Wren. The side stories are the third weave:
small plan-engines that cross the spine at oblique angles and pay off chapters later.
A mission that cannot name its chapter beat and its side-story thread does not get built.*

*Authority: `CREATIVE-DIRECTION.md`. Artifacts in testimony form. No author surnames.*

---

## Derivation rules (read first)

1. Beat → artifact → objective → fail state → in-register reward.
2. The real payout is information or a re-meaning, not only credits.
3. **Forbidden:** the lore-dump courier (mission exists to deliver exposition); the
   theme-lecture escort (NPC explains the thesis mid-flight).
4. Each thread has exactly one gut-punch in the terminal register. End on the number.

## 1. THE UNKNOWN VESSEL

**Premise.** At B0 the killfeed prints `UNKNOWN VESSEL DEPARTED` after the escorts die and the
cargo drone is lost. Someone watched the skim. Someone left. The sightings continue.

**Beats by chapter.**
| Beat | Artifact / event |
|---|---|
| B0 | Killfeed: `UNKNOWN VESSEL DEPARTED` |
| B2 | Hull-spotter log at Elroy's coordinates: same signature, no registry |
| B5 | Sweep log at Meridian: vessel pinged during Callum week, origin redacted |
| B7 | Ledger margin: `OBSERVER — UNRESOLVED. SAME HAND AS BAIT LOAD.` |

**Artifacts.**
- `SPOTTER LOG / S4 / CYCLE 17: UNKNOWN HULL. NO REG. MATCH B0 KILLFEED SIGNATURE.`
- `SWEEP / MERIDIAN / REDACTED ORIGIN. PING WINDOW: CALLUM WEEK.`

**Mission.** `GHOST TAIL` — board: independent. Objective: collect 3 signature matches
across sectors. Fail: lose the trail (signature goes cold 2 cycles). Reward: the B7 margin
line unlocks early as a findable page fragment. Credits: modest.

**Gut-punch.** The observer was not hunting the player. The observer was confirming the bait
loaded. The player was the cargo.

## 2. THE GRAFFITI HAND

**Premise.** The walls use the station's intake stencil font. Maintenance paints over them.
They come back. Ivo (Pit, third shift) paints. Possibly writes. Never resolved.

**Beats.**
| Beat | Event |
|---|---|
| B0 | `THEY KNEW THE MASS.` — stencil font matches intake numbers |
| B1 | `REDISTRIBUTED TO THE HIGHEST BIDDER.` — knows the refile before the player |
| B3 | `THE WELD KNOWS WHO CUT IT TWICE.` — Slate's line, different hand debate |
| B4 | Second hand annotates `EVERY MAN PAYS TWICE` at the clearing station |
| B6 | Player's own hand on the Tessera bulkhead: line they don't remember writing |
| — | Ivo ambient: paints third shift; wall back by first |

**Artifacts.**
- Paint-layer core sample: three hands, three cycles, one wall.
- Ivo's shift log: `BULKHEAD 4. PAINT. WALL WAS CLEAN AT START. WALL WAS NOT CLEAN AT END.`

**Mission.** `PALIMPSEST` — ambient investigation. Photograph / scan 5 graffiti sites. Compare
fonts. Fail: none (optional). Reward: the paint-layer sample; Ivo's log becomes readable.
No resolution of who writes.

**Gut-punch.** The wall knew the B6 line before the player's hand wrote it. The bureaucracy
had already filed the future.

**Beats.**
| Beat | Event |
|---|---|
| B4 | Clerk laughs. Three filings. Crate ID logged if player lingers |
| B4+ | Same ID on Sker floor market |
| B6 | Same ID in Tycho annex drawer under a Quiet secondary |

**Artifacts.**
- `SEIZURE NOTICE — CRATE 7714-H. CONTENTS: [REDACTED].`
- `RE-ROUTE TAG — 7714-H. NEW CATEGORY: MEDICAL SUPPLIES — HUMANITARIAN RELIEF.`
- `SKER FLOOR CHIT — 7714-H. SOLD. BUYER: [HOUSE SCRIP].`

**Gut-punch.** The humanitarian chit was the cleanest filing of the three. The filing was
the crime.

## 4. THE SECOND CLIENT

**Premise.** Rook double-billed Elroy. Two payers. One was Vale's intermediary. One was
redacted. The redaction-field style rhymes with B0's `CO-AUTH: [FIELD REDACTED — REF 44-C]`
and Callum's chip custody strip.

**Beats.**
| Beat | Event |
|---|---|
| B2 | Double-bill executes. Elroy dies. |
| B2+ | Board residue: two payment receipts, one redacted field |
| B5 | Chip custody strip: same redaction glyph |
| B7 | Ledger page: `DO NOT INTERDICT — LET FILE.` Co-author redacted. Same glyph. |

**Artifacts.**
- `ROOK BOOTH / RECEIPT A: VALE INTERMEDIARY. RECEIPT B: [FIELD REDACTED — REF 44-C].`
- `CHIP CUSTODY: ONE SIGNATURE SCRUBBED. GLYPH MATCH RECEIPT B.`

**Mission.** `SECOND SIGNATURE` — investigation. Collect the three redaction glyphs. Fail:
overwrite (one site painted). Reward: early access to the `DO NOT INTERDICT` page fragment;
does not name the second client until B7.

**Gut-punch.** The second client did not commission the kill. The second client permitted it.
The permission is the co-author field.

## 5. THE SMELL TICKET

**Premise.** Helios Bay 7. The grid sat fourteen years. Install abandoned after one week.
A lounge-air complaint is the only ticket that mentions it. Status: CLOSED — NO ACTION REQUIRED.
Wex filed it. This is the only side story allowed to be funny. The funniness is structural.

**Beats.**
| Beat | Event |
|---|---|
| B0 | Grid issued from Bay 7 as TITANIUM ALLOY (player doesn't know) |
| B5–B6 | Player can visit Bay 7. Empty rack. Tags: RECEIVED YEAR 3 / INSTALL PENDING / ISSUED YEAR 17 |
| B6+ | Maintenance terminal: smell ticket |

**Artifacts.**
- Rack tag: `RECEIVED YEAR 3 / INSTALL PENDING / ISSUED YEAR 17 — WEEK OF 47-A.`
- Ticket: `LOUNGE AIR — FAINT MACHINE OIL — ONE WEEK. STATUS: CLOSED — NO ACTION REQUIRED. FILED: WEX.`

**Mission.** `NO ACTION REQUIRED` — discovery. Enter Bay 7 (access via Helios dock rep or
Quiet secondary). Read rack + ticket. Fail: none. Reward: the ticket as inventory artifact;
re-means every prior 12.4t reference. Credits: 0.

**Gut-punch.** Forty thousand people went gray. The only record at the destination is a smell
complaint. Ticket number ends on the form.

## 6. THE DRAWER

**Premise.** B6 Tycho annex. Third drawer left, second row. Never locked. Nineteen years of
chits. Papers appear after B7 and after the operation page is read.

**Beats.**
| Beat | Contents |
|---|---|
| B6 first open | Berth-fee chits, Hale REG 44-C initials, moisture-loss column copies |
| Post-B7 | New paper: player's callsign on a roster |
| Post-operation-page | New paper: dead-drop schedule; mole channel timestamps |

**Artifacts.**
- `DRAWER 3L-2R / TYCHO ANNEX / UNLOCKED.`
- Chit stack mass: scales to 0.01t on the manifest if bagged (deadpan rounding).

**Mission.** `UNLOCKED` — return visits. Open drawer at 3 story states. Fail: none. Reward:
each state adds a paper; full set re-means B0 authorization chain.

**Gut-punch.** The drawer was never locked because locking it would admit it held something.
It held everything.

## 11. What this document is for

Mission designers: pick a row from the slate. Build the objective chain. Do not invent a
fourteenth thread until the thirteen above are wired. Writers: the artifacts are the dialogue.
Programmers: optional threads must not gate B0–B7; they reweight.
