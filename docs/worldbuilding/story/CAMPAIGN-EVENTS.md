# Campaign events and objects — canon register (row 537)

*Canon, adopted 2026-10-26. These are the added *events and objects* the expansion campaign
(`design/expansion/06_CAMPAIGN_SCRIPT.md`, `07_MISSIONS_AND_ENCOUNTERS.md`) proposed for row 537 —
story outcomes are unchanged; these are the things the player can touch on the route. Decisions
for every PROPOSED CANON line live in `docs/worldbuilding/CANON-LEDGER.md`.*

## Book by book

- **The unknown vessel's black Line and the 12.4 t strain reading (B0).** The vessel that took the
  sample ran a Line — a precursor-grade mass tether like the player's — and the strain gauge read
  **12.4 t** at the instant of the theft, true mass against a manifest that would lie about it.
  `story:skimResolved` carries it live; the spotter buoy keeps it in `INTERLUDE — THE UNKNOWN
  VESSEL`.
- **The Infested Hauler (B1).** A Bloom-colonised hauler is B1's world-work kill — feed evidence
  aboard, not a board contract.
- **The Compost Run and Moss-Keeps-Moss (B2).** The Understory garden feeds are a hauled verb —
  `understory:gardenFed` / `understory:compostRunComplete` — and the garden elder who pays by the
  tonne is **Moss-Keeps-Moss** (faces table, `NPCs-CANONICAL.md` §ADOPTED FACES).
- **The Nest (B2).** A wasp nest at the cinder edge is B2's authored encounter — `the_nest` —
  resolved through the encounter director, not a raid.
- **VARIANCE ADJUSTMENT (B3).** A Hawser-class tug named by Kessler's own word — welded twice, same
  seam, narrower on the second pass. **The seam holds to the third pressure spike. It always holds
  to the third. The fourth is where it goes.**
- **The Gate Audit closures (B3–B5).** The Directorate closes one outer gate per Book: **Kepler**
  in B3, **Io** in B4, **Eris** in B5. Each closure strands a community and wakes a little more
  Verge-Layer law; the player can delay a closure but cannot stop the policy
  (`story/CONTEMPORARY-HISTORY.md` §9).
- **Cade Haltred (B5).** Callum's collector — the man who comes around when Callum Oakes' name
  needs collecting on, mid-Reunion.
- **The Evidence Locker heist.** The administration's own locker can be opened — the records that
  file the player can be physically removed, an act the system prices like any other theft.
- **The Empire Seed (B6).** The player's station is built by hand from modules physically towed
  into place — a station assembled, not purchased.
- **The Hive-hulk in Shaft 7 (B6).** A colonised capital hull waits in the sealed shaft Voss's map
  keeps — dismantled node by node (families sheet, `sheets/species/bloom.md`).
- **The Long Field broken by shepherds (B7).** Ashfall's coupling field is anchored by shepherd
  machines; break the shepherds and the sky-filaments thin (`sheets/species/bloom.md`, T6).
- **The Iron Maw dismantled into the Gutted Maw (B7).** The eleven-year-dead dreadnought is taken
  apart plate by plate; what remains is **the Gutted Maw**, the endgame Cull arena
  (`sheets/worlds/sector-wonders.md`).
- **The Cull ladder.** The Pitborn broadcast league's ten-arena ladder is canon
  (`sheets/worlds/sector-wonders.md`, §The ten Cull arenas).
- **The Orphaned Shift trials.** Each machine-person's relationship is a single physical, optional
  trial built on the real verbs (`NPCs-CANONICAL.md` §ADOPTED FACES — the Orphaned Shift).

## World events

- **Rock Rain.** A lane weather event: a field of loose mass that falls across a lane and can be
  dodged, ridden, or thrown back.
- **Derailment cascade.** A convoy event: one struck coupling sends the train's mass spilling as
  debris — ammunition by accident, receipt by law.
- **The encounters are events in the world.** Every authored encounter happens *to* the map — the
  wreck, the booth, the pylon stays where the scene left it (receipts are read by other scenes).
