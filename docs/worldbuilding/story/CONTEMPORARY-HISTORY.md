# SPACEFACE — CONTEMPORARY HISTORY

*The universe right now. Fourteen years into the Slow Gray. The weeks around B0.
This is the missing layer between the setting and the plot: what every desk, faction,
and crook is doing **this cycle**. Writers open this when they need the present tense.*

*Authority: subordinate to `CREATIVE-DIRECTION.md` rulings R1–R15 and the named canon
prose. No author surnames. Numbers are law.*

---

## 1. THE CYCLE IN ONE PAGE

Cycle 17 of the Slow Gray. The Clear Air Index for Sector 0 (the Pit) sits at the floor
of the publishable range and has for six consecutive quarters. The Pit's viability score
is one review away from automatic reversion to Concord. Helios Prime breathes Vale's Breath
at 22°C full spectrum. The gap between the two is not a crisis. The gap is a market.

What everyone is talking about: a Reach clan took a Concord Silt transport at the Sker lane
and traded the canisters for weapons at three-to-one. What nobody is talking about: the
MTS declining-sectors desk booked another quarterly gain on ATMO TOKEN shorts keyed to the
Pit. The gain is small. The gain is recurring. The gain is the point of the desk.

Contract 47-A is on the board. It is not special. It is one of forty-seven standing route
designations that have been running since before most of the current operators owned their
ships. The designation is under review for fourteen cycles. Contact Kessler has never
resolved anything.

## 3. FACTIONS, THIS CYCLE

### Solar Concord Navy (CONCORD)
**Doing:** Guarding Silt distribution lines. Enforcing REG 44-C at gates. Processing
viability scores that will never improve the air they score.
**Want this quarter:** Quiet lanes. Full Silt quotas at Core depots. No headlines.
**Lying about:** The REF 44-C investigation into non-human material, open fourteen years,
status UNRESOLVED. Seventeen "probablys." Each probably is a job.
**Shop floor says:** `GATE 3. SECOND FINE. SAME HAND. YEAR 17.`

### Meridian Trade Syndicate (MTS)
**Doing:** Running the Clear Air Index futures. Holding the short on declining sectors.
Selling emergency Silt at markup to the same stations the short is killing.
**Want this quarter:** The Pit's viability score to drop one more band. Lien's desk has
the position sized for it.
**Lying about:** That the short is "market weather." It is a desk with a name.
**Shop floor says:** `THE PIT'S AIR FELL. THE TOKEN ROSE. SAME DESK. BOTH BOOKS.`

### Crimson Reach (REACH)
**Doing:** Desperate Silt piracy. Trading weapons for canisters at ruinous rates because
they are locked out of the allocation queue.
**Want this quarter:** One full Core-refined canister that doesn't get intercepted.
**Lying about:** Independence. They are a Silt shortage with a flag.
**Shop floor says:** `TEN CRATES OF HEAVY. ONE CANISTER. THE MATH IS THEIRS.`

### Drift Miners Collective
**Doing:** Extracting raw Silt ore in Skerris Deep. Selling to Helix/MTS at floor prices.
Buying back Refined Slurry at markup to keep habitats at 14°C / 60% spectrum.
**Want this quarter:** A grade acceptance that isn't a rejection with a seizure attached.
**Lying about:** Worker solidarity. The solidarity is the debt trap's marketing.
**Shop floor says:** `TWELVE TONS RAW. HELIX SAID DIRTY. TOOK THE CANISTERS ANYWAY.`

### The Quiet
**Doing:** Shadow logistics. Black-canister Silt to outer stations Vale's formula cut off.
ATMO TOKEN brokerage on the side — the real business.
**Want this quarter:** The REF 44-C file to stay UNRESOLVED (Brandt's interest) and the
civilian bait to keep moving (Marsh's channel, opposite interest, same file).
**Lying about:** Being smugglers who also sell air. They are an ATMO TOKEN desk that also
smuggles.
**Shop floor says:** `THE QUIET PAYS WELL UNTIL IT DOESN'T.`

### Ascendant Choir
**Doing:** Liturgy and Pattern-counting. Near-touch with Vethari material they will not
name. Recruiting the silt-headed and the already-gray.
**Want this quarter:** Names released. Bodies lightened. The Pattern's next count.
**Lying about:** That the Pattern is not a filing system with better metaphors.
**Shop floor says:** `THE ENGINE DID NOT NAME US. THE ENGINE DOES NOT NAME ANYONE.`

### Helix Directorate
**Doing:** Refining. Grade rejection. Taking the canisters anyway. The industrial middle
that makes the Core warm and the outer cold.
**Want this quarter:** Throughput. No Reach raids on the inbound.
**Lying about:** The grade. The grade is a price instrument.
**Shop floor says:** `HELIX REJECTED THE GRADE. HELIX TOOK THE SLURRY.`

### Vael Remnant
**Doing:** Stretching spent Silt (Chalk) with cheap additives. Selling "stretched slurry"
to stations that cannot afford Core-refined. Pulling currency business from stations that
short them (Outpost 9 learned this six years ago).
**Want this quarter:** A lane the Quiet hasn't undercut.
**Lying about:** That stretched slurry scrubs. It delays the Slow Gray by a week.
**Shop floor says:** `VAEL SLURRY. WHITE IN THE FILTER BY MORNING.`

### Free Frontier (R9 — canon now)
**Doing:** The gig-economy hall. Independent captains under a brokers' compact with no
customs arm of its own. Quiet vets the freight. Mira processes the runs. The Reach
recruits the bankrupt graduates.
**Want this quarter:** Temporary work that stays temporary. It never does.
**Lying about:** That anyone here is temporary.
**Shop floor says:** `FREE CAPTAINS DOOR. THREE TOOK IT. TWO CAME BACK. ONE JOINED REACH.`
