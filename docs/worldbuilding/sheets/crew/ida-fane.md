## The crew hook

Ida is the crew member the player trusts most and should trust least. The calm is genuine. The calm is also the Quiet's. The player who flies with Ida long enough to hear the child story has been handed the cruelest coercion in the corpus — and the player who learns (B7 ledger, or through the Quiet's retaliation arc) that the Quiet reads Ida's medical logs has to decide whether to keep Ida, confront Ida, or try to free the child. None of the options are clean. The Quiet designed it that way.
