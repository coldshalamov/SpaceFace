## The crew hook

Tor is the first crew member the player is likely to hire — the outer sectors break ships and Tor fixes them. Tor's competence is the bait. Tor's silence is the hook. The player who flies with Tor long enough starts to hear, in "Don't," the entire history of a person who learned that the right word once is worth any number of words twice.
