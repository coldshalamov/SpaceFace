## The crew hook

Selvik is the crew member whose private quest most directly intersects Wren's Thread B. The player who flies with Selvik long enough to hear the "system that doesn't appear on any chart" story has been handed the convergence — and the player who reaches B7, finds the coordinates, and has Selvik on board can show Selvik the coordinates. The game does not stage this collision. The collision is the reward for flying with the pedant.
