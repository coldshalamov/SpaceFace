## The crew hook

Yara is the crew member the player flies combat for. Her narration is the game's permission to feel something during engagements — she names the ships, she counts the kills, she makes the BOUNTY COLLECTED line land with weight the HUD strips out. The player who flies with Yara long enough hears, in the gallows humor, the four years of counting that haven't balanced, and recognizes the structural rhyme with Wren's own unpunished B2.
