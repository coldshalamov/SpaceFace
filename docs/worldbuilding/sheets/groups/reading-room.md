## The salon

Not a war room. A reading room. Bound inquiry reports on the shelves. A long table. Three senior analysts who speak in the cadence of people who have read every prior assessment on the subject and are now refining the wording of the next one. The Reading Room does not make decisions — the Reading Room *authors* the assessments on the basis of which decisions are made elsewhere. The distinction is the salon's core fiction: the salon has the power of authorship without the responsibility of decision. The decisions get made "on the basis of the assessment." The assessment gets written "in response to the decision." The loop is closed. Nobody in the loop has raised their voice.

## Aldiss (the mole, embedded)

**Aldiss** is the senior analyst who has been writing the Vethari-file assessments for fourteen years. Aldiss is the mole. Aldiss is documented here rather than as a standalone character sheet because Aldiss never appears on-screen — Aldiss is the file the operation is closing. Aldiss's signature is on every assessment that kept the file "UNRESOLVED." Aldiss taught Marsh to write the assessments. Aldiss is too tired to run, because running would confirm what the file already says.

Aldiss's betrayal is the slow, paperwork-grade treachery of a man who lost faith one file at a time. Aldiss saw the Vethari evidence, concluded the system would never acknowledge it, and decided the file should be passed to someone who would. The "someone" was Brandt, via the Quiet. The channel is the ATMO TOKEN brokerage. The dead-drop was the Kurtz figure (before the Kurtz figure went off-grid eleven years ago upon realizing they were the conduit).

## The sophistication

The Reading Room is the corpus's *sophistication layer* — the element the user's brief asked for ("elements of sophistication that live alongside the seediness and crime"). The salon is intelligent people doing intelligent work that produces the same downstream outcomes as Quinn's bar and Rook's bounty board. The contrast is the point: the worst decisions in the sector are made in rooms with good air by people who would be uncomfortable sharing an elevator with the cast that executes them. The Pit is downstream of the Reading Room. The Reading Room has never visited the Pit. The Reading Room has authored the assessments that kept the Pit's column empty after year 3.
