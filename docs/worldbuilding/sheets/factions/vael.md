**Signature faction graffiti:** *They didn't modify the workers. They modified the terms of the workers.* / Second line (smaller): *THE ADDENDUM IS ON PAGE 9. READ PAGE 9.*

**Canon note:** the Vael are humans from the Veil Nebula region, not biological aliens. Their "alien" quality is cultural and legal — contract language and organizational structure as incomprehensible to standard spacers as a foreign language. Starting rep -120 reflects contractual disadvantage, not species hostility.
