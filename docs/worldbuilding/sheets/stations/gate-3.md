## Notes

- Gate 3 is where the REF/REG distinction (R2) is visible to the player who reads columns. The
  two codes sit next to each other on the same apparatus. The game never explains them.
- Hale has never failed to initial a legally required entry. That is his virtue and that is the
  machine. The two things are the same thing.
- Dree's waiver is the only irregularity in seventeen years of correct filings. The irregularity
  is the mercy. The mercy costs Dree her promotion track. The cost is filed as JUDGMENT: DEFERRED.
