## Notes

- Outpost 9 is the shop-floor home: the place where the game breathes. Quinn, Spence, the UV
  light, the free drink. Leonard's economy rendered as a bar.
- The Vael currency pull is the model for how the system punishes: not by attack, but by
  withdrawal of service. The station didn't get raided. The station got unbanked. Six years later
  the station runs on a bar's markup, and the markup funds a crime, and the crime is three years
  from buying the bar it's committed in.
