## Notes

- Helios Bay 7 is the comic-horror showpiece. The only place in the game where the comedy and the
  horror share a room, and the room is very clean, and the cleanliness is the point.
- The smell ticket is the only record of the crime. The crime is the air. The ticket says NO
  ACTION REQUIRED. The ticket was filed correctly. The ticket number is 88,214. End on the number.
- The grid's dust line is a visual anchor (ART-PROMPTS §5): the outline where a 12.4-tonne
  object sat for fourteen years and wasn't moved, and then was moved, and the dust is the only
  witness, and the dust was swept by a man who liked his job.
