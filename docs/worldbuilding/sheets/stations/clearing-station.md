## Notes

- The clearing station has no proper name because naming it would make it a place. The system
  prefers it as a function. Functions don't get investigated. Places do.
- The clerk's laugh (the only comedy beat in B4) is structural, not performed: the humor is in the
  filing's precision, not in anyone's reaction to it. The clerk is not funny. The form is funny.
  The form is a war crime.
- The doubled contract (§12.1 new canon) is the most important new beat in the game: it is where
  innocence ends. The tag does not flicker. The player reads it. The player accepts.
