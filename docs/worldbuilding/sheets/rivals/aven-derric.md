## The coercion

Aven is the most emotionally weighted rival because the coercion is the cruelest in the corpus: a sibling who is already free, hunting to buy back a debt that no longer exists. The player who learns this (via the Routers' brokerage ledger at B7, or through Derric, K. if the player finds them first) has the option to tell Aven. The telling collapses the leverage. Aven stops hunting. The Quiet retaliates — against Aven, against Derric, K., against the player. The telling is the right thing and the dangerous thing, which is the Le Carré verdict on most right things.

## Coercion hook

Aven hunts the player if the Quiet tags the player (which the Quiet does at B4 if the player picks the Free Captains door, or at B6 if the player's fragment is detected). Aven is competent. Aven is also, in every engagement, two seconds away from not firing — the two-second channel-close. The player who reads Aven's pattern can exploit the hesitation. The player who tells Aven the truth removes the hunter and inherits the Quiet's retaliation.
