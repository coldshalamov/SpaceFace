# Ship Sheet — Recurring Signature Ships (index of named vessels)

The corpus's named ships, beyond the Tessera and the Iron Maw. Each is a character's signature vessel — the ship is the character's presence on scanner before the character's voice is on comms. The source-material lineage (Freelancer, Rebel Galaxy, Endless Sky) all personify vessels; this is the corpus's equivalent.

**canon_refs:** each entry cites its captain's sheet.
**appears_in_chapters:** each entry inherits its captain's chapter appearances.

---

## The Last Receipt — Sable Vohn (rival courier)

**class:** Pelican-class mining/tug hybrid, scorched, flown hard
**captain:** `npc_sable_vohn`
**the_ship:** The Last Receipt is the ship that's already at the board when you dock. The ship's hull is scored with the heat-scarring of a hundred hard transits. The ship's name is Sable's joke — the "last receipt" is the buyback payment she will never finish making. The ship is the joke made physical, flown through the sectors at a rate that suggests Sable is trying to outrun the receipt.
**sheet:** `sheets/rivals/sable-vohn.md`

## The Honest Wage — Grier Holt (bounty hunter)

**class:** Hornet interceptor, immaculate, never bloodied
**captain:** `npc_grier_holt`
**the_ship:** The Honest Wage is the cleanest ship in the outer sectors. Grier repairs after every job. The ship's hull has no score-markings because Grier does not let engagements touch it. The ship's name is Grier's claim about herself — that the wage is honest, that the work is clean, that the code is real. The ship is the claim's monument. The claim is not true. The ship is still immaculate.
**sheet:** `sheets/rivals/grier-holt.md`

## Probable Cause — Pek Wayland (wild card)

**class:** Mule freighter with mismatched hull plating from seven prior owners
**captain:** `npc_pek_wayland`
**the_ship:** Probable Cause is a patchwork. The hull plating is seven colors because Pek has replaced it seven times from seven salvaged sources, none of which Pek will name. The ship's name is Pek's only honest statement about Pek's life — there is always probable cause to stop Pek, somewhere, for something, and Pek is always already leaving. The ship is the getaway vehicle for a crime that has not been specified because the specification would take too long and Pek has another job.
**sheet:** `sheets/rivals/pek-wayland.md`

## Conditional Release — Aven Derric (coerced hunter)

**class:** Bastion corvette, well-maintained, flying Quiet colors Derric cannot remove
**captain:** `npc_aven_derric`
**the_ship:** Conditional Release is the ship the Quiet assigned Aven. The ship's Quiet colors are painted on; Aven is not permitted to repaint. The ship's name is the Quiet's joke at Aven's expense — the "conditional release" is the sibling the Quiet will release when the debt clears, which it will not. The ship is the leash made transportable. Aven flies it because the alternative is the Quiet reclassifying Derric, K. from "collateral" to "released — deceased."
**sheet:** `sheets/rivals/aven-derric.md`

## Variance Adjustment — the shipyard ship (NPC-adjacent, no captain)

**class:** tier-2 hull, listed for sale at the Pit shipyard
**captain:** none (for sale); prior owner was a weigher who named it
**the_ship:** Variance Adjustment is the ship the player walks past at B3. The ship's name is Kessler's terminology — the variance adjustment is the line item Kessler files when the scale's thumb has done its work. Someone who worked the scales named this ship before they sold it. The ship is not a character in the sense of having a voice. The ship is a *tell* — the only ship in the corpus whose name is a key to a system the player has not yet read.
**sheet:** referenced in `story/chapter-03-bigger-boat.md`, `sheets/chapters/B3.md`

## Design note

The signature ships function as the genre requires: the ship announces the captain before the captain speaks. The player who reads "Last Receipt on scanner" knows it's Sable. The player who reads "Honest Wage on scanner" knows it's Grier. The ship's name is the captain's thesis. The thesis is sometimes a joke (Sable, Pek), sometimes a claim (Grier), sometimes a leash (Aven), sometimes a key (Variance Adjustment). The ship is the character's first appearance in every engagement — the name on the IFF before the voice on the comms.
