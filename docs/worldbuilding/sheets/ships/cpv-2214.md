## Notes

- The Can is the backstory ship — it explains the four-year gap, the frozen damage, the
  salvage-crew debt that gave Wren the Tessera. It is never a gameplay location.
- The "designed for 200, carried 600" detail is the Concord penal system's quiet horror: the same
  procedural competence that files BOUNTY COLLECTED also staffs its transports at 300% capacity,
  and the procedure is correct, and the hold smells like a can.
- ART-PROMPTS.md §4 has the concept plate: blocky, oversized hold vents, patched overflow frames,
  dirty white Concord stripe, exterior markings CPV-2214.
