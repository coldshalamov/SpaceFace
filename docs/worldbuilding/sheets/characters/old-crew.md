## The four (one line each, from prose canon)

- **Sable** — pilot, taught Wren to fly. Now dead of something unrelated.
- **Caro** — fixer. Somewhere in the outer sectors; hasn't been seen in three years.
- **Dak** — muscle. Exiled from Concord space for something he won't discuss.
- **Torren** — the boss. Ran the crew "the way a good card player runs a table: loose, fast, and always one hand ahead of the room." Killed when a syndicate took over his territory. The killing was administrative.

## Design note

The four exist for one structural reason: to establish that Wren is capable of sustained human connection, has lost it, and has chosen solo operation since. This makes the game's crew system (NPCs who ride with the player, "people whose utility currently outweighs their cost") legible as a *compromise*, not a preference. Wren works alone because crew costs money and trust and both are finite. The old crew is what happened the last time he paid both. Treat them as backstory, not active canon — do not resurrect them in chapters unless a beat specifically requires Wren to reference his last crew.
