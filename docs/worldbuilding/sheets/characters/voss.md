## Notes

- Voss is the character for whom Thread A and Thread B can nearly intersect: the private map of sealed shafts, if followed into Shaft 7, would surface the year-3 transfer. The game does not stage this collision. It is the unredeemed possibility Voss carries.
- The Voss atmospheric-economy dialog sample is the canonical voice for the Silt-class reality: *"The claim is dry. We pulled twelve tons of raw ore, but Helix rejected the grade. Said the Silt was too dirty to refine. They took the canisters anyway. We're down to 12 degrees on the residential ring. Tell the crew to wrap their boots."*
