## Notes

- Callum gives Wren a data chip at B5 — not out of guilt but because Wren at his booth is bad for business and a data chip is cheaper than a scene. The chip traces the buyer to a facility under REF 44-C's "EVIDENCE PROCESSING — LONG-TERM STORAGE." Callum thinks this resolves Wren's problem. It does not. It hands Wren the next link in the chain Callum never understood.
- Callum does not reappear after B5. He is the unpunished man who stays unpunished. The game does not bring him back for satisfying closure. The absence of comeuppance is the point.
