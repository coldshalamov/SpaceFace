## Notes

- Dree is the Greene beat fully landed in the game: the decent face of the machine, the one the
  player can actually talk to, and her decency is the horror because the decency runs the machine.
- The waived fine is the first mercy in the game that costs the person who gives it. Every other
  mercy (the re-categorization at B1, the popup at B3) costs the player. Dree's mercy costs Dree.
- The carbon slip in her locker is the only warm object in the game that isn't Lida's drawing.
