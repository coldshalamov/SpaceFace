## Notes

- Sump is the heart the comedy law was missing. Every other comic register in the game is dry,
  bitter, or structural. Sump is warm. He is funny because he is kind, and the kindness is
  unprotected, and the system doesn't notice him, and that contrast is what makes the system
  visible.
- Sump survives every ending. He is still sweeping in the epilogue. The freighter still doesn't
  wave back. The dust still moves.
- Sump's whistling is the only permitted music in the game's audio design (VIDEO-CLIPS.md §V-20).
