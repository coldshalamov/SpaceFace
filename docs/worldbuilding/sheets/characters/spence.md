## Notes

- Spence is the only person in the game whose loyalty costs him nothing and protects nothing.
  That is the Leonard joke: the friendship is real, and the friendship is useless, and both of
  those facts are the friendship.
- The free drink Quinn slides him (V-14.04) is the only free drink in the sector. It is also the
  only transaction between them. Everything else is silence.
