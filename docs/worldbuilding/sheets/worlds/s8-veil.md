## Notes

The Veil Nebula is the Vael home region. The anomaly signal at the sector center (poi_anomaly, seamed asteroid) is the closest the game comes to pointing at Thread B (the Vethari) without naming it — the nebula heart readable from any gate bearing. The static is audible here; sensor range drops to 40%. The Vael addendum's page-9 clause about "non-standard biological contamination" (a quarantine protocol for organisms not in the standard database) is the one place Vael contract law and Vethari biology nearly touch — neither the Vael nor the player can confirm the connection.
