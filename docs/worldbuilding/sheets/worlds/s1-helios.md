## Notes

- Helios Prime is where the grid *arrived* in year 3 — and it was the wrong grid, shelved in bay 7, gathering dust for fourteen years. The maintenance ticket ("smells faintly of machine oil") is the only record at the destination. Forty thousand people went gray, and the only record is a smell complaint.
- Vale's Logistics Oversight offices are here. The door looks like every other door. The org chart doesn't list Atmospheric Logistics Allocation. It lists Logistics Oversight.
- The star Helios is never referenced in the game text. Intentional — the star predates everything, will outlast everything, has nothing to say about what happens in its gravity well. The player starts orbiting something they never see.
