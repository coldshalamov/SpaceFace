# Sector wonders and residents — canon (row 537)

*Canon, adopted 2026-10-26 from `design/expansion/04_SECTORS.md` under the PROPOSED CANON rule of
`design/expansion/00_README.md` §5. One wonder per sector (build_map row 450); the residents are
the placed cast — existing machine-people and the new Orphaned Shift are named canon in
`story/NPCs-CANONICAL.md` §ADOPTED FACES. The story-band name map: Tycho = Tethys, Meridian = Ceres,
Hollow = Pallas, Cinder = Charon, Skerris = Sker Haven
(`design/world-identity/STORY_SECTOR_MAP.md`).*

## The wonders (one per sector)

| Sector | Wonder | What it is |
|---|---|---|

## The ten Cull arenas

The Cull — the Pitborn broadcast blood-league born Year 13 (`story/CONTEMPORARY-HISTORY.md` §9) —
runs a ladder of wrecked arenas that grows with the Line rating. Each arena reuses its region's
throwables; the ladder and its laws are canon:

| Arena | Region | Scale | Signature throwable | Law |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pebble Yard | Helios | T1 | pods, buoys | none: the first room |
| Slag Bowl | Vesta | T1–T2 | slag bergs | heat lanes make throws hot |
| Container Graveyard | Tethys | T2 | containers on snapped spines | couplers re-link what touches |
| Reef Pinball | Pallas | T2 | reef debris | high bounce |
| Ice Orchard | Haumea | T2–T3 | ice spires | shards ricochet |
| Glass Garden | Phoebe | T3 | singing crystal | resonance chains |
| Garden Ring | Charon | T3 | wrecks | Understory gardens eat misses, growing the walls |
| Breaker Field | Ceres | T3–T4 | Breaker chunks | the Breaker keeps cutting new ammunition |
| Yardmaster's Belly | Sker Haven | T4 | ballast balls | the arena is inside a Pitborn Yardmaster |
| The Gutted Maw | Ashfall | T5 | dreadnought plates | inside the Iron Maw; the endgame Cull |
