## The gradient origin

The Pit is the unit the rest of the system is measured in. Every sector's temperature, light, smell, and air quality is readable against the Pit. The release (the colonists were released from the prison) changed the temperature. It didn't change the lesson.

The recycler catalyst grid left Shaft 7 in year 3 on a clean manifest under VALE-ALA-47A. The lower decks have been in the Slow Gray for fourteen years. The residents call it being tired. The work is hard. The work is also not why.
