## Notes

Skerris Deep is the pirate-haven sector. The "secondary market in breathable air" (the Quiet's ATMO TOKEN brokerage) is most visible here. The canisters are physical representations of ATMO TOKENS — buying them proves to Vale's allocation system that the sector is solvent. The price on request is always higher than Vale's division charges for the same canister weight. The buyer is paying for anonymity, not the air.
