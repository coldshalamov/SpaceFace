## Notes

Vesta Forge is the module-craft and ship-manufacture band. B3 (Bigger Boat) contexts it: the player buys a tier-2 hull here, sees the ship named VARIANCE ADJUSTMENT in the shipyard (Kessler's terminology), and the B3 comms popup from Vale arrives. The forge's slag-radiation is the local expression of "the work is hard. The work is also not why."
