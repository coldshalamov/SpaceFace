## Othrik the Witness (the heretic)

**Voice:** *The doctrinal debater.* Othrik speaks in the register of a man who left a seminary and took the syllabus with him. Every sentence is a footnote to a position paper. Othrik can cite Choir doctrine chapter-and-verse — and does, to explain why the doctrine is wrong. Othrik is the most educated man in the outer sectors, and the education is the wound. The schism wasn't theological. The schism was Othrik reading the certification logs and realizing the "inert" filings were structurally identical to the suppressions in every other REF 44-C file.

**The scheme:** the Vindel authenticate relics the Choir has certified inert. The re-authentication is real (the Vindel's methods are better). The re-authenticated relics are then "witnessed" — displayed, in the inverted-violet livery, as evidence of the Choir's lying. The witnessing is the caper. The caper is also, accidentally, the most credible open-source intelligence on non-human material in the sector.

**The strut:** Othrik thinks the player is either a convert or an assassin sent by the orthodox Choir. The player is neither. The player is bait carrying a fragment Othrik would kill to witness. The collision is the most dangerous in the outer sectors — not because Othrik is violent (he isn't), but because Othrik authenticating Wren's fragment would surface the file the operation is built to suppress. Marsh cannot allow this. Marsh has not yet decided how to prevent it.

**Coercion hook:** the player carrying the fragment (PERSONAL EFFECTS — 3.1 KG) into the Veil Nebula risks the Vindel detecting it. The Vindel's relic-scanners are better than the Choir's. If detected, Othrik will want to witness — and witnessing the fragment publishes it in a way the operation and the mole both cannot file away. The player who lets Othrik witness has accidentally weaponized their personal cargo. The player who refuses has a heretic with a seminary education and a grudge on their tail.
