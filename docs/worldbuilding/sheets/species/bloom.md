# The Bloom — canon families and behaviour (row 537)

*Canon, adopted 2026-10-26 from `design/expansion/03_PEOPLES_CREATURES_MACHINES.md` §1 under the
PROPOSED CANON rule of `design/expansion/00_README.md` §5. Biology stays canonical in

## Pathology — what the player reads

The coupling fungus links members by **coupling filaments**, visible as bioluminescent green veins
that draw between linked bodies. Killing a relay breaks the filaments: linked packs lose coherence
and scatter. Spore drift is a soft green haze that keeps its silhouette. Husks keep their old
faction paint under the growth — the host-shadow is how you read who the hull used to be.

## The families (the ladder)

- **T0** — Spore drift (hazard, nothing to fight); **Lostcalls** — harmless chirping lights whose
  calls mark a *dead pocket* where the field cannot reach. Renamed from the seed-doc's "Choirless"
  so the family never collides with the Ascendant Choir; live data id `lostcalls`.
- **T1** — Needle mite (`mite`), Hull leech (`leecher`), Spitter (`spitter`), **Lantern cyst** —
  drifts toward heat and bursts in a spore shove; you can grab and throw it as a bomb.
- **T2** — Charger (`charger`), **Glassback** — silicate-armoured grazers that reflect gunfire, so
  only mass kills them; Wake eel — a whip while held; **Bellows** — swells and bursts when struck
  hard; grab it before the burst and fling it into a pack.
- **T3** — Bristle ram, Casket worm (`brood_tendril`), Furnace maw, Spindle mother (`brood_queen`),
  **Husk** — a colonised human hull that flies its dead crew's patterns (host-shadow); old faction
  lights flicker green; treat it like the old faction.
- **T4** — Veil ray, Blind shepherd (a relay — kill it and linked packs panic; its pulse organ
  calls swarms to wherever you drop it), Anchor beast.
- **T5** — **Hive-hulk**, a colonised capital ship you dismantle piece by piece: break the nodes,
  rip the plates, empty the bays. The Hive-hulk waits in Shaft 7 (`story/THE-STORYLINE.md`, B6/B7;
  `story/CAMPAIGN-EVENTS.md`).
- **T5+** — **The Bolus**, a colonised Vethari carrier; an interior, not an encounter.
- **T6** — **The Long Field**, Ashfall's coupling field itself: sky-filaments anchored by
  shepherds. Break the shepherds and the field thins; the tide line falls back. Canonically the
  Long Field is broken by shepherds — the field's own anchor-machines are the counter.

**Non-hostile ecology is mandatory.** The Mourning kite (follows a grief route around old wrecks)
and the Archive crab (piles fragments into geometric heaps that hold salvage) are wonder and
texture, never targets.

## The tide map

Tides follow lanes where wrecks rot unclaimed — a function of player and Understory behaviour
(see `story/CONTEMPORARY-HISTORY.md` §9 for the cycle pressure).

- **Endemic:** Ashfall Reach, the Bolus.
- **Tidal:** Charon Expanse and Eunomia Gulf (the Understory gardens hold the line), plus Rhea
  Cinder and Haumea Rift.
- **Incursion:** Pallas Drift's reef, Sker Haven's gate camps (the Pitborn Cull farms them),
  Vesta's slag field (Furnace maws).
- **Clean:** the Core and the Veil — Vael instruments suppress the field, and Lostcalls are common
  there.

## Canon names on the ladder

"Lostcalls" is the canon name. "Choirless" is the retired seed-doc label, kept only as a footnote.
