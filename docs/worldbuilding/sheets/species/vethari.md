## Notes

- The Vethari are the game's cosmic horror, and cosmic horror works by absence. Every frame they
  appear in reduces them. The off-frame law is the craft, not the constraint.
- The suicide-as-arithmetic line is LITERARY-AUDIT §I protected. Never paraphrase it.
- Pell's Special Custody sub-office exists solely to keep this file at UNRESOLVED. The budget
  renews every cycle. The silence has a quarterly.
- Wren is the only human survivor of a Vethari shared-field collapse. This is the real reason
  Marsh's channel cast him: a man who has already lost everything once will do anything to find
  the one thing he lost. Lida is the lever. The Vethari are the wound.
