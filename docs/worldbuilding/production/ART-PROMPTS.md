# SPACEFACE — ART PROMPTS (image-generation pack)

*Self-contained prompts for concept art, headshots, ships, places, artifacts, graffiti plates.
Inline the GLOBAL STYLE into every prompt. The Vethari are never rendered as creatures.*

*Authority: `CREATIVE-DIRECTION.md` §4 casting, §5 comedy law, R6 Witness, rule 10 off-frame.*

---

## GLOBAL STYLE (inline essentials every time)

Grounded industrial science fiction; lived-in, unmaintained, repaired past service life.
Documentary/photographic, not illustrative. Lighting: interrogation-bright (harsh full-spectrum)
OR ambush-dark (emergency orange / low-spectrum); default 60%-spectrum sick light. Palette:
coolant-teal, sodium-orange, chalk-gray, wrong-green phosphor. Film grain, mild lens imperfection.
Format refs: 1970s industrial documentary photography, CCTV stills, Soviet-era factory photography,
archival NASA, forensic evidence photography. People: real faces, real wear, bad sleep; nobody
looks like a model; everyone looks like their job.

**GLOBAL NEGATIVE:** clean hero shots, beauty lighting, lens flare, space-opera gloss, neon
cyberpunk, fantasy armor, sleek Apple-store sci-fi, smiling portraits in terminal zones, aliens,
creatures, Vethari bodies, gore porn, brand logos from real Earth corps.

## 8. OFF-FRAME RULE (art team law)

**The Vethari are never rendered.** Not silhouette, not shadow-with-intent, not "distant giant,"
not flashback creature. Off-frame means off-frame.

**ONLY permitted Vethari-adjacent image:** tissue sample under glass — whitish-reddish filaments,
dried, lab slide, forensic lighting, label optional BIO-44C-7, no body, no ship interior with
occupants.

If a brief asks for "the day the sun went out," render: horizon shadow on wheat, temperature drop
as breath mist, sound design not image of the ship. Hatch exterior only.
