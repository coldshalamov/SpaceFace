## What each participant wants

### Kessler: a way out that depends on staying at the scale

**Canon:** He logs manifests before the scan clears and thumbs the seal twice. His skim funds a Core berth for his daughter, out of Tycho air. His arithmetic is precise, the exit indefinitely recedes, and he continues. The canonical characterization gives the private motive; it does not authorize him to confess it to the pilot. The existing filing reaction already recognizes the 47-A pattern and tells the player to retain the original before another copy disappears.

**Inference:** He wants a bounded variance he can manage, not inquiry into the person operating the scale. Independent measurement threatens his discretion, but the original slip also threatens whoever can close his file without asking. His useful contradiction is not “secretly good father” versus “bad clerk.” It is that protecting one child's future depends on maintaining the arithmetic that makes other people's air worse. He can genuinely advise the pilot while steering attention away from his own hand.

**Development consequence:** Let him acknowledge exact numbers and restrict the remedy to the filing. Keep the motive in diction: he talks about the column, the original and the seal, not his virtue or his daughter. Do not put the child on comms or turn a measured mass into a rescue count.

### Concord/Hale: correct procedure is the protective identity

**Canon:** Concord owns customs and gate control. Hale applies REG 44-C, initials legally required entries and does not break Concord seals. He is explicitly not written as a covertly corrupt inspector. The institution can record a correct entry while the underlying harm continues.

**Inference:** A discrepancy is institutionally tolerable if it can be assigned a filing category. The dangerous question is not “can you record this?” but “whose duty now changes?” A successful stamp can therefore be meaningful evidence without resolving anything.

**Development consequence:** State exactly what was filed. No “you are cleared,” no wanted-level relief, no seizure, no verdict on intent. Do not send Hale to Ceres merely to personify bureaucracy.

### Helios and Wex: comfort is another person's missing machinery

**Canon:** Wex shelved a 12.4-tonne catalyst grid because Helios already had a better one; his ordinary concern is the lounge smell ticket. The Helios audit issuer is the Variance Office; its authored declaration is 20 t. These are different facts and different objects. The audit is not automatically the catalyst grid.

**Inference:** Helios can afford to treat discrepancy as administrative maintenance. The protagonist's newly earned numbers disturb the maintained surface without proving the cargo's contents.

**Development consequence:** Preserve Kessler's Bay 7 opener rather than replacing it with a slip tutorial. The smell clue and the weight slip should coexist, not be declared causally identical. Never hardcode a measured 12.4 t, 20 t, or 40 t as a narrative answer: use the retained physical measurement.

### Ceres: contesting a declaration without adopting its liability

**Runtime canon:** The Ceres Dock Registrar issues the carrier-frame recovery with a 32 t declaration. Its brief says the registrar doubts the carrier's papers and the issuer's declaration is disputed, not the pilot's. Filing outcome is `mass_frame_disputed`, at Ceres only. The generic institutional issuer is not an established named villain.

**Inference:** Ceres wants a defensible chain of responsibility for a suspect load. That need can align with the pilot's evidence without making the registrar altruistic. Asking for a measurement costs the office less than admitting the paperwork was unreliable before acceptance.

**Development consequence:** Preserve the difference between Helios recording a discrepancy and Ceres recording a dispute. Do not invent a conviction, intentional fraud, secret cargo, or an immunity grant. No invented faction-wide reputation change follows from a single mass reading.

## Proposed conversation contract

Smallest useful slice: Helios Kessler, weight-audit slips only. Carrier-frame discussion is optional later scope, not required to make this slice work. Its distinction above is a guardrail against collapsing all slips into the same story.

1. In `generateContacts`, append a new choice to a copied list of Kessler's existing merchant choices only when valid retained Helios audit evidence exists. Preserve greeting, Bay 7 rumor and other actions.
2. In `buildCanonicalReply`, resolve the slip again from current durable state on click. Do not trust a stale contact object's cached evidence. A removed/invalid slip yields a neutral no-evidence reply, never fabricated values.
3. Read `normalizeMassScaleState(state.economyContracts?.massScale)`, then enforce `weight_audit`, `issuingStationId === station_helios`, a genuine mismatch, and the exact `mass_discrepancy_filed` outcome for any filed wording. Normalization alone is not a substitute for this station/kind pairing.
4. Select only the actual authored Helios audit offer and target slot 0, with its exact issuer/reference and declared 20 t. Other cases never displace it. Bind the clicked choice to slip ID, mission incarnation and exact measured/declared numbers, and recheck that identity against current state. Do not depend on insertion order, Date.now, or a new counter.
5. Show exact retained declared/measured numbers. Do not re-weigh a mission target or need the old entity to remain alive after delivery.
6. Reopening/reloading the conversation should preserve the answer from saved source state, with no rewards, events, mutation or claims that the player heard the existing ambient line.

**New proposed dialogue, not existing canon:**

- Retained, unfiled: “Declared {d} t. Your line read {m} t. You have a weight slip. Customs has no filing. Keep the original.”
- Helios filed: “Declared {d} t. Your line read {m} t. The office filed the difference. The seal still says what it said. Keep the original.”
- Optional second choice after filing, only if scope permits: “What changes?” → “The difference has a record now. The seal still says what it said.”

The second reply is an intentional character evasion, not a promise that the slip has no future consequence. The observable consequence now is recognition and a durable, inspectable distinction the player can revisit after the transient flight notice is gone.
