## EXCERPT NOTES (12)

1. **Sick / spiteful man** (*Notes* I.1, Garnett): “I am a sick man…. I am a spiteful man… I refuse to consult a doctor from spite.”  
   *Principle:* Physiology weaponized; agency as self-injury.

2. **Self-harm as only controllable ledger** (*Notes* I.1): knows he injures only himself; still refuses.  

3. **2×2=4 rejected** (*Notes* I.VII–VIII, paraphrase): man acts against advantage to prove not a piano-key.  
   *Principle:* Break clean formula to prove existence.

4. **Post-crime fever** (*C&P* Part II region): illness, pulse, physical repulsion.  
   *Principle:* **Guilt as physiology**, not moral meter.

5. **Conscience as punishment** (*C&P*, Porfiry discourse, widely cited Garnett): if he has a conscience he will suffer — that is punishment.  
   *SpaceFace inversion:* system refiles so conscience has nowhere to dock.

6. **Extraordinary man theory** (*C&P* Part III article arc): theory justifies stepping over blood.  
   *Principle:* Clean alibi precedes body-bill.

7. **Sonya’s true mercy** (*C&P* Part V): demands public truth, not anesthetic absolution.  
   *SF: no confessor; graffiti/ledger are secular residue.*

8. **Epilogue rebirth** (*C&P* Epilogue): **hard diverge** — do not import Siberian peace.

9. **Double recognized** (*The Double*): same face/name succeeds where original fails.  
   *Principle:* Recognition, not twist; bureaucracy prefers smoother copy.

10. **Social death by replacement** (*The Double* arc).  

11. **Miracle, mystery, authority** (Grand Inquisitor, Garnett): “We have corrected Thy work…”  
    *Principle:* **Wrong mercy** manages freedom away.

12. **Slaves but feed us** (Grand Inquisitor): freedom laid down for bread.  
    *Principle:* Bread = clean air/settlement; ledger page = freedom.
