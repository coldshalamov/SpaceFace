## EXCERPT / SOURCE NOTES (12)

### 1. BL3 as internal “parody” line (industry)
**Source:** IGN interview write-up on Borderlands 4 narrative direction (Aug 2025).  
**Paraphrase/quote (published):** On Borderlands 3, “in our worst hours, it sometimes felt like parody,” and that edged a red line for fans and the speaker.  
**Device named:** Joke/meme density that turns stakes into sketch comedy.  
**Effect:** Humor ceases to be character residual and becomes the product mandate.  
**Transferable principle:** If every beat must “land a line,” silence and procedural dread die.  
**SpaceFace risk:** High — house forbids this; shop-floor wit only.

### 2. Joke-per-minute mandate rejected (industry)
**Source:** BL4 writers interview coverage (2025 clips / press): move away from “joke per minute type of storytelling”; “sometimes silence is the choice.”  
**Device:** Forced quip cadence as default.  
**Effect:** No room for flat inventory register or airless sacred beats.  
**Principle:** Humor must arise from situation/character, not fill dead air.  
**SpaceFace map:** Vethari / crawlspace / Elroy flicker stay airless; bar/Quinn may breathe.

### 3. Tone must fit stakes (industry)
**Source:** VGC / Gearbox BL4 coverage (Jun 2025) — Clark: totalitarian world makes “everybody making jokes every minute” feel wrong; take BL3 criticism on board.  
**Device:** Register mismatch (Wild West gag mode under oppression).  
**Principle:** World stakes set joke budget. Slow Gray + 40k air death ≠ Whac-A-Mole banter.  
**SpaceFace map:** Core comedy is dry ledger math, not corpse punchlines.

### 4. Chosen One as silver-bullet systems fantasy (criticism)
**Source:** Reactor, “The Flawed Fantasy of the Chosen One” (Aug 19, 2020).  
**Paraphrase:** Trope presents one special person as enough to defeat evil; replaces interrogating structures with swapping a few bad pieces / restoring rightful rule.  
**Device:** Destiny replaces institutions.  
**Principle:** **Paperwork worlds fail if one freighter “saves” the allocation algorithm.**  
**SpaceFace map:** Player is replaceable labor with a unique paper trail — never a unique soul. Prefill = asset casting (le Carré), not prophecy.

### 5. Chosen One kills agency (craft)
**Source:** JC Murray, “Twists on Tropes: 5 Chosen Ones” (2020).  
**Paraphrase:** Default chosen-one arc: Call overrides free will; no real choice → weak conflict.  
**Principle:** Prophetic graffiti / “only you” is FAIL; administrative pre-allocation is OK if still optional/complicit.  
**SpaceFace map:** Pre-B0 COUNTERPARTY is filing, not fate.

### 6. Prophecy destroys tension (craft satire with real point)
**Source:** J.P. Beaubien, “Terrible Writing Advice — Chosen Ones” (2017).  
**Paraphrase:** Unchangeable prophecy makes choices meaningless; implies only special people change the world; downplays the many who keep civilization turning.  
**Principle:** Perfect for freighter games: **the many keep the wheel turning** — that is the horror and the dignity.  
**SpaceFace map:** Choice E / next run; eight crooks; Vale initials.

### 7. Infodump under no pressure (craft consensus)
**Source:** Standard SF workshop / craft press rule (secondary norm): exposition fails when it answers questions the character has not asked with behavior.  
**Device:** Lore slab before need.  
**Principle:** Silt/ATMO/Reading Room facts must arrive as cost, seal, price, or tremor — not bar monologue.  
**SpaceFace map:** `ATMOSPHERIC-ECONOMY.md` is bible; player gets board prices + Slow Gray body.

### 8. Purple / trying-too-hard style (tradition)
**Source:** Horace “purple patches” + modern craft warnings: style that draws attention to writer effort before scene force.  
**Device:** Earnest nebula poetry; soft abstract after hard number.  
**Principle:** Leonard’s “sounds like writing” + McCarthy residual: **end on the number.**  
**SpaceFace map:** Kill “it might weigh everything” class lines (audit; mostly resolved; residual meta captions still live — see master §5).

### 9. Second writer / self-commentary (internal primary)
**Source:** `docs/worldbuilding/LITERARY-AUDIT.md` §0–III — “two writers in one cover”; second writer explains first.  
**Quote (audit):** first writer shows crawlspace fit; second writes “the weight is the point” / “it might weigh everything.”  
**Principle:** Delete explanation; promote artifact.  
**SpaceFace live residual (2026-07-16):** `SHOP-FLOOR-CAPERS.md:120` “This is the point.”; `ANTAGONIST…:63` “This is also the point.”; `narrative.js` B6 hint “This was always the case.”

### 10. Maxim graffiti vs testimony (internal primary)
**Source:** `LITERARY-AUDIT.md` §III.5; `SYMBOLISM-MOTIFS.md` graffiti law.  
**Good form (canon):** “KESSLER. SCALE 4. TYCHO. TWENTY-TWO MONTHS. LOOK IT UP.” (`NPCs-CANONICAL.md`)  
**Bad form (audit cited historically):** maxim/chiasmus walls (“We made it empty”).  
**Principle:** Walls state fact/name/number; theme stays in the reader.  
**2026-07-16:** maxim pair 0 hits in `contracts/`; protect testimony form.

### 11. Mercy-leak endings (internal + tradition)
**Source:** `AGY-PROMPTS-FOR-USER.md` endgame critique (reactor death too merciful); `LITERARY-AUDIT.md` §II.7; McCarthy/le Carré non-exit.  
**Principle:** Death-as-exit and noble sacrifice let player off the hook; loop / next run carry complicity.  
**SpaceFace:** Choice C loop locked in `ENDGAME-B7-REDESIGN.md`; do not reintroduce reactor death in spine.

### 12. Heist wearing economy mask / cosmic free lunch (internal + Bacigalupi)
**Source:** `LITERARY-AUDIT.md` §II.2, §II.4; Bacigalupi packet mechanism checklist.  
**Principle:** Collapse needs quarterly beneficiary; silence needs budget. One year-3 heist alone is pastiche systems horror.  
**SpaceFace:** Prose has MTS short + legal grid (`ATMOSPHERIC-ECONOMY.md`); **play must make year-9 desk nameable** or false depth remains.

## CORE THEMES (control set)
1. Silence is a valid narrative choice under real stakes.  
2. Agency > destiny in freighter/complicity stories.  
3. Systems critique dies if one hero fixes the algorithm.  
4. Humor is residual, not a JPM mandate.  
5. Exposition requires present-tense cost.  
6. Second writer must be fired from body/player text.  
7. Testimony > maxim on walls.  
8. Endings must carry complicity, not grant exits.  
9. Economy needs a named beneficiary this cycle.  
10. Distinctive cold lines die first under “clarity” polish.

## SIGNATURE DEVICES (for detection)
Poster test · train-skip · said-adverb · hard-number→soft-abstract · year-9 beneficiary · destiny vs pre-allocation · silence cost (character vs author) · UI coach test · hoodie maxim · name-drop in body.

## WHAT THIS CONTROL SET NEVER DOES
- Treat Borderlands quip cadence as “fun SF default.”  
- Treat prophecy/chosen freighter as depth.  
- Treat trauma pile-on as theme.  
- Treat author surnames as quality certificates in body.  
- Treat death as moral exit in this world.
