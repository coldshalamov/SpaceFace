## RULE / EXCERPT NOTES (12)

1. **Master rule:** “If it sounds like writing, I rewrite it.” (NYT 2001)  
2. **Skip:** “Try to leave out the part that readers tend to skip.” (Rule 10)  
3. **No weather opens** unless character reacts (Rule 1).  
4. **Avoid prologues** that are pure backstory (Rule 2).  
5. **Said only:** “The line of dialogue belongs to the character; the verb is the writer sticking his nose in.” (Rule 3)  
6. **No said-adverbs** (Rule 4).  
7. **Exclamation / suddenly** austerity (Rules 5–6).  
8. **Dialect sparingly** — rhythm not phonetics (Rule 7).  
9. **Character by talk, not inventory** (Rules 8–9 + Steinbeck).  
10. **Hooptedoodle quarantined** — pretty language skippable (Steinbeck via Leonard).  
11. **Cold open sample:** “Foley had never seen a prison where you could walk right up to the fence without getting shot.” (*Out of Sight*)  
12. **Weather as inconvenience sample:** *Get Shorty* open — cold Miami only as jacket-stolen beat.
