# AUTHOR_PACKET — Cormac McCarthy

**Works consulted**
1. *The Road* (2006).  
2. *Blood Meridian* (1985).  
3. *No Country for Old Men* (2005).  
4. **Secondary:** Harold Bloom on *Blood Meridian* (violence not catharsis); Oprah interview (2008) punctuation self-report via Open Culture summary.

---

## EXCERPT NOTES (12)

1. **Ash / late world**  
   *Quote (widely anthologized):* “The ashes of the late world carried on the bleak and temporal winds… If only my heart were stone.”  
   *Location:* *The Road*, early.  
   *Principle:* Environment states metaphysics; narrator does not lecture.

2. **Absolute truth of the world**  
   *Quote:* “He walked out into the gray light… the absolute truth of the world. The cold relentless circling of an intestate earth…”  
   *Location:* *The Road*.  
   *Principle:* Verdict in setting properties, not speechifying.

3. **Good guys audit**  
   *Dialogue:* “Are we still the good guys?” / “Yes…”  
   *Location:* *The Road*.  
   *Principle:* Moral residual as short contract after institutions fail.

4. **Carry the fire**  
   *Quote:* “You have to carry the fire… It's inside you.”  
   *Location:* *The Road*, deathbed.  
   *Principle:* Symbol operational, undefined, not annotated.

5. **Trout maps / no repair**  
   *Paraphrase + clause:* brook trout maps of becoming; “could not be put back. Not be made right again.”  
   *Location:* *The Road*, close.  
   *Principle:* Beauty without restoration.

6. **See the child**  
   *Quote:* “See the child. He is pale and thin… mindless violence.”  
   *Location:* *Blood Meridian*, opening.  
   *Principle:* Withhold names; identity under duress.

7. **Evening lands**  
   *Quote:* “…toward the red demise of that day, toward the evening lands and the distant pandemonium of the sun.”  
   *Location:* *Blood Meridian* (Bloom’s “visionary center” ride).  
   *Principle:* High language can damn low agents by juxtaposition.

8. **War is god**  
   *Quote (Judge):* “War is the ultimate game… War is god.”  
   *Location:* *Blood Meridian*, Judge lectures.  
   *Principle:* Violence as structure; monologue ≠ endorsement.

9. **Residual refusal (paraphrase + critical)**  
   Kid’s shred of humanity; laconic defiance; still dies.  
   *Principle:* Decency may be incomplete, quiet, fatal.

10. **Coin toss**  
    *Quote:* “What’s the most you ever lost on a coin toss? … You’ve been putting it up your whole life. You just didnt know it.”  
    *Location:* *No Country*, gas station.  
    *Principle:* Procedural calm as menace; fairness as sadism’s etiquette.

11. **Bell’s failed stewardship**  
    *Quote:* “I always thought when I got older that God would sort of come into my life… He didn’t…”  
    *Location:* *No Country*, late Bell.  
    *Principle:* Good system admits insufficiency without becoming cartoon villain.

12. **Punctuation ethics (self-report)**  
    *Paraphrase:* periods, capitals, occasional commas; no quotation marks; “If you write properly you shouldn’t have to punctuate.”  
    *Source:* Oprah 2008 (secondary summary OK).  
    *Principle:* Restraint is engineering, not costume.

## CORE THEMES
Moral residual · Landscape as judgment · Refusal of consolation · Violence as structure · Fate vs code · Stewardship failure · Anonymity/erasure · Thin speech.

## NEVER DO
Tidy growth lessons · Heroic gore montage · Over-explained symbols · Easy innocence wins · Equal-length refutation of Judge/Chigurh.

## BAD IMITATION
Faux-Biblical wallpaper · No-punctuation affectation · Edgy void with no residual · Chigurh-speak everywhere · Heroic father sermons.

## SPACEFACE MAP (hypothesis)
Temp/spectrum as class court · HUD labels after safety gone · Flat register · Residual duties that cost · Anti-catharsis combat · Prefer *No Country* calm + *Road* residual over *Blood Meridian* baroque.

## CONFIDENCE
High on widely attested *Road*/*No Country* quotes; medium on micro late *Blood Meridian* fragments via Bloom; page numbers omitted (editions vary).
