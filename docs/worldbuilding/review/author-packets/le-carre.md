## EXCERPT NOTES (15)

1. **Control’s defensive hygiene**  
   *Quote (primary, widely attested):* “Thus we do disagreeable things, but we are defensive… We do disagreeable things so that ordinary people here and elsewhere can sleep safely in their beds at night… you can’t be less ruthless than the opposition simply because your government’s policy is benevolent, can you now?”  
   *Location:* *Spy*, Ch. 2 (Circus briefing).  
   *Device / effect / principle:* Soft institutional rationalization; benevolence as branding. **Paperwork and tone launder harm.**

2. **Come in from the cold**  
   *Quote:* “We have to live without sympathy, don’t we? … one can’t be out in the cold all the time; one has to come in from the cold…”  
   *Location:* *Spy*, Ch. 2.  
   *Principle:* Offers of rest/exit are often recruitment into deeper use.

3. **Promise as kill-switch (paraphrase, primary plot)**  
   Leamas extracts a promise that Liz Gold stay clear; Control agrees; the Circus later uses her.  
   *Location:* mid-book promise → tribunal → Wall.  
   *Principle:* **Delayed recontextualization** — early “kindness” re-means as prep for sacrifice.

4. **Leamas’s late deconsecration**  
   *Quote (widely attested late Wall speech):* spies as “a squalid procession of vain fools, traitors…” not monks balancing rights and wrongs.  
   *Principle:* Truth dump clarifies for the reader; does not save the characters.

5. **Idealism discarded (paraphrase)**  
   Fiedler (thoughtful East German) is sacrificed so London can keep ruthless Mundt as asset.  
   *Principle:* Systems keep working instruments; burn believers in stated values.

6. **Fake picture / Witchcraft**  
   *Quote (primary, also film):* “Ever bought a fake picture? … The more you pay for it, the less inclined you are to doubt its authenticity.”  
   *Location:* *Tinker Tailor* (Smiley to Esterhase on Source Merlin).  
   *Principle:* Prestige and budget make a fraud sacred (**sunk-cost intelligence**).

7. **Nursery-rhyme mole map (paraphrase)**  
   Control codes seniors Tinker/Tailor/Soldier…  
   *Principle:* Darkest work hides in trivial mnemonic codes.

8. **Betrayal as personal (secondary synthesis + primary plot)**  
   Haydon’s treachery wounds friendship, marriage, Prideaux — not only secrets.  
   *Confidence:* high on plot; motives partly opaque in-text.  
   *Principle:* Institutional crime reads as private desecration of trust.

9. **Distributed complicity (paraphrase)**  
   Only Haydon is Moscow’s man; others enable via ambition/vanity.  
   *Principle:* Catching one mole does not cleanse the house.

10. **Karla takes the lighter (paraphrase)**  
    Karla leaves with Smiley’s lighter (Ann’s gift) after Smiley reveals the soft spot.  
    *Principle:* What you love is inventory the system will use.

11. **Theatre of the real**  
    *Paraphrase/primary via close-reads:* operation framed as theatre; handlers own plot; asset improvises dialogue with life (*Drummer Girl*).  
    *Principle:* **Handler casts for traits already half-performed.**

12. **Kurtz as multi-passport energy**  
    *Quote excerpted in criticism of primary:* Kurtz “wheeled and dealed and lied even in his prayers” (early *Drummer Girl*).  
    *Secondary-only for exact wording if edition unavailable — treat as paraphrase if not re-verified against a locked edition.*  
    *Principle:* Director of ops is social engineer first.

13. **Family language for kill team (paraphrase)**  
    Unit called “family” while staging deaths to install Charlie.  
    *Principle:* Soft register does not reduce ruthlessness.

14. **Non-cathartic aftermath (paraphrase)**  
    Mission succeeds; Charlie breaks; no victory parade.  
    *Principle:* Institutional win ≠ human settlement.

15. **East/West method parity (structure)**  
    Western and Eastern methods mirror across *Spy*; decline/elite self-seeking in *TTSS*; same casting apparatus relocated in *Drummer Girl*.  
    *Principle:* Do not award ethical victory to better stationery.
