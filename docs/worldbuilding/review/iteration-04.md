**REVIEW ITERATION 04 — CONTINUITY, PACING, AND TIC-DEDUCTION**

*Pass conducted after iterations 01–03 and the LITERARY-AUDIT. The audit's
Section II structural fixes (arithmetic closed; recurring beneficiary wired;
Vale-as-algorithm; Vethari P&L; backfire-upward windfall; smell-complaint
punchline; endgame loop) were already applied prior to this pass, as were most
of Section III amputations (worst-line nominees gone; dead-author surnames
stripped from body prose; DESIGN RATIONALE blocks fenced). This iteration
targets what those passes left behind: residual verbatim couplets, an
un-resolved "not a conspiracy" reflex, two prose duplicates, three concrete
continuity errors, a first-chapter pacing bug, and the leftover Borderlands/
Firefly register in the canonical vibe doc.*

---

**Finding 1 — verbatim couplet de-duped (audit §III.1)**

The "count never ends / only the counters change" couplet appeared in three
canonical prose homes (SYMBOLISM-MOTIFS, STORY-SPINE, factions-CANONICAL) plus
the Kurtz figure's dialog and chapter-01 graffiti. The dialog and graffiti
instances are in-voice and kept. The three prose instances were re-written to
carry the *specific* version of the idea rather than the same skeleton:

- `STORY-SPINE-NARRATIVE-OVERLAY.md` (closing): replaced the couplet with
  *"The first contract is still pending. The next one is already on the board."*
- `vibe/SYMBOLISM-MOTIFS.md` (airlock section): re-grounded in the Pit's
  headcount ritual — *"heads counted against the last headcount. The numbers
  change. The counting doesn't."*
- `orgs/factions-CANONICAL.md` (sign-off): *"Every faction still takes the count."*

The canonical home for the count motif is now the chapter-01 graffiti line and
the Kurtz figure's parting line, where it is dramatized rather than recited.

**Finding 2 — "not a conspiracy" reflex rationed (audit §III.3)**

Five canonical instances of the *"This is not a conspiracy. It is Y."*
announcement reflex were reduced to one canonical home
(`SYMBOLISM-MOTIFS.md` "The Official Version" — *"This is not a conspiracy. It
is database architecture"*), which is where the idea is actually explained.
The other four instances (PROTAGONIST, STORY-STRUCTURE, NPC-ECOLOGY,
SYMBOLISM-MOTIFS REF-44-C section) were rewritten to *show* the apparatus
rather than *announce* that it isn't a conspiracy. The Vethari P&L passage was
tightened to drop the "men in a room" frame while preserving the load-bearing
budget framing the audit praised.

**Finding 3 — prose duplicate removed (vibe-04-the-pit.md)**

The "upper decks have better filters/administrative staff are healthier"
paragraph appeared twice in succession (lines 35 and 37), a leftover from an
editing merge. The weaker instance was deleted; the sharper version ("discipline,
diet, and the right attitude") retained.

**Finding 4 — "this is the point" tic retired (audit §III.3)**

The last in-prose instance of the reflex — `PROTAGONIST.md:191` *"The artifact
is boring. This is the point."* — was cut to *"The artifact is boring."* The
boringness (the audit's protected le Carré microdot) now lands without the
author leaning into frame to grade it. The boringness is still the point; it
just no longer says so.

**Finding 5 — Borderlands/Firefly register retired from canonical vibe (audit §III.6)**

The canonical vibe document was mid-molt: the colder voice the rest of the
bible had grown into was still being introduced by the older Whedonesque
register. Revised:

- **Core Tone:** dropped "Vietnam-era punk graffiti," "we're all gonna die /
  might as well get paid first," and "ex-gangster with ink under the scars."
- **Origin:** dropped "reactor that prays to Saint Jude" and "three idiots";
  the reactor is now "fine" for three owners (echoing the protagonist's
  *fine*-as-confession joke, which the audit protected).
- **Story Escalation:** replaced "steal its lunch money, sell it back to them
  at a markup" (cornball) with "skim it, sell the skim back to it at markup."
- **Anachronism fix:** "one actual UN inspector" → "one actual Concord
  inspector." The setting is a galactic region governed by Concord, not
  present-day Earth; the "UN" reference was a leftover from the early vibe
  drafts.

The superseded vibe-01/02/03 files retain the Whedon register but are clearly
marked SUPERSEDED with deprecation headers — no action needed there.

**Finding 6 — setting-scope consistency ("solar system" vs galactic)**

The protagonist canon establishes a galactic setting ("hundreds of systems,"
"this part of the galaxy," "a solar system he'd never heard of" — singular and
deliberate). The GDD confirms "Galaxy/Sector map" and "charted civilization."
Two canonical-homes used "solar system" loosely as shorthand for the settled
region; corrected to "settled reaches" in `SYMBOLISM-MOTIFS.md` and
`vibe-04-the-pit.md`. The deliberate singular in `PROTAGONIST.md:71` ("a solar
system he'd never heard of") is left intact — it is correct in context.

**Finding 7 — chapter-01 pacing / foreshadow bug (audit §I, "if every door opens onto a crime")**

The first airlock dumped five graffiti lines on the player on the very first
visit, including:

- `THE COUNT NEVER ENDS` — which the same file's staging section (lines 30–31)
  says appears on the *second* and *fourth* visits. Contradiction.
- `HELIOS DIDN'T NEED IT. THEY TOOK IT ANYWAY` — the Ritchie reveal punchline
  (the wrong-grid / shelved-by-Thursday twist from NPC-ECOLOGY). This is an
  endgame-tier realization that a first-run player cannot have earned. Giving
  it away on the first airlock collapses the entire reveal arc.

Rewritten so the first visit shows **one line** (`THEY KNEW THE MASS`), with
`THE COUNT NEVER ENDS`, `FORTY-SEVEN LEDGERS. ONE SHIP`, and `THE HOLE HAD NO
LOCKS ON THE INSIDE` staged across subsequent visits. The Helios line is
explicitly removed from Chapter 01 and reserved for a much later, missable
appearance "in a bay that smells faintly of machine oil."

**Finding 8 — endgame choice-count contradiction (audit §II.7)**

`ENDGAME-B7-REDESIGN.md` opened with *"Three choices, each of which is honest
about what winning actually costs"* and *"The player has three contracts
available. Only one can be accepted"* — but the document then defines **five**
choices (A Clean Uniform, B Same Silence, C Only Honest Option, D Ledger
Continues, E Next Run) and its own craft appendix refers to "the five-choice
structure." This was a leftover from the 3-choice draft before D and E were
added (per `AGY-PROMPTS-FOR-USER.md` Prompt 6).

Fixed:
- Opener now reads *"Five choices, each of which is honest about what winning
  actually costs."*
- The "THE CHOICE" intro now correctly describes the five paths (two board
  contracts A/B, graffiti-only C, ledger-gated D, decline-all E) and notes
  that *"Nothing tells the player the fifth exists."*
- The "Why these choices and not one" section previously described only A–D
  and called D *"more honest than the other three."* Choice E was missing
  entirely. Added Choice E to the section and corrected the comparison.

**Finding 9 — Vale over-insistence reduced (audit §II.3)**

The *"Vale is not a villain"* announcement appeared in three forms in the
antagonist doc: the opening characterization (line 15), the thesis heading
*"Why Vale is not a villain:"* (line 43), and the section body. The audit
flags the over-insistence itself as the costume ("You only have to say it once
if it's true"). With the structural fix (Vale as algorithm, not hand on the
lever) now in place, the insistence is unnecessary:

- The thesis heading *"Why Vale is not a villain:"* was renamed *"What there
  is to expose:"* — describing the mechanism rather than announcing the thesis
  (audit §III.4: delete the thesis headings).
- The opening characterization was re-led to land by *what Vale does* rather
  than by three negations (*"not a villain… not evil, not corrupt in any
  theatrical sense"*). The strong closing image ("eats food that doesn't taste
  like an apology") is preserved.

**Finding 10 — Ashfall Reach story/mechanics conflict resolved (reconciliation)**

`design/world-identity/STORY_SECTOR_MAP.md` listed Ashfall Reach's faction as
`faction_vael` with boss-arena POIs, matching `src/data/sectors.js`. The
worldbuilding story doc `ENDGAME-B7-REDESIGN.md` cast S9 as a quiet derelict
Kurtz-figure encounter with no combat. Investigation showed this is **not** a
case of stale data trailing the story canon — it's a genuine conflict between
two authoritative visions:

- The **mechanics side** is live and tested: `world.js:_spawnBossIfDue` spawns
  the Iron Maw dreadnought, gated by a CI golden (`check-gameplay-core.mjs:5831`),
  and the current spec thread `SPEC3-F7:111` defines Ashfall as "dreadnought
  arena (exists)… final-act staging, trophy claim."
- The **story side** (`ENDGAME-B7-REDESIGN.md`) described a quiet derelict with
  no combat.

Per `AGENTS.md` authority chain, SPEC3 + live data + CI outrank the
worldbuilding story doc — so the boss arena is the live vision and the story
doc was the stale side. (An earlier draft of this finding inverted the
direction; corrected.)

**Resolution (per owner decision): reconcile, don't pick a side.** The boss
becomes the gate; the witness sits behind it. Rewrote `ENDGAME-B7-REDESIGN.md`:
- "The Kurtz figure" section now opens with the dreadnought as the system's
  last enforcement on the approach, and stages the derelict-station encounter
  *past* the defeated boss ("the cost of admission to the part of the sector
  the system no longer patrols").
- The "Cross-reference notes" no longer say the boss POIs "must be overridden."
  They are **kept** (`poi_boss`/`poi_vault` retained in data); the Ancient
  Vault is re-skinned in narrative as the Kurtz figure's sealed records cache,
  with the ledger as the takeable cargo rather than legendary loot.
- The Implementation note was updated so the station can be *scanned* early
  ("SCAVENGER — UNKNOWN") but not *docked* until the Iron Maw is down — the
  boss is the gate.

`STORY_SECTOR_MAP.md`'s divergence annotation was rewritten from
"⚠ Unresolved conflict" to a "S9 reconciliation" note recording the staged
sequence. No game data (`src/data/sectors.js`) needed to change — the live
boss content was already correct; only the story doc's claim that it should be
removed was wrong.
