Structural arithmetic was largely *rewired in prose* by iterations 04–06 (legal 12.4t, MTS short, Vale initials, loop ending, eight struts). The remaining job is:

1. **Sew residual contradictions** (REG vs REF; Overlay four vs five endings; B0 mine vs alloy; phase labels).

3. **Densify +30%** as integrated units that reweight earlier hours (not new lore islands).
