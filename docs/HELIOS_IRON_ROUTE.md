# Helios: first iron route

An accepted authored Hitch ticket selects a genuine beam-workable iron face using
the current living-world field view, not the nearest ice or crystal body. The
original authored site is preferred; a depleted or missing face falls back to a
real eligible Helios face or an honest search hint. Foreign-sector travel points
back to Helios rather than targeting a foreign rock.

Ready typed indexes remain authoritative, including empty indexes. Dormant rocks
supply positions only; ordinary approach/mining owns promotion. Stale/recycled
occupants, depleted/unknown resources, oversized tow chunks and anchored structures
do not become controls targets. Receipt and equipment requirements are unchanged.

the existing mining navigation branch in `src/systems/missions.js`.
