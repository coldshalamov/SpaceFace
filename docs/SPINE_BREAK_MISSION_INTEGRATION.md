# Spine Break: one convoy, one mission

## Player route

The traders' Book 4 operation previously posted a generic escort: its lead could reach Ceres and settle without the separate 701 Spine Break encounter ever running. Simply requesting 701 would spawn an unrelated second cast and its unsaved tracker would disappear on Continue. The new mission-owned worksite instead connects the existing Meridian cargo-spine mechanics to the actual accepted escort.

The operation materializes one lead and six cargo boxes using the existing Spine Break layout: two initially linked and four loose. The authored Meridian goal is three real recouplings, not six. The existing Meridian coupler closes the gaps; linked bodies follow the lead via queued physics impulses, without a scripted position teleport. Dock settlement requires the actual mission-owned three-coupler outcome and the exact live lead. A lost or unrecoverable first attempt gets one whole-worksite retry; a second loss fails the mission. Refused pickups do not count as theft.

The mission records a bounded cast manifest keyed by mission, attempt and slot. Save/Continue remints runtime occupant generations; an explicitly scoped restore window rebinds the exact saved identities rather than trusting recycled numeric IDs. Mission freight is excluded from generic Meridian recovery claims and payments, so canonical mission settlement is the only payout. The ambient 701 route and ordinary Meridian freight remain their existing owners.
