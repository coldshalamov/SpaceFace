# BLACK FLAG: Shipjack & Chop Shop

Work in progress. See [implementation and acceptance limits](BLACK_FLAG.md).

Start an ordinary Adventure. The Empty Seat broadcasts near Corridor Pin 44C in the
starting traffic pocket. Choose **Acquire vessel signal**, then **Read frame & signal**.
The universe continues moving while the vessel link is open. Its displayed choices also
work through the normal decision deck's keyboard, gamepad and touch controls.

Guarantee safe passage for a peaceful approach. Match the drifting hull's velocity,
attach the normal Massline (Space on the default keyboard binding), close within 115 WU,
and hold steady. The link explains shields, drive, weapon, spin and tether problems.
Expose a service port, assert helm control, then choose transfer. The two-second crossing
can be cancelled by returning to flight. Custody permits use; it does not grant clean title.

Your outgoing ship stays in space with its damage, fittings and cargo. Mark its beacon
and use **Registry & recovery** in station Shipworks to plot its location or commission
a finite tow. Remote hulls do not follow you through jumps.

A disabled acquired hull offers a distress channel. Pulse the scanner to reopen it.
This marks its beacon; no service tow has accepted. Transfer back to your nearby retained
hull and tow the prize yourself, or commission paid retrieval from a station. A living-pilot
NPC tow into the final docking cradle has not passed physical validation.

At a receiving berth, keep the prize, return it to its claimant, extract individual
fitted components, repair components, settle a lien or offer a non-active empty hull for
sale. Extraction takes time and requires returning to the receiving berth to collect.
Pulling the drive disables propulsion. Hot property requires an illicit buyer; honest
yards check title. A licensed recovery registry can arrange **Compensate claimant / buy title**
for an eligible civilian claim. Pay the posted compensation and registry fee to keep the
damaged hull with lawful title and a reissued broadcast. Crew must be safe; government
frames withhold private title. This does not erase offenses, liens or removed-part evidence.
Forging a broadcast consumes real scanner hardware and fools only
shallow inspections. Paint, broadcast, underlying frame and legal title remain separate.

Open **Spare components / resale** in Registry & recovery to appraise a removed part.
The confirmation names its condition, exact payout and fence cut. A disputed part keeps
its source evidence when fitted to another hull or when the original shell is returned.
Establish lawful salvage title or take it to a named fence; clearing WANTED is separate.

Use C/the normal scanner action to reopen a selected vessel's link. **Return to flight**
closes only the link. Failure messages describe the surviving route rather than promising
an automatic capture. The Blackbird and later recovery opportunities use the same verbs;
station contracts pay through the existing mission system.
