## Ranked findings

### F1 — The stepped ledger refill tears the shared scratch while the memo key still describes the old committed disc: mid-suspension readers either pay the corridor walk synchronously inside their ctx mint, or collect the wiped/half-filled set under a valid-looking stamp

- **Evidence.** `_meshFarScratch`/`_meshRockScratch` (`src/world/presentationSources.js:28-29`) and the memo keys `_meshFarKey`/`_meshRockKey` (`:36`, `:46`) are module-level state shared by every driver. `warmNearbyLedgerRowsSteps` (`:622-660`) mints `yield* queryFarActorsSteps(state, _meshWalkOrigin, ..., _meshFarScratch)` at `:644-645` and `yield* queryAsteroidFieldSteps(..., _meshRockScratch)` at `:655-656`; each inner generator wipes the SAME array in its first step (`farActorTable.js:596`, `asteroidField.js:185` — `out.length = 0`), then fills it segment-by-segment across presented beats while `rememberMeshFarKey`/`rememberMeshRockKey` run only after the `yield*` completes (`:646`, `:657`). So during any suspended refill the invariant every reader assumes — *the scratch contains the rows the committed key describes* — is false: the key still stamps the old disc (state/far identity + origin + radius + `farVersion` all intact, `:296-302`, `:304-325`) while the scratch holds a partial prefix of the NEW disc. Readers: (i) **non-tolerating collect mints** — `collectMeshPresentationEntitiesChunked` calls `_nearbyLedgerRowsContext(state, opts)` at `:698` and the ctx executes the plan's miss verdicts *synchronously*: `queryFarActors` at `:491` and `queryAsteroidField` at `:501` — the unbounded corridor grid walk W79 sliced — inside the collect's FIRST `next()` (`:694-697` comment admits the ctx mint is deliberate hoist-point). Reachable mid-suspension from the cook collects (`renderer.js:11576`, `:13801`), the sweep collects (`:19439`, `:19620`), and `collectMeshPresentationEntities` at `reattachResidentGpuMeshes` (`:1720`, inside the sector:enter keep-GPU tail `:15498`). A second driver minting a collect while another's `warmNearbyLedgerRowsSteps` is parked pays the whole corridor query in one presented frame anyway — W79 only removed the cost for the driver that ran the warm. (ii) **tolerating riders** — `ridesFar`/`ridesRock` are true whenever `toleration === true` (`:407-408`, `:428-429`), and `farCovered`/`rockCovered` (`:402-406`, `:424-427`) test the *key*, not scratch completeness. The hold-exempt remint collect mints with `{tolerateMiss: true}` (`:2717-2718`) and always rides; the 'covered' mint (`:2557-2558`) rides whenever the old stamp contains the new disc and versions match — both then `.slice()` the torn scratch at `:699-700` and grade a partial subset as a complete collect → enqueue/decode-kick under-fire for beats (a row that should have joined the runway this beat waits → arrives late → pops in). (iii) **dueling refills** — two drivers can hold live `yield* queryFarActorsSteps` iterators into the same array at once (a cook's local iterator at `renderer.js:11553`/`:13780` + a sweep's `yield*` at `:19435`/`:19616`, or a parked `_holdExemptWarmIter`); each `out.length = 0` wipes the other's accumulated rows, the survivors union stale-graded rows, and the last `rememberMeshFarKey` stamps its plan's disc over a mixed set — the stamped disc then silently serves foreign rows to every 'covered' ride.
- **Mechanism.** Sweeps run every presented frame including 'loading' (`serviceRenderMeshResidency` at `:21671`), and `liveShellLatched` exists precisely because sweeps run while cooks are parked — so cook × sweep coexistence is real, not theoretical. During travel a quantized-cell flip or a `far.version` bump starts a stepped refill; a cook collect minting on the same beat re-pays the atomic walk (a presented-frame hitch, the exact class W79 targeted), and a remint serve delivers a half-filled disc as coverage. Player-visible: late admission → LOD stand-in or pop-in; the under-collect direction is the pop-in class this lane owns.
- **Fix sketch.** Give each refill a private scratch: mint `{x,z}` origin + two fresh arrays inside `warmNearbyLedgerRowsSteps`, swap them into the shared slots inside `rememberMeshFarKey`/`rememberMeshRockKey` (commit = swap + stamp, one step), and let ctx mints during an in-flight refill keep reading the PREVIOUS committed arrays (retain a second slot pair) instead of the live torn ones — ride semantics unchanged, row sets identical at every commit boundary, determinism unaffected. Cheap half-measure: an in-flight refill epoch counter — non-tolerating ctx mints on miss while a refill is in flight serve the previous committed snapshot (like the `tolerateMiss` ride) rather than a sync walk; tolerating mints already ride, they just must ride the *committed* set, not the torn one. Effort M · Magic-frame impact H · Risk M — the ride/'covered' contract must move to "serve last committed," and collect-mint ordering needs care (the collect's `.slice()` must snapshot the committed arrays, not the work-in-progress).

### F2 — A `far.version`/`field.version` bump mid-walk is committed under the *post-bump* version: rows inserted into already-walked cells are invisible while the stamp claims coverage they never got

- **Evidence.** `rememberMeshFarKey` reads `far.version` at commit time (`:315`), and `rememberMeshRockKey` likewise. The stepped walk reads the grid live per segment (`farActorTable.js:625-633`, `asteroidField.js:229-236`), so segments that already yielded never re-see cells. `table.version++` fires on insert (`farActorTable.js:454`), removal (`:562`), cell re-key (`:401`), and a within-cell advance crossing the recorded collectDisc rim (`:405-413`); `field.version++` fires on `addFieldRock` (`asteroidField.js:169`, `:254`, `:301`) — procedural rocks materialize on the collect horizon exactly while the player moves, i.e. mid-walk. A suspended refill that completes stamps `farVersion` = current — `meshFarKeyMatches` (`:296-302`), `farCovered` (`:403-406`) and `rockCovered` (`:424-427`) then all PASS on content whose head segments predate the bump.
- **Mechanism.** A far row inserted or re-keyed into an already-walked cell during a multi-beat suspension never enters the scratch; the committed disc claims the new version, so every subsequent 'covered'/match consumer re-reads the missing-membership set as complete until the *next* bump or cell flip. For a fast inbound row inside the decode runway, admission/decode starts late by however long the next invalidation takes — the deferred-decode → late-mesh pop-in path. Same shape on the rock side: a materializing rock missed at walk time pops into existence when the next refill finally lands.
- **Fix sketch.** Snapshot `far.version`/`field.version` at refill start; at commit, stamp the START version (not current) — the next plan's version check then re-catches the drift and refills under the normal slice clock. Two-line change per arm; strictly contract-preserving (the stamp then under-claims rather than over-claims — safe direction). Alternative: bail without stamping on drift and let the next mint re-walk — also safe, slightly more re-work. Effort S · Magic-frame impact M · Risk L — a version that churns faster than the refill completes could starve the stamp; bounded by the tolerateMiss rides and still strictly better than a false stamp.

### F3 — `residency.releaseDetachedBoundaryOwners` is one atomic O(owners) scan with a per-release O(assets) budget enforce — inside ONE `next()` of the stepped twin

- **Evidence.** `_releaseDetachedBoundaryOwnersSteps` (`renderer.js:19086-19164`) chunks the claimed-set census (`:19097-19135`, yields at `:19104`, `:19114`, `:19121`) and the per-owner teardown (`:19146-19160`, yield per owner), but the actual scan+release runs as a single call: `residency.releaseDetachedBoundaryOwners({isDetached, isClaimed})` at `:19137` → `assetResidency.js:397-412` loops every owner (`[...owners.entries()]` at `:404`) with no chunk; each hit calls `releaseOwner` (`:366-389`) which runs `evictIfUnowned` per entry AND `enforceSoftResidencyBudgets()` at `:387` — an O(assets) census over all resident entries (`:584-595`) per released owner. Worst case: a mass-detach wave (sector exit parking whole subtrees) → O(owners × assets) inside one generator step, after the stepped census already ran.
- **Mechanism.** The stepped twin yields around a hidden atomic core; the sweep cadence is ~1/s shared across reconcile+poll (`residencyPruneSweepDue` at `:19625-19632`), so roughly once a second one presented frame can carry the whole O(owners×assets) release storm in a single `next()` — same defect class W79's twin exists to remove (its own comment claims "identical claim semantics" and per-step yields, but the residency-side scan escaped the cut). Late-seen side is nil (reclaim is bookkeeping), but the stall lands in the frame the player sees.
- **Fix sketch.** Expose the owner scan to the generator (`residency.enumerateBoundaryOwners()` returning the released list is already the shape — split isDetached/isClaimed evaluation per owner into the stepped loop and call `releaseOwner` one owner per yield), and hoist `enforceSoftResidencyBudgets` to once per sweep — releases are monotonic so deferring enforcement across the batch preserves the budget contract (it already early-outs under `packageCacheBudgetDepth`, `:579`). Effort M · Magic-frame impact M · Risk L.
