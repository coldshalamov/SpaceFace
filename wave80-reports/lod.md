## Ranked findings

### F1 — Suspended stepped-warm refills share `_meshFarScratch`/`_meshRockScratch` with every interleaved ctx mint; an interleaved writer wipes the partial fill (`out.length = 0` at first next()) and the suspended warm resumes appending tail rows, then stamps its plan's disc over mixed content — corrupt memo → 'covered'/strict rides under-collect until the next key-invalidating event → missing ledger rows → decode-runway misses → in-frame debuts

- **Evidence.** The scratches are module-level shared arrays (`src/world/presentationSources.js:28-29`). `warmNearbyLedgerRowsSteps` drives `yield* queryFarActorsSteps(state, _meshWalkOrigin, walkRadius + collectOverlap, _meshFarScratch, batchRows)` at `:644-645` and only then calls `rememberMeshFarKey` at `:646` (rock twin `:655-657`). `queryFarActorsSteps` (`src/world/farActorTable.js:595`) opens with `out.length = 0` at **first next()** (`:596`), copies `pos.x/z`/`radius` once (`:602-604`), and yields per column (`:632`); `queryAsteroidFieldSteps` is identical (`src/world/asteroidField.js:185`, per-column yield `:235`). The append to `out` is by-reference into the shared array. Interleaved writers/readers exist and are reachable while a warm is suspended:
  - `_nearbyLedgerRowsContext` (`presentationSources.js:458-522`) — on memo-miss it runs the SYNC `queryFarActors(state, _meshWalkOrigin, walkRadius + collectOverlap, _meshFarScratch)` (`:491`) which inline-drains `queryFarActorsSteps` → wipe + refill + `rememberMeshFarKey` (`:492`). Reached by every chunked collect mint (`collectMeshPresentationEntitiesChunked` `:698`), `warmNearbyLedgerRows` (`:610`), and the only sync collect caller `reattachResidentGpuMeshes` (`renderer.js:1720`, under `_sessionRecookKeepGpu` loading recook, `:1858`).
  - A second `warmNearbyLedgerRowsSteps` instance: sweep `yield*` sites (`renderer.js:19435`, `:19616`), cook warm drives (`:11553` widen, `:13780` jump — suspended across real `await yieldLiveSectorGpu()`/`providerYield()` legs), hold-exempt warms (`:2549` tolerateMiss 'covered', `:2707` tolerateMiss true — parked across whole beats). `serviceRenderMeshResidency` runs every frame (`:21671`, no mode gate), so a parked hold/cook warm overlaps sweep warms and ctx mints routinely.
  - Read side: `appendNearbyLedgerRows` iterates the LIVE scratches (`:599-600`); `collectMeshPresentationEntitiesChunked` snapshots `slice()`s at mint (`:699-700`) — either consumes a wiped-mid-fill array as a complete set.
- **Mechanism.** `warmNearbyLedgerRowsSteps` only walks when `!meshFarKeyMatches && !ridesFar` (`:638`) — i.e., under a stale key. Suspend mid-walk → scratch holds a partial row prefix. Any interleaved ctx mint for a *different* needed disc also sees `!match` and refills: `out.length = 0` wipes the partial, refills its disc, stamps its plan. The suspended warm's `yield*` then resumes and appends its remaining tail rows **after** the refill's rows; its `rememberMeshFarKey` stamps the warm's plan disc over `[refill rows + warm tail]` — the warm's own prefix is gone. Thereafter `meshFarKeyMatches`/`covered` rides for the warm's disc return true while the scratch under-collects that disc's prefix columns → far/rock ledger rows silently missing → decode-runway kicks and build-queue candidates skipped → hull debuts inside the presented frame. Persists until the needed disc, `far.version`, radius, or bucket invalidates the key — i.e., as long as the player stays in the stamped cell and no far-table membership bump lands (seconds during a sector transit). Same-disc interleaves only duplicate rows (per-row verdicts filter) — the corrupt case needs two different walk discs, which is exactly what a quantized cell flip between the two warm mints produces. Pre-W79 the refill was synchronous so the interleave could not exist; this is a W79-introduced mechanism inside the "each memo key still commits only when its walk completes" claim (`:614-621`) — the stamp part holds, the scratch part does not.
- **Fix sketch.** Fill a walk-local array and publish atomically: `const staging = []; yield* queryFarActorsSteps(state, origin, radius, staging, batchRows); _meshFarScratch.length = 0; _meshFarScratch.push(...staging); rememberMeshFarKey(...)`. The swap lands inside the same step as the stamp, so no interleaved mint can observe a mid-fill (chunked-collect mints either see the old complete set or the new complete set). Alternative: a scratch generation counter bumped at every refill start and recorded into the memo key — ctx mints treat generation-mismatch as a miss. Local-fill is smaller and keeps row order identical to the sync twin.
- **Effort** S–M · **Magic-frame impact** H (arrival stand-ins/late debuts inside presented frames, the exact class W79's sliced refill was meant to keep; needs the two-different-discs interleave which transit produces routinely) · **Risk** M — must preserve the `slice()` snapshot contract at `:699-700` and the tolerateMiss:true `:640/:650` wipe paths verbatim.
