# P16 exact image-generation requests

S9 follow-on: the Crucible arena picker covers seven rooms; P15 shipped five arena tiles.
These two requests complete the set in the same camera family, grade, and material language
so the Mill and the Hive stop wearing the Foundry's picture. Until the masters land, the
picker shows each arena's own glyph and the hero shows the active mode's art.

Reference for both: `.devshots/ui-packets/S2-work/P15/masters/tile.arena.ricochet-foundry.png`
(the approved style anchor the other four arena tiles shared).

## tile.arena.asteroid-mill

Tool: built-in `image_gen`.
Reference image(s):
- `.devshots/ui-packets/S2-work/P15/masters/tile.arena.ricochet-foundry.png`

Exact request:

```text
Create ONE finished SpaceFace Crucible arena keyart tile, landscape 16:9, largest useful native output, requested 2560x1440 (at least 1280x720). Reference is our approved Ricochet Foundry tile: keep the same lens, high three-quarter view (40-degree downward camera, 50 mm), far-field horizon at 38% height, solid physically rendered game geometry, warm near-black shadows and copper-amber/ash-brown filmic tone mapping. This is a DIFFERENT arena with a new subject/layout, not a colour change. Compose specifically for a 160 px-wide tile: three strong masses, clear central playable channel, foreground/midground depth, crisp silhouette. Lighting must reveal real material and contact/occlusion. No UI, no text, no letters, digits, emblems, labels, logos, or watermark. No painting, concept illustration, movie wallpaper, vast sci-fi cities, cockpit, scanlines, hex grids, neon holograms, fuzzy glows, noise fields or fog blanket. Full-bleed opaque image, no border.
Subject: Asteroid Mill. A dense working ore seam on a slow industrial grind: two heavy opposed crusher plates of worn gunmetal and bolted amber-lit jaw liners closing on a diagonal seam of tumbling basalt rock and ore chunks, one clear navigable gap of open floor between the plates. Crushed fragments and dust trail off the seam with real depth; sparse broken rock continues to the shared horizon. Amber worklamps on the plate housings, near-black recesses under the seam, restrained copper bounce on fracture faces. This reads as a working rock mill the player threads — substantial unromantic machinery, physically worn materials, no lava, no fireworks.
```
