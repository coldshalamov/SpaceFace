# Large-place component review

These are CPU Blender renders of freshly imported exported GLBs, not screenshots of the game's runtime. All models are authoring-library candidates and remain subject to scene-specific runtime, collision and performance review.

The family follows the existing Forge fabrication yard's open load-bearing lattice, mechanical depth, restrained safety-orange handling parts and occupied warm glazing. Habitat pieces use the existing Helios ivory/teal language; launch infrastructure uses the slate/red military language. No donor meshes or external models are incorporated.

The drydock wall has an occupied control house, guarded service walkway, attached reel drive, articulated handling arms and pressure-service lines. Its large bays remain open. The pressure dome uses a seated annular foundation, segmented meridians, latitude clamps, a compression crown/oculus and a separate airlock. The pictured service socket sits on its finished housing, not inside it.

LOD1 and LOD2 are authored topology passes: removal of small fitted hardware and bevel complexity, preserving square load-bearing sections, sockets, silhouette and clearances. They do not rely on collapsing thin beams to ribbons.

Regenerate views with the source recipe's `--review drydock_side_wall` or `--review pressure_dome_habitat`; create labelled sheets with `--contacts`, `--contacts --lod2`, or `--contacts --source`. Composition previews use `--assembly-contacts` after `--assemblies` and require the explicit other-bank dependencies.

## Attachment and composition refinement

The final pass moved the capital berth flange onto its actual jaw and retained a non-rendered approach marker. Approach markers generate no decorative bolts. Drydock-wall joins gained end diaphragms and bulkhead plates; buttress joins now sit on their real feet. Service pads have structural plinths wherever the earlier surfaces left a gap.

The solar collector spine is physically joined to a habitat-spoke end. The coolant loop begins at a pump skid seated on the cylinder crown. The relief corridor mates to the habitat airlock. The quarry parent/wedge pair rests on the survey deck instead of floating beyond it; removing the wedge is represented in the after-use recipe. Opposing district banks intentionally remain independent orbital structures around the unobstructed approach, not falsely welded into one rigid body.

The frozen recipes record exact dependency LOD hashes, metre-native transforms, socket joins, proxy-clear approach and exit envelopes, and state membership. Unit-scale preservation and exact socket coincidence are technical proofs; interface pressure certification, runtime physics and normal-route visual acceptance are not claimed.
