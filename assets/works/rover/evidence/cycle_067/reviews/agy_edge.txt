### (1) Obvious Defects (Works-Edge View)

- **Side Walls:**
  - The transition flanks flanking the central hopper well remain flat, planar triangular card/clay wedges sloping inward.
  - Lacks structural plate thickness, welded seams, corner gusset stiffeners, and heavy industrial armor faceting.
- **Track Height & Undercarriage (Solid Rubber Belts vs. Wire Loops):**
  - Tracks are modeled as solid continuous rubber tread belts with transverse cleats (not wire loops), maintaining full belt height.
  - Inboard track channels feature flat light-gray filler card strips and open voids lacking mechanical drive sprockets, front idler rollers, track guides, or suspension bogie mountings tying the belts into the chassis.
- **Dark Cab House & Yellow Visor:**
  - The cab house is a dark, blocky rectilinear box primitive rather than an armored, chamfered enclosure.
  - A flat circular toy-like disc/button is glued directly onto the center of the cab roof.
  - Yellow livery is correctly restricted to a thin forward-projecting protective visor brow past the windshield glass (not yellow cab sides, not a plastic square on the roof), but the dark cab superstructure lacks structural corner pillars and bevels.
- **Dark Cab Pane:**
  - The forward windshield pane is visible beneath the visor brow as a dark recessed rectangular cutout.
  - Lacks an authored structural retaining bezel frame, rubber gasket seal, and Fresnel specular reflectivity.
- **Hopper Well:**
  - The central hopper well is an unresolved stepped box cavity flanked by flat planar transition ramps.
  - Lacks heavy AR-plate wear liners with fastener patterns, corner gussets, rim reinforcement coaming, and bottom chute/tailgate geometry.
- **Darker Boom, Steel Spine, & Bit Body:**
  - The boom extending forward (+X starboard) is an extruded, flat-sided dark polygonal slab.
  - The central steel spine along the top is a basic thin strip rather than a reinforced machined feed rail.
  - The cutter bit at the forward tip is a basic pointed cone rather than a defined, multi-faceted machined steel rotary cutter head.
- **Scar Plate:**
  - The aft push/scar plate is a flat, untextured light-gray rectangular card slapped directly onto the rear crossmember.
  - Lacks plate gauge thickness, perimeter bevels, modeled hex bolt fasteners, and recovery hitch/towing lugs.
- **Toy / Clay / Card / Box / Gray Plate Artifacts:**
  - **Rear Scar Gray Card:** Featureless, untextured light-gray rectangular card slapped on the aft crossmember.
  - **Inboard Deck Filler Gray Cards/Plates:** Light-gray flat card strips bridging the gaps between the hull body and track assemblies.
  - **Hopper Flank Sloping Wedges:** Flat planar triangular clay/card ramps forming the side walls of the hopper.
  - **Cab Roof Slapped-On Disc:** Flat cylindrical toy disc/button glued onto the cab roof.
  - **Rectangular Box Cab:** Primitive blocky CAD box volume without armored chamfers or structural faceting.
  - **Flat Slab Boom Arm:** Extruded polygonal dark slab giving a cardboard/clay mockup appearance.

### (2) MTX Defects Visible at this Play Size

- **Material Truth & Value Balance (MTX-33/34/35/56):**
  - Slapped-on rear scar plate and inboard track filler cards render as raw, unshaded light-gray cards completely detached from the industrial steel palette.
  - Dark cab body and hull blend into uniform muddy diffuse tones with insufficient separation between cast armor, structural girders, and machinery.
- **Glazing & Specular Response (MTX-07/36):**
  - Cab windshield pane lacks dielectric clearcoat / Fresnel specular response and sits without an authored retaining frame or gasket.
- **Surface Normal Relief & Hardware (MTX-05/10/12):**
  - Absence of micro-relief, weld beads, panel lap joints, hex bolt heads, and access hatch details across atlas tiles.
  - Roof disc lacks functional housing, louver, or lens geometry.
- **Thermal & Wear Gradients (MTX-40/41):**
  - Cutter bit lacks thermal heat discoloration (straw/purple/blue/soot) from high-friction ore contact.
  - Hopper mouth and track sponson edges lack curvature-driven edge wear down to bare steel/primer.
- **Forbidden Reads (MTX-46):**
  - Directly violates the forbidden read by displaying glued flat gray cards, planar clay wedges, and toy roof discs on a blocky box chassis.

### (3) Concrete Blender Actions (No Cabin Furniture)

1. **Reconstruct Side Walls & Chassis Deck:**
   - Remove the flat triangular clay/card ramps flanking the hopper well and the light-gray filler cards along the tracks.
   - Model solid, heavy-gauge steel side walls with chamfered reinforcing ribs, welded corner seams, and integrated chassis deck armor.
2. **Rebuild Rear Scar Plate:**
   - Replace the flat light-gray card with a thick, beveled steel push/scar plate proud of the rear crossmember.
   - Add modeled hex bolt fasteners at the corners, perimeter chamfers, and an integrated tow/recovery hitch bracket.
3. **Refine Cab House, Visor, & Glass Pane:**
   - Chamfer and facet the dark cab house into a heavy armored enclosure.
   - Delete the slapped-on flat roof disc; replace with a flush/recessed maintenance hatch or hooded intake louver.
   - Maintain safety yellow strictly on the crisp, projecting protective brow visor shelf extending forward past the glass.
   - Recess the dark glass pane into a bolted steel retaining frame with a rubber gasket, and assign a reflective tinted dark glass PBR shader with Fresnel falloff.
4. **Deepen Hopper Well & Add Liners:**
   - Deepen the central hopper well and model interior AR-plate wear liners with visible bolt patterns, corner stiffener gussets, and a heavy coaming rim around the hopper mouth.
5. **Detail Boom Arm, Raised Steel Spine, & Drill Bit:**
   - Rebuild the boom as a heavy structural boxed girder arm with reinforced pivot brackets.
   - Model a distinct, raised machined steel spine/feed-rail along the upper surface of the boom arm.
   - Model a heavy-duty multi-faceted steel/tungsten rotary cutter bit head with defined cutting flutes, collar mounting, and heat-stain gradient.
6. **Tie In Track Undercarriage:**
   - Anchor the solid rubber continuous track belts into the chassis using modeled structural side frames, idler wheels, drive sprockets, and bogie mountings to eliminate open card gaps.
7. **PBR Material & Atlas Alignment:**
   - Assign distinct PBR shader channels: dark textured abraded rubber for tracks (`track`), heavy industrial dark equipment enamel for hull/cab/boom (`livery`/`steel`), bare machined steel for the boom spine and bit body (`steel`/`bit`), safety yellow strictly on the visor lip (`livery`), and reflective tinted dark glass for the cab pane (`glass`).

### (4) Verdict

**REVISE**

*Rationale:* While safety yellow is properly constrained to the visor brow and tracks are solid rubber belts rather than wire loops, the model still suffers from slapped-on flat light-gray cards (scar plate and inboard deck filler strips), flat clay triangular hopper side ramps, a primitive CAD box cab with a glued roof disc, and a blocky slab boom. It requires structural remodeling before passing review.
