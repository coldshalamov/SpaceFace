### (1) Obvious Defects (Works-Edge View)

1. **Toy / Clay / Card / Box / Gray Plate Aesthetic**:
   - The main chassis deck, rear shelf, and side deck transitions are flat, untextured, featureless gray planar cards and stepped box slabs. The rover reads as an assembly of cardboard cutouts and plastic toy plates rather than heavy-duty industrial mining machinery.
   - The cab roof features a primitive, unintegrated flat circular stud/cylinder artifact.

2. **Side Walls**:
   - The side walls and deck flanges flanking the hopper well and connecting the chassis to the track sponsons are flat, uniform gray plates with crude polygon cutouts. They lack structural thickness, stiffener ribs, chamfers, panel seams, and welded flange relief.

3. **Track Height (Solid Rubber Belts vs. Wire Loops)**:
   - The crawler track units lack solid vertical body depth and mass. They still read as thin perimeter loops / flat tread bands wrapping around a hollow housing with edge clips, rather than heavy, thick solid rubber crawler belts with substantial cross-sectional profile and drive depth.

4. **Cab House & Yellow Visor**:
   - The yellow accent on the cab front is modeled as a thick protruding block/tab strip rather than being restricted strictly to a thin, crisp safety-painted visor brow projecting past the glass.
   - The cab housing remains a basic boxy shell with unresolved flat top geometry.

5. **Dark Canopy Pane**:
   - The dark canopy pane is visible on the front cab face, but lacks a defined structural bezel/gasket or recessed depth, reading as a flat dark rectangular cutout directly abutting the yellow block.

6. **Hopper Well**:
   - The hopper well cavity reads as a stepped punch-hole cutout with a flat dark partitioned floor and an asymmetrical stepped ramp rather than a continuous, heavy-duty industrial sloped chute lined with wear-resistant steel coamings.

7. **Drill Boom, Steel Spine & Bit Body**:
   - While the boom housing is darkened, it lacks a distinct, raised bright steel spine running along its top centerline into the bit assembly.
   - The drill bit terminates without a clearly articulated, machined steel bit body and mechanical collar assembly.

8. **Scar Plate**:
   - The bolted transition plate (scar plate) between the cab and hopper well is a flat, basic rectangular card with four simple circular indentations, lacking beveled wear edges, heavy-gauge plate thickness, or structural mounting brackets.
