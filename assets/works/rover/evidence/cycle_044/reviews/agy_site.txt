### (1) Obvious Defects from Site View (~22 px Scale)

1. **Gray Stamp / Floppy Disk Read:** The chassis reads as a flat, desaturated gray square stamp / electronic cassette with an interior cutout rather than a heavy industrial tracked mining crawler.
2. **Yellow Crescent / Hook Defect:** The yellow livery registers as an awkward curved yellow crescent/hook wrapping around the central well cutout and a yellow blotch at the aft-starboard corner, completely missing the required crisp, thin yellow visor brow line over the windshield band.
3. **Starboard Track Dark Crush:** The starboard (bottom) crawler belt suffers severe value crushing against the hull shadow (`TRACK_CONTRAST_STARBOARD` = 3.20 vs $\ge 18.0$), reading as an indistinct dark smear rather than a solid rubber wall with a 1-pixel steel catch lip.
4. **Port Track Wire Loop:** The port (top) track reads as a bright perimeter wire/outline framing a dark void rather than a solid, dense rubber crawler belt.
5. **Flattened Cab & Windshield:** The cab lacks vertical step relief (`CAB_IS_RAISED` = 0.1529 vs $\ge 0.28$), collapsing into a flat dark rectangular square patch flush with the deck without visor shading.
6. **2D Punch-Hole Well:** The central well reads as a flat 2D punch-hole cutout (`WELL_IS_A_HOLE` = 0.0694 vs $\ge 0.24$) rather than a recessed hopper with raised steel coaming.
7. **Boom Offset & Spine Definition:** The boom arm is tucked too tightly against the hull (`BOOM_OFFSET` = 0.1418 vs $\ge 0.15$) and lacks a raised specular steel spine along the dark arm to catch a 1-pixel highlight line connecting into a faceted steel bit.
