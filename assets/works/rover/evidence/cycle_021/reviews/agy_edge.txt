### (1) Obvious Defects (Works-Edge View)

* **Toy / Clay / Card / Box / Gray Plate Read:** The rover heavily reads as a primitive gray blockout with flat yellow sticker plates dropped onto the sponsons. The silhouette is dominated by sharp 90-degree boolean cutouts, monolithic gray slabs, and unchamfered geometry lacking mechanical mass, structural framing, panel seams, or industrial hierarchy.
* **Side Walls & Fenders:** The side sponsons are flat, unchamfered rectangular gray boxes. The yellow plate strips on top read as flat toy decals/stickers rather than heavy-gauge, recessed safety access panels/inner-track safety paint on a structural steel machine.
* **Cab House (Starboard Front):** The cab structure is a hollow, razor-thin cardboard-style cutout with a square roof opening. It lacks structural pillar thickness, roll-cage/armored cowl framing, visor overhangs, grab handles, or external work-light/sensor pods. (No cabin furniture requested).
* **Hopper Well (Rear Hull):** The hopper is an unarticulated rectangular boolean cutout dropped into the rear chassis. It lacks internal sloped deflector plates, liner wear-ribs, reinforced perimeter rim flanges, hinge blocks, and hydraulic lift cylinders.
* **Boom & Linkage (Midsection):** The center linkage consists of rudimentary yellow pins/pegs set into black bracket blocks. It lacks functional hydraulic cylinders, chrome piston rods, actuator housings, fluid hoses, and structural mounting clevises.
* **Scar Plate / Snout (Front Tool):** The forward tool is a faceted, low-poly gray snout/wedge with a flat tip plate stuck directly onto the front body. It lacks an articulated push-frame, hydraulic rams, bolt-on wear plates, and distinct carbide scarifier teeth/cutting bits.
* **Track Height & Assemblies:** The track bands appear as simplified extruded loops with basic round hub insets. They lack defined grouser tread teeth/pads, track tensioner assemblies, bogie clusters, and visible drive sprocket teeth engaging the tread.

### (2) MTX Defects (Play Size / Works-Edge)

* **Monochromatic Gray Value Slump:** Chassis, cab structure, sponsons, and snout share an undifferentiated, flat medium-gray diffuse tone, causing the silhouette to collapse into a low-contrast mass at gameplay zoom.
* **Unanchored Safety Panels:** The yellow safety zones lack dark perimeter recessed frames, bevel highlights, or bolt borders, reading as floating bright yellow pixel rectangles instead of painted steel hazard covers.
* **Flat Void in Hopper:** The hopper interior reads as an unlit, flat dark hole without ambient occlusion falloff, rim highlights, or interior structural geometry.
* **Zero PBR / Material Differentiation:** No discernible material contrast between cast structural steel, sheet-metal access panels, hardened cutting steel, and dark rubber/steel tracks.
* **Sub-Pixel Aliasing on Thin Features:** The thin cab wall cutouts and tiny linkage pins lose definition and alias at standard gameplay viewing distances.
