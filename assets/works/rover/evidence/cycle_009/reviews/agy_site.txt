### (1) Obvious Defects Visible from Site View (~22 px Read)

1. **Fragmented "Twin-Dot / Gray Block" CAD Read (Chassis Cohesion Failure):**
   - The vehicle does not read as a cohesive heavy industrial mining machine. The central chassis is a flat, muted gray rectangular block broken only by two isolated, tiny yellow squares/dots on the upper and lower mid-flanks. 
   - Rather than reading as structural hazard-painted bodywork or heavy cowlings, the yellow elements look like floating toy stickers or random bright pixels on a CAD blockout (`ONE_BODY` failure at `0.3939` vs `0.90` floor).
2. **Drill Bit Visual Detachment:**
   - The boom arm extending to the starboard side is dark gray and very thin, while the cutter head at the tip is a bright pale circle/dot.
   - At 22 px, the boom shaft blends into the dark background while the cutter bit pops as a disconnected, floating white dot rather than the rigid tool head of a heavy articulated arm.
3. **Weak Excavation Well Rim Definition:**
   - The excavation well registers as a dark circular cavity ("dark bite"), but the framing collar lacks top-lit specular pop and bevel width. The boundary between the well and the surrounding gray deck is soft and muddy rather than a crisp, reinforced structural coaming (`WELL_IS_A_HOLE` at `0.2190` vs `0.24` floor).
4. **Starboard Track Shadow Bleed & Value Loss:**
   - While the port (top) track displays distinct sprockets and tread segments, the starboard (bottom) track run lacks sufficient value separation from its own contact shadow and the dark ground plane, losing clean track silhouette definition (`TRACK_CONTRAST_STARBOARD` at `17.27` vs `18.0` floor).
5. **Indeterminate Central Superstructure & Cab:**
   - The forward bridge and operator cab area are unresolved flat gray masses with no distinctive glass specular highlight, roof cowl, or structural beveling to convey forward orientation or operational volume.
