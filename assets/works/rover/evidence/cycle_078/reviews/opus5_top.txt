VERDICT: REVISE

Reviewer: opus5-top. Still: works_top_1to1.png (320x320 native, ~120 px of rover). Framing valid --
tilted top-down works camera, whole rover in frame, not a cabin interior, not a studio three-quarter.

0. CYCLE 78 vs CYCLE 77 -- THE SAME PICTURE

Diffed the two 1:1 crops pixel by pixel. 1,031 of 102,400 pixels differ = 1.0%. Mean delta over those
pixels 7.9 summed RGB (~2.6 per channel), max 78. Every cluster except one is dust-level noise at
anti-aliased edges. The only visible change is a thin bar at x173-210, y176-179: the boom spine got
brighter. Nothing else moved.

Everything cycle 77 named is unchanged -- tan/pale card body, black-rectangle hopper mouth from the
top, yellow bar visor, absent steel lip. The single change traded "too faint to see" for a hard white
pinstripe, which is the continuous-luminous-outline read this brief bans by name, applied to a
different part. Section 0 anti-gaming bans iterations with the same stills defect; a 1%-of-pixels
delta whose only structural content is one line's brightness is that row with a measurement on it.

1. DEFECTS AT PLAY SIZE

Silhouette -- PASSES. Squarish body slung between two track pills, boom off one corner. Legible as a
tracked works rover.

Tracks -- solid belts, but no outer lip, and the structure is paint.
  * Belts are right: two dark stadium shapes ~20 px, solid, grouser cross-hatch. Not paperclips, not
    wire loops, not a luminous outline.
  * The raised steel outer lip DOES NOT EXIST. Vertical profiles at x=115/135/155/175 on both tracks:
    ground R72-77; crossing the outer edge into the belt the value only ever DROPS (72,72,71->44 at
    x=135; 72,72,70->44 at x=155; 72,69,68,62->36 at x=175). Nothing on the outer edge rises above
    ground value along the straight run. Single-pixel spikes of 88 and 83 are corner glints,
    indistinguishable from aliasing sparkle.
  * The bright bead is on the WRONG EDGE -- the light-catching line runs the inner (deck-side) edge,
    y=130 reading 81,93,94,99,102 then a mottled 65-83 run. Inner, not outer, and it varies, so it
    reads as a stitched seam or decal edge.
  * In clay the tracks are two featureless white lozenges -- no belt, no grousers, no lips, no road
    wheels, no sprockets. All track structure lives in the surface, not the form.

Mid tub -- THE GRAY PLATE, AND IT IS INVERTED. Row y=140 holds twenty-eight consecutive pixels of
essentially one value (R97-98), about a quarter of the rover width: no gradient, no panel break, no
seam channel, no thickness, no material change. It is also BRIGHTER than the terrain it sits on (tub
97-98 vs ground 72-77). The brief asks for a dark cab house on a MID steel tub; this is the brightest
large surface in the frame. That is a spec inversion, not just flatness. The two diagonal creases read
as scuffs on a card.

Cab house -- dark and correct, but a plain box. Reads R~40 against the deck. Roof is a flat brown
rectangle with nothing on it; in clay it is a plain box.

Windshield band -- visible, and a black rectangle sticker. Genuinely there and dark (x174-179, six px,
y~152-166) but measures R1 G1 B5: flat across all six pixels, hard 90-degree corners, no frame, no
mullion, no rim thickness, no depth cue, pasted on a flat roof. You cannot tell it is a hole rather
than a painted square.

Yellow visor line -- structurally CORRECT, executed as a glowing filament. Eight rows checked; order
is right everywhere: cab roof (~40,24,4) -> glass (1,1,5) -> visor -> ground (76,60,42) from x=183.
Thin vertical line, full height of the glass, only past the glass. NOT a roof postage stamp, not a
U-frame, not two track bars, not yellow cab sides -- the ask is met. The problem is intensity: x=181
reads R244 G197 B76 where the next brightest large surface is 98, ~2.5x anything else on the model.
It reads as a lit filament, not a painted metal lip.

Hopper well -- THIS ONE WORKS. DO NOT TOUCH IT. A real hole: lit near wall, both side walls, a ribbed
floor, a deep shadow band at the far edge, a chamfered rim. In clay it has a dark floor at depth. It
is distinct from the pane -- the hopper shows walls and a floor, the windshield shows nothing. That
contrast is the single best thing in this still. Caveat: at native brightness it collapses toward a
dark brown rectangle on a pale plate because the rim is the same cream deck material as its surround.
That is a contrast problem in the surround, not a form problem in the well.

Boom -- a dark card with a white racing stripe. The arm is a flat dark olive parallelogram (R~33), no
thickness cue, no top-versus-side value break. The spine measures R123 G110 B94 over three pixels, 4x
the arm, dead uniform its entire length: no break, no segmentation, no taper, no shading. Desaturated
gray-white in an entirely warm frame, so it reads as a foreign decal rather than steel.

Bit -- a white paper scrap, not a steel body with a bright tip. All cream-white, so there is no dark
steel body for a bright tip to contrast against and "bright tip" carries no information. Shape is a
small irregular ~6 px blob reading as a torn card chip. Not a tan mushroom and not a yellow hook --
the previous two failure modes are gone -- but a white card scrap is its own banned read.

Greebles -- confetti. Two dark clusters flanking the cab carry tiny cream specks with no readable
hardware shape. Noise, not fasteners.

Value structure -- ground 72-77, tub 97-98, belts 33-46, cab 40, arm 33, hopper 30-55. The model
occupies two bands (dark mechanical, one bright plate) with no mid value of its own; the "mid" in the
frame is the terrain. Everything outside those bands is a sticker: the 244 yellow, the 123 white, the
1 black.

Named banned reads, explicitly: GRAY PLATE = the mid tub, 28 px of constant R98, brighter than the
ground. CARD = boom arm, cab roof, bit. BOX = cab house in clay. TOY = tracks as two smooth pills with
tread printed on them. CLAY BLOCKOUT = the clay pass reads lozenge + lozenge + plate + box + wedge +
stick.

    primitives, no surface technique may be marked implemented.
  * Section 0 anti-gaming -- FAILS (1%-pixel cycle, one line brightness).
  * MTX-46 forbidden reads -- FAILS, and this alone fails the leaf. Two smooth pills + plate + box in
    a supported works view is the LEGO-stack read.
  * MTX-01 mid-poly edges -- FAILS. In clay the pills are smooth lozenges with no bevel-run highlight;
    the tub plate has no edge highlight at all.
  * MTX-03 skin-breaking boolean -- SPLIT. Hopper PASSES (opening, rim, wall thickness, empty mouth).
    Windshield FAILS the same row: a six-pixel R1 black rectangle with no rim thickness is the
    forbidden fake, "a dark plate named Recess".
  * MTX-31 classify the substance -- FAILS. Rubber belt, steel lip, painted tub, glass and warning
    yellow all sit in one warm taupe family. One Principled, five names.
  * MTX-33 authored ORM -- no evidence of channel variation in this view; the tub 28-pixel constant
    plateau is the flat-G defect class showing through to the beauty pass.
  * MTX-39 cavity dirt is brown/gray not black crayon -- FAILS at the windshield (R1 G1 B5).
  * MTX-52 macro form from reference -- FAILS. Rounded-rectangle pills, a box and a plate are
    blockout, not authored mass.
  * MTX-54 no silent revert -- not violated; the concern is the opposite, that nothing moved.

3. CONCRETE BLENDER ACTIONS

Honouring cycle 77 standing rulings: keep the dark hopper interior, no steel lip ring on the hopper,
do not steel the well walls, do not put the boom cap back. Leave the hopper entirely alone. No cabin
furniture, no seat.

  1. Build the TRACK outer lip as geometry (this is the track lip, NOT the hopper lip ring c77
     banned). Inset each belt outer face and extrude a 2-4 cm rail proud of the belt; bevel 2
     segments; weighted-normal. Target: outer edge reads 1-2 px ABOVE ground value along the whole
     straight run, not just at corners.
  2. Move the bead off the inner edge so the eye reads outer-lip-then-belt, not belt-then-seam.
  3. Put the belt in the mesh: grousers as extruded cleats at LOD0, plus drive sprocket and idler
     bosses at the pill ends. The clay must stop reading as a smooth lozenge.
  4. Break the tub plate: inset panel regions, solidify 2-6 cm, leave 1-2 cm seam channels, add a
     raised coaming where deck meets each track. Nothing on this deck should hold one value for 28 px.
  5. Darken the tub BELOW ground value -- from 97-98 into the 55-70 band -- so the cab house still
     reads dark against it but the model stops being the brightest thing in the frame.
  6. Cut the windshield instead of painting it: boolean the aperture through the cab roof with a
     4-8 cm rim so the mouth shows wall thickness from above; add one mullion bar; lift the interior
     off pure black into the 12-25 band. Rim and mullion only -- no seat, no console.
  7. Model the visor as a LIP, not a stripe: extrude the roof edge past the glass 4-6 cm, bevel it,
     paint that lip yellow. Drop the peak from 244 to ~150-180 and widen to 2 px. Keep its position
     exactly -- past the glass, full glass height. That geometry is correct.
  8. Break the boom spine into 4-6 segments with joints or bolt pads, taper toward the tip, let it die
     2-3 px before the bit, pull its hue warm, drop its peak from 123 to about 90.
  9. Give the boom arm thickness: solidify, let top face and side face carry different values.
 10. Give the bit a dark body in the 25-40 band, keep only the last 2 px of cutting edge bright. This
     is a bit body, not the boom cap c77 removed.
 11. Consolidate the greebles into two or three recognizable fittings, or delete them.

4. VERDICT

REVISE. A taupe box with a black rectangle for glass, a yellow filament, a white pinstripe and a white
paper bit is a gray box with stickers. Hitch-plus is the floor and this is well under it.

NOT REVERT, and the diff is the reason: cycle 78 is cycle 77 to within 1% of pixels, so there is
nothing to go back to. The one thing that changed -- the boom spine going from invisible to
visible-but-wrong -- is not a regression; a legible wrong line is recoverable, an invisible one was
not. Reverting would forfeit the only movement and land on an identical picture.

The hopper well is the one element that is genuinely done. Protect it.
