### 1. Obvious Defects From Site View (~19 px/cell, ~22 px Rover)

1. **Yellow Postage-Stamp / Box Read:**
   - The primary visual read is a solid yellow rounded rectangle/square. The vehicle lacks the characteristic negative space of an industrial crawler.
2. **Track Assemblies Fail to Separate:**
   - The upper and lower crawler tracks collapse into low-contrast, dark brownish bands that blend directly into the ground cell without a readable standoff or gap from the main chassis. They do not read as distinct parallel tread bands.
3. **Dorsal Well Reads as Flat Painted Decal:**
   - The interior cargo/service recess appears as a muddy brown square patch inside the yellow hull rather than a deep, shadowed physical cavity or skin-breaking bite.
4. **Boom Arm Resolves as a Smudge / Stub:**
   - The front offset boom arm extends only ~2ΓÇô3 faint, low-contrast pixels to the right. It lacks sufficient length, thickness, and silhouette prominence to read as an articulated mining tool or offset finger; at map scale it looks like an anti-aliasing artifact.
5. **Lack of Industrial Articulation:**
   - The overall asset presents as a toy-like monolithic yellow block with an inscribed dark square rather than a heavy tracked mining machine.

- **MTX-55 (Role silhouette at 40 px / site scale):**
