### (1) Obvious Defects Visible from `works_edge` View

1. **Toy / Clay / Card / Box / Gray Plate Read (Primary Visual Defect):**
   - The vehicle overwhelmingly reads as a primitive gray CAD blockout box with flat yellow highlighter strips pasted onto the upper deck shoulders flanking the hopper.
   - The yellow elements read as floating stickers or toy plastic cards on a gray polymer slab rather than painted safety panels mounted to a structural steel machine.
   - The chassis lacks structural integrity, reading as disjointed flat track slabs, yellow sticker strips, and an unarticulated gray deck bridge without panel breaks, weld seams, fasteners, or structural transitions.

2. **Side Walls & Flanks:**
   - Lateral side walls connecting the central tub to the track sponsons are flat, featureless gray surfaces lacking structural depth, stiffeners, outrigger gussets, and mount brackets.
   - Hull transitions and waist tapers toward the forward cab are rudimentary polygon steps rather than formed, heavy-duty industrial plate geometry.

3. **Track Height & Running Gear:**
   - Track assemblies read as flat brownish-gray blocks with uniform rectangular cleat tabs along the outer perimeter.
   - Circular corner sprockets/idlers look like flat stamped washers/donuts lacking tooth engagement, recessed bearing caps, and bolt patterns.
   - Material separation between rubberized track belts and steel end caps is muddy and indistinct at play size.
   - Undercarriage lacks internal mechanical depth (no road wheel bogies, guide horns, or track tensioner cylinders).

4. **Cab House:**
   - The forward cab is a hollow rectangular gray box with an unglazed cutout opening into a dark void.
   - Missing a welded structural retaining bezel, rubber weather seal/gasket, and a dark dielectric glass pane.
   - Missing exterior roof utility equipment: no armored work light pod, no louvered vent stack, and no lift eye hardware. (No cabin furniture or seat requested).

5. **Hopper Well:**
   - The hopper well is an empty dark rectangular cutout in the aft deck.
   - Reads as a flat black void rather than an excavated cargo well; lacks sloped abrasion-resistant (AR) wear plate liners, deflection ramps, perimeter bolt flanges, and a reinforced rim collar.
   - Missing rear dump tailgate hinge lugs and latch hardware on the aft transom.

6. **Boom & Cutter Head:**
   - The boom arm is a flat, solid gray tapered card/wedge rigidly protruding from the starboard forward deck.
   - Lacks mechanical articulation: no dual-clevis base pivot mount, hinge pin, hydraulic elevation cylinder, or hydraulic line bundles.
   - The cutter head is a simple blue-gray conical chisel/plug rigidly attached to the boom tip without a rotating drum, carbide pick teeth, or heat discoloration.

7. **Scar Plate:**
   - Flank wear/scar plates are visually absent or flush with the hull, lacking independent plate thickness, bevels, perimeter hex fasteners, and abrasive wear.

### (4) Verdict

**REVISE**

*Rationale:* The asset reads as a yellow box with plates, unlined void cutouts, and an unarticulated boom wedge. It lacks mechanical track running gear, has unlined void cutouts for the cab and hopper, and lacks essential hydraulic cylinders, hose routing, and edge bevels. Substantive structural remodeling across the chassis, boom kinematics, track running gear, and hopper interior is required.
