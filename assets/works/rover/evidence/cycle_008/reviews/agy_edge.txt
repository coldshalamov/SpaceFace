### (1) Obvious Defects Visible from `works_edge` View

1. **Toy / Clay / Card / Box / Gray Plate Read (Primary Visual Defect):**
   - The rover reads as a toy CAD blockout composed of flat gray cardboard plates and square collar cutouts glued over yellow substrate backing cards.
   - The hull fails to read as a heavy fabricated steel machine with bolted yellow safety panels; instead, yellow appears as flat decorative strips/cards under a crude gray plate frame.
   - The cutter head reads as a low-poly faceted gray gem/frustum glued onto the tip of a flat polygonal wedge stick.
   - The dark cutout voids in the yellow forward deck read like toy injection-mold punch holes rather than mechanical access cutouts or structural weldments.

2. **Side Walls:**
   - The lateral flanks between the main body and the track sponsons lack structural depth, rocker sills, and mechanical connection hardware.
   - Flanks are flat planar vertical drops without access hatches, mounting brackets, lift eyes, or heavy weld seams.

3. **Track Height & Running Gear:**
   - Track assemblies read as toy rubber ladder belts wrapped around two end idler/sprocket bobbins with an open hollow gap between the runs.
   - Missing internal undercarriage running gear: no road wheels/bogies, return rollers, track tensioner cylinders, or center guide teeth.
   - The lower (starboard) track run collapses into low-contrast shadow against the ground plane.

4. **Cab House:**
   - The cab is a simple rectangular gray collar surrounding a hollow black window cutout.
   - Lacks an authored structural bezel, rubber perimeter weather gasket, or dielectric glass pane with specular clearcoat.
   - Devoid of roof-mounted utility fixtures (no ROPS protective rock cage, work light fixture, or louvered intake vent).

5. **Hopper Well:**
   - The hopper is an unlined rectangular hole cut into the rear gray bridge plate, dropping into an unrendered dark void.
   - Missing internal structural volume: no angled AR wear plates, internal deflection chute, structural stiffener ribs, or top rim flange lip.
   - Rear dump tailgate hinge lugs and locking pins are completely absent.

6. **Boom & Cutter Head:**
   - The boom arm is a solid, unarticulated dark gray trapezoidal wedge cantilevered rigidly off the starboard deck.
   - Missing mechanical joint articulation: no dual-clevis base pivot hinge, heavy hinge pin, hydraulic elevation ram/cylinder, or hydraulic service lines.
   - The cutter head is a simplified faceted octagonal cone with a flat blunt nose, lacking rotary carbide pick teeth drums and friction thermal oxide heat staining.

7. **Scar Plate:**
   - The scar/armor plate on the port/rear flank is a flat, featureless rectangle lacking plate bevels, perimeter hex fastener clusters, and heavy abrasive surface wear.

   - Apply a 2-segment Bevel modifier (`0.015 wu` width) and Weighted Normal modifier (`Keep Sharp`) to all structural deck plates and hull edges ([MTX-01](file:///C:/Users/93rob/Documents/GitHub/SpaceFace/docs/visual-assets/ADVANCED_MODEL_TECHNIQUE_CONTRACT.md#L139)).
   - Extrude structural lower rocker sills and undercarriage mounting brackets connecting the hull flanks to the track frames.

### (4) Verdict

**REVISE**

*Rationale:* The asset reads as a flat gray plate sandwich with yellow card cutouts and an unarticulated boom wedge. It lacks mechanical track running gear, has unlined void cutouts for the cab and hopper, and lacks essential hydraulic cylinders, hose routing, and edge bevels. It requires substantive structural remodeling across the chassis, boom kinematics, track running gear, and hopper interior.
