### (2) MTX Defects Readable at Site Size

* **Negative Track-to-Hull Luminance Contrast:** The tracks fail to provide the required dark ground-contact framing against the hull (failing `TRACK_CONTRAST` metrics), causing the vehicle to lose its tracked-base silhouette at gameplay resolution.
* **Hull-to-Boom Value Slump:** Chassis and forward boom tool share identical medium-gray diffuse values, merging the entire front half into an undifferentiated gray silhouette step.
* **Absence of Edge & Highlight Definition:** Lack of prominent edge bevels and material specular variation means high-contrast light catching is absent, preventing the eye from parsing mechanical depth, hopper walls, or track sprockets at low pixel counts.
* **Material Hierarchy Collapse:** The vehicle reduces to only three flat value steps (high-saturation yellow bars, flat medium-gray body, dark void/dots), reading as a flat icon rather than PBR heavy machinery.

3. **Hopper Well Rim:**
