### (1) Obvious Defects (Works-Edge View)

* **Toy / Clay / Card / Box / Gray Plate Read:** The rover currently reads as a primitive gray blockout with flat yellow stickers slapped on top. The entire silhouette is dominated by sharp 90-degree boolean cutouts and monolithic gray slabs lacking mechanical mass, structural framing, panel seams, or industrial hierarchy.
* **Side Walls & Fenders:** The side sponsons are flat, unchamfered rectangular gray blocks. The yellow strips placed on top read as flat toy stickers/decals rather than heavy-gauge, recessed safety access panels bolted onto a structural steel chassis.
* **Cab House:** The cab is a hollow, razor-thin cardboard-style cutout with a square roof hole. It lacks structural pillar thickness, roll-cage framing, armored visors, cowl bevels, grab handles, or external sensor/light fixtures.
* **Hopper Well:** The hopper is an unarticulated rectangular boolean hole dropped into the rear hull. It lacks interior sloped deflector plates, liner wear-ribs, reinforced top-lip rims, hinge lugs, or hydraulic lift points.
* **Boom & Linkage:** The midsection linkage consists of rudimentary yellow pins stuck into black brackets. It lacks hydraulic cylinders, actuator housings, fluid lines, and structural mounting clevises.
* **Scar Plate / Snout:** The front tool is a low-poly tapered gray wedge stuck directly onto the front-right body. It shows flat faceting, no articulated mounting bracket/push-frame, no hydraulic rams, and no hardened carbide cutting teeth or wear plates.
* **Track Height & Assemblies:** The tracks appear as thin, simplified extruded bands with generic circular hub insets. They lack individual tread links/grousers, track tensioners, bogie clusters, and visible drive sprockets engaging the tread.

### (2) MTX Defects (Play Size / Works-Edge)

* **Monochromatic Gray Value Slump:** Chassis, cab structure, and front scar plate share the same flat, medium-gray diffuse tone, flattening the silhouette into an undifferentiated mass at gameplay zoom.
* **Unanchored Safety Panels:** The yellow safety zones lack dark perimeter gaskets, bevel highlights, or bolt borders, causing them to read as floating bright pixel smudges instead of painted steel hazard covers.
* **Hopper Void Contrast:** The hopper interior is an unlit, flat black void lacking rim highlights, depth falloff, or material separation from the surrounding hull.
* **Zero PBR / Material Differentiation:** No discernible material contrast between cast-iron chassis framing, sheet-metal access panels, hardened cutting steel, and rubber/steel tread pads.
* **Track Silhouette Loss:** Outer tread teeth blur into the ground/shadow plane at play distance due to lack of distinct grouser geometry and ambient occlusion.
