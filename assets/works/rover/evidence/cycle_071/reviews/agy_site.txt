### (1) Obvious Defects FROM THIS SITE VIEW

1. **Gestalt & Fractured Stamp / Toy Read:**
   - At the ~22 px site scale, the rover resolves as a muddy, disconnected rectangular stamp with an isolated yellow patch rather than a cohesive heavy industrial tracked mining crawler (`ONE_BODY` = 0.3200 vs. 0.9000 min).
   - The silhouette lacks integrated industrial mass, reading as fragmented gray/brown patches and an asymmetric toy-like block.

2. **Track Belt Dissolution & Missing 1 px Outer Steel Lips:**
   - Neither the port (top) nor starboard (bottom) stadium belt holds cleanly as a solid dark rubber wall bounded by a thin raised steel lip catching a 1 px highlight.
   - The outer track edges dissolve directly into the terrain background and shadow envelope with severe negative contrast (`TRACK_CONTRAST_PORT` = -26.9908, `TRACK_CONTRAST_STARBOARD` = -23.7782 vs. +18.0000 min floor).

3. **Yellow Visor Failure & Misplaced Livery Block:**
   - The intended 1 px forward yellow visor line directly in front of the windshield band fails to register (`LIVERY_SAT_RENDERED` = 0.0400 vs. 0.3000 min).
   - High-contrast yellow paint is pooled on the starboard cab side / boom mount, reading as an asymmetrical yellow corner block / postage stamp rather than a defining forward hazard visor.

4. **Sunken Cab & Undefined Windshield Band:**
   - The cab structure is depressed below the deck plane (`CAB_IS_RAISED` = -0.1065 vs. +0.2800 min), and the windshield aperture in clay remains undersized (`CAB_PANE` = 0.0107 in clay vs. 0.0200 min).
   - The dark windshield lacks a clean mid-steel bezel, blending into adjacent chassis geometry rather than reading as a clear forward-facing canopy band.

5. **Intake Well vs. Cab Topographic Ambiguity:**
   - The intake well aperture in clay is undersized (`WELL_HOLE` = 0.0277 in clay vs. 0.0400 min) and lacks a raised mid-steel coaming lip.
   - Because the cab is sunken instead of raised, both the well and the cab read as depressed dark hollows without opposite sign relief (`WELL_CAB_DIFFER` fails).

6. **Boom Arm Clumping & Missing Articulated Steel Bit:**
   - The boom arm lacks sufficient lateral clearance (`BOOM_OFFSET` = 0.1376 vs. 0.1500 min floor), clumping directly against the starboard track.
   - The arm lacks a readable raised steel spine and sharp faceted bit tip, resolving as a dark stub rather than an offset finger with a bright steel mining tip.
