### (1) Obvious Defects (Site View Readability at ~22 px)

* **Yellow Highlighter Bar / Toy Read:** The safety-yellow areas still read as two prominent, hyper-saturated horizontal rectangular stickers/bars slapped onto the flanks. They dominate the visual footprint and create a toy/postage-stamp impression rather than integrated industrial safety plating.
* **Track Disconnection (Four-Nub Failure):** The tracks do not read as continuous, grounded dark stadium bands with bright end caps. Instead, the track assemblies dissolve into four isolated, faint gray corner nubs/ears protruding from the hull corners, with no continuous dark belt silhouette connecting them.
* **Hopper Reads as Flat Black Tile/Cutout:** The central hopper well reads as a flat 2D black square punched into the rear deck. There is no visible raised lip, lit coaming highlight, or sloped interior wall shading to read as a recessed hopper basin.
* **Boom Arm / Bit Muddy Articulation:** The forward boom extends as a generic, stepped gray tongue/finger on the starboard side. The transition between the boom arm, articulation knuckle, and the cutting head lacks visual definition, reading as a blunt gray tab with a slightly lighter tip rather than an articulated mechanical tool.
* **Chassis Value Flatness:** The central chassis and cab structure merge into an undifferentiated cluster of mid-gray pixels, lacking edge separation or distinct mechanical housing forms.

### (2) MTX Defects Readable at Site Size

* **Track Belt Luminance & Continuity Breakdown:** Track tread material fails to provide a readable dark band against the terrain and hull; the belts are lost in shading, leaving only the outer corner geometry visible.
* **Material Value Collapse:** The asset collapses into three crude, flat value steps: ultra-saturated yellow bars, uniform mid-gray structural metal, and pure-black voids (hopper/windows), giving it an icon/sticker read rather than realistic heavy machinery.
* **Missing Coaming & Wear Highlights on Hopper Rim:** Lack of a brightly catching, beveled metallic lip around the hopper mouth prevents the eye from perceiving depth and volume for the collection well.
* **Boom & Tool Material Hierarchy Slump:** The boom arm and cutter head share nearly identical diffuse/specular values, failing to establish a clear material hierarchy (dark structural arm $\rightarrow$ dark recessed joint $\rightarrow$ bright hardened carbide cutter).
