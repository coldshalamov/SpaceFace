### (1) Defects From This Site View

1. **Solid Yellow Stamp / Missing Dark Windshield**:
   * The yellow livery renders as a solid, flat yellow block/crescent attached to the front-right of the cab. Because there is no dark recessed cockpit glass or glint visible behind or beneath it, the yellow reads as an applied yellow postage stamp / toy brick rather than a functional visor hood framing an enclosed industrial cab.
2. **Monolithic Gray Stamp / Boxy Hull Read**:
   * The central hull and rear deck form a flat, unbroken gray slab between the tracks. It reads as a gray rectangular stamp rather than an articulated, heavy-duty mining crawler chassis.
3. **Starboard Track Trough Washout & Weak Outer Boundary Dominance**:
   * While the port (top) track loop exhibits a thin bright lip with internal shadow, the starboard (bottom) track interior is severely washed out and fails to hold shadow contrast against the lower chassis. Neither track loop asserts full silhouette dominance across the vehicle's lateral edges.
4. **Shallow Hopper Cavity**:
   * The cargo well appears as a flat dark square cutout on the deck plate rather than a deep, volumetric ore hopper with occluded shadow depth and crisp rim bevels.
5. **Faint Boom Spine Definition**:
   * The offset boom finger and bright drill bit are discernible, but the connecting steel structural arm lacks a sharp specular spine highlight, weakening the mechanical read of the tool arm at 1:1 pixel scale.
