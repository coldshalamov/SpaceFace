### 1. Obvious Defects (Works-Edge View)

* **Steel Cab House with Yellow Sides, Thin Yellow Visor & Dark Pane:**
  * **Visor Read:** The yellow feature at the front of the cab reads as a thick, blunt plastic square/tab attached to the roof/front rather than a thin, formed sheet-metal visor overhanging the windshield.
  * **Yellow Sides:** The cab side walls are unpainted raw gray steel; the cab lacks safety-yellow painted side panels called for in the spec.
  * **Dark Pane:** The pane area behind/beneath the visor reads as an empty dark square cutout hole in the roof plate rather than an angled windshield with structural window framing, glass specular response, or depth under an overhanging brow.
  * **Cab House Structure:** The overall cab assembly still reads as primitive gray stacked box geometry rather than an engineered industrial steel operator enclosure.

* **Toy / Clay / Card / Box / Gray Plate Read:**
  * The main central deck, chassis floor, rear aft deck, and outer tub surfaces are flat, featureless matte-gray slabs with sharp CG polygon edges.
  * The entire vehicle reads as an unpainted clay block, plastic toy kit, or cut-cardboard mockup due to the lack of panel breaks, weld seams, stiffener ribs, or functional surface relief.

* **Side Walls:**
  * The flanking side walls are flat, 2D extruded gray plates with simple 45┬░ angular chamfer notches.
  * They lack physical sheet thickness, rolled edges, structural stiffener ribs, or mounting flanges, directly reinforcing the flat cardboard plate look.

* **Track Height & Track Assemblies:**
  * Track assemblies remain low in vertical height and lack visible internal bogie/roller structure or undercarriage depth.
  * While the perimeter raised steel lips catch light, the rubber track loop itself is a flat stadium surface lacking discrete grouser/cleat tread geometry.

* **Hopper Well:**
  * The hopper well is a simple square cutout punched into the deck plate with a flat sloped back wall.
  * It lacks an extruded, raised perimeter coaming lip to deflect debris, as well as interior chute wear ribs / AR liner plates.

* **Darker Boom with Narrow Bright Steel Spine:**
  * The boom arm extending forward is a flat gray tapered wedge that lacks material separation.
  * The arm body is not sufficiently dark to contrast with the narrow central steel spine; the bright spine does not read as a distinct, raised, polished ridge.
  * The forward conical drill bit/cutter reads as a detached primitive cone glued onto a flat gray extension.

* **Scar Plate:**
  * The dual black rectangular mounting plates with yellow pivot dots behind the cab read like paper-thin 2D stickers/decals placed onto a gray plate rather than heavy, bolted, beveled steel wear plates.
