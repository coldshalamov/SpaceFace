### (1) Obvious Defects (Works-Edge View)

1. **Toy / Clay / Card / Box / Gray Plate Aesthetic**:
   - The central chassis, fenders, and deck surfaces read as flat, untextured gray slab primitives with sharp right-angle cutouts. The geometry resembles cardboard cutouts or plastic toy plates rather than heavy industrial machinery.
2. **Drill Boom & Bit Body**:
   - The boom is a flat, uniform light-gray rectangular bar lacking a darker structural boom housing and a raised steel central spine.
   - The tool head ends in a basic yellow plastic cap instead of a machined steel bit body.
3. **Cab House & Visor**:
   - The cab house is a generic dark box with a floating gray cylinder primitive on the roof.
   - Lacks structural yellow side panels framing the dark cab house.
   - The front yellow strip reads as an exterior plastic roof lip/bumper glued to the top edge rather than a thin, defined yellow visor line set in front of a visible, recessed dark glass pane.
4. **Track Height (Rubber Belts vs. Wire Loops)**:
   - The track assemblies appear as flat, thin loops with faint rung lines rather than tall, thick, solid rubber conveyor-style belts with physical height, tread depth, and drive sprocket definition.
5. **Hopper Well**:
   - Modeled as a basic box cavity with an unintegrated dark floor square and an asymmetrical stepped ramp, lacking industrial depth, sloped chute transitions, and reinforced liner plating.
6. **Side Walls**:
   - The side walls connecting the central hull to the track sponsons are flat, featureless gray slabs with no chamfers, structural ribs, or panel seams.
7. **Scar Plate**:
   - Missing dedicated front/leading-edge scar wear plates to protect the forward hull against abrasive regolith impact.

### (2) MTX Defects (Play Size)

- **Contrast & Value Hierarchy**: The dark windshield, dark cab roof, and hopper floor bleed into indistinguishable black patches at 1:1 scale, while the flat gray chassis washes out against the dark ground plane.
- **Boom Readability**: The drill boom is thin and stick-like with insufficient silhouette presence; the yellow tip is too small and reads as visual noise rather than an industrial mining tool.
- **Material Flatness**: Gray surfaces show uniform matte diffuse shading with no metallic variation, bevel highlights, or structural fasteners, giving the asset an unfinished blockout look in-game.

---

### (3) Concrete Blender Actions

1. **Boom & Bit Reconstruction**:
   - Darken the main boom arm structure.
   - Model a raised steel spine running along the top center of the boom.
   - Replace the yellow corner block with a dedicated steel bit body and mechanical collar assembly.
2. **Cab House & Visor Overhaul**:
   - Model structural yellow side panels/cheeks framing the dark cab house.
   - Recess the dark canopy glass pane and add a distinct, thin yellow visor line properly integrated within the glass housing.
   - Remove the unintegrated gray cylinder primitive from the roof.
3. **Track Belts & Undercarriage**:
   - Increase track height and cross-sectional thickness to read as solid, heavy-duty rubber belts.
   - Deepen track wells and define drive sprocket housings beneath the sponson guards.
4. **Hopper Well Rework**:
   - Deepen the hopper cavity and bevel the internal transitions.
   - Add segmented wear-plate ribs and a functional sloped floor chute.
5. **Chassis & Scar Plate Detailing**:
   - Add bevels, panel seams, and chamfers across the flat gray side walls and hull cards.
   - Model a heavy-duty reinforced scar plate on the leading edge of the chassis.
