### (1) Obvious Defects from Site View (~22 px Scale)

- **Gray Stamp / Floppy-Disk Silhouette:** The main chassis reads as a flat, desaturated grayish-brown rectangular card / floppy disk with a cutout in the middle, rather than an industrial tracked mining crawler.
- **Hollow "Paperclip" Track Loops:** Both port and starboard tracks still read as wire loops / paperclips. The thin bright outer lips dominate the outline around dark recessed slots rather than reading as solid, heavy dark rubber crawler belts with crisp 1-pixel steel catch lips.
- **Visor Reads as an Isolated Yellow Hook / Crescent:** Instead of a thin, forward-projecting yellow visor line shading the windshield band, the yellow livery reads as a chunky yellow hook/crescent sitting awkwardly on the forward deck.
- **Cab & Windshield Collapse:** The dark cab lacks elevation and clear structural separation from the deck, collapsing into an ambiguous dark block adjacent to the yellow hook rather than reading as an elevated operator cabin over a mid-steel tub.
- **Cookie-Cutter Well Cavity:** The central well reads as a flat, 2D punch-hole / washer opening in the middle of the tub rather than an engineered mechanical hopper with coaming and stepped depth.
- **Weak Boom & Muddy Tip:** The boom reads as a flimsy dark stick/finger with a faint orange/yellow dot at the tip. It lacks the raised steel spine needed to catch a 1-pixel highlight line leading into a bright, faceted cutting bit.
