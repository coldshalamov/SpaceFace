### (1) Obvious Defects FROM THIS SITE VIEW

1. **Gestalt & Chunky Stamp / Yellow Blob Read:**
   - At ~22 px site scale, the rover fails to read as a cohesive, heavy industrial tracked mining crawler.
   - The silhouette fractures into an indistinct dark/gray rectangular stamp disrupted on the starboard forward side by a chunky yellow block/hook rather than reading as articulated functional machinery.

2. **Track Belt Dissolution & Missing 1 px Outer Steel Lips:**
   - Neither the port nor starboard stadium belt holds cleanly as a solid dark rubber wall defined by a thin raised steel lip catching a 1 px specular highlight.
   - The outer track edges bleed into the terrain shadows with negative contrast (`TRACK_CONTRAST_PORT` = -26.99, `TRACK_CONTRAST_STARBOARD` = -23.78), while inner track edges form overly thick, light bands.

3. **Yellow Visor Failure & Misplaced Livery:**
   - The 1 px forward yellow visor line is completely missing/washed out in front of the dark windshield band (`LIVERY_SAT_RENDERED` = 0.0), losing the defining forward-facing brow read.
   - High-contrast yellow paint is clumped at the boom mount / starboard flank, reading as an asymmetrical yellow block rather than a purposeful hazard visor.

4. **Cab Sunken & Undefined Windshield Band:**
   - The cab structure is depressed below the deck (`CAB_IS_RAISED` = -0.1193 vs. +0.28 min) and the windshield aperture remains undersized (`CAB_PANE` = 0.0131 in clay).
   - The dark windshield band lacks a crisp mid-steel framing bezel, blurring directly into adjacent chassis geometry.

5. **Intake Hopper Well Lacks Defined Coaming Lip:**
   - The well opening is undersized in clay (`WELL_HOLE` = 0.0277 vs. 0.04 min) and lacks a raised mid-steel coaming lip, reading as an ambiguous dark smudge rather than an engineered hopper cavity.

6. **Boom Arm Clumping & Missing Articulated Steel Bit:**
   - Insufficient offset (`BOOM_OFFSET` = 0.1389 vs. 0.15 min) causes the boom arm to clump against the starboard track.
   - The boom body lacks a readable raised steel spine and faceted specular bit tip, resolving as a stubby yellow hook rather than an offset articulated finger with a mining bit.

7. **Silhouette Fragmentation:**
   - The rover fragments into disconnected light and dark patches (`ONE_BODY` = 0.3556 vs. 0.9 min) rather than reading as a single unified crawler chassis.

### (3) Concrete Blender Actions that Help this Site Silhouette

1. **Re-establish Track Separation & 1 px Outer Steel Lips:**
   - Sharpen and elevate the outer steel flanges on both port and starboard stadium tracks so they reliably catch a 1 px specular highlight against the terrain.
   - Deepen the longitudinal shadow reveal channels between the chassis tub and crawler belts to achieve positive track contrast (`TRACK_CONTRAST` >= 18.0 on both sides).

2. **Confine Safety Yellow Strictly to a 1 px Leading Brow Visor:**
   - Strip yellow shader assignments from the boom mount, cab flanks, and side structures.
   - Confine safety yellow strictly to the leading 1 px horizontal visor shelf extending forward directly above the windshield band (`LIVERY_SAT_RENDERED` >= 0.3).

3. **Elevate Cab & Frame Windshield Band:**
   - Raise the cab roof geometry above the main deck (`CAB_IS_RAISED` >= 0.28) and expand the windshield glazing geometry (`CAB_PANE` >= 0.02 in clay).
   - Add a crisp mid-steel bezel surrounding the dark windshield glass to separate the cab cleanly from the chassis tub.

4. **Enlarge and Rim the Intake Well:**
   - Expand the hopper opening (`WELL_HOLE` >= 0.04 in clay) and add an extruded mid-steel coaming rim around the perimeter to ensure the well reads as a distinct mechanical bite.

5. **Offset Boom Arm & Accentuate Machined Steel Spine / Bit Tip:**
   - Rotate/offset the boom arm outward to ensure `BOOM_OFFSET` >= 0.15, establishing negative space against the starboard track.
   - Assign dark structural steel to the boom arm body, add a raised machined steel spine along its top ridge, and terminate with a faceted steel bit tip that catches a 1 px specular point highlight.
