### (1) Obvious Defects FROM THIS SITE VIEW

1. **Muddy Dark Stamp & Chunky Yellow Smudge Read:**
   - At ~22 px site scale, the rover fails to read as an articulated heavy industrial tracked mining crawler.
   - It collapses into a muddy dark rectangular stamp with an ambiguous vertical yellow patch on the forward starboard side rather than crisp, functional machinery.

2. **Track Belt Dissolution & Missing 1 px Outer Steel Lips:**
   - Neither the port nor starboard stadium belt holds as a solid dark rubber wall capped by a thin raised steel lip that catches a 1 px specular highlight.
   - Severe negative contrast causes both track belts to bleed into the terrain and chassis, losing track separation and outer boundary definition.

3. **Yellow Visor Failure (Chunky Vertical Patch vs. 1 px Horizontal Brow Line):**
   - The safety yellow livery forms an irregular, chunky vertical smear across the starboard cab area rather than a sharp 1 px horizontal visor line spanning cleanly in front of the dark windshield band.
   - Yellow color bleeds onto vertical cab surfaces rather than remaining strictly confined to the leading visor brow.

4. **Indistinct Cab Glazing & Mid-Steel Tub Definition:**
   - The dark windshield band lacks crisp contrast and mid-steel bezel framing, blending into the surrounding dark chassis and adjacent body geometry.
   - The cab structure fails to separate cleanly from the mid-steel tub.

5. **Intake Hopper Well Lacks Depth and Distinct Separation:**
   - The intake well does not read as a distinct, deep mechanical bite separate from the windshield band.
   - It registers as an indistinct dark notch lacking stepped interior depth and raised coaming rim relief.

6. **Boom Arm Clumping, Lack of Steel Spine, & Indistinct Bit Tip:**
   - The boom arm lacks negative space clearance against the starboard track, clumping into the chassis silhouette.
   - It reads as an ambiguous dark/yellow hook rather than an offset articulated finger featuring a dark arm body, a raised steel top spine catching light, and a faceted steel bit with a 1 px bright specular tip.

### (3) Concrete Blender Actions that Help this Site Silhouette

1. **Re-establish Track Separation & 1 px Outer Steel Lips:**
   - Model distinct longitudinal shadow channels between the chassis tub and crawler belts to achieve positive track contrast (`TRACK_CONTRAST` >= 18.0 on both sides).
   - Bevel the outer track edges to catch a 1 px specular steel highlight over solid dark rubber belt walls.

2. **Confine Yellow Strictly to a 1 px Leading Brow Visor:**
   - Remove yellow paint from cab vertical faces, cab sides, and boom geometry.
   - Confine safety yellow strictly to the leading 1 px horizontal brow visor shelf extending forward directly above the windshield band (`LIVERY_SAT_RENDERED` >= 0.3).

3. **Enlarge and Frame Dark Windshield Band:**
   - Expand windshield glazing geometry (`CAB_PANE` >= 0.02 in clay and textured passes) and frame it in a crisp mid-steel tub bezel to cleanly separate the cab glazing from body cavities.

4. **Deepen and Rim the Intake Well:**
   - Deepen the hopper cavity and add a raised mid-steel coaming lip around the opening to guarantee `WELL_IS_A_HOLE` >= 0.24 and clay `WELL_HOLE` >= 0.04.

5. **Offset Boom Arm & Accentuate Machined Steel Spine / Bit Tip:**
   - Angle the boom arm outward to ensure `BOOM_OFFSET` >= 0.15, creating visible negative space against the starboard track.
   - Give the boom a dark structural arm body topped with a raised machined steel spine leading to a distinct bit tip that catches a 1 px bright specular highlight.
