### 1. Obvious Defects from this Site View (~22 px)

- **Disconnected / Fragmented Silhouette (`ONE_BODY` Defect):** Deep negative-space gaps separate the port and starboard track assemblies from the central hull. At ~22 px, the crawler breaks into disjointed floating pieces (two outer track bars, two yellow strips, a central grey cabin/deck, and an offset boom wedge) rather than reading as a single heavy-duty tracked chassis.
- **Isolated "Yellow Bar" / Toy Sticker Read:** Yellow livery is confined strictly to two high-saturation parallel strips flanking the hopper well. Lacking structural integration or framing across the rest of the hull, these read like two glowing plastic highlighter stickers slapped onto a dull grey bracket.
- **Weak Track Band Definition:** The tracks read as uniform grey structural slabs rather than distinct dark continuous track belts with bright metallic sprocket/idler end caps.
- **Boom and Bit Articulation:** The starboard offset boom arm reads as a blunt grey peg with a faint bluish tip rather than an articulated industrial tool arm with a distinct mechanical shoulder and cutting head.
- **Flat Hopper Well Cutout:** The hopper well appears as a flat, unmodeled black square hole rather than a volumetric recessed cargo hopper with chamfered lips and interior depth.

---

### 2. MTX Defects Readable at Site Size

- **`ONE_BODY` (Fail ΓÇö 0.3476 vs 0.90 floor):** Severe silhouette fragmentation across negative-space voids between tracks and hull.
- **`TRACK_OUTERMOST_PORT` & `TRACK_OUTERMOST_STARBOARD` (Fail ΓÇö 0.7712 and 0.7708 vs 0.80 floor):** Track running gear lacks sufficient continuous outer perimeter coverage relative to the chassis bounds.
- **`WELL_IS_A_HOLE` (Fail ΓÇö 0.0393 vs 0.24 floor):** Rendered depth difference between the hopper floor and the surrounding deck plating remains insufficient to register as a deep hopper cavity.
- **Livery Distribution Imbalance (`YELLOW_MINORITY` 0.0704):** Squeezing yellow livery down into two isolated strips overcorrects the yellow budget, compromising machine-wide hazard livery cohesion.

---

### 3. Concrete Blender Actions

1. **Bridge Hull to Tracks:** Add structural cross-members, heavy fender mounts, and suspension bulkheads in the gap between the hull body and track carriages to close the visual voids and restore single-body cohesion (`ONE_BODY`).
2. **Integrate and Frame Livery:** Add dark steel chamfer bevels around the yellow sponson plates and distribute subtle yellow accents to track fenders or the cab frame to integrate the color scheme across the vehicle.
3. **Contrast Track Bands and End Caps:** Darken the main continuous tread material and increase specular brightness on the front idler and rear sprocket end caps to produce clear "dark band with steel end caps" readability.
4. **Deepen Hopper Well & Bevel Rim:** Drop the hopper well floor lower in Z and add a slight top bevel/chamfer to catch edge lighting, ensuring a clear recessed well read.
5. **Articulate Boom Shoulder & Bit:** Inset a dark mechanical pivot socket at the boom base and sharpen the cutter bit geometry/value contrast at the tip.
