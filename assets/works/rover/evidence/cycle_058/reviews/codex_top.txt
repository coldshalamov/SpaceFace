3. Concrete Blender actions

- Raise the rover’s key and fill exposure enough that the dark machine remains readable against the ground while preserving deep hopper and cab cavities.
- Rebuild the central tub as one formed load-bearing steel volume with applied scale, restrained two-segment bevels, angle-based shading, and weighted normals.
- Cut the hopper through the tub using an Exact boolean. Extrude a visibly thick rim inward and build separate dark sidewalls plus a lower floor so the opening cannot resemble a pane.
- Rebuild the cab as a distinct dark house seated on the tub. Cut a real windshield cavity and place thin dark-glass panes inside a metal frame.
- Restrict yellow to one narrow visor blade extending beyond the already visible windshield band. Remove yellow from cab sides, roof blocks, track-like bars, and unrelated plates.
- Give each track a solid stadium-shaped rubber belt with real thickness and a slightly irregular segmented tread surface. Add a separate narrow steel outer lip above the rubber, bevel it tightly, and light it so only selected arcs catch highlights rather than forming a continuous outline.
- Add visible track suspension/contact structure where it joins the tub so the belts do not look pasted beside a box.
- Replace broad card-like overlays with inset or solidified steel plates that have restrained standoff, thickness, and dark seam channels. Keep large quiet areas.
- Rebuild the boom as clearly offset machinery: a thick dark tapered arm, a separate raised steel spine, and a readable pivot housing fixed into the tub.
- Add hydraulic hoses as beveled curves routed along the arm, with fittings touching both the chassis manifold and tool body.
- Model the bit body as a substantial stepped steel assembly with collars, flats, and a clear mechanical connection to the spine. End it in one compact bright metal tip, not a hook or mushroom.
- Enlarge only the boom’s role-defining masses enough to survive the works camera; do not add cabin furniture or microscopic decoration.
- Establish separate materials for matte rubber, coated dark cab metal, mid-value painted tub steel, brighter worn steel lips/spine/bit, and dark low-roughness glass.
- Author roughness and metallic variation so the steel lips, spine, and bit catch narrow highlights while the belts remain dark and broad.
- Replace sticker-like squares with one restrained stencil applied directly to the paint, using slight wear and no visible thickness.
- Rebalance the top-view value structure so the hopper remains the deepest hole, the windshield is a distinct dark glass band, the tub is mid-value steel, and the boom spine and tip provide controlled bright metal notes.
- Recapture at the identical works camera and confirm that the rover, hopper hole, windshield band, thin visor, two solid tracks, and complete boom hierarchy are all recognizable in the uncropped play-size frame.
