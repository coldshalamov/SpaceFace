### (1) Obvious Defects (Works-Edge View)

- **Side Walls:**
  - Transition flanks bounding the central hopper well remain flat, planar triangular clay/card wedges sloping inward.
  - Lacks structural plate thickness, weld lines, gusseted stiffeners, and faceted armor geometry.
- **Track Height & Undercarriage (Solid Rubber Belts vs. Wire Loops):**
  - Continuous rubber tread belts are correctly modeled with solid vertical height and transverse cleats (not wire loops).
  - Inboard track channels contain featureless light-gray card strips and open gaps lacking mechanical drive sprockets, idler rollers, track tensioners, and chassis bogie mountings.
- **Dark Cab House & Yellow Visor:**
  - Cab house remains a dark rectilinear primitive box rather than a chamfered, faceted heavy equipment enclosure.
  - A flat circular toy-like button/disc is pasted directly onto the center of the cab roof.
  - Safety yellow is correctly restricted to a thin forward-projecting protective visor brow shelf extending past the glass (not yellow cab sides, not a plastic yellow square on the roof), but the dark cab superstructure lacks structural corner posts and beveled armor seams.
- **Dark Cab Pane:**
  - Forward dark rectangular windshield pane is clearly visible beneath the yellow visor brow.
  - Lacks an authored structural retaining bezel frame, rubber gasket seal, and dielectric Fresnel specular reflectivity.
- **Hopper Well:**
  - Central hopper cavity is a basic stepped interior flanked by flat planar transition walls.
  - Lacks heavy AR-plate wear liners with fastener patterns, corner gussets, rim reinforcement coaming, and lower chute/tailgate discharge geometry.
- **Darker Boom, Raised Steel Spine, & Steel Bit Body:**
  - Boom structure extending forward (+X starboard) is an extruded, flat-sided dark polygonal slab.
  - Central steel spine running along the top of the boom is a thin flat strip rather than a heavy machined feed-rail.
  - Rotary cutter bit at the forward tip is a basic pointed cone without defined cutting flutes, carbide insert teeth, or a distinct machined mounting collar.
- **Scar Plate:**
  - Aft push/scar plate is a flat, unshaded light-gray rectangular card slapped directly onto the rear crossmember.
  - Lacks plate gauge thickness, perimeter bevels, modeled hex bolt fasteners, and recovery hitch/towing lugs.
- **Toy / Clay / Card / Box / Gray Plate Artifacts:**
  - **Rear Scar Gray Card:** Featureless, unshaded light-gray rectangular card pasted on the aft crossmember.
  - **Inboard Track Filler Gray Cards/Plates:** Light-gray flat card strips bridging the gaps between the hull body and track assemblies.
  - **Hopper Flank Sloping Wedges:** Flat planar triangular clay/card ramps forming the side walls of the hopper.
  - **Cab Roof Slapped-On Disc:** Flat cylindrical toy disc/button glued onto the cab roof.
  - **Rectangular Box Cab:** Primitive blocky CAD box volume without armored chamfers or structural faceting.

- **Forbidden Reads (MTX-46):**

### (3) Concrete Blender Actions (No Cabin Furniture)

1. **Reconstruct Side Walls & Chassis Deck:**
   - Remove the flat triangular clay/card ramps flanking the hopper well and the light-gray filler cards along the tracks.
   - Model solid, heavy-gauge steel side walls with chamfered reinforcing ribs, welded corner seams, and integrated chassis deck armor.
2. **Rebuild Rear Scar Plate:**
   - Replace the flat light-gray card with a thick, beveled steel push/scar plate proud of the rear crossmember.
   - Add modeled hex bolt fasteners at the corners, perimeter chamfers, and an integrated tow/recovery hitch bracket.
3. **Refine Cab House, Visor, & Glass Pane:**
   - Chamfer and facet the dark cab house into a heavy armored enclosure.
   - Delete the slapped-on flat roof disc; replace with a flush/recessed maintenance hatch or hooded intake louver.
   - Maintain safety yellow strictly on the crisp, projecting protective brow visor shelf extending forward past the glass.
   - Recess the dark glass pane into a bolted steel retaining frame with a rubber gasket, and assign a reflective tinted dark glass PBR shader with Fresnel falloff.
4. **Deepen Hopper Well & Add Liners:**
   - Deepen the central hopper well and model interior AR-plate wear liners with visible bolt patterns, corner stiffener gussets, and a heavy coaming rim around the hopper mouth.
5. **Detail Boom Arm, Raised Steel Spine, & Drill Bit:**
   - Rebuild the boom as a heavy structural boxed girder arm with reinforced pivot brackets.
   - Model a distinct, raised machined steel spine/feed-rail along the upper surface of the boom arm.
   - Model a heavy-duty multi-faceted steel/tungsten rotary cutter bit head with defined cutting flutes, collar mounting, and heat-stain gradient.
6. **Tie In Track Undercarriage:**
   - Anchor the solid rubber continuous track belts into the chassis using modeled structural side frames, idler wheels, drive sprockets, and bogie mountings to eliminate open card gaps.
7. **PBR Material & Atlas Alignment:**
   - Assign distinct PBR shader channels: dark textured abraded rubber for tracks (`track`), heavy industrial dark equipment enamel for hull/cab/boom (`livery`/`steel`), bare machined steel for the boom spine and bit body (`steel`/`bit`), safety yellow strictly on the visor lip (`livery`), and reflective tinted dark glass for the cab pane (`glass`).
