### (1) Every Obvious Defect FROM THIS SITE VIEW

1. **Gestalt Breakdown & Box / Stamp / Yellow Patch Read:**
   - At ~22 px site scale, the vehicle fails to read as a cohesive, heavy-duty tracked industrial crawler (`ONE_BODY` = 0.5526 vs. 0.9000 min).
   - Rather than projecting an articulated mining chassis, the silhouette resolves as a dark/gray box flanked by two dark bars, with an asymmetric yellow vertical bar/patch stuck on the starboard-forward flank.

2. **Track Belt Dissolution & Missing 1 px Outer Steel Lip Highlights:**
   - Both port and starboard tracks hold mass as solid dark stadium belts (avoiding hollow paperclips or wire loops).
   - However, the outer steel lips fail to catch a distinct 1 px specular highlight against the terrain shadows, resulting in severe negative track contrast (`TRACK_CONTRAST_PORT` = -26.99, `TRACK_CONTRAST_STARBOARD` = -23.78 vs. +18.00 min floor) and causing the crawler edges to blend into surrounding ground darkness.

3. **Yellow Visor Failure & Misplaced Livery Block:**
   - The safety yellow does not resolve as a crisp, continuous 1 px horizontal visor shelf directly forward of the windshield (`LIVERY_SAT_RENDERED` = 0.0132 vs. 0.3000 min).
   - Instead, the yellow color pools as a chunky vertical patch on the starboard cab flank/boom root, producing a toy-like postage-stamp defect rather than a functional vehicle hazard brow.

4. **Sunken Cab House & Cramped Windshield Band:**
   - The cab house geometry remains sunken relative to the deck (`CAB_IS_RAISED` = -0.3747 vs. +0.2800 min).
   - The dark windshield band is cramped and lacks a defined mid-steel bezel or specular sheen, causing it to be visually swallowed by adjacent chassis shadows and the yellow flank block.

5. **Intake Well Rim Legibility:**
   - The hopper well registers as a dark opening separated from the cab, but it lacks a defined, illuminated mid-steel coaming lip and visible wall thickness. At this scale, it risks reading as a flat black cutout rather than an unmistakable deep mechanical well.

6. **Boom Arm Clumping & Low-Contrast Spine / Bit:**
   - The boom arm lacks sufficient lateral clearance (`BOOM_OFFSET` = 0.1466 vs. 0.1500 min floor), clumping against the starboard crawler track.
   - The raised steel spine along the dark boom arm is too faint to establish a clear load-bearing line, and the cutter bit lacks a crisp 1 px specular spark at the tip.

7. **Toy-Like Center Plate / Marking Read:**
   - The center deck plates and dot markings read as flat card slabs and printed sticker details rather than an integrated industrial casting with structural joints.

   - Restrict safety yellow strictly to a thin 1 px horizontal visor brow running directly across the leading edge of the windshield glass (`LIVERY_SAT_RENDERED` >= 0.30).
3. **Elevate Cab & Frame Windshield Band:**

4. **Extrude Hopper Coaming Rim:**
   - Model a raised, chamfered mid-steel coaming lip around the hopper opening to establish visible wall thickness and ensure the intake reads as an unmistakable deep cavity.
5. **Offset Boom Arm & Accentuate Steel Spine and Bit Tip:**
