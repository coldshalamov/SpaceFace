### 1. Obvious Defects (Works-Edge View)

* **Side Walls:**
  * Flanking the hopper well, the side walls consist of flat, planar triangular card/clay wedges sloping into the body rather than structural, heavy-duty industrial sidewall plating.
  * Lacks plate thickness, chamfered reinforcement flanges, weld seams, or realistic debris shedding angles.

* **Track Height & Assembly (Solid Rubber Belts vs Wire Loops):**
  * Tracks read as blocky, rounded stadium treads with segmented pads, but lack proper integration with an undercarriage suspension, drive sprockets, and tensioner assemblies.
  * Treads float alongside the body without solid structural track frames anchoring them into the chassis.

* **Cab House & Yellow Visor:**
  * The cab house is a dark, primitive rectilinear block with an unresolved flat circular cylinder/disc button sitting on the roof.
  * Yellow livery is applied as a chunky frame/square block around the front glass rather than being strictly restricted to a crisp, thin overhanging visor extending forward past the dark glass.
  * Lacks armored structural pillars, chamfers, or industrial equipment enclosure shaping.

* **Dark Cab Pane:**
  * The dark pane is rendered as a flat, unshaded pitch-black rectangle on the cab face.
  * Missing a structural steel bezel, rubber gasket seal, recessed mounting depth, or specular glass reflectivity.

* **Hopper Well:**
  * The central hopper well is a primitive stepped square depression bordered by flat planar aprons.
  * Lacks interior AR-plate wear liners, corner gussets, welded stiffeners, and mechanical depth connecting into the lower chassis.

* **Boom & Drill Bit Assembly:**
  * The boom extending to the starboard side (+X forward) is a flat, dark olive/brown trapezoidal slab.
  * Lacks a darker structural boom arm with a distinct, high-contrast raised machined steel spine/feed rail terminating into a dedicated multi-faceted steel drill bit body.
  * The bit at the tip is currently a tiny, undefined conical nub.

* **Scar Plate:**
  * The rear scar plate is a completely flat, featureless light-gray card/plate slapped onto the aft crossmember.
  * Lacks plate thickness, hex fastener hardware, corner bevels, or tow/recovery mount integration.

* **Toy / Clay / Card / Box / Gray Plate Artifacts:**
  * Planar gray/tan card wedges flanking the hopper well.
  * Flat, unfilleted light-gray decking filler strips between the hopper walls and the track frames.
  * Flat rectangular gray card applied as the rear scar plate.
  * Primitive cylinder/disc glued onto the cab roof.
  * Overall silhouette still reads as a collection of primitive boxes and slapped-on flat card geometry (toy/clay/card look).

### 2. MTX Defects (Visible at Play Size)

* **Flat Diffuse Shaders:** Materials lack proper PBR separation; chassis, cab, and boom share muddy, low-contrast diffuse tones with minimal metallic or roughness response.
* **Untextured Gray/Clay Cards:** Flank wedges, deck filler strips, and the rear scar plate appear as untextured, default gray/tan clay surfaces.
* **Yellow Livery Response:** Yellow visor accent reads as a flat, non-dielectric plastic block rather than industrial coated safety yellow alkyd enamel over zinc primer.
* **Glass Shading:** Cab pane is an unlit, flat black hole with zero Fresnel response, roughness variation, or environmental reflection.
* **Lack of Normal Relief:** Missing micro-relief, hex bolt heads, plate overlap offsets, weld seams, and edge bevels across the atlas.

### 3. Concrete Blender Actions (External Only ΓÇö No Cabin Furniture)

1. **Rebuild Side Walls & Hopper:**
   * Remove the flat triangular card wedges flanking the well.
   * Extrude structural, heavy-gauge steel sidewalls with chamfered stiffener ribs, plate courses, and welded seams.
   * Deepen the internal hopper well; model interior AR-plate wear liners and corner reinforcement gussets.

2. **Refine Deck & Chassis:**
   * Delete flat gray filler card plates between the hopper well and track housings; model a continuous, beveled structural hull plate with mechanical cutouts and mounting brackets.

3. **Reconstruct Cab House, Visor, & Pane:**
   * Bevel and shape the dark cab house into an armored equipment pod.
   * Remove the roof cylinder disc; replace with a flush/recessed maintenance hatch or intake grille.
   * Remodel the yellow livery strictly as a thin, crisp protective visor shelf projecting forward past the glass; remove yellow from cab sides and roof framing.
   * Recess the dark cab pane into a dedicated rubber seal and bolted steel retaining frame with tinted specular glass shading.

4. **Detail Boom & Steel Spine / Bit Body:**
   * Model a darker structural boxed boom arm with heavy-duty pivot brackets.
   * Add a distinct, raised machined steel rail/spine along the top of the boom arm leading directly into a faceted steel drill bit head.

5. **Reconstruct Rear Scar Plate:**
   * Replace the slapped-on flat gray card with a thick, beveled steel scar plate secured with modelled hex bolts and integrated tow points.

6. **Refine Track Undercarriage:**
   * Model solid rubber continuous track belts with defined cleats/grooves, supported by structural track frames, idlers, and roller guides.

7. **PBR Material & Atlas Alignment:**
   * Assign distinct PBR properties: dark abraded rubber for tracks (`#3a3530`), dark coated metal for cab/boom (`#2a2722`), bare machined steel for the boom spine and bit (`#848b93`), dielectric safety yellow for the visor (`#ffd23f`), and tinted specular glass for the cab pane (`#161009`).
