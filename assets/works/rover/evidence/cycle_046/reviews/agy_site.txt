### Visual Review: Asteroid Works Rover (PQ-131.01 Cycle 46)

Evaluated at 1:1 original resolution in [works_site_1to1.png](file:///C:/Users/93rob/Documents/GitHub/SpaceFace/assets/works/rover/evidence/cycle_046/review_1to1/works_site_1to1.png) (with reference to [works_site.png](file:///C:/Users/93rob/Documents/GitHub/SpaceFace/assets/works/rover/evidence/cycle_046/works_site.png)).

### 1. Obvious Defects from this Site View

* **Gray Cassette / Stamp Read:** The overall silhouette still reads as a monolithic, rectangular gray block with a dark central cavity cutout rather than an articulated tracked mining crawler.
* **Track Definition Failure:** Neither port nor starboard track reads as an independent stadium belt. The dark rubber track walls are missing contrast against the chassis, and there is no 1 px steel lip catching light to define the running gear boundaries. The starboard track completely bleeds into the right hull volume.
* **Misplaced Yellow Livery / Cab Read:** The cab structure does not register as a dark cab resting on a mid-steel tub. The intended thin yellow visor line across the windshield band is missing; instead, yellow appears as a chunky vertical patch clustered along the right bulkhead/interior wall.
* **Well vs. Windshield Merged:** The equipment well fails to read as a distinct recessed architectural pocket. It collapses into the cockpit void, appearing as a single generic dark hole in the center-left deck.
* **Muddy Boom Silhouette:** The boom arm on the right does not read as an offset articulated finger. Its arm body lacks dark-to-steel value contrast, and the spine/bit tip lacks the bright steel specular highlight needed to separate it from the ground shadow and hull edge.

---

### 2. MTX Defects Readable at this Size

* **Albedo & Value Inversion:** Deck and hull plating albedos remain too bright/flat relative to the cab and tracks, crushing the value hierarchy required at 22 px.
* **Missing Specular Edge Cues:** No specular highlights on track outer perimeters or chassis bevels, causing the vehicle boundary to blend into surrounding ground pixels.
* **Livery Texture Mapping:** Yellow livery is mapped onto vertical side geometry rather than a crisp 1 px horizontal forward visor bar.
* **Starboard Track Contrast Collapse:** Starboard track lacks any dark rubber albedo separation from the adjacent hull metal.

---

### 3. Concrete Blender Actions to Fix Site Silhouette

1. **Define Dual Stadium Tracks:**
   * Lower the base color albedo and raise roughness on the track rubber belts (both port and starboard) to create dark rubber walls.
   * Add a bevel / raised perimeter rim with a bright steel material along outer track edges to catch a sharp 1 px specular highlight.
   * Deepen the geometric separation / shadow groove between the track pods and the central chassis.
2. **Rebuild Cab & Visor Structure:**
   * Lower the value of the cab roof/walls so the dark cab sits distinctly on a mid-value steel tub.
   * Remap the yellow livery to form a single continuous 1 px horizontal visor line directly across the front rim of the dark windshield band.
   * Strip all yellow paint patches from the interior bulkhead and right chassis flank.
3. **Separate Equipment Well from Windshield:**
   * Add a dividing structural deck bridge between the cockpit glazing band and the equipment well, stepping the well down as a dedicated shadowed pocket.
4. **Sharpen Boom Arm & Bit Tip:**
   * Thicken/raise the boom spine ridge geometry and assign a high-metallic steel material to catch top lighting.
   * Give the tool bit a bright steel tip highlight so the boom reads cleanly as an articulated offset finger against dark terrain shadows.
