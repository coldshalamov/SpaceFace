### (1) Defects From This Site View

1. **Yellow Postage Stamp / Toy Tab Read at Cab Visor:**
   * The safety-yellow geometry on the front-right of the cab reads as a solid, flat yellow tab / postage stamp stuck onto the hull rather than a thin, formed sheet-metal visor/brow overhanging an enclosed cab.
   * Because the dark windshield band beneath/behind it is cramped and unarticulated, the yellow feature fails to read as an industrial cab visor and instead presents as an applied toy block.
2. **Monolithic Gray Stamp / Boxy Hull Read:**
   * The central hull and rear deck between the track assemblies form a flat, unbroken gray rectangular slab. It reads as a gray stamp rather than an articulated, heavy-duty mining crawler chassis.
3. **Starboard Track Trough Washout & Weak Shadow Contrast:**
   * While the port (top) stadium track catches a thin 1-px outer steel lip and holds a defined internal shadow trough, the starboard (bottom) track interior is washed out, failing to hold shadow depth against the lower hull and weakening the crawler read.
4. **Shallow Hopper Well Cavity:**
   * The central cargo well appears as a flat dark 2D square cutout on the deck plate rather than a deep, volumetric ore hopper with occluded shadow depth and crisp rim bevels.
5. **Boom Arm / Spine Read:**
   * While the forward offset boom finger and bright drill bit are discernible, the connecting structural arm is rendered in a uniform light tone. It lacks a darker structural arm body contrasted against a narrow, high-contrast bright metal spine catching a distinct 1-px highlight into the bit.
