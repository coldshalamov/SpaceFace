   - Although transverse tread pads/grousers are present, the track assemblies lack solid vertical cross-sectional depth and volumetric rubber mass. They read as thin perimeter loop shells wrapped around empty voids rather than heavy, solid continuous rubber crawler belts.

5. **Dark Canopy Pane Actually Visible**:

6. **Hopper Well**:

7. **Darker Boom with Raised Steel Spine into Steel Bit Body**:

8. **Scar Plate**:

### (2) MTX Defects Visible at This Play Size

- **High-Contrast Gray Box Read**: The stark contrast between the bright light-gray cab roof box / rear gray card and the dark chassis creates an unfinished, toy-like appearance at in-game resolution.
- **Papercraft Silhouette & Zero-Gauge Edges**: Razor-thin planar sheets on the hopper flare, rear transom, and side skirts create papercraft edges under lighting, destroying the illusion of heavy industrial machinery.
- **Floating Plastic Accent**: The yellow visor reads as a floating plastic pixel/square rather than a functional vehicle component.
- **Low Feature Contrast in Tool Assembly**: Dark boom geometry and bit collar blend together under top lighting, lacking bright metallic edge highlights and structural spine definition.
- **Track Cross-Section Profile**: Thin track belt loops lack solid rubber mass and side-wall depth when viewed in relation to the main hull.
