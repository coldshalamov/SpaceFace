### (1) Obvious Defects Visible From This Site View (~22 px Rover Read)

1. **Yellow Postage-Stamp / Blocky Toy Read (Chassis Massing Failure):**
   - The central chassis remains a chunky, high-saturation yellow block. While it has cutouts (the circular well at aft and the cab notch at forward), the yellow armor reads as a flat stamped plate rather than articulated, panelized heavy industrial machinery (`ONE_BODY` failing at `0.5151` vs `0.90` floor).
2. **Starboard (Bottom) Track Shadow Bleed & Value Loss:**
   - The lower (starboard) track assembly lacks sufficient value contrast against its contact shadow and the dark ground tile (`TRACK_CONTRAST_STARBOARD` failing at `16.98` vs `18.0` floor). Unlike the top track, the bottom track's outer grousers and steel end sprockets lose crisp definition and bleed into the terrain.
3. **Flat "Hole Punch" Read on Excavation Well:**
   - The excavation well appears as a flat 2D black circle stamped into the yellow body rather than a deep, shadowed hopper cavity with a raised, top-lit, beveled structural coaming rim.
4. **Faint / Thin Boom Arm Linkage (Detached Bit Read):**
   - The boom arm extending to the forward starboard side is very thin and dark steel. Because the linkage arm blends into the dark site terrain, the circular cutter bit at the tip risks reading as a floating, disconnected grey dot rather than the rigid tool head of an articulated boom.
5. **Indistinct Forward Cab / Cockpit Area:**
   - The forward center notch between the yellow cowlings is a muddy dark grey zone lacking a distinct glass specular glint or sharp structural canopy frame to convey forward vehicle orientation at 22 px.
