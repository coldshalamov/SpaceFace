### (1) Obvious Defects Visible from `works_edge` View

1. **Toy / Clay / Card / Box / Gray Plate Read (Primary Visual Defect):**
   - The vehicle reads as a primitive toy CAD blockout constructed from flat dark-gray slab boxes with bright yellow cardboard/plate cutouts laid flat over them.
   - The yellow surfaces do not read as industrial safety-painted panels fastened over an underlying structural steel chassis; they read as decorative yellow toy cards and flat bordered tiles glued to a hollow gray box.
   - The cutter head reads as a low-poly faceted gray geometric frustum rigidly attached to the end of a solid wedge boom arm.
   - The central waist and deck transitions lack structural depth, weld seams, service access plates, and mechanical hardware, reading as an injection-molded plastic shell.

2. **Side Walls:**
   - The lateral flanks connecting the central chassis to the track sponsons are flat vertical slabs lacking depth, structural rocker sills, outrigger mounting brackets, or hardware.
   - The port flank features a flat rectangular yellow patch (scar plate) with zero thickness, no bevel, and no fastener detail.
   - The waist area between the track sponsons lacks structural cross-beams, chassis tie-ins, and service access hatches.

3. **Track Height & Running Gear:**
   - The track assemblies read as thin belt loops stretched over end wheels with hollow, unmodeled gaps between the upper and lower track runs.
   - Undercarriage running gear is missing interior mechanical detail: no road wheel bogies, top return rollers, track tensioner cylinders, or center guide teeth.
   - Grouser pads are flat, uniform rectangular segments lacking link relief, hinge pin recesses, or mechanical surface depth.

4. **Cab House:**
   - The forward starboard cab is a thick yellow rectangular collar surrounding an unglazed, pitch-black cavity opening.
   - Lacks an authored structural retaining bezel, rubber perimeter weather gasket, or dark dielectric glass window pane.
   - Missing exterior roof utility fixtures: no ROPS (Roll-Over Protection Structure) rock cage / guard bars, no armored work light pod, no antenna mount, and no ventilation intake cowl.

5. **Hopper Well:**
   - The hopper is an unlined square hole cut into the rear deck plate, dropping into an empty dark void.
   - Lacks interior structural volume: no abrasion-resistant (AR) wear plate liners, no sloped deflection chute, no stiffening ribs, and no reinforced lip flange.
   - Dump tailgate hinge lugs and locking hardware are completely absent.

6. **Boom & Cutter Head:**
   - The boom arm is a solid, unarticulated dark gray trapezoidal wedge rigidly fused directly to the forward deck.
   - Lacks mechanical joint articulation: no dual-clevis base pivot mount, heavy hinge pin, hydraulic elevation cylinder/piston ram, or flexible hydraulic lines.
   - The cutter head is a simplified faceted cone/frustum with a flat nose, lacking a rotating pick drum, conical carbide teeth studs, and thermal oxide heat staining.

7. **Scar Plate:**
   - The scar/armor plate on the port flank is a flat, un-beveled rectangular card lacking perimeter hex bolt patterns, edge chamfers, and abrasive impact wear.

### (4) Verdict

**REVISE**

*Rationale:* The asset reads as a yellow-and-gray box with flat plates stuck on top and an unarticulated boom wedge. It lacks mechanical track running gear, has unlined void cutouts for the cab and hopper, and lacks essential hydraulic cylinders, hose routing, and edge bevels. Substantive structural remodeling across the chassis, boom kinematics, track running gear, and hopper interior is required.
