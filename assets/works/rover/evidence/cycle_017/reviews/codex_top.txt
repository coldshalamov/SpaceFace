3. Concrete Blender actions

- Rebuild each track as a thick stadium loop with a clearly wrapped belt, readable tread blocks, recessed bogies, and separate steel end-cap or tension-wheel housings. Increase the track assemblies’ lateral mass and separation from the chassis.
- Boolean the hopper completely through the upper skin. Add a substantial rim and four visibly sloped steel walls descending to a darker floor so the opening reads from this exact camera.
- Raise the cab above the adjacent deck. Cut a real roof opening or tub and install a dark pane as a thin shell inside a separate steel frame.
- Replace the current boom plate with a thick, tapered steel arm offset from the centerline. Add a distinct shoulder mount, pivot pin, hydraulic actuator, hose runs, and fittings that physically meet their ports.
- Model the bit as recognizable working hardware with a socket, stepped shaft, cutting head or teeth, and visibly different worn-metal treatment. Keep it attached to the boom and clear of the chassis silhouette.
- Replace stacked slabs with a unified chassis skin and intentional stepped armor or service panels. Remove hidden overlapping faces and retain actual gaps only where parts join or move.
- Apply scale before beveling. Use narrow, size-appropriate bevels and weighted/custom normals so broad steel faces remain flat.
- Give the yellow panels physical borders, seams, and fasteners at their mounting interfaces. Use safety-yellow dielectric paint over steel, with restrained edge exposure and grime; do not use flat yellow cards or raised sticker slabs.
- Separate materials for painted chassis steel, exposed machined steel, track belt, cab glass, hydraulic components, and worn tool metal. Author visibly different roughness and metallic responses.
- Add construction-scale details around functional areas: boom pivots, hopper rim, track mounts, access hatches, and service connections. Avoid scattering decorative studs.
- Re-light the evidence view enough to preserve the dark mechanical wells while separating both tracks and the lower chassis from the ground.
