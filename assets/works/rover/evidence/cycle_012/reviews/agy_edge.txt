### (1) Obvious Defects Visible from `works_edge` View

1. **Toy / Clay / Card / Box / Gray Plate Read (Primary Visual Defect):**
   - The vehicle still reads as a gray CAD blockout box with two bright yellow trapezoidal toy plates/tiles stuck onto the shoulders flanking the hopper.
   - The yellow shoulder elements do not read as structural alkyd-painted safety panels bolted onto an industrial steel machine; they read as flat yellow toy tiles sitting on a dark gray slab.
   - The cutter head is a pale blue-gray conical plug rigidly stuck onto the boom tip without a rotating drum, teeth, or mechanical fittings.
   - Transitions between the aft hopper tub, midsection, and forward nose lack structural weld seams, panel breaks, fastener hardware, and fabrication lines.

2. **Side Walls & Flanks:**
   - Lateral side walls connecting the central tub to the track sponsons are flat, featureless gray surfaces lacking structural depth, stiffeners, outrigger ties, and gussets.
   - The hull waist taper is a simple polygonal cutout rather than formed, heavy-duty industrial plate geometry.

3. **Track Height & Running Gear:**
   - Track loops read as flat rubber belts with uniform blocky cleat teeth protruding along the exterior run.
   - Drive sprockets and idlers are simple circular end caps/cylinders lacking teeth engagement, recessed bearing caps, and bolt patterns.
   - Undercarriage lacks internal mechanical depth: no road wheel bogies, return rollers, track tensioner cylinders, or center guide horns between track runs.

4. **Cab House:**
   - The forward cab is a hollow dark gray rectangular block with an unglazed cutout opening into a dark void.
   - Missing a welded structural retaining bezel, rubber weather seal/gasket, and a dark dielectric glass pane.
   - Missing exterior roof utility equipment: no armored work light pod, no louvered vent stack, no antenna mount, and no lift eye lugs.

5. **Hopper Well:**
   - The hopper well is a dark rectangular cutout in the aft deck between the yellow shoulders.
   - Lacks internal structural volume: missing sloped abrasion-resistant (AR) wear plate liners, deflection ramp, perimeter bolt flanges, and a reinforced lip collar.
   - Missing rear dump tailgate hinge lugs and latch hardware.

6. **Boom & Cutter Head:**
   - The boom arm is a solid gray trapezoidal wedge rigidly fused to the starboard forward deck.
   - Lacks mechanical articulation: no dual-clevis base pivot mount, hinge pin, hydraulic elevation cylinder, or hydraulic line bundles.
   - The cutter bit is a low-poly conical bulb lacking a cylindrical pick drum, conical carbide teeth studs, and thermal oxide heat discoloration.

7. **Scar Plate:**
   - Flank wear/scar plates are visually absent or flush with the hull, lacking independent plate thickness, bevels, perimeter hex bolts, and abrasive surface wear.

### (4) Verdict

**REVISE**

*Rationale:* The asset remains a yellow-and-gray box with flat plates stuck on top and an unarticulated boom wedge. It lacks mechanical track running gear, has unlined void cutouts for the cab and hopper, and lacks essential hydraulic cylinders, hose routing, and edge bevels. Substantive structural remodeling across the chassis, boom kinematics, track running gear, and hopper interior is required.
