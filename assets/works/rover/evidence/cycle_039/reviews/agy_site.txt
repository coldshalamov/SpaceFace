### Visual Review: Asteroid Works Rover (PQ-131.01 Cycle 39 ΓÇö `works_site`)

---

### (1) Obvious Defects from Site View (~22 px Scale)

- **Gray Stamp / Floppy-Disk Chassis:** The main body reads as a flat, desaturated gray rectangular cassette with a square hole cut in the center rather than an industrial tracked mining crawler.
- **Hollow "Paperclip" Tracks:** Both port and starboard tracks render as thin wire loop outlines enclosing empty/hollow interiors. They fail to read as solid, heavy dark rubber stadium belts with crisp, raised 1-pixel steel catch lips.
- **Missing Top Livery Distribution:** Primary industrial yellow livery is absent from the main hull deck plates, causing the vehicle's top silhouette to register almost entirely as unpainted, desaturated metal with an isolated yellow patch.
- **Visor Reads as an Isolated Yellow Hook / Block:** The yellow visor bar does not read as a crisp, thin overhanging brow shading a dark windshield band; instead, it reads as an isolated yellow hook/stamp stuck onto the forward side.
- **Cab & Windshield Collapse:** The dark cab roof and windshield band lack clear definition and framing, merging into a dark amorphous notch behind the yellow block.
- **Cookie-Cutter Well Cavity:** The central well reads as a raw, flat dark square punch-hole in the mid-steel tub ("square washer" read) rather than an engineered, stepped mechanical bay.
- **Indistinct Boom & Muddy Tip:** The offset boom arm reads as a curved yellow/tan claw rather than an articulated industrial finger. The spine fails to catch a clean 1-pixel light streak, and the cutting bit terminates in a muddy, indistinct speck rather than a faceted steel tip with a bright specular catch.

### (2) MTX Defects Readable at this Size

- **Track Coverage & Outer Dominance:** Port and starboard track outer boundaries fail silhouette dominance (`TRACK_OUTERMOST_PORT` = 0.3694, `TRACK_OUTERMOST_STARBOARD` = 0.3667 vs. 0.80 floor), directly causing the hollow wireframe / paperclip artifact.
- **Starboard Track Contrast Inversion:** Starboard track contrast fails severely (`TRACK_CONTRAST_STARBOARD` = -3.6458 vs. 18.0 floor), causing the lower track to lose separation against the shadow/hull.
- **Rendered Livery Saturation:** Hull deck lacks yellow coverage (`LIVERY_SAT_RENDERED` = 0.1294 vs. 0.30 floor), producing the monochrome "gray stamp" read.
- **Chassis Cohesion & Depth Relief:** Body mass cohesion (`ONE_BODY` = 0.4129 vs. 0.90 floor), well depth (`WELL_IS_A_HOLE` = 0.0694 vs. 0.24 floor), and cab elevation (`CAB_IS_RAISED` = 0.1570 vs. 0.28 floor) all fail, causing the flat cutout appearance.
- **Boom Offset:** Boom lateral clearance fails target (`BOOM_OFFSET` = 0.1446 vs. 0.15 floor).

---

### (3) Concrete Blender Actions

1. **Solidify Track Belts & Add Backing Infill:** Widen the track belt geometry and add solid internal road wheel/bogey and dark rubber backing geometry so the crawler belts read as dense, solid dark stadium pads with raised steel lips catching a 1-pixel edge highlight.
2. **Correct Starboard Track Shading & Contrast:** Adjust normal angles, bevels, and material tones on the starboard track to eliminate negative contrast and ensure clean separation from the chassis shadow.
3. **Rebalance Deck Livery Plating:** Assign the industrial yellow livery material to key upper hull deck plates and the cab roof to eliminate the flat gray stamp read while keeping yellow within the minority budget.
4. **Refine Visor to a Thin Overhanging Brow:** Thin the yellow visor geometry into a sharp forward-projecting brow that catches a clean 1-pixel highlight across the top of a recessed, dark glazed windshield band.
5. **Chamfer & Step the Central Well:** Add chamfered outer lips and stepped interior mechanical ledges inside the well bay to ensure it reads as a functional recessed equipment bay rather than a flat black hole.
6. **Re-articulate Boom Spine & Cutting Bit:** Thicken the structural boom arm, assign a high-specular steel spine along the top ridge to catch a continuous 1-pixel highlight line, and enlarge/brighten the faceted cutting bit tip.
