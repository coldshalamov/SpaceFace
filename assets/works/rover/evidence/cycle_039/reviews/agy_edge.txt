### 1. Obvious Defects (Works-Edge View)

* **Cab House & Visor Bar:** The cab is currently a flat, black box with a solid yellow plastic strip pasted on the front edge like a roof sticker. It fails to read as a dark cab house with yellow side walls and a thin yellow visor bar set in front of/behind a readable dark glass pane. The circular gray greeble button on the roof and the flat yellow hinge tabs lack structural integration and read as toy pegs.
* **Toy / Clay / Card / Gray Plate Aesthetic:** The central chassis and deck are constructed from flat, thin, un-beveled gray polygonal plates and stepped cardboard-like boxes. There are no realistic structural joints, chamfers, weld beads, or industrial panel breaks. The cutout triangles along the inner track sponsons look like hollow card stock.
* **Side Walls:** The side walls flanking the hopper and bridging the tracks are flat, vertical sheets with sharp geometric cuts rather than reinforced, load-bearing rover hull armor.
* **Hopper Well:** The hopper is an empty, untextured rectangular pit with stepped, flat ramp geometry. It lacks a defined reinforced rim, wear lining, grate, or internal hopper mechanics, reading like an injection-molded plastic bin.
* **Boom & Bit Body:** The boom is an undetailed, uniform light-gray plank extending off the right side. It lacks a darker structural boom frame, a raised steel spine running down its length, and a distinct steel bit body/drill head at the tip. The bit tip is a tiny, muddy yellow/dark nub with no mechanical weight.
* **Track Height & Track Pods:** The tracks are flat lozenge bands with faint tread grooves wrapped around generic silver rim loops. They lack depth, sprocket teeth, tensioners, and realistic ground clearance/height definition relative to the main belly pan.
* **Scar Plate & Material Definition:** No scar plates, impact scuffing, edge wear, or steel material variation exist. Everything shares a flat, clay-like diffuse gray/black shading.

* **Silhouette & Contrast Loss:** At actual play scale (`works_edge.png`), the rover reduces to a muddy gray block flanked by two lozenge tracks. The thin boom arm completely washes out against the hull shadow.

* **Hopper Mush:** The hopper reads simply as a blurry dark hole with no clear rim contrast or depth cue.

### 3. Concrete Blender Actions

1. **Cab & Visor Rework:**
   * Model the cab house with dark structural geometry and distinct yellow side paneling.
   * Inset a dark, reflective glass pane on the cab front and suspend a crisp, thin yellow visor bar across/behind it with proper standoff mounts rather than a solid top-edge cap.
   * Remove the flat circular greeble peg from the cab roof.
2. **Hull & Plate Detailing:**
   * Bevel all sharp card-like edges on the side walls and chassis deck.
   * Add panel depth, bolting flanges, and reinforced corner brackets to eliminate the flat-plate/cardboard appearance.
   * Thicken the side walls surrounding the hopper with angled, reinforced armor coamings.
3. **Boom & Bit Assembly:**
   * Assign a darker base material to the main boom beam.
   * Extrude a raised, metallic steel spine running centrally along the boom top.
   * Model a distinct, chunky steel bit housing/body with mechanical couplers before the drill head.
4. **Hopper Well Upgrades:**
   * Add a reinforced, beveled hopper rim/coaming and textured interior chute plates with industrial edge trim.
5. **Tracks & Sponsons:**
   * Increase track tread profile depth and define the drive sprockets and guide rollers inside the track assemblies.
6. **Scar Plate & Materials:**
   * Add a steel scar/deflector plate on high-wear forward surfaces and assign proper roughness/metallic values to distinguish structural steel, yellow painted armor, and dark cab housing.
