### (1) Obvious Defects FROM THIS SITE VIEW

1. **Muddy Gray/Brown Stamp & Asymmetric Yellow Patch Read:**
   - At ~22 px site scale, the rover fails to read as an articulated industrial tracked mining crawler.
   - It collapses into a muddy gray/brown rectangular stamp with an asymmetric yellow block/patch on the starboard forward quadrant and indistinct dark voids.

2. **Track Belt Contrast Collapse & Missing Outer Steel Lip:**
   - Neither port nor starboard stadium belt holds as a crisp, solid dark rubber stadium wall capped by a thin raised steel lip catching a 1 px specular highlight.
   - Both track belts blend into the terrain and chassis due to severe negative contrast, losing perimeter definition.

3. **Yellow Visor Failure (Chunky Yellow Patch / Stamp vs. 1 px Brow Line):**
   - The safety yellow livery forms a chunky rectangular vertical patch/stamp across the cab face rather than a razor-thin 1 px horizontal visor line spanning cleanly across the front of the dark windshield band.
   - The yellow color spills over cab geometry instead of functioning strictly as a leading protective brow.

4. **Cab Glazing Band & Mid-Steel Tub Definition:**
   - The dark cab windshield band lacks definition and mid-steel bezel framing, blending into the surrounding dark chassis and the yellow paint stamp.
   - The windshield pane fails to separate cleanly from the body tub.

5. **Hopper Well Cavity vs. Windshield Bite:**
   - The intake hopper well does not resolve as a deep mechanical bite distinct from the windshield.
   - It appears as an indistinct dark notch lacking stepped interior depth and raised coaming lip relief.

6. **Boom Arm, Spine, & Bit Tip Read:**
   - The boom arm lacks sufficient lateral clearance and negative space against the starboard track, clumping into the hull silhouette.
   - It reads as an ambiguous dark/yellow hook rather than an offset articulated finger with a dark arm, a raised steel top spine catching light, and a faceted steel bit with a 1 px bright specular tip.

### (3) Concrete Blender Actions that Help this Site Silhouette

1. **Re-establish Track Separation & 1 px Outer Steel Lips:**
   - Model distinct longitudinal shadow channels between the chassis tub and crawler belts to achieve positive track contrast (`TRACK_CONTRAST` >= 18.0 on both sides).
   - Bevel the outer track edge to catch a 1 px specular steel highlight over a solid dark rubber belt wall.

2. **Confine Yellow Strictly to a 1 px Leading Brow Visor:**
   - Remove yellow paint from cab vertical faces, cab sides, and boom geometry.
   - Confine safety yellow strictly to the leading 1 px horizontal brow visor shelf extending forward directly above the windshield band (`LIVERY_SAT_RENDERED` >= 0.3).

3. **Enlarge and Frame Dark Windshield Band:**
   - Expand windshield glazing geometry (`CAB_PANE` >= 0.02 in clay and textured passes) and frame it in a crisp mid-steel tub bezel to cleanly separate the cab glazing from body cavities.

4. **Offset Boom Arm & Accentuate Machined Steel Spine / Bit Tip:**
   - Angle the boom arm outward to ensure `BOOM_OFFSET` >= 0.15, creating visible negative space against the starboard track.
   - Give the boom a dark structural arm body topped with a raised machined steel spine leading to a distinct bit tip that catches a 1 px bright specular highlight.

5. **Deepen and Rim the Intake Well:**
   - Deepen the hopper cavity and add a raised mid-steel coaming lip around the opening to guarantee `WELL_IS_A_HOLE` >= 0.24 and clay `WELL_HOLE` >= 0.04.
