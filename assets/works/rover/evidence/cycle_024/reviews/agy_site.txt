### (1) Obvious Defects From This Site View
* **Chassis Reads as a Gray Cassette / Electronic Stamp:** The main body collapses into a flat, monolithic gray rectangle with two square cutouts, reading more like an IC chip or cassette tape than a heavy mining vehicle.
* **Hollow Yellow Highlighter Box:** The yellow livery renders as a high-contrast hollow rectangular outline (a "National Geographic" frame) surrounding a dark center. It reads like a floating UI selection box or a hollow toy sticker rather than a solid, structural cab-roof armor patch.
* **Invisible / Swallowed Track Undercarriage:** The port and starboard stadium tracks have virtually no contrast against the dark cell background and shadows. The outer track loops, cog wheels, and grouser cleats vanish entirely, eliminating the tracked-crawler silhouette.
* **Weak, Indistinct Boom Finger:** The offset boom on the right is a tiny 2-pixel gray nub with a single pale pixel at the tip. It fails to read as an articulated industrial steel boom with an excavation bit.
* **Flat 2D Well Cutout:** The excavation well reads as a flat 2D black pixel patch rather than a deep, volumetric chassis bite with internal mechanical relief.

* **`TRACK_CONTRAST_PORT` (-1.14) & `TRACK_CONTRAST_STARBOARD` (-20.63) [Floor >= 18.0]:** Negative contrast values confirm the tracks are darker than or lost inside background shadow, failing to define the outer silhouette.
