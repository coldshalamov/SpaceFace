### (1) Defects From This Site View

1. **Solid Yellow Stamp / Missing Cab Windshield**:
   * The yellow livery is rendered as a flat, solid vertical yellow block attached to the front of the cab. Because there is no dark recessed windshield or glass glint beneath/behind it, the yellow reads as an applied yellow postage stamp rather than an overhanging cab visor on an industrial steel house.
2. **Monolithic Gray Box / Flat Rear Deck Read**:
   * The central and rear hull between the tracks remains an unbroken, square-cornered gray slab. It reads as a flat gray rectangular stamp/box rather than an articulated heavy mining chassis.
3. **Shallow Hopper Cavity**:
   * While the well appears as a dark square in the mid-deck, it lacks internal shadow depth and sidewall definition, reading as a flat dark square painted onto the deck plate rather than a deep, functional ore hopper.
4. **Poor Track Belt Contrast & Boundary Definition**:
   * The track belts (especially starboard) lack sufficient value drop/shadow separation from the chassis, softening the read of two distinct, heavy-duty crawler assemblies flanking the hull.
5. **Boom Spine Ridge Definition**:
   * While the offset boom finger and bright tip bit are discernible, the connecting steel spine arm lacks a crisp specular top edge, leaving the structural connection weak against low-contrast asteroid terrain.
