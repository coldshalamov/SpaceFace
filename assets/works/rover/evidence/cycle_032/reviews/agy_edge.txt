### 1. Obvious Defects (Works-Edge View)

* **Steel Cab House, Yellow Sides, Yellow Fore Visor & Small Dark Pane:**
  * The cab house reads as a raw gray primitive box with a flat yellow square/block sitting on the roof rather than an integrated industrial steel cab house.
  * The yellow reads as a plastic square/patch pasted on the roof rather than a functional forward visor/brow overhanging a steel cab.
  * Cab side walls lack safety-yellow painted steel surfaces.
  * The small dark windshield pane lacks defined bezel framing, glass depth, or specular structure, remaining overshadowed by the roof yellow block.
* **Toy / Clay / Card / Box / Gray Plate Read:**
  * The chassis, central deck, tub, and rear surfaces are flat, featureless matte gray plates with sharp CG edges.
  * Completely lacks panel seams, weld lines, material breakup, or surface depth, reading like an unpainted clay block or cardboard toy assembly.
* **Side Walls:**
  * Flanking side walls are flat extruded gray slabs with simple 45┬░ angular chamfers.
  * They lack structural thickness, stiffener ribs, or mounting flanges, contributing directly to the card/plate aesthetic.
* **Track Height & Track Assemblies:**
  * Track units appear as flat 2D oval stadium loops with minimal height/undercarriage volume.
  * The perimeter steel lips catch light but the rubber stadium belts lack discrete tread cleats/grousers.
* **Hopper Well:**
  * The hopper well is a stark, black cutout hole punched into the middle deck plate.
  * It lacks an extruded protective perimeter coaming lip, sloped interior chute plates, and internal liner ribs.
* **Darker Boom with Bright Steel Spine:**
  * The front boom is rendered as a stepped gray wedge without clear material contrast.
  * It lacks a distinct, raised bright steel spine running along a darker arm body; the forward drill cone appears visually detached.
* **Scar Plate & Mount Points:**
  * The dual black rectangular plates with yellow bracket dots behind the cab read like paper-thin 2D stickers/cards on a gray plate rather than heavy, bolted steel wear plates.

### 2. MTX Defects Visible at Play Size

* **Toy Plastic Read:** The yellow roof block resolves at small scale as a plastic toy brick/sticker rather than an overhanging safety visor.
* **Void Ambiguity:** The black hopper cutout and dark windshield area read as flat, uninformative black holes lacking depth cues.
* **Subpixel Aliasing:** Thin track lip highlights and tiny yellow pivot dots produce subpixel jitter/aliasing at native play resolution.
* **Monolithic Gray Silhouette:** The flat gray chassis, deck, and side walls merge into an undifferentiated low-contrast silhouette without functional panel breakdown.
* **Boom Readability:** Without a bright steel centerline spine, the dark boom arm loses definition against dark backgrounds.

### 3. Concrete Blender Actions (No Cabin Furniture)

1. **Cab House & Visor:**
   * Reshape the yellow geometry into an overhanging forward brow/visor extending past the cab front.
   * Apply yellow painted steel to the cab side panels and structural gray steel to the cab roof/frame.
   * Model a defined, beveled window frame with a dark tinted reflective glass shader for the forward pane.
2. **Boom Assembly:**
   * Apply a darker industrial base shader to the boom arm.
   * Model and bevel a prominent bright polished steel centerline spine along the top ridge connecting into the nose bit.
3. **Hull, Deck & Side Walls:**
   * Apply `Bevel` modifiers (weighted normals) to all sharp deck, side wall, and chassis edges.
   * Inset structural panel seams, weld lines, and stiffener flanges across the flat gray deck plates to eliminate the toy/clay/card read.
4. **Hopper Well:**
   * Model an extruded raised perimeter coaming lip around the mouth and add sloped interior chute liner plates inside the well.
5. **Scar Plates:**
   * Extrude physical plate thickness with beveled edges and add recessed hex fastener geometry to the mounting plates behind the cab.
6. **Tracks:**
   * Add discrete tread cleats/grousers along the stadium belt circumference and adjust track height and inner hub recess depth.
