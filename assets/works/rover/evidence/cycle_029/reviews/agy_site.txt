### (1) Every Obvious Defect From This Site View

* **Hollow C-Bracket / Disjointed Chassis (`ONE_BODY` Failure):** At ~22 px, the crawler reads like a hollow gray horseshoe/bracket surrounding a black cutout, flanked by two detached stadium loops. It fails to read as a single cohesive heavy mining vehicle.
* **Track Lip Dropout & Starboard Ground Blending:** The outer lips of the stadium tracks fail to catch a crisp, continuous 1 px light highlight. On the starboard (lower) track, the outer edge drops out and blends directly into the dark brown pad terrain.
* **Flat Black Hole Well:** The excavation hopper well reads as a flat 2D pitch-black punch-hole rather than a deep, mechanical cavity with bevel highlights and volumetric depth.
* **Muddy / Desaturated Livery Paint:** The yellow side paint is rendered so dark and desaturated that it reads as a murky olive-gray smudge next to the boom root rather than crisp high-visibility industrial side paint.
* **Boom Arm Ridge & Offset Definition:** The boom arm lacks sufficient lateral clearance and ridge shading against the forward hull shoulder, causing the cutter bit to read like a detached light dot rather than a tool mounted on an offset articulated finger.

### (3) Concrete Blender Actions That Help This Site Silhouette

1. **Bridge Hull Mass to Tracks:** Add solid structural deck plates and gusset transitions connecting the central chassis to the track sponsons to eliminate the hollow C-bracket look and unify `ONE_BODY`.
2. **Raise & Highlight Outer Track Lips:** Flange and bevel the outer steel rims of both stadium track guards with a higher metallic/specular response so they consistently catch a sharp 1 px edge highlight against the ground.
3. **Bevel Well Rim & Add Floor Relief:** Add a prominent top bevel/chamfer to the hopper well rim to catch top directional light, and elevate internal well floor geometry to provide readable cavity depth rather than a flat black hole.
4. **Boost Livery Saturation & Material Response:** Increase the basecolor saturation and tuning on the yellow side paint material so it registers as vibrant construction paint under site lighting.
5. **Thicken & Offset Boom Arm:** Increase the lateral offset of the boom pivot and sharpen its upper spine ridge to cast a distinct 1 px shadow line separating the boom arm from the main hull.
