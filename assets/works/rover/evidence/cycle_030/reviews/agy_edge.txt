* **Side Walls**:

  * The starboard side wall terminates abruptly at the cab notch.

### (2) MTX Defects Visible at Play Size

* **Toy-Like Flat Albedo & High Contrast**: Stark, unweathered color blocking between uniform flat gray hull plates, pitch-black cavities/tracks, high-saturation yellow cab trim, and beige nose cone.
* **Lack of Specular Response & PBR Micro-Detail**: Hull plates, track bands, and boom surfaces exhibit flat diffuse reflectance with no metallic sheen, roughness variation, or anisotropic highlights on machined steel edges.
* **Missing Contact AO & Edge Shading**: Recesses, seams, underside track cowlings, and the scar block joins lack contact ambient occlusion shadowing, making components look like floating, unattached layers.
* **Zero Weathering**: No regolith dust accumulation in crevices or tread valleys, no grease/hydraulic seepage around hinge pins, and no edge paint chipping or dark primer reveal on the yellow cab.
