### (1) Obvious Defects Visible from `works_edge` View

1. **Toy / Clay / Card / Box / Gray Plate Read (Primary Visual Defect):**
   - The vehicle still reads as a primitive toy CAD blockout composed of flat dark-gray extruded slab boxes with yellow cardboard plates and strips stuck on top.
   - The yellow shoulder plates flanking the hopper do not read as structural industrial safety panels fastened over a steel chassis; they read as flat yellow toy tiles with black printed rectangular marks.
   - The rear yellow transom plate reads as a flat rectangular card/sticker attached to a gray slab.
   - The cutter head is a low-poly faceted pale gray cone rigidly glued to the boom tip, with no mechanical drum or pick teeth.
   - The transitions between the aft tub, midsection, and forward nose lack structural fabrication lines, weld seams, service access plates, and mechanical hardware, reading as an unibody plastic toy shell.

2. **Side Walls & Flanks:**
   - Lateral side walls connecting the central tub to the track assemblies are flat, featureless vertical slab faces lacking structural depth.
   - Missing outrigger mounting brackets, lower rocker sills, gusset plates, and chassis tie-in bulkheads to the track sponsons.
   - The hull taper from the wide hopper shoulders to the narrower cab waist is a simple faceted box transition lacking formed sheet metal or welded plate construction.

3. **Track Height & Running Gear:**
   - The track loops read as toy rubber belts with uniform blocky cleat teeth protruding from a flat band.
   - The drive sprockets and idlers are flat bronze/brown disks with single center dots, lacking teeth engagement, recessed bearing caps, or hub bolt rings.
   - Undercarriage lacks internal mechanical depth: no road wheel bogies, top carrier/return rollers, track tensioner cylinders, or center guide horns between the track runs.
   - Sponsons look hollow and lightweight rather than heavy structural steel cast frames.

4. **Cab House:**
   - The forward cab is a dark gray box with a rectangular cutout opening into a pitch-black interior void.
   - Missing a welded structural retaining bezel, rubber weather gasket, and dark dielectric glass window pane.
   - Missing exterior roof utility fixtures: no ROPS (Roll-Over Protection Structure) rock deflector bars, no armored work light pod, no antenna mount, and no ventilation intake cowl.
   - The two thin yellow strips on the cab roof read as decal tape rather than functional safety plating.

5. **Hopper Well:**
   - The hopper well is a black rectangular void cut into the rear deck plate.
   - Lacks internal structural volume: no abrasion-resistant (AR) wear plate liners, no sloped floor/deflection ramp, no internal stiffener ribs, and no reinforced lip flange collar.
   - Rear dump tailgate hinge lugs and locking hardware are completely absent.

6. **Boom & Cutter Head:**
   - The boom arm is a solid dark gray trapezoidal wedge rigidly fused directly to the starboard forward deck.
   - Lacks mechanical joint articulation: no dual-clevis base pivot mount, heavy hinge pin, hydraulic elevation cylinder/piston ram, or flexible hydraulic line bundles.
   - The cutter bit is a low-poly pale gray conical bulb with no rotating pick drum, conical carbide teeth studs, or thermal oxide heat discoloration.

7. **Scar Plate:**
   - Flank wear/scar plate is visually absent or completely flush with the gray hull flank, lacking independent plate thickness, bevels, perimeter hex bolts, and abrasive wear.

### (4) Verdict

**REVISE**

*Rationale:* The asset remains a yellow-and-gray box with flat plates stuck on top and an unarticulated boom wedge. It lacks mechanical track running gear, has unlined void cutouts for the cab and hopper, and lacks essential hydraulic cylinders, hose routing, and edge bevels. Substantive structural remodeling across the chassis, boom kinematics, track running gear, and hopper interior is required.
