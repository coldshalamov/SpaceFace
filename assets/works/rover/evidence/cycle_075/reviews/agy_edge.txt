### Visual Review: Asteroid Works Rover (`PQ-131.01`, Cycle 75)
**Viewpoint:** `works_edge` (top-down / game-edge crop)

---

### (1) Obvious Defects from Works-Edge View

- **Flat Gray Deck Plate / Toy/Card Appearance:**
  - Glaring flat, untextured gray rectangular deck plate between the cab house, boom base, and the starboard track. It reads as a raw primitive card/clay slab with zero mechanical detail, fasteners, or texture breakups.
  - Hopper side walls and chassis transitions are flat extruded planar sheets lacking industrial flanges, bevels, or structural rim thickness.
- **Cab House & Visor Structure:**
  - Cab house is an overly sharp, blocky extrusion (toy-box silhouette).
  - The yellow visor rim sits directly along the front brow past the dark glass pane without mounting bracketry or weathered beveling, creating a high-contrast plastic stripe.
- **Cab Window Pane:**
  - The dark front glass pane is legible and distinct, but the framing surrounding the glass lacks depth/recess into the dark cab shell.
- **Hopper Well:**
  - Hopper rim is razor-thin at the top perimeter. The sloped sidewalls and interior stepped floor lack weld seams, structural stiffeners, or material wear in the impact zone.
- **Boom & Steel Spine / Bit Body:**
  - Boom structure is a stark, faceted block.
  - The raised steel spine/cylinder running along the boom into the cutter bit body enters cleanly with no sleeve, collar, or hydraulic mounting housing at the junction.
- **Tracks & Suspension:**
  - Track height and treads read properly as solid segmented rubber belts rather than wire loops, but the inner track wells lack mechanical clearance detail and debris guards.
- **Rear Scar Plate:**
  - Rear scar plate is a simple flat rectangular cutout/insert with no bolt patterns or perimeter weld geometry.

---

### (2) MTX Defects Visible at Play Size

- **Uniform Diffuse / Plastic Shader:** Materials across the cab, boom, and chassis lack surface roughness breakup, edge micro-wear, and asteroid grit/dust accumulation.
- **Flat Shading on Gray Plate:** The starboard gray deck plate has a flat clay shader with zero specularity variation or grime mapping.
- **Over-Saturated Visor Accent:** The yellow visor brow is overly flat and saturated, reading as unshaded yellow plastic rather than coated/safety-painted industrial metal.
- **Lack of Micro-Occlusion:** Interior corners of the hopper well, track wells, and cab-chassis junction lack baked ambient occlusion depth.

---

### (3) Concrete Blender Actions (No Cabin Furniture)

1. **Bevel & Thicken Chassis Edges:** Add bevel modifiers or weighted normal bevels to the cab house corners, boom housing, and hopper rim to break up razor-sharp 90-degree CG edges.
2. **Break Up / Detail the Starboard Gray Deck Plate:** Replace or detail the flat gray card deck beside the cab with embossed diamond-tread plate, structural stiffener ribs, and recessed cable/hydraulic conduit runs.
3. **Add Flanges to Hopper Well:** Extrude an outward lip/reinforcement flange along the upper rim of the hopper side walls to give structural thickness.
4. **Detail Boom-to-Bit Transition:** Model a collar/gland seal housing where the raised steel spine enters the cutter bit body.
5. **Texture & Shader Pass:**
   - Add roughness roughness noise and subtle edge scuffing to the yellow visor.
   - Bake ambient occlusion into the hopper well corners and undercarriage track cavities.
   - Apply grime/dust gradient map to the flat gray deck and track wells.

---

### (4) Verdict

**REVISE**
