### (1) Obvious Defects FROM THIS SITE VIEW

1. **Fractured Gestalt & Yellow Postage-Stamp / Box Read:**
   - At ~22 px site scale, the rover fails to read as a cohesive tracked mining crawler (`ONE_BODY` = 0.1667 vs. 0.9000 min).
   - Rather than reading as an integrated machine, the silhouette resolves as a muddy, low-contrast gray/brown box with an isolated, chunky yellow postage stamp / vertical block stuck onto the starboard-forward corner.

2. **Track Belt Dissolution & Missing 1 px Outer Steel Lips:**
   - Neither the port (top) nor starboard (bottom) crawler track holds as a solid dark rubber wall bounded by a thin raised steel lip catching a 1 px highlight.
   - The crawler belts dissolve into the surrounding terrain shadow envelope with severe negative contrast (`TRACK_CONTRAST_PORT` = -26.9908, `TRACK_CONTRAST_STARBOARD` = -23.7782 vs. +18.0000 min floor).

3. **Yellow Visor Failure & Misplaced Livery Block:**
   - The intended thin 1 px yellow visor line directly in front of the windshield band fails to resolve (`LIVERY_SAT_RENDERED` = 0.0156 vs. 0.3000 min).
   - High-contrast safety yellow pools as an asymmetrical patch across the starboard cab flank and boom root, creating a toy-like postage-stamp defect rather than a functional vehicle hazard brow.

4. **Sunken Cab & Undefined Windshield Band:**
   - The cab structure remains depressed below the deck plane (`CAB_IS_RAISED` = 0.0396 vs. +0.2800 min), and the windshield aperture in clay is undersized (`CAB_PANE` = 0.0125 in clay vs. 0.0200 min).
   - The dark windshield lacks a crisp mid-steel bezel and is visually swallowed by adjacent chassis shadows and the yellow corner block.

5. **Intake Well Topographic Ambiguity:**
   - The intake well aperture in clay is undersized (`WELL_HOLE` = 0.0279 in clay vs. 0.0400 min) and lacks a defined mid-steel coaming lip.
   - The hopper opening fails to register cleanly as a distinct negative bite separated from the cab windshield.

6. **Boom Arm Clumping & Indistinct Tool End:**
   - The boom arm lacks sufficient lateral clearance (`BOOM_OFFSET` = 0.1466 vs. 0.1500 min floor), clumping tightly against the starboard track.
   - The boom arm lacks a readable raised steel spine and distinct bright specular tip, reading as a dark nub/hook fused into the yellow corner block rather than an offset articulated mining tool.

1. **Re-establish Track Separation & 1 px Outer Steel Lips:**

   - Deepen the shadow reveal groove between the crawler tracks and the chassis tub to pull track contrast well into positive territory (`TRACK_CONTRAST` >= +18.0).

2. **Confine Safety Yellow Strictly to a 1 px Forward Visor Shelf:**
   - Strip yellow material assignments from the boom mount, cab flanks, and side chassis.
   - Place safety yellow solely on a thin 1 px horizontal visor shelf running across the leading brow of the windshield (`LIVERY_SAT_RENDERED` >= 0.30).

3. **Elevate Cab & Frame Windshield Band:**
   - Raise the cab roof geometry above the chassis deck (`CAB_IS_RAISED` >= +0.28) and enlarge the windshield glazing aperture (`CAB_PANE` >= 0.02 in clay).
   - Surround the dark glass band with a clean mid-steel bezel to clearly define the forward cabin.

4. **Enlarge and Rim the Intake Well:**
   - Expand the hopper opening in geometry (`WELL_HOLE` >= 0.04 in clay) and add an extruded mid-steel coaming rim around the opening to ensure the intake reads as a distinct negative bite.

5. **Offset Boom Arm & Accentuate Machined Steel Spine / Bit Tip:**
   - Rotate/translate the boom arm outward to achieve `BOOM_OFFSET` >= 0.15, creating readable negative space against the starboard track.
   - Keep the boom arm body in dark structural steel, add a raised light-catching spine along its top edge, and crown the bit tip with a bright specular steel facet.
