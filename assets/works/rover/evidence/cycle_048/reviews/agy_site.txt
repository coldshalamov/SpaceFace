### 1. Obvious Defects from this Site View

* **Gray Cassette / Stamp Read:** The chassis reads primarily as a square brown-gray floor stamp with a prominent gray cassette frame on the left, failing the read of a heavy articulated mining crawler.
* **Track Wall / Lip Failure:** Neither port nor starboard track holds as a solid dark rubber wall. The starboard track completely bleeds into the ground shadow, and both tracks lack a continuous raised steel outer lip catching 1 px highlights to define the outer track perimeters.
* **Yellow Livery as Clumpy Stamp:** The yellow livery reads as a clumpy, vertical patch/dot on the right cab flank rather than a crisp, continuous 1 px horizontal visor line running across the leading edge of the dark windshield band.
* **Cab Hierarchy & Windshield Obscuration:** The cab does not read clearly as a distinct dark cab volume on a mid-steel tub; the dark windshield band is crushed and disrupted by the misplaced yellow patch.
* **Equipment Well as Floating Gray Box:** The well does not read as a deep architectural bite into the deck; instead, its bright gray frame reads like a surface-mounted rectangular box/cassette that visually overpowers the rest of the chassis.
* **Boom Arm Indistinct / Lacks Silhouette Separation:** The boom on the right fails to read as an offset articulated finger. It lacks sufficient reach and offset, blending into the starboard track and ground shadow without a readable dark arm, raised steel spine, or bright 1 px bit tip highlight.
