1. Obvious defects from the Works view

- Silhouette reads as a small yellow rectangular chassis between two parallel tracks, not a convincing heavy industrial rover. The body lacks a strong excavator/hauler mass hierarchy.
- Both dark stadium-shaped tracks are visible, but they resemble soft toy treads or linked brown beads. The belts, shoes, rollers, idlers, and suspension do not separate clearly at play size.
- The hopper well reads as a genuine dark hole, but its rectangular mouth is extremely plain. The rim has weak thickness and little evidence of a sloped hopper, reinforced walls, or material-handling structure.
- The raised cab is recognizable on the right half, including a dark roof pane, but it remains a yellow box with square cutouts. The pane looks inserted into a block rather than framed into a manufactured cab shell.
- The offset boom is readable projecting to the right, but it is a flat yellow card/plank with almost no taper, section depth, hinge structure, hydraulic linkage, or root articulation.
- The steel bit is only a tiny gray plate at the boom tip. It does not read as a shaped cutting, drilling, or crushing tool.
- Large yellow surfaces look like flat cards and stacked boxes. The chassis, cab, hopper surround, and boom have little visible lofting, edge treatment, joint construction, or shell thickness.
- The uniformly saturated yellow and weak material separation produce a plastic-toy/clay-blockout read.
- The brown tracks also have a clay-like, low-definition surface read rather than rubber, dark steel, or dusty composite.
- Several small gray elements read as generic gray plates instead of authored steel hardware.
- The central assembly has a LEGO-like stack: yellow rectangular frame, yellow cab box, black square openings, and attached yellow boom.
- The rover is legible as “tracked machine,” but not yet at the Hitch-plus quality floor.

3. Concrete Blender actions

- Rebuild the chassis as a continuous manufactured shell with changing planform width, a lower central belly, reinforced track shoulders, and controlled bevels after applying scale.
- Preserve the hopper opening, but boolean it through the upper shell, add a visibly thick rim, taper the inner walls toward a lower collection volume, and add a dark ribbed floor or auger mass that reads from above.
- Reshape the cab into a raised, tapered protective enclosure rather than a cube. Cut a real roof-pane opening, add a thin dark pane, metal frame, gasket, and roof overhang. Do not add cabin furniture.
- Replace the boom card with three or more lofted sections: thick boxed root, tapered load-bearing arm, and narrower tool mount. Add a separate pivot body and visible clearance gap.
- Add a large hinge pin, bearing collars, two hydraulic cylinders, and hoses routed between real fittings. Keep these large enough to survive the Works camera.
- Replace the gray tip plate with a recognizable steel tool: tapered wedge, toothed cutter, drill head, or crusher jaw appropriate to the rover’s function. Give it thickness, a mounting collar, and exposed-metal roughness variation.
- Rebuild each track as a dark continuous belt with broad shoes, recessed wheel channel, visible front idler and rear drive sprocket masses, plus restrained dust in cavities. Avoid rounded bead-like links.
- Add structural brackets between chassis and track units so the body no longer appears suspended between two stadium loops.
- Break up the yellow box stack using inset service hatches, stepped armor edges, dark seam channels, and one designed off-center repair or replacement panel.
- Establish distinct materials: worn coated yellow steel, dark rubber/composite track belts, darker steel shoes and running gear, machined steel tool surfaces, and dark glass.
- Use weighted/custom normals with narrow, scale-correct bevels. Keep broad plates flat and place highlights only on manufactured edges.
- Cluster wear around the hopper lip, track shoulders, boom pivot, hydraulic fittings, and cutting bit. Keep large body panels comparatively quiet.
- Check the revised rover again at this exact 120-pixel Works scale; discard any hinge, hose, rim, or material distinction that disappears there and strengthen the underlying form instead.

4. Verdict

**REVISE**

The rover is currently a yellow box-and-plate assembly with toy-like stadium tracks, a card boom, and a generic gray bit. The hopper gives it one useful volumetric cue, but the complete asset does not yet reach the Hitch-plus floor.
