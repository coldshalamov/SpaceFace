### (1) Obvious Defects Visible from `works_edge` View

1. **Toy / Clay / Card / Box / Gray Plate Read (Primary Visual Defect):**
   - The vehicle reads immediately as a stamped yellow plastic toy / CAD box blockout with flat black card cutouts stuck to the surface.
   - The hull is a single monolithic yellow slab with boolean cutouts, lacking heavy industrial fabrication language, structural steel plate courses, or chassis-to-track transitions.
   - The dark chevron and deck plates read as flat 2D cardboard decals/stickers glued directly onto the yellow deck.
   - The cutter head reads as an unarticulated, low-poly gray cone/plate primitive.

2. **Side Walls:**
   - The side flanks between the upper deck and track frames are featureless, sheer vertical yellow walls.
   - Missing structural rocker sills, undercarriage mounting brackets, access covers, heavy lift lugs, and welded chassis seam lines.

3. **Track Height & Undercarriage:**
   - The track belts consist of basic extruded tread pads over an oval band with zero visible mechanical undercarriage between the hull and tracks.
   - Missing drive sprockets, front idler wheels, track return rollers, bogie bobbins, guide flanges, and tensioner cylinders. The track assemblies float alongside the hull.

4. **Cab House:**
   - The cab is a simple rectangular yellow block with a raw square cutout in the roof.
   - The roof opening is a hollow black void lacking a structural perimeter frame, rubber seal gasket, rollover protection structure (ROPS), or dielectric glazing highlights.
   - Roof utility penetrations (lamp socket / vent) are plain circular puncture holes resembling peg holes on a plastic toy rather than manufactured fixtures.

5. **Hopper Well:**
   - The hopper well is a plain rectangular boolean hole cut into the yellow deck dropping into flat darkness.
   - Lacks interior AR-plate wear liners, angled deflection ramps, corner gussets, structural top rim flanges, and rear tailgate hinge hardware.

6. **Boom & Cutter Bit:**
   - The boom arm is a solid, flat yellow wedge cantilevered off the starboard flank without mechanical mass or structural articulation.
   - Missing a boxed girder/truss profile with web cutouts, dual-clevis pivot yoke, hydraulic lift cylinder (barrel and chrome piston rod), and hydraulic service hoses.
   - The cutter bit is a low-poly gray conical frustum/plate primitive, completely lacking carbide pick teeth, rotary bearing housing, and friction heat discoloration.

7. **Scar Plate:**
   - The scar plate on the flank fails to read as a distinct, replaceable, bolt-on damaged steel plate; it has no readable thickness, corner fastener definition, or scorch/grime material identity at play distance.

### (2) MTX Defects Visible at Play Size

- **MTX-01 (Mid-Poly Edges & Weighted Normals):** Edges on the cab, hull, and boom lack scale-correct bevels with weighted normal highlights, retaining a sharp CAD/blockout look.
- **MTX-02 (Authored Cross-Section Loft):** The main chassis fails the authored station requirement; it is a single scaled box with cutouts rather than a sequenced chassis weldment.
- **MTX-03 / MTX-04 / MTX-57 (Skin-Breaking Booleans, Readable Volume & Wall Thickness):** The hopper well and cab window are empty box cutouts with no readable internal mechanical geometry or authored 4ΓÇô12 cm structural wall thickness.
- **MTX-05 (Panelization with Gaps):** The yellow deck and flanks lack stepped plate courses, overlapping armor plates, and recessed seam channels.
- **MTX-07 / MTX-36 (Thin-Shell Glazing & Framed Tub):** The cab window lacks an authored perimeter frame, gasket channel, and clearcoat dielectric response.
- **MTX-10 (Sensors & Hardware):** Roof utility ports are raw cylindrical voids rather than modeled lamp cans with bezels/lenses and hooded vent caps.
- **MTX-11 (Cables and Hoses as Curves):** Total absence of Bezier curve hydraulic hoses or conduits feeding the boom actuator and cutter head.
- **MTX-12 (Fasteners Only at Interfaces):** Missing structural hex bolt patterns along plate seams, bearing caps, and track sponsons; existing fastener marks are arbitrary dots on flat faces.
- **MTX-33 / MTX-34 / MTX-35 / MTX-38 (Substance Classification & Authored ORM):**
  - Yellow paint lacks clearcoat/orange-peel roughness differentiation.
  - Steel, rubber, and painted metal lack distinct PBR material responses in the ORM channels.
- **MTX-39 / MTX-40 (Cavity Dirt & Curvature Edge Wear):** Missing curvature-driven edge wear revealing bare steel/primer along the hopper rim, track sponsons, and boom edges; cavities lack contact dirt.
- **MTX-41 (Heat Stain):** Cutter bit lacks an authored thermal oxide gradient (straw/purple/blue/soot) from high-friction ore extraction.
- **MTX-46 (Forbidden Reads):** Direct violation of the forbidden "plastic toy / yellow box with plates / CAD blockout" read.
- **MTX-56 (Value Hierarchy):** Collapses into high-contrast yellow vs pitch-black cavities; missing intermediate mechanical steel, exposed chassis, and edge highlights.
- **MTX-61 (Joint Language):** Missing weld beads, flange joints, and mounting brackets at the cab-to-deck, boom-to-hull, and track-to-chassis interfaces.
- **MTX-62 (Separated Moving Parts):** The boom is modeled as a fused rigid stick with no articulated pivot yoke or hydraulic cylinder separation.
