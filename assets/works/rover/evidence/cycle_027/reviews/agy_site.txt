### (1) Every Obvious Defect From This Site View

* **Yellow Postage-Stamp / Toy Blob Cab:** The yellow cab roof reads as a flat, unsegmented blob/sticker stamped onto the starboard forward deck. The window pane (`CAB_PANE`) is completely lost at this resolution, and the yellow lacks a readable surrounding dark bezel, making it pop as a bright toy paint dot rather than an integrated armored operator cab.
* **Floating / Disconnected Cutter Bit:** The mining bit (the pale cross `+` on the starboard front) visually detaches from the chassis. The boom arm connecting it is swallowed into dark shadows or lacks sufficient thickness, causing the bit to read as a floating icon/sparkle rather than a mechanical tool mounted to an articulated arm.
* **Severe Track Contrast & Silhouette Dropout:** While the stadium track geometry is present, the track edges suffer from severe contrast loss against the dark ground (particularly starboard), failing to present crisp, balanced 1 px light-catching steel lips that hold the outer crawler silhouette.
* **Flat 2D Well Void:** The excavation hopper well reads as a harsh, flat black hole punched into the middle of the gray hull plate rather than a deep, structural mechanical cavity with rim bevels and volumetric depth gradients.
* **Fragmented Vehicle Read (`ONE_BODY` Failure):** The vehicle does not resolve into a single cohesive heavy mining crawler. At ~22 px, it breaks apart into disconnected visual fragments: an open gray C-bracket hull, an empty black void, a yellow paint sticker, and a floating disconnected bit.

### (3) Concrete Blender Actions That Help This Site Silhouette

1. **Bezel & Pane the Cab:** Frame the yellow livery inside a dark structural steel bezel/collar, and widen/deepen the dark cab visor glass geometry so the window reliably resolves as a 1 px dark slit inside the yellow patch.
2. **Thicken & Highlight Boom Arm:** Increase the mesh cross-section of the boom arm and apply a distinct mid-tone/specular ridge so it visibly connects the forward chassis to the cutter bit, eliminating the floating `+` bit read.
3. **Broaden & Raise Outer Track Lips:** Widen the outer track guard flanges and sharpen their metallic rim highlight to catch a solid, continuous 1 px edge that defines both stadium loops against the pad.
4. **Bevel Well Perimeter & Add Cavity Gradient:** Chamfer the upper perimeter of the hopper well to catch top directional light, and deepen the ambient occlusion within the well to give the cavity volumetric depth rather than a flat black cutout.
5. **Bridge Deck & Track Housings:** Add substantial deck plating and transition fillets between the central chassis and track sponsons to unify the silhouette into a single solid crawler body.
