### (1) Obvious Defects from Site View (~22 px Scale)

- **Gray Stamp / Floppy-Disk Silhouette:** The main chassis reads as a flat, desaturated grayish-tan rectangular card / 3.5" floppy disk with a center cutout rather than a heavy, rugged tracked mining crawler.
- **Hollow "Paperclip" Track Loops:** Both port (top) and starboard (bottom) tracks read as wire loops / hollow paperclips. Thin bright perimeter lips enclose dark empty troughs rather than reading as solid, heavy dark rubber crawler belts with crisp 1-pixel steel catch lips.
- **Isolated Yellow Hook / Crescent Instead of Visor Brow:** The yellow livery registers as an isolated, curved yellow hook/crescent sitting awkwardly on the forward deck/well coaming rather than a crisp, thin forward-projecting yellow visor line shading a recessed dark windshield band.
- **Cab & Windshield Collapse:** The operator cab lacks vertical relief and structural definition from the mid-steel tub, reading as a flat dark square patch adjacent to the yellow hook rather than an elevated, glazed enclosure.
- **Cookie-Cutter Well Cavity:** The central well reads as a flat, 2D punch-hole / washer cutout through the chassis rather than an engineered mechanical hopper with coaming and stepped depth.
- **Weak Boom Silhouette & Muddy Tip:** The boom reads as a flimsy dark horizontal stick with a dull orange/yellow dot at the tip. It lacks the distinct offset geometry, raised steel spine (needed to catch a 1-pixel specular highlight line), and bright faceted steel bit.
