### (1) Obvious Defects FROM THIS SITE VIEW

1. **Gestalt & Fractured Stamp / Toy Read:**
   - At ~22 px site scale, the rover fails to read as a cohesive, heavy industrial tracked mining crawler.
   - The silhouette fragments into disconnected patches of mid-steel, dark hollows, and an asymmetric yellow corner block (`ONE_BODY` = 0.3077 vs. 0.9 min), reading more like a broken gray stamp with an attached yellow toy block than an integrated vehicle.

2. **Track Belt Dissolution & Missing 1 px Outer Steel Lips:**
   - Neither the port (top) nor starboard (bottom) stadium belt holds cleanly as a solid dark rubber wall defined by a thin raised steel lip catching a 1 px specular highlight.
   - Outer track edges blend directly into the terrain background and ground shadows with severe negative contrast (`TRACK_CONTRAST_PORT` = -26.99, `TRACK_CONTRAST_STARBOARD` = -23.78 vs. 18.0 min floor).

3. **Yellow Visor Failure & Misplaced Livery:**
   - The intended 1 px forward yellow visor line directly in front of the windshield band fails to register (`LIVERY_SAT_RENDERED` = 0.0385 vs. 0.3 min).
   - High-contrast yellow paint is clumped on the starboard forward boom shoulder/mount, reading as an asymmetrical yellow corner block rather than a defining forward-facing hazard brow.

4. **Sunken Cab & Undefined Windshield Band:**
   - The cab structure is depressed below the deck plane (`CAB_IS_RAISED` = -0.0881 vs. +0.28 min), and the windshield aperture in clay remains undersized (`CAB_PANE` = 0.0125 vs. 0.02 min).
   - The dark windshield band lacks a crisp mid-steel framing bezel, blurring directly into adjacent chassis geometry.

5. **Intake Hopper Well Lacks Coaming Lip & Ambiguous Cavity Read:**
   - The intake well aperture in clay is undersized (`WELL_HOLE` = 0.0276 vs. 0.04 min) and lacks a raised mid-steel coaming lip, causing the well and cab glass to read as similar dark hollows rather than a distinct intake bite.

6. **Boom Arm Clumping & Missing Articulated Steel Bit:**
   - The boom arm lacks sufficient lateral clearance (`BOOM_OFFSET` = 0.1466 vs. 0.15 min floor), clumping against the starboard track.
   - The boom arm lacks a readable raised steel spine and faceted specular bit tip, resolving as a yellow-shouldered hook rather than an articulated finger with a bright steel mining bit.

5. **Offset Boom Arm & Accentuate Machined Steel Spine / Bit Tip:**
