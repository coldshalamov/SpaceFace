### (1) Obvious Defects (Works-Edge View)

- **Side Walls:**
  - Hopper side walls are flat, planar triangular card/clay wedges sloping into the cavity without structural plate thickness, rolled flanges, reinforcing gussets, or welded structural seams.
- **Track Height & Assembly (Solid Rubber Belts vs. Wire Loops):**
  - Continuous rubber tread belts are correctly modeled with solid vertical height and transverse traction cleats (not wire loops).
  - Inboard track channels between tread sponsons and the main hull are bridged by unshaded, flat light-gray card/filler plates rather than modeled drive sprockets, idler wheels, track tensioners, and chassis bogie mountings.
- **Dark Cab House & Yellow Visor:**
  - Cab house remains an extruded, rectilinear primitive dark box with sharp, unchamfered edges.
  - A flat circular toy-like disc/button is glued directly onto the center of the cab roof.
  - Safety yellow is correctly restricted to a thin forward-projecting protective visor shelf extending past the glass (not yellow cab sides, not a plastic yellow square on the roof), but the dark superstructure beneath it lacks faceted armored plating, corner posts, and panel seams.
- **Dark Glass Pane:**
  - Front windshield is clearly visible as a dark rectangular tinted pane recessed beneath the yellow visor lip.
  - Lacks an authored structural retaining bezel frame, rubber perimeter gasket, and dielectric Fresnel specular reflectivity.
- **Hopper Well:**
  - Central hopper cavity is a basic stepped interior floor flanked by featureless sloping planar side walls.
  - Lacks interior AR wear liners with fastener patterns, corner gussets, vibratory floor ribs, and heavy rim coaming around the top opening.
- **Darker Boom, Raised Steel Spine, & Steel Bit Body:**
  - Starboard forward boom arm is a dark, extruded polygonal slab lacking structural pivot brackets, hydraulic actuation cylinders, and high-pressure hose lines.
  - Central raised steel spine running down the upper face of the boom is a thin, flat strip rather than a heavy machined feed-rail.
  - Rotary cutter bit at the forward tip is a basic tapered conical body lacking distinct machined cutting flutes, carbide insert teeth, and a defined mounting collar.
- **Scar Plate:**
  - Aft push/scar plate is a featureless, flat, unshaded light-gray rectangular card pasted directly onto the rear transverse bumper beam.
  - Lacks steel plate gauge thickness, perimeter bevels, modeled hex bolt fasteners, and recovery hitch/towing lugs.
- **Toy / Clay / Card / Box / Gray Plate Artifacts:**
  - **Rear Scar Gray Plate:** Flat, unshaded light-gray rectangular card pasted onto the rear crossmember.
  - **Inboard Track Filler Gray Plates:** Flat light-gray placeholder card strips bridging the hull-to-track gap on both flanks.
  - **Hopper Flank Sloping Wedges:** Flat planar triangular clay/card ramps forming the hopper side walls.

- **Glazing & Specular Response (MTX-07/36):**

  - Cutter bit lacks thermal heat discoloration (straw/purple/blue tempering gradients and friction soot) from rock contact.

- **Forbidden Reads (MTX-46):**
  - Directly triggers the forbidden read due to pasted flat gray cards, planar clay wedges, a toy roof disc, and a blocky primitive box chassis.

### (3) Concrete Blender Actions (No Cabin Furniture)

1. **Reconstruct Hopper Side Walls & Coaming:**
   - Delete the flat planar triangular card/clay wedges flanking the hopper well.
   - Model solid, heavy-gauge steel side walls with a Solidify modifier, beveled rim coaming, corner stiffener gussets, and welded structural seams.
2. **Rebuild Rear Scar Plate:**
   - Replace the flat light-gray card with a thick, beveled steel push/scar plate proud of the rear crossmember.
   - Model hex bolt patterns at the corners, perimeter chamfers, and an integrated towing hitch / recovery lug.
3. **Eliminate Gray Deck Cards & Detail Track Undercarriage:**
   - Delete the flat light-gray filler card strips between the chassis and tracks.
   - Model structural sponson frames, drive sprockets, idler rollers, track tensioners, and chassis bogie mountings to seat the solid rubber continuous tracks mechanically.
4. **Refine Cab House, Visor, & Glazing:**
   - Chamfer and facet the dark cab house into a heavy armored enclosure with defined corner pillars.
   - Remove the flat circular roof disc; replace with a recessed maintenance access hatch or an armored sensor pod housing.
   - Maintain safety yellow strictly on the forward-projecting protective visor brow shelf extending past the glass.
   - Recess the dark glass pane into a bolted steel retaining frame with a rubber gasket, assigning a reflective tinted glass PBR shader with Fresnel falloff.
5. **Deepen Hopper Cavity:**
   - Deepen the central hopper well and model interior AR-plate wear liners with visible bolt patterns and vibratory floor ribs.
6. **Detail Boom Arm, Steel Spine, & Rotary Bit:**
   - Rebuild the boom arm as a heavy structural boxed girder with reinforced pivot brackets, hydraulic cylinders, and routed hose lines.
   - Model a distinct, raised machined steel spine/feed-rail along the top surface of the boom.
   - Model a multi-faceted steel bit body with spiral cutting flutes, carbide insert teeth, and a machined mounting collar.
7. **PBR Material & Atlas Alignment:**
   - Assign distinct PBR shader channels: dark textured abraded rubber for tracks (`track`), heavy industrial dark equipment coating for hull/cab/boom (`livery`/`steel`), bare machined steel for the boom spine and bit body (`steel`/`bit`), safety yellow strictly on the visor lip (`livery`), and reflective tinted dark glass for the cab pane (`glass`).
