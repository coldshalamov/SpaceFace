## Offline verdict

The material-truth pass is a substantial construction upgrade over the primitive engine cylinders,
bright disks, floating rectangular rails, and generic gun tube. The rebuilt components use distinct
sections, cavities, attachment structure, material roles, and size hierarchy while preserving the
ship's recognizable donor silhouette and exact nine-socket contract.

Independent offline review found no remaining P0/P1 at G1/G2/G4. P2 follow-up remains: make the
surviving red cover faces more visibly latched/flanged, deepen throat soot and local ceramic chipping,
and address low construction frequency on a few broad donor-hull slabs during a later macro-hull pass.

The family is not visually accepted. Lode and Rig still require current versioned evidence; live
