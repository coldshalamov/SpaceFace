  material contrast. Ashline V2 currently reads as a coherent faction blockout by comparison.
- Maul and Hook need more authored secondary forms and controlled bright wayfinding without
  sacrificing their new source-derived silhouettes.
- The family has distance-readability evidence but no dedicated LOD-transition contact render.
