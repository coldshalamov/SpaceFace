# Pelican remaster — 3 brainstorm revisions

Identity frozen from live `hull_miner` envelope + `ship_pelican` visuals + Working Trades prospector fiction.
Not an Ironback barge. One crew, two mining mounts, hold that has to be enough.

## Revision 1 — role silhouette

- Short stocky prospector, not a capsule toy and not a barge.
- Tapered pressure vessel with chines / plate breaks (Hitch midship language).
- Two bow mining arms with rotating carbide cutter drums (not excavator buckets).
- Short dorsal survey wand. Port claim-stake cheek. Starboard filter drums.
- Twin axial ion drives. Compact dark canopy. Hand-painted return chevron.
- Heat-stained orange-brown over older grey plates, cyan scuffs.

## Revision 2 — manufactured assemblies

- Armor as brake-formed plates on stand-offs, not a painted tube.
- Recessed service hatch with rim and latches (selected midship ref).
- Filter drums as pressure cases with clamp bands and glove-worn latches.
- Drives: nickel casing + ceramic isolator segments + tapered vanes with roots (selected drive ref). Recessed throat emissive only.
- Radiator cassettes recessed in the shoulders.
- Arms: saddle mount, boxed boom with flanges, hydraulic cylinder + clevis, machined knuckle, cutter drum with teeth.
- Canopy: laminated glass in a metallic pressure frame with seals.

## Revision 3 — material truth and hierarchy

- Distinct bills: coated hull dielectric, dark armor, machined steel, ceramic, refractory vane, radiator sheet, glass, cable, warning chevron, cyan scuff.
- Causal wear: knuckle polish, belly micrometeor, filter-latch rub, heat-blue vanes. Quiet plate areas.
- Shade smooth-by-angle ~28°, not unconditional soap-smooth.
- LOD0 keeps clamps/seams/teeth/cables; LOD1 keeps load path; LOD2 keeps macro identity.
- Forbidden: purple toy bells, glowing torus, bucket arms, leather grain, DCC default, neon hoop.
