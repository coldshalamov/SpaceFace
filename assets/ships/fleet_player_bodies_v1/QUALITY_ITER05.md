# Quality iteration 5

Iter 4 stills: drives readable, colors distinct, but the hull is still one
smooth loft with a few stuck-on rectangles. Hitch midship wins because plates
overlap, rails run, clamps cross, and a repair patch breaks the paint.

## Brainstorm 1
Add a midship kit: two spine rails, three overlapping dorsal plates, cheek and
shoulder armor, a warning repair patch, an accent inset.

## Brainstorm 2
Add clamps and bolts only on LOD0 so the close camera sees fasteners, not a
texture pretending to be fasteners.

## Brainstorm 3
Keep quiet plate. Do not tile the whole hull. Concentrate construction on the
dorsal and cheeks the three-quarter camera actually sees.
