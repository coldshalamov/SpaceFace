# Quality iteration 8

Hornet iter07 stills: cutter sat *inside* the hull (never broke the skin),
then the liner filled the internal void. Reads as a dark plate, not a bay.
Canopy is a solid transmissive wedge — hull texture shows through as leather.
Wings still read as cards. Hitch / A-list still win on every P0.

## Brainstorm 1
Break the skin. Cutter must straddle the surface: 16 cm outside, real depth
inside. Line the hole with five thin walls and leave the mouth empty. Put
racks, crates, and a radiator *in* the well.

## Brainstorm 2
Canopy cannot be a solid glass brick. Cut a cockpit tub into the hull, put
seat and console in it, then glaze with thin dark panes and metal frames.
No transmission-through-a-volume (that is the leather-teal read).

## Brainstorm 3
Wings need a leading-edge pipe, a visible flap gap, and an underside pylon.
Forward chines only — they were covering the side bays. Midship plates step
higher so they read as stacked sheet, not decals.
