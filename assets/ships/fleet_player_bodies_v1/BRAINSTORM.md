# Remaining player ships — 3 brainstorm revisions each

Hitch left untouched. Pelican already live. Mule has its own package. This packet covers the rest.

## Hornet
1. Swept interceptor, canards, dorsal turret, twin front guns, single hot drive.
2. Dark paneled armor, cyan rails, manufactured wing roots not stuck slabs.
3. LOD keeps wing negative space and turret ring.

## Ironback
1. Wide barge, four cutter arms, hopper bay, twin industrial drives.
2. Heat-stained orange-brown over dark plate; not a Pelican clone.
3. Arms are saddles/booms/cutters; hoppers have rims.

## Drifter
1. Mid utility with nacelles and winglets, front+rear guns.
2. Muted teal-grey plates, recessed service.
3. Nacelles are lofted housings, not cylinders glued on.

## Atlas
1. Longer Mule: three pod rows, four engines, high bridge.
2. Distinct from Mule by bulk and engine count.
3. Pods have locks and chevrons.

## Ranger
1. Slim explorer, tall survey mast, twin nacelles.
2. Sand-grey plates, teal marks.
3. Mast is a real instrument, not a glowing hoop.

## Bastion / Warden / Colossus / Leviathan
1. Warship family: tower, broadsides, growing length and engine count.
2. Navy-grey plates, restrained warning orange.
3. Each keeps its own envelope so they do not share one silhouette.
