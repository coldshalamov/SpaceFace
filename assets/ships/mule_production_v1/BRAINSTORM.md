# Mule remaster — 3 brainstorm revisions

Identity frozen from live `hull_freighter` envelope + `ship_mule` visuals.
A T1 hauler, not the Atlas bulk barge.

## Revision 1
- Long box-section cargo hull, two stacked container rows, forward raised bridge, twin industrial engines, rear-facing gun blister.
- Dirty grey-green plates, yellow handling chevrons, rust at locks.

## Revision 2
- Containers as pressure boxes with twistlocks and rims, not painted cubes.
- Bridge as a framed glass cab on a pedestal, not a slab.
- Drives: steel casings, ceramic collars, throat vanes.
- Walkway and locking frame between hull and containers.

## Revision 3
- Distinct bills: coated hull dielectric, container steel, yellow chevron paint, glass, ceramic, radiator sheet.
- Quiet plate areas on the hull side; detail at locks, bridge, and drives.
- LOD0 keeps locks/vanes/frames; LOD1 keeps containers/bridge/drives; LOD2 keeps the barge silhouette.
