## Product outcome and bounded identity

The accepted PQ-019 route already uses two physical receiver facilities. The lawful catcher is the
visible, regulated end of the capture route: a capsule enters a broad mouth, is damped and clamped,
and is held for inspection. The fence is the covert end: a capsule is screened behind an offset
baffle, transferred through a recessed handoff bay, and hidden in a shielded storage cassette.

The current assets fail those reads. Both are variants of the same torus, faceted-rock, dark slab,
blank cuboid, and cylinder vocabulary. The catcher has no credible capture corridor, jaw, damper,
or impound path. The fence adds drums and hoops but no protected handoff, shutter, or custody path.
Near-black material allocation erases their construction, and their warm/cyan accents function as
graphics rather than material truth. The repair is a bounded whole-asset reauthor under frozen
runtime and interface contracts; it is not a new place, facility behavior, mission, or route.

The two assets keep their broader dual-use identities. `place_claim_outpost_base` must remain a
plausible modular claim anchor when it is not serving the lawful catcher. Its capture assembly is a
field-installed impound module rooted in that base, not a renamed police-only monument.
`place_claim_outpost_refinery` must remain a plausible claim refinery and transfer site. Its covert
receiver is a shielded transfer/storage wing attached to a working industrial frame, not a separate
black-market station identity.

## Fiction-development agreement

The live route defines lawful capture, covert handoff, and physical custody. It does not define a
manufacturer, exact fabrication era, or every mechanism below. All such choices are **ART
EXTRAPOLATION** made to express existing gameplay truth. They add no faction, commodity, crime,
station, or mission canon.

### Lawful catcher / modular claim base

The base is a modular asteroid-claim anchor built around a quiet load-bearing shell. Its receiver
module opens toward `+X`, where the frozen dock approach already sits. Two separated guide rails
and an open capture mouth establish the path without color. Paired jaw carriages close across the
path but never form a perfect hoop. Replaceable contact liners sit on visibly rooted jaw frames.
Short-stroke dampers and clevis-linked actuators carry capture loads into crossmembers and then into
the claim-base shell. A stepped quarantine/impound enclosure surrounds the rear of the path while
leaving a legible inspection gap, service access, and restrained lawful status hardware.

The memorable cue is the broad open fork and paired capture jaws. The manufacturing signature is
coated folded load frame, cast/machined jaw hardware, replaceable abrasion liners, and a quieter
service shell. The service-history cue is selective liner and damper replacement rather than
universal edge wear. The assembly must read when emission is disabled and at a distance where text
and small lights disappear.

### Covert fence receiver / claim refinery

The refinery is an asymmetric transfer machine rather than the base with tanks. A staggered outer
baffle hides the direct line of sight into an offset recessed handoff bay. The bay has a real rim,
side walls, floor, shadowed return, shutter pocket, transfer rails, and capture clamps. A shielded
storage cassette sits off-axis on a rooted load frame; it can receive a held capsule without
creating a second physical-custody representation. A compact service manifold and protected
conduits explain transfer, cooling, and inspection. The rest of the refinery retains quiet process
mass and structural support so the receiver reads as one bounded industrial wing.

The memorable cue is the offset baffle-and-bay silhouette. The manufacturing signature is dark
folded shielding over warmer worn transfer hardware and distinct service alloy. The service-history
cue is a replaced shutter edge and clamp liners inside an otherwise guarded, low-visibility facility.
No neon ring, skull-sign language, faction recolor, or label may carry the covert role by itself.

## Material allocation

The five retained semantic material slots have asset-specific bills; they are not recolors of one
shared noise texture.

| Role | Catcher allocation | Fence allocation | Required optical response | Prohibited shortcut |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `Material_Hull` | neutral lawful coated shell and impound panels | dark guarded refinery shell and baffle plates | broad substrate-specific roughness, directional manufacture, quiet plate, sparse causal coating loss | flat near-black, clay, global grunge |
| `Material_Mechanical` | jaws, pivots, damper roots, rail hardware | clamps, shutter guides, cassette frame, manifold hardware | machined/cast alloy separation, contact polish, recess contrast, edge response under grazing | one grey metal, uniform scratches, chrome plastic |
| `Material_Accent` | replaceable capture liners and restrained lawful service plates | guarded access plates and cassette/shutter service zones | non-emissive identity through substrate and assembly, not faction tint | color-only role, painted stripes carrying readability |
| `Material_Glass` | inspection windows and hooded sensors | bay/cassette inspection optics | smoke-dark depth, gasketed recess, restrained reflection; readable fixture when emission is off | bright flat plane, transparent hole, emissive panel |
| `Material_Warm` | damped-contact/service hardware and sparse status lenses | worked transfer surfaces, insulated service interfaces, low-output markers | warm alloy/ceramic response localized to function and history | universal orange wear, neon trim, identical warm noise |

Generated maps must be deterministic, square, uniformly sized, and bound to the candidate hash.
Normal/roughness/metallic/occlusion decisions follow substrate and manufacturing direction; no block
cells, per-pixel hash noise, recolored shared atlas, or DCC default material may survive.

## LOD and deterministic evidence plan

- LOD0 retains the full load path, jaw/bay construction, service interfaces, sparse hardware, and all
  five visible material roles.
- LOD1 retains the open catcher fork/jaws/impound shell and the fence baffle/bay/cassette/manifold;
  it removes small fasteners, secondary conduits, and fine surface hardware.
- LOD2 retains the two assets' unconfusable macro silhouettes, meaningful negative space, primary
  cavity depth, and material boundaries. The approach corridor remains open at every LOD.
- Evidence is rendered only after re-opening the exact isolated candidate source. Each asset receives
  matched surface and neutral-clay front/rear three-quarter, side, top, role-close, and dock-axis
  views; hard grazing, material-ID, matched emissive-off, and named LOD1/LOD2 views are additional.
- Each asset also receives runtime-equivalent close/default/far framing derived from the accepted H1
  camera contract: a 1440×900 authority viewport, 24 WU subject radius, and applied orthographic
  zooms `72 / 132 / 264`. The evidence renderer records the equivalent 1920×1080 projected-frame
  fractions and camera transforms. These bands are source review, not a substitute for the later
  Browser/Electron route recapture.
- All review images are deterministic 1920×1080 PNGs. Camera transforms, color management, lights,
  render engine/version, candidate source hash, and script hash are recorded in the build report.
- Independent whole-asset review inspects original resolution, all supported views, dominant zones,
  material allocation, emission-off evidence, and the LOD views. It records `keep|revise|revert`
  separately for G1, G2, and G4 and binds the exact candidate GLB and evidence hashes.
- Technical validation requires frozen root/socket/collision/AABB contracts, decreasing LODs,
  deterministic source/release candidate mirrors, Foundry and Khronos zero-error reports, and a
  validation binding. Missing or inconsistent evidence fails closed.
- The path named `release_candidates/receiver_facility_material_truth_v1/` is only an isolated,
  byte-identical source mirror used to prove handoff identity. It is not compressed release output,
  release-manifest authority, runtime wiring, or release proof; those remain Phase C work.

   dampers, guide rails, and a stepped impound shell.

6. No dominant primitive reads as rock, torus, blank box, slab, cylinder, hoop, or pipe unless its
   specific manufacture and interface are visible and billed above.
7. Independent exact-hash visual review returns `KEEP` for G1, G2, and G4. A technical green receipt
