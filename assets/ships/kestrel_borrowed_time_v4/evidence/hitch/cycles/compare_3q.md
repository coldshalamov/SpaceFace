# Same-camera 3Q: live starter vs Hitch C40 vs Dart vs Hornet

All four ships from the cycle 3Q camera (48 mm, same lighting).

- Live V9 is already a bigger, busier ship than Dart or Hornet.
- Hitch C40 keeps that scale and adds wells, a lidded repair case, and a continuous spine skin.
- Hornet and Dart in this tree are simpler silhouettes. They do not look more finished at this camera; they look smaller and cleaner.
- The remaining gap is not “more parts.” It is a unified formed hull and store-shot materials. Hitch is not 50% more stunning than Hornet yet. Do not wire.
