## Ranked findings

### F1 — Durable rebind latch restart-livelock (W109 self-defect, pop-in class)
- **Evidence:** `src/render/renderer.js:26809-26831`. When `!rebindFaulted && this._openingRebindOwed === true`, every `prepareFrame` pass runs `priorRebind.return()` and mints a fresh `_rebindPresentationMeshesSteps` walk — the arm predicate is the durable latch itself, which stays true until the leg *completes* (cleared inside the generator at 26829-26830).
- **Mechanism:** The latch replaced the one-shot consume flag (pre-W109 minted only on a fresh `remapped`/`rebuilt+delta` consume). Now the mint re-fires on *every* pass while owed. A rebind that needs more than one pass's ledger (fat `_meshes` census — the exact case the comment at 26852-26854 designs park/resume for) is killed mid-walk each pass, re-mints, re-does the same ~4 ms prefix, and parks again at the same budget point. It can never complete: (a) rows past the first-pass reach never bind — they sit in `_meshes` unbound, a persistent pop-in hole; (b) every presented pass pays a fresh mint + partial re-walk (~up-to-4 ms of dead repeated work); (c) `_openingSpawnedBindPending` starves — the else-if at 26832 only runs while `!owed`, so announced spawned rows accumulate unbound for the whole latched window. Paradoxically the only escape is a fault (which parks the re-mint and lets the parked iter resume) or the once-per-epoch sync twin under `rebindFaulted`.
- **Fix sketch:** Mint only on an arm edge — gate the mint block on `!this._presentationRebindIter` (or `armEdge = publication.remapped || (publication.rebuilt && publication.appliedDelta !== 0)`), so a parked walk resumes to completion and only a fresh publication supersedes it. The latch semantics survive unchanged.
- **Effort:** S · **Magic-frame impact:** H · **Risk:** M (must keep supersede-on-new-publication intent; a resuming walk iterates the live Map so late rows still bind)

### F2 — Shared forced wallet uncovered: every once-per-epoch sync twin bypasses `FORCED_FRAME_BUDGET_MS`
- **Evidence:** `runCookSyncTwin` call sites at `renderer.js:2396-2397` (protected first-flight drain), `26802-26807` (presentation rebind), `3566-3572` (hold-exempt enqueue), `2628-2633` (resident reattach), `27003-27011` (fence pack), `3071-3072` (arrival-asteroid-reserve), `28907-28908` (census sync fallback — once/`_viewSyncSeq`), plus fixture fallback `2687`.
- **Mechanism:** W109 wired the forced wallet across the five skip-cap lanes — all verified consult-before-mint and debit-at-exit (dispose 2238-2346, mesh-release 22994-23047, depth arm 29006-29467, env rebind 30830-30870, drawnCull visualCullRadius.js:125-189). But the *rescue twins* the fault-parked lanes fire are atomic whole-work runs that neither consult nor debit `forcedFrameSpend`. Under a multi-lane fault storm (the exact frame these lanes exist for), a fence-pack twin (O(activeCount) copy), a rebind twin (O(_meshes)), a hold-exempt twin (2×O(entityList)+sorts), a reattach twin (O(_meshes)+census collect) and a census fallback (O(scene)) can all clear on the *same* presented frame — a second, unaccounted forced channel beside the wallet. The "double-spend" question in the brief is answered no for the wired lanes (JS-serial consult→run→debit is atomic), but yes for this parallel channel.
- **Fix sketch:** Consult `forcedFrameSpend()` before firing any epoch-gated twin (defer one frame — the epoch latch makes deferral free) and `noteForcedFrameSpend(elapsed)` after it runs; that turns the twin channel into wallet members with zero contract change.
- **Effort:** S-M · **Magic-frame impact:** M (rare path, but lands on the worst frames by construction) · **Risk:** L

### F3 — Hold-exempt degrade loses three pieces of collect-side work
- **Evidence:** `renderer.js:3543-3612` (degrade) vs `3660-3905` (collect commit cycle).
- **Mechanism:** (a) **Row coverage:** the degrade sweeps `state.entityList`; the commit list comes from `collectMeshPresentationEntitiesChunked` = journal entities **plus nearby ledger rows** (`presentationSources.js:787-805` — `_meshRockScratch` field rocks + `_meshFarScratch` far actors). Exempt rows living only in the ledger — the common in-hold arrivals near a field — enqueue nothing in the degrade and stay meshless until unpark. (b) **No decode kick:** the commit runs `kickDecodeRunwayAssetsSteps(owner, commitList)` (3846); degrade-enqueued rows hit `_drainMeshBuildQueue` with cold decode runways — they queue but their builds stall on cold decode inside the bounded drain, stretching exactly the in-hold pop-in latency the kick exists to kill. (c) **No repartition stamp:** the collect stamps `_holdExemptRepartition` so the drain hoists exempt ids; degrade rows join the FIFO tail unsorted and wait behind the non-exempt backlog. (The skipped warm refill is minor — the degrade doesn't consume the ledger.)
- **Fix sketch:** After the degrade enqueue completes (and inside the once-per-epoch sync twin), stamp `owner._holdExemptRepartition = true` and drive `kickDecodeRunwayAssetsSteps(owner, enqueuedRows)` on the same slice clock. For coverage, evaluate over the memoized ledger ctx (`_nearbyLedgerRowsContextSteps`) in addition to `entityList`.
- **Effort:** M · **Magic-frame impact:** M (bounded to fault-parked epochs — rare, but that is the storm window) · **Risk:** L

### F4 — Census memo: stale-sig serves pair with a live-minted epoch
- **Evidence:** `renderer.js:28804-28921` (`_shadowCensusForFrame`) + call sites pairing `lightSig = this._shadowCensusForFrame()` with `lightSigEpoch = shadowCensusEpoch()` (29070-29075, 17327, 17388, 582, 634, 29154-29155, 29287-29288; the `lightSigFor` closures inherit it).
- **Mechanism:** A mid-pass light mutation bumps `shadowCensusEpoch`; the next consult in the same pass either (a) hits `memo.lastDrainSeq === _viewSyncSeq` and serves `memo.sig` minted pre-mutation, or (b) serves the in-flight sig while a fresh pendingIter mints. Either way the caller then pairs that stale sig with a **fresh** `shadowCensusEpoch()` read — minting a `(sig_E1, epoch_E2)` tuple. `censusStale` (`shadowDepthAdmission.js:656`) compares live epoch vs the tuple's epoch — E2==E2, undetectable — so the arm stages/marks under sig_E1 while believing E2. Consequence is wasted ceremony, not wrong output: `heldSession.sig === lightSigOverride` mismatches on the next consult → duplicate staging session + private shadow render + leftover re-collect on the boundary pass. Fix: surface the sig's own epoch (serve `memo.epoch`/`pendingEpoch` alongside, or a `_shadowCensusEpochForFrame()` twin) so the tuple is self-consistent.
- **Adjacent:** `memo.lastDrainSeq` dedupes per `_viewSyncSeq` — if consult sites run while `syncEntityViews` isn't bumping the seq (loading/degraded windows), the pendingIter never drains after the first drive and every consult serves the stale sig until the next sync pass; worth a wall-time co-fallback so the mint can't starve.
- **Effort:** S · **Magic-frame impact:** M-L · **Risk:** L
