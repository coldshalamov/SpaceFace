## Ranked findings

### 1. Row-fault quarantine parks a visible mesh at its last pose for the whole sector epoch

- **Evidence:** `sfRowFaultedEpoch` is stamped with `serviceFaultEpoch` = `` `${enterSerial|'x'}|${contextRestoreSeq}` `` (renderer.js:2344-2348, stamped at 25503/25916). The hidden-loop gate (25425-25435) and visible-loop gate (25544-25549) both skip quarantined rows; nothing clears the stamp in-epoch — only an `enterSerial` flip or `contextRestoreSeq++` does.
- **Mechanism:** a mesh-transient fault — a single one-off throw inside `_applyPresentationPose`/`updateFromEntities` for one row (e.g. during a material/texture transition) — quarantines the row for the remainder of the sector. The row is served its last painted pose for the entire epoch: a still-visible ship frozen mid-motion on screen for a whole sector is exactly the persistent-presented-stand-in class this lane hunts. Same-epoch hidden→visible compounding: a row that faults in the hidden loop *before* its pose write/hide applies arrives at the visible loop already stamped — it stays `visible` **and** stale-posed for the epoch, with `world.clearDirty` never having run.
- **Fix sketch:** make the stamp structured — `{epoch, faults, retryAt}` on `userData.sfRowFaultedEpoch`. Same-epoch rows retry while `faults < ROW_FAULT_MAX` (~3) with `retryAt = now + ~1000`; hitting the cap parks for the epoch (current behavior) and escalates into the existing `entityViewRow` lane counter.
- **Effort:** S. **Magic-frame impact:** M (rare trigger, high-visibility consequence). **Risk:** low — a deterministically-faulting row retries at most `cap` times per epoch; bounded by construction.

### 2. Fallback-published rows stay unbound (invisible) for a whole epoch when the presentation-rebind lane parks

- **Evidence:** rebind mint gate parks at `_rebindFaultRetries >= 3 || now < _rebindRetryAt` (renderer.js:26787-26816). On a diff-apply fallback the publisher path runs `world.updateFromEntities` directly and `spawnedSlots` stays empty (presentationPublisher.js fallback consume; `resetResult` 93-105), so the non-fault-gated `else if (_openingSpawnedBindPending)` leg (26759-26779) covers nothing — the *only* bind path for fallback rows is the minted `_bindPublishedPresentationMeshesSteps` (26780-26784).
- **Mechanism:** `rebuilt && appliedDelta !== 0` on a fallback consume arms `_openingRebindOwed`/`_openingRebindFallback`; with the lane parked the arm never mints, and every fallback-published row carries no presentation bind for the rest of the sector epoch — invisible real entities, the worst shape of stand-in-in-frame. Every sibling lane got a once-per-epoch sync twin in W109 (protectedFirstFlight 2369-2376, holdExempt 3530-3543, keepGpu reattach 2601-2610, keepGpu reconcile 2639-2670, fence pack 26934-26953); the rebind lane did not.
- **Fix sketch:** add `_rebindSyncTwinEpoch`: when the lane is parked and `_openingRebindOwed` is still armed, run `_bindPublishedPresentationMeshes` synchronously once per epoch, honoring the existing `hasCommittedVisualBasis`/`_sfUntrustedIngestState` guards (26769-26772).
- **Effort:** S-M. **Magic-frame impact:** M-H. **Risk:** moderate — the twin pays the whole-set bind atomically in-frame, but it is bounded to once per epoch, the same trade the W109 siblings already took.

### 3. Depth-stage arm ceremony has no fault bound — a persistently-faulting root throws, requeues, and re-arms every idle slice forever

- **Evidence:** the arm consults `forcedFrameSpend() >= FORCED_FRAME_BUDGET_MS` (renderer.js:28947-28957) but carries no epoch fault counter; the arm `finally` (29362-29389) unconditionally requeues the untried slice and re-arms via `scheduleIdle`.
- **Mechanism:** every other paced lane gained `FaultRetries`+park in W109; the depth-stage arm's outer try has none. A deterministic throw inside the ceremony (a depth-stage root whose finalize/`scheduleSyncDepthStageRoot` walk throws every pass) produces an uncaught throw per ~48ms idle arm → requeue → re-arm → throw, forever: zero progress, per-frame warn/error spam, and the ceremony cost paid each frame. This is the (b)-inverse residual — not starvation, but unbounded per-epoch retry.
- **Fix sketch:** `_depthStageArmFaults`/`_depthStageArmFaultEpoch` on the owner; ≥3 faults in a `serviceFaultEpoch` skips the arm until epoch flip. Queued entries drain next epoch; meshes still render — only staged depth policy is lost.
- **Effort:** S. **Magic-frame impact:** M. **Risk:** low — parking is the established sibling pattern and the degrade is strictly better than a throw-loop.

### 4. `laneFaults` rollup is half-wired and write-only — `parkedEpoch` never stamped, only 6 of ~15 fault sites stamp, zero consumers

- **Evidence:** `noteLaneFault` writes `faults`/`lastWarnAt`/`lastError`/`parkedAtMs` (renderer.js:2099-2107) but never `parkedEpoch` despite the docstring at 2575-2579 promising it. Stamping sites: protectedFirstFlightDrain (2373), holdExemptEnqueue (3532), entityViewSync (25331), entityViewRow (25503/25916), presentedTail.* (2135), shadowPolicyRoot (30809). The private-retry lanes — arrivalReserve (~3062), residencyReconcile (~3128), residencyPoll (~3199), residentReattach (~2556), presentationRebind (~26810), depth-stage arm — count retries privately and never reach the rollup. `grep -rn laneFaults` across src/, scripts/, test/ finds no readers.
- **Mechanism (residual c):** the rollup exists to answer "which lanes faulted this epoch", but (i) nothing consumes it, (ii) it misses the retried lanes entirely, and (iii) without `parkedEpoch` even a future consumer cannot distinguish parked-this-epoch from faulted-earlier.
- **Fix sketch:** stamp `parkedEpoch: serviceFaultEpoch(owner)` when a lane enters its park; call `noteLaneFault` from the private-retry catches; add the first consumer — a once-per-epoch residency-audit log line, or a cap that hard-parks a lane after N parked epochs.
- **Effort:** S. **Magic-frame impact:** L-M (observability contract, not a direct frame cost). **Risk:** low.
