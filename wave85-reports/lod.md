  - Deferred finish writes `state.render.openingSubmissionValidation = validation` at `:24101`, gated by `!this.state.render.openingSubmissionValidation` at `:24065`.
  - Recook/mode-transition reset block `:17110-17137` clears `openingSubmissionPlan`, `openingSubmissionReceipt`, `openingFirstVisibleGpuCounts`, `openingFirstDrawIdentityCensus`, `openingSubmissionValidation`, `_openingPreSubmitRefusals`, `openingSubmissionFirstDrawSubmittedAt`, `_openingShadowAdmission`, `firstPlayable*` — but NOT `_openingFirstDrawIdentityIter`, `_openingFirstDrawIdentityResult`, `_openingFirstDrawDiagnosticsDeferred`.

- Mechanism: `captureOpeningAdmissionIdentitySteps` (`src/render/openingGpuAdmission.js:155-190`) mints its planned-Set from the *captured* plan and walks the *captured* scene. If a recook lands mid-suspension (after at least one refused frame partially drained the iter), the gate re-arms (`openingFirstVisibleGpuCounts`/`SubmittedAt` cleared) and resumes the stale iterator at `:23540` — the census completes over the new sector against the old plan's scope, then the post-submit delta writes `openingFirstDrawIdentityCensus`/counts for the wrong plan (`:23896`, `:24054`), and the deferred `finishDiagnostics` stamps a receipt validation computed against the old census into the fresh slate (`:24101`). Because `:24065`'s guard is `!openingSubmissionValidation`, the occupied slot also suppresses the fresh sector's real receipt validation. Two escape hatches soften it: a *completed* (truthy) iter short-circuits to `done` and falls into the sync `captureOpeningAdmissionIdentity` fallback (`:23885-23891`), and renderer teardown kills the deferred callback via `lifecycle.guard` — but a mid-suspension recook with an intact renderer is the live window. Impact is diagnostic-only: no presented pixel changes, but the soak/witness consumers of `openingSubmissionValidation`/`openingFirstDrawIdentityCensus` can see a mis-attributed verdict on the second sector.
- Fix sketch: add `this._openingFirstDrawIdentityIter = null; this._openingFirstDrawIdentityResult = undefined;` to the reset block, and capture the plan object identity at arm time so `_finishOpeningFirstDrawDiagnostics` can bail (`if (this.state.render.openingSubmissionPlan !== armedPlan) return;`) when the slate it was armed under is gone. The deferred flag already self-clears in `finally` at `:24142`; the bail keeps the stale verdict out of the new slot and lets the fresh arm mint its own iter.
- Effort: S. Magic-frame impact: L for pixels, M for probe/diagnostic trust. Risk: low — the fields are renderer-private, read only in the blocks cited.

### 2. `bindAuthoredMotion` pays `O(bindings × subtree)` inside attachPackagedBody's un-yielded leg one

- Evidence: `src/render/visualFactory.js:4138-4148` — `bindInstanceMotion(packageRoot, record.motionBank)` runs before the first `await yieldToBrowser()` at `:4154`. `bindInstanceMotion` → `bindAuthoredMotion` (`src/render/authoredMotion.js:1013-1016` → `src/contracts/motionBank.js:606`), which calls `collectNodesByName(root, binding.node)` **per binding** (`motionBank.js:609-611`); `collectNodesByName` (`:582-592`) is a full explicit-stack DFS of the subtree.
- Mechanism: banked packages (mining drone `place_mining_drone.glb`, wasp frags, dead-hulk victims whose hulk carries the victim's own bank) pay one whole-subtree traversal per motion binding inside the first presented-frame leg of the packaged commit. Measured bank sizes: `mining-drone.motion.json` = 2 bindings, `wasp-frag-*.json` = 2, `mining.motion.json` = 3, max across all 69 motion files = 18 (`kestrel.motion.json`), mean ≈ 2.6. Worst case is therefore ~18 subtree walks of a ship-hull-sized packaged subtree (the largest packaged bodies are wreck/aft sections and hulks of full authored ships) in one un-yielded span.
- Fix sketch: hoist the collect — one DFS building `Map<name, node[]>`, then `map.get(binding.node)` per binding: `O(N + B)` instead of `O(B·N)`, identical semantics (same-named pivots all match, per the `authoredMotion.js:11` comment). Alternatively yield between bindings — but the map version removes the quadratic term outright and needs no pacing machinery.
- Effort: S. Magic-frame impact: M (bounded, on the drone/fracture/hulk mount path; small for B=2 banks, real for hulk-of-kestrel cases). Risk: low — same matching order semantics preserved if the map preserves DFS order per name.

### 3. Atomic mount tail still pays two subtree traverses on the graft seam; `canonicalizeObjectSurfaceProgramKeys` is relocatable pre-graft

- Evidence: `src/render/visualFactory.js:4259-4276` — after the last stale/orphan guard, the atomic tail runs `hideProceduralChildren(root)` (top-level children only — trivial), `root.add(packaged)`, `carryAdmittedOnceStamp`, `canonicalizeObjectSurfaceProgramKeys(packaged)` (`:4264` — full `packaged.traverse` over materials, `src/render/illustratedSurface.js:180-196`), then `packagedShadowSync` → `state.render.syncPackagedBodyShadowPolicy` (`src/render/renderer.js:11046-11071`), which calls `invalidateShadowCasterPolicy(root)` + `_syncShadowCasterPolicyChecked(root, …, {preCountRoot: addedSubtree})` — a first-touch checked sync that walks the **whole root subtree** (procedural children + fresh packaged), `src/render/renderer.js:24283+`.
- Mechanism: the checked shadow sync is genuinely pinned to the seam — it must see the grafted topology or the newly visible meshes draw `castShadow` minted-but-unstaged (an unlinked depth variant on the first presented shadow refresh, exactly the cold-link it prevents). The canonicalize pass, by contrast, only mutates `packaged` subtree materials (`canonicalizeInstalledSurfaceProgramKey` per material) and reads nothing about parentage — running it in leg C/D (before `root.add`) is behavior-identical and lifts one `O(N_packaged)` traverse plus per-material key work off the atomic seam. Worst case remains the hulk path (full authored ship subtree) where root+packaged is the largest mount in the system.
- Fix sketch: move `canonicalizeObjectSurfaceProgramKeys(packaged)` to run immediately after `freezeStaticChildMatrices(packaged)` (leg D, `:4203`) — still pre-graft, pre-compile, identical program-key canonicalization. The checked-sync traverse is irreducible; no claim otherwise.
- Effort: S. Magic-frame impact: M (one packaged-subtree traverse removed from the only remaining atomic presented-frame seam in this lane). Risk: low — verify no packaged material is mutated between leg D and graft (none found: `fitPackagedGroup` writes transforms, not materials).

### 4. Remaining paced legs each still carry an un-stepped whole-subtree walk (worst: `fitPackagedGroup`)

- Evidence and worst-case ranking of traverses inside the commit continuation (`attachPackagedBody`, `visualFactory.js:4116-4276`):
  1. `fitPackagedGroup` (leg C, `:4197` → `:3835-3849`): `group.updateMatrixWorld(true)` forced compose + `new THREE.Box3().setFromObject(group)` — two traverses of `packaged`. Bounding boxes are cached from decode (`src/render/GLTFLoader.js:240` computes `boundingBox` at load), so the walk itself is `O(N)` twice — the largest single-leg cost at hulk scale.
  2. `deadenPackagedHulk` (leg B, deadHulk path only, `:4181` → `:3924-3991`): traverse + `Material.clone()` per unique material (clones map `:3929`); clone is a full material copy — `O(N)` nodes + `O(U)` clone cost.
  3. `freezeStaticChildMatrices` (leg D, `:4203` → `src/render/staticChildMatrices.js:57-69`): `root.traverse` + recursive post-order `markStaticMatrixSubtrees` — `O(N)` twice.
  4. Mount-tail `canonicalize` + checked sync — covered in finding 3.
  5. `createInstance` (`src/render/renderPackageLoader.js:766-905`) is a flat `O(planEntries)` `clone(false)` loop, no recursion/decode/GPU — bounded, no finding.
  6. `instantiatePackagedPrimitives` (`:3800-3833`) and `hideProceduralChildren` (`:3851-3860`) are flat `O(primitives)` / `O(top-level children)` — bounded, no finding.
- Mechanism: each leg lands inside one presented frame's JS turn; none of 1–3 has a stepped form yet (the wave paced *between* legs, not *inside* them). Packaged subtree sizes are wreck-section/ship-hull scale — the absolute worst mount (dead hulk of a multi-LOD ship) stacks leg-B clones + leg-C double-walk + tail sync in consecutive frames, each individually bounded but none internally paced.
- Fix sketch: `fitPackagedGroup` could reuse package-authored bounds if the manifest carries them (skip both walks) or ride `updateMatrixWorldSteps` for the compose; `deadenPackagedHulk`/`freezeStaticChildMatrices` admit chunked iterator twins (collect node list once, process per-slice). All are contract-preserving — same values, same end state.
- Effort: M each. Magic-frame impact: M on the hulk path, L on the common drone/wreck-section paths. Risk: medium-low — the compose step must keep its forced semantics (frozen-subtree skipping under `force` is already handled by the vendored override, `staticChildMatrices.js:8-13`).

### 5. Orphan-after-`fractureRuptureFired` consumes the re-fire flag — a remounted fracture piece loses its rupture kick (W84-widened edge)

- Evidence: `src/render/visualFactory.js:4172-4179` sets `entity.data.fractureRuptureFired = true` and fires `controller.handleEvent('wreck:rupture', …)` on freshly bound controllers — *before* the orphan recheck at `:4192-4195`. If the commit orphans at `:4192`, `disposeDetachedPackagedGroup(packaged)` runs and the fired controllers die with the subtree; on a later re-admission of the same entity, fresh controllers bind but the `fractureRuptureFired !== true` guard skips the re-fire.
- Mechanism: pre-W84 the fire and the graft were atomic — the flag could not be consumed without the mount landing. The new yield between them opens an orphan window where the flag is spent but the fragment never mounted. Consequence is narrow (fragment remounts without the flap/mast kick animation; no geometry/LOD wrongness), requires entity detach mid-admission followed by re-admission of the same entity.
- Fix sketch: clear `entity.data.fractureRuptureFired` on the orphan-dispose paths that follow the fire (`:4193-4195`, `:4200-4201`, `:4206-4207`), or stamp the firing admission epoch beside the flag and re-fire when a newer epoch binds.
- Effort: S. Magic-frame impact: L. Risk: low — flag is entity-data bookkeeping.
