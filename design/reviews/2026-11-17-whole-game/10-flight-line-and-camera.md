# Flight, the Massline and the camera

A good gun is easy to replace. A ship that can swing a pursuer into a rock, use the same rope to steal an inside line in a race, then bring a damaged tender home gives SpaceFace an identity a player can demonstrate to a friend. I would make the first successful *intentional* throw the product's first milestone. The repeat is more revealing than the tutorial success. Could the pilot improvise it against a moving threat after the prompt disappeared?

## Three things to build, in order

**1. Show what the hands are about to do.** Extend the existing opening and target preview. Put one legal piece of ammunition in negative space, on the pilot's near side, with its silhouette clear of the first threat. On hover or stick selection, draw the marker on the exact object that would latch. A short pull makes its mass and direction legible. On release, let the thrown body travel on screen long enough for its collision and the target's change in motion to read. When a release fails, give the reason at the moment it matters and withdraw the prompt once the player has demonstrated the action. Preserve the option to win by simply flying and firing. A rock lesson which blocks a capable pilot's escape will soon feel like an exam.

Work through the existing input, aiming, Massline and opening owners. First record unassisted baseline attempts in ordinary Adventure and Swarm. Capture the button timestamp, selected body and generation, latch result, release result, contact, and displayed target, plus what the tester thought they aimed at. Keep gameplay owner events authoritative and output diagnostic samples only while the session is recorded. Check a close overlap, a turning target, neutral/sticky stick, a remap, a menu return and a frame hitch. If a cue predicts a target different from the owner, fix the first point at which they diverge. [Input](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/systems/input.js) · [Target scoring](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/combat/masslineTargetScoring.js) · [Release ownership](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/systems/masslineThrow.js#L235-L274)

**Acceptance proposal.** Reuse the project's staged targets of at least 95% successful presses given an eligible target. Report wrong-body hits separately. In a formative round with five first-time players using their preferred supported controls, aim for four to make one intentional throw and then a second unprompted throw at a *different* target. Five people can identify repeated misunderstandings. Publish individual trials, including abandonments. Time first actionable frame separately from time from the title so a loading regression cannot masquerade as a physics failure. [Project targets](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/design/program/fleet/FINISH-LINE.md#L7-L16)

**2. Resolve the “world hitches while the HUD keeps spinning” report through displayed evidence.** Record thrust alone, thrust + turning, thrust + boost, then holding a taut line during a boosted turn on the same flight path. Compare simulation tick advancement, authoritative ship position, interpolated displayed ship position, camera target, a background landmark's screen position, and event-loop/frame intervals on one timeline. A correct simulation with a stuck camera and a stalled simulation behind a live HUD require different fixes. Repeat at default and wide zoom and at both ends of a tether. Save the pre/post motion clips with device, resolution, browser or Electron version, and quality preset. Keep the same geometry, silhouettes and raster resolution for the comparison. Existing combined-input observations reported better worst gap alongside worse p95. Both measures deserve space in the decision. [Open Gauntlet evidence](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/design/program/fleet/QUEUE.md#L53-L58) · [Displayed pose selection](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/render/camera.js#L100-L140)

**Acceptance proposal.** Before changing timing constants, name which of those six signals first stopped advancing. Capture the same input tape after the fix. Accept when the symptomatic discontinuity is absent across three repeated passes on the agreed target machine, ordinary coast and free camera still feel stable, and p95/p99 frame times, longest world-presentation gap and simulation/wall-clock ratio are reported together. Confirm with the original pilot on their machine when available. A benchmark from another machine cannot close their symptom.

**3. Give handling a reason to matter outside combat.** Recut one existing haul or recovery so the last berth has a long safe approach and a short inside swing beside the dock's illuminated face. A clean threaded entry brings the cargo in quickly and intact. Overshooting leaves room to recover by paying out the line and circling. Let the clasp and dock cradle show the handover. The player who learned the swing in the Pit should recognize a new use for it in a quieter scene. Keep one existing mission, save/reload path and payout. This is an authored variation of existing verbs, sized for a mission owner after the opening tests pass. [Flight design](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/design/game/10_THE_GAME.md#L35-L45) · [Shared throw](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/systems/masslineThrow.js) · [Cargo domain](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/tree/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/physicalCargo)

**Acceptance proposal.** Record one cautious heavy-hull delivery and one quicker light-hull delivery on the ordinary route. Show the expected recovery after an overshoot and a Continue while towing. A new player should be able to identify which maneuver saved time, which object they controlled and what arrived. If the mission has to move the ship or cargo into place to award completion, reopen the geometry or handover condition.

## Races as an independent check on game feel

The circuit board resolves **nine circuits**. Textile Belt, Tally Circuit and Veil Wash sit alongside six temporary time-trial circuits. The board contains fits, opponents, practice/story entries, medals, a cup, personal ghosts, and previews of hazards including a cargo crossing or customs shutter. Board contracts can also derive a simpler six-gate crossing or ring from sector geometry. Keep these two content types distinct when counting tracks or describing the feature to a player. [Circuit catalog](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/data/raceLevels.js#L308-L451) · [Board](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/ui/screens/raceBoard.js#L242-L285) · [Derived courses](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/data/raceCourses.js#L18-L35)

A fresh run of the native rival fixture contradicted an older open concern in the queue. At the pinned revision all three original Hard-course cases exceeded their **0.8 s mean paired advantage** from using anchors. Results were 1.206 s at Helios, 1.542 s at Tethys, and 1.513 s at Veil. One Veil pair lost 1.911 s with anchors. These are simulated rivals. They demonstrate that an anchor can buy time and that a particular line can cost time. A human still has to see the anchor early enough to make the choice. [Race experiment](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/test/al-race-1-contested-line-4242.test.mjs#L137-L226) · [Earlier queue concern](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/design/program/fleet/QUEUE.md#L99-L106)

**Race owner, after the flight pass.** Polish Textile Belt first. Stage a visible opponent taking its anchor and an outside route that stays open. Compare camera approach, time until the exit gate becomes visible, voluntary second lap, and the player's explanation of what they would change. Use the existing ghost to show the chosen lines diverging. Verify that a player can recover from a missed anchor and finish, that practice respects the selected camera, and that leaving a view-locked race returns the prior preference. Expand other circuits only after the opener makes a deliberate second lap attractive.
