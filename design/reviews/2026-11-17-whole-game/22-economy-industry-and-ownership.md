# Trade, mining, fabrication and owning a piece of the world

The most promising commercial economy in SpaceFace is a price the player can remember changing. A cargo goes missing, a worker closes a shutter, and the price of oxygen at that berth makes sense. A pilot who can anticipate the next shortage has acquired knowledge of the world. A list of 66 commodities gives that knowledge room to matter, but it cannot teach it. The reviewed build also defines 20 station blueprints. Site manufacturing is a separate recipe catalogue. Neither count measures the number of worthwhile player decisions. ## Two concrete review findings

**There is a real test contract to fix before retuning industry.** In a five-file run covering market presentation, site production and planning, returning after a law incident, and crew, 85 of 86 checks passed. The failing test is named “every commodity manufacturing stage adds at least twenty percent gross value.” It filters equipment pods, then treats the bore-charge press output as a commodity. In a direct calculation at the pinned commit, the two inputs had a combined nominal value of 188 credits. The output `charge_puck` was unknown to the commodity valuation. The assertion that failed was the preceding completeness check. The actual production owner deposits completed pucks on the site's ordnance rack. Assign the test author and the industry owner together. Classify commodity outputs and functional equipment explicitly using existing recipe semantics. Prove both the tradable margin rule and one press-to-use cycle with a full store. Selling an ordnance puck for an invented price would change the economy to satisfy the wrong test. [Failing expectation](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/test/site-industry-planner.test.mjs#L154) · [Bore-charge definition](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/data/sites.js#L209) · [Production owner](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/systems/asteroidSites.js#L2399)

**The measurements already distinguish cash flow from profit.** The economy pulse stores 48 fifteen-minute bins of real transaction and work counters. Quiet intervals are zero. Drone cycle records list fuel used separately and their credit-net field subtracts upkeep. When comparing jobs, normalize fuel consumed and replacement expenses using a stated price rule. Count the complete journey from acceptance through unload and return to a position from which another job can be taken. A screenshot of a large sale says nothing about the opportunity cost of the empty return leg. The design document's wage imbalance is a prior design observation. Rerun contemporary trials before treating the cited ratios as current balance. [Pulse semantics](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/economy/economyPulse.js#L1) · [Operating costs](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/systems/automationOperations.js#L158) · [Design target](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/design/game/17_THE_LADDER_AND_THE_LONG_GAME.md#L24)

## A vertical slice for trade and production

Use the already commissioned *Wrong Address* at Tethys. A cold crate for Nurse Ilse Park is moving through the wrong siding. Its blue band and the clinic's mark identify it on approach. A pattern and a label duplicate the color. A careful pilot can switch the whole consist, costing another working ship its slot. A skilled pilot can pull the crate alone through the crossing. The failure case leaves freight delayed, a damage report on file or a consignment visibly missing. No mission title should promise “clinic supplied” unless the right receiver accepted the right crate. The present clinic readout validates specific Nyx-origin routes and receipts. Reuse its provenance discipline. The proposed Tethys job needs its own receiver and route identity. [Commissioned street scene](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/design/game/13_JOBS_AND_HEISTS.md#L42) · [Existing clinic receipt rules](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/data/clinicFreightReadout.js#L13)

The reward is a second decision. A successful delivery leaves the clinic supplied, and the station's **actual** order book determines whether another run is worthwhile. At a delayed clinic, let the pilot see an occupied berth and choose a longer receiving route next time. Show a named market observation only if the simulation registered one. A small trader should see modest movement in a large market. Let a convoy or a real shortage make the dramatic case. Keep the everyday response truthful even when modest volume makes it visually unspectacular.

Use the Ceres claim for the next step in ownership. Let the first shift end with a visible pile of material that the pilot cannot yet move efficiently. Sell the first lot personally. Then supply a core and extractor, hand the repetitive step to the machine and let the pilot choose what to do with the freed time. On the next visit the first mechanical problem should have a comprehensible physical cause such as an unsuitable rock face, full store, missing input or missed pickup. An audible idle machine beside a full bin teaches more than an “efficiency 72%” tooltip. Place the input, output and intervention where the player already flies. Preserve the owner's decision to leave enemy base construction out of combat.

Keep this milestone on the existing commodity list. Let a new profession, building or resource earn its cost by changing where someone goes, what they fly, what they risk, or whom they choose to help. Those decisions are observable in a playtest and are harder to fake with content volume.
