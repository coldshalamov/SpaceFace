## What follows the first batch

| Milestone | Work the agents can take | Evidence that earns expansion |
|---|---|---|
| **A demo worth handing to a stranger** | Integrate the five packets. Build/release agent identifies the exact playable artifact, confirms install/first picture, Save/Continue and the public entry. Triage current CI by causal owner. Fix old campaign and boss fixtures separately from genuine runtime defects. The lead watches normal direct entry into both modes. | From a clean profile a player performs an intentional throw, understands a death, successfully retries, makes an informed purchase and can close/reopen at a recognizable berth. Run the existing owner's ten-minute card. Write down the first failure even when the rest of a fixture passes. |
| **An evening with an arc** | Validate each ten-round stretch to a thirty-round win, extraction and optional endless continuation. On Adventure, prove the ordinary route through one moral choice and one revisited town. Then extend Ceres's industrial chain and Tethys's accountable shipment on separate owners. | A win survives Continue. The credit bank and stats settle once. At least two viable builds produce visibly different solutions to a shared encounter. In Adventure, two players can describe different choices at the same job and find the next objective after returning. |
| **Progress that survives a week** | Work the hangar/competitive parity test, full campaign endings, one ownership dispute and a changing price or service. Make a late-game scenario fail gracefully. Add an equitable optional seed-and-ghost comparison once clean and veteran profiles produce comparable starts. | A returning player benefits from prior work without losing a route after a patch. Capture one adverse outcome and its recovery as carefully as a success. Exercise a genuinely old save on the packaged update path. Keep failure evidence and a reproducible fallback. |
| **A commercial release candidate** | Package only the supported channels with precise device/language claims. Capture trailer and store stills from this candidate. Run blind comprehension and a small external playtest, then replenish the portions people choose again. | Recorded build fingerprint matches publicly reachable bytes. Latest review notes identify known defects honestly. Actual traffic/return and feedback have defined denominators. Quality on an advertised device, access mode or language is verified at that scope. |

**Capacity decision.** Provisionally assign two implementation owners to first contact and runtime trust, one to the earned Adventure return, and one to art/readability and adversarial play across the same routes. Rebalance after the first evidence review. Defer new factions, generic mission pools, another currency, networked competitive services and asset-wide makeovers. Preserve the racing, gallery, side-career and creator systems. Promote one only when it gives the player a better reason to use a skill already learned.

**Stop or reverse a change when** it makes a benchmark faster by degrading silhouette resolution across zoom. It rewards waiting or immediate failure more than a competent attempt. It breaks an existing save's earned property. Or its only proof is a scripted teleport past the part a human could not find. These are product decision rules. A credible exception should name the player benefit, the affected population, and how the lead will tell whether it delivered.
