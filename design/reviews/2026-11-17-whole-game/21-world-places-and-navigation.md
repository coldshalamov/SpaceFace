# Places, navigation and a world worth revisiting

The galaxy has 25 sector definitions and 25 sector “way of life” records. The latter describe local work, rhythm, law, crime, ships, hazards and a signature physical toy. A separate additive registry contributes 40 zone records. The count establishes authored data for those places. Their recognizable identities still need visitor evidence. The project already makes the right scope decision in its plan for five towns, six outposts and fourteen small waystations. I would judge a town by whether a returning pilot flies differently there and recognizes someone doing a familiar job. A waystation can earn its entire existence with one excellent landing. For the six outposts, use the commissioned silhouettes and one interaction each before expanding a second quest. Use Dione’s firing line for cadence and the wrecks at Pallas for rebounding. Io changes who controls a delivery. Charon teaches navigation through living growth. Veil’s sweep opens a passage through fog. At Ashfall the pilot works against the mass of the surrounding hull. Give the fourteen frontier stops one reason to slow down, one face, and one thing a local would do better than a visitor. Run a blind image-and-input test against two neighboring stops. If everyone identifies them by UI labels alone, spend the next asset budget on silhouette, approach geometry or traffic motion. ## A working example and its remaining proof

Lantern Ward's actual commit requires the original eligible lamp, the right receiver, an active mount, at least 18 seconds of work, a seated position within two world units, speed at most 0.8, and at least 65% hull. Work distinguishes a first installation from a repair or reinstall. Completing the service records whether the player helped. The registered mission owner validates the receipt and caps the grant at 180 credits. Residents can remember theft or neglect while reporting a different current fault. These details let two pilots tell different true stories about one lamp. [Physical commit](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/systems/lanternWardWork.js#L521) · [Settlement](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/missions/lanternWardStory.js#L16) · [Local memory](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/missions/lanternWardStory.js#L125)

In this review the production Node scenario passed. It starts a production-profile new game, issues a course request and drives bounded physics impulses before checking save/restore and exactly-once reward. It provides a better engineering reference than a mocked objective counter. The script knows the approach coordinate. A player must discover it. Ambient conversation waits for 40 seconds of observation inside the local envelope, although hailing is available earlier. If the resident and object do not read from the approach, a hurried player can miss this work. Give the scene one public invitation. Use a blind run to decide whether it needs stronger lighting, a different departure bearing or an earlier hail. ## Chart, exploration and encounter direction

The route planner searches known sectors and accounts for drive capability, wormholes, fuel and interdiction. The map preview can still display useful hop information when an authoritative cost is unavailable. The exploration journal deliberately excludes repeatable signals and infrastructure from its count of durable discoveries, with explicit exceptions for curated sites. Preserve that distinction in the presentation. One screen should answer “how do I get my load home?” before it asks whether the player wants to fill a map. [Planner](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/systems/routePlanner.js#L142) · [Preview](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/ui/map/routePreview.js#L58) · [Discovery rules](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/world/explorationJournal.js#L32)

Three concrete edits would improve journeys. First, pin the station **approach or receiver** as well as the sector when a job specifies one. A chart destination that gets the player into the correct sky still leaves them lost at the wrong end of the docks. Second, when a route is unavailable, reveal only information the pilot could have observed, such as the last bearing, a known gate, the remaining fuel or a previously encountered restriction. Third, give the first return trip a tiny piece of dramatic advantage. A player who noticed the commuter swing on the outbound leg should be able to use its gap on the way home. Existing route, exploration and survey-fix owners already distinguish physical location, discoveries and sold bearings. [Place coordinate contract](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/data/PLACE_REGISTRATION.md#L27) · [Survey fixes](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/systems/surveyFix.js#L112)

Crowded space needs scheduling. The lived-world director already selects and places vignettes. Judge an encounter by the time it occupies the player's attention and the work it interrupts. Ambient events can continue in the background. Optional speech can wait while the pilot seats a fragile load. When the player looks up, the same commuter or repair crew should still be doing something comprehensible. [Encounter selection and placement](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/systems/livedWorldDirector.js#L51) · [Bounded world catch-up](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/world/worldCatchup.js)

## The politics and ecology should be readable from the cockpit

A faction can gain a contested sector through war momentum. Station growth and player-supplied depots can contribute to faction power. The law system hears damage, collisions, theft, surrender and impound events. Crew have dockside decisions, loyalty progression and casualties. Shipjacking supports safe passage for crew, a distinction between surrender and title, and recovery beacons. These owners give Sker and Io real material. Stage one pilot's prior action in one of these systems as the recognizable *cause* of a changed checkpoint. Never demand that the player read four meters to understand why a familiar patrol has changed sides. [Faction flip](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/systems/factions.js#L895) · [Law events](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/systems/lawSecurity.js#L405) · [Crew](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/systems/crew.js#L71) · [Vessel custody](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/systems/shipjack.js#L190)

Planetary work and alien ecology also have executable foundations. Planet sites bind physical fields and skimming behavior, and ecology already distinguishes a bloom from the durable fact that a relay was severed. The fleet explicitly still needs a packet for story-led infestation on eligible structures. Commission that packet on one familiar freight berth. First visit, a worker moves a cold load past a clear joint. Second visit, an attached growth constricts the same route. Let a pilot cut the affected connection or take the longer clean route. Return after Continue. An observer should be able to identify the old obstruction and what action actually changed it. Keep intact Understory gardens visually intentional. Publish severity from the owning simulation, keep attached decor bounded, and verify cold loading and distant detail. | W4 · World change | FUNGUS-01 and the existing war/custody owners. Pick one ecology change and one governance change on separate stops after W2. | Revisit after sector exit, time advance and Continue. A short clip shows the original cause and a practical second-visit decision. Spreading decor obeys the frame budget. Simulating distant activity introduces no duplicate body when returning. |

Stop promoting new sectors as major features until players can name the five towns by what they do there. That frees regional authors to make one small place strange without promising another town's worth of production.
