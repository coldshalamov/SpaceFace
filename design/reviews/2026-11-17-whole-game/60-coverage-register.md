## Systems whose most interesting test crosses a boundary

**Steal a ship and live with it.** The BLACK FLAG coordinator delegates control, combat, cargo, law and settlement to their owners. Law has a jurisdiction model, a distinction between threatening cargo and neutral traffic, and patrol responses to actual damage. Test acquisition of a vessel, inspection of what is still aboard, use or sale of a traceable component, a dock in a different jurisdiction, save and return as one public route. The valuable outcome is a player explaining why this particular hull has become expensive to keep. Give a witness, document or physical marking enough specificity to make that explanation possible. Compare the same flight with an empty, legally owned vessel. Penalizing both identically would erase the choice. [Shipjack coordinator](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/systems/shipjack.js#L1-L32) · [Law authority](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/systems/lawSecurity.js#L1-L20) · [Component provenance](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/shipjack/componentProvenance.js)

**Give a rival one learnable habit.** The Nemesis implementation separates evidence about the player, plans for the rival, an encounter host and signaling. Unknown kills supply no confident evidence for a style. Have one test pilot repeatedly throw bodies while a second changes tactics. On the next encounter ask each what the rival anticipated, and how they could exploit its response. Let radio, a visible fitting or an opening maneuver convey the guess early enough to act. Verify each pilot's history through Continue. A bespoke counter that the pilot perceives as arbitrary difficulty provides little reason to care who survived the previous fight. [Rival learning](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/nemesis/learning.js#L20-L68) · [Encounter binding](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/systems/nemesisEncounter.js) · [Ordering contract](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/runtime/authoritativeSystemManifest.js#L149-L159)

**Put the crew's economics in the cockpit.** The crew owner describes four possible hires, local pools, wage arrears, morale, strikes, loyalty, perks and injury. Give a fresh player a cash-poor choice between paying crew and fitting a safer component. Can they predict who might walk, which capability they would lose and where to repair the relationship? Observe whether the next battle feels different and whether repeated docking duplicates pay or morale events. Use a named crewmate's skill in an encounter before handing the player a balance sheet. That gives them a reason to remember whose wages are overdue.
