## Public playtest, via normal controls

| Step | Direct observation | Design implication |
|---|---|---|
| Open the page and choose Play | A polished “First light” card offered calmer settings. The public title then showed the gold orrery dial and separate Swarm and Crucible entries. | Preserve the accessibility invitation. Use the player's goal as a menu label when the internal names are close cousins. Review public collateral against the version people will actually play. |
| Select Swarm | The loading screen called the mode Crucible. Its visual-preparation indicator remained at 87.8% across screenshots while the counter changed from 260/260 to 1,081/1,081. The screen's elapsed clock also advanced. It eventually entered the fight. | The counter tells an engineer about passes. To a player it looks finished several times. Track real time to control, and show a truthful last-stage message or stable progress denominator. The clock shown included time outside this loading segment. |
| Enter Ricochet Foundry | The panel showed round 1 and 15/15 hostiles. A gold player ship and red hostile rings stood out against dark space. A white GRAB diamond sat immediately beside the status card. The radar's accessible label still named Helios Prime as an objective. | Target identity survives a busy frame. Check whether campaign destinations and labels leak into the current release's arena. Judge graphical HUD and accessibility-tree output separately. |
| Right-click the marked body, tap thrust and release | A bright line appeared. The HUD showed a body of 1,690 t and 55% strain. The line disappeared after release and the grade said “MESSY · NEVER SWUNG.” | The simulation exposed a teachable mistake. Show the pilot what enough sideways motion looks or sounds like before their second cut. No kill was attributed to this release by the reviewer. |
| Lose round 1 | The death view named the Wasp Swarmer, Pulse Laser S, port direction and hull breach. It recommended turning and showed a directional diagram. The same-seed retry was the most prominent button. It showed 8 kills, score 0, and 384 cr banked from 768. | Keep this unusually specific death explanation. Explain which kills score and which funds were earned. Attribution for those eight kills remains unresolved by the displayed result. |
| Select same-seed retry | The game returned to the Foundry. Before any further deliberate flight or fire input from the reviewer, round one cleared and the Armory appeared. Its receipts included “ROOM KILLS ×7.” | The environment acting on its own is exciting. Test idle controls across seeds against deliberate play. One clear cannot establish the typical difficulty or a systemic idle strategy. |
| Buy and launch | Pulse Laser M said 28 cr and the post-purchase balance would be 989. One selection fitted it and changed 1,017 to 989. Launch round 2 led to “ROUND 02 · INBOUND,” named a Reaver Pirate and carried 989. A large round banner crossed the ship while Hull Critical was also visible. | Preserve exact-price and mount feedback. At low health, display the repair offer against the next-wave threat. Review whether the center banner obscures the moment the player regains control. |
