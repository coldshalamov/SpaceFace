# Adventure, contracts and the 47-A campaign

**Editorial verdict.** SpaceFace has a mystery that only this game could tell. A courier's paperwork says one thing and the weight on her rope says another. A player can distrust an institution because of something she felt in her hands. Preserve that premise. Concentrate the demo on one occasion when a person, a place and a piece of freight give three different accounts of the same event. The story loses much of its distinction when acceptance, travel, a text receipt and a reward can do all the storytelling. [Campaign staging brief](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/design/game/16_THE_STORY_SHOWN.md#L11)

## A reproducible reason to protect the second half

On the reviewed commit, running `node test/story-campaign47a-live.test.mjs` reproduced three failing named checks. Printing the full stacks in a scratch copy showed that **all three reach a B7 fixture and fail the assertion that `deep_reach_operation_complete` is true**. The check named for B5 first calls `advanceToB7`. It would misdirect an agent to file this as three broken systems or a demonstrated B5 progression bug. The same run passed a continuous B0–B7 fixture and the five-ending checks on other setups. The harness injects events, sets funds and reputation, and stages bodies. It establishes a branch integration discrepancy. Only the normal pilot route can establish whether a real save is stranded. [B7 fixture](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/test/story-campaign47a-live.test.mjs#L619) · [Second B7 assertion](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/test/story-campaign47a-live.test.mjs#L767) · [B5 preamble](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/test/story-campaign47a-live.test.mjs#L894)

The cause was **fixture drift**. The fixture sends the player ship as the moving mass in its impact event. The production blockade handler requires the mission’s own `jam_hulk` striking its `approach_wedge`. In a scratch copy, replacing only that event with the two bodies already spawned by the mission cleared all three failures, including the check that iterates patrol and traders. The independent, unmodified blockade impact suite passed 17/17. The repo still needs the fixture correction. Keep the invalid old impact as a rejection case. Then earn the impact from the ordinary pilot controls on both a fresh and a resumed save. This leaves a genuine player-route question after resolving the misleading engineering signal. [Old event](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/test/story-campaign47a-live.test.mjs#L321) · [Current physical contract](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/systems/missionObjectiveOps.js#L2867) · [Focused tests](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/test/book7-blockade-impact.test.mjs)

## The first hour, edited down to three decisions

1. **What did I just tow?** Follow the later first-hour storyboard. Play Cold Start as the mandatory first physical scene. Let the ship move before the first line of dialogue. A comparison pull on nearby known freight makes the sample's strange swing meaningful even to someone who doesn't know what a normal rope feels like. If the player misses the Skim, have an escort wreck or empty attachment point give them something to investigate on the way back. Preserve the canon theft and its physical survivors. Carry the advance cue through both a visible event and sound. [Opening storyboard](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/design/game/20_THE_FIRST_HOUR.md#L63)
2. **Whom do I stop for?** “The first job” in the town brief becomes the first *optional harbour job* after docking. Put Orla's dark lamp on the departure bearing. A person already following the alloy run can pass by. A person who stops learns a social use for the same Line that just wrecked a ship. Keep the old starter work and the alloy continuation available through the board. Show one tracked destination at a time. Preserve a direct course back to the story after the detour. [Empty Plinth brief](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/design/game/13_JOBS_AND_HEISTS.md#L9) · [Opening fleet work](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/design/program/fleet/QUEUE.md#L94)
3. **Which account do I trust?** At Tethys the player places the load on Kessler's Scale. Let the board visibly hesitate long enough for a player to anticipate the reading. The discrepancy should remain inspectable later so an interruption or a missed subtitle costs no story knowledge. Use the canonical scene and contact. Measure how many first-time players can explain the discrepancy without being prompted with “mass” or “scale.” [Tethys scene](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/design/game/16_THE_STORY_SHOWN.md#L17) · [Scale owner](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/systems/kesslerScaleWorksite.js)

For the first formative test, invite six unfamiliar pilots, three with a controller and three with mouse and keyboard. Ask each to find their next job after Continue without coaching. Record the last meaningful input and the first point they ask for help. Use four of six independently retelling why the sample matters as a provisional editorial checkpoint. This small group finds confusions. Sales, broad retention and playtime claims require a larger cohort. Eliminate redundant introductions before commissioning another contract template.
