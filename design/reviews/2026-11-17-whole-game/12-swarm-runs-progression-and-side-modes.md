# Swarm, repeat play and the value of the side modes

Swarm deserves top billing. Its authored first five rounds already have the outline of a good arcade game. Three Wasps appear first, the remainder follow through the same gate, two gates then compete for attention, a Detonator introduces a throwable threat, and freight makes the floor dangerous. The first champion should reward something the player learned during those four rounds. Today that chain breaks at the boss, and the economy can pay a beginner handsomely for dying before they learn any of it. These are unusually tractable problems for a project of this size. ## The economy currently pays for dying on a full starting wallet

The default beginner stake grants **2,400** run credits. The first rank of each Hangar track costs **100**. The results UI explicitly says defeat banks half of the remaining purse including starting funds. I passed a Swarm result with seed 4242, zero kills, zero waves cleared, Exhibition and an untouched 2,400 balance through the production `settleCrucibleRun` function with an isolated fresh profile. The permanent bounty became **1,200**. This was a settlement test. The time an idle pilot takes to die on the real route remains unmeasured. A beginner who spends most of their purse experimenting can retire from that loss with less permanent buying power than someone who did nothing. [Stake table](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/data/swarmStakes.js#L18-L34) · [Track prices and bank](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/data/swarmHangar.js#L4-L14) · [Settlement owner](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/systems/survivalRecords.js#L1834-L1881) · [Results copy](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/ui/screens/crucible.js#L4264-L4276)

**Build the first economic repair before adding a progression tier.** Preserve the opening shopping allowance. Tag funding as an opening advance, earned salvage, or already-secured salvage at the wallet owner. Spend the advance first so trying the shop feels generous. Bank the specified proportion of remaining earned salvage and add previously secured earnings once. Compute the on-screen projection from the same settlement function. If consolation for an early loss is desired, make it a small, explicit first-action or first-learning reward that survives only once per profile. Old records retain exactly the balances the player already earned. Handle new runs, repeat result delivery, a round-30 victory followed by Keep Going, partial extraction, and a practice run. A zero-action, zero-earn defeat should bank zero under the proposed law. Two pilots who earn the same salvage while spending different amounts of *advance* should keep the same permanent salvage. Compare bounty/minute across idle, playing safely and taking a calculated risk. ## Competition needs comparable starting conditions

Daily mode deliberately removes stake choice and Threats, queues the challenge by seed and date, then starts the run. The survival run owner also reads the local Hangar and the Swarm opening can apply its ranks to the ship. I exercised production run-begin and Hangar application with the same seed and a daily date in the request on two synthetic 100-hull/100-shield pilots. Rank 0 produced 100/100. Rank 5 produced 125/125. With no other funding listener, the isolated wallet also showed a 500-credit difference. This proves a profile-sensitive owner path. Verify the complete Daily through the UI on two profiles before making claims about any particular leaderboard or live daily payout. [Daily route](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/ui/screens/crucible.js#L2398-L2422) · [Challenge queue](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/ui/screens/crucible.js#L2438-L2459) · [Profile load](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/systems/runSession.js#L167-L203) · [Hull application](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/systems/swarmHangar.js#L24-L44)

**Build a stock Daily and keep personal builds in open runs.** For shared-conditions play, stamp the seed, rules revision, arena, start wave, fixed ship, fitted modules, effective armor/shields, starting purse, perks, allowed assists or relevant comparison class, and the actual offered modifiers in the challenge descriptor. Preserve old codes for playback when supported and identify incompatible revisions at import. Two different profiles replaying the same stock challenge should produce the same opening state, costs, relevant random rolls and outcomes under the same action tape. Test against the current run-share and ghost envelopes. A local ghost or challenge code gives an affordable social proof of skill. Spend on network multiplayer only if players keep trading those challenges and pressing Retry. ## An experiment worth spending one production cycle on

Use one Foundry room and the existing rounds 1–5. Make a Detonator's first commitment readable in round three. Let the pilot catch one and hit either a Wasp or a fixed panel. Keep the same visual language when the carrier lane starts in round four. In round five the mouth announces a feeding window. The ammunition is alive and dangerous, and the player can solve the encounter without a menu. Briefly let the room fall quiet after the first successful feed. The player gets to see exactly what they did. At the results plate, show the captured moment and a replayable seed. A fresh observer should understand the feat from six seconds of muted footage. If the production team keeps the broadside version of Iron Maw, use the same three-round structure to teach blind-side approach and a flank throw.

| Observation | How to collect it | Go/no-go proposal |
|---|---|---|
| Is the first fight reached? | Fresh profile, public door, real click, real hostile and first acknowledged input. Record cold and warm separately. | Use the project's ≤10 s cold/≤5 s warm PLAY-to-hostile targets on its nominated machine. Escalate the loading owner when the combat route is otherwise ready. [Reference targets](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/design/program/fleet/FINISH-LINE.md#L7-L16) |
| Does the player learn something repeatable? | Observe five uncoached first-timers. Ask what they intended after two different throws. Record training choice, input device, losses and completions. | Seek four intentional repeats and at least three explanations of the first champion's tell/counter from those who see it. Record how many never reach the boss. Staged practice is a different sample. |
| Is the Armory costing momentum? | Measure actual seconds and input count from wave-clear to next controllable flight. Separate deliberate shopping from searching for Launch. Compare raw times within the same players. | A pilot who wants to skip should spend at most one deliberate activation once the Armory is ready. Experienced players should still be able to fit, Fortify and inspect the next threat safely. |
| Is the mode worth replaying? | Offer a natural exit after loss or after the first boss, no instruction to retry. Record voluntary retries, different builds attempted, and the player's exact reason for continuing or stopping. | Iterate when people who understood the controls say the next run would repeat the same decision. A second voluntary attempt with a named new tactic is stronger early evidence than idle time on the results screen. |
| Do high rounds actually hold together? | One unmodified public run through the economy and boss milestones, plus separately labelled stage starts for rounds 5, 10, 20, 25, 26, 27 and 30. | Beat the ordinary course, see the ending, choose Keep Going, and settle once. Audit the alternate outcomes on the same ruleset. The head's ACCEPT-07 packet says earlier acceptance runs in this series ended by round 3. [Coverage limitation](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/design/program/fleet/packets/ACCEPT-07-later-content.md#L1-L18) |

These are proposed decision thresholds for formative sessions, except where project targets are cited. Observed player rates still require a cohort. Compare distributions after there is enough traffic to estimate them. Count exits, failed loads and players who chose an alternative mode.

**Dispatch order.** First fix the zero-earned bounty and record a full public launch/retry loop. In parallel, reconcile the round-five encounter and the Warden discrepancy from the combat chapter on disjoint files. Then test a small, blind cohort against the joined build. Take Daily equality and a polished side attraction after the first five rounds sustain voluntary retries. Each step offers a stop point that can improve the sellable game on its own.
