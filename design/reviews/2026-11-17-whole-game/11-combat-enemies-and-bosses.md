The production problem is making the player see the same rule the simulation applies. Two discrepancies on the Pit course illustrate the cost. A correct tactical judgment based on the game's own words can produce the wrong move.

## What to preserve in the combat stack

**Enemy intelligence.** The doctrine system expresses approach, windup, commitment, recovery and egress. It even treats the committed firing lane separately from a hull's heading. Specific specialists already publish phase and target state. Capital scores can expose a subsystem on a committed pass. These are the ingredients of an enemy you can outplay. A swarm of individually coherent enemies can nevertheless become unreadable collectively. Use the Warden, Detonator and Lancer as the visual language test. A player should recognize protection, imminent straight-line impact, and a locked line of fire at normal zoom, including with reduced effects or without audio. Read the existing G08 silhouette row before commissioning more hull art. [Doctrine](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/ai/combatDoctrine.js#L68-L101) · [Specialist phases](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/ai/swarmSpecialistRuntime.js#L19-L51) · [Art review](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/design/program/fleet/QUEUE.md#L101-L106)

**Weapons and fittings.** The game compiles attack specifications, tracks descendants that split or bounce, and limits propagation so one shot cannot multiply forever. Its draft offers include Web, Bank, Freeze and Ram. These are materially different invitations to act. Design rooms where the merit of a weapon becomes visible before asking anyone to interpret an equipment comparison. An open lane flatters penetration. A narrow right-angle wall flatters a bank shot. A heavy and two light enemies give Web and a shove a formation to exploit. Use the authored estimates only as a starting point for timed trials. The Iron Maw score even labels its reference DPS as an inference. ## Build assignments

1. **Reconcile the six champions, starting with round five.** Assign one encounter owner and a reviewer who watches a blind playthrough. Author the round-specific interaction once and feed the spoken twist, loss line and results hint from the same choice. For the mouth version of Iron Maw, the first failed safe-mass impact should visibly be swallowed. The last round's dart arrives from the side, the mouth opens on a tell, and a correctly timed throw causes a brief break, a visible ejecta burst and authoritative damage. A miss leaves a recoverable dart supply. Keep a conventional victory route. Reuse contact and attack lineage so a gun explosion near the mouth cannot earn credit for feeding it. Test a legal throw, a near miss and a normal gun-only victory. Verify that the Adventure score keeps its broadside contract.
2. **Stage a fair double-Warden fight.** Bind the proposal to the round encounter. Use one diagnostic seed and two real route seeds. Show the two links before fire begins. The first broken link must create an actual interval in which its ward takes damage. Aim for three of five first-time observers to identify the protected and vulnerable targets after a five-second muted clip, then repeat in motion. Revise the clip and report the denominator. Validate death, despawn, separation beyond range and save/restore where supported. Keep generic Wardens on the current target selector.
3. **Build a small weapon viability matrix.** Against the *same* starts, give the tester a bank build, a control build (Web/throw), and a gun-only baseline. Include a duel, a pack near a wall, and a boss telegraph with an exposure window. Record completion, useful damage, self-damage, off-screen hits, controller effort, worst frame gap and whether the build actually used its advertised verb. A legitimate specialist should win at least one room on something the pilot values even if it loses another. A universal winner gets its interaction or room examined before a blanket damage change. Verify defeat and retry with the same loadout. Use the existing combat lab to reproduce. Prove the selected improvement in an ordinary run. [Combat Lab setups](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/data/combatLabSetups.js) · [Run setup and retry](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/4bbe545d550a4c43e720df18c254b1c8d190d8a1/src/ui/crucibleLaunch.js#L380-L396)
4. **Give each priority threat a perceptible second language.** On the three pilot archetypes, assign distinctive silhouettes and windup motions, one positional sound per action and a restrained danger shape that survives reduced motion. In overlaps give lethal windups priority over loot and spectacle. Compare a muted small-screen capture, a full-color desktop capture and a reduced-effects capture against the actual impact geometry. A player saying “I should have crossed the beam” after a loss is a better acceptance signal than a reviewer recognizing the model on an asset sheet. Resolve these three first, then apply the grammar to the wider roster.
