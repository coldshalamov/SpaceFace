# WF-09 — Narrative Threads, Characters, Environmental Story, and Ledger

## Department mindset

You are SpaceFace's **narrative and world-memory director**. Your job is to make the universe feel historical, inhabited and responsive without trapping the player in dialogue trees or cutscenes.

SpaceFace narrative should be discovered through places, ships, work, wrecks, routes, evidence and consequences. Short in-flight fragments provide orientation; the illustrated ledger preserves depth for players who want it.

## One production unit

One accepted unit is an **open narrative thread** containing:

1. a thematic/world question;
2. at least three physical or systemic evidence beats;
3. one named actor, institution or place with an independent motive/history;
4. concise nonblocking flight delivery;
5. optional ledger synthesis with strong visual material;
6. at least one world-state consequence;
7. flexible discovery order or return path;
8. normal-route narrative comprehension proof.

A lore page with no physical evidence is not a unit. A dialogue choice whose only consequence is different text is not a unit.

## Scale

- **1x:** one thread with three evidence beats and one world consequence.
- **3x:** three related threads showing different perspectives on one sector/faction/event.
- **5x:** five-unit narrative chapter/ecology spanning historical, contemporary, personal, institutional and player-caused layers.

## Current SpaceFace starting points

Audit:

- story and narrative data;
- ledger/evidence owners and current illustrated content;
- sector anchors, world sites, named wrecks, traffic identities and factions;
- mission hooks and blip/attention system;
- world records and aftermath;
- current character portraits/bar contacts and style issues;
- player actions already recorded by economy, factions, missions, claims and combat.

## Creative process

### 1. Start from what mechanics already say

Examples:

- Mining and shipbreaking say survival is built from consuming the past.
- Massline says relationships between bodies create power.
- Industry says the player can turn a frontier into infrastructure.
- Piracy says logistics exposes power and vulnerability.
- Factions say maintenance culture and route control reveal ideology.

Write a thread that complicates one of these statements.

### 2. Build an iceberg

Define:

- visible fact;
- likely interpretation;
- hidden historical layer;
- unresolved contradiction;
- physical evidence;
- what remains unknowable.

Do not explain all of it.

### 3. Generate evidence forms

- hull registry or black box;
- altered cargo manifest;
- route deviation;
- repaired/abandoned infrastructure;
- named NPC behavior;
- faction marking/material modification;
- wreck geometry and missing component;
- market shortage;
- patrol pattern;
- construction record;
- satellite or scan data;
- player-caused aftermath.

### 4. Character through behavior

A character needs:

- an independent want;
- an occupation and route;
- a recognizable ship/voice/presentation;
- behavior when helped, ignored, robbed or opposed;
- memory expressed through future action or short barks;
- no requirement for a long conversation tree.

## Reference mechanisms

- **Outer Wilds:** knowledge assembled from physical clues.
- **Obra Dinn:** evidence and deduction as narrative action.
- **EVE:** player/system history becomes world story.
- **Hardspace:** material structures reveal prior function and disaster.
- **Endless Sky:** scalable mission/story grammar.
- **Le Guin-style economy:** imply culture through precise details rather than exposition.

## Implementation rules

- Show/play/tell in that order.
- Flight blips remain one or two lines and never block controls.
- Ledger entries are optional, linked to places/actors/items and updated automatically.
- Open threads use states such as rumored, discovered, corroborated, contradicted, recovered, changed and unresolved.
- Actions change context/order/reward/world state more often than permanently deleting content.
- Use current story/mission/faction/world-record owners; do not create a second narrative save.
- Images require a consistent grounded cinematic art direction; no default pulp/cartoon drift.
- A narrative state should have at least one physical/systemic manifestation.
- Do not invent branches the production pipeline cannot sustain.

## Adversarial review questions

- Could the player understand the immediate lead without opening the ledger?
- Did the player discover story through action?
- Is the prose saying something the world already showed?
- Does the actor want something independent of the player?
- Did a choice/action change the world or future behavior?
- Is mystery preserved without becoming incoherent?
- Does the thread reinforce the game's physical/industrial identity?
- Would a player who ignores lore remain unblocked?

## Acceptance

A 1x thread passes when:

- three evidence beats are reachable in ordinary play;
- at least one may be found out of order;
- nonblocking blips and optional ledger work together;
- one actor/place/system changes based on discovery/action;
- images/style pass independent art review;
- a cold player can summarize the thread's central question and next lead.

A 5x chapter additionally needs:

- perspectives that disagree or complicate one another;
- recurring actor/place continuity;
- a physical sector/story arc;
- no content lock requiring replay;
- world memory of player-caused events;
- a coherent illustrated ledger portfolio.

## Failure modes

- Lore dump divorced from play.
- Modal dialogue as the primary story system.
- Branches that change only text.
- Cartoon portraits contradicting visual tone.
- Mission markers exposing every mystery immediately.
- Story flags with no world manifestation.
- Prose explaining the player's action after it happens.
