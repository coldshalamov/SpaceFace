# WF-03 — Sector Identity, Activity Pockets, and World Composition

## Department mindset

You are SpaceFace's **world and level director**. Your job is to turn sectors from distributed records or central object piles into memorable places with an economic thesis, recognizable spatial structure, visible routes, distinct activity rhythms and opportunities that enter the camera naturally.

Space is allowed to be empty. Emptiness must be deliberate contrast between authored concentrations, not the primary content.

## One production unit

One accepted unit is an **activity pocket** containing:

1. one-sentence function and emotional identity;
2. dominant landmark or silhouette hierarchy;
3. local route/approach structure;
4. one visible job or economic process;
5. one player opportunity;
6. useful terrain/physics composition;
7. meso props and state dressing;
8. entry, internal and exit rhythm;
9. ordinary-route proof.

A scatter of props around an anchor is not a unit.

## Scale

- **1x:** one accepted activity pocket inside an existing sector.
- **3x:** three contrasting, connected pockets with at least one route crossing and one quiet interval.
- **5x:** one complete sector portfolio: 3–5 pockets, one sector-wide landmark hierarchy, visible traffic topology, legal/criminal tension, physical/traversal rule, aftermath layer and 5–10 minute route proof.

At 5x, the units may be five pockets or four pockets plus one sector-wide throughline/landmark system.

## Current SpaceFace starting points

Audit sectors, anchors, zones, authored places/activity pockets, camera-visible bands/travel speeds, traffic/job routes, encounter director, stations/fields/sites/hazards/machinery, current art identity and zero-visible-activity evidence. For Ceres, preserve and deepen the canonical Refinery Pocket, Working Seam, Ambush Run and Cathedral Grave.

## Creative process

### 1. Write the sector thesis

Use one sentence with a causal verb. Example: “Ceres extracts ore, processes it and moves it through a dangerous corridor where law, predation and salvage contest the flow.” If it could describe five sectors, it is not specific enough.

### 2. Map macro, meso and micro

- Macro: sector silhouette, dominant planet/station/wreck, route geometry, pocket spacing, visual rhythm.
- Meso: worksites, job cast, traffic lanes, combat terrain, checkpoint, event location.
- Micro: cargo, lights, debris, machinery, markings, state changes, work effects.

Do not use micro detail to compensate for weak macro composition.

### 3. Design approach and discovery

For each pocket answer: what is visible before arrival; what enters the frame first; what activity explains the place; what can the player do besides dock/fire; what route leaves it; what remains visible on departure.

### 4. Candidate lenses

Civic/service, production/extraction, logistics/transit, law/customs, crime/predation, repair/construction, wreck/history, anomaly/environment and player development. Select only those required by the thesis.

## Reference mechanisms

- **Freelancer:** legible systems, routes and destinations.
- **Subnautica:** strong regional identity and useful danger.
- **Outer Wilds:** landmarks/anomalies pulling curiosity.
- **DUST 514:** coherent modular environment families.
- **Dishonored:** holistic spaces built around verbs.
- **Left 4 Dead:** authored spaces supporting dynamic pacing.

## Implementation rules

- Compose to the normal camera, not radar/sensor radius.
- Place primary interactive activity within current visible bands during pocket play.
- Connect pockets with traffic and intermediate cues; do not isolate each as a mini-level.
- Create negative space intentionally; remove objects that blur hierarchy.
- Use background life for scale but keep approach/materialization honest.
- Terrain should create movement, cover, collision, Massline and force opportunities.
- Use current coordinate/route owners.
- Hero landmarks are scarce; ordinary infrastructure carries most density.
- Every pocket needs a deliberate absence.

## Adversarial review questions

Could the reviewer identify the pocket/sector with HUD hidden? Does the route reveal a spatial story? Is ordinary activity visible? Is there room to fly/improvise? Does dressing explain function? Are gaps useful or merely empty? Is there something optional to investigate or alter?

## Acceptance

A 1x pocket passes when identity is visually/behaviorally readable; at least one activity cycle occurs in-camera; one optional player opportunity exists; approach/interior/exit are composed; terrain supports physics verbs; and performance passes.

A 5x sector passes when players describe it by activity/look; pockets are distinct but connected; activity rhythm avoids accidental dead intervals; legal/criminal/industrial/historical layers coexist; one ten-minute route creates choices/anecdote; and a propagation recipe is recorded.

## Failure modes

- Central cluster with larger scatter radius.
- Sector identity through color grade only.
- More icons, same empty frame.
- Uniform prop scatter.
- Every pocket contains station + asteroids + patrol + enemies.
- Giant landmark with no activity.
- Background actors that never become interactable.
- Fixing dead travel only by increasing global speed.
