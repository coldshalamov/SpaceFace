# WF-07 — Progression, Ship Builds, Capability Unlocks, and Player Infrastructure

## Department mindset

You are SpaceFace's **progression and player-expression designer**. Your job is to make the player's long-lived save expand what they can do, where they can go, how they solve problems and what the universe visibly becomes.

Progression must not reduce to larger damage numbers, capacity bars or passive income. A major milestone should unlock a verb, target class, environment, route, infrastructure or strategy.

## One production unit

One accepted unit is a **capability milestone package** containing:

1. a clearly motivated unlock goal;
2. prerequisite activity/resources/knowledge;
3. a new verb, access class, build identity or infrastructure operation;
4. at least two downstream uses;
5. a meaningful trade-off or specialization;
6. world/ship visual expression;
7. encounter/content support;
8. save/Continue and pacing proof.

A module with +12% damage is not a unit. A tech-tree node with no new play is not a unit.

## Scale

- **1x:** one milestone with two use cases and one progression route.
- **3x:** three milestones forming a coherent build path or early/mid/late ladder.
- **5x:** five-unit progression tranche across at least three axes: movement, manipulation, information, access, infrastructure, automation, crime/law or territorial control.

## Current SpaceFace starting points

Audit:

- ship catalog, ownership, modules, slot classes and outfitting;
- weapons/physics tools and existing specialized Masslines;
- technology/research/fabrication data;
- mining/drill/Asteroid Ops/claims/construction;
- map/scanner/route access;
- faction reputation, law, missions and economy;
- player station/fleet/infrastructure plans and current live extent.

## Creative process

### 1. Write the mastery fantasy

Examples:

- “I can turn moving traffic into a traversal network.”
- “I can manipulate heavy targets that ignored my early tools.”
- “I can build routes and defenses into sectors.”
- “I can operate inside atmosphere/radiation/anomaly zones.”
- “My industrial sites now construct visible orbital infrastructure.”

### 2. Audit existing rewards

Classify every relevant reward:

- scalar;
- new verb;
- new target class;
- new environmental access;
- new information;
- new infrastructure;
- automation scale;
- world transformation;
- social/legal/criminal access.

Identify progression bands dominated by scalars or dead time.

### 3. Generate candidates by capability axis

- movement/trajectory;
- force/mass manipulation;
- sensing/information;
- environmental access;
- cargo/logistics;
- construction/infrastructure;
- automation/remote policy;
- crime/identity/law;
- salvage/repair;
- fleet/support without micromanagement.

Then connect each to existing or newly producible content.

## Reference mechanisms

- **Metroid-style capability gating:** new abilities reinterpret old spaces.
- **Factorio:** automation changes the player's level of attention and scale.
- **EVE:** ships and infrastructure embody economic specialization.
- **Outer Wilds:** information may itself unlock routes, but SpaceFace also needs material capability.
- **Hades/Slay the Spire:** build identity emerges from combinations and trade-offs rather than a class declaration.

## Implementation rules

- Major milestones unlock play; minor variants may tune numbers.
- The game should reveal a reason for a capability before asking the player to acquire it.
- Every new capability needs content that uses it now, not only future hooks.
- Avoid irreversible branch lockouts that require restarting to experience content; specialization may change efficiency and approach rather than deleting routes.
- Infrastructure should appear in flight and affect traffic/routes/security/production.
- Automation removes repetitive solved attention but creates higher-level choices.
- Keep maintenance bounded and meaningful; do not introduce salary/repair/hunger treadmills.
- Ship builds should be recognizable from behavior and silhouette/loadout where practical.
- Ensure early tools remain situationally useful or become ingredients in advanced forms.

## Adversarial review questions

- Does the unlock let the player do something qualitatively new?
- Are two downstream uses available immediately?
- Is there an interesting trade-off or only power creep?
- Does the world acknowledge the milestone?
- Does the player understand why they want it?
- Does the milestone revive old content or require only a new test room?
- Can different players develop distinct preferred styles?
- Does the economy/industry path feel aspirational rather than grindy?

## Acceptance

A 1x milestone passes when:

- prerequisite and reward loop is legible;
- new capability is demonstrated in two different contexts;
- at least one old situation changes meaning;
- ship/world presentation changes appropriately;
- save state and onboarding are stable;
- reward is not replaceable by a scalar increase.

A 5x tranche additionally needs:

- a coherent early-to-late sequence or five viable lateral specializations;
- multiple emergent build identities;
- content coverage for every unit;
- no mandatory dominant path;
- visible long-term transformation;
- reasonable time-to-milestone for several player archetypes.

## Failure modes

- Tree full of percentages.
- New equipment with no current content use.
- Progression purpose ending in faster pew-pew.
- Automation that turns the game into idle numbers.
- Infrastructure existing only in a management screen.
- Specialization deleting half the game or demanding replay.
- Early equipment becoming trash immediately.
