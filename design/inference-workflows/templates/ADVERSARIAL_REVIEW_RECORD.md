## Verdict

- `KEEP` — premise works; remaining defects are nonblocking.
- `REVISE` — premise works but specific execution defects block acceptance.
- `REBUILD` — premise is valuable but current implementation path is wrong.
- `CUT` — unit is redundant, low-value, or harmful.
- `BLOCKED` — evidence or upstream foundation is insufficient.
