# 54 — QUALITY OF LIFE: friction removal, no tradeoffs

Small, unambiguous wins. Each is a standalone packet; none changes game balance.

- **Loadout presets**: save/name/swap fits at stations (weapons + modules + cargo policy).
- **Buy-back**: last-sold items recoverable at sale price for one dock session.
- **Sell-all-junk**: one button clears commodity-tagged "scrap" lines, keeps quest/rare items.
- **Compare everywhere**: hovering any item shows equipped delta (outfitting has it — extend
  to shops and loot screens).
- **Waypoint queue**: shift-click route waypoints on the local map; autopilot chains them
  (existing nav routes).
- **Loot-history ticker**: a *dismissed-by-default* side log of what you collected (I-4-safe:
  it's in the log, not the world).
- **Dock-assist**: approach vector hint when inside a docking corridor (exists — surface it
  as an option, default on for new saves only).
- **Recent-death rewind hint**: after death, the death screen offers one tip drawn from the
  actual kill vector ("the Anchor's field pinned you — Gyro dampeners resist it").
- **Pause-time inventory**: full cargo/module management from pause without station fees.
- **Photo mode**: freeze + free-cam + hide-UI. Cheap given the renderer; the community will
  do the marketing.

## Acceptance

- Each: one focused test + one manual route. No human gate required except photo mode.
