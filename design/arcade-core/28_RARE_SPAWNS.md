# 28 — RARE & FESTIVE SPAWNS: the "you won't believe what I found" layer

Rare = seen a handful of times per playthrough. Each is screenshot-bait and story-fuel. All
spawn deterministically from the seed with low probability + rumor foreshadowing (27).

- **Gold asteroid** — one glittering rock in a normal field, mineable for a jackpot core.
  Every pirate in sensor range wants it too. The fight *over* it is the content.
- **The Merchant Prince** — a fat, over-lit luxury convoy with real guards. Robbing it is the
  biggest single heist outside story content; guarding it pays almost as well and is safer.
- **Ghost ship** — a cold derelict that still answers hails with static. Nothing aboard but a
  black box and one weird commodity. Codex entry writes itself.
- **Drifter migration** (19) — a shoal river crossing a whole sector for a day. Pure
  spectacle; ships detour to fly through it.
- **The Double Wreck** — two ships that killed *each other*, still locked together, tumbling
  slowly. Loot both manifests; the black boxes tell one story from two sides (53).
- **Aces' rendezvous** — two named aces (16) meeting to trade. Interrupt it and you're
  fighting two gimmicks at once for two bounties.
- **The Quiet Patch event** — for one hour, one region goes totally silent (19). Then a
  single, very hard contact. That's all we'll say in the docs.

## Rules

- Probability low enough to stay special; rumors can narrow the search (never exact coords).
- Each rare spawn leaves evidence in the world (news item, codex entry, or wreck) even if the
  player never sees it.

## Acceptance

- Seed audit: spawn rates within stated bands; each spawn's rumor chain resolves.
