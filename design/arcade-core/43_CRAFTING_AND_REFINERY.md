# 43 — CRAFTING & REFINERY: short chains, real purposes

`crafting.js`, `blueprints.js` exist. VISION bans a crafting treadmill — so: **few recipes,
each a decision**. Crafting exists to convert *what you have* into *what your build eats*.

## The chains (all 2–3 steps max)

- **Ordnance**: ore → explosives compound → impulse charges / missile ammo / vector mines.
  The physics kit literally runs on mining.
- **Fuel**: volatiles (42) → refined fuel → jump fuel + boost tanks.
- **Tether kit**: alloys + electronics → cable tiers, winch parts.
- **Field tech**: crystals + electronics → field emitter charges (well/repulsor reloads).
- **Repairs**: scrap → patch kits (field repairs between docks, at an efficiency penalty vs
  station repair).

## Rules

- Blueprints come from play: salvage (26), faction rep (47), aces (16), caches (30). Never
  from a grind vendor alone.
- Factory sites (08) can *automate* a chain you've unlocked — automation converts your time,
  not your judgment.
- Every recipe has a market alternative: crafting is a discount/flexibility play, never the
  only source (keeps 06's economy load-bearing).

## Acceptance

- Route: mine → refine → craft charges → use them in a fight, same session, no station visit.
