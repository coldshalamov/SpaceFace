# 49 — SMUGGLING & BLACK MARKETS

`customsRisk.js`, `commodityMoralTags.js`, `pirateDisguise.js`, and black-market stations (22)
are the seams. The pitch: smuggling is the game's *stealth-physics* career.

## The verbs

- **Cold running**: engines-dark drift through scan chokepoints (18 customs skiffs have
  visible scan cones) — your velocity at burn-cut is your only friend. Pure I-3 flight skill.
- **Hidden compartments** (45): capacity-vs-detection tradeoff module line.
- **Decoy pods** (41): jettison a tagged pod; the scan chases it.
- **Storm diving**: ion storms (19) fuzz sensors — routes through them are the pro move.
- **Drop caches**: pre-stash goods at a moon/rock, sell the *location* to a contact; or buy
  others' cache locations.

## The markets

Black markets (22) price contraband high and ask nothing. Getting caught: fine + confiscation
+ I-7 local heat. Getting away: the best margins in the economy (06's model prices illegality
honestly).

## Rules

- Scan detection is *physical and visible* (cones, drift, emissions) — never a dice roll in a
  menu.
- Smuggling stays optional-spicy: penalties sting, never spiral (I-7).

## Acceptance

- Routes: cold-run success/failure branches; decoy works; storm route vs open route detection
  rates measurably differ.
