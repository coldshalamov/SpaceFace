# 44 — SHIP CUSTOMIZATION: "this is my fucking ship"

VISION's words are the bar. The ship is a long-lived personal machine — identity systems make
it *yours*. (spacepunk_markings assets exist; wiring them is known work.)

## Identity layers

- **Paint & decals**: authored paint schemes per hull + decal slots (kill-mark tallies as
  *silhouette decals* — a row of tiny wrecks on your flank, visual not text, on-brand).
- **Scars** (38): keep-or-repair choice at the dock. Scarred ships get respect barks from
  certain factions ("that hull's seen work").
- **Name & registry**: name the ship; it appears in hails, station logs, and bounties *on
  you* (48). NPCs react to the name's reputation.
- **Engine trail tint** (35): cosmetic unlocks from deeds (burn-up survivor = ember trail;
  drifter-shoal event = bioluminescent tint).
- **Wear patterns**: long ownership accumulates subtle grime/panel variation that repair
  preserves unless you pay for showroom finish. Time made visible.
- **Unique parts**: salvage-only weapons (exist) and cache tech (30) are *visibly* different
  on the hull — other players of the world's fiction (16 aces) would recognize them.

## Rules

- Cosmetics never cost performance: decals/marings baked into hull textures at dock time, not
  live layers.
- Nothing cosmetic is sold for premium currency; everything earned in play or bought with
  credits.

## Acceptance

- Human gate: two same-hull ships with different histories are distinguishable at a glance —
  owner confirms "that one's been through it."
