# 47 — REPUTATION & FACTION PLAY

8 factions, 9-tier rep, conflict zones exist (`conflictZones.js`, `pirateDisguise.js`).
Standard: rep buys **access and texture**, in I-7's memory rules.

## What rep gets you (per faction, authored)

- **Licenses**: restricted modules/weapons (45) gated by standing, not just tech.
- **Prices & services**: rep-tiered discounts; high rep unlocks station backrooms (22 — the
  black market of a *legit* station).
- **Work**: faction mission boards weight toward their doctrine (17) — navy gives escort and
  interdiction, guilds give defense and prospecting.
- **Barks & hails**: the world's voice changes with standing (29) — feared, respected,
  mocked. Cheap, huge texture payoff.
- **Conflict zones**: authored front regions where two factions' patrols actually skirmish;
  picking a side locally pays rep + salvage rights; staying out is viable (loot the
  aftermath, you vulture).

## Disguise & double play

- Pirate disguise exists — extend: transponder spoofing module (Ghost tree), forged manifests.
  Getting *made* while disguised is an I-7 event (local, decaying) — funny, not fatal.

## Rules

- Rep moves on deliberate acts (missions, kills of their enemies in their space, trade
  volume), never ambient proximity. Negative rep moves follow I-7's deliberateness bar.
- Max-negative rep = hostile-on-sight *from that faction's patrols only*, buyable back through
  restitution missions. No permanent locks.

## Acceptance

- Route: raise rep → license unlocks → buy restricted fit → use it. Route: conflict-zone
  skirmish participates correctly for both sides.
