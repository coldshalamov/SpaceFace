# 16 — NAMED ACES: bounties with a face

`namedAces.js` and `bountyHunt.js` exist. Standard: a bounty target is a **character**, not a
stat lump (GDD §6.4). Each ace = named ship + custom paint + one gimmick + a bark set +
an authored spawn story.

## The roster pattern (examples to author; ~12 for launch breadth)

- **"Long" Varo** — Lancer pilot who never closes. Gimmick: double-range railgun, always
  positions near a lane exit. Beats: cut angles, jam its sightline with debris, or well it.
- **The Tugger** — flies a hijacked industrial tug with a *stolen* Massline rig. Yanks
  *you* toward hazards. The physics mirror-match. Counter: cut-proof your line with
  distance, or feed it a vector mine mid-reel.
- **Sister Cinder** — Ember-swarm cultist; her wing is all volatile hulls. Gimmick: she
  *wants* the chain reactions. Fight her in open space, never near your loot.
- **Halfmass Haque** — flies a crippled heavy that can't turn, only burn and brake.
  Gimmick: invulnerable front prow; the fight is getting behind it — or pushing it into
  a gravity gradient.
- **The Quiet Ship** — no barks, dark running lights, appears on no rumor. Gimmick: cloak
  adjacent (cloak.js exists). Reward: its unique drive signature (a cosmetic trail unlock).

## Rules

- Aces **flee at a real threshold and can genuinely escape** (dock, lane out, cloak). An
  escaped ace recurs later with a bark acknowledging the last fight — inside I-7 caps (it's
  *that one character*, not a systemic grudge).
- Ace kills pay the three channels big: unique commodity or module (materials), fat bounty
  (credits), and a named-tech unlock hook (XP).
- Every ace gets a codex entry (53) written like a bar story.
- No aces gating mandatory progress. They're the spicy optional layer.

## Acceptance

- Per ace: the gimmick is demonstrable in a bot route; escape logic tested (it can leave);
  recurrence tested (it comes back once, capped).
