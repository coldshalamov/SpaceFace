# 51 — CONTRACT VARIETY: missions that use the verbs

10 mission types exist. Add the physics-verb generation — each contract below is *about* the
mechanics, so missions teach and showcase the kit.

| Type | The job | The verb it exercises |
|---|---|---|
| **Wreck tow** | Drag a disabled ship to a yard before scavengers strip it | Heavy Massline work + route defense |
| **Rock diversion** | Nudge an asteroid off a collision course (20's event, contracted) | Charges, mass drivers, tow-burns |
| **Debris recovery** | Pull N specific pods from a tumbling debris field | Vacuum + tether in moving cover |
| **Atmosphere rescue** | A tumbling ship is falling in; get it out before burn depth | 04 zones + tether, hard timer, huge tension |
| **Disable, don't kill** | Capture contract (48) | EMP/RCS + tow under fire |
| **Salvage race** | You vs an NPC scavenger crew, same wreck, same time | Fast cutting (26), denial play |
| **Quiet delivery** | Fragile cargo (41), no hard maneuvers | Gentle-flying discipline |
| **Loud delivery** | Contraband through a scan net (49) | Cold running, decoys |
| **Pest control** | A mote/wasp nest is eating a claim's yield | Pure swarm dopamine, paid |
| **Escort the idiot** | A tourist liner insists on the scenic route through a raid zone | Protection with collateral risk |

## Rules

- Contract text names the physical situation, not just the objective ("she's tumbling, two
  minutes from the burn line").
- Failure mutates per VISION: failed rescues leave wrecks and follow-on contracts ("recover
  the black box of the ship you didn't save").
- Boards recommend one job fitting your recent play (existing recommendation engine).

## Acceptance

- Per type: one bot route + the failure-mutation branch produces its follow-on.
