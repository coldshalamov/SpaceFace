# 25 — LANDMARKS & POIs: the map worth memorizing

Every sector gets at least one. A landmark = striking visual + one interaction + one rumor.
`uniqueWrecks.js`, `sites.js`, `worldSiteManifests.js` are the seams.

## The catalog pattern (author one of each, then variants)

- **Wreck Cathedral** (exists in data — finish it): a capital-class dead hulk grown into a
  shrine/settlement. Interaction: dock, trade weird goods, hear the best rumors. Physics:
  it's *terrain* — fights inside its superstructure are the setpiece.
- **The Bone Yard** — a moon-sized ring of fused wrecks. Interaction: salvage free-for-all
  with scavenger drones (19) and claim-jumpers. 
- **Relay Monument** — an old nav relay, still pinging. Interaction: scan it to reveal a
  frontier route. Rumor: it pings in a pattern.
- **The Anchor** — a station-sized mass locked around a gravity eddy (19). Interaction:
  smuggler black market (22). Physics: free slingshot practice.
- **Memorial Field** — lantern buoys for a dead fleet. Interaction: none, and that's the
  point. Quiet. One survivor-pod rumor (26).
- **Fuel Stack** — an industrial refueling tower over a gas giant, flame vents. Interaction:
  cheap fuel, volatile stack (it can blow — a *big* physics event if someone makes it blow).
- **Dead Gate** — a jump gate that doesn't work. Interaction: scan → tech materials + a story.
  Rumor: sometimes it works.

## Rules

- Each POI must be findable twice: on the map (charted space is charted — GDD §7.2) and by
  rumor text that means something ("follow the relay's ping" actually works).
- POIs are load-bearing for missions (51), rumors (27), and secrets (30) — author hooks, not
  just art.

## Acceptance

- Per POI: interaction works, rumor resolves, and it reads in one frame (human gate).
