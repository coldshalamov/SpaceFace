# 30 — SECRETS & EASTER EGGS

Hidden things that reward the kind of player who reads rumors and flies at weird rocks.
`pallasHiddenCache.js` exists — this generalizes the pattern. Tone: the one place the game
may wink (29's voice ban lifts here, carefully).

## The hiding places

- **The Face** — the game's signature secret: one moon (23) whose cratered far side, seen
  from a specific approach arc, is unmistakably a face. No UI tells you. Scanning it grants
  the codex entry "It Was There First" and a decal.
- **The Developer** — a tiny, immaculate ship of no known make parked behind a dead gate
  (25). It has no weapons, infinite dodge, and if you somehow kill it, it drops one of every
  credit chip denomination and a bark: "rude." It respawns next seed.
- **Hidden caches** (generalize pallasHiddenCache): 6–10 across the graph, each clued by a
  bar rumor or a black-box log (26). Contents: unique cosmetics, old-tech modules, one
  forbidden tech item (45).
- **The Listening Post log** — a relay (25) whose ping pattern decodes (codex mini-puzzle)
  to coordinates of the game's one truly hidden sector-edge station.
- **Names in the stars**: dev/tester handles as distant background "constellation" labels on
  the nav chart. Harmless, charming, zero cost.
- **The 47a tribute** — a training scenario named after the repo's most-rewritten golden
  route, complete with a fake validation checklist as flavor text. In-joke for contributors.

## Rules

- Secrets never gate progression, power, or achievements anyone needs.
- Every secret is discoverable *legitimately* (rumor, puzzle, or exploration) — no
  inspect-element-only content.
- Secrets obey invariants: no control theft, no jumpscares, nothing that breaks determinism.

## Acceptance

- Discovery audit: each secret has a tested clue → find path.
