# 45 — MODULES & FITS: agency shopping list

`modules.js`, `synergies.js` exist. Rule from VISION: a module changes **what you can do**,
not a percentage. Every module below is a verb or a physics property.

## Physics-agency modules (the identity tier)

- **Ram plate** (GDD-named): collision damage dealt up, taken down; bow VFX scar. Teaches
  mass-as-weapon early (cheap, low tier).
- **Anchor plates**: massively increase your coupling resistance — *you* become the immovable
  object; wells/shoves barely move you. The juggernaut fantasy module.
- **Gyro dampeners**: tumble resistance + faster recovery.
- **Winch tier line**: Massline max-load/reel-speed grades — tow bigger things.
- **Magnet array line**: vacuum radius/authority (01's progression hook).
- **Field amplifier**: your deployed fields +radius/+strength; pairs with emitter charges (43).
- **Inertial comp**: boosts effective thrust without touching class identity (I-5) — it's a
  *responsiveness* mod, not a speed mod.
- **Cargo smuggler compartment** (49), **fuel scoop** (42), **survey array** (better scan →
  47/53 loops), **point-defense grid** (anti-missile), **decoy launcher** (41).

## Forbidden/experimental tech (the spice)

Rare, cache/ace-sourced (16, 30), each with a real downside: **Overcharge coil** (fields
stronger, emitters melt — charges cost ×3), **Mass faker** (reads as heavy to NPC target
priority; you aren't), **Dead-man reactor** (on death, big cook-off — your wreck is a weapon).

## Fit rules

- Every module shows a live physics delta in outfitting (exists — extend to the new stats).
- Synergies stay few and discoverable (synergies.js): named pairs like Ram plate + Anchor
  plates = "Wrecking Ball" fit bonus.

## Acceptance

- Per module: one bot route where the fit demonstrably does the thing.
