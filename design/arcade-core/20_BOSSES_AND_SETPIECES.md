# 20 — BOSSES & SETPIECES: capital fights and physics events

Big fights are **phased physical setpieces**, not bigger HP. Each phase changes what the
target *is* in physics terms.

## Capital engagement (the template)

1. **Screen phase** — the capital runs a PD/flak screen and escorts. Ordnance is dead weight;
   the play is guns/beams + using its own escorts as ammo against its hull.
2. **Strip phase** — destructible PD clusters, turret banks, and drive sections (all physical:
   parts blow off with momentum). Kill the drives → the capital becomes **stationary terrain**.
3. **Choice phase** — board-lite (dock at a breach: timed interior-lite salvage minigame
   through the industrial beam), tow it (heavy Massline, slow, dramatic, escorts may
   counter-attack), or finish it (reactor cook-off chain — the biggest explosion in the
   non-scripted game).
4. **Aftermath** — persistent wreck site (25), scavengers arrive (19), news runs it (27), the
   faction remembers within I-7 rules.

## Event setpieces (scripted spawns, physics solutions)

- **Falling rock** — a big asteroid is on a slow collision course with a station. Solutions:
  stacked impulse charges, a mass-driver barrage, towing with multiple burn stages, or (evil)
  let it hit and salvage the aftermath. The physics event the game was built for.
- **Station siege** — wave defense around a friendly station; the station's own turrets are
  real, its wrecked modules become terrain mid-fight.
- **Convoy last-stand** — a freighter group you chose to defend, ambushed by a screen comp.
  Failure = a physical debris field and a story, not a reload (VISION).
- **Runaway reactor** — a damaged ship venting toward a populated lane: tow it away, shove it
  sunward (04), or shoot it at safe distance. Timer is diegetic (glow + radio), not a UI clock.

## Rules

- Every phase transition is *physical and visible* — parts, motion, and sound carry it; no
  "PHASE 2" UI (I-4).
- Setpieces are rare and flagged (rumors/news first); none gate core progression.
- The physics solution must genuinely work — no scripted "you nudged it so it misses"
  cutscene. The sim decides (I-3).

## Acceptance

- Falling-rock route: three different solutions each measurably alter the rock's trajectory in
  the sim; the let-it-hit branch produces the debris field + news items.
