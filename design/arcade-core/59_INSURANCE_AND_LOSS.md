# 59 — INSURANCE & LOSS: death that makes stories

VISION: failure mutates, never hard-stops. `playerDefeat.js` owns the seam. Death should be
an *event with choices*, priced so losing hurts and recovering is fun.

## The defeat sequence

1. **Pod-out**: your ship dies; you eject in a survival pod (26's object) — a short drift
   where you watch your wreck get picked at. You can *call* rescue (fee) or wait for a
   patrol/rescue NPC (18) if you're liked.
2. **The wreck question**: your hulk persists with its cargo. You (or scavengers, or pirates)
   can reach it. A recovery-tow contract is auto-offered: get your own ship's corpse home.
3. **Insurance tiers** (bought at stations, priced by hull value and your wanted status):
   - *Basic*: payout covers a stock replacement hull, no modules.
   - *Full*: hull + modules at current fit.
   - *Loyalty*: + your cargo's assessed value.
4. **No-insurance path**: you keep a starter-equivalent loaner and your debts. Bad, playable,
   motivating.

## Rules

- Loss math is legible at purchase time ("this policy pays X if you die tomorrow").
- Scars/customization (44) survive only on recovered hulls or full-tier policies — a real,
  emotional reason to insure or to go back for the wreck.
- Dying to a named ace gives the ace a bark about you next time (16). The world noticed.

## Acceptance

- Full route: die → pod → rescue → recover-own-wreck; and die → insurance payout → refit.
