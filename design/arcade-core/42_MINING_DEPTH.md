# 42 — MINING DEPTH: the chill counterpart to combat

GDD §5 owns the core loop (seams, heat rhythm, fracture, cores, tether-haul). This doc extends
it into a *career* with variety matching 24's rock families.

## New extraction content

- **Comet ice** (27 event): a moving field — mine it while matching its drift; the window is
  the event's duration. Volatiles feed the fuel chain (43).
- **Gas skimming**: dive a giant's *upper* atmosphere band (04's zones, safe rim) with a
  scoop module; heat builds, payout scales with depth. The mining analog of combat's
  atmosphere play — one mechanic, two fantasies.
- **Crystal resonance**: crystal nodes (24) hum; mining on the resonance beat (audio-visual
  timing) multiplies yield, off-beat shatters to dust. A rhythm minigame for the DRG crowd.
- **Claimed-field management** (08): your extractors work seams you prospected; richer seams
  you *found* produce better than generic ones — prospecting (18) feeds your own economy.
- **Salvage-mining**: graveyard fields (24) yield ship parts with the beam — bridge to 26.

## Danger pacing (GDD §5.6 made concrete)

Noise/light meter = how visible your operation is: full-bore beam in a pirate sector raises
interdiction odds; pulse-mining quiet. The meter is *visible* (a hull-glow read, not a bar)
and the risk is legible: you choose loud-rich or quiet-poor.

## Bans

- No mining content that removes the vacuum (01's "ball-chase is dead" applies everywhere).
- No yield multiplication from minigames beyond ~3× baseline — mining mustn't eclipse combat
  income at equal skill.

## Acceptance

- Per new extraction: one bot route with yield + risk metrics; heat/scoop routes run the 04
  zone logic (reuse, don't fork).
