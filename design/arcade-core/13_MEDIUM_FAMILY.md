# 13 — THE MEDIUM FAMILY (mass 20–60): the setup class

Mediums are where the physics kit becomes a *combo game*. Too heavy to flick with one shot,
light enough to *work*. Each medium teaches or demands one setup verb.

| Entry | Mass | Role | The intended kill | Tell |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Marauder** | 40 | Brawler: closes, shotguns, tanks a little | Disrupt its RCS → shove into terrain while it drifts | Boxy, forward armor slab, loud engine |
| **Lancer** (sniper exists) | 35 | Long-range railgun; repositions after every shot | Close fast under its turn rate; or well-clump it with its own spotter | Long barrel spine, charge-up glow telegraph |
| **Interceptor** | 25 | Fast medium: chases, cuts escape | Momentum sink → it inherits your frame → walk it into a rock | Twin nose, oversized drive cone |
| **Bulwark** | 55 | Shielded escort: projects extra shield on its wing (see 15 projector) | EMP/subsystem strip → the wing collapses; or vector-mine it *away* from its wing to break the link | Glowing emitter ring, slow roll |
| **Corsair** | 45 | Pirate medium: grapples *cargo*, tries to flee with it | Chase, break its tow, catch the spill; or slam it mid-flee for the full burst | Tow rig on the tail, mismatched panel paint |
| **Torcher** | 38 | Area-denial: lays flame/plasma trails, herds you | Bait the trail, then blast it through its own fire line | Twin vent stacks, heat shimmer |

## Family rules

- A medium should survive one raw shove (it corrects), but not a *prepared* shove
  (marked + disrupted + concussed = gone). That difference IS the medium-class gameplay.
- Every medium has a flee behavior below ~30% hull that is *visible* (smoke, dump, bark) —
  and fleeing mediums are prime atmosphere/burn-up candidates (04).
- Mediums carry the interesting loot: munitions, components, field parts, sometimes bounty
  contracts on their heads.

## Acceptance

- Per entry: bot route executing "the intended kill" column, plus a control route showing the
  naive approach taking meaningfully longer.
