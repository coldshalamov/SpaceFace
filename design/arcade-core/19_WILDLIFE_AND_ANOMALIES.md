# 19 — WILDLIFE & ANOMALIES: rare, environmental, and on-tone

Not a combat class — the *strangeness budget*. These make the world feel bigger than the
economy. Rarity is the design: each should be a story the player tells, not a farm.

## Ambient life

- **Drifter shoals** — slow bioluminescent jelly-like clusters that ride gravity gradients.
  Harmless. They *react to physics*: fields ripple them, a ship passing scatters them like
  sparks, a well makes them spiral gorgeously. Shooting them is possible and the game treats
  it as a small ugliness (a bark, a flicker) — no loot, no reward.
- **Crystal shoals** — prismatic free-floating growths near ice belts; chime when debris
  passes through. Rare "singing field" POIs.
- **Scavenger swarm** — tiny non-hostile drones-of-no-one that pick at fresh wrecks. They
  scatter from the player and return when you leave. The world tidying itself.

## Anomalies (hazard/curiosity, physics-integrated)

- **Gravity eddy** — a small, always-on Well (existing kernel!) that bends shots and drifts
  loose objects into orbit rings. A free physics toy; pirates sometimes lurk at the rim.
- **Ion storm pocket** — sensor-fuzz + pretty lightning (jammer visuals at world scale);
  shields recharge slower inside. Smugglers hide here (49).
- **Debris river** — a long stream of slowly moving wreckage between two points; cover,
  collision hazard, and salvage all at once.
- **The Quiet Patch** — a stretch where even ambient radio goes silent. Nothing happens.
  That's the point. It gets a codex entry and exactly one rumor.

## Rules

- Rare: each appears in a minority of sectors, some only once in the whole graph.
- No HP bars, no loot pinatas. Their value is texture, physics play, and rumor-fuel (27).
- Everything physical obeys the kernels (fields, drag, collision) — zero bespoke physics (I-3).

## Acceptance

- Each entry: one observation route + a determinism check (fields/eddies through the kernel).
- Human gate: the drifter-shoal field interaction capture; owner's bar is "pretty."
