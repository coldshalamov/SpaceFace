# 12 — THE SWARMER FAMILY (light class, mass ≤ 20)

The dopamine class. Cheap, fast, numerous, physically throwable. The family rule: **every
swarmer dies fast and dies *visibly* — and is more dangerous as a projectile than as a
shooter.** Wasp exists; this fills out the family.

| Entry | Mass | Count | Behavior | Tell | Loot read |

| **Dart** | 10 | 4–7 | Pure speed: high-velocity passes, never orbits, thin hull (dies to 2–3 hits) | Needle silhouette, white-blue trail | Mostly credit chips (they carry payroll) |

| **Ember** | 15 | 2–4 | Volatile: on death, its reactor cook-off shoves *nearby* bodies (a free physics event — chain bait) | Glowing orange core visible through hull | Fewer mats (it burns), more credits |

## Family rules

- TTK on any swarmer with starter weapons: 2–4 s (05_COMBAT_PACING).
- Every swarmer crosses `tumbleDeltaV` from one center-mass concussion hit. If it doesn't,
  the weapon is wrong, not the ship.
- Swarmer deaths should *frequently* be chain starters: spawn posture keeps them near rocks,
  near each other, near traffic. Bowling is the intended play (02_STYLE_KILLS).
- Motes are the onboarding swarm: the first "holy shit" for a new player is field-clumping a
  mote pack and watching the pickup cloud inhale into their hull.

## Bans

- No swarm variant whose threat is HP or damage. Their threat is count, speed, and geometry.
- No identical recolors shipped as new content: each entry needs a behavior + a tell.

## Acceptance

- Per entry: one bot route demonstrating the intended counter and the intended kill read.
- Human gate: a mote-pack fight capture that the owner finds *satisfying to watch with the
  sound on*.
