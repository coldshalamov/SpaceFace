# 57 — ENDGAME & REPLAYABILITY

`postEndingReplay.js` exists. The answer to "what after the story" is *more world*, not a
power cliff.

## The endgame loops

- **Challenge sectors**: two late sectors re-rollable with nasty compositions (11 grammar):
  specialist-heavy, hazard-dense, elite spawns. They pay the best bounties and the only
  source of capstone-tier materials (46).
- **Legendary hunts**: rumor-chained unique targets (28 pattern, harder): a pre-cataclysm
  capital hulk that still fires; an ace *crew*; a gold-asteroid rush against three pirate
  wings at once.
- **Factory defense escalations** (08): your success raises raid quality — your own economy
  becomes the endgame arena.
- **New Game+**: keep codex, cosmetics, titles, and one chosen module; world reseeds with
  elite composition toggle.
- **Seeded runs**: a daily-style fixed seed (deterministic world + spawns) with a local
  leaderboard for score/chains — the determinism contract makes this nearly free and it's
  the streamer mode.

## Rules

- Endgame power caps flat: top-tier fits are ~20% above mid-tier in raw output, the rest is
  agency (45). The challenge comes from composition and execution.
- Nothing in endgame is required to "finish" — the story ends, the world keeps breathing.

## Acceptance

- Seeded-run determinism: same seed → same spawn/world hash across runs.
- Route: NG+ carryover list correct; challenge-sector comp matches its recipe.
