# 52 — STORY & CHARACTERS: people in the machine

`story.js` (8-beat spine), `narrative.js` exist. The spine stays; this adds the *recurring
cast* layer that makes a systemic world feel personal.

## The recurring-cast pattern

- **The Rival** — an ace-tier pilot (16) who keeps showing up: racing you to salvage,
  bidding against you, occasionally saving you. Not a villain; a *peer*. Arc resolves in a
  final two-ship event (race or duel — player chooses).
- **The Fixer** — a bar regular who sells rumors (27), cache locations (30), and "jobs that
  aren't on the board" (51's spicier contracts). Remembers what you buy.
- **The Quartermaster** — outfitter who comments on your fit and scars (44); gives the
  tech-tree diegetic voice ("you're ready for the Bond line, kid").
- **The Survivor** — someone you pulled from a pod (26) who shows up later with a gift, a
  tip, or a debt called in. Branching on whether you saved them is the point.

## Rules

- Cast members are *spawned by your history*, not a fixed script: the Survivor only exists
  because you did the thing. Memory stays I-7 bounded — they remember *themselves*, not your
  every deed.
- Dialogue is short, bark-voiced (29), and skippable; story is garnish on play, never a gate
  (VISION's ban on dialogue-heavy RPG).

## Acceptance

- Route: rescue → later encounter → the branch content fires with correct memory scope.
