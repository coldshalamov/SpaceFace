# Phone deck — the ORRERY pass (2026-10-06)

What changed on top of the flight-deck lane (`README.md`, `AGENT_TASKS.md`), and what is still unproven.

## The deck, in ORRERY's language

Light is the only material; the rest light is warm bone; the Hand (amber) is the one thing that is yours;
ice/azure is data in motion; red is threat only. Bloom is a wider second stroke, never a filter.

| Piece | Where | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Stick | `src/systems/mobile/instrument.js` | An orrery under the thumb: thrust ring (band + edge) with a tick scale, a thrust arc that widens with the push and takes its colour from the flight dial's `SPEED_RAMP`, the amber Hand from the pivot out to the ring (so the heading reads under a thumb), a dashed boost ring that wakes as the thumb nears it and lights solid when boost latches, four chevrons for brake, and a hollow heading mark at the ship's real nose. Readout is a thin numeral. |
| Wheel | `src/systems/mobile/wheel.js` | An ORRERY radial menu: six powers on an arc rail, a lit sector, and the Hand on a `swing` spring. Bank tabs and a readout stand above it in portrait. |
| Keys | `view.js`, `styles.js` | GUN is the Lamp Key (amber, one 45-degree cut) with a drag-aim ring and a beam from the hull; TETHER is a ring that orbits a bead of ice when latched, with a reel ladder while held; DOCK is a Lamp Key that exists only while a berth is in range. |
| Status | `view.js`, `styles.js` | A thin speed numeral and four lit scales (hull, shield, energy, boost). |

Any empty space starts the stick (HUD text that is not a control does not block it); a strip along the
handle's screen edge opens the wheel even if the thumb misses the handle. Taps in open space put a pinned wheel
away and fly in the same touch.

## Phone screens

`isPhoneLayout()` (`model.js`: the deck is on and the short side is 600 CSS px or less) selects phone
compositions: the title and pause drop the arc-rail dial, whose rim is wider than a phone, for the plain
Deckplate column; `.dp-frame__body` is one fluid column; keyboard legends (`.dp-kbd`, the `::after` ESC cues)
are removed; New Game's hull yard becomes a row of three choices; the splash fits 375 px and says "Tap to begin";
the Touch controls pill appears on the title only.

## Verification

- `node --test test/mobile-flight.test.mjs test/mobile-deck-instrument.test.mjs` — gesture model, bridge, the
  instruments' drawing, wheel, ship projection, DOCK feed.
- `node scripts/ui-bench.mjs --phone --shot=mobile-stick,mobile-boost,mobile-brake,mobile-wheel,title,pause,new-game`
  — real screens at 390x844 on a touch device profile. `--viewport=844x390` for landscape.
  `--query=layout=lefty` mirrors the deck.
- Real-route walk in a phone-sized Browser pane with synthetic touch PointerEvents (see the session notes in the
  commit trail); real iOS Safari / Android Chrome hardware is **not** verified. `AGENT_TASKS.md` MOBILE-02
  remains the acceptance for that.
