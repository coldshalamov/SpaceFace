# Reference-Sector Acceptance Scorecard

This scorecard complements the repository's existing deterministic, route, performance and G0–G7 evidence. It does not replace those gates.

A candidate cannot pass because its total score is high while one critical category fails. All **critical** rows must pass.

## D. Player opportunity — critical

Without accepting a mission, the player must encounter:

| Opportunity | Pass condition |
|---|---|
| Follow | actor has visible destination and completes route |
| Help | player can improve a live situation through current systems |
| Crime | actual value can be stolen/intercepted and lawful heat/response follows |
| Fight | real hostile encounter, not passive traffic label |
| Physics exploit | displacement/Massline/field/terrain changes outcome |
| Salvage/examine | value or evidence exists in aftermath/Cathedral area |
| Escape/traverse | route supports a memorable movement choice |

At least three opportunities must be interruptible and produce system-owned consequences.

---

## E. Visibility and pacing — critical

The existing harness derives exact zero-visible intervals; human review owns the threshold. Record and judge:

- time from active-pocket entry to first purposeful visible motion;
- longest zero-visible interval;
- longest interval with actors visible but no readable purpose;
- time between meaningful player opportunities;
- percentage of activity samples in 0–95, 95–125 and 125–165 WU bands;
- number of events that begin and finish while camera-renderable;
- moments of overload where too many cues compete.

Suggested production targets for the first review, not immutable global laws:

- first purposeful activity within roughly 5–10 seconds of entering an active pocket;
- no repeated active-pocket silence longer than roughly 12–15 seconds without deliberate scenic intent;
- at least one meaningful optional opportunity every 60–90 seconds during the composed route;
- the majority of primary activity readable inside the 165 WU presentation envelope.

Human review may revise these after retained evidence; do not fossilize them into universal constants without proof.

## I. Technical and performance — critical

Use current `design/PERF_BUDGET.md` and validation broker.

Record matched before/after:

- CPU frame bands;
- GPU scene/effect attribution;
- draw calls;
- materials/programs;
- active/sleeping entities;
- colliders;
- particle/VFX admission;
- texture and residency growth;
- asset streaming/admission;
- query visits and allocations;
- teardown and lifecycle.

Passing strategies:

- batching/instancing;
- LOD/HLOD;
- culling;
- cadence/sleeping;
- bounded concurrency;
- pooled effects;
- compressed/streamed assets;
- material-role reuse.

Disallowed passing strategies:

- lower default quality;
- remove accepted actors or pockets;
- shrink effects until unreadable;
- replace authored assets with fallback geometry;
- reduce the target visual identity.

## J. Ten-minute human verdict — critical

A reviewer watches/plays the uninterrupted route and answers:

1. Can I tell where I am and what this pocket does?
2. Can I see the universe working without me?
3. Did I notice more than one occupational behavior?
4. Did something happen that I could choose to interfere with?
5. Did my action change the situation?
6. Did the physics create a memorable result?
7. Did the visuals look bright, kinetic and materially finished?
8. Did anything look like a blockout, generic freelancer sci-fi or clutter?
9. Were there long stretches where nothing relevant was visible?
10. Would I voluntarily stay and experiment after the required route ended?

Final disposition:

- **KEEP** — slice demonstrates intended game and production method.
- **REVISE** — specific bounded defects remain.
- **REPLACE** — fundamental composition/asset/interaction premise is wrong.
- **DEFER** — valuable but not necessary for the benchmark.

“Tests pass” is not a human verdict.
