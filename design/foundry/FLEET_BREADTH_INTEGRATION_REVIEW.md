# Fleet Breadth Foundry integration review

The source foundry is accepted as a reusable authoring package. It is not a
claim that every candidate is wired into, or accepted on, the player route.

## Integration disposition

- Preserve the generated kit, variants, scenery, surface language, textures,
  evidence, and reproducible generators on `master`.
- Keep runtime wiring pending. Promotion must be one coherent family at a time,
  with current browser and Electron evidence at the normal gameplay camera.
- Treat triangle, draw-group, material, and texture counts as telemetry. The
  foundry does not impose generic aesthetic ceilings. A hard limit is valid
  only when a task supplies a platform-specific constraint derived from
  measured runtime evidence.
- Do not confuse the neutral authored sheets with final PBR or player-route
  acceptance. The donor variants are useful construction substrates; several
  still need stronger material separation and game-camera refinement.

## Highest-value promotion order

1. Wire and review the three Wasp construction variants as a controlled fleet
   identity vertical.
2. Wire the Helios Span and Ashline Rig faction forks, keeping hostile roles
   structurally distinct rather than tint-only.
3. Bind the trade-hub overlays through faction-aware station presentation.
4. Promote navigation, claim, container, wreck, and gate scenery through the
   place/sector ownership seams with deterministic placement.
5. Use the texture and microdetail kits during individual asset remasters; do
   not attach every detail indiscriminately.

## Remaining visual review notes

- The varied Wasp lineup is materially more distinct in construction than the
  cloned donor lineup, but its neutral preview does not yet prove final surface
  quality or runtime readability.
- The trade-hub overlays provide clear silhouette differentiation, while their
  final material, lighting, collision, and gameplay-scale behavior remain to be
  proven in context.
- The Reach Span and Corsair donor variants read sparsely at the supplied game
  camera and need a promotion pass that judges silhouette, surface hierarchy,
  and faction identity against the live scene rather than the standalone gray
  background.
