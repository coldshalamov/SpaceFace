# 01 — Professional Build Program

**Status:** DRAFT refinement of `design/vision/ALPHA_PROGRAM.md`
**Strategy:** foundation → memorable route → reusable family → operational hardening → independent acceptance

## Goal

Create a professional-quality foundation that can scale into the full game without repeatedly
rebuilding weak implementations. The immediate target is not “all possible content.” It is an
accepted first ninety minutes, representative complete asset families, and production machinery
that can multiply them honestly.

## M0 — Accepted truth, production integrity, and observability

### M0.1 Production-integrity repair

- Prove an isolated transactional runner before any auto-approved mutating terminal-agent campaign.
- Remove iteration-derived visual scoring from campaign scripts.
- Make required-view failure block candidate submission.
- Validate actual chamfer geometry rather than stamping a flag before validation.
- Distinguish authored maps from procedural nodes, factors, and synthesized neutral textures.
- Promote required LOD/collision/pivot/socket checks by asset profile.
- Lint evidence provenance, required files, source/GLB hashes, render framing, and change deltas.
- Reclassify existing Top-50/evidence outputs with the stable asset-classification schema; prior
  DONE labels and scores are not grandfathered.

### M0.2 Current Alpha truth closure

- Clear real source-manifest failures without weakening the contract.
- Make locks, staging outputs, temporary exports, and caches unambiguously ephemeral.
- Generate a summary over existing evidence, manifests, runtime maps, checks, and ownership locks;
  do not create a competing source of gameplay truth.
- Correct lifecycle/surface drift such as UI-only assets described as generally visible.
- Capture the canonical browser and packaged-Electron route:
  `boot → New Game → flight → first dock → save → load`.

### M0.3 Gameplay Observatory v1

- Record public-input sessions with aligned intent, execution, presentation, perf, and asset streams.
- Preserve incident windows from full video and extract at least four evidence frames per second.
- Add first detectors: unfair lethality, AI oscillation/telegraph gaps, mining cadence/hitches,
  dead air, asset fallback/LOD/repetition, and missing feedback.
- Run three matched passes: observer+media, observer/no-media, and observer-off/no-media, separating
  capture overhead from observer overhead while requiring identical deterministic hashes/receipts.

### M0.4 Agent and generated-media calibration

- Verify persistence, structured output, vision, Blender, image, and video tool paths for every model.
- Run separated code/product, Blender, and generated-media bake-offs behind the safe runner.
- Route Fable 5, Grok 4.5, OpenCode Kimi, agy, Codex, and Codex subagents from measured results.
- Prove provenance, color-space/channel, downstream edit/bake, and runtime/UI ingestion for one
  generated-media candidate; do not promote direct generated 3D PBR output.

**M0 exit:** trusted evidence and production gates; reproducible browser/Electron baseline; honest
P0/P1 register; no current visual candidate accepted through self-score or contract theater.

## M2 — Seamless persistent twenty-four-region world

### M2a — prove the architecture on one continuous corridor

- Global-coordinate/save-migration ADR.
- Stable world-record identity and deterministic floating origin.
- Full/reduced/record-only residency zones.
- Route prefetch and bounded eviction.
- Continuous Helios → Ceres → Tethys corridor.
- One-map cutover.
- Persistent convoy, wreck, mission target, and mid-route save/load.

Do not populate the remaining regions before this route is accepted.

### M2b — complete the authoritative world scope

- Expand the accepted streaming/persistence architecture to all 24 stable regions.
- Preserve the 10 authored story-region identities and 14 stable frontier regions from the save seed.
- Traverse every connection, retain persistent records/aftermath, and save/load in every residency mode.
- Validate density, ecology reuse, map truth, route recovery, and bounded memory across full-galaxy soaks.

**M2 exit:** the three-region corridor is only an architecture gate. M2 is not complete until all 24
regions are present, traversable, persistent, mapped, and accepted on held-out browser/Electron routes.

## Milestone clean-wave floors

A clean wave uses a candidate hash the author can no longer change, seed/route selections committed
after submission, fresh reviewer sessions, and a retained manifest of every run. Any new P0/P1 or
category-level omission resets the milestone's consecutive-wave counter to zero after repair.

| Milestone | Consecutive clean waves | Minimum held-out matrix per wave |
|---|---:|---|
| M0 | 3 | browser + packaged Electron canonical route; destructive safe-runner fixtures; good/bad asset-contract fixtures; observer seeded positive/negative suite |
| M1 | 3 | five cluttered flybys across at least three seeds; keyboard/mouse + gamepad; normal + reduced motion; worst/median/best clips retained |
| M2 | 3 | three seeds × novice/competent traversal policies; corridor and full-24-region routes; mid-route save/load; browser + Electron residency/soak evidence |
| M3 | 3 | three origins × two held-out seeds × novice/competent policies; complete 90-minute routes; death/recovery and save/continue included |
| M4 | 3 | every ship/station/POI/ecology family in sparse, normal, and crowded exposure routes; repetition and aftermath cohorts retained |
| M5 | 3 | B0–B7 continuity plus all five ending routes, post-ending sandbox, outpost specializations, and progression/save variants |
| M6 | 5 | browser + packaged Electron, target + floor profiles, resize/alt-tab/corrupt-save/accessibility/localization/soak matrix, actual-play store capture |

Before each wave, the controller compiles a hash-bound manifest with exact check commands, seeds,
policies, viewports, platforms, evidence paths, and reviewer IDs. A transcript or partial matrix
cannot count as a wave.

## Coverage ledger requirement

The canonical live ledger is generated at `.devshots/production/coverage-ledger.json`, validates
against `design/production/schemas/coverage-ledger.schema.json`, and is owned only by the campaign
controller. It derives rows from the Alpha ledger, activated specs, manifests, runtime registries,
checks, and accepted evidence; workers cannot omit or hand-edit rows. Each milestone stores the
ledger hash and a versioned summary snapshot in its Alpha evidence record.

Every row records:

`id · player importance · family · maturity L0–L7 · canonical owner · evidence · defects · next gate`

Counts distinguish hulls, module variants, faction skins, and loadout changes. Swapping a gun does
not become a new ship silhouette; a GLB without behavior/product surface does not become a complete
content family.

`check:coverage-ledger` must reject duplicate IDs, unmapped Alpha/spec requirements, missing source
hashes, impossible maturity/status combinations, accepted rows without L7 evidence, and any omitted
manifest/runtime entry. Supporting prose may summarize the ledger but never becomes competing truth.
