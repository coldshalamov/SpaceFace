## Outcome

“$30 professional game quality” stops being a phrase agents can reinterpret. Slice-A and novice
mining each receive a versioned quality card with actual admired frames/clips, actual current
SpaceFace failure examples, identity constraints, critical moments, objective gates, subjective
dimensions, held-out routes, and defect definitions. Every later author/reviewer sees the same
hash-bound bundle at initial dispatch and every resume.

## Research cell

Run independent read-only passes with distinct briefs:

- creative director: combat/flight/mining visual and interaction qualities;
- systems designer: pacing, telegraph, choice, consequence, and monotony failure modes;
- technical art director: readability, material/silhouette/detail hierarchy, repetition, runtime fit;
- skeptic: ways the proposed references/rubrics could be gamed or imitate another game's identity.

Use popular open-source space games for inspectable implementation/asset-pipeline lessons and
official developer/store/trailer sources for closed-source player-facing comparisons. Record source,
date, rights/provenance, exact quality extracted, and SpaceFace adaptation constraint. References are
internal evaluation inputs and never shipped as game assets.

## Required bundles

For each card:

- at least two distinct admired screenshots/clips, each demonstrating a named quality;
- at least one current SpaceFace failure capture showing a named defect;
- contained regular files with bytes, SHA-256, media signature/decode proof, source/provenance, and
  non-shipping status;
- a contact sheet/short native-rate clip for temporal qualities that a still cannot prove;
- exact SpaceFace identity constraints explaining what to learn without copying composition/design;
- critical setup→action→consequence moments and packet-specific P0/P1 definitions;
- held-out seed/route selection committed before candidate authoring;
- applicable observatory gates with version/benchmark hashes or explicit `pending` blockers.

## Cards

1. `slice-a-flight-combat-v1`: threat recognition, maneuver readability, telegraph→counterplay,
   two-ship composition, hit consequence, non-diegetic HUD clarity, asset readability at game scale.
2. `helios-novice-miner-v1`: mining input→beam→seam/core/yield rhythm, escalating choices,
   presentation coherence, world density, travel/action ratio, sell/upgrade consequence, no cartoon
   tonal mismatch.

- Both cards use `cardMode: player_facing`, empty technical-control arrays, and validate against

- Packet compiler fixture proves the exact bundle is attached to initial and resumed contexts and
