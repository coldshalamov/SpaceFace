# Inference-to-Convergence Method

This document preserves the useful production method proposed in
[PR #92](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/pull/92) without importing that draft's
superseded reference-sector plan or creating a second dispatch and acceptance system.

**Provenance:** selective synthesis of PR head `5054a52eb21b079b167a030a16faf01b7c081e5e`,
reviewed against current product, policy, program, and Ceres authority on 2026-08-09.

## Authority boundary

This is supporting creative and production doctrine. It can help an admitted packet find a stronger
solution; it cannot admit work or decide that work is accepted.

- The GDD and current user direction own the intended game.
- [`GAME_DIRECTION_EXPANSION.md`](./GAME_DIRECTION_EXPANSION.md) owns the durable cross-system
  direction and portfolio axes.
- `design/program/roadmap/program-queue.json` and the selected active packet own integration order,
  task identity, and expected paths. `NOW.md` records only threads mutating now and exact dirty hunks
  to preserve; it does not decide whether a product task exists.
- The active packet and exact-revision receipts own required proof. The finishing agent updates the
  exact lifecycle and acceptance rows supported by that proof; a separately named human or reviewer
  remains separate only where the packet requires one.
- A workflow label, an `Nx` request, an agent review, or this document grants no priority, claim,
  lease, path ownership, completion, or acceptance.

Fixed candidate counts, reviewer counts, route durations, iteration counts, and subtype counts from
the draft are **optional sizing heuristics**, never universal gates. A packet selects the smallest
amount of divergence and review that materially reduces its actual risk.

## When to use it

Use this method inside an already admitted packet when the first obvious implementation is likely to
be technically valid but experientially weak—for example when a place feels empty, several roles
look or behave alike, an activity exists only on radar, or isolated good parts fail to compose into
a memorable route.

Do not invoke it to manufacture more planning, reopen accepted evidence without a causal defect, or
turn nineteen design domains into nineteen new queues. If the dispatcher has not admitted a bounded
outcome, return to the program front door first.

## The bounded convergence loop

### 1. Diagnose ordinary-player truth

Observe the normal route before proposing solutions. Record what the player sees, does, understands,
repeats, misses, and can influence. Separate live game truth from code-only state, source-only assets,
test fixtures, labels, radar contacts, and plan prose.

### 2. State the target experience

Write a short player-language contract:

- the fantasy and immediate action;
- the thirty-second and five-minute read;
- the decision or opportunity created;
- the intended visual, audio, and emotional character;
- what must remain simple;
- the exact GDX axes or launch-story frame being strengthened.

These are proposed criteria for the active packet to adopt, not a parallel acceptance bundle.

### 3. Inventory existing vocabulary and owners

Map nouns, verbs, states, and relationships already present. Then locate the current simulation,
presentation, persistence, accessibility, and performance owners. The strongest expansion is often a
missing relation or visible state, not a new noun or framework.

### 4. Transfer mechanisms, not surfaces

Research a small number of relevant games or production examples. Extract the causal mechanism that
produces the desired experience, name what is unique to the reference and must not be copied, then
express the transferable part through SpaceFace's existing verbs and owners.

### 5. Diverge enough to make a real choice

Generate a varied candidate pool larger than the intended output. Consider ordinary, risky,
failure/emergency, rare, and player-transformed expressions where relevant. Record value, owner fit,
distinctness, systemic multiplication, cost, and risk separately. Do not pad the pool to satisfy a
number, and do not select a low-value idea merely because it is easy.

### 6. Select, cut, and sequence

Choose the smallest slate that advances the target experience. Identify the highest-dependency
exemplar and complete it first. Preserve replacements, cut filler, and defer any candidate whose
value depends on an unowned future framework.

### 7. Implement through the admitted owners

Characterize the baseline, make the smallest complete player-visible change, and preserve single
writers, determinism, save/Continue, accessibility, performance, and ordinary-route reachability.
Implementation and evidence stay inside the packet's exact paths and checks.

### 8. Obtain cold, evidence-bound critique

Where risk justifies it, ask a reviewer to inspect the target experience plus baseline/current
ordinary-route evidence before reading implementation detail. Test counts, file volume, effort, and
the creator's self-score are not persuasion.

Advisory dispositions are:

- `KEEP` — the premise works and should be proposed for packet integration;
- `REVISE` — one or more causal defects have a bounded repair;
- `REBUILD` — useful learning remains but the implementation model misses the premise;
- `CUT` — the candidate is redundant, incoherent, or harmful.

These are review dispositions, not program acceptance states. Name at most the highest-value causal
defects. Re-review the repair against those defects instead of generating a fresh unbounded audit.
When repeated evidence shows the premise or model is wrong, rebuild or cut rather than looping on
ornament.

### 9. Review composition before multiplication

For a family, pocket, or slice, run the accepted candidates together and inspect attention hierarchy,
role overlap, cadence, quiet contrast, density, performance, accessibility, and player opportunity.
Multiply only a proven exemplar and record which grammar is reusable versus deliberately authored.

## Optional `Nx` shorthand

`Nx` may be used by a user or packet to request approximately `N` independently reviewable
production units. It never means files, commits, candidates, recolors, or test cases. The admitted
packet decides whether `N` units are coherent, whether fewer are more truthful, and what proof each
one needs. No `Nx` result is accepted until the normal program evidence and promotion path says so.

|---|---|
| NPC life, enemy ecology, sectors, destinations | GDX world/agency axes plus the admitted Ceres or later world packet |

| Narrative, exploration, aftermath | GDX story frames plus current story/world-state owners |
| Assets, VFX, audio, UI, game feel | graphics/accessibility/performance contracts plus the owning packet |
