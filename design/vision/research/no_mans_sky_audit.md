# No Man's Sky — Technical Audit & Content Inventory

This report compiles a detailed catalog of the factions, ships, planets, and wonders populating the procedural space simulation game **No Man's Sky** (Hello Games). It traces how the game structures its database and visual assets to establish its universe depth.

---

## 1. Directory Tree & Data Loading Mechanics

No Man's Sky uses a procedural asset pipeline. Ship meshes, planetary terrain generation parameters, and faction data are defined in structured XML descriptors (packed into binary `.pak` files in retail installations).

```
[no man's sky unpacked]
  └── METADATA/
       ├── SIMULATION/
       │    ├── SOLARSYSTEM/
       │    │    └── VOXELGENERATORSETTINGS.MBIN <-- Voxel terrain generation
       │    └── ECOSYSTEM/
       │         └── CREATUREGENERATIONDATA.MBIN <-- Procedural fauna settings
       ├── REALITY/
       │    └── TABLES/
       │         ├── NMS_REALITY_GCTABLE.MBIN    <-- Factions and items table
       │         └── NMS_REALITY_GCPRODUCTTABLE.MBIN <-- Crafting products
       └── SCENE/
            └── SHIPS/
                 └── SPACESHIPDESCRIPTOR.MBIN   <-- Ship procedural attachment assemblies
```

### Procedural Assemblies
Spaceships are generated by assembling modular components (cockpits, wings, thrusters, noses) defined in scene descriptor files (`.mbin` compiled XML). The engine procedurally attaches these nodes at runtime based on the system's seed value.

## 2. Major Factions & Political Lore

The political layout features three main sentient species and various robotic/alien forces.

1. **The Gek (`faction "Gek"`)**
   * *Lore:* A species of short, amphibious merchant traders. Formerly a militaristic empire (Gek First Spawn) that conquered the outer rim, they were genetically reprogrammed by the Korvax to value trade and profit above all.
   * *Aesthetic:* Warm colors (yellows, copper), beak-like facial structures, and modular, bulky ship cockpits.
2. **The Korvax (`faction "Korvax"`)**
   * *Lore:* A robotic species sharing a collective consciousness (the Convergence). They focus on scientific exploration, technology, and study of the Sentinels.
   * *Aesthetic:* Sleek blue-and-white chrome textures, metallic faces, and symmetrical ships.
3. **The Vy'keen (`faction "Vy'keen"`)**
   * *Lore:* A warlike species of honor-bound soldiers. They wage a constant war against the Sentinels and value physical strength, honor, and combat readiness.
   * *Aesthetic:* Rugged metal, red hazard striping, and fighter ships with aggressive wing shapes.
4. **The Sentinels (`faction "Sentinels"`)**
   * *Lore:* An automated robotic force created by the universe simulation computer (Atlas) to monitor and protect planets. They attack anyone who harvests resources.
   * *Aesthetic:* Gunmetal grey surfaces with orange glowing scanner eyes.
5. **The Autophage (`faction "Autophage"`)**
   * *Lore:* A hidden faction of discarded Gek, Vy'keen, and Korvax mechanical components that have achieved consciousness. They live in hidden camps.
   * *Aesthetic:* Mismatched scrap metal plating and exposed wires.
6. **The Abyssal Horror (`faction "Abyssal"`)**
   * *Lore:* An ancient, biological entity residing in the depths of water worlds, corrupting local fauna.
   * *Aesthetic:* Pulsing flesh-like textures and single glowing eyes.
7. **Atlas (`faction "Atlas"`)**
   * *Lore:* The central artificial intelligence simulation computer that hosts the entire universe.
   * *Aesthetic:* Black, abstract geometric structures with glowing red cores.
8. **The Anomaly Guild (`faction "Anomaly"`)**
   * *Lore:* A group of travelers outside the three main species, residing in the Space Anomaly.
   * *Aesthetic:* Symmetrical white hulls and customized suits.
9. **Outlaws (`faction "Outlaws"`)**
   * *Lore:* Pirate factions operating from lawless systems, trading in contraband.
   * *Aesthetic:* Spiked hulls, solar sails, and red paint patterns.
10. **The Sentient Flora (`faction "SentientFlora"`)**
    * *Lore:* Planetary plant structures that have achieved basic consciousness.
    * *Aesthetic:* Glowing vines and defensive spore pods.

## 5. Space Wonders & Anomalies

1. **The Atlas Interface**
    * *Lore:* A giant artificial chamber hosted by the Atlas computer.
    * *Visual & Mechanics:* A black geometric obsidian structure with a floating red sphere.
2. **The Space Anomaly**
    * *Lore:* A pocket dimension station that exists outside regular space-time.
    * *Visual & Mechanics:* A massive metallic sphere. Serves as a multiplayer hub.
3. **Black Holes**
    * *Lore:* Gravitational collapses generated by the decay of the simulation.
    * *Visual & Mechanics:* Swirling gravitational singularities that warp the ship to random coordinates.
