## 1. What the best space games share

| Pillar | Exemplars | Player feeling |
|---|---|---|
| Travel grammar | Freelancer | Thrust → boost → cruise → lanes/gates; space is connected |
| Dense local space | Rebel Galaxy, Freelancer | Always something; systems feel small in a good way |
| Living economy / agency | X4, EVE (light), Endless Sky | My trade/fight changes the board |
| Ship fantasy / fit | Star Valor, ES, Elite | My build is the game |
| Combat readability | Starsector, Everspace 2 | I understand risk and intent |
| Wonder + polish | NMS (redeemed), Elite | I want to look at it |
| Signature toy | KSP build, Starsector flux, NMS scan | One verb they own |

### Common indie failure modes (SpaceFace hits several)

- Systems depth without **playable surface**  
- **Empty** procedural or oversized voids  
- Placeholder / clay visuals kill wonder  
- Checks green while **fun red**  
- UI either barren or **prose-crammed**  
- Scope creep (multiplayer, surfaces) before core loop joy

## 5. What “professional” means for SpaceFace (operational)

A stranger can:

1. Launch, undock, survive 10 minutes.  
2. Set course to a station without a wiki.  
3. Latch massline on a flyby with Flyby Focus.  
4. Read market prices and take a profitable action in one dock.  
5. Screenshot a scene that doesn’t look like a prototype **and has no sore thumbs**.  
6. Jump systems without a loading-screen slap.  
7. Die rarely; when they die, understand why and retry in seconds.  
8. Pilot without fighting the flight model for the first minute.  
9. Believe the game has its **own name, ships, and faces** — not a Freelancer mod.

If any of 1–4 or 8 fail, do not expand content scope.
