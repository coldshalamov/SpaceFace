# Top-five visual material-truth program

**Snapshot:** 2026-07-29
**Authority:** historical research companion to `VISUAL_ASSET_CATALOG.json`; not dispatch,
acceptance, priority, or a permanent lease
**Workflow:** `.grok/skills/spaceface-blender-material-truth/SKILL.md`

This packet turns the catalog's first five rows into bounded in-place remasters. It does not authorize
a new ship identity, a wholesale legacy merge, or a live promotion. Every slice preserves its
runtime role, silhouette anchors, sockets, collision, scale, and forward axis until evidence
explicitly authorizes otherwise.

## Shared acceptance rule

For every component, fiction and implementation must agree:

1. name its function, origin, substrate, manufacture, finish, interfaces, service history, expected
   optical response, and forbidden reads;
2. rebuild the earliest failed form/construction gate in editable Blender source;
3. derive physical maps from the exact authored surface rather than generated imagery or generic UV
   noise;
4. compare matched clay, neutral material, grazing-light, supported-size, and normal-route views;
5. validate exact source/release hashes, LODs, sockets, collision, texture roles, and measured cost;
6. leave Browser/Electron, performance, promotion, and independent G7 open when their real route is
   unavailable.

Generated component studies may clarify manufacture and interfaces. They remain versioned
reference-only inputs; generated pixels and generated PBR data do not ship.
