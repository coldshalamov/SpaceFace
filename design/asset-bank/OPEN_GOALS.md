## Publication versus acceptance

All bank models remain authoring-library candidates until their consuming feature registers and validates them. Bounds cover exported bind-pose LOD geometry, not arbitrary motion. Compound collision is approximate and must be checked for the intended interaction. Clear passage samples do not certify all hulls, bearings or physics routes. Keep source, export, review, publication, integration and acceptance statuses distinct.
