# Natural quality revision: two review candidates

Scope: bored_rock_collar and supported_cyst_nest only. Root visually reviewed the revised close/top comparisons and approved the models for the next review-candidate publication. This is not live-runtime visual or placement acceptance.

The collar is an engineered core excavation through a genuinely fractured rock mass, with cleaved ledges, a spalled lip, inset longitudinal tool facets and fresh cut wall faces. The former smooth annular tabletop and painted-looking black inner hoop are removed.

The cyst nest has differently sized and oriented thick overlapping skins, irregular split rims, a recessed opening through the mature skin, branched roots that enter the bodies, and muted age-related material colors. Root sections are 16-sided to remain below Forge's 35-degree smoothing threshold. Rim fibers follow the actual skin edge.

References personally rendered and inspected: tools/blender/forge/ships/place_asteroid_rock_a.py and the thick fan anatomy in tools/blender/forge/ships/borrowed_voice.py. CPU source and exported close, top, 60-degree perspective and LOD2 views were inspected. The current strict reusable-bank validator passes both models and all six GLBs.

Limitations: CPU inspection uses reference lighting, not the gameplay Look. LOD2 simplifies fine rims and strata. Collision compounds are approximate; attachment placement and clearances are not gameplay-certified. The other 34 natural models are not included in this quality revision. Source changes provide improved stem construction for future regeneration, but do not imply those other outputs have changed.

Evidence resides in .devshots/natural-quality-two/views, including supported_cyst_nest_comparison.jpg and bored_rock_collar_comparison.jpg. No GitHub write or upload was performed by this worker.
