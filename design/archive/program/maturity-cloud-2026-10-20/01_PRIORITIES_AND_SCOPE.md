# 01 · Maturity sequence and scope

## Product thesis

SpaceFace's advantage is personally manipulating mass inside a world with real work and memory.
The game is not improved merely by increasing the number of ships, currencies, sectors or menus.
A good addition makes an existing decision more expressive, readable, consequential or replayable.

The repo's October 19 report card and October 16 A-list roadmap are useful dated diagnoses, not
fresh playtests of the baseline used here. This pack does not inherit their scores as current fact.
Its source-backed corrections and hypotheses are separated in [09](09_EVIDENCE.md).

## Preserve authority and current product decisions

`ARCHITECTURE.md` owns technical contracts; `design/VISION.md` owns product emphasis;
`design/GDD_2_0.md` owns design; the build map and existing program surfaces own dispatch/status.
A request to plan expansion does not repeal release-preparation limits or authorize a new engine.
Preserve personally piloted play, the top-down Adventure camera, planar physical interactions,
existing optional racing presentation, earned momentum, and the single-writer contracts.

Do not introduce walking/interiors, multiplayer, a live-service dependency, a second Massline,
a dialogue-tree framework, bullet-sponge progression or invisible immunity to force the demo to work.
New home/crew/inspection mechanics remain staged proposals under their existing rows.

regression remains ahead of feature work. A blocked rendering hunk does not stop disjoint data,
projection or content work.

## Rank proposals without a fake precision score

For each candidate, write the player decision it improves, exposure on the ordinary route,
reuse of existing owners, likely implementation/review burden, and material uncertainty.
Prefer high-frequency friction removal and interactions between already-built systems.
Do not assign invented ROI percentages or time estimates. Source size and test count are not fun.

Reject proposals whose main benefit is another dashboard, another narrator, or more prose that
cannot change a decision. Preserve quiet atmosphere when it gives contrast, anticipation or place
identity; not every line needs a reward and not every thirty seconds needs an explosion.

## A practical definition of maturity

1. **Available:** reachable through the public route, not only a debug hook.
2. **Trustworthy:** displayed facts match canonical state; failure/retry/load do not duplicate value.
3. **Legible:** the player can explain the action, threat and consequence without reading source.
4. **Expressive:** at least one alternative action produces a materially different result.
5. **Persistent:** only appropriate consequences survive, under stable identities.
6. **Compelling:** observed play shows voluntary reuse, experimentation or replay.

The first five can receive strong engineering evidence. The sixth requires observed player behavior
and judgment. Do not manufacture a 'fun score' from event counts or award Gold automatically when
three systems share an event. Use the existing Bronze/Silver/Gold language as a review rubric,
not a mathematical proof of enjoyment.

## Scope-cut rule

Cut breadth before causal integrity: one excellent boss before eight reskins, one complete supply
chain before another producer, one fail-forward contract before a universal quest generator.
Keep future ambition in the existing backlog; do not inflate a bounded cloud packet to absorb it.
