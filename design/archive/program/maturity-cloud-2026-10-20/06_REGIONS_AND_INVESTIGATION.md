# 06 · Places worth visiting; evidence worth finding

**Parents:** 419, 423, 444, 450, 452, 464, 468, 476. Existing galaxy topology and canon remain intact.
Comparison lenses: Outer Wilds' knowledge-driven curiosity and Obra Dinn's evidence discrimination.
These are proposals for SpaceFace, not a license to copy those games' content or interface.

## Region identity contract

A featured place needs a distinctive physical relationship, a local way of making a living, a
readable danger/opportunity, an informative sound/visual signature, and a reason to return.
Do not equate a hazard circle or a colored background with all five.

Build the first pass in existing places:

| Place | Proposed focal question | Reused foundation | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Helios | Can I learn a physical verb and recognize a consequence? | 47-A opening, current contacts and station | Preserve tutorial and known public paths |
| Ceres | Can I intervene in real work rather than watch background motion? | Refinery, tender, hauler and physical cargo | Coordinate active native-work PRs; no duplicate workers |
| Tethys | What does the manifest claim, and what can I prove? | Customs/count-lane and law/claim owners | Inspection career waits behind evidence and law dependencies |
| One existing alien site | What changes when I observe or disturb this organism? | Fauna grammar, scanner and ecological state | No new species simulation or unobserved knowledge leak |

These are proposed content emphases, not assertions that every listed encounter is currently accepted.
CLOUD-02 makes arrival information locally truthful. CLOUD-04 makes observed anatomy legible.
Those are cloud-sized beginnings; full region art and encounter composition are separate work.

## The physical-investigation vertical slice

Start with one existing manifest discrepancy, not a galaxy-wide detective system.

1. **Observation:** player performs a supported weighing/towing/scan action. Record only what the
   actual owner can establish. Tension alone is not a calibrated mass measurement: acceleration,
   thrust, constraints and uncertainty matter. Prefer an existing calibrated comparison or scale
   contract; otherwise expose an anomaly, not a fictional precise tonnage.
2. **Contradiction:** retain the declared manifest and observed result with provenance, uncertainty
   and object identity. Similar-looking objects are not interchangeable evidence.
3. **Inference:** a limited existing evidence/ledger surface lets the player connect two compatible
   records. A valid relation can unlock an existing accusation or mission transition. The UI does
   not solve the entire conspiracy on behalf of the player.
4. **Consequence:** the accusation has an actual response through law, factions or story. Wrong,
   incomplete and stale evidence get specific refusals rather than free success.
5. **Return:** Continue preserves the evidence without granting it again; the resulting world fact
   remains inspectable where appropriate.

The larger board in row 444 and the inspection shift in row 452 follow this proof. Do not make a
new general evidence database; reuse the established scanner, story and loss records.

## Content-authoring workflow

For each place, write a one-page encounter card: ordinary route, visible roles, physical affordance,
what the player knows, alternative action, consequence, return hook, failure/recovery and budget.
Then trace owners and compile the smallest data/placement change. Validate navigation clearance,
identity, source depletion and ordinary reachability before commissioning additional models.

Stage landmarks at gameplay scale. Existing flight-plane rules still apply: atmosphere and distant
life must not become accidental collisions. Terrain and physics rules may differ between places,
but they must announce that difference before punishing the player.

## Acceptance

An observer can identify the place from its activity and interactions, not only a label. Scanning
changes actionable knowledge without revealing hidden positions or a complete species catalog.
The player can articulate why an evidence link is valid. Test legal unknown/false/zero values,
removed objects, revisits and save/load. A concept sheet or offline model render is not a played
region; a completed region is not permission to increase the size of the galaxy.
