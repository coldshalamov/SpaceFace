# Independent Seam: physical finite work

## Outcome and area identity

The existing independent Pallas miner and surveyor now work the real metallic field
`f_pallas_1`, rather than a visually empty pocket roughly 1,057 WU from their claimed source.
This is a bounded correction to the existing cast and industrial identity, not a new settlement,
faction, mission economy or historical claim. Stable actor identities and existing hull assets remain.
The miner releases finite ore loose; the surveyor measures the field physically and contributes to
existing shared survey knowledge. Nearby cargo workers remain context, not an invented custody chain.

The selected miner cuts one real source through `mining.applyMining`. The ordinary finite resource
record, damage, yield and pickups remain authoritative. Completion stops that cut. No clock creates
ore, cargo lots, payment, hauler handoffs or a second ledger. The target panel distinguishes cutting,
finished work, missing/depleted source, danger and available field readings. A fresh reading can
legitimately be 0% if measured before depletion. Contact consumption can remove loose pickups;
18 units released does not mean 18 units remain lying in space.

## Authority and compatibility

Exact actor/job/data/generation and resource identity checks guard work. Source replacement, loss,
bracing, berth holds, leases and stale native reads cannot authorize extraction. Reentrant mining
callbacks cannot finish a replacement job. Legitimate crew relinking resets only transient identity
bindings. Survey readings reject malformed, future and stale values.

Ordinary metal/crystal surface tags previously blocked all mining. All four mining validity,
acquisition and extraction guards now discriminate authored optical structures by nonempty
`opticStructureId`, retaining optical materials/reflections and site-anchored protections.

Continue preserves saved hull poses. Old surveyor jobs with the former miner kind remain supported.
Saved itineraries are redirected, never hulls. Observed source coordinates permit navigation while
native work readiness remains false; only an authenticated current native body authorizes cutting.
Extraction is local to the existing player-based physics residency. A worker may arrive while a
source remains dormant offscreen; this is not autonomous offscreen ore production.
