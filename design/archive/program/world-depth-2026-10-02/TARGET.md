# SpaceFace: the mature game already implied by its best ideas

## Recommendation

Build toward **a fast, inhabited, physically manipulable frontier whose routes, people and mysteries become deeper as the player's ship becomes more capable**. Adventure should offer the freedom to improvise a spectacular escape, recognize the people harmed by it, return to the changed place, and discover that familiar machinery sits on something much older. Swarm should concentrate the same physical vocabulary into a deliberately extravagant, replayable arcade main attraction.

The next expansion must create **new shapes of play**, including substantial new ships, creatures, terrain, machinery and structures. Recombining current objects is useful composition, but cannot substitute for missing physical affordances. The Anvil's Stormshift is a strong first production slice; it is not the mature target, nor a template that makes every destination a work simulator.

This is the experience target for the owner-requested complete expansion, delivered through the finite implementation packets, grounded at `c488ecc10c23ad613dc35c61c04f124a3b77723a`. It is not a fresh runtime audit, a claim that every named feature is absent, or authorization to overwrite newer work. “Missing” below means an experience family needing actualization and integration across the game, not necessarily a nonexistent implementation. Existing source-grounded studies are credited separately. New encounter arrangements and build names are proposals.

## 1. What the whole game should feel like

The mature loop is **learn a place → acquire a capability → attempt something audacious → deal with its consequences → return with new understanding**. Mining, combat, commerce, story and exploration are ways into that loop, not competing games with unrelated reward systems.

A representative session starts at a familiar berth. A repaired trader recognizes the player's hull. A routine contract would fund a new fitting, but a rumor identifies an alternative source in a derelict. The player takes the strange route, learns a useful physical fact, and steals a component during a fight they could not win conventionally. That component changes their build. On the return journey an institution acts on the theft; the player can accept the cost, improvise an escape or use an established relationship. Later they find a real scar where the encounter happened. The quieter sections make the violent minutes matter.

This follows VISION's immediate physical agency, ordinary life, specific places, consequential industry and personal ship. Depth's “galaxy keeps receipts” contributes discoverable history; it must not displace piloting with reading. A clue matters because it changes a route, target, relationship or capability. A memorable quiet object may simply remain memorable. [S1, S3]

## 2. Foundation to preserve, target to finish

The game already has much of the substrate: flightV3, Rapier dynamic physics, Massline control, physical fields and ordnance, tactical roles, cargo custody, traffic work, world sites, encounters, faction law, persistent history, authored campaign material and separate Swarm systems. Existing research specifically verifies physical traffic towing, Ceres's causal work chain, embodied Helios relief, named lane contacts and The Anvil's player-facing gravity/harvest adapter. Do not rebuild these under new labels. [S2, S10]

Species- and machine-specific procedural assemblies already exist through `faunaVisuals`, `machineVisuals` and `visualFactory`; the creature/machine art goal below is richer authored anatomy, animation and spatial behavior, not their first existence. The asset-family inspection also finds a substantial current fleet and place inventory. Raw catalog totals mix different asset types and do not measure experienced variety.

The maturity gap is the **breadth, embodiment and continuity of what those systems act upon**. A biological taxonomy is not yet an unforgettable living region; a hull catalog is not yet a set of recognizable combat relationships; an income record is not yet infrastructure the player authored. Some older plans label implemented systems as proposals, and some modern tables have stale comments. Inspect live owners before writing tickets. In particular, “add enemy telegraphs,” “add towing,” and “add planets” are too imprecise to be useful tasks.

## 3. Movement landscapes: new ways to fly, not more empty distance

Every major destination should give a skilled pilot something worth doing without a mission marker. New movement content is a first-class expansion pillar alongside enemies and story.

**Moving anchors and convoy terrain.** Give selected heavy transports, salvage barges and ring shepherd machinery usable mass, protruding tow structures and readable movement. The player can borrow momentum from a moving giant, transfer to another anchor, pass through an open truss or release into an escape corridor. The giant has its own destination; it is not a convenient grapple point spawned for the player. New assets: a very large skeletal carrier, articulated tow yoke, long loose plate assemblies, rotating industrial spindle and distinctive tug attachment hardware. Establish the visible gap and actual collider together.

**Changing passages.** A shipbreaking cradle, derelict spine and operational foundry should contain doors, shutters, split hull corridors, blast vents and moving work arms that change traversable geometry. Author multiple viable approaches: timing, displacing an obstruction, disabling a machine, or taking a longer clear route. A loose panel becomes shield, ammunition, temporary bridge of cover or a troublesome obstruction. Large authored hull sections matter here; scattering small salvage boxes cannot make the same playground.

**Flow and gravity routes.** Extend the existing Anvil, Cinder current and field vocabulary into distinct routes: ice circulating through a ring gap, a dangerous inward spiral, a vent-driven slalom, a comet-tail crossing, a paired-field saddle. These are authored planar forces with visible effects on debris and traffic. Planetary destinations should differ by flight problem, not all become scoop-and-sell jobs. Razor-Ring can be a traversal/recovery destination; a quiet forbidden limb can be an exploration destination; another planet can remain remote scenery.

**Player-shaped routes.** Later, movable anchors, a small service depot and deployable cover let the player improve an approach, stage a heist or establish a recovery corridor. Infrastructure earns its keep through changed traversal and traffic before passive income. It must be possible to see what was built and who uses it.

The memorable action is a chained self-sling through machinery, followed by using that same machinery against pursuers. Preserve manual intent, predictable mass, the ordinary Massline's near-unbreakability and the absence of magical speed on release. Form, motion, light and sound teach an affordance; another floating “INTERACT” label is not the solution. [S1, S2 §4, S4 §§1–5]

## 4. Combat and progression: builds you can recognize in motion

Take the useful part of WoW-like progression: distinct role fantasies, understandable equipment synergies, signature acquisitions, encounter-specific choices, attractive long-term goals and the pleasure of completing a build. Do not import a hotbar rotation, mandatory trinity, raid treadmill or stat inflation that makes physical improvisation irrelevant.

Four proposed example builds explain the target:

- **Wrecker:** concussion, ram protection and a strong handling rig. Turns light enemies and loose structures into impact ammunition; needs an answer to cutters and separated artillery
- **Field engineer:** attractive/repulsive geometry, controlled ordnance and positional defenses. Sets up devastating packs; must protect the setup from mobile specialists
- **Interceptor:** acceleration, precise disabling fire and an extraction rig. Intercepts a courier or takes a prize intact; cannot treat every heavy hull as disposable prey
- **Frontier salvager:** large-load handling, exposure protection and versatile repair/recovery tools. Can reach and remove things other ships cannot; combat power partly comes from what is found locally

These are fitted identities, not four exclusive character classes. A player can hybridize. Each needs an early coherent version, a midgame transforming item, and a late aspirational acquisition. Upgrades increase usable mass, placement freedom, sustained load, survivable conditions or combination potential as well as appropriate numerical strength. New equipment should visibly alter the ship or its action where the gameplay camera can convey it. The player's favored hull should acquire scars, repairs and recognizable fittings instead of being compulsorily discarded every tier. [S1 “Progression,” S2 §11]

The roster should create different problems. Light darting hulls provide ammunition and pressure; a committed rammer controls a lane; a cutter threatens a prepared tow; an anchor ship stabilizes allies; a launcher makes debris and interception relevant; a repair tender keeps a dangerous formation functioning; a carrier changes an area through launch bays and escort traffic. A capital's body, mounts, exposed machinery and shadow should become a place to fight around, not merely a larger target panel. These functions partly exist today; expand their **distinct silhouettes, combinations, authored encounters and consequence states**, rather than declaring an entirely new roster necessary.

Create bounty and campaign targets with a signature ship, escort relationship, tell and contextual objective. “Take the pressure rack intact while its thief retreats around The Anvil” tests something different from “reduce all HP.” Other families include disable-and-extract, breach-and-escape, defend-a-moving-body, dismantle-an-enforcement-system and hold-a-route-long-enough. Physical kills, ordinary gunplay and stealth preparation should all be valid tools. Enemy immunity must never be an unexplained veto on the game's central toys.

Rewards join the world: a recovered weapon has a provenance; a faction offers a particular engineering specialty; knowledge opens a dangerous salvage route; a protected worker later provides access. Finite valuable objects remain finite through theft, delivery, wreckage and reload. [S2 §6, S3 §§3–4, S4 §§2–3]

## 5. Inhabited places: institutions with bodies and recurring people

World density should become specific relationships, not uniformly busier spawn counts. Preserve Helios as calm harbor, Ceres as worked belt and Tethys as customs/trade junction. Build on the existing Vesta, Pallas and Sker identities without treating their proposed names as more authoritative than current owner direction. [S10]

**Harbor and relief.** Extend the vocabulary of passenger transport, service tenders, waiting berths, memorial craft, supply transfers and recognizable official patrols. Existing Last Light/Mercy relief already demonstrates damage, work and loss; do not clone its rescue beat. A passenger ferry or enormous tanker can give scale and normality without creating a mandatory mission.

**Ceres's changing industrial landscape.** Complete the causal survey/calving chain where live owners still need it, then make one distinctive retired ship an evolving shipbreak. Cutting its frame moves mass, changes lanes, exposes history and produces contested salvage. Build a breaker hull, cradle, cuttable structural sections and instruments whose deployed/damaged states are legible. This creates a place with a past, not another ore quota.

**Tethys's circulation and custody.** Anvil collectors, cold-service hardware and berth workers create the new flight district; customs cutters, courier traffic, impound structures and black-market approach routes connect it to the broader sector. Different people want the same physical thing for different reasons. A stolen cooling rack can be industrial necessity, evidence, contraband and unwieldy weapon without becoming four duplicate items.

**Vesta/Pallas and beyond.** Foundry feedstock, movable slag hardware and cycling machinery support dangerous combat and fabrication. Salvage tenders, containment cages and a reef-edge route support recovery, custody and exploratory play. Not every place needs production chains; race traffic, pilgrimages, an abandoned military approach, a quiet memorial and a spectacular crossing provide other rhythms.

Use the existing canonical cast before inventing replacements. Rell, Kess, Tann and Vey Senna already offer recurring local anchors. Expand relationships through behavior: a worker avoids the wreck the player made; a courier changes route; a repairer recognizes their own patch; a patrol treats the hull differently. New characters deserve specific ships, places and motives. Portraits and dialogue support this presence but cannot substitute for it. [S10]

## 6. Exploration, ecology and ancient competence

Adventure's world should gradually become harder to explain while remaining mechanically readable. The source gradient is human certainty → traces → connected ecology → ancient containment → domain threshold. Geography supplies the reality; story and equipment supply interpretation and access. A skilled reckless pilot can encounter something before understanding it. [S5, S6]

**Life before enemies.** Actualize a curated ecological ensemble: Veil Rays feeding on gradients, Wake Eels following traffic, Glassbacks grazing rock, Lantern Cysts drifting toward heat, Hull Leeches attaching, Casket Worms responding to vibration, and a rare carrier with juveniles. Their different silhouettes, movement and stimuli create choices. A ray flock should sometimes be beautiful and harmless. A Furnace Maw becomes dangerous because sustained reactor output attracts it, not because every organism is assigned hostile IFF. Predator/prey relations and migration should produce readable local changes, not an unbounded universal biology simulation.

**Colonized terrain.** Build material-specific growth with geometry and stages: nerve bundles through broken hulls, calcified collars around machinery, vent lungs, membranes across passages, cyst clusters and severed scars. Cinder Nursery, Warm Freighter and related authored sites provide starting identities. Cutting a relay changes coordination; it does not magically kill every organism. Recovering a warm component may move a biological risk with it. Leaving a carrier alive can preserve an extraordinary scene while changing the available route.

**Machine procedures.** Give Custodians, Witnesses, Auditors, Shepherds and rare Masons their own functional bodies and exact movement. A Custodian dismantles a human modification because it obstructs maintenance. A Shepherd maintains a safe corridor by suppressing coherence. An Auditor asks the player to isolate cargo or vacate a volume. A Mason's rare awakening rearranges a route and forces a human berth to evacuate. Learning a procedure becomes a form of mastery, visibly performed through space and objects. These are not reskinned factions offering reputation errands.

**Deep scale.** Gateworks beneath human gantries, quarantine pylons, null corridors, machine ossuaries, sealed vaults and immense containment rings should lead to the realization that inherited routes may be containment geometry. Canon Synthesis §3 explicitly permits a Vethari vessel at extreme scale, shadows and passage effects; use that allowance sparsely, never as a routine biological combatant or a creator-species reveal. A quiet gap or an impossible alignment can be more powerful than another boss.

Protect canon: one Vethari-linked fungus, lossy neural connection, no instant tactical evolution; Vael are human; living creators remain undefined; no normal diplomatic fungus polity. Current `alienEcology.js` explicitly says fungus colonizes dead hulls and never flies its own ship, overriding older expansive “re-flown faction” concepts for this target. Brood is a separately authorized creature family in Swarm, not a second copy of that fungus. [S5–S8]

## 7. Campaign: authored pressure, unscripted execution, real aftermath

Retain the 47-A spine and its uncomfortable institutional meaning. The B0 mass discrepancy, B1 recategorized cargo, B2 Elroy consequence, B3 acquired hull, B4 faction choice, B5 records, B6 income clearing and B7 physical Deep Reach/ledger encounter already provide a coherent arc. Expansion should make their people, objects and locations more palpable; it should not replace them with a new chosen-one war. [S9]

The playable campaign needs authored operation families around that spine: intercept an identifiable vessel, extract a particular physical record, pass a contested checkpoint, obtain a hull with history, escort someone through machinery, and enter the deep place whose operation makes its final choice possible. Designers author motives, initial positions, constraints and durable outcomes; the player supplies approach, build and improvisation. A failure should commonly produce another playable state: a prize drifting toward danger, a surviving witness, an impounded lot, a blocked route, a damaged ally or a recoverable record.

Do not make every optional disaster globally catastrophic. Choose a few consequences to embody strongly: a repaired berth reopens; a particular worker is absent; a replacement crew arrives; a wreck remains exploitable; a patrol changes its route; a former safe approach is sealed. Pair these with existing memory/ledger/news surfaces. The emotional weight should still be legible if the player skips the log.

Political resolution does not solve cosmic history. Wren's personal search and unresolved data retain their ambiguity; the fungus is not secretly responsible for human bureaucracy. Postgame should expose the accumulated state of the player's region and new capability-driven expeditions, not erase history or replace the campaign with endless inflated enemies. [S6 §10, S9]

## 8. Swarm: the physical vocabulary at its loudest

Swarm is a main attraction with its own finished identity: clear named levels, defeat distinctive threats, collect the haul, fit a visibly stronger build, tackle a harder level, bank progress and return. Its first upgrade beat should land before it tries to become a construction game. The owner-approved later Fortify layer can add real building after the arcade core is compelling. It remains single-player. [S11, S12]

Its mature content families are the Brood's dense light creatures, Machine assemblies, intelligent pilot specialists, handcrafted bosses and biomes that change flight. The authored target proposes mites/chargers/leeches/tendrils, linkers/repair drones/saws/assemblers, and bosses such as a segmented Tendril, room-sized Crusher and Gravity Engine. Build their bodies and attack geometry; do not disguise a ship roster with colored icons. Complete the established ten-boss roster through the implementation packets; refine redundant attacks rather than quietly dropping a promised boss. Keep each boss a distinct physical problem.

A Circuit through a mill, storm lattice, derelict spine, ring or event-horizon rim should feel like a journey. Objective variation and physical environmental interactions matter more than ten background palettes. Dense Brood can use a cheaper deterministic body model, but visible force, impact, throw and kill causality must remain honest. The advertised 100–400 is a source ambition to benchmark, not an unmeasured promise.

Shared technology and selected assets feed Adventure; stakes and presentation differ. Brood incursions are exceptional world events, ancient machines are not automatically the Swarm Foundry faction, and arcade celebrations do not overwrite Adventure's restraint. The latest Saucer decision is earned exclusively through Swarm, then purchasable in Adventure; currency does not cross. [S11 §11, S12 §§4–9]

## 9. The player's early, middle and late journey

**Early:** the harbor teaches confident flight, mining and a first meaningful physical interaction. Nearby work exposes cargo, law and recoverable mistakes. The first coherent fit arrives quickly enough that the player can choose how to solve a problem before being asked to optimize a spreadsheet. B0–B3 supply the personal and institutional hook. Strange life is rumor or residue, not the starter enemy pool.

**Middle:** a recognizable ship/build can undertake specialized interception, recovery, dangerous travel or industry. Faction relationships and B4–B6 put competing obligations on those capabilities. The player revisits familiar places with stronger tools, creates their first visible infrastructure and follows optional clues to frontier ecology. Breadth grows through new problems, not simply longer trips. An unusual recovered item should suggest a new approach immediately.

**Late:** the ship can carry, survive, manipulate or understand something physically inaccessible earlier. Multi-stage operations combine prior skills under genuine stakes. Human holdouts, networked ecology and exact machine procedures reshape navigation; deep revelations reframe known infrastructure. B7 resolves the human arc without solving every mystery. Postgame retains consequential places and offers demanding expeditions, powerful fitting goals and self-authored enterprises. Swarm independently provides a shorter, repeatable progression ladder and its unique crossover unlocks.

These are capability and geography bands, not fixed hour counts or mandatory completion of every profession.

## 10. Staged realization and completion tests

**Stage A: deliver one new movement ecology in human space.** Stormshift is suitable: distinctive collector, actual work cycle, movable cooling rack, functional berth, recurring workers, interception and recovery. Alongside it, improve a bounded early acquisition path so a new physical capability immediately has a place to matter. Success: the player can tell different stories in the same district, including one not centered on a job.

**Stage B: broaden play families.** Ship the Ceres structural shipbreak/changing passage and a combat target built around new hardware. Deliver early/mid build identities and representative equipment. Strengthen harbor/custody/traffic continuity. Success: three destinations produce genuinely different piloting/combat decisions; a return visit reveals a consequence.

**Stage C: cross the frontier.** Actualize a small complete food-web ensemble, a colonized route and a machine protocol operation, with new biological and machine assets. Connect research/recovery tools and human motives. Success: the player can predict a creature, infer a machine rule and exploit both without reading an instruction panel.

**Stage D: complete the dramatic reach.** Embody the remaining campaign operation gaps, deep-scale landmarks, post-choice world states and late aspirational fits. Success: B7 requires being and doing somewhere specific; the ending preserves a comprehensible history; late mastery opens possibilities rather than deleting risk.

**Parallel showcase track: mature Swarm.** Use the same production pipeline for distinct Brood/Machine silhouettes, evolving arsenals, readable bosses and biome mechanisms, with its own ladder/meta/feedback. Reuse proven technology across modes without making either wait for the other's whole roadmap.

## 11. Expanded play commitments: make the six families compound

The owner has requested the full development of this expansion, beginning with a reviewed plan PR and continuing in small production commits. The six briefs above are **connected experience families**, not six prototype demos after which work stops. [Implementation packets](IMPLEMENTATION.md) own the finite delivery sequence and detailed model roster. The commitments here sharpen what is worth making before production begins; they do not authorize a second runtime, new galaxy, on-foot mode or multiplayer.

### F1. Stormshift: momentum becomes public infrastructure

Commit the collector/rack/cradle work, recovery and theft loop, plus a linked optional stunt line: the outer sling, a moving worker's readable wake/trajectory, the cradle's generous opening and the outward escape. A normal gun-and-flight ship can exploit the line; Massline and fields add expressions. Do not award scripted acceleration or require a timed hoop challenge. The moving body must be a genuine work vessel with mass and destination.

The rack's broad face serves as cover, its edge opens a narrower firing line, and its off-center handling makes rotational control useful. A thief carrying it loses maneuverability and can choose to release it; the resulting moving prize divides the player's attention without conjuring a second loot object. Recovering the rack restores one berth slot. A later **single bounded upgrade** adds a working arm/storage saddle and a real additional delivery leg. The player gets a changed place and a recurring practical recovery stop, with finite inputs and ordinary economic ownership. This is the first construction payoff, not a universal station builder.

### F2. Ceres: dismantle a landmark, author a route

Commit one recognizable long shipbreak with **three consequential removable structural masses and two viable passages**, an open-rib breaker and a clamp cradle. Three is an authored encounter bound, not a fracture engine for every ship. A visible bracing/cut/release sequence changes which passage is clear and which mass may swing into it. A crossbeam can remain cover, be freed as a heavy projectile, or be recovered for another use. The player can shoot/cut an exposed connection, move an already freed section, or take the long way; a specialist fit expands control rather than supplying the only entrance.

One recovered structural piece can become a **fixed-site public recovery anchor** after delivery and visible installation at an existing service pocket. Its useful bulk provides cover and an anchor on a route where the player has already learned the problem. This is an alternative use of that finite piece, not sale proceeds plus a free installed duplicate. The site shows the retained piece, local traffic routes around it, and damage calls for real repair. No arbitrary free placement, self-replicating factory, or perpetual unattended hazard is needed.

The other districts retain separate verbs: Tethys manages custody and passage; Vesta throws and catches industrial mass; Pallas holds awkward salvage through a surge; Razor-Ring tests crossing geometry. Complete their bounded episodes rather than extending shipbreaking into every destination.

### F3. Combat and builds: enemies have priorities the player can exploit

Commit the carrier's visible working bay, an exposed service subsystem, and a cutter/tender escort relationship. The tender seeks an actually repairable ally; the cutter clears a threatened handling path; the carrier protects or extracts its finite prize while launches spend its actual complement. Disable the service geometry and the formation must change what it does. Destruction, disablement, theft, a recovered escaping prize and withdrawal all use the same bodies and records. At least one operation must be completable by breaking the relationship and taking the prize without exterminating the fleet.

Telegraphs must show the important decision: a cutter aligning its sweep, a tender committing to a repair approach, a launch mouth opening, or a loaded thief beginning a wide turn. Preserve ordinary Massline near-unbreakability: a specialist cutter uses its established, clearly telegraphed counterplay contract, not arbitrary ambient cable breakage. Do not fake intelligence with perfect input reads, omniscient target changes or immunity to the player's main toys.

Deliver Wrecker, field engineer, interceptor and frontier salvager as **four fitted play identities with early, transforming midgame and aspirational late acquisitions**. These are twelve acquisition beats, not necessarily twelve invented items. Trace existing fitting/economy support first and use established hulls/items wherever their combination creates the promised play. Each beat names a reachable source, a visible/action-level change and an encounter where it helps; every identity must have an honest limitation. Put a safe opportunity to try the changed capability near its acquisition. A missing mechanical role deserves a new item; a numerical recolor does not.

### F4. Living corridor: understand, redirect, preserve

Commit one bounded ensemble of Veil Rays, Hull Leeches, Casket Worms and a large carrier organism around one colonized hull passage. The quiet baseline includes non-hostile feeding or migration. Heat/power and vibration expose different responses; the player can choose a quieter approach, move an already hot physical cargo body to redirect attention, or disturb a passage on purpose. The hot-body response uses one honest stimulus contract shared with the player's heat source; it must not become an invisible quest lure or imply that the fungus pilots a ship.

A severable local growth relay changes coordination in its connected pocket without killing everything. The choice to leave the carrier intact preserves one living route/event opportunity; disrupting it changes that bounded pocket, without simulating galaxy-wide predation. A quarantined recovered component carries its identity and contamination state through custody, storage, departure and Continue. Research/recovery yields access or a useful fitting specialization through existing progression, not a parallel alien currency.

Prove two **different causal solutions** to a recovery: low-disturbance separation, and forceful extraction with a manageable aftermath. Neither requires extermination. The difference must remain visible on return, with survivors and remaining growth rather than two flags under identical scenery.

### F5. Ancient route: procedure is a physical puzzle with alternatives

Commit the Shepherd, Custodian and pylon/gantry assembly as a shared operation: an actual human obstruction occupies a maintenance volume; a suppression boundary has a gap; the player can clear the volume, restore the receiver and witness a machine resume its procedure. Its vanes, apertures and tools display those facts. The safe passage opens because the geometry or field contract changed, not because a dialogue choice set a hidden quest bit.

The direct route can also be crossed at real risk before restoration if the existing physics/canon allows it; the longer perimeter remains legible. No universal key-item lock should erase clever piloting. Restoring the boundary affects the adjacent ecology pocket locally and alters a human approach, creating a useful connection between machine knowledge, life and ordinary work. The ancient machine is neither a faction quest giver nor universally hostile.

Commit one later Mason-scale route transformation at a single authored deep site, with warning, a safe exit and a persistent alternate approach. The spectacle is enormous **changing architecture**, supported by bounded moving/collision parts and cheap distant structure. An off-frame creator explanation and routine Vethari boss are excluded. Existing campaign/deep-place canon determines placement and meaning.

### F6. Swarm: finish the arcade journey, then give building a purpose

Complete the canonical Brood, Foundry Machine and intelligent-pilot coverage, the ten authored bosses and the physically distinct Circuit biomes enumerated in the implementation program. First prove the collect-fit-fight-bank loop, then the authorized Fortify layer. Share reliable forces and asset-production methods with Adventure, while preserving Swarm's separate identity, unlock rules and currencies.

Make one environmental interaction express each biome's rule: released Mill mass, opening/closing Derelict routes, moving ring cover, biological Hive gates and real Corona shadows/relay machinery where established. Boss damage must visibly change the dangerous geometry or behavior. A severed Tendril segment, disabled Crusher piston or spent carrier bay is more valuable than an extra screen of health. Build choices should produce distinct solutions to the same boss rather than required loadout checks. Dense small enemies need readable approach silhouettes, honest impacts and bounded effects before crowd counts grow.

Fortify's structures must change defense/route decisions under the existing canonical rules and consume the mode's own resources. Do not retrofit Adventure's economy into it or imply cross-mode wealth transfer. The Saucer retains its established Swarm unlock and subsequent Adventure purchase boundary.

### Compounding consequences, bounded by specific objects

Deliver a legible material chain across the existing districts: an Anvil load enters Tethys custody, an admitted delivery can support a finite Vesta fabrication lot, and a recovered Pallas/Ceres component can repair the selected service site. These are **authored dependencies through current economy/custody owners**, not a mandatory five-stop quest, new commodity system or galaxy-wide supply simulation. Use existing commodities/recipes; add only an indispensable receiver/consumer seam. Maintain alternatives so losing one unique object does not permanently block the expansion or campaign.

Every link has an observable local result: a berth works, a queue clears, a batch is actually produced, a returned ship carries a load, an installed arm/anchor appears. A failure can produce salvage, short delivery, repair, changed route or remembered witness. It must never also count as the successful delivery. The player can interrupt, steal, recover or ignore a link; ordinary life continues within its own finite resources. Calm scenes, memorials and unrelated traffic remain valuable without being recruited into the chain.

### Graphics and performance are part of each feature

For **every model**, review outline at the actual gameplay camera, light/material response, loaded/working/damaged states, authored sockets and opening/collider agreement. Inspect its real animation in its route; fix the model rather than declaring a turntable sufficient. For **every system**, run quiet baseline, normal play, its worst authored interaction and departure/Continue with the same seeded setup. Measure frame-time distribution, spikes, draw calls, live body counts and effects where existing tools expose them. Record the relevant device/settings and before/after comparison; do not invent a universal performance pass from one fast machine.

Improve one observed cause at a time: simplify an overbuilt collision proxy, share geometry/materials, instance repeated small bodies, pool burst effects, reduce avoidable allocations, and use bounded near-field authority plus coherent distant state. Preserve visible openings, useful silhouette, honest contact and authored default quality. Re-run the same comparison and the other affected families after each optimization. A visually attractive but unreachable object, or a fast scene stripped of its promised bodies, fails completion. The detailed model/system acceptance inventory lives in [ASSET_FAMILIES](ASSET_FAMILIES.md) and [IMPLEMENTATION](IMPLEMENTATION.md).

## 12. What must not grow

Do not grow the map faster than its destinations; add disconnected currencies, menus or minigames; make every planet a factory; turn recurring people into infinite emergency generators; homogenize early space with late aliens; explain every mystery; require multiplayer; replace physical decisions with damage inflation; or make the world permanently tidy after every encounter. Also do not reduce ambition to avoid making assets.

The finished target is not every document implemented literally. It is **enough distinctive physical content, connected across a complete progression and campaign, that the player keeps discovering new uses for a universe they increasingly recognize**.
