# Art provenance and use

The 20 PNG plates, 20 SVG orthographic studies and 20 GLB form blockouts were generated programmatically in this session from original geometry and the written concept briefs. They use no downloaded third-party art, franchise assets or external model library. They are procedural concept renders, not diffusion-painted production concept art and not screenshots from SpaceFace.

Two image-generator attempts produced unrelated named-concept posters instead of these designs. They failed the brief-consistency check and are deliberately excluded. Do not treat their names, text or pictured “ZIP” button as a delivered asset or game canon.

The package's correct art is `art/01_latch_nine.png` through `art/20_hundred_hand_mile.png`, indexed in `data/asset_manifest.json`. The renderer and concept data are included in `tools/` for reproducibility. Blockouts are deliberately lower-detail than the proposed final triangle targets. They have no approved animation, collision or material pipeline and must be rebuilt through Forge.

The model recipes and collision/behavior contracts are authoritative over approximate visuals. A future concept artist can refine these forms while preserving silhouette, negative space, pivots, sockets and gameplay function. No font files are included. Use an available system sans-serif or pass local font paths when regenerating.
