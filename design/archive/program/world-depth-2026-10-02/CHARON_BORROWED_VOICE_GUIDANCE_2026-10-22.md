# Charon Borrowed Voice recovery guidance

## Player outcome
The first deliberate R2 scan explains the three original housings, antenna alignment, a continuous 1.2-second clear signal, and scanning within 110 m. Full recovered canonical words stay visible beside physical guidance. Truly lost original sources and receiver are distinguished from temporarily absent or unready bodies; recovered words survive housing loss. Quarantine cannot resume recovery; archival can add evidence but not another payment. Terminal and unavailable states dismiss neutrally, and out-of-site scans retain evidence without offering inadmissible dispositions.

The projection is bound to the existing three sources and existing signal authority. No bindings are acquired, entity-list searches, rays, clocks, entities, or persistence fields added. Original body, fragment, threshold, payout, and disposition writers remain unchanged. The existing 5 Hz signal remains authoritative.
