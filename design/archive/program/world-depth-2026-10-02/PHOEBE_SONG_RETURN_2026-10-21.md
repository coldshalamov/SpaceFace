## Scope and established world

This connected increment extends the existing Shard Sphere investigation and Echo Shrine mission, not a new quest authority or destination. The four fragments come from repeated eligible scanner pulses at the same actual Sphere, not four invented sources. The shrine tender and resonance listener work near the shrine; rim prospector and core hauler work the north rim; Nim retains the research courier role. ## Player experience

- First physical discovery retains the existing lore plate. Eligible scans recover the existing ordered fragment lines.

- At four notes, the same fourth-fragment voice event and persistent plate explain Echo Shrine, the actual Vael standing +150 requirement, mission-board acceptance and close return scan. Rejected voice does not create new knowledge or a heard flag.
- Accepted reconstruction asks for the real physical return pulse. Existing mission and scanner owners still decide completion and payment.
- Filed reconstruction retains the original unfinished-phrase artifact and explicitly disallows a second reconstruction payment.

## Demonstrated persistence defect and bounded repair

The scanner retains a bounded contact history. Previously a partial one-, two- or three-note Song reading could be evicted by unrelated newer contacts while flavor presentation receipts still suppressed replay. The repair protects only the exact stable Phoebe archive reading with its authored sector/source identity and finite positive count in the existing prune owner. It creates no save field and does not mark a source investigated, completed or tracked. Ordinary contact pressure still yields 64 records; inherited behavior when existing protected completed/tracked rows fill the cap is unchanged.

An older four-note save may already lack its scanner row but retain the validated earned mission-board offer. Read-only progress uses that existing authority. Already-evicted partial counts are not safely reconstructible and are not fabricated. No flavor-queue receipt is treated as proof of recovered notes.
