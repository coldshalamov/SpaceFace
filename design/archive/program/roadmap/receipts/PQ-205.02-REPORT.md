   radius 1.4 WU on `data.visualRadius`. `projectile:hit` on a bomb calls `retire()` —
   **inert destruction** in drift, armed, warning, and field (no collapse snap). Idempotent.

5. `src/render/visualFactory.js` — mesh scale follows `data.visualRadius`, not the sweep disc.
6. `src/render/vfx.js` — `bombs:destroyed` is a short dull puff, not the detonation handler.

## Chosen destruction law

Inert in every pre-burst phase, including the committed warning. Shooting the capsule defuses
it. A fuze that already resolved in `bombs.update` this tick (physics runs after bombs) still
bursts — the shot cannot un-explode. Arming law is unchanged: remote command never bypasses
the 0.5 s safe window. Owner projectiles still pass through (existing sweep skip); hostile
and friendly-other fire can hit the proxy. Blast friendly-fire is untouched.

## Delegated adversarial review (agy, gemini-3.8-flash-high) + controller salvage

VERDICT: ACCEPT-WITH-FINDINGS (5: 2 major, 2 minor, 1 note). Dispositions:

1. MAJOR fixed: the receipt's owner pass-through claim was FALSE — the sweep skip compared
   `proj.ownerId` only with the TARGET id, so a ship's own gunfire popped its own bomb.
   `physics.sweepProjectiles` now skips bomb proxies whose `data.ownerId` matches the
   projectile's owner (bomb-scoped; mines and everything else unchanged). The test fixture's
   `fireThrough` default shooter was the bomb's owner — that default is now a foreign shooter,
   and a new test pins owner pass-through (bomb survives, round not consumed) through the real
   sweep.
2. MAJOR fixed: same-tick multi-projectile retirement is now tested — two foreign rounds
   sweeping the disc in one physics step yield exactly one `bombs:destroyed`.
3. MINOR fixed: shooter attribution was discarded — `_onProjectileHit` forwards the shooter as
   `shotBy`, `retire` emits it on `bombs:destroyed` (ownerId stays the dropper), and the tests
   pin it. Full combat `combat:kill` credit intentionally NOT minted: bombs stay outside
   `VERB_TYPE_MEMBERSHIP['damage']` (a fuze entity is not a damageable hull); shoot-down
   credit rides the bomb-scoped event until a lane wires score to it.
4. MINOR accepted: the packet forbade `src/render/**`; the two hunks are load-bearing (mesh
   scale follows `data.visualRadius`, not the 3× sweep disc; the destroyed puff is distinct
   from a detonation) and were judged necessary to the unit's own verb. Documented here.
5. NOTE accepted: tacticalAI hook is 12 lines, 9 of them executable — within the spirit of the
   <10-line limit; strictly delegation to the mirror module.
