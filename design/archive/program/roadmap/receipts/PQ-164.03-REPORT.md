## What changed

- `src/systems/gamepad.js`: exports `computeHapticFrame` (pure 0..1 frame from momentum, line
  load, boost and slam; reduce-motion returns an all-zero disabled frame). `createGamepad` latches
  the strongest recent player-involved `physics:impact` / `combat:collisionConsequence` impulse and
  decays it on `state.tick` (never wall time), then each tick resolves the frame from player speed,
  `state.player.tether.load` and held boost and drives `vibrationActuator.playEffect('dual-rumble')`
  (or `hapticActuators[0].pulse`), resetting the motors when the frame goes quiet. Off by default
  under `settings.video.motionReduce`; a `controls.gamepad.haptics === false` opt-out is honored.
