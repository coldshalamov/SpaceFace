## Status: INTEGRATED

The capture fork's two rails and rear arrestor are real static colliders. A refused
155 WU/s crossing is arrested by the machine's steel at the custody-head plane
instead of passing through it; the load stays alive and a later ≤100 WU/s approach
still settles into custody. Player structural give (PQ-137.11) untouched; the knock
ruling's runtime guard green.

## Collider scheme

- `BREAKAWAY_FORK_COLLIDERS` in `src/data/heistFacilities.js`, derived from
  `BREAKAWAY_CAPTURE_FORK` and reconciled with the authored GLB
  (`place_breakaway_fork.glb`, bounds x −3.5..89.5, z ±37.5):
  - Rails: 75×10 WU capsules, long axis along the inward normal, centres at
    axial 36 / lateral ±32 → inner face at |z| = 27 = authored `halfWidth`.
  - Arrestor: 74×10 WU capsule across the bay, leading face at
    `depth + rearClearanceWu` = 76 = the custody head's leading face.
- `src/systems/heistFacilities.js`: `_materializeForkColliders` spawns the three
  bodies in the same block as the fork visual (same `projectBreakawayForkMouth`
  projection), records ids on the facility record, live-id → `_findOwnedEntity`
  reuse on rematerialize (no duplicates/orphans), removal in
  `_dematerializeSector`, dead-id clearing in `_onEntityDestroyed`.
- Static bodies (`dynamic:false`, `material:'station'`, `shape:'capsule'`,
  `radius:1` + `data.proportions` absolute WU — verified against
  `buildCraftCapsuleColliderDesc`/`resolveCraftProportions`).
- `collisionMask: Masks.PAYLOAD` + `data.payloadCustodyOnly: true` retained as
  receipt semantics only: Rapier contacts are not mask-filtered (empirically
  established by worker probes); player safety is the structural-give layer.
