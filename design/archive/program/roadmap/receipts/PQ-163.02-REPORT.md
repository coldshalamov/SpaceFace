## The three verbs

1. **Boost** — hold boost. Completes on a rising boost edge (`input.boost`, `flags.boosting`, or `ship:boostStart`). Range rung: BOOST KEEPS THE SPEED.
2. **Draw-to-fly (the stroke)** — a drawn path with at least two points, or a path marked drawing. Range rung: DRAW THE STROKE.
3. **The well** — `fields:deployed` with `kind: 'well'`. A light scrap body is staged so the pull has a reason. Range rung: THE WELL PULLS LIGHT.
