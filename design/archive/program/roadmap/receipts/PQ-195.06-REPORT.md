## Status: INTEGRATED

A genuinely destroyed SP-07 assembly now leaves exactly one thing to do: its
wreck, recorded by the ordinary aftermath owner at the position the assembly
actually died, carrying a rotor's worth of scrap and electronics — salvageable
through the shipped scanner/salvage path. Never a second full reward, never
the original payload resurrected, and never more than once.

## Done-when numbers

| Claim | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Offered once per destruction | Deterministic `(sector, victim)` markerId — no tick/killer — so a duplicate destroy callback or a second direct offer converges through `rememberMarker`'s own dedupe; durable `record.recoveryWreckMarkerId` is the record-level bound |
| Reload never duplicates | Marker serializes inside `state.aftermathWrecks.bySector`; record flag is durable; save→load keeps exactly one marker and rematerializes exactly one wreck (mirrored the real `save:restoring` → `save:loaded` sequence) |
| Never a second full reward | Authored `BREAKAWAY_WRECK_RECOVERY.salvagePool` = `{scrap:4, electronics:2}` — commodity salvage by hand, not a credit payout |
| Never a resurrection | Wreck is a distinct `type:'wreck'` entity bound to the marker (`provenance.markerId`); zero live `payload` entities with the SP-07 stableId after destruction |
| Existing ownership | Marker → `aftermathWreck:recorded` → headline → `_spawnForSector` — the stock path; wreck reads through the shipped scanner/salvage flow |
| Only genuine destruction | Sector-exit absence, expiry, and abandonment record no marker; the Capsule Run keeps its "nothing left to sell" semantics |

## Implementation notes

- `heistMissionRuntime._offerWreckRecovery` fires at settlement step 3d only
  when `outcome === 'payload_destroyed'` on the Third Shift variant.
- `drive()` now tracks `capsuleLastPos/Vel/Mass` every live tick, so the wreck
  lands where — and how — the assembly died even when the `entity:destroyed`
  callback finds the body already swept (the common case: `inMap === false`
  at event time).
- `aftermathWrecks.offerRecoveryWreck` is a new public seam that keeps
  `SHIPLIKE_TYPES` closed: the victim is explicitly offered, not scanned.
- The recovery wreck is `missionPinned`: far-actor shelving would lean away
  `provenance`/`salvagePool`/`markerId` and silently destroy the opportunity.
  One pinned wreck per destruction, bounded by the one-marker guarantee —
  verified against `tickFarActors`' `virtualize` kills in-test.
- Debug finding worth keeping: `receipt.proof` does NOT carry the candidate's
  proof — it lives in the candidate journal. Death position is sourced from
  the record's tracked live state, not the receipt.
