# PQ-177.01 — Charts with a forecast cone

```text
STATUS DONE

WHAT I FOUND     The market chart drew the forecast as a solid fill down to the floor.
                 That shape was not a band, and it did not say how often the later price
                 would land inside it. A fresh listing's past was backfilled from the
                 formula but looked the same as a price the exchange had actually sampled.
                 A survey packet and the live berth quote did not wear different words.
                 The sale line could show one unit times quantity, and it did not subtract
                 travel or operating cost.

WHAT I CHANGED   predictPriceCurve still draws the "if stock holds" mid. Around it, the
                 band runs from that mid to the price stock drift would reach (recovery,
                 regional pressure, and mods already on the listing), plus or minus the
                 wave amplitude times the base price. The chart says 99%. That rate is
                 the coverage, not a target: a shortage on another seed left one listing
                 just outside a four-minute haul (179/180). These two held-out seeds
                 contained every arrival.
                 The backfilled past is marked "Modelled past". A later tick is "Observed".
                 The berth you are standing in reads "fresh quote". A survey older than
                 ten minutes reads "stale survey".
                 The sale line quotes the whole batch after slippage, then subtracts
                 travel and operating cost. At this counter both of those are zero:
                 you are already berthed, and settling does not charge machine upkeep.

THE NUMBERS      seed 17701011 chart 180/180 haul 180/180 stated 0.99
                 seed 17701012 chart 180/180 haul 180/180 stated 0.99
                 chart arrival is the last step of the drawn cone (2 minutes).
                 haul arrival is the economy's reference haul (4 minutes).
                 scenario | test/pq-177-01-forecast-cone.test.mjs

NOT TOUCHED      Prices, taxes, and yields were not retuned. Saves still store history
                 as time/mid pairs. The live screen is src/ui/station/screens/market.js;
                 there is no src/ui/screens/market.js.
```
