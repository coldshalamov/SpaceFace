IMPLEMENTED (partial) PQ-176.03 — the fit screen's four handling bars read the profile the ship actually flies, and the screen states the fight speed, travel ceiling and reversal time it predicts from that profile.

DONE WHEN (verbatim): Energy draw, heat and cooling as sustained-fire margin; the screen predicts turn radius, reversal time, top speed and tow class before commit; every module has a one-line sentence. Blind test: a player predicts handling from the screen and is right within 20 % on the bench.

WHAT I FOUND — The four bars (Agility · Inertia · Top speed · Brake) read the compatibility flight model (`derived.flightModel`: angularAccel / inertia / maxSpeed / angularBrake). The default route flies the V3 kernel over `derived.propulsion` (AGENTS.md §5), and since PQ-176.00 / PQ-176.01 that is the profile the drive, the thruster bay and the mass law move. So on the live Shipworks screen (Helios, 1280x800, 2026-09-12) the Hitch showed "top speed 145" while the ship it flies fights at 95 and travels at 439, and loading 200 t of ore into the hold — which PQ-176.00 makes double the reversal time — moved no bar at all. The old check (`check:handling-profile`) pinned that: "axes stay tied to the shipped flightModel fields".

WHAT I CHANGED — `src/ui/panels/handlingProfile.js` reads the live profile: Agility = `propulsion.yawAccel`, Inertia = the operational mass the kernel integrates (hull + modules + cargo), Top speed = `propulsion.travelCeiling`, Brake = the family's own brake key (`reverseAccel` / `maxBrakeAccel` / `rcsReverseAccel`), with the compatibility model only as a fallback for a hull that has no profile. Each axis names its live source. The profile also carries `predictions` (fight speed, travel ceiling, reversal time = 2 × fight speed / reverse thrust, mass load factor) for the sentences. The check moved with it and now also proves the fit moves the bars (200 t aboard raises Inertia and lowers Agility) and prints the roster's live travel order.

WHAT YOU WILL FEEL — Fill the hold and the Agility bar drops and Inertia rises before you undock; fit a bigger drive and Top speed moves. What the screen says is what the stick does. Not yet: energy/heat as a sustained-fire margin, a one-line sentence on every module, and a turn-radius prediction (the profile's `maxYawRate` is there; the sentence is not written).
