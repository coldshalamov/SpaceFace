the long bright snake-like history is intentional, and the problem is the disconnected appearance of
the jet and history. Their visual unity is the first improvement in `build_map.md` §13.1. They retain
separate coordinate frames and owners; no historical position may be invented to conceal a seam.

**Intended improvement:** give future effect work a reproducible style and an actionable quality
plan: continuous propulsion, attributable impacts, distinct protection/influence/work signals,
and material-specific aftermath. **Deliberate tradeoff:** more per-class authoring and a short local
appearance handoff instead of a universal effect recipe. No default-quality cut, shader simplification,
global bloom adjustment or shortening/dimming of the flight snake was made in this leaf.

`three.quarks` and its `quarks.core` dependency through the shared browser/Electron entrypoint. The
new renderer/presenter/library installation is foreign work and was preserved. Production bundling
