## Verdict

REVISE the exact Wreck Cathedral source/release candidate. Do not revert it: the split capital-hull
premise, huge negative-space rupture, traversable cavity, asymmetry, three-engine stern, Marker,
component sockets, and monumental runtime scale are valuable authored work. The candidate already
reads as a broken capital structure and its normal-route presence passes. It nevertheless retains
whole-asset P1 defects at the current visual standard.

### G1 — FAIL, whole asset

The broad split-hull silhouette and rupture are strong. Inside that silhouette, both halves are
dominated by long rectangular slabs, box stacks, and uniformly thin bar families. At gameplay
distance this reads more like a separated construction-yard blockout than a capital hull whose armor,
frames, pressure volumes, propulsion, service paths, and rupture expose a coherent prior vessel.
Forward/propulsion direction is readable, but manufacturing and scale hierarchy are not.

### G2 — FAIL, whole asset

The cavity provides real negative space, yet the dominant exposed construction consists of repeated
straight bars and plates with weak roots, brackets, section changes, thickness, and fracture
transitions. Several rupture members and panels look suspended rather than torn from identifiable
frames or shells. The huge bow wedges and side blocks remain polished primitives; close evidence does
not establish finished joints or serviceable assemblies across the dominant views.

### G4 — FAIL, whole asset

Most supported views allocate nearly all visible area to one mottled grey/blue response. Copper,
blue accent, dark interior, and emissive zones exist, but they do not separate armor, exposed alloy,
mechanical structure, heat-affected propulsion, fracture, and maintained/failed systems at the
player camera. Neutral renders read as clay/concrete-like slabs; the production black-space path
collapses the same dominant surfaces to very dark brown/black. Texture/channel existence is retained
as technical evidence, not accepted substance.

## Supported evidence and dominant-zone coverage

Original-resolution inspection covered:

- source neutral close three-quarter, gameplay-distance, cavity, and propulsion views;
- LOD0 silhouette and wireframe three-quarter/cavity views;
- base-color and roughness isolation;
- accepted Browser and source-Electron far/default/close/arrival route views.

Dominant reviewed zones were the stern hull half and engine bells, forward hull half and bow wedges,
central rupture, exposed rib/rail families, side armor blocks, cavity frame, bridge/crown, Marker,
and the scattered rupture members. No dominant inherited/retained zone was excluded from the
whole-asset verdict. No generated reference was used.

## Repair acceptance direction

Preserve the exact asset/place identity, overall envelope, two-half silhouette, cavity clearance,
LOD ordering, sockets/markers, coordinate reservation, collision/component proxies, and the useful
existing source. Re-author the dominant hull shells and rupture construction so primary plates have
manufactured sections and thickness; exposed members have rooted interfaces and fracture logic; the
macro/meso/micro hierarchy supports capital scale; and armor, exposed frame/alloy, propulsion,
service, damage, and emissive zones remain materially distinct in both neutral and production-dark
lighting. The revised exact candidate requires fresh source evidence, targeted Browser/Electron
game-camera capture, and causal whole-asset review.

No asset, runtime, or evidence file was mutated by this review. H3 performance remains separate.
