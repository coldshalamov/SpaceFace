# PQ-160.00 — Ring buffer and replay (receipt)

DONE PQ-160.00 — A 30 s ring buffer now holds the window's snapshots and inputs, replaying that
tape reproduces the live run hash-for-hash at fixed seed 16000, and a Replay control opens from the
pause sheet.

WHAT I FOUND — The repo already had deterministic snapshot and applied-input capture primitives
(`simSnapshot.js`, `inputCommandSnapshot.js`) and a lab differential-replay verifier, but no bounded
history of them and no player route to any replay surface; the pause "Photo" mode only hides the
HUD and no free photo camera exists to drive.

WHAT I CHANGED — Added a fixed-size, reused 30 s snapshot ring and a matching applied-input history
ring, a lab record/replay verifier that re-runs the recorded tape and compares every tick's hash to
the live run, and a Replay action on pause that opens a replay surface using the existing
HUD-hidden photo presentation.

WHAT YOU WILL FEEL — Pause now offers a Replay word that opens over the paused game with the HUD
hidden, showing the last recorded window and playing its timeline back. The replay is the
deterministic sim re-run from the tape, not a video file.
