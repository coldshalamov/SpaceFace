## Hypothesis

When a hull is gravity-marked, `couplingScale` multiplied base by 1.9 then clipped to `markedCap` 0.95 and 1.0, so a Hornet medium stuck at 0.95 instead of 1.5 and a Warden heavy stayed at 0.152. Changing the mark to 3.0, raising `markedCap` to 3.0, and dropping the 1.0 clip moved well-golf couple from 1.9× to 3.0× and the marked Hornet's 1 s pull from 1.60 to 1.98 hull-radii.
