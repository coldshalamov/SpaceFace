DONE PQ-176.02 — mounts gate by size and type; fixed vs turret. A hardpoint is a turret ring or a fixed mount, a weapon is an aimed gun, a turret, a launcher or a spinal gun, the ring carries only guns and turrets, and an aimed gun on the ring aims itself at three-quarters of its output. The fit screen refuses an illegal mount in a sentence, on the route.

DONE WHEN (verbatim): S/M/L mounts with type classes; fixed hardpoints get more output than turrets so aim skill is paid. Fit screen refuses illegal mounts with a sentence; a fixed-mount build out-damages its turret twin by the authored margin.

WHAT I FOUND — Sizes existed (S/M/L on slots and weapons) and so did facings (`front|left|right|rear|turret` on hull hardpoints), but the fit rule was size-and-type only: a Missile Rack rode a turret ring, a Railgun rode a turret ring, and a gun on the ring auto-aimed at the target for free — the weapons system already lead-solves any `facing: turret` mount at the selected target while a fixed mount fires where the pilot aims. So the ring was strictly better than a fixed hardpoint and nothing about a weapon's kind mattered to the hull. Three hulls carry a ring (Hornet slot 2, Ranger slot 2, and the Ironback's only weapon slot). A sweep of every shipped fit (new game, all twelve role-lattice kits, all eight combat-lab packages, the two 47-A scene hulls) found aimed guns on rings in three places (the Hornet lattice kit, `web_weaver`, `physics_toolkit`) and never a launcher or a spinal gun on one — so the law below breaks no authored fit.

WHAT I CHANGED —
- `src/data/weapons.js`: every weapon has a `mount` class (`gun` / `turret` / `launcher` / `spinal`; missing → derived from `tracking`), `HARDPOINT_ACCEPTS` says a ring carries guns and turrets and a fixed hardpoint carries everything, and `TURRET_RING_OUTPUT = 0.75` is the authored margin. Authored explicitly: Flak/PD Turret = turret; Missile Rack, Nestbreaker Rack, Torpedo, Vector Mine = launcher; Railgun, Siege Lance = spinal.
- `src/systems/ships.js`: the one fit rule (`fits`) now refuses by mount class after size; `mountRefusal` / `fitRefusalText` are the sentences; `makeWeaponRuntime` multiplies dmg, splash, knock (`impulsePerHit`) and tumble by the mount output (an aimed gun on a ring ×0.75, everything else ×1; a concussion cannon on the ring shoves a third less too) and carries `mountClass` / `hardpointClass` / `mountOutput`; `buildWeaponList` never fires an illegal mount; `reconcileIllegalFittings` runs on `save:loaded` and moves a now-illegal fitting to inventory with its sentence. The preset path and the fit intent speak the same sentence.
- `src/ui/station/outfittingGuidance.js`: "has a slot" uses the one fit rule (a launcher on a ring-only hull has no slot, not a full one).
- `src/ui/station/screens/shipworks.js`: the chooser lists right-size weapons the mount refuses as locked rows with the sentence and a "Won't mount" mark; a ring slot says its law once above the list; the kicker names the hardpoint kind; every DPS shown on a row is the DPS on THIS mount (a gun on the ring shows 75 %); the fit line adds "The ring aims it for you at 75 % output."; the hardpoint pin reads "OPEN / M / RING".
- `styles/station.css`: found in passing on the route — with the chooser open, grid auto-placement slid the whole hull stage into the narrow rail column (nameplate, gauges, systems board and stats piled up at 360 px on Intel). The chooser now takes the rail's cell and the stage keeps its width.
- Left alone on purpose: `combat.js resolveEnemyWeapon` (enemy archetype specs are not fittings; the boss `turret: true` flag stays a spec flag).

WHAT YOU WILL FEEL — On a Hornet, the third weapon mount reads "OPEN / M / RING". Open it and the screen says: "The ring aims for you. An aimed gun keeps 75 % of its output here; launchers and spinal guns need a fixed hardpoint." A Pulse Laser S shows 33 dps there and 44 dps on a front mount; the Missile Rack, Vector Mine and Railgun sit at the bottom of the list marked Won't mount, each with its sentence. Put the gun on the ring and it fights for you; put it on the nose and it hits harder because you aimed it.

FOUND IN PASSING (data, not this leaf) — A gun on a ring bears within ±90° of the nose (`gimbalArc = π`, checked as `arc/2` in weapons.js), while a player fixed mount gimbals the full circle (`PLAYER_GIMBAL_ARC`). So for the player the ring is narrower AND cheaper; its value is that it fires at the selected target without being aimed. If the hull owners want the ring to be omnidirectional, `turretArcDeg` on the hardpoint is the authored knob (no hull sets it today).

THE FEEL — The hull finally has an opinion about what goes where, and the opinion is written on the screen before you pay: a ring is convenience you buy with a third of your damage; a fixed hardpoint is damage you buy with your aim.
