Two delegated reviewers in parallel (queue internals; integration/leaf-fit). Round findings and
their dispositions:

- Unguarded `priorityOf`/callback throws could strand entries and recurse through lane exits —
  guarded, and every entry resolves before user callbacks run.
- Compile-failure bookkeeping mislabeled already-readied entries and could double-report or

- An ambient entry crossing onto the glass mid-link waited out the whole link — escalation polls

- **Batch visibility coupling (the load-bearing finding):** the original merged-batch compile
  detached roots into a staging group and held every `pipelinesPending` mark for the batch's
  slowest link — the geometry latch cleared but the roots stayed invisible. Fixed by per-root
  deadline compiles; a source pin forbids re-wiring the batch variant.
- Disposal counter lacked the live-screen gate — added; a source pin locks it.
- `isUrgent` radius could diverge from the gauge on non-HLOD roots — now shares
  `entityVisualCullRadius`, the same value the gauge's `world.radii[slot]` holds.

## Tradeoffs and boundaries recorded

- The 0.25 s bound is on the `geometryPending` latch — geometry residency — which is the defect
  the leaf names (un-uploaded buffers paying inside presented frames). A root whose *program* is
  genuinely new still waits on its own link under `pipelinesPending`: drawing an unlinked
  program blocks the presented frame, which is the worse defect the sibling leaf forbids. In
  practice almost every on-glass arrival reuses an already-linked program, so residency-clear
  and show-time coincide.
- The gauge measures wall-clock age on presented frames; a global main-thread freeze (foreign
  hitch work) inflates it like any other stall — the probe records `maxRafGapMs` so a frozen
  page cannot masquerade as a slow queue.
- The gauge and `isUrgent` deliberately share the renderer's live cull bounds — the leaf's
  counter is defined on the renderer's own glass, so the probe is self-referential by design;
  the predicates are kept textually identical so they cannot drift.
