## Seed 15900 measurement

`node --test test/pq-159-00-impact-kick.test.mjs` prints:

```text
[seed 15900] exchanged momentum dp=2124.0 mass·wu/s; table kick=1.850 wu; peak applied offset=1.833 wu
```

The exchange is produced by the real physics authority (`createGameState(15900)` +
`physics.resolvePair`, mass-20 hull at 90 wu/s into a mass-220 asteroid) and consumed by the live
feel host wired to the REAL chase camera (`createChaseCamera` under the established headless
window stub). The applied offset reaches 99% of the authored impulse inside the bounded rise,
kicks −x (recoil, away from the contact), and returns the frame to <0.02 wu with `c.focus`
bit-identical to its pre-impact value — the kick cannot pollute the damped follow.

Rate limiting, measured: a same-tick duplicate receipt and a +8% grind step cannot re-kick inside
the cooldown; a >1.5× escalation does; after cooldown an ordinary contact answers again (1 → 1 →
2 → 3 kick calls in the test sequence).

Reduce motion, measured: identical seeded exchange with `settings.video.motionReduce` produces
zero `impactKick` calls, zero trauma, zero hitstop (`timeScale` stays 1), and the live camera's
`kickOffset` reads exactly 0 wu; a kick forced straight at `camera.impactKick` also no-ops — the
gate holds at the queue, the resolver, AND the controller.
