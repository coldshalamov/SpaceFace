```text
DONE  PQ-171.01 — telemetry counts encounter shapes per hour; the check is green on the default route.

WHAT I FOUND
PQ-171.00 already stamps every authored encounter with situation × place × twist × actor
(49 modules, 46 unique catalog keys). Nothing counted those shapes over time.
check:content:repetition did not exist. Variety could still only be argued by file count.

A 10-hour planner walk on seed 17101, before the meter, showed the gap in player units:
Ceres (default workplace) scheduled 171 beats / 14 live grammar keys. Hottest shape
patrol|border_checkpoint|none|faction_scn at 3.40/h (peak hour 7). Helios 3.00/h.

file, same twist and actor, one place. Measuring trader_run as 4.50/h on Ceres would have
been counting files. Splitting by the zone the beat lands in is the combination the packet
asked for.

A 10-hour director-only soak (step 1s, referee kills) only fired 5 beats then starved on
spawn-budget / pacing in that thin harness. Planned shapes over 10 hours of sim time are
the honest stand-in: they are the storyteller's schedule, not a catalog census.

1. Encounter script fire now records a grammar-shape sighting on state.encounterShapeMeter

   and a green deterministic default-route run.
4. package.json gained check:content:repetition only.
```

## Honesty gaps

- The gate uses the director **plan**, not every fire-time gate. Planned ≥ fired. A shape can be over-scheduled and still fizzle on cargo, proximity, or spawn cap.
- Default route is **Ceres Belt** (first real content sector), not a 10-hour tour of Sker. Sker's 6.50/h mining-belt trader would fail this budget if it were the gated sector.
- Live telemetry wraps `ENCOUNTER_SCRIPTS.fire` only. Depth E1 self-registered runtimes are counted in the planner stand-in when they schedule; they do not increment the meter on their own runtime path.
- Peak hour on Ceres is 7 for the Concord checkpoint patrol. The fail rule is **average over 10 hours**, matching "over a 10-hour sim." A single stuffed hour does not fail.
- No `check:baseline` link was added (not in the write set). The npm script exists for the named check.
