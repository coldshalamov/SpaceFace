## Status: INTEGRATED

While a Third Shift run is live, someone else wants the assembly. One bounded
pressure element — at most two light pursuing hulls plus one optional
tether-control specialist — spawns once the moment the SP-07 is physically
loose, on its own bearings around the load, and commits an intelligible
ATTACK_RUN at the tug anchored on the assembly. The ordinary spawn-budget
arbiter admits it or it doesn't exist; the existing tactical owner flies it and
its own doctrine owns abandoning a bad attack.

## Done-when numbers

| Claim | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Committed, intelligible run | Every spawned hull carries `kind: 'attack_run'`, `targetId` = the tug, `anchor` = the assembly position (proven < 1e-6 WU off the live load) — an authoritative assignment the doctrine honors, not a loiter flag |
| Actually closes / can be crossed | Under the real Rapier owner and the real tactical stack, at least one raider reaches nonzero velocity and measurably closes the distance to the tug over 6 s of sim — the player can put the spindle on its line either way |

| No decorative traffic | Spawned exactly once at launch, on the live assembly only — an unlaunched row and the Capsule Run (same arbiter, different variant) spawn nothing |
| Released on every exit | Settlement and sector exit both hand the requester's slots back (proven 3→0); surviving raiders become ordinary hostiles, never force-despawned mid-combat |

## Design notes

- The payload entity cannot be an AI target (`engagementAuthority` requires
  ship-to-ship), so the assignment names the tug and anchors on the assembly:
  the raider's run crosses the load's line whether it is loose or on the
  player's tether — "going for the spindle" reads identically.
- The specialist is budget-gated, not attempt-gated: the third grant — when the
  sector's budget can afford it — is the tether-control raider.
- Debug finding worth keeping: `aiPorts` drops every maneuver while the
  dynamic authority is not ready (`physics_owner_unavailable`); the focused
  test prepares the real Rapier backend up front, matching the pq195-04 seam.
