## Review tightening (2026-09-09)

Reviewed against the leaf `.00` done-when. 47-A is re-expressed without loss and no second pipeline was invented. Three claim-versus-enforcement gaps were closed in the validator, each of which the live sheet already satisfied:

- **A beat could claim template kind and skip the whole standard.** The exemption that keeps `TEMPLATE.beat.json` fillable was keyed on the sheet's own `kind` field, so a hollow `beats/b1-*.beat.json` with `kind: "template"` validated with zero issues — contradicting the "Fail the sheet if" list above. The exemption is now granted by filename; a mislabelled beat fails with `template` plus every content issue (13 on the probe).
- **Bark lines were cited but never compared.** Actor ids, mechanic ids, and `durationSeconds` were cross-checked against the leftover scenario, but bark text only had to be a non-empty string — the one axis where a parallel corpus grows. Any bark whose id resolves in `47a.scenario.json` is now compared to the leftover line; drift fails as `rewritten_bark`. All five matched exactly before the check landed. The scavenger demand is cited from `scenarioRuntime.js` and is skipped, not assumed. `seedCapture.seed` is now compared to the tape the sheet names (47 in both).
- **`REQUIRED_47A_ACTORS` held 8 while `BEAT-STANDARD.md` demanded 10.** The standard says "Kessler and the handoff beacon are leftover too — keep them"; the validator did not enforce it. Both ids are now required. The sheet and the scenario already carry them.

Files changed by this review: `src/story/beatStandard.js`, `test/pq-178-00-beat-standard.test.mjs`, and this receipt. `47a.scenario.json`, `campaign47a/`, `barks.js`, `narrative.js`, `B0.md`, `BEAT-STANDARD.md`, the beat sheets, the queue, and `NOW.md` were not touched.
