## Status: INTEGRATED — ROUTE ACCEPTED

The Third Shift is playable end to end on the ordinary route, by an
ordinary recovery pilot, on public inputs alone. The acceptance
playthrough also caught and closed a real production gap: the SP-07
load was **categorically invulnerable**. `DEFAULT_MASK.projectile`
carries no payload membership and the damage verb's type allowlist
excluded payloads outright, so the failed-then-reduced-recovery route
the feature promises could never be produced by a player — the load
could only ever be delivered, never lost.

The fix is two lines of membership, applied exactly where the
obligation lives: the heist load's spawn spec gains
`Masks.PROJECTILE` in its collision mask — in **both** the fresh
launch path and the save-load resumed materialization, so a resumed
run keeps the same damageable body — and the interaction catalog
admits `payload` into the `damage` verb. A 480-hull cage is durable,
not invulnerable: the stock pulse laser the test pilot carries kills
it in roughly nine seconds of on-target fire.

## What the playthrough actually found

- **Invulnerable load (fixed here).** The wreck route was unreachable
  by any player mechanism — projectiles, beams, whips, contact
  consequences all excluded payloads by mask and by verb allowlist.
  The mask fix is load-spawn-scoped: only the heist body gains
  `Masks.PROJECTILE`; the verb admission is catalog-wide but the
  catalog already documents `destructible` payloads as cargo bodies.
- **Turret physics are real.** The Hawser's M mount bears only inside
  its gimbal arc and leads only a resolved target; a fixed-seed
  playthrough that never rotates the nose onto the load simply never
  fires. The test solves it the way a pilot does — match velocity,
  point the nose, lead the shot.
- **Contact-force receipts need the production flag.** The fence's
  custody chain runs on `physics:impact` receipts, which only exist
  under `weaponImpulseConsequences` (on in `PRODUCTION_FEATURES`, off
  in the legacy test profile). The scene seeds the production
  configuration; earlier leaf tests emitted the event synthetically.
