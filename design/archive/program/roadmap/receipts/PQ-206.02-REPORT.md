# PQ-206.02 — Degenerate flee-cell egress — IMPLEMENTED 2026-09-26

Commit `b0281d014`. Verdict: **implemented** — the bound is landed and pinned; the
representative-encounter play proof the `src/ai/` ownership law asks for is owed on a headed
route (host contention; the bench queue runs when the perf lane finishes).

## What the player feels

A wing that cannot finish the fight no longer kites the player to 10k WU. Enemies that commit
to fleeing — morale-scattered survivors, disengaging pirates, pacing-pinned attackers, ace
captains running on sight — now actually leave: past 960 WU of separation they burn off-field
for a few seconds and exit through the engine's ordinary despawn sweep. A scatter still reads
as a shock beat, not a rout: survivors re-press when the hold expires instead of fleeing
forever. If the pilot chases a fleeing hull back inside the bound, the exit releases and the
fight is still theirs to finish.

## The defect traced

Every flee-stamp path left the hull alive and fleeing indefinitely:

- `wingMorale._scatter` wrote `ai.fsm = 'flee'` alongside its timed `forceFlee` — but the
  `until` expiry deleted every field *except* `fsm`. `effectiveActivityForAI` reads
  `fsm === 'flee'` at every decision gate, so a scattered survivor was forced-flee forever.
  The squad's authored break was a bounded shock (`SCATTER_S ≈ 6 s`, one break per squad);
  the missing `fsm` restore made one break a permanent rout.
- `pirateDisengage.markFleeing`, `pirateParley`, `difficultyDirector` pacing pins,
  `aceMemory` beaten-captain flight, and `surrenderRecovery._escape` stamp flee/disengage
  orders whose entities then ran to the edge of the world and beyond — the retreat maneuver's
  `arrivalDistance: Infinity` keeps re-projecting while contacts are perceived, so a faster
  fleeing hull kited a pursuing player without bound. Nothing ever retired them.

## The fix

- **`src/ai/egressExit.js` + `tacticalAI.update` wiring** — a cadence-gated (15-tick) pass over
  the already-fetched ship-like list. A hull under a live flee order
  (`forceFlee` / `fsm:'flee'` / activity `flee`/`disengage`) with ≥960 WU player separation
  gets `data.despawnAt = now + 12 s` — the same canonical exit `surrenderRecovery._escape` and
  `encounterDirector.despawnAll` already use. The stamp is marked (`_egressExitAt`) so only our
  writes release: a pilot who closes back inside the bound, or an order that ends before the
  fade fires, releases the stamp and the authored fight resumes. Foreign despawn clocks are
  never touched. Guards: arena `runCohort:'survival'` cohorts (finite waves must stay
  reachable), `ai.moraleImmune`, encounter-roster and `data.encounter` actors (scripts own
  their exits), and player-team hulls are never egressed. Runs before the tactical quiet
  latch — a latched-quiet world still owes its fleeing hulls the exit. Emits `ai:egressExit`
  for observability.
- **`wingMorale.update`** — the scatter expiry now deletes `ai.fsm` alongside `forceFlee`,
  `_wingMoraleUntil`, `_moraleUntil`, and `data.morale`. The scatter stays a bounded shock:
  survivors re-press when it expires (the combatDoctrine "press, break, re-press" cycle), and
  only flee orders that genuinely persist meet the egress bound.

## For the next engineer

- The bound measures separation from the **player**, matching the leaf's "the pilot cannot
  finish the wing" framing — a pilot who can close re-opens the fight. Extending to
  NPC-vs-NPC fights would need a threat-relative measure and is a different leaf.
- `despawnAt`/`_egressExitAt` ride ordinary `entity.data` serialization, same as every other
  despawn clock in the tree; no save-schema change (v14, 303 paths unchanged).
- If a future system wants "this ship egressed vs this ship was culled", the `ai:egressExit`
  bus seam names it (reason field: `fsm:flee` / `forceFlee` / `activity`).
