## Route and wiring

- **Pointer after first latch**: Onboarding subscribes to `tether:latched`. The first time the player latches an object in the session, `firstLatchDone` is stamped, `rangePromptActive` is armed, and `onboarding:rangePrompt` is emitted with `text: 'Range (F4)'`. The status panel displays the non-intrusive `.sf-ob-range-prompt` affordance.
- **Entry rung selection**: `resolveRescueEntryRung(state)` maps prompt-active and mid-swing states directly to `swing_do_not_pull` (index 2 in `RAIL_ROWS`). Opening Range while idling or on other beats defaults to the top of the rail (0 = `heavy_turns_wide`), preserving PQ-163.00 assertions.
- **Funnel recording**: `rangeScreen.onShow(ctx)` and `recordRangeOpened(bus, state)` emit `range:opened` carrying `{ type: 'range:opened', atS, fromPrompt, rungId, rungIndex }`. Onboarding captures `rangeOpenedFromPrompt: true` and stores the event in `state.onboarding.rangeFunnel`. Opening from the prompt dismisses the active prompt.
- **Tow-rig rescue preserved**: The six-body tableau (`rock`, `derelict`, `scout`, `asteroid`, `pod`, `beacon`), the three-verb cadence (swing, shove, grab), scout invulnerability/team 1, and 47-A golden safety remain completely intact.
