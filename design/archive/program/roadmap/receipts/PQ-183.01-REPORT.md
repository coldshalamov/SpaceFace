## Notes

- `check:visual-regression` ran in this session; watch lines render only when pins exist, so
  pin-free goldens are untouched.
- Pin affordance lives only on the dossier apron — the door is where you hang the watch. The HUD
  block is pointer-events:none and aria-hidden (the same data is reachable in the dossier; the
  pinned line is a glance, not a second source).
- Price pins remember the market they were taken in (the docked station at pin time) and show
  ▲/▼ drift against the pinned price.
- The asteroid-screen `<style>` injection fixed here was a foreign-lane defect caught by the
  packet's own baseline; moved verbatim into asteroid-ops.css with its load-bearing `!important`
  intact (styles/ui.css carries a legacy `*:focus-visible !important` the rule must beat).
