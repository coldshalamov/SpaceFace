# S-10 drafts — Ashfall Reach, three personas

## The Drifter (working-class regular)
Ashfall is where the Drifter doesn't go unless paid triple — and the people who live there
scare him. He hears it on the Static: toll collectors who ask twice, blade names, the burn
that moves like weather. Draft: two Static lines (the twice-asked toll, the vault's false
keyways) plus pitborn taunt lines that grade your blade-work like scrap. The challenge is the
poetry — every taunt should read like a verse that prices you.

## The Archivist (continuity and memory)
The sector's three authored monuments (Iron Maw, Deep-Mother Vault, Vault Maw) already carry
plates and lore. The nine sites around them — the breathing station, the ranked dormant frames,
the burned-clean crater — file nothing. These are the totem wrecks the register asks for: a
graveyard the raiders arrange themselves around. Draft: authored `discoveryPlate` bodies on
all nine, written as raider-cartography — each site named by what the Vael and the salvagers
do about it.

## The Accountant (systems reality-check)
The Accountant reads the schema before the poetry. Plates are free (mark-blocked types only).
Bark base-cell appends are cheap but shift fill-line indices — acceptable since no test pins
non-helix seeded picks; voice-corpus shape rules apply (one line, ≤160 chars, ≤2 enders, no
in-cell dupes). Hail cells: one per stance keeps ≥3. Band: plain lines only — no repBand, no
eventKey, no Tessera in margin. Draft: the challenge-bark tranche lands in `faction_vael` +
`faction_pitborn` base cells ONLY; `BARK_SITUATION_FILL` additions would be inert (cells
saturate at 12). Reject anything else — no new encounters, no props.
