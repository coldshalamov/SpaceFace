# S-08 drafts — Sker Haven, three personas

## The Drifter (working-class regular)
Sker is where you go when nobody else will berth you — and the Bazaar remembers why you're
there. The Drifter wants the working details: cold bunks, name boards, toll windows, the toll
jokes the Static already tells about Sker air costing nerve. Draft: band-first. Two Static
lines (berth policy, bazaar receipts), a Margin note about the same berth sold four times, and
one Ballad line welding the dead into the gate. Plus Reach hails that say "vouched or weighed."

## The Archivist (continuity and memory)
The Throne already has its record — a fortress of raid-trophy hulls with a blank registry plate.
But the six other POIs file nothing: a survivor-culture wreck field, a refugee dead-drop cache,
a machine causeway the map is older than, a spore field, a drydock berthed for no human hull.
Draft: authored `discoveryPlate` bodies on all six — the Haven keeps records the way it keeps
the dead: welded in, facing out.

## The Set Dresser (visual storyteller)
The Haven should read "refuge built from what was stolen" at first sight. The existing dressing
vocabulary is closed (kit dressing is another lane's dirty file; new shrine props mean renderer
work). What the Set Dresser can still do is dress the *signal space*: the anomaly sites all have
authored zone bodies but no discovery title — the plate IS the set piece's nameboard. Draft
converges with the Archivist on plates, and argues the copy should carry shrine-practice
texture (name boards facing the Quiet Dock, elders naming the Harvest Deep like a debt).
