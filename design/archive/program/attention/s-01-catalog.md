# S-01 catalog — Helios Prime (tier 0, home)

Row register: **third-shift working class; manifests wrong.**
Sanctioned detail: ambient encounters tier-0, named hulls, gossip barks.

## What the player literally perceives now

- **Geography.** Two stations (Helios Station trade hub at 1280,-420; Coalition HQ military at
  -920,1080), three gate legs (~3.4k WU to Ceres and Tethys gates, ~2.9k to Vesta), a starter seam
  field at (720,-260) with three authored tether boulders, and an outer metallic field.
- **POIs (16).** Tutorial Beacon, Vesper's Rehearsal, the Candle Fleet memorial, Outer Yard
  Derelict, Corridor Pin 44-C, Helios Weigh-Point, Starter Seam Claim, Bonded Cold Locker,
  Helios Bay 7, Ash Pin SPAN-HOLD, the Outer Yard Whistle, the Pit Line, Checkpoint Niner,
  Smear Marker 7, Relief-Freighter Choir-Tender (unique wreck). Most carry discoveryPlates in
  the dry, melancholy-wry house register ("the odor is consistent with transit").
- **Traffic.** trafficPerMin 18 — the densest lane in the game. Named faces: Mira Bluepack,
  Kess of the Span (runs to Tethys), Warden Keel, the dedicated Cinder Run Courier, plus
  Arclight / tanker / customs / memorial-tourist fixtures stamped by traffic.js. The 47-A
  campaign stages at boot. The passenger liner runs Helios↔Coalition.
- **Voice.** Concord Bulletin is home-tuned here (bandRadio homeSectors). SCN bark cells are
  the fullest in the corpus (Ref 44-C bureaucracy). Ad board has 8 Helios-tagged ad_pulse
  notices; 11 local_wall graffiti tags. Maera Vols has a sector:enter line.

## What is thin

- **The gate legs are bare past Checkpoint Niner.** All 16 POIs cluster inside ~2.5k WU of
  spawn; the three ~3k WU departure corridors every outbound pilot flies have no authored
  beat — no weigh, no farewell, no last-chance customs.
- **No third-shift texture.** The register names it; nothing shows it. No shift-change
  marker, no night-crew prop, no "the day shift wrote this down wrong" evidence.
- **"Manifests wrong" is asserted, not dramatized.** Bay 7 gestures at it (odor, shelved
  grid); no POI shows a misdeclared thing the desk never fixed.
- **Named traffic is rich; anonymous traffic is mute.** SCN cells cover procedure but the
  civilian working voice on the lane — the people who stamp the manifests — has few barks.

## Tone register observed

Bureaucratic-horror played absolutely straight, undercut by working-class gallows humor.
Second-person ledger voice ("your prior operator already answered this one"). Memorials are
sincere; procedures are sinister by procedure, never by snarl. Any new line that sneers,
quips, or goes genre-pirate breaks register.
