# S-04 catalog — Vesta Forge

Row register: **hot industry, pride.**
Sanctioned detail: foundry texture, guild marks, hazard lore.

## What the player literally perceives now

- **Geography.** DMC industrial sector, worldRadius 4300. Forge Foundry (-480,720, fab,
  module_craft), Forge Outlying Yard (-820,280, fab), Refuel Depot (1120,-560, mining —
  **Choir-run**, "posts shift work in verses"). Gates: Helios north (0,-3526), Tethys NE
  (2934,-1956), Charon SE (1956,2934). Three metallic/crystalline fields.
- **The hot field.** Radiation hazard centered dead on f_vesta_1 (-540,-480, r600, i0.4) —
  the sector's best ore face sits inside the burn. Nothing authored explains who works it,
  how, or what the toll is.
- **POIs.** Slag-Run Nineteen (derelict freighter nobody will tow — radiation shadow),
  Slag-Choir Relay (Choir, ore signal, rim_poi_lore bound: "spikes in threes"),
  hidden Shift-End Ore Cache, the Resonant Cathedral (Choir hero landmark tuned to the
  Forge's old shift rhythm), Red Cable Yard, HOOK-LEFT Fin-3.
- **Voice.** DMC barks (shared with the belt), Choir Vespers on the band, the relay's
  three scan lines.

## What is thin

- **No guild marks anywhere.** "Pride" is the register and nothing carries a maker's mark:
  the foundry ships plate and fittings by the chart note, but no stamp yard, no assay post,
  no signed work exists. The sector makes everything and signs nothing.
- **The hazard has no lore.** A r600 radiation zone on the best field is pure mechanics —
  no rotation roster, no lead hedge, no burn toll. The catalog's own POI (Slag-Run
  Nineteen) is the only thing that even mentions the radiation.
- **Foundry texture is absent.** No quench, no slag, no reject line — the foundry is a
  station icon and a services list, not an industrial place with waste and pride.
- **The Charon leg is bare** — the convoy route toward the Expanse has nothing.

## Tone register observed

Shop-floor pride over institutional dread: crews sign work, count shifts in pours, and
treat the burn as a roster problem, not a horror. The Choir's harmonic register sits
underneath ("the Pattern reads knees") — reverence for the same shifts the DMC treats as
Tuesday.
