# S-04 drafts — Vesta Forge

Three persona drafts. The Drifter wants the burn worked like a job; the Set Dresser
wants the foundry's waste-and-signature grammar; the Archivist wants the Choir layer
and the radiation's human cost remembered.

## The Drifter — "the hot face is a shift, not a scary zone"

Grounds: a r600 radiation pocket on the best ore face is a workplace. Dress it like one —
rotation, lead line, burn tally — and the sector's pride becomes legible.

1. **"Hot Face Claim"** inside the hazard rim (~-480,-320): a claim pin worked on
   rotation — plate says crews pull the face in twenty-minute watches, the pin is
   re-stamped every rotation.
2. **"The Lead Hedge"** at the hazard's north edge (~-540,140): a line of parked
   lead-shielded containers where crews stage before a watch (container rack) —
   the fence between the shift and the burn.
3. **DMC forge barks** — `scan`/`patrol-greeting` fills about watches, burns, and
   the roster: pride-voice, not dread-voice.

Schema: sectors.js POIs + faction_dmc fill. The burn gets a workforce.

## The Set Dresser — "a foundry leaves slag, stamps, and rejects"

Grounds: industry is legible through its waste stream and its signature. The foundry
currently has neither.

1. **"Guild Stamp Post"** beside the Outlying Yard approach (~-700,420): the tally
   post where plate gets the maker's mark — every shift's mark on one post, oldest
   stamps at the base.
2. **"Reject Run"** on the Tethys leg (~1500,-900): a parked container rack of
   mis-stamped plate that failed inspection and was dumped mid-route rather than
   shipped unsigned. Pride made physical: unsigned work does not leave Vesta.
3. **"Kilnward Buoy"** on the bare Charon leg (~1600,2400): a guild-marked nav buoy
   the plate convoys form on — signed even at the edge of the map.

Schema: POIs only, all from the admitted prop vocabulary.

## The Archivist — "the burn keeps a count the roster doesn't"

Grounds: the sector's own plate calls the radiation a tow-nobody-will-pay problem and
the Choir relay counts "spikes in threes." The human cost should be named in place,
and the Choir layer should keep singing it.

1. **rim_poi_lore** entries for the new POIs — the burn's count told in three-line
   scan text.
2. **Choir Vespers** +3 verses: the watch rotation, the names the slag keeps, the
   stamp that outlives the stamper (the Choir already claims the sector's shift
   rhythm via the Cathedral).
3. **"Slag Widow Plate"** — no new POI; instead the vigil surface is the Lead Hedge's
   own plate (spliced from Drifter 2): the hedge's containers carry chalk tallies of
   watches survived, some columns ending mid-tally.

Schema: rim_poi_lore + 040-band choir_vespers. Rejects a separate memorial POI —
the belt just shipped one; redundancy axis.
