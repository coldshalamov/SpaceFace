# S-06 drafts — Io Reach, three personas

## The Drifter (working-class regular)
The Reach is where the work is ugly and the berth is "temporary" for seventeen years. The
Drifter hears the sector through the people who work it: the welder paid in air shares, the
quiet contractor who vetted his standing before his hull, the meter that never sleeps. Draft:
voice-first. (a) The Static gets three Reach-specific roasts — the contested board, the vetting
post, the air-share pay stub. (b) Quiet hails get outpost texture — the contracting post that
read your file before you opened the channel. (c) Frontier hails get the contested-floor lean.
No POI plates: the Drifter doesn't read the codex, he listens to the band while he welds.

## The Archivist (continuity and memory)
A discovery is a record, and Io Reach's two anomaly sites currently file an empty record — the
player triangulates a signal, flies down a physical site with an authored `reason`, and the
journal writes a generic fallback line. The Wreck Cathedral already proves the plate pattern.
Draft: authored `discoveryPlate` on both anomaly POIs, bodies consistent with the zone fiction
already shipped in `authoredPlaces.js` (a containment ring that outlived its contents; seven
receivers pointed at a coordinate that is not on the chart). Nothing else — the Archivist does
not want band chatter diluting the discovery register.

## The Set Dresser (visual storyteller)
The sector should read "worked, contested, held together with welds" before any text. The
dressing vocabulary is already closed (kit modules, exclusion radii, GraffitiRenderer walls) —
new physical dressing means renderer machinery, which the program forbids. What remains
player-visible at range is the radio: the Static's home-sector signal is the set dressing that
plays while you fly past the fields. Draft converges on the band but argues for *hostile-edge*
lines — toll humor that tells a pilot the sector flips flags between meals — rather than the
Drifter's shop-talk warmth.
