# S-03 catalog — Tethys Junction

Row register: **commerce bustle, toll friction.**
Sanctioned detail: convoy radio, customs checks, lane stories.

## What the player literally perceives now

- **Geography.** Four-gate crossroads (Helios NW, Ceres W, Vesta SW, Io Reach SE).
  Tethys Trade Hub (1050,380, size L, contracts_hub board, DMC–MTS front dispatch),
  Customs Gate SCN station (-640,-1180). One field, no hazards, enemy density 0.20,
  trafficPerMin 14 — the busiest sector on the core graph.
- **The Tally (WF-03).** The deepest authored lane grammar in the game: queue head/inner
  buoys, red/green scan-gate pins, The Tally Post (WT-TETH-19 plate), Held-for-Count
  Rack, Seized Cargo Pod, Count-Face Worklight — the whole inbound Helios→customs
  corridor physically runs the count. Plus RUBRIC's Marking Line, Customs Log Relay,
  Weigh-Slip Buoy (rim_poi_lore bound), hidden black-market contact, Split Station.
- **Voice.** MTS bark corpus (faction_mts — pricing, account, convoy register). Band
  coverage: Concord Bulletin (SCN), The Margin, Fulfillment Routing (surreal logistics),
  The Static. Tethys-tagged rim_poi_lore exists (weigh-slip buoy).

## What is thin

- **The bustle is one-sided.** Every authored beat sits on the Helios-inbound leg. The
  other three corridors — Ceres ore leg (west), Vesta alloy leg (southwest), and the
  Io Reach convoy leg (southeast, the route toward the frontier) — have zero furniture.
  The busiest junction reads as one queue and three empty roads.
- **No convoy on the net.** trafficPerMin 14 with a contracts_hub, but no convoy radio
  surface: nothing marshals, counts, or escorts out loud. Fulfillment Routing is abstract;
  there is no working convoy voice in the sector register.
- **Customs is seen, not heard.** The Tally is physical set-dressing; the customs check
  as a *voiced* event (SCN + MTS on the same queue, toll friction spoken) barely exists
  beyond generic patrol lines.
- **Lane stories stop at the gate.** The weigh-slip buoy's lore hints at a lane economy
  (MTS paint over Concord metal, sold twice) that no other POI or channel continues.

## Tone register observed

Two bureaucracies sharing one queue: Meridian pricing-smooth ("every mass is sold
twice") over Concord procedure ("the buoy still cites Ref 44-C"), with Fulfillment
Routing's deadpan logistics-surrealism as the third voice. The friction is procedural,
never violent — tolls, counts, holds, re-files.
