# M-05 drafts — starter jobs per hull

## Drifter

First jobs should end with a name and a thank-you, not just a second identical step. The
Kestrel chain is a yard that keeps calling you back; give step three the boss's voice —
"the yard lead asked for you by transponder". The Pelican's buoy work earns a harbor
line. The Wasp's third runner should be the one who's heard about you.

## Archivist

A third step is where the employer proves they were paying attention — the title can
reference the job you just did ("the second seam held", "both buoys logged"). Named
runners already work (`runnerName`); the last one can carry a callbacks name — Voss-Bara
kin, a Tollcut cousin — so the starter arc plugs into the board's named world.

## Accountant

`nextStarterStep` walks `steps[done]`; a third `{step:3}` row is schema-native and paid
by the same path. Titles stay inside the board's line budget. Reward curve: step2 → step3
keeps the +~40% slope (420→640→840). No helper changes needed — `shipCarriesModule`
foreign clause untouched. Verdict: one epilogue step per hull.
