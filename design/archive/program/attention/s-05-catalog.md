# S-05 catalog — Pallas Drift

Row register: **drift culture.**
Sanctioned detail: drift-specific props and local faction texture.

## What the player literally perceives now

- **Geography.** Tier-2 fringe sector (security 0.42, MTS), gates to Ceres NE
  (2047,-3070), Io Reach E (3690,0), Sker Haven SW (-2047,3070). Drift Market
  (620,-880, trade_hub — the fringe's kitchen: food surplus 2.4, cheap end of the
  Belt ration run), Smuggler Den (-1080,540, Quiet blackmarket: "no manifest, no
  memory"). One metallic + two icy fields, a r800 nebula pocket at (400,600).
- **POIs.** RAVEL's Unraveller, Pirate Wreckage, hidden cache, The Quiessence
  (landmark — seventeen freighters in formation around a violet buoy, warm bunks,
  no names), Empty Habitat, Empty Foundry. Foreign ATTENTION S-11 work already
  shipped: Faith Filing Ninth (claims filed on trust) and Drift Tag (labor census
  rock) — the filed-in-faith and tag-culture surfaces are covered.
- **Voice.** MTS barks (shared), the Quiet numbers station on the band, Static
  heckles. No Pallas-bound rim_poi_lore yet.

## What is thin

- **The market's kitchen role is invisible.** The food surplus is a real, authored
  fact (every unlisted crew eats through Drift Market) with no physical trace — no
  ration line, no mess queue, nothing the ration-run pilots actually use.
- **The Quiet have a station and no doorstep.** The Smuggler Den's "no manifest"
  culture stops at the dock: no threshold furniture, no local etiquette for
  approaching a Quiet house.
- **The Quiessence's social shadow is untouched.** Seventeen berths, no eighteenth —
  drift culture would keep a mooring for the missing. Nobody has authored it.
- **The nebula drift itself is bare** — the sector's namesake murk has no
  off-books furniture inside or at its edge.
- **The Sker leg is empty** — the corridor toward Sker Haven (the other fringe
  port) has nothing; locals would mark it as the last cheap air.

## Tone register observed

Off-ledger warmth: feeds everyone, logs nothing, keeps a berth for whoever didn't
come back. Not sinister — the drift's menace (Quiessence, pirate wreck) sits next
to genuine hospitality, and the same culture holds both.
