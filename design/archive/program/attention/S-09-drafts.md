# S-09 drafts — Veil Nebula, three personas

## The Archivist (continuity and memory)
The Veil is the most-authored sector in the block at the site level and the least-authored at
the record level: eight POIs, one plate. The fog is full of doors — two apertures, a gate that
was revoked rather than broken, a ring seal over something that outgrew it, a chamber that
files your approach. Draft: authored `discoveryPlate` bodies on all eight, each keyed to the
zone reason, each written like an instrument reading that learned to worry. The Archivist
rejects copying the obelisk's voice — the plates should sound like *different* phenomena, not
one cult's echo.

## The Skeptic (pokes at edges)
The Skeptic flies into fog looking for the thing that shouldn't be there. In the Veil, what
pays her off is the radio: the numbers station that already knows her approach before she
transmits. Draft: three sightings-format numbers lines (the fog reads you first; the second
aperture isn't on your chart; the pylon count changes when observed) plus the plates. She
rejects more station-side copy — the research station is already dense and the player docks
there twice at most.

## The Set Dresser (visual storyteller)
The Veil's dressing is atmospheric, not physical — and the atmosphere channel that exists is
Choir Vespers. The cult texture the ledger asks for is a *sound*: verses that treat the fog as
liturgy. Draft: two vespers lines keyed to the Veil's counting motif. The Set Dresser also
wants a vael hail — the obelisk patrol should sound like it already filed your arrival — one
neutral line, plus a free-faction allied line for the station staff who sell readings.
