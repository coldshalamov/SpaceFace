# A16 — Pressure-sac device body

**Family:** biological derivative. **Disposition:** OPTIONAL after directional impulse.

This is a model-family production brief, not a missing-asset assertion or an actual mesh. Resolve existing IDs/source assets and reuse what already works. Consult current Forge/graphics instructions, asset manifests and nearest nested policy. [S01, S22, S25]

## Silhouette at the real camera

Inflatable off-center bladder in a human cage, with a readable mounting base.

## Visible function

Inflation communicates the fuse; mounting normal communicates the coming impulse.

## Motion and articulation

Volume increases unevenly under pressure, release ruptures a directed opening instead of shrinking backward.

## Collision, sockets and authority

Macro attachment plus deliberate detachment; cosmetic membrane does not add separate collision bodies.

Declare source axes, scale, pivot, attachment/work sockets, collision policy and state identity. Use current exporter/runtime conventions; art must not create extra solver bodies by accident.

## Materials and light

Translucent tissue with actual thickness cues and darker veins; human clamps visibly restrain it.

## Required states

Compressed, priming, swollen, ruptured, spent attachment.

## Scene consumers

None required by this packet.
