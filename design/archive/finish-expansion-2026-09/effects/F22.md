**Disposition:** SELECTED. This is a VFX/audio-family brief, not delivered media or proof the current family is deficient. Read the current VFX technique and lifecycle standards and reuse the selected production owner. [S01, S21]

## Authoritative signal

Actual attachment, containment seal and removal result.

This is a semantic data requirement, not a fabricated event/API name. The implementation agent must find the live receipt/state owner; add a narrowly required receipt there only if genuinely absent. Renderer and audio never decide gameplay success.

## Onset

Capture device seats; purge prepares at the contaminated interface.

## Sustained form and motion

Finite local treatment removes or isolates the admitted material, leaving the rest of the hull intact.

## Source-off and aftermath

Success ends active contamination cue; harmless residue may remain and is clearly inert.

## Sound design

Mechanical seal versus wet removal; success and inadequate treatment have distinct sounds.

Use current mix and voice-priority owners. Audition in context and at controlled loudness; release every loop with its emitter. Captions/visual alternatives preserve critical information without inventing source precision.

## Target review cases

Wrong material, insufficient resource, full cage, released specimen and repeat purge.

## Non-negotiable production tests

Normal gameplay camera, moving and stationary sources, full-density combination, bloom off/on, pause/resume, origin rebase, target/source death, pool reuse and reduced-motion/flash modes. Appearance may be rich, but cosmetic detail cannot extend damage/force or consume sim RNG.

A designed effect has distinct preparation, active change and cooling, not the same grow-spin-shrink loop. Primary form is substantial material/geometry appropriate to the class; a soft card or particle glitter is not the object. Preserve the solid world and the user's bright physical identity.
