## Build-plan candidates

| Local ID | Work | Player outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [SFQ-B041](../builds/SFQ-B041.md) | Give each encounter an ingress and exit | Threats arrive from somewhere and leave for a reason rather than appearing in the pilot's lap. |
| [SFQ-B042](../builds/SFQ-B042.md) | Stabilize tactical commitment | Enemies carry out a readable plan long enough for counterplay to matter. |
| [SFQ-B043](../builds/SFQ-B043.md) | Make formations physically breakable | A field or thrown wreck disrupts a group instead of a script restoring the formation instantly. |
| [SFQ-B044](../builds/SFQ-B044.md) | Author specialist intention tells | A cutter, sniper and anchor fighter each create a distinct question before attacking. |
| [SFQ-B045](../builds/SFQ-B045.md) | Make thrown bodies a perceived threat | NPCs respond to a moving wreck as a collision problem rather than ignoring it. |
| [SFQ-B046](../builds/SFQ-B046.md) | Give morale an observable consequence | Crew confidence changes attack commitment and escape routes rather than just dialogue. |
| [SFQ-B047](../builds/SFQ-B047.md) | Make civilians protect what matters | A hauler avoids fire, a tug tries to recover cargo, and a patrol chooses what to protect. |
| [SFQ-B048](../builds/SFQ-B048.md) | Escalate problems rather than health bars | Difficulty adds coordinated situations while retaining throwable light enemies. |
| [SFQ-B049](../builds/SFQ-B049.md) | Separate combat and ecology intent | Animals, machines and crews have different reasons to move or harm the player. |
| [SFQ-B050](../builds/SFQ-B050.md) | Ship the roster as a readable collection | Each admitted enemy has a recognizable outline, job and counter. |
