# Independent review that tests the game, not the prose

Read the native assignment and supporting packet, then try to reproduce the claimed improvement without following the author's optimistic path. Check actual selected owners and source identity. Do not assume the report's screenshot, mock fixture or source-comment guarantee is the runtime.

## Ask four questions

What can the player do now that was broken or weak before? Can an ordinary player reach and understand it? Does it remain correct when the obvious thing goes wrong? Does it still belong to SpaceFace?

For physics, trace force/impulse and capability admission. For missions, move or destroy the object, act early or late, and save mid-operation. For money/cargo, repeat/cancel/reload. For VFX, watch onset through source-off and residue in a dense actual scene. For sound, listen in mix. For UI, use real input, focus, cancellation and large-text/reduced-motion variants. For performance, compare the same picture and honest host conditions.

## Verdicts

**Integrate:** the bounded outcome is good and relevant evidence supports it. Note residuals outside its scope honestly.

**Iterate:** name the actual defect, reproduction and smallest correction. Do not send vague “make it more AAA” feedback.

**Reject/cut:** the feature duplicates another, violates current product direction, cannot be controlled sensibly or does not justify its system cost. Preserve the causal reason in the existing decision record so it is not proposed again by another agent.

**Evidence missing:** distinguish absent source/host/device/audience from a known failed behavior. Continue productive work rather than demanding a ceremonial human signoff for every change. Never claim a human playtest or min-spec acceptance that did not occur.

Quality judgment requires looking, listening and acting. It is not reducible to generated screenshots, test count or a review score. The task's done sentence is the minimum; a still-obvious defect in the assigned experience is work remaining.
