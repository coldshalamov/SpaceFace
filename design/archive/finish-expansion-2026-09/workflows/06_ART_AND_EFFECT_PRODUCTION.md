# Art and effect production

## Models

Choose a brief and the scene that needs it. Check whether the source asset already exists and merely needs correction. Read Forge and current graphics instructions. Solve plan-view silhouette, major values, function and attachment before adding texture detail.

Create or amend the source in the existing pipeline. Export with correct units, pivots, hooks, material roles, LOD and collision policy. Use actual production renderer review tools, then inspect the result at chase and close scale beside released assets. Fix obvious floating parts, hollow shapes, clipped stripes, washed values or unreadable working interfaces. Do not stop after a fixed number of review cycles when the scene remains visibly wrong.

Publish through current tooling with exact asset mapping. Cold-load and disposal checks are part of delivery. A source file without reachable released output is incomplete.

## VFX

Choose the physical/material signal first. Bind to its actual owner and coordinate frame. Design preparation, unequal active motion, source-off and separate residue; avoid a generic grow-spin-shrink envelope. Every visible force direction and contact should be justified by data. Cosmetic fine detail does not change the simulation.

Review a sequence beside actual hulls/rocks at normal zoom. Test bloom off and on, moving and stationary source, heavy/light contact, pause, rebase, pool reuse and reduced flash/motion. Fix clipping and lifecycle before adding more particles. Preserve the game's generous bright wake and substantial effects; optimize work and overdraw, not identity.

## Sound

Build the corresponding cue from current recipes and allowed assets. Listen in the full scene, not just solo. Test start/loop/stop, voice competition, distance, device settings and captions. A machine's silence can be intentional; a broken missing cue cannot be called restraint.

## Handoff

Deliver source and reachable output, exact hooks/signals, lifecycle rules and the ordinary scene used to review them. The packet is accepted for the moving game, not for a hero render or a shader name.
