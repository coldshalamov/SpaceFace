## Discover and commit

A machine repeats a failed delivery cycle. An interpreted directive asks for RETURN only after the player earns enough understanding.

Expose actual equipment, cargo, permission and capacity prerequisites before charging money or moving cargo. Required tools need a legitimate acquisition/service route. Preserve a decline/abort and a recovery path.

## Physical layout

A Courier carries one visible token between two known local endpoints. A safe interception leg and a recipient holding volume are readable at normal speed.

Measure actual reference hull/workpiece sizes, mass, cruise/earned speed, turn radius and camera depth before choosing final coordinates. Use [play-space guidance](../engineering/04_PLAYSPACE_AND_BALANCE.md); no coordinates in this brief silently override the current level.

## Playable phases

Follow the Courier; discover why the recipient refuses; physically recover the token; repair or satisfy the recipient condition; deliver it and watch downstream work resume.

## At least two approaches

Escort the Courier through an obstruction; take custody of the token and complete the route yourself.

## Failure and aftermath

Delay stalls the operation but does not destroy the campaign. Lost token has a declared recovery/remint policy with duplicate protection.

## Reward and progression

A narrow machine permission and a restored work cycle, not a universal robot reputation boost.
