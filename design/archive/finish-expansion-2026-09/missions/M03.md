## Discover and commit

A legitimate buyer pays for intact material; the preflight clearly names handling sensitivity and current capacity.

Expose actual equipment, cargo, permission and capacity prerequisites before charging money or moving cargo. Required tools need a legitimate acquisition/service route. Preserve a decline/abort and a recovery path.

## Physical layout

One volatile crate group moves through a broad lane with optional shorter high-traffic passage. The hazard comes from rough handling and heat, not invisible mines.

Measure actual reference hull/workpiece sizes, mass, cruise/earned speed, turn radius and camera depth before choosing final coordinates. Use [play-space guidance](../engineering/04_PLAYSPACE_AND_BALANCE.md); no coordinates in this brief silently override the current level.

## Playable phases

Inspect the load; pick route; handle traffic or a minor disruption; deliver intact units or divert compromised freight to a safe receiver.

## At least two approaches

Take the longer cooler lane; use cover and careful propulsion on the shorter path. An emergency jettison is an intentional rescue option.

## Failure and aftermath

Partial spoilage reduces payout; dangerous freight can be isolated for salvage. No irreversible campaign blocker.

## Reward and progression

Net profit after ammunition/repairs is measured; a good run funds a useful early choice.
