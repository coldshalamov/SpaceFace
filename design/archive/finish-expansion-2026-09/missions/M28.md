## Existing runtime integration

## Discover and commit

A Custodian repeats a stalled repair. Its work light points to the absent part.

Expose actual equipment, cargo, permission and capacity prerequisites before charging money or moving cargo. Required tools need a legitimate acquisition/service route. Preserve a decline/abort and a recovery path.

## Physical layout

A missing structural insert and its receiving socket share a clear outline. A powered machine field helps alignment but does not teleport the piece.

Measure actual reference hull/workpiece sizes, mass, cruise/earned speed, turn radius and camera depth before choosing final coordinates. Use [play-space guidance](../engineering/04_PLAYSPACE_AND_BALANCE.md); no coordinates in this brief silently override the current level.

## Playable phases

Identify matching component; tow it within work reach; align or activate the field; hold clear while the robot performs actual repair; observe restored function.

## At least two approaches

Use the Massline to orient and deliver; power the safe alignment field and guide the piece through it.

## Failure and aftermath

Bad alignment is refused clearly; a damaged substitute can work with reduced output or further repair.

## Reward and progression

Reopened service/corridor and a documented equipment permission, granted once.
