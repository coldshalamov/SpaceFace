## Physical layout

Two ordinary traffic lanes briefly run alongside each other. A transfer window is defined by relative speed and separation, not a pause in the universe.

Measure actual reference hull/workpiece sizes, mass, cruise/earned speed, turn radius and camera depth before choosing final coordinates. Use [play-space guidance](../engineering/04_PLAYSPACE_AND_BALANCE.md); no coordinates in this brief silently override the current level.

## Playable phases

Match traffic; approach the marked transfer side; receive or release the lot; avoid inspection or declare; deliver to an existing service.

## At least two approaches

Hold a steady formation for a clean transfer; catch a jettisoned package from stand-off with greater detection risk.

## Failure and aftermath

A missed handoff leaves a real recoverable package for a bounded time. Detection starts the existing inspection/law process.

## Reward and progression

Premium for intact covert transfer, ordinary payment for declared delivery where allowed.
