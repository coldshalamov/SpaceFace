An alien adhesive incident bridges two medium bodies on diverging trajectories. Multiple accessible endpoints allow cutting, towing or counter-impulse; no permanent player control lock.

Measure actual reference hull/workpiece sizes, mass, cruise/earned speed, turn radius and camera depth before choosing final coordinates. Use [play-space guidance](../engineering/04_PLAYSPACE_AND_BALANCE.md); no coordinates in this brief silently override the current level.

## Playable phases

Assess bridge tension; reduce relative speed; choose a severing or stabilizing method; remove residue or tow the pair to service; settle damage honestly.

## At least two approaches

Purge/cut the bridge at low load; synchronize both bodies and carry them as a pair with deliberate mass costs.

## Failure and aftermath

A violent snap scatters recoverable cargo and creates rescue pressure instead of a scripted explosion.

## Reward and progression

Condition-sensitive recovery pay and material evidence; no bonus for harming civilians deliberately.
