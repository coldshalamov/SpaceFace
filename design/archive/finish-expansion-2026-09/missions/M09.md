## Discover and commit

A claim beacon identifies a legal conflict; a survivor cue establishes immediate stakes.

Expose actual equipment, cargo, permission and capacity prerequisites before charging money or moving cargo. Required tools need a legitimate acquisition/service route. Preserve a decline/abort and a recovery path.

## Physical layout

A valuable wreck and survivor occupy different sides of a field while two claimants arrive on real trajectories. Keep all three objectives visible enough to compare.

Measure actual reference hull/workpiece sizes, mass, cruise/earned speed, turn radius and camera depth before choosing final coordinates. Use [play-space guidance](../engineering/04_PLAYSPACE_AND_BALANCE.md); no coordinates in this brief silently override the current level.

## Playable phases

Read claim and danger; recover survivor or component; let claimants inspect, or remove contested material; settle the actual chosen outcome.

## At least two approaches

Rescue and return evidence to earn lawful recovery; extract the component while containing witnesses/hostility through existing rules.

## Failure and aftermath

Delayed salvage may be claimed by another actor, but no invisible despawn or arbitrary murder of a survivor.

## Reward and progression

Condition-dependent salvage, rescue recognition or restitution obligation; not all simultaneously by default.
