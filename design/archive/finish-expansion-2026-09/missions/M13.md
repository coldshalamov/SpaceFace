## Playable phases

Inspect obligation; recover the remaining goods or supply replacements; deliver; clear the named portion of the incident.

## At least two approaches

Recover original lots carefully; earn money elsewhere and purchase legitimate replacements with a lower-margin outcome.

## Failure and aftermath

Further damage increases the particular obligation within declared caps; abandoning preserves the debt without preventing all play.

## Reward and progression

Incident resolution and restored service/standing, not a farming opportunity.
