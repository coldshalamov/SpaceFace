## Discover and commit

A passenger or fragile cargo needs passage without provoking a local population.

Expose actual equipment, cargo, permission and capacity prerequisites before charging money or moving cargo. Required tools need a legitimate acquisition/service route. Preserve a decline/abort and a recovery path.

## Physical layout

A heat-sensitive population creates a safe but slow corridor and a fast exposed bypass. Safe coasting is not enforced with artificial brakes.

Measure actual reference hull/workpiece sizes, mass, cruise/earned speed, turn radius and camera depth before choosing final coordinates. Use [play-space guidance](../engineering/04_PLAYSPACE_AND_BALANCE.md); no coordinates in this brief silently override the current level.

## Playable phases

Inspect current route knowledge; choose equipment and path; respond to an unexpected heat source; deliver or divert.

## At least two approaches

Coast through a known quiet window; deploy an admitted lure and take the quicker lane at material cost.

## Failure and aftermath

An alerted animal changes routing rather than instantly killing the passenger; an alternate refuge remains reachable.

## Reward and progression

A route-use payment plus improved observation confidence; no unlimited per-crossing discovery bonus.
