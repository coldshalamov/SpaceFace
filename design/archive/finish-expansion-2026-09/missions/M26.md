## Discover and commit

A maintenance signal continues despite the station being abandoned.

Expose actual equipment, cargo, permission and capacity prerequisites before charging money or moving cargo. Required tools need a legitimate acquisition/service route. Preserve a decline/abort and a recovery path.

## Physical layout

A station valve cycle drives a visible pressure/flow effect and nourishes attached biology. Model only the exterior and accessible workpieces; no new walkable-interior game.

Measure actual reference hull/workpiece sizes, mass, cruise/earned speed, turn radius and camera depth before choosing final coordinates. Use [play-space guidance](../engineering/04_PLAYSPACE_AND_BALANCE.md); no coordinates in this brief silently override the current level.

## Playable phases

Observe valve timing; trace biological dependence; isolate one branch or vent safely; recover a service recorder/workpiece; decide whether to leave the cycle running.

## At least two approaches

Synchronize recovery with the existing cycle; shut down the heat/valve branch and accept ecological consequences.

## Failure and aftermath

Rupture alters local exposure and routes but leaves an escape; the station cannot become an invulnerable cutscene object.

## Reward and progression

Industrial salvage and one causal finding about infrastructure occupation.
