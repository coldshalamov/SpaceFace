# 11 — Content Catalog

This is a quarry, not a requirement list. Ideas are intentionally numerous so implementation agents can select combinations that fit the live game without repeatedly reinventing the creative space.

The catalog follows the program laws:
- Vethari remain off-frame;
- one fungus;
- ecology before enemy roster;
- precursor creators remain mythic;
- machines are procedural, not mystical quest-givers;
- every strong concept should attach to an existing SpaceFace verb.

# A. Fauna — 20 seeds

## A01 Veil Ray
Translucent electromagnetic grazer. Flocks synchronize under field pressure. Scanner use itself changes their behavior.

## A02 Lantern Cyst
Drifting reproductive sac. Destroying it is easy and often strategically wrong because rupture seeds a bloom.

## A03 Hull Leech
Attaches to hot ships and produces a heat/energy penalty. Removal is a movement/utility problem.

## A04 Needle Swarm
Tiny noncombat sensor cloud. Makes a hidden colony perceptually larger than its real combat footprint.

## A05 Casket Worm
Segmented wreck-dweller awakened by vibration. Mining and impulse tools can bait it.

## A06 Anchor Beast
Sessile asteroid-scale animal. Can be mined around, studied, starved, or attacked. Functions as local habitat.

## A07 Mourning Kite
Repeats a route inherited through a network stain. Following it leads somewhere old.

## A08 Furnace Maw
Heat predator. Punishes sustained reactor output and rewards thermal discipline.

## A09 Glassback
Mineral-armored grazer from a lineage historically selected in high-energy environments. Physical weapons/impulse work better than beams.

## A10 Wake Eel
Surfs ion/gravity wakes. Harmless by itself, but can lead predators toward traffic.

## A11 Spindle Mother
Slow carrier surrounded by juveniles. Killing it changes future site contamination.

## A12 Bristle Ram
Massive impact defender. Designed for Massline, collision, and impulse play.

## A13 Archive Crab
Collects debris into repeated geometries. May be route-memory rather than intelligence.

## A14 Blind Shepherd
Mobile relay species. Nearby fauna coordinate better while it is present.

## A15 Cold Bell
Large hollow organism that rings electromagnetically when pressure or radiation changes. Acts as weather warning.

## A16 Suture Mite
Small organisms that close breaches in colonized hulls. Non-hostile unless removed from active repair.

## A17 Black Sail
Ultra-thin photophore that spreads across shadowed space. Appears like debris until it changes orientation as one enormous sheet.

## A18 Stone Lung
Asteroid-buried filter organism that periodically vents processed gas and spores.

## A19 Pilgrim Spine
Slow migrating line of linked organisms following a route no current star chart explains.

## A20 Empty Skin
Molted shell of a giant organism. Not an enemy at all; becomes temporary shelter, salvage site, or false wreck.

# Selection rule

When choosing content from this catalog, prefer combinations that:
- reuse two or more existing systems;
- create a decision rather than only spectacle;
- communicate through behavior before text;
- leave at least one fact uncertain;
- have a persistent aftermath or unlock;
- deepen a location's identity;
- avoid making every alien phenomenon hostile.

A strong alien encounter should often be describable as a sentence with a verb other than "fight."
