# 06 — Unlocks, Rewards, and Economy

## Goal

Alien content needs rewards strong enough to support progression without turning cosmic horror into a vending machine.

The rule:

Unlock understanding first, capability second, raw power last.

The best rewards change how the player reads and manipulates alien systems.

## 1. Progression lanes

### Lane A — xenobiology literacy

Unlocks:
- scanner classifications;
- strain recognition;
- host anatomy overlays;
- field-coherence estimate;
- safe sample handling;
- migration prediction.

This is knowledge progression.

### Lane B — contamination survival

Unlocks:
- better filters;
- sealed cargo;
- decontamination;
- attachment removal;
- spore-resistant subsystems.

This expands route access.

### Lane C — ecological manipulation

Unlocks:
- lures;
- masking signals;
- relay disruption;
- capture tools;
- carrier diversion tools.

This expands verbs.

### Lane D — precursor access

Unlocks:
- machine protocol recognition;
- gate handshakes;
- structure access;
- containment controls;
- inertial/field technology.

This expands deep-world topology.

### Lane E — high-risk biotech

Unlocks:
- engineered materials inspired by biology;
- nonliving derivatives;
- dangerous living modules only if later design explicitly admits them.

This should be the smallest and most morally/operationally complicated lane.

## 2. Scanner unlocks

### Bio-Spectral Pass

Adds:
- organic vs mineral discrimination;
- live/dead estimate.

Early reward.

### Filament Contrast

Reveals:
- conductive fungal strands;
- contamination path through a wreck.

### Host Map

Identifies:
- host tissue vs fungal tissue;
- major organs;
- attachment interfaces.

### Field Coherence Meter

Shows:
- low/medium/high network synchronization;
- relay direction estimate.

Do not display a magic exact "hivemind HP" number.

### Echo Recorder

Captures:
- repeated field patterns;
- route-memory signatures;
- rare deep traces.

Used for missions and lore.

### Precursor Correlator

Overlays:
- machine suppression fields;
- ancient quarantine markers;
- matching biological signatures.

High-tier bridge between both mystery layers.

## 3. Ship survival equipment

### Quarantine Locker

Cargo sub-compartment for contaminated salvage.

Benefits:
- avoids station refusal;
- reduces contamination event chance.

Costs:
- cargo volume;
- mass;
- price.

### Filter Stack I–III

Benefits:
- longer exposure tolerance;
- reduced module fouling.

Cost:
- maintenance consumables;
- power.

### Hull Purge Ring

Short-range mechanical/thermal purge around hull.

Use:
- detach small parasites;
- clear spores.

Constraint:
- not a weapon against large organisms.

### Sacrificial Intake

Disposable filter cartridge protects cooling/intake system from bloom.

Creates consumable economy.

### Bioseal Coating

Nonliving engineered surface inspired by infected mineral-binding tissue.

Effect:
- lowers attachment duration;
- corrosion resistance.

Keep it material science, not "wear fungus armor."

## 4. Ecological tools

### Heat Lure

Deployable decoy reactor signature.

Use:
- divert predators;
- steer carriers slowly;
- create openings.

### Wake Beacon

Imitates a traffic wake pattern used by migratory organisms.

Use:
- guide;
- bait;
- investigate route-memory.

### Quiet Mask

Broadcasts a low-interest signature learned from the Quiet.

Effect:
- lowers investigation probability for selected fauna.

Not invisibility.

### Relay Needle

Temporary local interference pulse.

Effect:
- reduces field coherence in a radius;
- makes packs less synchronized.

Tradeoff:
- can panic simple fauna.

### Grief-Static Prototype

Late-game research tool inspired by Vethari grief-isolation observations.

Effect:
- creates a short noisy field window.

Constraints:
- rare;
- unreliable;
- does not "turn off the hive";
- stronger hosts may react unpredictably.

### Capture Cradle

Massline-compatible cage for small/medium organisms.

Use:
- live-capture contracts;
- relocation.

### Spore Sampler

Collects airborne particulate without opening cargo to contamination.

### Cyst Suppressor

Prevents reproductive sacs from rupturing during tow/capture.

## 5. Physics-combat unlocks

Alien ecology should feed SpaceFace's physical verbs.

### Resonant Massline Tip

Improves attachment to biological/mineral hybrid surfaces.

### Tendril Cutter

Short-range utility tool for freeing ships or structures from growth.

Not a general DPS upgrade.

### Impulse Shepherd Charge

Low-damage directional impulse designed to move fauna.

### Bio-Anchor Harpoon

Temporary anchor into giant sessile organisms for slingshot/tow mechanics.

### Pressure-Sac Mine

Recovered/engineered nonliving copy of a biological pressure bladder used for high-impulse deployment.

Keep tech derivative rather than carrying live infected tissue by default.

## 6. Salvage resources

### Calcified Filament

Use:
- lightweight structural composite;
- bioseal coating.

### Conductive Mycelfiber

Use:
- sensor component;
- field-analysis equipment.

Name may change to avoid "mycelium" overuse.

### Relay Nodule

Rare intact tissue.

Use:
- research;
- field tools.

High quarantine risk.

### Membrane Laminate

Use:
- damping;
- heat sail;
- lightweight armor adjunct.

### Cyst Resin

Use:
- sealant;
- filter consumable.

### Mineralized Nerve Glass

Deep-region material.

Use:
- high-tier sensors;
- precursor-biological interface experiments.

### Host Archive Sample

Not a commodity stack.

Unique research object carrying deep trace.

## 7. Precursor-machine unlocks

These should feel like permissions and principles before power.

### Gate Handshake Token

Allows:
- one class of dormant gate to recognize player.

### Route Revocation Key

Allows:
- inspect why a route was closed;
- maybe temporarily close/open approved gates.

Heavy world consequence.

### Surveyor Prism

Advanced scan focusing.

Effect:
- precise geometry/anomaly mapping.

### Containment Seal

Allows:
- reactivate ancient quarantine field at select sites.

### Inertial Datum

Navigation upgrade derived from precursor reference frame technology.

Effect:
- better deep-route solution;
- perhaps safer wormhole traversal.

### Lattice Coupler

Allows ship systems to interface with one class of precursor structure.

### Witness Mark

Not gear.

A machine-recorded status showing the player satisfied a protocol. Other robots respond differently.

### Null Corridor Map

Reveals safe routes through high-field ecology based on ancient suppression geometry.

### Revocation Beacon

Late-game deployable that can make certain precursor systems treat a temporary volume as closed.

Severe limits and cooldown/cost.

## 8. Mythic rewards

Reserve a few rewards that feel legendary.

### The Unbroken Lens

A precursor survey instrument that can reveal one layer of hidden world geometry no human scanner sees.

### The Closed Route

Knowledge reward: coordinates to a path removed from every human chart.

### The Quiet Equation

A field-analysis result that predicts when a networked region will enter a low-coherence window.

### The First Safe Sample

A fully isolated active fungal sample.

Narratively enormous; mechanically unlocks research line.

### The Returned Signal

A decoded route-memory pattern that points toward an ancient event.

### The Gate's Exception

One precursor machine grants the player an explicit protocol exception. This should feel more important than a gun.

## 9. Economy integration

Alien materials create new trade tensions.

Markets:
- licensed xenobiology;
- black-market samples;
- quarantine premiums;
- contaminated salvage discount;
- specialized filters;
- research contracts;
- precursor relic brokerage.

Price drivers:
- recent outbreaks;
- station quarantine;
- research demand;
- route closure;
- loss of a sample lab;
- new deep-sector access.

Factions:
- Meridian pays for exclusivity;
- Concord pays for custody/compliance;
- Free Frontier pays for open research;
- Quiet pays for things nobody can legally move;
- DMC pays for industrially useful material;
- Choir values relic significance;
- Vael pays for practical containment information.

## 10. Reputation and legality

Alien goods should carry flags such as:
- sterile;
- exposed;
- active;
- precursor;
- restricted;
- custody-sealed.

Consequences:
- station denial;
- inspection;
- fines;
- seizure;
- special buyer access.

This turns the existing customs/economy systems into content.

## 11. Tech-tree philosophy

Do not add one giant "Alien Tech" tree.

Instead:
- scanner branch gets analysis upgrades;
- survival/ship branch gets filters/seals;
- Massline/physics branch gets ecological manipulation;
- exploration branch gets precursor access;
- research unlocks recipes.

Alien discovery should deepen existing play styles.

## 12. High-risk optional path

A later expansion could let players install living or semi-living modules.

Only admit if the game can support meaningful downside.

Possible:
- self-sealing membrane;
- biological sensor fan;
- regenerative hull patch.

Costs:
- quarantine;
- unpredictable behavior near field relays;
- station refusal;
- maintenance with biological feedstock;
- contamination risk.

Without serious downside, living modules turn horror into free stats and should be excluded.

## 13. Reward cadence

Early alien rewards:
information.

Mid:
access and safety.

Late:
manipulation.

Very late:
precursor permissions and rare transformative technology.

This mirrors the player's shift from ignorance to competence without making them master the ecology.

## 14. Acceptance

Rewards succeed when:
- the first exciting unlock is not a gun;
- alien materials create new economic decisions;
- deep exploration produces capabilities that open new routes and interactions;
- precursor rewards feel qualitatively different from fungus-derived rewards;
- no reward trivializes the Vethari or permanently switches off contamination.
