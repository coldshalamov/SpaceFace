# 16 — The Unclosed

A damaged Verge-Layer containment instrument. The procedure was: fold a volume inside-out so contamination has no outside to escape through. The termination condition was lost. It has been folding the only volume it still owns — itself — ever since.

Most leftover machines are exact, still, and not hostile. This one is the exception the ossuary racks exist to store. Its category table maps a *moving* mass inside its volume to "contamination outside the fold." A hull that holds still, or that leaves, drops out of the category. The sane machines do not repair it and do not take the kill as a protocol violation.

## What you see

From above it is the shadow of a tesseract, not a shimmer.

- Obsidian outer square, nacre inner square scaling through it.
- Four cubic cassettes, one at each corner. Borg only in the sense of repeated exact modules.
- One seam that does not meet, lit amber. Every other seam is cold teal.
- During the grow (the first half of a 12s fold) the *next* hazard is already on the ground: a finite chord, a ribbon to a parked corner, or a hatch ring. The pass only hardens it.
- Recover (phase 0.50–0.72) is a dark hollow. That is the window. Hull is 300. No regenerating armor.

## What it does

One attack per fold, on the rising edge into phase 0.50. Never two on the same beat.

| Fold | You see | You do |
|---|---|---|
| Shear | A pale chord from one face to a fixed point, lit the whole grow. It does not start on you. | Sidestep it, or cross and your velocity mirrors off the line and you take 22. |
| Corner | One corner lifts onto a *fixed* orbit with a ribbon home. The ribbon is the hazard. The corner is a light payload (mass 24). | Stay off the ribbon. Shoot it or tether it and the snap fizzles. |
| Inside-out | Radial hatches fill a ring one screen wide (120), inside the 200 standoff, so the edge is a choice. | Be sliding sideways. At the swallow, radial speed flips once. Tangential speed stays. No damage. |
| Misrepair | Only if you sat inside 160 for the whole charge. A plate tracks you. One line: `APPEAL WINDOW CLOSED.` | Boost past 40, or take one hard hit, and the plate shears off as something you can tether. Otherwise the fold fires a shear instead. |

It slides about 90 along a lit leading edge during the grow, then stops. It does not chase past a 640 leash. Leaving is VACATE. Holding under 12 through one recover drops the classification.

## What killing it means

`FOLD COMPLETE.` The body does not come back (the flag is `machineSites.ashfall_unclosed.foldCleared`, written only from `entity:killed` on the body). A smaller hatch scar stays. One protocol splinter — an existing inertial datum, not a new gun and not a scrap wreck — drops once. Wingmen, civilians, and the Verge-Layer reputation ledger are not part of the category.

## Where

Ashfall Reach, on the rim of the machine ossuary, in the empty slot. Sector-local anchor `(-1520, -1200)`.
