# Pixel review and truthful status

## Selected direction

The generated lamellar-shear concept is materially stronger than the rejected M7/A6/C5 language. It supplies a visible anatomical construction target: overlapping keeled shells, shrouded jaw roots, compressed soft interfaces and structural membrane ribs. It is **reference-only**, with approximate cross-view consistency and excessive generated scratch detail. The lower thumbnail in that image is illustrative and is not a measured 170 px test.

## Actual geometry iterations

- **R1 rejected:** broad continuous faceted shield, glaring polygon highlights, poorly separated lamellae. Jaw roots and fins exist as geometry, but that did not make the whole object good
- **R2 rejected:** stronger overlap and controlled normals remove some polygon glare, but the front shield is still a broad plain blanket. Merely darkening the pink or smoothing highlights is insufficient
- **R3 rejected:** the dorsal channel and growth-flute experiment adds angular disruption without enough anatomical logic. Some jagged highlights and locally intersecting ridge parts make the surface worse. Do not use its added detail as a production donor
- **R4 retained as an unfinished construction study:** the head is a smaller shield, shoulder guards are distinct, and real dark interfaces separate the abdominal units. The model reads consistently in top/side/angled views, and paired jaws/vanes retain their direction at 170 px. At 96 px the main silhouette survives but shell and root details are substantially lost

## R4 defects that remain visible

1. The outer shell still looks too homogeneous and manufactured in close view. The production artist must articulate grown lip thickness, local crown/shoulder variation and directional material response without noisy scratches
2. The jaw cuff and distal shield remain too geometrically uniform. The close root view shows hose-like soft tissue, not the fully integrated compression anatomy in the concept. Root folds need continuous sculpted construction, not rings sitting over a tube
3. The dorsal shell near the new dark interfaces needs cleaner seating; the top rear seam reveals awkward small dark wedges. These are P1 form/craft issues, not a GPU limitation
4. The mouth is genuinely open but its hood/floor is still too geometric. The side view also shows that the body is very flat, so production needs a deliberately authored ventral transition rather than leaving a display-only underside
5. Vane spars read at close zoom, but the root-to-membrane transition is still blunt and the repeated straight ribs are too mechanical. Keep their load path; reshape them as continuous grown support fingers
6. No motion rig, UV/bake, authored LOD or truthful collider has been produced. Forty separate mesh objects are an authoring convenience, not a draw-call budget. The prototype has four materials and 20,236 triangles

**Do not call R4 a visually accepted or production-ready asset.** The direction/reference is ready for a production builder; R4 is editable geometric evidence and a starting point with specifically rejected craft. It cannot be used to claim the user's criticism has been fully resolved.

## Handoff decision

Proceed with the lamellar-shear direction as the reference brief, while requiring a fresh production source that closes the above craft issues. Do not clone R4 into the other species. The next review should show one substantially completed Mite beside the concept and a strong finished SpaceFace hull, in neutral clay and the real Look, before propagation to Splitter/Charger.
