**Current lane:** THE WORLD / THE LONG GAME

## Existing foundation, not a blank slate

Durable wrecks, bounded loss provenance, lawful action authorization and remembered relations already exist. A failure should change the next playable situation; avoid merely adding another notification or reputation scalar.

This is a source-informed working description, not a fresh gameplay acceptance claim. Read current source before treating any subfeature as missing. [Source provenance](../SOURCE_PROVENANCE.md) records scope and limitations.

## Domain contract

Preserve canonical heat/faction writers and provenance. Knowledge is local evidence, not an omniscient police feed. Do not overwrite mission-bearing labels with unrelated ledger events. Consequence rewards are idempotent.

## Reusable implementation workflow

1. Identify the existing event and stable loss/actor/site identity. Use actual causal provenance rather than inferring blame from proximity.
2. Define who knows what, when knowledge becomes actionable and what physical change follows. Bound memory and use existing eviction semantics.
3. Implement a second situation with an actionable resolution or further cost; do not turn every failure into a reset or an automatic reward.
4. Send heat, rep, credits and mission consequences through their owners. Preserve lawful final-fire authorization and witness absence cases.
5. Test absent/mistaken witnesses, actor death, repeated discovery, save/revisit and two consequences arriving together. Verify no double restitution or inherited false guilt.
6. Play the first and returning visit. The changed berth, route, job or body must carry the story even with subtitles and optional lore skipped.

## Ordinary-route proof

Cause or witness the relevant event, leave, meet the affected actor/service or find the wreck again, then take a physical action that changes the consequence.

Choose one seed/route and compare it before and after; keep the scenario's meaningful variables fixed. Use both a competent intended action and a plausible mistake. Source or headless proof cannot establish visual, audio, feel or frame-pacing quality; inspect/listen/play the relevant route where tools permit, otherwise report that portion unproven.
