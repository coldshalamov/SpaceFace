# Resolved specification — Charon Bloom without a second game engine

Companion to [SF-181 — The Charon Bloom as one complete first encounter](../plans/13-ecology/SF-181-the-charon-bloom-as-one-complete-first-encounter.md). Conditional expansion: reconcile with the current admitted ecology program before implementation.

## Canon and scope

Read [canon synthesis](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/c92756afb46a9115d47e9d1757369678023efce4/design/alien-ecology-program/00_CANON_SYNTHESIS.md), [engineering architecture](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/c92756afb46a9115d47e9d1757369678023efce4/design/alien-ecology-program/08_ENGINEERING_ARCHITECTURE.md) and [the existing vertical slice](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/c92756afb46a9115d47e9d1757369678023efce4/design/alien-ecology-program/09_VERTICAL_SLICE.md). This is one Vethari-linked fungal phenomenon with bounded noisy shared influence, not a second independent fungus species or omniscient hive. Infection does not automatically add brain mass or intelligence. Advanced fallen hosts remain a later story horizon, not ordinary opening enemies.

## Selected playable scene

Use the planned dead DMC service barge/Cinder Nursery near wrecks, residual heat, maintenance recorder, local relay and small fauna cast. Arrival is initially calm. A synchronized turn hints at finite local coordination. Restoring actual local power changes heat and activity. Recovering the recorder can be rushed, quieted by changing the site, or relocated through real towing. Keep the original ordinary-maintenance identity of the recorder; do not make every discovery an alien superweapon.

Reuse the canon's small cast and module family as the first slice, not all ecology proposals at once. A presentation-only needle cluster need not become a separate high-frequency simulated agent. Distinguish visual dressing from actors whose collisions and decisions matter.

## One bounded law, existing owners

Separate physical contamination from what the pilot has learned. Site state belongs under the current world/site owner, discovery under existing knowledge/scanner owners, behavior under existing encounter/AI laws and consequences under current custody/aftermath. A pure shared helper may compute bounded local coherence from existing factors if the admitted engineering plan calls for it; it is not a new registered global infection manager.

Local heat can attract a susceptible actor through its actual perception. Relay disruption changes coordination, not universal life support: surviving individuals may flee, panic or follow basic drives. Suppression by a separate precursor machine uses that machine's real local powered operation; it does not reveal a universal origin or provide a global cure.

## Approaches and consequences

A careful pilot changes power/heat, isolates the relevant compartment and recovers useful information with limited disturbance. A forceful pilot can tow or break the obstruction and accept a more active site. A destructive pilot can burn or rupture fragile growth, gaining immediate safety/material at the cost of an intact sample or future quiet state. All rewards and cargo remain under existing terms and owners.

The durable aftermath should be small and explicit: stable/quieter recovery, low-coherence survivors after relay loss, disturbed/ruptured site, or burned safer site with lost research value. Store only the actual selected consequence and necessary module states; reconstruct transient motion and influence on return.

## Acceptance sequence

Play calm arrival, partial observation, activation, a counteraction, recovery and return. Repeat while ignoring the suggested route, destroying the relay, towing a component, losing a fauna actor, leaving mid-action and restoring a supported save. A synchronized response outside the helper's actual range or with no valid local connection is a failure. No hidden adaptation should instantly change armor or perfectly counter the player's last weapon.

The slice needs meaningful physical action, an interpretable mystery and a changed place—not a taxonomy document, a new faction label or a global simulation. Keep the deeper host-civilization horror offscreen until separately admitted. Do not delay current flight/economy/render repairs on this optional expansion.
