# 15 — The seven arena floors

**From the lead designer, 2026-11-10.** Each arena's identity, hazard and room changes are already designed in `design/program/finish/bible/ARENAS.md`. This page adds what makes a horde mode a *place you learn*: where the guns hang, where the freight runs, what the doors open, what the levers do, and the route a good player runs. Read with [12 — Swarm: the Pit](12_SWARM_THE_PIT.md).

**The Course names and the recipe book are the same thing.** The on-screen round names in 12 are what the player sees; the compositions in ARENAS §"wave-recipe book" are how they are made (*Lights Out* is Blackout, *Tug of War* is The Leash, *Long Shots* is Sniper Alley, *Breadcrumbs* is Minefield, *The Umbrella* is The Warden Wall, *Hands Off* is Anchors Aweigh, *The Law* is Customs Check, *Hot Potato* is Dart Rush).

## Rules for every floor

- **One starting room, two or three wings behind doors.** The start is small enough to learn in a minute.
- **A loop.** Once two doors are open there is a circuit a player can run with a train of enemies behind them. Every floor is drawn round its loop.
- **Fixed places.** Wall guns never move. The Forge never moves. Only the Crate moves, and it moves between marked spots.
- **The lane crosses the loop.** So every lap has a moment where the traffic matters.
- **Everything you can buy is lit, priced in figures you can read across the room, and on the radar.**
- **The secret is never hinted at in text.** The room shows it.

## 1. Ricochet Foundry — the pilot floor

| | |
|---|---|
| **Start** | the foundry floor between the bank plates. Gates N, E, S, W |
| **Wall guns** | *Pulse Laser* by the south gate (cheap, first buy); *Concussion Cannon* in the NE corner between two plates — you bank shots in from the first minute |
| **The lane** | the conveyor line, west to east through the middle. Container strings every forty seconds, amber lights, a klaxon |
| **Doors** | **The Casting Hall** (north): the furnace and its door trap, a Tap. **The Scrap Yard** (east): loose throwables everywhere, the second Crate spot, a Tap |
| **Traps** | the furnace door (Casting Hall); the crusher (centre, wakes at round ten) |
| **The Forge** | behind the furnace — you cross the hot side to reach it |
| **The loop** | floor → Casting Hall → behind the furnace → Scrap Yard → floor, crossing the conveyor twice |
| **Room changes** | ten: the crusher wakes and the conveyor carries bodies toward it. Twenty: a crucible tips and a slag river splits the floor |
| **Secret** | feed the furnace three different heavy wrecks in one round. It pours you a plate |
| **Round thirty** | the Forge Regent, here, with the furnace as its weapon and then yours |

## 2. Lagrange Crucible — open space, two moons

| | |
|---|---|
| **Start** | the still point between two gravity wells |
| **Wall guns** | on the two anchored rocks, which drift on a loop — the shop moves, and you learn its timetable. *Railgun* on one, *Disc Launcher* on the other |
| **The lane** | an ore string slingshotting round the warm moon, across the still point, round the cold one — a figure of eight you can read from anywhere |
| **Doors** | there are no walls, so doors are **beacons**: pay, and a third rock is towed in with a Tap and the Forge on it; pay again and the debris ring opens a gap to the far well |
| **Traps** | a well pulse: pay to make one moon *pull* for three seconds |
| **The loop** | the figure of eight. A player at speed rides it faster than anything chasing them |
| **Room changes** | ten: the rings tighten. Twenty: a shard calves off a moon and crosses as a moving wall |
| **Secret** | keep one object in orbit for three whole rounds. The still point opens |
| **Round thirty** | *Corsair Wing*, grown to nine, using the same slingshots you do |

## 3. Cinder Sluice — the river of slag

| | |
|---|---|
| **Start** | the middle island |
| **Wall guns** | *Heavy Beam* on the upstream island (melting things next to slag is the joke); *Bank Stream* on the downstream bank |
| **The lane** | the river itself carries slag bergs downstream on a rhythm — burning, throwable, and in the way |
| **Doors** | **The Upper Gate** (the sluice controls, a trap, a Tap) and **The Quench Hall** (cold, safe, the Forge, a Tap) |
| **Traps** | the sluice gate: flood the second channel on demand |
| **The loop** | island → upstream bank → across the gate → downstream bank → island. You cross the river twice a lap |
| **Room changes** | ten: the flow doubles and a second channel floods. Twenty: eruption — bergs break loose everywhere |
| **Secret** | ride the river against the flow to its source and shut the gate. The dry bed is full of what it swallowed |
| **Round thirty** | *Furnace Maw*: it drinks the river. Dam it |

## 4. Cryo Drift — the ice

| | |
|---|---|
| **Start** | a clearing between ice spires. No grip: whatever you push keeps going |
| **Wall guns** | inside the two frozen hulks, through a crack. *Concussion Cannon* in one — on ice, Kick is everything. *Plasma Cone* in the other: it thaws |
| **The lane** | an ice barge towed through on a cable, slow and enormous. Things freeze to it |
| **Doors** | **The Hulk** (interior: tight, a Tap, the Crate) and **The Shelf** (open ice, long sightlines, the Forge) |
| **Traps** | a spire charge: drop a spire and it shatters into a spray |
| **The loop** | clearing → through the Hulk → across the Shelf → clearing. On the Shelf you slide; in the Hulk you bounce |
| **Room changes** | ten: the floor breaks into floes. Twenty: a frozen warship thaws and sheds hull |
| **Secret** | something is frozen under the floor. Shatter all four corners in one slide |
| **Round thirty** | *Casket Worm*: it tunnels under the ice. Break the ice ahead of it |

## 5. Storm Lattice — the lightning

| | |
|---|---|
| **Start** | the insulated platform at the centre |
| **Wall guns** | *Railgun* on the east platform (a metal slug draws the arc after it); *EMP Disruptor* on the west |
| **The lane** | a cable car on the lattice itself, pylon to pylon. Arcs follow it |
| **Doors** | **The Relay** (control room: redirect which pylons are live; a Tap) and **The Cloud Bank** (fog, Ghosts, the Forge) |
| **Traps** | pylon switches: choose which span is live this round |
| **The loop** | platform → Relay → along the live span's edge → Cloud Bank → platform |
| **Room changes** | ten: arcs come faster and you can place conductors by throwing metal into gaps. Twenty: a pylon falls and the web redraws |
| **Secret** | redraw the web into one closed ring. It charges your Line for the rest of the run |
| **Round thirty** | *Adjudicator 44-C*: it rules which spans are live. Overrule it |

## 6. Asteroid Mill — the machinery

| | |
|---|---|
| **Start** | the loading dock |
| **Wall guns** | *Autocannon* on the dock; *Singularity Mortar* up on the control gantry, exposed |
| **The lane** | three belts carrying ore toward the drum. They reverse at round ten |
| **Doors** | **The Gantry** (high, exposed, the mill's controls and a Tap) and **The Breaker Field** (rocks being cracked; endless ammunition; the Forge) |
| **Traps** | the grinder: speed it up on demand. What goes in comes out as chunks you can use |
| **The loop** | dock → along the belt → Breaker Field → up to the Gantry → down to the dock |
| **Room changes** | ten: the main drum spins up and the belts reverse. Twenty: the drum breaks loose and rolls |
| **Secret** | jam the wheel with a boss wreck. There is a door behind it |
| **Round thirty** | *The Yardmaster* — here it owns the belts as well as the freight |

## 7. The Hive — the living room

| | |
|---|---|
| **Start** | the dead station's surviving control room: metal, lit, safe for a minute |
| **Wall guns** | in the station's surviving rooms. *Plasma Cone* (it burns sacs) in the first; *Nestbreaker Rack* in the second |
| **The lane** | none — instead, the **Breath**: the walls close and open on a slow rhythm, and corridors exist only on the exhale. That is the timetable here |
| **Doors** | you do not buy doors; you **cut** them. A wall that is cut stays cut for the run |
| **Traps** | a sac, popped early, strands its share of the wave where you want it |
| **The loop** | control room → first cut → the nest heart → second cut → control room, timed to the Breath |
| **Room changes** | ten: the walls breathe. Twenty: the Brood Queen wakes |
| **Secret** | there is a hull number under one wall. Pull that ship free and the Hive is missing that wall in every later run |
| **Round thirty** | *Brood Queen*. The room is her. Kill the room |

## How to build a floor and check your own work

1. **Grey boxes first.** The start room, the loop, the lane, with no art. Run the loop with thirty enemies behind you. If the loop is not fun as boxes, art will not save it.
2. **Then the fixed places**, lit and priced. Then the doors. Then the trap. Then the room changes. The secret last.
3. **Check with real runs:** `npm run player-truth -- --mode=swarm --arena="<name>" --rounds=3 --seed=4242`, and the stress probe for later rounds.
4. **The floor test:** after ten minutes a stranger can draw the floor from memory — the loop, where the Thump gun is, where the lane crosses.

**Ways this gets faked.** A wing with nothing in it. A door that opens onto more of the same room. A loop that is a circle with no decision on it. A lane that never matters on the route players actually run.
