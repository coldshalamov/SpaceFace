# 14 — Outposts and waystations: every other place, written out

**From the lead designer, 2026-11-10.** Companion to [13 — Jobs and heists](13_JOBS_AND_HEISTS.md). Same shape: *see it → the problem → two ways → what goes wrong → what is left.* People, landmarks and toys are from each sector's card. Where a job shares a name with one in the brief, it is that job.

An **outpost** gets two jobs and its street. A **waystation** gets one job and one street moment — small on purpose.

---

# The six outposts

## Dione Lane — the navy at home

### Job: Target Practice
**See it:** the range flag goes green and the scoreboard lights. Lt. Sorrel: *"One rule. Not on red."*
**The problem:** drones on rails, and a score that counts *style* — a drone thrown into another drone is worth five shot ones.
**Two ways:** shoot clean for a modest score — or Line a drone and bowl it down the rail.
**What goes wrong:** the flag goes red with your drone still in the air. Where it lands is your responsibility.
**What is left:** your name on the board, and a call sign the navy uses for you from now on.

### Job: Last Bus
**See it:** Late Benny at the apron light, a drill formation wheeling across the lane in front of him. *"Every week. Every single week."*
**The problem:** the formation will not break for a bus, and the bus cannot dodge.
**Two ways:** lead the bus through the gaps on Chief Baig's count — *"On my four"* — or tow it round the outside, through the reserve berths, where nobody is supposed to be.
**What goes wrong:** a cadet drifts out of line straight at the bus.
**What is left:** the bus runs on time through Dione, permanently. Baig, grudging: *"Again. With him."*

### On the street
- Reveille: every strobe syncs for ten seconds. Fly the lane during it and they sync to you.
- Sgt. Pol's tender COUNTERSIGN, half open, parts on tethers. Bump one and hand it back.

## Pallas Drift — the street through dead ships

### Job: Funeral Freight
**See it:** Mother Calloway's tow, a draped hull behind it, crawling down Rib Street while every light dims. *"Slow is the respect."*
**The problem:** she asks you to take the tow the last stretch. In its shadow, somebody is passing crates between two windows.
**Two ways:** tow slow and see nothing — or tow slow and bump the shadow, and find out what is so important.
**What goes wrong:** a salvage ram comes up the street fast. It will not dim and it will not slow.
**What is left:** Calloway's nod, and a berth she keeps for you. Or what was in the crates, and a street that goes quiet when you pass.

### Job: The Salvage Ram
**See it:** Fitch's crew lining up a ram on a hull with one lit window. *"Empty. Probably."*
**The problem:** they will cut in thirty seconds.
**Two ways:** knock — bump the hull so whoever is inside shows a light — or Pin the ram where it sits and argue afterwards.
**What goes wrong:** there are two hulls with lit windows, and the ram is already moving.
**What is left:** a painted mark on that hull — *KNOCKED* — that the salvagers honour. Mara's people notice who painted it.

### On the street
- Bounce plates on every corner. A ferry banks off one in front of you.
- A painted arrow that points somewhere no chart shows.

## Io Reach — the flag changes; the mail comes

### Job: Neither Side's Mail
**See it:** painters on the Authority Plate, and Wren Adeyemi with a mail sling and nowhere to send it. *"Flag's not my department."*
**The problem:** both flags want the bag. The chute is past both of them, and the Plume is due.
**Two ways:** wait, ride the Plume, and arrive faster than either can react — or sling the bag from long range and let it fly in alone.
**What goes wrong:** the Plume comes early. Everything loose in the lane goes with it.
**What is left:** your streak chalked under the Plate. Both sides know the mail got through, and who carried it.

### Job: The Floor Changes Hands
**See it:** Gus Varga at a dead pump. *"Pump first. Politics after."* Two groups of ships facing each other across the dock.
**The problem:** the part has to be held in place across a berth that is about to be fought over.
**Two ways:** fix the pump for whoever holds the floor now — or hold the part, wait for the Plume to scatter both, and fix it for nobody.
**What goes wrong:** somebody fires first, and it is not clear who.
**What is left:** the flag that flies over Io is the one you left standing. The pump works either way. Gus only cares about that.

### On the street
- Mailbags sling into chutes from further back each try. The chute keeps score.
- The Plume. Ride it once and you will plan every trip round it.

## Charon Expanse — the garden

### Job: Green Debt
**See it:** pale growth bridging two hulls with lit windows behind it. Em Sato: *"That one's a wall. That one's a mouth."*
**The problem:** the garden needs feeding to hold the homes up, and it has started on the freight route.
**Two ways:** cut the growth back from the route with your Line — or feed it a wreck on the far side, so it grows *away*.
**What goes wrong:** Checker Ruth has not knocked on that wreck yet. *"Knock first. Always."*
**What is left:** the garden's shape is the shape you gave it, for good. A bridge where you fed it; a scar where you cut.

### Job: Second Skin
**See it:** a berth under quarantine lamps, a ship inside, growth closing over the door.
**The problem:** pull the ship out and the growth tears — and what it was holding up comes down.
**Two ways:** Pin the structure first, then pull — or cut a new door on the far side and take the ship out through it.
**What goes wrong:** the ship's pilot is awake, and does not want to leave.
**What is left:** a ship freed or a pilot who stayed. A reading on the growth, which is heavier than it should be.

### On the street
- The Breath: the whole garden pulses. Things you leave near it at night are further in by morning.
- A gardener's tender pruning a lane exactly one ship wide.

## Veil Nebula — the lighthouse

### Job: The Reading That Came Back Heavy
**See it:** the beam sweeps past. Dr. Suhl: *"Not what it means. What it weighed."*
**The problem:** tow her instrument to the cold-draw mouth, hold it in the pull for a count of ten, and bring it back. It comes back heavier than it went.
**Two ways:** hold against the draw on thrust — or swing round the mouth and let the curve do the holding.
**What goes wrong:** the storm wall pulses, and now you are towing something heavy through lightning.
**What is left:** a buoy on the circuit with your name. And a number Suhl will not say aloud, which is 12.4.

### Job: Lightning Rod
**See it:** a survey buoy jammed dark, and Pips circling it. *"Four. Five. Huh."*
**The problem:** it needs a jolt. The storm has plenty.
**Two ways:** tow a rod and hold it beside the buoy until the storm obliges — or throw the rod past the buoy and let the strike chase it.
**What goes wrong:** lightning chains through metal. You are metal.
**What is left:** the buoy lit; a trick you can now use on anything that needs a strike — including enemies.

### On the street
- The Sweep. Fly in the beam and the fog ahead of you is clear.
- The sealed courier, on its line, on time, carrying one box.

## Ashfall Reach — the sky is a hull

### Job: Last Relief
**See it:** one row of windows in the scar, half of them dark. Maud Ibekwe: *"I count people. Not flags."*
**The problem:** water tanks, across open armour plate, through ash that slows everything.
**Two ways:** the long way round the plates — or hand the tanks to Lazlo's crane and ride with them over the top. *"Hold still. I've got you."*
**What goes wrong:** an ash drift buries the rail. The crane stops with you on the hook.
**What is left:** Wick lights one window per tank. The count is on the hull for anyone to read.

### Job: Surface Rights
**See it:** a salvage claim wants to lift one armour plate. The scar's power line runs under it.
**The problem:** they have the paper. The scar has the people.
**Two ways:** move the power line first, by hand, pylon by pylon — or let the plate lift and catch the line as it parts.
**What goes wrong:** the plate is far heavier than its paperwork says.
**What is left:** a scar that keeps its lights, or a claim that owes you. Either way the weight goes in your log.

### On the street
- Plough a trail through an ash drift. It stays.
- The crane. Anything you leave on the rail gets carried.

## How to build these and check your own work

The same loop as the towns (see any sector card): look first, toy first, then the job, then the mark; a fresh reviewer answers the five Stranger questions from captures alone. For a waystation, the Stranger Test is three questions: **what is this place, who is here, and what did I leave behind.**
