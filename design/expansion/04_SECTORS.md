# 04 — Sectors

Twenty-five sectors in eight regions (01 §1). Each sector's **sentence** is the existing one from
`src/data/sectorWayOfLife.js`: it is canon and stays. This file adds what a physics-and-swarm game
needs from each place:

- **Throw:** the signature ammunition, the bodies that make this sector's fights look like no
  other's.
- **Hazard:** the sector's physical rule (row 464 "Every sector has a law" owns the wiring).
- **Bloom:** its tide status (03 §1.4).
- **Who lives here:** resident cast, existing and new.
- **Wonder:** its one landmark (row 450 "One wonder per sector").
- **Set piece:** the sector's best authored moment.
- **Keeps:** what persists from the player's acts (law 6).
- **Build:** the assets it still needs (05 rows).

**Canon added in this file (PROPOSED CANON, for row 537):** each sector's named wonder (the Directorate Tower, Gate 3's dormant Auditor, the Cathedral as an ore-hollowed rock, the Furnace Crown, the Cut, the Shoal, the Switchyard, the Flag Station, the capsized station, the Trophy Wall, the Memorial Gardens, the Blue Throat, the Lit Veils, the Song Field, the Wake, the License Board, the Lit Path, the Hush, the Well mouth); Hale and Dree at Dione's Gate 3; the residents placed per sector; ice that loses mass in sunlight; the ten Cull arenas.

The story band mapping comes from `design/world-identity/STORY_SECTOR_MAP.md` (Tycho = Tethys,
Meridian = Ceres, Hollow = Pallas, Cinder = Charon, Skerris = Sker Haven).

## The Core

### Helios Prime — "The harbour: law, tutorial, calm."
- **Throw:** cargo pods, lane buoys, dock furniture, and Concord picket formations, the first
  bowling pins.
- **Hazard:** customs weirs and dense licensed traffic. A collision files an insurance claim, and
  the claim follows you.
- **Bloom:** clean.
- **Who lives here:** Wex (Bay 7), Vesper SV-3, the Memorial Thief, the Choir-Tender relief berth,
  Hasp (Second Rinse, on the Helios–Ceres chord). The Directorate tower stands here (Helix and Vale's
  office).
- **Wonder:** **the Directorate Tower**, a white glass spire whose windows are lit in rows like a
  ledger. Every light is a desk.
- **Set piece:** B0's departure with 47-A. The first glimpse of a Too-Perfect Pack running the
  harbour edge.
- **Keeps:** the dents you put in the harbour. Damaged lane furniture stays damaged until a repair
  tug fixes it, and you can watch it work.
- **Build:** the Directorate tower (P1), the Bay 7 rack interior (P1), the Commander picket (P1).

### Dione Lane — "The cleared lane: customs reads, then the convoy leaves."
- **Throw:** toll pylons, scale-deck counterweights, convoy containers.
- **Hazard:** the customs funnel (the existing weir) and **The Weighing** at Gate 3.
- **Bloom:** clean.
- **Who lives here:** Hale and Inspector Sula Dree at Gate 3. **TARE** (new), the weighing robot on a
  dead scale deck.
- **Wonder:** **Gate 3**, the oldest working gate in the Core, with a Verge Gate Auditor dormant in
  its arch.
- **Set piece:** B6's waived fine, where Dree waves you through.
- **Keeps:** your customs record, and the gate log Dree reads.
- **Build:** Scale Barge (P1), Gate 3 arch dressing (P2), TARE (P2).

## The Belt

### Ceres Belt — "The working belt: mining, haulers, the Cathedral."
- **Throw:** ore rock (metallic and silicate families), hopper lids, Breaker chunks.
- **Hazard:** the Breaker's cutting lines and hauler cross-traffic.
- **Bloom:** incursion around derelict haulers.
- **Who lives here:** Ruckus RU-7, Solstice SL-9, the Ceres workfleet, Drift (the Meridian weigher),
  Nine-Finger Abe (new).
- **Wonder:** **the Cathedral**, an ore-hollowed rock the size of a station, cut into vaults by sixty
  years of mining.
- **Set piece:** B1's first Bloom-infested ore hauler: mites spill out of the hold into a working
  yard. The Claim Jump.
- **Keeps:** rock scars, Deed Names and field depletion (all exist).
- **Build:** ore rock material families (P0), claim pylons (P1).

### Vesta Forge — "The foundry: furnace hazards, industry."
- **Throw:** **slag bergs** (hot: they ignite spore drift and soften on impact) and feedstock pallets.
- **Hazard:** furnace heat lanes. Anything thrown through a heat lane comes out hot.
- **Bloom:** **Furnace maws** hunt the reactor approach.
- **Who lives here:** Slate the welder, the Blind Welder, the Eighth Bell, the Runaway Kiln, Pallet
  Jack, the Mirrorjaw Foreman and the Forge Regent.
- **Wonder:** **the Furnace Crown**, a ring of foundry stacks venting light like a crown on fire.
- **Set piece:** B3's Variance Adjustment shakedown: a fourth pressure spike, live in a slag storm.
- **Keeps:** slag fields grow where you fought.
- **Build:** slag rock family (P0), Furnace maw (P1).

### Hyperion Cut — "The west cut: ore in, refinery out."
- **Throw:** cut ore and canyon rubble.
- **Hazard (new):** **the Cut**, a canyon through a shattered moonlet. Hauling through it can trigger
  rock slides that roll down the cut behind you.
- **Bloom:** thin.
- **Who lives here:** claim crews. **The Breaker Derby** (new event): Drift crews race to split the
  biggest rock.
- **Wonder:** **the Cut** itself, seen end-on: a straight canyon of light two thousand units long.
- **Set piece:** the Avalanche Haul. Outrun a slide with a full tow (07 E-12).
- **Keeps:** slide debris stays where it fell and changes the route.
- **Build:** canyon wall kit (P2).

### Rhea Cinder — "The scorched claim: ore that pays same-shift."
- **Throw:** scorched lots (radiation-hot ore).
- **Hazard:** thin approach lanes where raiders eat slow haulers.
- **Bloom:** tidal.
- **Who lives here:** **TALLYHO** (new), a loose Cull scoreboard robot.
- **Wonder:** **the Cinder Field**, black rock glowing orange from beneath.
- **Set piece:** the scorched convoy run under a Reach raid with a Bloom tide behind it, so two
  enemies at once can be thrown at each other.
- **Keeps:** convoy wrecks feed the next tide unless someone clears them.
- **Build:** scorched ore material (P1).

### Nereid Shoal — "The ice shoal: ore sold the same shift."
- **Throw:** ice blocks. **Ice loses mass in sunlight** (Drift's moisture-loss mechanic made
  physical), so ice cargo is a race.
- **Hazard:** sublimation on sunlit legs.
- **Bloom:** clean to incursion.
- **Who lives here:** ice crews and the waystation scale.
- **Wonder:** **the Shoal**, a shallow sea of ice floes that reflects the star.
- **Set piece:** the Melt Race (07 E-14).
- **Keeps:** cut ice floes stay cut.
- **Build:** ice material family (P0), sublimation VFX (P1).

### Io Reach — "The contested floor: mail still runs, the flag does not."

### Kepler Scar — "The capsized bazaar: surplus nobody asks about."

- **Bloom:** clean.
- **Who lives here:** **RECEIPT** (new), the receipt-printer robot.
- **Wonder:** **the capsized station**, upside down in a scar of rock and still trading.
- **Set piece:** a bazaar brawl where the no-weapons rule means only thrown objects are legal.
- **Keeps:** RECEIPT's receipts. Every visit adds to the bazaar's posted list.
- **Build:** capsized station dressing (P2).

`storm_lattice`, `asteroid_mill`, `the_hive`) keep their laws. The Katamari arena ladder (§33 row
485) needs arenas that **grow with the Line rating**, and each new one reuses its region's
throwables.
