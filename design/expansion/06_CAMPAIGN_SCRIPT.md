# 06 — Campaign script

The story is canon and does not change here. The canon sources are
`docs/worldbuilding/story/THE-STORYLINE.md` (Books 0–7, the interludes, the five doors and the
epilogue), `CREATIVE-DIRECTION.md` R1–R15, and the chapter files. **What this file adds is how each
Book plays**:

- its physical promise (the new thing your hands can do);
- its **Line Class** milestone (08 §2);
- the enemy family it introduces;
- its missions, set piece and boss;
- the world thread it advances;
- the receipt it leaves on the map.

Protected lines are quoted exactly. Terminal zones keep the canon's airless register: no jokes,
no bright colour, no music stings. **Vethari stay off-frame.** **Vale is never shown**: only a
signature, an authorization line and one spoken line.

**Canon added in this file (PROPOSED CANON, for row 537).** Story *outcomes* are unchanged. These are added *events and objects*: the unknown vessel's black Line and the 12.4 t strain reading; the Infested Hauler; the Compost Run and Moss-Keeps-Moss in B2; the Nest in B2; VARIANCE ADJUSTMENT as a Hawser-class tug whose seam fails at the fourth strain spike; one Gate Audit closure per Book from B3 (Kepler, Io, Eris); Cade Haltred collecting for Callum in B5; the Evidence Locker heist; the Empire Seed built from towed modules; the Hive-hulk in Shaft 7; the Long Field broken by shepherds; the Iron Maw dismantled into the Gutted Maw; one playable act per door; the Cull ladder; the Orphaned Shift trials.

That is about 20 hours of main route. Faction arcs, machine-people, the Cull ladder and Fulfillment
orders add 15–25 more.

**Two world threads run through every Book** (01 §2). They are physical and visible on the chart:

- **The Bloom tide** rises book by book. Clearing it and feeding the Understory gardens pushes it
  back locally, and nothing pushes it back globally.
- **The Gate Audits** close one outer gate per Book from B3 on. The player can delay a closure
  (E-32) but cannot stop the policy.

The Slow Gray ticker falls every Book and never recovers (epilogue canon).

## Prologue — The Manifest

The manifest card plays over the title (it already exists as the canon opener):

```
MANIFEST — TESSERA / VHL-4471-T
SLOT01   TITANIUM ALLOY                     12400KG
SLOT99   UNCLASSIFIED COMPOSITE                3.1 KG   [PERSONAL EFFECTS — DO NOT TRANSFER]
```

The cut goes straight to the cockpit at Helios Bay 7, 04:00 ship-time.

## Book Zero — Cold Start

- **Physical promise:** the Line. Thrust, brake, latch, swing and release, with the first throw in
  the first two minutes.
- **Line Class:** I (20 t). Pods, buoys and pebbles.
- **Missions.**
  1. **Pre-flight at Bay 7.** The crate is already aboard. Latch and stow a loose pod. The Line's
     strain readout shows its mass, which is the first lesson: *the Line reads true*.
  2. **Contract 47-A.** Escort formation out of Helios. Ordinary life on the lane: tugs, a patrol,
     a survey ship scanning a field. The quiet is there so the ambush means something.
  3. **The Skim.** The escorts die. `ESCORT-02 ELIMINATED. ESCORT-01 ELIMINATED. CARGO DRONE 03
     LOST. UNKNOWN VESSEL DEPARTED.` An unknown hull with **a black Line of its own** takes exactly
     the alloy off your tow. You may fight it physically in a tug-of-war you are built to lose. At
     the instant of the theft your strain gauge reads **12.4 t**, so the player now knows the mass.
  4. **The Pit.** The manifest reads `SLOT01 TITANIUM ALLOY 00000KG`. Payment is withheld. On the
     airlock: **THEY KNEW THE MASS.** Sump is sweeping chalk dust across the bay. He waves at the
     Tessera. Nobody waves back.
- **The first swarm.** On the lane back, a derelict cargo drone spills a small mite flock. It is the
  first swarm pocket (E-01 lite), and you clear it with pods. LOG: `MITE COUNT REVISED DOWNWARD.`
- **The receipt it leaves.** The ambush site keeps its escort wrecks, which are B1's first salvage.

## Book One — Honest Work

- **Physical promise:** weighing. The Line's strain gauge is a scale, and the world's scales lie.
- **Line Class:** II (100 t). Containers.
- **Introduces:** needle mites and hull leeches in a real infestation, plus Glassbacks (guns ping
  off them, so you smash them).
- **Missions.**
  1. **Routine Alloy Run** (Tethys / Tycho). Twelve tonnes, not twelve point four. The category
     changes mid-transit, and the HUD does not notify you. LOG notes it anyway, flatly.
  2. **Scale 4.** Kessler weighs the cargo, with his thumb on the seal twice. Then you put the crate
     back on the deck with your own Line and read the difference yourself. The variance is a clue
     object you keep.
  3. **The Infested Hauler** (Ceres). A derelict ore hauler near the yard is crawling with mites. A
     Casket worm sleeps in its hold, and at Class II it is too big to move. **Lesson: some things
     you cannot move yet.** Fling the infested ore pods into the refinery's furnace intake (the
     scenery wants a body, CR-FEED), then seal the hold.
  4. **Moisture Loss** (optional, Nereid). Drift's ice haul. The cargo sublimates on sunlit legs, so
     race it (E-14).
- **Machine-people met:** Ruckus (Ceres) and Solstice (Ceres), both optional.
- **Wall line:** **REDISTRIBUTED TO THE HIGHEST BIDDER.**
- **The receipt it leaves.** The furnace-fed hauler becomes a stripped wreck in the yard, and the
  scale variance is logged.

**Interlude — The Unknown Vessel** (playable). Find the hull-spotter's buoy in Sector 4 and read
its log. It is a scan object, and the scene is quiet.
**Interlude — The Hand** (playable). At the Pit's third shift, Ivo paints over the wall. You watch,
and you cannot interact. The wall comes back.

## Book Two — First Blood

- **Physical promise:** lights are ammunition. At Class III you can throw Wasps.
- **Line Class:** III (400 t).
- **Introduces:** full Reach packs, the **Nest Carrier** mini-boss, the Understory (first contact),
  Lantern cysts.
- **Missions.**
  1. **The Cinder Board** (Charon). Rook's booth posts bounties. Run two ordinary bounties: tow a
     disabled raider to the booth, and throw a pack into the radiation lane.
  2. **The Compost Run** (E-26). Moss-Keeps-Moss asks for wrecks. Tow your bounty wrecks into the
     garden ring, and it grows its first stage. This is the first time the world visibly keeps what
     you did.
  3. **The Nest** (E-08). A Reach Nest Carrier stages raids on the radiation lane. Rip its belly
     doors off with the Line and throw the launching Wasps back into the nest.
  4. **Pirate Interdiction — Sector Interference.** The target's tag reads UNKNOWN, and the HUD
     resolves it HOSTILE. You close and fire, with guns or a rock. For one dropped frame the dying
     transponder resolves `IFF: CIVILIAN VESSEL — REGISTERED.` Then BOUNTY COLLECTED. **This is the
     terminal register**: no music, no LOG joke, colour drained for the beat. It was Elroy. The
     second kill-feed line, *They were carrying medicine*, appears and is overwritten in the same
     second (R11).
- **Wall line:** **THEY WERE CARRYING MEDICINE.**
- **The receipt it leaves.** Elroy's wreck stays at the coordinates. It is salvageable, and its cargo
  scans as "contraband", which is the refile. The Understory will not take it into a garden. "Not
  this one."

**Interlude — The Second Client** (playable). Rook's receipts as a findable object. The redaction
glyph matches 47-A's co-author field.

## Book Three — Bigger Boat

- **Physical promise:** wrecks are weapons. You get a Line-built hull.
- **Line Class:** IV (2,000 t). Wreck sections, Bristle rams, light hulls in pairs.
- **The ship.** **VARIANCE ADJUSTMENT**, Slate's second-pass hull: a Hawser-class tug with the Line
  frame (05 §3). Its seam holds to the third pressure spike and goes at the fourth. That is a real
  stress value on the hull, shown on the Line's strain readout, so the player learns to fly *under*
  the fourth spike.
- **Introduces:** Furnace maws, Bristle rams, slag ammunition, the Mirrorjaw Foreman.
- **Missions.**
  1. **The Berth.** Slate talks over the torch (canon monologue). Take delivery, then do a shakedown
     in the slag field (heat lanes, hot throws).
  2. **Feed the Maw** (E-38). A Furnace maw hunts the reactor approach. Stun it with hot slag, then
     tow it out of the lane. It is too big to kill, and just big enough to move at Class IV.
  3. **The Foreman.** The existing Mirrorjaw Foreman fight, as a yard feud the Collective cannot
     settle.
  4. **The Fourth Spike** (optional). Slate dares you to push the hull past three spikes. If you do,
     it fails exactly as he said. Come back, and he welds it again and puts it in the column.
- **The world thread.** The **first Gate Audit closes the Kepler gate.** Its Verge Gate Auditor wakes
  and is seen once, far away.
- **Vale's second sighting:** the comms popup, *Your recent work has been noted…* (canon, R8).
- **The receipt it leaves.** Slag fields grow where you fought. Slate's column has your entry.

## Book Four — Pick a Side

- **Physical promise:** you can move a hauler, so convoys are yours to steal, save or break.
- **Line Class:** V (10,000 t).
- **The clearing station** (Tethys) has three doors: **Traders Guild** (Meridian), **Patrol
  Authority** (Concord) and **Free Captains** (Frontier). One administrator stamps all of them:
  `V. DIRECTOR, ACTING`, REF 44-C. The player picks one door for the Book's main operation. The
  other two stay open as faction arcs.

| Door | Operation | Set piece | What it teaches |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Traders Guild** | escort a Meridian cargo spine through a Reach ambush | **Spine Break** (E-05): re-couple the train under fire | the flail; Coupler Head reward |
| **Patrol Authority** | run a customs weir with Concord | **The Weighing** (E-04), then **The Too-Perfect Pack** (E-10), the first time Helix is unmasked | mass-signature stealth; Concord formations |
| **Free Captains** | raise a homestead at Io before a gate closure strands it | **Barn Raising** (E-21) | towing modules into structures; Weld Head reward |

- **The doubled contract.** In plain type on the board: `INTERDICTION — PRIORITY CLIENT. TAG:
  HOSTILE (REGISTERED CIVILIAN — FILED AS HOSTILE). FEE: DOUBLE STANDARD.` The target is a survey
  hull with a crew of two. **Taking it is the player's choice.** The game never forces it, and the
  canon is the path where it is taken. If taken, six days later the wall reads **YOU READ THE TAG.**
  Wren's hand shakes for an hour (a subtle aim tremor, then none, which is worse).
- **The world thread.** The second Gate Audit closes the Io gate behind the Free Captains' homestead,
  whether or not you raised it. A raised homestead survives stranded and asks you for supply runs.
- **The receipt it leaves.** Your door's faction treats you as theirs. The other two remember you
  chose. The survey hull's wreck stays (if taken).

**Interlude — The Ticker** (playable at any Meridian board). The market moves on news you made.

## Book Five — The Reunion

- **Physical promise:** heists, measured in tonnes.
- **Line Class:** VI (40,000 t). Derelict freighters.
- **Introduces:** the Archive (Brother Index), redaction (E-29), the Index Head.
- **Missions.**
  1. **The Salvage Manifest.** `ADMINISTRATIVE RECORDS — 3 YEARS / SEALED`. Break the seal or deliver
     the crate. The game does not say which is right.
  2. **Callum's booth** (Meridian Exchange, Ceres). The canon dialogue, unchanged: "Wren." "Callum."
     Then the chip changes hands below the counter line.
  3. **The Ninth Toll.** Cade Haltred, Meridian's debt collector ace (an existing rival ace),
     collects on Callum's behalf in the Exchange approach. The ticker prices every container you
     knock loose.
  4. **The Reading Room** (Pallas). Brother Index will trade a secret for a secret. A Censor
     redacts you inside the reef (E-29). You escape by swinging, and the Index Head is the prize.
  5. **The Locker** (E-31, optional). The Concord Evidence Locker holds what was seized from you
     since B0. Steal a whole locker section at Class VI.
- **The receipt it leaves.** Callum's ledger files the day: `BUSINESS DECISION — RESOLVED.` The
  Locker's missing section is missing for good.

**Interlude — The Drawer** (playable, Tycho annex). Third drawer on the left, second row. It holds
nineteen years of chits, Hale's initials and the moisture-loss column. You can read everything and
take nothing. It weighs 0.01 t.

## Book Six — The Empire Seed

- **Physical promise:** you build by hand. Tow station modules into place and your claim becomes a
  station.
- **Line Class:** VII (150,000 t). Station chunks.
- **The commission.** Concord Auxiliary, under a signature that is only ever a signature. Stations
  greet you wrong: `WELCOME BACK, TESSERA-7`. On your own bulkhead, in your handwriting: **THEY
  KNOW WHY YOUR HANDS SHAKE.**
- **Introduces:** the Hive-hulk (T5), Blind shepherds, outpost construction (the existing claims and
  outposts systems).
- **Missions.**
  1. **The Seed.** Claim a site, then tow in the first four modules (habitat, dock, tank, mass
     driver). The station is assembled physically and persists.
  2. **First Tide on the Seed.** A Bloom tide hits your new station. Defend it with a rock wall,
     your own mass driver, and a Blind shepherd hunt (E-45) that breaks the pack's coherence.
  3. **Gate 3.** Inspector Sula Dree reads your file for eleven seconds too long, then waives the
     second fine: `REG 44-C, second fine, waived`. It is the first fine-free entry in seventeen
     years. Her review reads `JUDGMENT: DEFERRED.`
  4. **Shaft 7.** That week the kill feed prints a line nobody reads: `VOSS, E. — CLAIM RECORDER —
     SURVEY ACCIDENT — SHAFT 7.` His map of sealed shafts reaches the market as scrap paper. Buy it
     and it becomes a salvage route through sealed shafts. The Hive-hulk in the deepest shaft is the
     Book's boss (E-46).
- **The world thread.** The third Gate Audit closes the Eris gate, and the Margin goes dark.
- **The receipt it leaves.** Your station, at its exact shape, with traffic of its own. Voss's shafts
  are opened or left sealed.

**Interlude — The Smell Ticket** (playable, Helios Bay 7). The empty rack: `RECEIVED YEAR 3 / INSTALL
PENDING / ISSUED YEAR 17 — WEEK OF 47-A`. Maintenance ticket 88,214, `CLOSED — NO ACTION
REQUIRED.` This is a terminal-register scene. You can stand in the cleanest room in the sector and
do nothing.

## Book Seven — The Deep Reach

- **Physical promise:** the biggest thing you will ever move.
- **Line Class:** VIII, for the Maw.
- **Missions.**
  1. **The Iron Maw.** The dreadnought, eleven years dead, still broadcasting enforcement to no one,
     crewed by the children who inherited the shift. It guards the Ashfall lane. **Take it apart.**
     Rip its turrets off with the Line (the existing destructible turrets), throw its plates into
     its own escorts, and empty its bays. It becomes **the Gutted Maw** (04 arenas).
  2. **The Long Field.** Past the Maw the air changes, and it smells like the Pit. The sky is drawn
     with coupling filaments. Break the Blind shepherds that anchor the field so the lane opens. Your
     route home stays open because you broke them.
  3. **The Derelict Station.** The comms crackle before the clamps touch: *"I know what you're
     carrying. I knew before you got here. The mass is always the same. Only the manifest
     changes."*
  4. **The Witness.** **Terminal register, airless.** No toys, no swarm, no Line. You read the ledger.
     The roster carries your callsign, dated six weeks before B0. The coordinates weigh 0.01 t.
     *I was carrying it.*
- **The receipt it leaves.** The Maw's plates, wherever you left them. The Long Field is thinner
  where you broke it.

### The Cull ladder (the swarm career, in fiction)

The Cull is the swarm mode's story. Ten leagues, each an arena from 04 at its scale step: Pebble,
Slag, Container, Reef, Ice, Glass, Garden, Breaker, Yardmaster, and the Gutted Maw (which unlocks
after B7). Ma Sprocket announces every league. Clatter is the rival on the ladder. League prizes are
Line heads, starting Line rating and pilot cosmetics. Adventure and the Cull share heads, but they do
not share in-run rating (08 §2).

### The Orphaned Shift (machine-people)

Optional, one per sector, each a single relationship built on the real verbs (03 §4.3). They are on
the chart as names, never as quest markers. Three of them unlock a Line Class trial (TARE,
SHEPHERD-12 and HOLDFAST), so the optional route also earns progression.

## Post-game — The Count Never Ends

After the door: the world after (`docs/worldbuilding/story/THE-WORLD-AFTER.md`). The Bloom tide and
the Gate Audits keep moving. The Fulfillment's oldest open order counter keeps ticking. The Cull runs
forever. Your stations, gardens, lit paths and scars are still on the chart. The board posts the
next contract at 04:00 ship-time.
