# Build the places, then connect their lives

This is the execution companion to THE OPEN REACH. The creative seed and authoring tool are
delivered; the 18 remaining packets are implementation commissions. The existing build map and
FINISH_LANES continue to coordinate work. `program.json` expresses dependencies inside this
requested package, not a parallel global backlog or ownership service.

## 1. A place is the unit of delivery

A model, a routine or a mission can be developed separately, but the delivered unit is a complete
place the pilot can approach, understand, affect and revisit. Every district packet includes:

* A recognizable entrance and landmark; two or more useful paths with different tradeoffs.
* Ordinary activity that starts without a quest and continues without the player watching.
* Physical sources, targets and destinations. Visible work must correspond to committed work.
* People or institutions whose needs give that work meaning.
* At least one useful object, service or machine the player can acquire, manipulate or control.
* Multiple valid approaches to its main operation and a reason to return after it resolves.
* Failure that preserves honest outcomes and leaves a playable recovery or alternative.
* Behavior, art, sound, input, accessibility, memory and ordinary-route reachability in the same slice.

This package is deliberately not “first write generic systems, then eventually add content.”
Lantern Ward proves one neighborhood using the current owners. LW-02 extracts only the recurring
seams that the neighborhood actually needs. Subsequent districts reuse those seams while retaining
different topology, institutions, motion, sound and physical opportunities.

### Why not start with the giant object?

The Iron Maw is a strong destination but also concentrates the most integration risk: scale,
streaming, camera occlusion, surface collision, existing boss identity and inhabited aftermath.
Ward first proves that observable work produces real consequences. Nine-Mouth then proves
traversable enclosure. The Maw combines those working pieces into an ambitious destination.
Its models can be built in parallel from day one; the gameplay acceptance remains ordered.

For LW-A1–A4, completion means **asset-ready**: source/release delivery, measured contracts and representative runtime/native-proxy integration. The dependent numbered packet owns the complete ordinary district route. An asset packet must not wait for its own downstream district to exist; a district must not call a Blender render finished art integration. A1 also owns the shared OR-A23 human-work assembly kit, and Ward/Switchyard must visibly use it in a real service phase.

## 3. The cross-district economy is also the plot

The following is a **proposed relationship map**, not the current jump topology or a claim these
shipments are already implemented. Native cargo and economy owners must realize each connection.

| Physical relationship | What the player sees | Opportunities it creates |
|---|---|---|
| Ceres and Vesta supply metal, machinery and repair stock | Loaded tugs, a refinery receiver, a repaired mast or waiting crane | Escort, salvage, diversion, theft, shortages, repair |
| Tethys sorts household, medical and industrial freight | Distinct receivers, mixed traffic, wrong address and backed-up pods | Reroute, shunt, smuggle, intercept, correct the record |
| Dione governs one set of crossings | Queues, inspection work, responders leaving actual berths | Comply, infiltrate, bypass, disable, raid, borrow authority |
| Pallas offers illicit routes and repair access | Hidden courts, lookouts, occupied wrecks, a legal-looking funeral tow | Escape, fencing, restitution, medical smuggling, alliances |
| Charon turns wreck material into useful growth | Gardeners tow, inspect, feed, prune and reinforce | Rescue before feeding, ecological tools, local quarantine |
| Phoebe accepts damaged travelers and keeps a refuge | Repair tenders, incoming crews, bells, meal service | Recovery, mutual aid, portable resonance and a quiet homeward route |
| Iron Maw consumes coolant and labor while yielding heavy machinery and evidence | Utility barges, failing power, working cranes, residents in the keel | Heists, surface operations, evacuation, a consequential finale |
| Helios brings these distant effects home | A lamp, a shuttle route, a queue, a neighbor and an open or closed service hatch | Recognition of what the player changed, plus new practical work |

Do not implement each row as an invisible stat modifier. A shortage is readable when a known
receiver lacks its normal shipment and work pauses; a recovery is readable when a replacement
arrives and the same work resumes. Prices are a secondary native consequence, not the only sign.
The world must be allowed to recover from small interruptions. One stolen lunch crate cannot
plunge 25 sectors into collapse. Large policy and ecological changes need bounded rates, previews
and distinct geographical scope.

## 4. The player's direction and progression

The player begins with practical needs: a working ship, useful tools, a berth and the ability to
move what others cannot. The opening uses visible local work to teach the Line and introduce
people worth recognizing. A first intervention earns a capability, favor, safe route or access,
which opens a new kind of action in another district. The campaign grows out of those connections.

The main progression question is **“What can I now move, enter, manipulate, steal or protect?”**
Avoid replacing it with a stack of stat percentages. Each flagship offers a different answer:
cradle, tender, shunter, winch, salvage ram, Shepherd organ, surface crane and resonator. The
signature toys use existing load, power, custody, control, physics and progression rules. They
remain useful outside the mission that introduced them, with clear limitations and counterplay.

The campaign gives context and pressure without turning free play into constant urgency. A
departure that matters is explicitly signaled and simulated; an offline real-world clock does not
kill a neighborhood while the owner is away. A chosen quiet visit can be valuable. Discovery
should happen through sight lines, traffic, barks and local advice; the map consolidates what the
player learned. Permanent strings of technical activity labels are not an acceptable substitute.

## 5. Authoring contracts agents can execute

`districts.json` includes exact stable proposed district and opportunity IDs, canonical sector/zone
references, a spatial brief, arrival scene, landmark compatibility note, population proposal,
resources with existing owners, routine state sequences, interruptions, recoveries, approaches,
commit evidence, revisit evidence, asset status and negative acceptance cases.

The authoring command checks those structures against current source. It deliberately cannot
prove that prose is interesting, that a GLB named in a catalog is visible, that an owner path performs
the claimed behavior, or that a receipt is emitted through the actual player route. Those require
implementation and appropriate evidence. Planned model IDs are not added to runtime loaders.

Use four representations of a scene, with one owner per kind of truth:

1. **Brief:** what the player should understand and feel; the authored specification in this pack.
2. **Native state:** exact actors, goods, machines, routes, constraints and authoritative results.
3. **Presentation:** geometry, motion, light, audio and restrained text reading native state.
4. **Evidence:** a normal input route plus focused denial/interruption/return cases tied to source.

Do not build a new universal scene engine merely to host these records. First reuse existing
places, jobs, encounters, machinery and story adapters. If a narrow integration seam is missing,
add it at the native owner and prove its first real use. Only then generalize it.

## 6. Models: how local agents should divide the work

The [environment bible](04-SPACE-AND-ASSETS.md) maps **15 proposed runtime model IDs to 23 component
families**. The distinction matters: a component brief is not automatically another runtime object.
Four asset packets partition those IDs. The 46 existing everyday-space identities supply much of
the ordinary civic vocabulary; release/source manifests remain authoritative for actual availability.

Each model handoff includes source construction, scale/orientation/pivots, visual materials, working
sockets, moving-part bounds, collision proxies, load clearances, damage/service variants, LODs,
release metadata and a representative runtime integration. Its quality is judged at the game's
actual camera and light. Arbitrary polygon counts are not proof of craft, and a file placed on the
shelf is not a rendered building. Use the existing Forge publication path; no competing asset loader.

The implementation/modeler interface is a short exact contract: identifier, transform frame,
socket names, valid states, occupied volumes and one representative scene. If either side changes
it, update both sides in the same integration. Never repair a mismatch with hidden scale multipliers
and invisible collision spheres. A functional blockout can support temporary development but is
explicitly incomplete visual work, not a substitute accepted into the final district.

## 7. Performance and fairness are design constraints

The current simulation runs on a fixed step and already has bounded actor admission. The current
shared NPC defaults are 24 with a hard ceiling of 40; the new district envelopes are design proposals,
not changes to those settings. A “dozens of ships” base can show ready berths, parked hulls, supply
and repair traffic and stages of response while its admitted active forces remain bounded. Parked
collidable hulls still cost work and must be counted in the proper native census. Reserve stock is
logical identity/inventory; it must not be counted as free extra live objects.

Simulation detail follows relevance. Nearby interactive bodies use real physics and native jobs.
Far institutions use bounded records and valid aggregate work. Background scale uses coherent
macro geometry without independently pretending to be another interactive body. Promotion and
demotion preserve identity and outcomes. No offscreen shortcut may spend nonexistent goods,
resurrect a captured hull, erase damage or award an encounter twice.

Frame-time and residency numbers in the environment document are provisional investigation
budgets. Qualification must name hardware, viewport, quality, source, route and simultaneous
activity. Measure before raising the default census. Do not meet a frame target by deleting the
occupations, removing the authored picture, or making the place stop working when unobserved.

Fairness is part of the same contract. A threat exposes its boundary and windup. A passage exposes
the load size it admits. A heavy stolen tender has an exit. A restricted base can be approached and
studied before deliberate commitment. A mission cannot award success to a replacement target
whose old entity ID was reused. A world that reacts precisely can afford to be chaotic.

## 8. Acceptance by observable claim

| Claim | Required evidence | Insufficient evidence |
|---|---|---|
| A neighborhood is alive | Normal entry, a complete source/work/destination cycle, interruption and recovery | Actors orbiting; a populated registry; dialogue declaring work happened |
| An optional military raid works | Visible challenge, finite responders, genuine theft/control and escape/revisit | An arena spawned under a debug flag |
| A choice mattered | Native committed result and an actual reader changing work, place, access or behavior later | A boolean in a save with no consumer |
| An interior is playable | Actual hull/load traversal, entry/reverse/exit, collision and Continue | A pretty cave rendered from a free camera |
| A surface feels huge | Normal camera/headlight route across coherent macro and local geometry | A giant model in the asset viewer |
| A story remains open | Multiple routes, failures, skipped/dead contacts and post-ending state exercised | One scripted happy path or a forced end-state flag |
| A district is performant | Trace of representative simultaneous work/chaos on named hardware and settings | Unit-test duration or a blank-scene FPS figure |

Use focused tests for state ownership, finite custody, identity, denial and restoration. Use the
existing public-route/player-truth tools for gameplay acceptance. Use actual representative render
evidence for appearance. Use the existing quiet-host rules when measuring performance. No step
requires waiting for the owner to test the game; agents investigate and fix their work themselves.

LW-14 is a **play-and-fix** pass, not a report-writing finish line. Its deliberately mixed journey
is designed to reveal combinations that isolated feature tests miss. It should repair the defects
it finds in the narrow owning source and leave durable regressions for those specific causes.

## 9. Integration into the existing program

Build-map §35 and the program README point here. The older world-depth, THE WEIGHT and
strategic-chaos work retain their source and outcomes. New district packets map onto those existing
WORLD, LONG GAME, HAND, PICTURE, FIGHT, EAR and MACHINE responsibilities; they do not grant
permanent ownership of broad directories. Exact active file claims still govern concurrent edits.

For each finished packet, publish a scoped commit/PR under the current user direction, preserve
concurrent work, update the relevant existing row and record what was actually accepted. A status
change in `program.json` follows the work; it never performs the work. `--next` then exposes the next
dependency-ready brief. A packet with missing art can have implemented mechanics and incomplete
visual acceptance; say both explicitly, and continue the independent work.

The complete world is the goal. A diagram, a validator, a source table and a compelling story are
useful seeds. They become a game when the pilot flies into the middle of someone else's day and
discovers that the place can answer.
