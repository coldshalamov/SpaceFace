## Outcome

Land five independently reviewable improvements that make ordinary SpaceFace play more physical,
vivid, rewarding, legally legible, and densely populated: craft collisions hurt craft; post-processing
and ship paint carry readable energy; destroying a clean civilian ship immediately triggers WANTED;
hostile kills throw a valuable three-pickup magnetized burst; and light-enemy squads arrive in
readable groups of four to six without exceeding one sector-wide spawn authority.

The units form one play loop rather than five unrelated constants: dense light squads give the player
physical ammunition; impacts can finish enemies; kills visibly spray rewards; collateral against a
clean civilian has a sharp legal consequence; and the brighter but restrained presentation makes
those causes readable at the normal flight camera.

## Propagation recipe

Reuse this recipe for later inference units:

1. Find a mature but dormant production seam whose limiting constant, gate, or missing connection
   blocks a named fantasy beat.
2. Change the smallest live owner or data surface; preserve single writers, save/determinism, normal
   route reachability, accessibility, and established adjacent mechanics.
3. Pin the intended result with a public owner regression, including rematerialization, retry,
   saturation, or receipt-replay cases when the seam crosses lifecycle boundaries.
4. Send the candidate to a cold adversarial reviewer; repair every validated in-scope P1/P2 finding.
5. Perform one causal re-review, then prove the ordinary route and matched cost proportionately.
6. Commit and push the independent unit before widening the next one.

## Player route and state model

- Collision: one unordered same-tick contact has one consequence owner. Ordinary craft contact routes
  baseline damage; an exact solid Massline receipt suppresses only that baseline; player immunity is
  retained; causal attribution survives a pushed A-to-B impact.
- Law/reward: first accepted player aggression freezes lawful/hostile truth under a durable victim
  identity. Retaliation cannot launder the incident. Only an eligible player kill owns authored loot
  and shards, and replayed receipts are idempotent.
- Swarms: every ship obtains a slot before materialization, binds that slot to its entity, and
  releases it exactly once on failure/destruction/boundary. Deferred producers retry; durable mission
  target slots never collide with live survivors.
- Presentation: bloom amount changes bloom contribution, not the rest of the grade. All renderer
  routes use the same exposure/tone/grade/toe/vignette policy, and reduced-motion/flash scales both
  Massline representations.

## Performance and quality budget

- Collision adds no per-frame scan and keeps contact state bounded to active/recent pairs.
- Reward choice is stateless per receipt and adds two pickups per eligible kill; existing pooled pickup
  and magnet owners remain authoritative.
- Swarms raise the default sector cap from 12 to 24 and the hard ceiling to 40, but every producer is
  ledger-gated. No producer may materialize past its grant; deterministic retries replace overflow.
- Post-processing does not add a second presentation pass to an already-composited route, double
  scale targets, or allocate per Massline frame. Bloom zero may skip bloom work but not presentation.
- Default quality, draw distance, authored effects, and required population are not reduced to pass.

## Review questions

- Can any collision receive both baseline craft damage and an established Massline damage receipt?
- Can retaliation, rematerialization, save/load, or receipt eviction launder one clean civilian kill?
- Can any non-player/mission kill feed the player credits, items, shards, or pickups?
- Can any producer spawn beyond its grant, leak a reservation, duplicate a durable identity, or pay a
  distress outcome without its threat controller?
- Does bloom-off preserve the same grade, does render scale apply once, and do reduced-motion/flash
  settings affect every default Massline path?
