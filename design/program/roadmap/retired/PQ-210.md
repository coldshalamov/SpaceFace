## Outcome

`place_mining_drone` and `place_conveyor_barge` — the last two live world-dressing props on the
2026-07-05 bevel pass — are re-authored through the material-truth V2 Blender pipeline so they read
as designed objects at the gameplay camera, with UV-authored PBR, honest LOD1/2, and authored
colliders. The release GLBs the game loads are the new ones, and their manifests match.

## How agents get this wrong

Skipping the material-truth preflight. Publishing a technical receipt as a visual pass. Leaving the
release GLB stale while the source GLB changes. Changing a filename without the coordinated
`partsLibrary.js` hunk. Touching neighbouring props.
