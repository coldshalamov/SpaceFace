## How agents get this wrong

Read this before touching code. The integrator rejects a unit that matches any line.

- Writing a direction sheet before the owner has picked, or from prose rather than from the frames.
- Building the comps outside the game's lab, or as images, or without real data.
- Three directions that share a layout and differ by tint.
- Reaching for glow, gradients, glass, tracked caps or a cockpit to make a comp "bold".
- Citing `INSTRUMENT_GRAMMAR.md` §3/§4, a per-screen spec or a review as an aesthetic constraint.
- Consolidating or "cleaning" the old stylesheets under this packet.
- Treating a green matrix as the owner's yes.
