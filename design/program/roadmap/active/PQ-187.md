## Outcome

The frontend has one decided direction — **Cinematic Minimal, tuned for SpaceFace: the world is the
interface** — written as a sheet a builder can work from and a non-designer can picture. The kit is
built from the sheet; the title screen goes live on it first, so the direction is seen in the actual
game after one unit and can be vetoed before anything else moves; every later surface migrates onto
the kit under its own packet; a blind side-by-side against the best games' screens proves the result.

## How agents get this wrong

Read this before touching code. The integrator rejects a unit that matches any line.

- Asking the owner to choose between stylesheets, cards, palettes or comps. The owner reviews the
  game. Anything that needs a human pick before it can proceed is a defect in the packet.
- Reopening the direction: proposing a fourth direction, a "warmer variant", a second display face.
  The sheet is the direction; improve the execution, not the decision.
- Building the kit as a new stylesheet that coexists with the old ones forever. Each screen deletes
  its old CSS as it migrates; `PQ-184` deletes the dead fonts last.
- Reaching for glow, gradients, glass, blur, tracked caps, mono-as-a-look or a cockpit to make a
  frame "bold". Boldness here is scale, negative space, the world in the frame, one signal colour.
- A title screen that hides the hull behind a plate, or that adds a panel around the menu words.
- Treating a green matrix, a green check or a passing budget as the frame matching the sheet. Only
  a hash-bound visual review of the actual capture closes a leaf.
- Consolidating or "cleaning" the old stylesheets under this packet.

## Performance

Reuse `_uilab.html` and the existing capture scripts. The kit lands inside the floor it is measured
by: every token-driven surface at ≤ 2 ms UI frame cost and ≤ 1,500 DOM nodes; no animation without
a named state variable, none over 160 ms, none infinite; the variable Bricolage subset so the title's
first paint does not regress beyond the current cold boot; the sound palette adds no per-frame
allocation; the hangar-rig hull on the title reuses the existing preview mount and its budget. Never
reduce content, density or authored imagery to meet a budget.

## Stop conditions

- A unit that cannot proceed without a human choosing between options → stop, rewrite the unit so
  the sheet decides, continue.
- A candidate face without an open licence → replace it; never ship a face the repo cannot vendor.
- The lab cannot render the hull or the crests → fix the lab seam first; do not substitute
  placeholder art.
- An exact-path collision with a live `NOW.md` row → work the disjoint leaf; never adopt the subsystem.
- The owner vetoes the title in the game → record the words verbatim in the receipt, revise the
  sheet's title picture and `.03`, and do not start any surface packet until the revised title is live.
