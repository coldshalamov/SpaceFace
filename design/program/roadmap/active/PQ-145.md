## Outcome

The player can look at a region and recognise their own history in it: a depot exists because they
built it, a route is busy because their operation needs it, a scarred asteroid has been worked for
hours, a wreck still exists because of a battle they caused. Infrastructure the player creates
produces NPC traffic that persists, and that traffic creates new opportunities and new crime.
