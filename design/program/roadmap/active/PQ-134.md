## Outcome

Kills, hard collisions, and bank shots in flight read as **graphic structural events** — five-point
blades, open broken arcs, tumbling shards — instead of soft sprites and expanding rings, at the chase
camera, under saturation, with no growth on the present beat. The same pool becomes the VFX half of
Crucible's causal grammar (`CRU-051`): direct, bank, chain, collision, terrain, tether, field and reaction
each have a family the eye can separate when forty things die in a second.

shards:64 }`, `class ArcadeStructuralFx` with priority-aware slot admission, `worldSizeForPixels` pixel

## Non-goals

- No camera-facing soft cards, no full shock rings, no radial alpha fields (the rejected language).
- No new cue recipe that moves the frozen cue-count contracts; reuse `deriveVfxAdmissionMetadata`.
- No change to `cueArbitration` priority semantics other cue kinds depend on.

## Work breakdown/phased implementation

`.01`: pool instantiated in `src/render/vfx.js` beside the explosion/beam pools; domain-bus driven
(`entity:killed`, `combat:collisionConsequence`, later a bank/ricochet receipt); high-water in
`inspect()`; `clear()` on the three lifecycle events; roots in `_vfxOwnerRoots()`; table cull; a
read-at-play-size pass (pixel floor, intensity, hold) so the event is unmistakable at 144 WU.
`.02`: causal family table and audio cues; forced-colors / reduced-motion variants; four-way capture.

## Performance

Capacity is fixed (blades 128 / arcs 48 / shards 64) and never grows on the present beat; one shared
program per primitive; instanced draws; high-water ≤ capacity over a five-minute fight; zero per-frame
allocation in the burst path (no hex-string Color parsing per primitive).
