## How agents get this wrong

These are the ways a lazy first try fails this packet. Read them, then do the work as if the owner will play it tonight.

- A mission whose headline is 'fly there and hold fire': the headline verb is physical or the mission is not admitted.
- One solution per set piece: two solutions, both reachable on a trackpad.
- A boss with immunity phases: physics is the fast way, guns the slow way, immunity never.
