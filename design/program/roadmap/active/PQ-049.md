## Outcome

The default Helios pocket's existing passenger-only express service becomes visibly its own civic
pressure-drum liner rather than a Mule fallback, while preserving the already-accepted courier Lark
and the current traffic itinerary. The player can naturally find and label it along its dock/service
route, inspect its passenger-only custody truth, boost past, tether to, and later resume the same
saved express itinerary in Browser and Electron.
