## Outcome

Deliver twenty newly completed, independently reviewable production packages that make the default
SpaceFace route feel like a physics-first life inside a working industrial frontier. The portfolio
concentrates on Ceres/Tethys/Pallas/Vesta routes already present in the build: visible work must move
real value, interruption must create a player choice and aftermath, discovery must change future
play, and characters or guidance must react to authoritative events rather than merely add text.

This packet does not count pre-existing commits, files, candidates, catalog rows, tests, cues, or
receipts as units. A leaf closes only when its whole player contract is implemented through current
owners and independently reviewed at the exact candidate.

## Sequence and review

Leaves run in numeric order because adjacent units share owner files and composition. For each leaf:

1. characterize the missing end-to-end behavior with a seconds-scale owner test;
2. implement the smallest complete package, including persistence and failure branches it owns;
3. run the focused L0-L2 checks named in the queue plus `git diff --check`;
4. obtain an independent spec review, repair any bounded causal defect, then obtain independent code/
   quality review;
5. prove the ordinary player route proportionately; WebGL visual claims require a current screenshot;
6. publish one focused commit and leaf receipt, then promote only that leaf.

At leaves 05, 10, 15, and 20, run a tranche composition review for attention hierarchy, repetition,
player opportunity, accessibility, save/Continue, and bounded density before multiplying further.

## Work breakdown/phased implementation

### Phase 1 — working logistics (`PQ-048.01`–`.05`)

Complete one traceable ore flow, then add opportunity, handoff, service, and recovery packages without
duplicating cargo, economy, damage, or traffic ownership.

### Phase 2 — interruption and civic traffic (`PQ-048.06`–`.10`)

Complete law, salvage, market-demand, courier, and liner packages; review their concurrent cadence so
routine motion remains subordinate to one readable causal event.

### Phase 3 — discovery and activities (`PQ-048.11`–`.15`)

Turn existing places and physical seams into five distinct curiosity/activity chains. Each must
change later play or world memory, not merely add a marker, plate, or board row.

### Phase 4 — people and retrieval (`PQ-048.16`–`.20`)

Bind named contacts and first-use guidance to authoritative world events, physical follow-up, sourced
knowledge, persistence, accessible controls, and expert bypass.

## Performance and quality budget

Each foreground leaf may adopt existing actors and one primary/one secondary causal event inside the
production camera bubble. No leaf may add an unbounded per-frame search, permanent query, timer per
entity, new draw/material family, or quality reduction. Tranche reviews compare actor/collider/query,
draw, save-size, and frame-tail growth on the same route when the changed seam can affect it.

## Stop conditions

- The exact source hunk is actively owned by another live writer; preserve that hunk and continue a
  disjoint leaf or arrange an explicit handoff.
- The leaf requires a second cargo/economy/law/damage/story owner, nondeterministic wall time, or an
  unbounded per-frame/world simulation; redesign through the current owner instead.
- Current route evidence proves the proposed outcome already exists; replace the duplicate leaf with
  another complete package before counting it.
- The outcome exists only in a catalog, test, debug route, receipt, marker, cue, or prose; it remains
  incomplete.
- A repeated unchanged validation failure fingerprint appears; retain it, reduce to the owning seam,
  repair or switch to another safe leaf rather than retrying.
