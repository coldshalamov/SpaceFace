## Outcome

A player enters Ceres through ordinary play with no mission accepted, and within ten minutes sees ore
being worked, cargo moving between identifiable places, a service response, a law response and an
aftermath — then interferes with one of them, uses the game's physics to change the result, and leaves
with a story that arose from play. The proving route is the existing five-minute Ceres acceptance
harness at fixed seed 47 over 18,000 ticks through all four authored pockets, closed by a human
verdict that the longest zero-visible-activity interval reads as a brief intentional void rather than
an empty sector.

This packet does not build that region from nothing. Ceres already has four authored pockets 2,074 to
4,540 WU apart, nine authored identities of which eight materialize and physically fly, five object
slots, two collision anchors, a nine-actor cast contract, and a complete acceptance harness. Commit
`2cda03cb` now connects three traffic-owned Ceres jobs across five exact route-target relationships
to those live targets. The remaining false physical promise is the Pitborn tender servicing
`activity:disabled-hull` even though no disabled client exists. The other seven `activity:` refs are
intentional abstract scan, throughline, and perimeter choreography, not missing objects.
