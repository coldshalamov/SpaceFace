## Outcome

A player enters Ceres through ordinary play with no mission accepted, and within ten minutes sees ore
being worked, cargo moving between identifiable places, a service response, a law response and an
aftermath — then interferes with one of them, uses the game's physics to change the result, and leaves
with a story that arose from play. The proving route is the existing five-minute Ceres acceptance
harness at fixed seed 47 over 18,000 ticks through all four authored pockets, closed by a human
verdict that the longest zero-visible-activity interval reads as a brief intentional void rather than
an empty sector.

This packet does not build that region from nothing. Ceres already has four authored pockets 2,074 to
4,540 WU apart, nine authored identities of which eight materialize and physically fly, five object
slots, two collision anchors, a nine-actor cast contract, and a complete acceptance harness. What it
lacks is that **the actors are not connected to the things they act on**. This packet closes that gap
first, then spends art on the result.

## Player route and state machine

Entry through the `ceres_reference_pocket` sandbox scenario or ordinary travel, then Refinery Pocket →
Working Seam → Ambush Run → Cathedral Grave. Beat rhythm, current coverage and the two beats that
need genuinely new content are in
[`WAVE0_CERES_BASELINE.md`](../../../reference-sector/WAVE0_CERES_BASELINE.md) §5.

Stable ids are the authority across rematerialization: `worldRecordSlotId` and `jobId`, never live
numeric entity ids. Ledgers introduced by the causal chain stay transient and out of the save envelope,
matching the existing traffic causality ledgers.

## Performance and quality budget

Declare before implementing: added entities and colliders (new object slots and the tender's disabled
hull), added queries per tick (the `targetRef` resolution — it must be cached per job, not re-resolved
per frame), added draw calls and materials from promoted props, texture residency from the prop and
wreck families, and save bytes (target: zero — causality ledgers stay transient).

Structural mitigation only: LOD/HLOD, instancing, batching, culling, cadence, pooling, bounded queries.

> "Do not gain performance by reducing content, population, effects, draw distance, render quality, or
> default visual quality."

The promoted props and wreck assets are the real risk. The wreck pack alone carries 1,891 unmerged
meshes with no LODs; nothing from it ships until that is authored down.

## Review questions

1. Does an actor visibly work *on* the thing its mark names, at the ordinary camera, without a label?
2. Can a player tell the four pockets apart from motion alone, with the HUD hidden?
3. Does the causal chain still reach a believable outcome when the player does nothing?
4. Does player interference produce a consequence owned by an existing system, not by event state?
5. Is at least one physics-based environmental outcome viable at the collision anchors?
6. Do reduced-motion and reduced-flash profiles keep every cue legible?
7. Does the longest zero-visible interval read as intentional quiet or as an empty sector?
