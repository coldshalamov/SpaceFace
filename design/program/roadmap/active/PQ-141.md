## Outcome

The sequence the owner wrote as the A-list version of SpaceFace happens **naturally and
repeatedly** at the reference site: a working op, a hauler leaving, pirates intercepting, a shove
that spins one, a rope-swing-release that makes a projectile, collateral, cargo spilling, the hauler
fleeing, a patrol arriving, the player grabbing a pod and running WANTED. Nobody scripts it; the
world and the verbs produce it. Bar **B12** in `design/FEEL_CONTRACT.md`. This is the acceptance
gate for the whole §13C program: the other packets exist so that this one can pass.
