## Outcome

The two speeds coexist in the default flight mode: **nimble in a fight** (turn NOW, stop when I
brake, drift when I choose) and **fast across the galaxy** (speed I earn by swinging, slingshotting
and being flung is mine until I brake). Shoving a light ship visibly throws it; it loses its helm in
proportion to how hard it was hit, and if it meets a rock at speed it dies. The rope is a rope, not a
bungee. A sketched stroke is flown the way a pilot would fly it — fast, cutting corners, slowing only
where a pilot must. Every claim is a number in [`design/FEEL_CONTRACT.md`](../../../FEEL_CONTRACT.md)
§B, measured on the default route.
