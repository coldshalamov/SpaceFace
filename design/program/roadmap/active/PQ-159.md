## Outcome

The camera is part of the art direction. Exceptional speed opens it toward 3× (B3); impacts kick in proportion to delta-V, not damage; a taut line between two bodies frames both; a rated moment gets a 150 ms beat and a bass drop; death gets a cam; photo mode with free camera, filters off by default and the composition ramp tuned so every screenshot sells the game.

## Source

`CANONICAL_BUILD_MAP.md` §15 (the finishing program, admitted 2026-09-03) → **D · Presentation**. Gap it closes: Speed zoom tops out at 1.55×; kicks are damage-driven; no auto-framing for two-body moments; no photo mode. Reference: Rocket League replay cam, Just Cause, Hades death cam, Everspace 2 photo mode. Routes through existing sources: SPEC3-18 camera & juice; S2-02; PQ-139.00; PQ-146.03 moment detector.
