## Outcome

Enemies differ by the physical problem they pose, not by their arithmetic. A light interceptor is a
positioning problem. A heavy is moving terrain. Specialists threaten the player's plan: one cuts
tethers, one disrupts fields, one anchors its allies, one protects cargo. Light fodder resolves
fast and reads as ammunition. Verified in the Crucible, where the roster is already the whole game.

## Source

`design/VISION.md` Part II, "Combat should be physical before it is numerical." PQ-135.03–.05
delivered hull-relative envelopes, one choreographed wing and one flowing cohort; this packet gives
each roster slot a *physical* identity on top of that motion.

## Leaves

| Leaf | Outcome | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| `.00` | **Interceptor = positioning problem.** Crossing lanes, extend-and-return, never a stationary target; its threat is where it will be, not its DPS. | Scenario metric: time the player spends off its attack line; a capture where the lane geometry is readable. |
| `.01` | **Heavy = moving terrain.** Mass ≥ 150; gun-scale impulses do nothing (B11); the player can swing around it, hide behind it, and throw lights into it; its wake and turn radius matter. | A rope-swing around a heavy works in the Crucible; a light thrown into it dies (B6). |
| `.02` | **Specialists, one each.** Tether-cutter, field-disruptor, anchor, cargo-protector — each breaks one specific player plan and is readable by silhouette and behaviour before any label. | Four scenarios, each with the plan it breaks; silhouettes reviewed at the shipping camera. |
| `.03` | **Fodder is ammunition.** With the hitstun law and force table in, the twelve-body cohort resolves quickly under physics play (shove into rock, chain, well) and the free gun no longer out-performs the physics kit. | Crucible balance receipt re-run; median time-to-resolve a light under physics ≤ under guns. |
