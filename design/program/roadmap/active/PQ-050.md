## Outcome

Every flyable player and NPC ship **except Hitch/Kestrel** looks like a manufactured 2026
A-list craft, honestly better than the live Hitch body. The player can tell each ship’s job
from silhouette and construction in ordinary flight, hangar, and traffic views. Hitch stays
the untouched quality floor. Current factory bodies are `integration_candidate` only; none
may self-promote to accepted.

## Honest current state (2026-08-13)

- Hitch is owned by another lane. Do not edit it.
- Every non-Hitch flyable already has its own live wholeship mapping. That wiring is not
  the remaining work.
- The interceptor (Hornet) was the probe ship through nine factory passes. Pass 8–9 cut a
  real top bay and replaced the teal plastic canopy with dark glass. Side bays still read as
  plates. Hulls still read as pale tubes with boxes.
- The same latest factory method was rebuilt onto the other factory ships as source GLBs.
  Those newest files are **not all shipped into the live parts/release slots yet**.
- Pelican and Mule have older remasters live. Open-bay code is written; they are not rebuilt.
- Wasp already has a better remaster than the factory ships. A later full rebuild dropped
  hull triangles below the 800-tri floor and was reverted. Upgrade it carefully or leave it.

Quality bar for every leaf: matched stills vs live Hitch, not a percentage claim. If Hitch
still wins on construction, materials, or cavities, the ship is not done.

## Work breakdown

### Per-ship loop (required inside `.04` and `.05`)

For each ship, in order:

1. Read Hitch stills + that ship’s existing refs. If a needed component has no ref, generate
   up to 10 component-only images and keep one that matches the frozen identity.
2. Brainstorm three times: list every construction/material fix that would beat Hitch, then
   add a second and third pass of new ideas before touching the mesh.
3. Implement only those ideas. Frozen: silhouette envelope, sockets, collision, role, stats.
4. Render three-quarter, starboard, rear, grazing. Compare to Hitch at the same views.
5. Repeat that loop at least five times, or until Hitch no longer wins. Count stills, not
   script runs.

Kill these P0/P1 reads before calling a ship done:

- Clay / plastic / leather grain on metal
- One lofted sausage with boxes glued on
- Card wings
- Recess that is a plate, not a hole
- Solid transmissive canopy brick
- Glowing disk / torus sensor
- One recolored material pretending to be many substances

## Performance and quality budget

LOD0 keeps cavities, frames, vanes, and material boundaries. LOD1 keeps load path and
openings. LOD2 keeps silhouette. Do not pass a cost gate by deleting authored construction
or lowering default quality. Expect more triangles than the old modular hulls; stay inside
the ordinary fleet draw budget already used by live Hitch/Wasp wholeships.
