## Outcome

An upgrade makes the player imagine a possibility, not compare a percentage: a heavier tow class, a
faster slam survived, a stronger line, a field that can be deployed, cargo that can be carried
without going immobile. The ship accumulates history — scars, repairs, odd fittings, a reputation
by hull — until it is *my fucking ship*.

## How agents get this wrong

Read this before touching code. The integrator rejects a unit that matches any line (§1.6, §19).

- Shipping a capability as a percentage: every unlock is a verb with a sentence and a before/after scenario.
- Adding a cosmetic system instead of history: scars come from real impacts and repairs, not a paint shop.
