## Outcome

The universe looks larger than the simulation budget and stays honest where interaction becomes
possible: foreground activity is real and interruptible, midground is cheaper but coherent,
background implies a bigger world through moving lights, distant ships, machinery and traffic
crossings. **The closer the player gets, the more truthful the world becomes.** Nothing in §13C
regresses frame pacing, and no gate is passed by cutting entity count or quality.

## Performance and quality budget

No new per-tick allocation in the sim; entity, collider, query, draw-call and residency growth bounded to the bodies the leaves name; the runtime witness is run before and after (`PQ-144` guard) and no new top frame-time bucket may appear. Never reduce default content, population, effects, draw distance or visual quality as the mitigation.

## Verification budget

`npm run check:baseline` before and after every edit; the packet checks named above; per leaf the deterministic scenario that prints its bar number and one normal-speed capture at the shipping camera when the claim is player-felt; `npm run check:all:smoke` for sim/save/render seams. ## Stop conditions

- An exact-path collision with a live `NOW.md` row → work the disjoint leaf; never adopt the subsystem.
- Nondeterminism in the sim path, a golden moving without a causal record, or a passing route that reduces default quality → stop.
- Two failed repair cycles with the same causal model → record the falsified assumption and narrow the scenario.
