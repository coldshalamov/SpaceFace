## Outcome

Every round creates an interesting physical situation: an approaching stream, a flanked escape,
an anchor sheltering a support ship, a current crossing a bank lane. Each arena offers usable
machinery and throwable props. Swarm's armory sells known weapons, rigs and survival upgrades after
every clear, so saving supports a chosen strategy. Gauntlet retains its authored draft rhythm.
Weekly mutators change the problem and invite a different build. The five arenas, thirty-wave
authored arc, endless rounds and build breadth remain the scope.

## How agents get this wrong

Read this before touching code. The integrator rejects a unit that matches any line (§1.6, §19).

- Writing waves as spawn lists: a wave without a stated physical question is rejected by the validator, not by taste.
- Filling drafts with +10 % cards because they are easy to author.
- Dressing arenas with props that cannot be touched; a prop that does not move mass is scenery, not content.
