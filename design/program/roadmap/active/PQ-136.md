## Outcome

The 80 authored models the player never sees reach the game. Owner, 2026-08-24: *"if there was ever
restarted or abandoned graphics work, there should not be abandoned and unused files like that... we
already have a dearth of variety in this game for most things so we need to have everything utilized
if we can."* Ordinary sectors gain authored wreck variety, infrastructure props, and occupational
craft — from work already paid for, not new commissions.

## The measurement

`check:asset-reachability` reports 276 referenced runtime assets routed correctly — runtime wiring is
healthy; the problem is authored work never routed at all. Counted 2026-08-24:

| Pack | Models | Referenced by `src/` | Unused |
|---|---|---|---|
| `assets/incubator/wreck_aftermath_pack` | 44 | 0 | **44 — the entire pack** |
| `assets/incubator/everyday_space_kit` | 62 | 32 | 30 |
| `assets/incubator/npc_activity_pack` | 15 | 9 | 6 |

**Do not measure with a filename grep.** A first pass reported "852 of 1,412 models unused" and was
wrong — models resolve through manifests and LOD families. `check:asset-reachability` is the
instrument.

## Live seams

| Domain | Current owner | Reuse | New |

## Leaves

- `.00` — **the wreck pack reaches the player.** 44 models, zero used. Wrecks are the cheapest
  variety in the game — no AI, no balance pass, no new systems; routing and placement only. Done when
  a player flying an ordinary sector encounters authored wreck hulls and fragments that are not the
  same three shapes.
- `.01` — the 30 unused `everyday_space_kit` props placed where infrastructure belongs — stations,
  lanes, work sites. Done when sectors read as inhabited rather than decorated with one repeated prop.
- `.02` — the 6 unused `npc_activity_pack` craft become fielded NPCs with a job, reusing traffic and
  jobs systems. Done when new occupational craft appear in traffic without a new AI path.
- `.03` — half-finished or superseded hulls triaged, not abandoned: field, variant (faction repaint,
  damage pass, wreck conversion), or a recorded reason it cannot be used. Deleting is the last
  option. Done when every authored hull has a disposition on the record.

## Non-goals

- No new model is commissioned for a slot an existing unused asset can fill — that is the point.
- No pipeline exceptions: the asset contract and reachability checks stay green; this adds routing.
- No deletion of restartable work: mark `FUTURE` or `PARTIAL` per `PLAN_REGISTRY.md` rule 6.
- No perf regression: placement respects the table/runway residency rules (§8.2); declare entity,
  draw, and residency growth per §8 before implementation.

## Work breakdown/phased implementation

`.00`: read the wreck pack's manifest and audit; route the 44 models through the existing
manifest/LOD resolution (promotion through the canonical release lane if the live loader requires
it); place hero wrecks and fragment fields in ordinary sectors through the existing scenario/prop
owners; verify reachability count and chase-camera stills. `.01`: place the 30 unused infrastructure
props at stations, lanes, and work sites through the same placement grammar. `.02`: add the 6
occupational craft to traffic roles/jobs data so they spawn with existing AI; no new path. `.03`:
walk the authored-hull inventory against `VISUAL_ASSET_CATALOG.md`; record field / variant / cannot-
use dispositions; open follow-up rows for variants worth making.

## Performance

Placement respects table/runway residency (§8.2): wrecks and props are sector-local placements that
admit through the normal residency path, never a permanently resident +80-model set. Declare entity,
draw, texture, and residency growth per placed sector before implementation; prove with one matched
before/after on the densest placed sector (draw calls, presentation p95). Occupational craft ride
the existing traffic cadence and spawn budgets — no new spawns beyond current caps.

## Verification budget

`check:asset-reachability` (before/after counts are the headline evidence), `check:playable`,
per-leaf placement stills at the chase camera, one matched perf comparison for the densest placed
sector.

4. Is the triage record honest about what cannot be used, with reasons?
