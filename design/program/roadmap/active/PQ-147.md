The field kit composes with traps and webbing: pull a pack together, bind it, prime it or blast it into terrain. Power Rail shows actual bindings, resources and cooldowns. ## How agents get this wrong

These are the ways a lazy first try fails this packet. Read them, then do the work as if the owner will play it tonight.

- Shipping a field as a damage volume or an aura: every field bends motion visibly and has a counter.
- Rendering a field as a glowing sphere: cone, ring or sheet by the VFX standard.
- Letting NPC fields trap the player without a verb: the counterplay leaf is not optional.
