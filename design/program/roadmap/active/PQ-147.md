## How agents get this wrong

Read this before touching code. The integrator rejects a unit that matches any line (§1.6, §19).

- Shipping a field as a damage volume or an aura: every field bends motion visibly and has a counter.
- Rendering a field as a glowing sphere: cone, ring or sheet by the VFX standard.
- Letting NPC fields trap the player without a verb: the counterplay leaf is not optional.
