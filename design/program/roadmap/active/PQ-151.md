## Outcome

Being wanted is a game with verbs, not a number. Tier one is a fine and a scan cone to slip; tier two posts a bounty and hunters with your name; tier three brings tether-net checkpoints and patrol wedges you must break physically; tier four impounds and the recovery is a heist. Corrupt ports launder cargo and heat for a price; insurance and restitution make 'oh fuck, I did that' recoverable.

## Source

`CANONICAL_BUILD_MAP.md` §15 (admitted 2026-09-03) → **B · The world and its people**, wave **BETA**. Gap it closes: Heat is a decaying scalar; incidents chase always; custody/surrender/recovery systems (54 KB) are rich but unadvertised; no tiers, no nets, no laundering. Reference: Elite Dangerous crime, GTA wanted stars, Watch Dogs escapes. Routes through existing sources: GDX-A07 collateral and law; PQ-138.00 witness choice; SPEC3-12.

## How agents get this wrong

Read this before touching code. The integrator rejects a unit that matches any line (§1.6, §19).

- Police that teleport or spawn on the player: responders fly from somewhere.
- A tier without a physical escape: every tier's escape is a verb, never a purchase.
- Heat as a hidden number: the tier is visible on the chart and in the world.
