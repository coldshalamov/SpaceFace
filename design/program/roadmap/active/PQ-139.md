# PQ-139 — Impacts answer: weight you can hear and feel

## Outcome

Every physical impact answers instantly and tells the player how heavy it was. A scout kissing a
rock and a freighter broadsiding a station are different events on screen and in the speakers. A
Massline release snaps. A shoved ship spins with a corkscrewing trail. A gravity well bends space.
A death is sized to the mass that died. Bar **B9** in `design/FEEL_CONTRACT.md`.

## Source

Owner, 2026-09-03 (`design/VISION.md` Part II, "The game should be fast" and "Colorful without
becoming cartoonish"). Audit 2026-09-03 (spectacle inventory): the feel layer subscribes to damage,
kills, fire, jumps and tethers but **never to collisions**; collision audio is one sample with a
clamped gain and ignores the exchanged momentum the physics step hands it; the Massline release is
hard-coded `hsDur: 0`; wrecks spawn flat; wells do not use the distortion pass that already exists.

## How agents get this wrong

Read this before touching code. The integrator rejects a unit that matches any line (§1.6, §19).

- Using camera shake as the impact answer: the owner's exact complaint; hitstop and trauma scale with momentum, and reduce-motion turns them off.
- Adding a sphere/disc/glow-card stand-in for a directional effect: cones, sheets, rings, ribbons or nothing.
- Breaking headless determinism with a time-scale dip: the release snap sits behind the existing headless-safe guard.
- Making death spectacular for a boring event: PQ-137 makes the event; this packet only makes it legible.
