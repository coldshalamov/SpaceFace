## Outcome

The Wreck Cathedral becomes a monumental off-lane Ceres destination that is visible at distance, physically traversable, composed of truthful World Site components, supports five independently earned evidence actions, survives save/Continue and damage/recovery, and is worth flying to. The preserved source asset is promoted rather than rebuilt from zero.

The ordinary route is: New Game/Continue → travel to Ceres → select/navigate to the Cathedral → approach and fly the cavity → target/inspect/operate named components → earn individual receipts and physical salvage → leave/save/Continue/return → observe unchanged site identity, state, and evidence.

## Preserved source facts

The committed candidate already supplies a project-original Blender source and GLB, three decreasing LODs, semantic materials/textures, a large traversable cavity, sockets/markers, validation reports, and normal/close/far evidence. It is still source-stage: release compression, runtime registration, collision/component proxies, Ceres placement, route evidence, and measured residency/performance are not established by the source handoff.

## Component and evidence model

Fit the integrated World Site bounds rather than expanding the kernel casually. The current concept
uses these seven components; merge or revise them when live owner limits or player readability demand
it rather than treating the count as a universal ceiling:

1. `cathedral_hull` — structural identity/damage/recovery and fly-through envelope;
2. `bridge_navigation_record` — route record and **The Missing Convoy** receipt;
3. `registry_scan_array` — authoritative hull identity and **Capital Hull Located** receipt;
4. `emergency_relay_clock` — stabilization/telemetry and **The Clock Stopped First** receipt;
5. `cargo_clamp_forensics` — inspect/cut evidence and **Released From Inside** receipt;
6. `black_box_or_device` — physical recover/deliver path and **What Was Carried** receipt;
7. `marker_service_spine` — Marker/propulsion/service visual and optional recovery/utility state without inventing a sixth story page.

Exact names may change to fit current data conventions. The five stable page IDs must not:

- unlock from proximity, broad `visited`, catalog presence, asset load, or one global completion flag;
- copy long prose or image URLs into save state;
- depend on callback order or live entity IDs;
- disappear when unrelated components are destroyed;
- replay transient flight fragments on open/Continue.

Preferred owner record:

```js
siteRecord.evidenceReceiptsByPageId[pageId] = {
  receiptId: pageId,
  pageId,
  revision: 1,
  earnedTick,
  earnedAtS,
  siteRecordId,
  componentId,
  operationId,
  stateFrom,
  stateTo,
  provenanceRef,
  catalogRevision: 1
};
```

The World Site owner writes causal IDs/state only. The immutable catalog/media owner supplies title, body, fragment, provenance copy, map refs, alt/caption, and accepted asset ID.
