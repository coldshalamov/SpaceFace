## How agents get this wrong

Read this before touching code. The integrator rejects a unit that matches any line (§1.6, §19).

- Silently replacing keyboard steering with mouse steering (or any binding) to make an old paragraph true: this packet changes labels and prose to match the bindings, never the bindings.
- Fixing one screen's label by hand: a label typed as prose is the defect; the fix is one resolver every surface calls.
- Appending a new comprehensive plan instead of fixing the authoritative door in place: retire a contradicted instruction with a short replacement pointer where it lives.
- Treating a historical diagnosis as an order: a §A row marked FIXED is history; verify the present feel before re-applying its remedy.
- Restyling labels while resolving them: the look belongs to the frontend direction packets.
