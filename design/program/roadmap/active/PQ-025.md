## Outcome

Prove—not script—that three careers can traverse the integrated Helios–Ceres–Tethys game for native 30- and 90-minute sessions through ordinary controls, meaningful economy/progression, one legal purchase/fit/capability change, combat/failure/recovery, save/Continue, World Site/Cathedral/Ledger/Asteroid Ops outcomes, and successful authoritative Massline use in both Browser and Electron. Held-out seeds, immutable attempt retention, source-stable profiles, accessibility, performance, and independent human judgment prevent the harness from grading its own inventions.

PQ-025 is an observational acceptance compositor. It may normalize evidence from current owners. It may not become a gameplay bot with hidden state, an alternate simulator, a second save model, a new event system, a feature-fix branch, or permission to inject missing milestones.

## Entry conditions

- [ ] PQ-019, PQ-020, PQ-021, PQ-022 required leaves, PQ-023 required leaves, and PQ-024 have integrated receipts at the exact candidate revision.
- [ ] Each dependency names stable owner events/projections/receipts for required semantic outcomes.
- [ ] Each career has a reachable legal 90-minute purchase+fit/activation+capability change; research/preview/affordability text is not enough.
- [ ] Authoritative successful Massline attach/release evidence is observable read-only.
- [ ] Browser/Electron public actors can enter seed, use ordinary controls/accessible names, save, cold Continue, and clean up without hidden state deciding actions.
- [ ] Target and floor hardware/execution profiles, runtime versions, viewport/settings, accessibility profiles, capture tools, players/reviewers, evidence retention, and held-out salt/seed process are frozen before qualification.
- [ ] Candidate source fingerprint is clean and stable; required dependency receipt hashes are exact.
- [ ] Git-index, Browser-GPU, save/profile/port, and acceptance-artifact leases are exclusive.

Pure contract/harness scaffolding may be implemented before feature dependencies land. No qualification cell may run or pass until all entry conditions are true.

## Security domains

### Actor

Uses visible UI, accessibility roles/names, keyboard/pointer/controller, pixels, and public seed entry. It cannot read hidden state, internal IDs, owner events, diagnostics, future outcomes, or held-out answers when choosing actions. A deterministic actor policy is allowed only when it is a plausible public-surface player and cannot consult the observer.

### Observer

Read-only collection of approved owner events/projections, save receipts, errors, focus/visibility/timeScale, process/GPU health, performance, and media. It cannot emit gameplay events, call mutating owner methods, change timeScale, expose hidden telemetry to the actor, or manufacture a semantic success.

### Judge

Validates schemas, immutability, matrix completeness, native duration, deterministic hashes, owner evidence, parity, save, accessibility, performance, media, and human forms. It cannot delete an inconvenient attempt, relabel a scenario after observation, choose profile class from measured FPS, average away a failed hard cell, or convert unknown to pass.

Keep these domains as separate modules/process interfaces with tests that reject information-flow violations.

### Phase 4 — native 30-minute matrix

At minimum: three careers × success/failure-recovery × Browser/Electron, with fixed parity pairs.

Each cell requires:

- focused, visible, playable native wall duration >=30 minutes with `timeScale === 1` and sim reconciliation;
- at least one meaningful career loop and next-action continuity;
- a cold save/Continue checkpoint and post-Continue meaningful action;
- scenario-specific success or failure/recovery evidence frozen before run;
- accessibility, source/process health, media, performance, cleanup;
- no hidden actor oracle or direct transition injection.

### Phase 5 — native 90-minute matrix

Predeclare career/scenario/runtime cells. Every success cell requires:

- meaningful career identity and viable economy;
- legal purchase charged once, owned once, fitted/activated legally, measurable capability delta, and persistence through cold Continue;
- authoritative successful Massline attach/use, not opportunity or attempt only;
- combat/adverse-state recovery where assigned;
- integrated corridor/world outcomes applicable to the route;
- final cold Continue and meaningful action;
- target/floor profile-specific performance and long-session resource stability;
- human player/reviewer critical rubric pass.

Failure/recovery cells must fail/recover according to their predeclared scenario, not be relabeled after observation.

### Phase 6 — immutable aggregate and decision

- [ ] Validate every required cell, parity pair, unique capture, owner reference, attempt sequence, human form, and dependency hash.
- [ ] One hard failure/unknown/missing cell fails qualification; averages provide diagnosis only.
- [ ] Publish immutable aggregate receipt with exact candidate and artifacts.
- [ ] Controller decides milestone promotion. Harness never promotes itself.

Thirty-minute minimum:


Ninety-minute minimum:

Include predeclared cross-runtime save parity using isolated copied saves where current save-origin contracts permit it.

## Failure taxonomy and rerun policy

| Class | Treatment |
|---|---|
| `QUALIFICATION` | wrong/dirty revision, dependency/seed/scenario/profile/native-time/timeScale/GPU/source/capture mismatch; retain invalid attempt |
| `ENVIRONMENT` | independently evidenced external fault; one predeclared replacement; retain both |
| `HARNESS` | actor/observer/judge/stream/cleanup defect; block affected cells; repair harness; new harness hash; rerun affected cells only |
| `PRODUCT_ONBOARDING/NAV/ECONOMY/PROGRESSION/COMBAT/MASSLINE/SAVE/A11Y/PERF/PARITY` | product failure; route to exact owner with focused regression; new game candidate required |
| `HUMAN_JUDGMENT` | critical discoverability/fairness/readability/coherence failure; acceptance fail |
| `UNKNOWN` | evidence cannot attribute or prove required fact; hard fail |

No best-of-N. No deleting red attempts. No unchanged candidate rerun. A product fix changes the candidate commit and invalidates qualification cells. A harness fix changes harness hash and invalidates only cells whose evidence path was affected, as proven by contract tests.

## Performance profile contract

Profile class is assigned before the run and never inferred from measured cadence.

- **Target:** p95 <=16.7 ms and target-specific hitch policy; cannot pass at floor threshold.
- **Floor:** p95 <=33.3 ms with floor-aware delivery/hitch classification; raw >32 ms remains reported but is not blindly zero-gated.
- **Diagnostic:** never acceptance-eligible.

Every attempt records p50/p95/p99/max, raw threshold counts, missed-vsync, multi-step/backlog, phase costs, entity/draw/triangle/particle/light counts, residency baseline/peak/end, save blocking, process/GPU health, and capture overhead as available. Default quality/settings remain fixed.

Long-session qualification rejects monotonic growth in entities, listeners, timers, observers, resources, save payload, queues, or retained evidence buffers beyond declared bounded high-water behavior.

## Accessibility and human judgment

Automation checks roles/names, focus, contrast, reduced motion/flash, non-color/non-audio cues, text scale, input reachability, and semantic checkpoints. Human player and independent reviewer decide discoverability, role identity, fairness, cue clarity, recovery comprehensibility, perceived continuity, and whether the route feels coherent rather than merely executable.

Critical rubric questions and failure thresholds are frozen before held-out runs. Human judgment cannot waive hard technical failures; hard automation cannot certify fun.

## Adversarial contract minimum

Reject at least:

- runtime included in seed derivation or parity seeds differ;
- scenario/profile relabeled after observation;
- required failure/recovery cell omitted;
- failed/invalid attempt deleted or replaced;
- actor reads observer/hidden state;
- direct transition/state/event injection;
- native duration short, pause/unfocus/loading counted, timeScale not exactly one, sim reconciliation failure, focused idling;
- purchase without charge/ownership/legal fit/capability/Continue;
- research/preview counted as purchase;
- Massline attempt without authoritative attach success;
- target evaluated at floor threshold or floor blindly zero-gated >32 ms;
- missing p99/max/missed-vsync/multi-step/residency/save blocking;
- capture reused across cells;
- quality reduced;
- bounded event buffer erases evidence;
- stale media/receipt/save reused;
- unknown owner evidence treated as warning/pass.

## Review questions

- Can the actor succeed using only what a real player can see and control?
- Is every semantic success grounded in a current owner receipt/projection and raw reference?
- Can the harness discard, relabel, time-compress, reuse, average, or otherwise game evidence?
- Are career identity, purchase/capability, failure/recovery, physics/Massline, and corridor outcomes genuinely meaningful?
- Are Browser/Electron parity and save continuity semantic, not superficial?
- Are target/floor measurements assigned and comparable, with stable source/hardware/execution identity?
- Do players/reviewers judge a coherent and fun route, not merely a test script?

## Stop conditions

- Any dependency receipt missing/mismatched.
- A career lacks reachable legal progression.
- Authoritative Massline success or required owner evidence is unobservable.
- Actor/observer separation cannot be enforced.
- Native time/timeScale/sim reconciliation or hardware/profile identity cannot be proven.
- Candidate source changes during qualification.
- Product defect found: return to owner; do not patch here.
- Harness nondeterminism or evidence loss.
- Required cell or human reviewer unavailable.
- Performance can pass only with quality changes.
