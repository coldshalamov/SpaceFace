## Outcome

Flying is **nimble and intentional**. The owner's playtest verdict (2026-08-24): *"it's not possible
to fly in this game in a way that's nimble, every ship is heavy like underwater or something and the
auto-target flight system just kind of lazily inches along the line like a Waymo in a school-zone."*
The target, from `design/vision/CRUCIBLE_SURVIVAL_MASTER_PLAN.md` §21A: **"Every ship should look
like it meant to do what it just did."** Not faster — intentional. The player half is crisp low-speed
response, honest momentum, and hulls that feel different; the enemy half is hull-relative capability
envelopes, one choreographed four-ship wing, and one twelve-body cohort that flows.

## Leaves

- `.00` — **the speed governor stops crawling.** Measured: cruise 112–133 WU/s, floor 14 (~1/8);
  `worstCurvatureAhead` takes MAX curvature over the lookahead and a hand stroke sampled every 8
  screen px reads its own jitter as a hairpin, pinning the hull to the floor for the whole stroke.
  Done when a drawn stroke is flown at a speed a player would choose AND still tracked. The existing
  tracking test measures cross-track and never speed — it would pass at 1 WU/s; add the speed bar.
- `.01` — player flight feel: crisp low-speed response, honest momentum, strong brake/yaw settle,
  hulls you can feel the difference between (§21A.5). Proof: a repeatable slalom and reversal course,
  Hitch vs Wasp visibly different, no loss of honest momentum.
- `.02` — Motion Lab: deterministic movement scenarios and motion telemetry so feel is measured
  rather than argued (§21A.23–.25). Scenarios M1, M4, M6, M8, M11 run deterministically and produce
  comparable numbers.
- `.03` — hull-relative enemy capability envelopes and desired-state trajectory control (§21A.6–.7).
  Enemy motion derives from the hull it is flying, not a shared constant.
- `.04` — one four-ship wing with real choreography: wedge entry, fan widening, two ships on distinct
  crossing lanes while two screen, extension without instant turn-back, reform through merge
  corridors (§21A.9–.13). A player impulse can break the sequence; disrupted members do not snap back.
- `.05` — one twelve-body fodder cohort that reads as a river or crescent and stays physically
  throwable (§21A.14). It flows as a shoal, not a dozen independent seekers jittering at one point.

Sequencing: `.00` first (small, contained, immediately felt). `.01` and `.02` belong together —
feel that is not measured is feel that regresses. `.03`–`.05` are the enemy half and depend on `.01`.

## Non-goals

- No new direct position/velocity writes — motion comes through the canonical physical control path
  or it does not ship (§21A.28).
- No campaign AI fork. One game path.
- No performance regression hidden by cutting entity count or quality.
- No permanent tuning promoted from §21A without a leaf's measured proof.

## Work breakdown/phased implementation

`.00`: characterize current corner-speed behavior with a focused test (stroke in, commanded speed
out); replace the MAX-curvature-over-lookahead read with a model that filters stroke jitter (smoothed
or percentile curvature over a physical window, not raw 8-px samples); add the speed bar to the
tracking test; verify tracking still holds on deliberately jagged strokes.
`.01`: per-hull feel envelopes through the existing assisted governor — low-speed response, brake and
yaw settle — tuned against a repeatable slalom/reversal course; Hitch vs Wasp compared on the same
course. `.02`: deterministic Motion Lab scenarios M1/M4/M6/M8/M11 with motion telemetry, built beside
`.01` so every feel change lands with numbers. `.03`: hull-relative capability envelopes and
desired-state control in `tacticalAI`, replacing shared constants. `.04`: the four-ship wing state
machine (wedge → fan → crossing lanes → extension → merge-corridor reform), breakable by player
impulse. `.05`: the twelve-body cohort flow field, still physically throwable.

## Performance

No new per-frame allocation in the control path; the governor change is arithmetic on data already
read. Enemy leaves reuse the existing tacticalAI tick and cadence rules (§8.2 `PQ-080`) — no extra
per-frame scans, no growth in query volume beyond the cohort's own members; the twelve-body cohort
must not regress crowded-flight sim p95 (5 ms budget), proven by a matched before/after on the same
seed.

## Verification budget

Focused node tests per leaf (speed bar added to the tracking test in `.00`; deterministic scenario
hashes in `.02`); `check:sim` / 47-A goldens stay green; `check:playable` before any done; proof of
feel is normal-speed capture at the shipping chase camera, never a flattering angle.
