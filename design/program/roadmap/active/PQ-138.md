## Outcome

Violence has witnesses and consequences. When the player kills something in a patrol's sight, the
patrol visibly chooses between staying with the wreck and chasing the attacker. When cargo spills,
an NPC comes for it. When guns go off near a hauler, the hauler flees. A wreck drifts and tumbles
instead of parking flat. A broken contract clause turns the mission into a different mission instead
of failing it. At one reference site, every one of those leaves a trace the player can see later.
Bar **B10** in `design/FEEL_CONTRACT.md`.

## How agents get this wrong

Read this before touching code. The integrator rejects a unit that matches any line (§1.6, §19).

- Building a new system where a listener is missing: every leaf here is a consumer of an event that already fires.
- Spawning responders or scavengers at the player: they fly from somewhere; teleporting NPCs are a bug.
- Making civilians flee everything: radius and cause matter; ordinary traffic beyond the radius must be unaffected (that is in the done-when).
- Failing a mission on a clause break with a nicer message: mutate, never fail.
