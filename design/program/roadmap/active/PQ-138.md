# PQ-138 — The world reacts: make the systems that already exist collide

## Outcome

Violence has witnesses and consequences. When the player kills something in a patrol's sight, the
patrol visibly chooses between staying with the wreck and chasing the attacker. When cargo spills,
an NPC comes for it. When guns go off near a hauler, the hauler flees. A wreck drifts and tumbles
instead of parking flat. A broken contract clause turns the mission into a different mission instead
of failing it. At one reference site, every one of those leaves a trace the player can see later.
Bar **B10** in `design/FEEL_CONTRACT.md`.

## Source

Owner, 2026-09-03 (`design/VISION.md` Part II: "A damaged ship is not merely an entity with low
health. It is now a problem"; "The player is not the center of the universe"; "Failure should
create content"). Audit 2026-09-03: **every one of the nine consequence beats is already a
registered production system**, and three world-reaction events have **zero listeners**. This packet
is connection, not construction.

## Leaves

| Leaf | Outcome | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| `.00` | **The witness has a choice.** `lawSecurity` consumes `aftermathWreck:spawned` and `survivorPod:ejected`, writes a `victimAnchor` onto the incident, and `_dispatchIncident` splits responders: one holds at the anchor (station-keeping, scanning, pod pickup), the rest pursue. The choice is visible: the holder decelerates and circles the wreck. | B10a: within 10 s of a witnessed kill, one responder holds and one pursues, in a deterministic scenario and a capture. |
| `.01` | **Spilled cargo is noticed.** `traffic` consumes `freight:cargoSpilled` (and `salvage:fieldVulture`) and dispatches a salvor job to the spill; `_isSalvageableBody` accepts freight pickups; pirates near the route may take the job instead of a civilian salvor. | B10b: an NPC reaches spilled cargo within 30 s; the player can beat it there. |
| `.02` | **Civilians react to nearby violence.** `traffic` consumes `combat:damage` / `law:incidentOpened` with a radius test; haulers within 300 WU flee or re-route, workers hold, a tug keeps its tow. Uses the existing `ai:flee` / job-suspend paths. | B10c: course change within 3 s in scenario + capture; ordinary traffic unaffected beyond the radius. |
| `.03` | **Wrecks drift and tumble.** The wreck spec inherits the dying ship's velocity and spin (bounded), carries an off-plane pose, and still saves per sector. Tumble bleeds off with the shipped angular damping. | A kill at speed leaves a wreck that keeps moving and turning; save/reload round-trips it. |
| `.04` | **Failure mutates.** A broken contract clause mutates the mission (escape, restitution, salvage, recovery) instead of failing it; one authored mutation per clause type; the mission log reads "So then…". | No `_failMission` from a clause break on the default route; each clause type has a scenario. |
| `.05` | **The evidence ledger at one site.** At the reference site, within one session: a wreck persists, traffic visibly thins on a disrupted route, a price moves, a rumor appears, a structure shows a patch. | A checklist scenario detects all five traces from one player-caused incident. |
