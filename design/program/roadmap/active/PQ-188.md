## How agents get this wrong

Read this before touching code. The integrator rejects a unit that matches any line.

- Changing what the instruments *do* — the centerpieces and verbs survive; only the presentation moves.
- Breaking the attention pass: louder instruments, keys on the windshield, a second receipts channel.
- Restyling the HUD before the kit exists.
- Reaching for glow to make speed or threat "bold"; scale, face and temperature do that.
- Calling a leaf done on a green check without the visual review against the sheet.
