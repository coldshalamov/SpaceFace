## Outcome

A fresh asteroid in Asteroid Works reads as geology, not confetti: contiguous metal veins with a grain,
strata bands by depth, sealed gas pockets of 2–6 cells, ice lenses, a rare exotic core deep in the body,
and plain matrix everywhere else — so "geology is the tech tree" and "feed through faces" are decisions
the player can see at the site register. Measured 2026-08-21: the current generator in
`src/systems/drill.js` rolls each cell independently (189 seam bodies, largest 3 cells).

## Targets

A 28×45 field has roughly 6–12 metal seams of 4–14 cells, 3–6 gas pockets, 1–3 exotic bodies deep,
basalt bands, matrix everywhere else. Evidence: seam-size histogram, site-register still, golden
telemetry unchanged or rebased.

## Work breakdown/phased implementation

Phase 0: histogram of seam sizes on the current generator (characterization). Phase 1: pure seeded field +
vein growth + strata + pocket placement as a function `generateFormations(seed, family, w, h)` with
deterministic tests. Phase 2: wire behind `asteroidFormationModel`; preserve or explicitly rebase goldens.
Phase 3: site-register still and histogram in the receipt.
