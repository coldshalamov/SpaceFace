## Outcome

A player recognises Ceres from thirty seconds of activity and Helios from thirty seconds of
different activity — not from a colour grade. Between incidents the world does boring jobs on
screen (inspections, transfers, repair, waiting, a tug moving something enormous), so that when
something goes wrong the change is legible. A handful of memorable one-offs make the universe
specific.

## Source

`design/VISION.md` Part II, "Every place needs a reason to exist", "The game needs ordinary life",
"Not everything needs to be systemically important." Existing seams: `src/systems/npcJobsRuntime.js`
