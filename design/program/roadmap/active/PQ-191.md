## The taste calls on the table (`.01` only — approve, adjust, or overturn)

These are the decisions that batch made where the law allowed more than one answer. Each names the
alternative it declined. The reviewer's judgment, not this list, is the record.

1. **Grammar debt is reported, not fatal** (PQ-184.00): 24 surfaces over the 2 ms budget print as
   named debt in every `check:ui:budgets` run; `--strict` makes it red. The packet's own order
   (instrument before fixes) motivated this; the alternative was gating on the grammar budget
   immediately and accepting a red chain until PQ-184.01/.02 land. Check whether the debt table is
   being read, and whether `--strict` has an owner.
2. **The Cinder Run Courier is a deterministic traffic fixture** (PQ-143.02): a new authored-slot
   seam in traffic.js (`_ensureCinderRunCourierFixture`), start-sector only, express role. The
   alternative was a pick-pool member (rejected in review: it broke Ceres's authored cast) or no
   courier at all. Is one far-too-fast fixture the right amount of whimsy, and is the seam the
   right shape to generalize if more fixtures ever want it?
3. **One-off set-piece art reuses packaged props** (PQ-143.02): the tug is `place_dead_hulk`, the
   shrine is `place_memorial_array` (in Ceres, so the art is unique per sector), the tanker is the
   freighter-bow hero. The alternative was authoring new GLBs or fielding the held-back
   tug/tanker hulls (an owner call, deliberately not taken). Do the set pieces read as specific
   enough at the shipping camera, or do they read as recycled dressing?
4. **The plume corkscrew's numbers** (PQ-139.04): 4.5 WU card amplitude / 2 rad/s saturation /
   0.35 rad-per-WU screw for enemies; 6 WU / 2 rad/s for the player contrail; 0.35 reduce-motion
   scale. All test-pinned but all chosen by eye against the shipping camera. Too much? Too little?
5. **The bars-as-checks polarity** (PQ-186.00): B11 pins "exactly one owed clause may be unmet"
   rather than pinning the owed clause red; B10's PQ-138.03 rider is asserted under its own claim,
   not B10's sentence. Both were review findings implemented as stated — confirm the shape is
   what the contract intended.
6. **PQ-144.00's budget table lives in PERF_BUDGET §8** with per-§13C-packet witness guards
   rather than as thresholds in code. Confirm prose-with-guards is the right home versus a checked
   artifact.

## How agents get this wrong

Read this before touching code. The integrator rejects a unit that matches any line.

- A paper review: reading receipts, grepping diffs, or listing defects while those defects are
  still on camera. Play, then fix.
- Answering thin feel with new content, a new faction, a new screen, or more particles.
- Treating `implemented` or `done` as "the integrator already signed off." This leaf is the
  second pair of eyes.
- Reviewing a leaf you landed. Take the other leaf, or leave this packet for another agent.
- Re-recording `test/*.expected.json` goldens to pass. Re-shooting reference frames without a
  causal record.
- Retuning drill cadence (`MOVE_HOLD_DELAY_S` 0.18, `MOVE_CRUISE_INTERVAL_S` 0.24, `BORE_BITE_S`
  0.18), making `noteSiteCourier` pay, or changing contact-ring preview so it no longer clones
  the field.
- Colliding with occupied capture/repair leaves: `PQ-184.01` / `.02` / `.03`, `PQ-022` / `PQ-018`
  capture units, and any exact path a live `NOW.md` row names. Split; do not swallow foreign hunks.
- Opening a second backlog, a new finishing-program phase, or a parallel queue of "follow-ups."
  Adopted fixes land in the owning packet's files; only a surface with no owner becomes a new
  packet, graded as §15.9.
- Dropping the six `.01` taste calls, or treating this packet as a rubber stamp.
