### Selecting render path and normalized metrics

- Both selecting manifests pin `renderGraph: false` and every selected frame must report `_lastRenderPath` as `bloom` or `straight`. A graph-on trace may be reported as corroborating evidence only: the current graph-path `drawPreparedFrame` query encloses scene, normal/AO, bloom, and composite work and therefore may not be counted as scene-only or added to child groups.
- On the live default `bloom` path, `sceneGpuMs = bloomScene` and `postGpuMs = bloomDownsample + bloomComposite`. `bloomComposite` is one physical fused draw and owns bloom sampling/addition, tone mapping, grade, grain, vignette, sRGB encoding, and the final default-framebuffer presentation; there is no separate upsample, grade, vignette, grain, or final-copy query. On the `straight` path, `sceneGpuMs = drawPreparedFrame` and `postGpuMs = 0`. `totalGpuMs = sceneGpuMs + postGpuMs`. Every summed group must share the same immutable origin display/render-frame identity; a missing, duplicate, aliased, overlapping, fabricated, or mismatched group invalidates that frame.
- The post-isolation selecting cell uses the `bloom` path in both default and diagnostic copy-only/post-bypass forms. The bypass preserves the same `bloomScene` render, omits `bloomDownsample` and `bloomComposite`, and records one physical `bloomBypassCopy` presentation group. Thus `defaultTotal = bloomScene + bloomDownsample + bloomComposite`, `bypassTotal = bloomScene + bloomBypassCopy`, and switching to the straight or graph path cannot satisfy post-isolation evidence.
- `rendererCpuMs` is the origin-linked render submission/prepare owner time, excluding simulation, UI, and background jobs. `coveredFragmentPixels` is the diagnostic sum of opaque, transparent, lighting, bloom-input, and effect covered pixels. `pipelineTransitions` is the normalized count of program, blend, depth/stencil, render-target, and texture-binding transitions under a versioned key schema.
- For block `b`, `I_b` is the Type-7 median no-op display interval in that block; runtime/capture `I` is the Type-7 median of the seven `I_b` values. Absolute timing predicates compare each block statistic with its paired `I_b` before cross-block estimation; no predicate substitutes an assumed 16.7 ms interval.

### Frozen statistical operators

1. `Q_p(x)` is the Hyndman-Fan Type-7 empirical quantile: sort `n` finite samples, set `h = (n - 1) × p`, `j = floor(h)`, and linearly interpolate `x[j]` to `x[min(j + 1, n - 1)]`. `Q_0.5` is the median; p95 and p99 are `Q_0.95` and `Q_0.99`.
2. Within each block/cell, duration and count metrics use `Q_0.5` unless a p95/p99 tail is named. Framewise shares use the median of origin-matched per-frame ratios. Owner-explanation coverage uses the exact qualifying-frame count divided by the exact eligible-frame count. Frames lacking all required origin-linked terms are invalid, not silently dropped.
3. Every high/low operator is calculated **inside each paired block first**. For positive cell medians `H_b` and `L_b`: `FE_b(y; H,L) = (H_b - L_b) / H_b`; `growth_b(y; H,L) = H_b / L_b`; `symChange_b(y; H,L) = max(H_b / L_b, L_b / H_b) - 1`; and `elasticity_b(y,x) = FE_b(y) / FE_b(x)`. A non-finite/non-positive cell median or non-positive `FE_b(x)` used as an elasticity denominator invalidates that predicate. The manifest freezes which cell ID is `H` and `L`; a reversed/mismatched cell definition is invalid, but a finite opposite-signed observed `FE_b(y)` is valid evidence handled only by the named six-of-seven rule and interval bounds.
4. The cross-block point estimate for every declared scalar is `Q_0.5` of its seven block-level values. The pairing/resampling unit is one complete counterbalanced block: a bootstrap draw resamples seven block indices with replacement and carries every cell/metric needed by that predicate together.
5. Before capture, each manifest contains a sorted literal registry of every statistic key. A key is the ASCII string `pq042-r5<US>runtimeKind<US>predicateId<US>scalarId<US>cellPairId<US>transformId`, where `<US>` is byte `0x1f`, every identifier matches `[A-Za-z0-9_.:-]+`, and the exact UTF-8 bytes are hashed into the manifest contract. No implementation-generated aliases or post-capture keys are allowed.
6. Each statistic key gets exactly 20,000 deterministic paired resamples. FNV-1a is `h = 2166136261; h = Math.imul((h ^ byte) >>> 0, 16777619) >>> 0` for each key byte. Set `x = (47 ^ h) >>> 0`; if `x === 0`, set `x = 0x6d2b79f5`. For each PRNG draw apply 32-bit xorshift in order—`x = (x ^ (x << 13)) >>> 0`, `x = (x ^ (x >>> 17)) >>> 0`, `x = (x ^ (x << 5)) >>> 0`—then `u = x / 2^32` and `blockIndex = floor(7 × u)`. Consume exactly seven consecutive draws per replicate in replicate-major order. The 95% percentile interval is Type-7 `Q_0.025`/`Q_0.975` over the 20,000 bootstrap point estimates.
7. Transform first, then bootstrap: effects, growth ratios, elasticities, shares, symmetric changes, absolute timing ratios, cross-multiplied dominance margins, and agreement margins are computed per paired block before the median/resampling step. Ratios of pooled frames or independently resampled cells are forbidden.
8. The six-of-seven direction rule applies only to named signed contrasts: `FE_b`, the post-bypass reduction, selected-scope reduction, selected-aggregate reduction, and the same-metric structure-minus-pixel effect. One finite opposite-signed block remains in the estimate and does not invalidate the predicate. Absolute ceilings, shares, growth ratios, symmetric equivalence bands, cross-multiplied margins, and owner-coverage proportions do not receive a sign vote; they pass only their declared point and interval bounds.
9. A predicate with `point >= a, lower >= b` or `point <= a, upper <= b` must satisfy both clauses. A confidence interval crossing its named lower/upper confidence boundary is `BLOCKED`; it is not rounded or converted to a pass. All keys, reference seed/index vectors, transforms, invalid-data rulings, and bounds are hashed into the manifest contract.

### Frozen post-correction acceptance for A/B/C

The clean pre-fix selector is intervention authority; it is not a condition that the pathology must survive its own correction. Before mutation, the selected-branch packet revision must freeze one logical `selectedScopeId`, one duration-valued `selectedScopeMetric`, its within-block statistic, the selected aggregate domain (`totalGpuMs` for a GPU scope or `rendererCpuMs` for a CPU/submission scope), its default-quality selecting and ordinary cells, and a complete pre-to-post physical-owner lineage.

The lineage is an executable graph, not a display-name comparison. Every pre-fix physical owner/query group in the logical scope is mapped exactly once as unchanged, renamed, split, merged/fused, or structurally removed; every post-fix scope member is mapped from a pre-fix member or declared as a newly introduced descendant required to perform the same logical work. Split and merge edges may be many-to-many, but no physical duration may be counted twice or belong to both the selected scope and an unselected owner. Every mapped GPU group retains immutable origin identity and non-overlap proof. A removed member contributes zero only when a structural test proves that its pass/command/resource invocation is absent and that its work was either eliminated as redundant or represented by named mapped descendants. Missing, duplicate, overlapping, or unmapped pre/post scope coverage returns `BLOCKED`; renaming, fusion, splitting, or removal may never manufacture an apparent reduction.

The following seven-block baseline/candidate comparison is then evaluated independently in each runtime:

1. PQ-034 alternates the exact pre-fix and post-fix candidates inside seven paired counterbalanced blocks with the same seed/save/input/camera/content/runtime contract. Only scale `1.00`, default population, default post, and production bloom/straight frames participate.
2. For each origin-complete frame, `preScopeMs` and `postScopeMs` are the non-overlapping sums of all lineage members on their respective side. For each block, `selectedReduction_b = (preScope_b - postScope_b) / preScope_b`, using the frozen within-block statistic. The point estimate must be at least `0.10`, at least six block contrasts must be positive, and the 95% lower bound must be strictly greater than zero in both runtimes.
3. For the frozen selected aggregate domain `d`, `aggregateReduction_b(d) = (preP95_b(d) - postP95_b(d)) / preP95_b(d)`. Its point estimate must be at least `0.01`, at least six block contrasts must be positive, and its 95% lower bound must be strictly greater than zero. Thus a local scope win cannot pass while its cumulative same-domain cost grows.
4. Every non-selected aggregate domain is guarded by `aggregateRegressionMargin_b(d) = max(0, postP95_b(d) - preP95_b(d)) / max(0.03 × preP95_b(d), 0.25 ms)`, with point and upper bound at most `1.00`. `totalGpuMs` is always either the selected improvement domain or a mandatory guarded domain; when GPU is selected, `rendererCpuMs` is also guarded. This aggregate gate applies before per-owner margins so individually small regressions cannot sum to an unbounded slowdown.
5. For every measured unselected duration owner `u`, including newly introduced owners outside the lineage, `regressionMargin_b(u) = max(0, postP95_b(u) - preP95_b(u)) / max(0.03 × preP95_b(u), 0.25 ms)`. Its point and upper bound must be at most `1.00`. Missing a previously reported unselected owner is invalid rather than treated as zero.
6. An unresolved dominant owner exists when an unselected origin-linked owner has post-fix p95 / `I_b` point and lower bound at least `0.20`, post-fix `totalGpuMs` p95 / `I_b` point and lower bound at least `0.70`, and exceeds the corrected selected scope duration in at least six of seven blocks. Such a result is `BLOCKED` pending an exact separately admitted owner/follow-up; it is not hidden by declaring the selected correction successful. If total GPU is below the corrective floor, no owner is dominant under this rule.
7. Semantic, presentation, fixed-camera image, temporal, compilation, context-recovery, and default-quality checks must pass. The frozen selector is rerun and reported, but A/B/C may become false, D may become healthy, or another raw fact may change. Those outcomes do not fail a correction that satisfies items 1-7; incomplete lineage or an unresolved dominant owner blocks terminal closure.

D0/D1 use the original selecting matrix and evidence-only closeout; no artificial pre/post reduction is required or manufactured for D.
