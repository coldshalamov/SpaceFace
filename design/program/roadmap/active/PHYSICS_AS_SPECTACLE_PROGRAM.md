## Global art direction

The authoritative direction is **bright, kinetic, colorful arcade-industrial science fiction**.
The value and radiance hierarchy is:

1. Deep space remains darkest.
2. World geometry uses varied industrial materials rather than default-grey or clay sameness.
3. Ships retain strong faction paint, silhouette, and identity.
4. Engines and working machinery are bright.
5. Massline, force fields, weapons, and destruction are brightest.

The visual test is bright force against colored, materially varied hulls. Bloom supports the value
hierarchy; it must not erase faction paint, material separation, contact direction, or silhouettes.
