## Owner revision — 2026-09-10

Prioritize player hull, incoming danger, bank contacts, web endpoints, active traps and useful terrain. Bright VFX and damage numbers are part of the arcade fantasy; use shape, timing and hierarchy to keep them readable. A wave announcement is not a lethal-attack telegraph; death advice may admit that no matching warning was recorded.

Read the owner revision in [the Crucible master plan](../../../vision/CRUCIBLE_SURVIVAL_MASTER_PLAN.md) and [build_map.md §16](../../../../build_map.md). It supersedes contradictory older acceptance wording below. Product breadth stays in scope.
