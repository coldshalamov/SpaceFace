## Outcome

On the default Helios / Kessler flyby, every ship the player can lock is a complete manufactured
body from the same game as Hitch, reverse thrust reads as force leaving a nozzle, and no tube,
hoop, or floating accessory stands in for a designed object. Shelf hulls and place remasters
reach the camera only after that flyby is true.

## Why this packet exists

The 3D side had many doors and no single board. `PQ-050` remasters flyable ships one at a time.
`PQ-136` fielded unused packs (leaves `.00`–`.03` done). `VISUAL_WORLD_CLEANUP` and
`GRAPHICS_3D_CAMPAIGN` named live defects but were not queue units, so `--next` never handed them
out. `needed-assets.md` still calls Hitch / Pelican / Wasp blocked. The owner asked for the map of
what is missing, below bar, unused, or invisible.

Census 2026-09-09 (live maps, not a filename grep):

- All 13 roster ships have dedicated models and render packages. Hitch and Wasp are the only
  *required* whole-ships at bootstrap. The empty-admission allowlist still omits Pelican, Mule,
  Drifter, the nine factory hulls, and the Massline liner — a targeted ship can still publish as
  blank space.
- Only Wasp is `accepted`. Everything else that draws is a candidate. Hornet is wired and has not
  closed Hitch-plus (`PQ-050.01` residual).
- Owner playtest 2026-09-08: Hitch reads as a ship; a nearby NPC reads as leftover blocks; reverse
  reads as two needles. Volumetric retro already exists and still fails on camera.
- Code-built cans, gates, drones, mines, wrecks, and mass seeds have no model file
  (`WORLD_VISUAL_CENSUS.md` A).
- Factory remasters of already-live Ashline / Helios / work boats sit on disk. An earlier remap
  onto them made traffic invisible. Do not remap until that exact body is packaged and beats live.
- Corsair still shares the pirate Rig. Arclight has no route. Tanker and inspection cutter are
  held. Faction kits exist and nothing reads them. Dock / hulk / debris remaster is blocked at
  live presentation. Station fallback is still a fat cylinder plus hoops.

## The bar

1. **One game.** A stranger shown Hitch and the nearest NPC does not ask which title each is from.
2. **Complete hulls.** No floating engine, wing, socket, or glow blob. Empty targeting locks fail
   this packet.
3. **Jets are jets.** Brake / reverse is force leaving a nozzle, same family as the main drive.
4. **A valid file is not accepted art.** Packaged and wired is not Hitch-plus.

Hitch is the floor, not the photocopy. Do not dump Hitch. Do not paste Hitch panels onto NPCs.
Chase camera only (`play_chase`, `play_chase_abeam`, `play_chase_close`). No seats.

## How agents get this wrong

- Wiring a factory remaster that is not packaged, then calling the blank lock a renderer bug.
- Starting Corsair / Arclight / faction kits while the opening flyby still shows a kitbash NPC.
- Remastering Hornet interiors or studio three-quarters. The player sees 60° chase at 144 WU.
- Lowering texture size to dodge a Blender crash. That was already the wrong inference on Hornet.
- Treating `needed-assets.md` or the 2026-08-08 catalog as live status.
- Measuring unused models with a filename grep.
- Answering a missing piece with a glow blob or a second needle trail on the failed retro volume.
- Editing Hitch / Kestrel.
- Cutting default quality or deleting Hitch detail to pass a perf gate.
- Commissioning a new hull for a slot an unused authored body already fills.

## Non-goals

- Hitch remaster.
- Cabin kits, seats, studio beauty.
- New ship commissions while a shelf body can fill the slot.
- Generic runtime LOD1/2 selector (later; not “looks broken”).
- Frontend, hitch smoothness (`PQ-129`), Asteroid Works interiors.
