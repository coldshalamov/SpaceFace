## Outcome

Turn freight from background traffic into one ordinary, physical story:

> A manifest-bearing convoy attracts a raider; cargo can be disabled, stolen, recovered, or lost;
> the player can intervene with flight, weapons, collision, and Massline; law and markets react; and
> a destroyed ship leaves the same causally identified wreck after travel or Continue.

The five units are independently reviewable and compose into a 5–10 minute route:

1. autonomous, bounded pirate predation;
2. physical manifest cargo custody;
3. Massline civilian disable recovery;
4. immediate-to-persistent wreck identity;
5. scripted convoy-loss economy pressure and news.

## Unit contracts

### A — bounded pirate predation

- One authored raider deterministically acquires one eligible manifest carrier.
- The actor publishes motive, exact target, approach telegraph, response window, and bounded objective.
- Engagement authority grants only that exact NPC-to-civilian relationship; team 2 never becomes
  globally hostile.
- Target death, disable/custody, escape, sector change, save materialization, or stale identity clears
  or rebuilds ephemeral pairing.
- Tactical AI remains the sole movement/fire intent writer; law can observe the same damage receipts.

### B — physical manifest cargo custody

- Scripted haulers carry deterministic manifests built by the current freight-causality owner.
- A bounded breach/disable/death outcome ejects conserved physical cargo; no cargo is invented twice.
- Pickups/pods use existing cargo/mining collection authority.
- Custody can be held by the hauler, spilled, player-recovered, or raider-secured; state transitions
  are deterministic and idempotent across duplicate events.

### C — civilian disable recovery

- A genuine civilian drive-disable creates a timed Massline recovery option without requiring the
  civilian to become a surrendered hostile.
- Latch, reel, and tow into a lawful station ring use the existing custody geometry and attachment
  authority.
- Success prevents the kill and settles rescued cargo; release/timeout/destruction produces a
  distinct abandonment/loss state.
- No direct credit, cargo, reputation, heat, or physics writes bypass their owners.

### D — immediate-to-persistent wreck identity

- The immediate kill wreck and durable aftermath marker share one stable incident identity.
- Travel/return and Continue rematerialize the same unsalvaged wreck once.
- Completing salvage clears both live and durable representations exactly once.
- Provenance retains victim, killer/cause, sector, manifest/convoy identity when available.

### E — convoy-loss economy bridge

- Scripted `lost` and `robbed` convoy outcomes reuse `buildLossIntent`.
- The destination receives bounded scarcity pressure and one news cause through existing owners.
- Duplicate resolution, save replay, and encounter cleanup cannot apply pressure twice.
- Successful arrival remains positive and is not reclassified as loss.

## Ordinary route

- **0:00–1:00** enter an ordinary trade-lane pocket and read a manifest-bearing hauler.
- **1:00–2:00** a raider shadows, telegraphs, and commits against cargo rather than the player.
- **2:00–4:00** defend, steal, ram, disable, or ignore; no debug setup or accepted mission required.
- **4:00–6:30** cargo changes custody and a disabled hauler can be Massline-towed to safety.
- **6:30–8:00** recovered, stolen, or lost quantity produces settlement, shortage/news, and law truth.
- **8:00–10:00** travel/return or Continue shows the same wreck, or its durable absence after salvage.

## Cost and quality budget

- No unbounded entity scan, history, receipt, pickup, or spawn growth.
- Cargo quantity is conserved at every transition; economy, cargo, law, and credits keep one writer.
- New AI pairing work is deterministic, spatially bounded, and does not add a second intent writer.
- Save data uses stable identities, bounded receipts, and normalization for missing legacy fields.
- Default population, visual quality, physics breadth, and accessibility are not reduced to pass.

## Verification budget

- Each unit starts with one seconds-scale owner regression and expands only to its adjacent owners.
- Required cross-unit proof covers production order, duplicate events, stale entity replacement,
  sector travel, Continue, New Game/reset, and partial failure.
- Deterministic exit: focused suites, `npm run check:baseline`, and
  `npm run check:sim:v3:compare`.
- Route exit: one current Browser route at normal camera/settings, then the relevant reduced-motion
  or input variant; Electron parity is claimed only if actually observed.
- Review lenses: (1) deterministic authority/single-writer/save; (2) blind ordinary-route
  player-story/physical-craft review.

## Non-goals

- Global team-2 hostility, a second tactical AI, direct market/cargo/law writes, or player collision
  damage.
- A second generic loot fountain, replacement of derelict beam salvage, or inflated reward duplication.
- New ship/prop art, PQ-045 identity assets, broad activity-pocket propagation, or a new UI mode.
- Contraband, fence networks, survivor pods, regional routing, or every possible patrol/escort response.
