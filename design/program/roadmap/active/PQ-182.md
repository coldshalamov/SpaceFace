## Outcome

The Crucible's screens make the next play obvious. The door leads with a distinctive starter,
five arenas and launch; records and challenges live in a disclosure. Swarm's armory shows wallet,
fixed prices, fitting consequences and concise descriptions of changed actions. Players can buy
several items, save for a known toy, refit and start the next round without an enforced timer.
Results lead with farthest round, actual death cause and immediate retry, followed by a memorable
moment and build identity. The HUD keeps hull, boost and ready tools readable amid bright combat.

The owner's 2026-09-10 direction supersedes aesthetic matrix gates and mandatory captures above.
Use the existing kit where it helps; add imagery, color, depth and hierarchy appropriate to an
arcade game. Inspect and iterate on the real screen. A stylesheet match is not the done condition.
