## Outcome

The drift-bomb bay is not just routable, it is reviewed: the eight safe/armed/committed/burst states
and the gravity/tar fields read on the default route at combat zoom and under reduced motion/flash;
the real cue IDs are in the catalog with bounded, source-following sounds and victim-attached

## How agents get this wrong

Inventing a second ordnance simulation, a second HUD, or eight target-scanning loops. Turning
`physicsBody` back on without removing the kinematic owner. Declaring missing audio "graceful
degradation". Copying the fuze/cooldown/effect implementation into NPCs instead of calling the verb.
Penalising every experimental drop with ammo before the core verbs feel good.
