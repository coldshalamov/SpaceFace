## Outcome

The player learns the economy is real by watching it move and profits by understanding it: a rotating ticker of real events, a market chart with the last ten minutes and a forecast cone, supply-chain glyphs that teach what feeds what, an event card when docking under a shortage or blockade, a profit readout on sale, contracts whose clauses fire mid-mission, and a black market whose entrance is a smuggling run. Six interesting decisions an hour is the bar.

## How agents get this wrong

### Senior implementation direction for `.07` (2026-09-06)

Keep custody, exactly-once settlement and equipment persistence. Start with the existing programmed
miner operation: show output, stored shipment, current limiting stage, last realised sale and
operating cost together. A stopped operation must retain its machine and resume after the missing
resource returns, including after Continue. Prove the replacement limits against one machine,
several competing machines and a saturated destination before retiring the bucket/discount for
that route. Do not remove unrelated safeguards or invent recurring punishment to balance income.

Read this before touching code. The integrator rejects a unit that matches any line (§1.6, §19).

- Inventing news that is not a sim event: every ticker line must cite the event it came from.
- Drawing a forecast that the sim does not compute: use predictPriceCurve; never fake a trend.
- Making the black market a menu: its door is a physical smuggling run.
