## Outcome

A voice on the industrial channel says Berth Three is down a flywheel and the replacement is in transit. You can see it: a caged spindle drifting off the catcher line, a receiving machine with its rails open, and a short queue of work that cannot proceed. You tow it, shove it, or fight over it; you bring it through the fork and watch the machine arrest a moving mass; or you take it to the Quiet and let the legitimate operation keep waiting. Berth Three either starts working or does not, and that is visible the next time you fly past.

Slice A of this feature is live: the contract exists on the Tethys board, the assembly is a real body, the fork brakes and settles it, custody is re-proven at handoff, it pays once with a condition bonus, and it survives save and load. What is missing is everything that makes it a *situation* rather than a delivery — the art, the choice, the pressure, the consequence, and a player who has actually played it.

## How agents get this wrong

These are the ways a lazy first try fails this packet. Read them, then do the work as if the owner will play it tonight.

- Wiring the existing cargo pod scaled up to 16 WU and calling `.00` done: the pod is authored for a 6 WU capsule, and stretching it is exactly the silhouette lie the art doc rejects.
- A glowing goal circle, a magic bubble or an invisible wall across the mouth instead of a machine that arrests a mass.
- Touch-to-delete custody: a receiver contact that consumes or teleports the load fails the whole feature, however green the test is.
- Secretly slowing a fast body so the capture test passes. A refused capture is a refused capture; the player recovers and tries again.
- Paying for the same physical assembly twice by changing which receiver it was labelled for.
- Answering `.05` with more enemies or `.04` with a bigger reward: the consequence is a visible local state, and the pressure is two hulls with intelligible intent.
- Importing the packet's lab runtime into the game, or shipping the lab's standalone load card as a fourth HUD anchor.
