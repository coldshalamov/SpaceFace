## Outcome

Accepted authored ship and place assets compile reproducibly into versioned render packages before gameplay, so the ordinary route loads shared immutable geometry/material/texture resources and lightweight stable roots without runtime clone/apply/merge/attribute-normalization/de-index work. The proof route compares source and compiled packages for the Kestrel, one dynamic-surface freighter, and one place at near/mid/far and in motion, then measures cold/warm admission, residency, upload, construction, and first-visible latency with identical semantics and visual quality.

Current Blender, place-source, release-artifact, and release-manifest surfaces remain blocked while the primary visual writer is active. Compiler, schema, deterministic fixture, and isolated loader work may proceed in their own bounded lane; no packet phase may claim a real release asset or runtime cutover until those locks clear and the exact accepted asset revision is rebound.

## Render-package contract

```text
<asset-id>/
  render.glb
  render-package.json
```

Metadata includes compiler/schema version, source/release hashes, stable semantic node IDs, sockets/hardpoints/docking/trail/light/effect anchors, moving/dynamic group boundaries, material pipeline keys, texture references/color space, LOD/HLOD records, bounds/collision references, spatial-cluster IDs, and geometry/material hashes for PQ-034. Authored GLBs remain source truth; packages are reproducible generated outputs and disposable caches.

## Mutexes

- `blender` and `asset-manifest` are intentionally held during the allowed foundation phase.
- `renderer` is required for runtime cutover, not pure compiler/schema/fixture work.
- `browser-gpu` is required only for visual/admission acceptance.
- `package` is not required unless a later explicit build alias is admitted.

## Non-goals

- Reauthoring current ships/places or modifying dirty primary visual work.
- Mesh simplification, texture reduction, material-model change, LOD-distance change, or default-quality reduction in the equivalence phase.
- Generic proxy replacement that breaks stable authored root identity, sockets, interaction, collision, map/place, or save continuity.
- A second asset registry, residency manager, renderer, or permanent source/package fork.
- Generating production packages before exact source/release hashes and manifest ownership are current.
- Claiming steady-state FPS from a transition/admission optimization.

## Performance and quality budget

- Compiler output is byte-identical across two clean builds from the same inputs and versions.
- Runtime package path performs no accepted-route geometry clone/apply/merge/normalization/de-index compilation.
- Immutable resource decode/cache occurs once per content hash; stable roots and declared dynamic groups remain lightweight and generation-safe.
- Package bytes/residency may not grow without a measured admission/submission/traversal benefit and explicit justification.
- Cold/warm admission, first-visible latency, heap/transient memory, upload bytes, draw/program structure, and GPU time are reported separately.
- Near-view silhouette/material response, anchors, animation, dynamic surfaces, collision, sockets, scale, and save identity remain equivalent.
