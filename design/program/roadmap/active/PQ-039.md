## Outcome

Materialized NPC jobs select exactly the same nearest hostile as the current full scan, but one owner-facing batched spatial query replaces per-job `state.entityList` scans. The ordinary route runs natural miner/hauler/patrol jobs through quiet traffic, dense mixed-team traffic, combat, spawn/destroy, sector transitions, equal-distance ties, boundary cells, and save/Continue; selected targets and deterministic digests remain identical while candidate visits grow with nearby density rather than total world population.

This packet begins with the proven NPC-jobs hostile query only. Other scans migrate only when owner counters show a material repeated hot path.

## Query contract

Each request contains stable request/job ID, origin X/Z, radius, relation/team filter, and result limit. Results are ordered by squared distance then stable entity ID. The service resolves live IDs at the current tick, rejects dead/stale entities, reuses scratch storage, and publishes bounded counters. No result object reference survives the tick.

## Non-goals

- Rewriting every entity scan or introducing a general query DSL.
- New spatial index, ECS, Worker, WASM, or AI behavior.
- Changing hostile relation rules, radius, cadence, target persistence, tie order, or job intent.
- Lowering NPC population or update frequency to pass.
- Sorting/materializing all candidates when bounded top-1 selection suffices.
- Retaining stale entity object references across ticks.

## Performance and quality budget

- One batch per eligible NPC-jobs cadence, not per job RPC.
- No per-tick `filter`, closure, full sort, or unbounded allocation in the selected path.
- Scratch capacity grows only to a recorded high-water and then reuses storage; overflow is explicit.
- Candidate visits scale with intersecting cells/local density, not total `entityList` size.
- At 5x population, NPC-jobs query remains a small stable fixed-tick share and selects identical targets.
- Gameplay, save, AI/job outcomes, RNG, and event order remain unchanged.
