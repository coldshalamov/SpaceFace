## RESULT: DONE — `encounter:spawned` is non-zero in every hour 5–9 on both hunter seeds.

## What was broken

The encounter director plans combat beats anchored to named zones and gates each fire on player
proximity (`shape.proximity` + `_playerNearItemZone`). A player parked far from every authored
zone — the hunter bench parks at a deep field for hours — let every combat beat gate-defer to
`MAX_GATE_DEFERS` and fizzle, while prop-only ambients kept the telegraph counter alive. Measured
pre-fix (hunter s8008, 2026-09-18 baseline): `encounter:spawned` 9,13,13,1,1 then **0,0,0,0,0**
in hours 5–9, with `encounter:telegraph` still firing 3–6/hour — telegraphs whose ships never
materialized.

## The fix (landed in three commits ahead of this leaf's battery rerun)

- `d18284845` — proximity-starved combat beats relocate deterministically beside the player once
  the sector's starvation clock matures (`PROXIMITY_STARVE_S` 90 s, `dir.proxStarve` per-sector,
  re-armed by delete-and-re-stamp). Pacing law untouched: gaps, rolling window quotas, pressure
  spend, and authored gates all still gate the fire. Ambient/civilian props keep zone anchoring.
  Also: harass-mercy release valve for unresolvable pins.
- `993927b1d` — starvation clock keyed by sector so the 600 s sector-day replan (which resets
  per-item defer counts) cannot reset it.
- `446c6e5b7` — `combat_busy` counts fights, not liveness: a live combat encounter vetoes the
  lane only while within 2200 WU or exchanging damage inside 240 s; stale combat encounters
  (no exchange for 420 s) resolve `escaped` so one stalled squad cannot veto the lane forever.

## Hardening landed inside the reviewed mechanism (this leaf's mutation)

Independent review of the three fix commits surfaced two defects in the fix's own scope; both
fixed here:

1. **The authored Ceres throughline ambush was relocation-eligible.** `ambush_snare` is
   `deck:combat` + `proximity:true`, so a matured starvation clock could relocate the one-shot
   authored crossing beside a parked player — detaching the telegraph from the durable cohort
   still holding the authored band and spending the crossing on a fight that cannot physically
   happen. The item now waits on plain `defer()` (the replan carries it; it never gate-fizzles).
   `test/encounter-proximity-relocate.test.mjs` covers it: no telegraph/spawn off the killbox,
   item stays queued, `defers` stays 0.
2. **False `harasser:disengaged` for adopted actors.** The mercy valve emitted "the harasser
   breaks off" for Ceres cohort members it cannot stand down, and the strikes bump re-qualified
   the pin in 90 s — a false toast on repeat. Adopted actors now return before the strikes bump
   and the emits; their authored depart/deadline lifecycle owns stand-down.

`node --test test/encounter-proximity-relocate.test.mjs` 4/4, `ceres-activity-ambush-director`
7/7, `encounter-harass-mercy` + `encounter-shapes` 11/11 green. The `pq-141-*` suite reds are a
pre-existing foreign manifest gap (`resolveRuntimeManifest: missing system "stationServices"` —
documented in NOW.md by the PQ-206.00 lane), unrelated to this change.

## Honest residuals (named, not blocking the leaf)

- **Tension-recovery veto can still hold combat beats indefinitely** (`tensionPacingBlockReason`
  → plain `defer`, never fizzle): hull < 50 % or pinned fatigue suppresses `allowCombat` for as
  long as the state persists. The parked-hunter sessions clear recovery (no damage in = fatigue
  decays), which is why the verify numbers pass; a player under sustained no-win pressure could
  still see supply gaps. Candidate follow-up leaf.
- **`_onSaveLoaded` never replans**: post-load dead air up to ~600 s until the next `day:tick`
  plus pressure warm-up from zero. Bounded, pre-existing, unexercised by the battery.
- Telegraph→spawn fizzle persists at a lower rate post-fix (spawn-budget denials between
  telegraph and spawn); verify-8008 h7 shows spawned=3 vs telegraph=8.
- The stricter scratch bar (≥ 4 spawned/hour in h5–9) fails on verify-8008 h7=3. The leaf
  contract is non-zero; the bar file records the stretch target.
- Bench pilots park and never engage the relocated squads (`shots`/`damage` 0 in h5–9) — the
  leaf measures the world *offering* fights; whether the scripted pilot takes them is the pilot's
  policy, not the director's supply. `combat_salvage_economy` chain completion is leaf `.01`.
