# PQ-138.04 — Failure mutates the mission

## WHAT I FOUND

When a job went wrong, the game scolded you and threw the situation away. A red box said
**MISSION FAILED**, the contract vanished from your log, and everything the failure had actually
created — the convoy's wreck still drifting out there, the manifest customs had just seized — became
nothing. The worst moment in the job produced the least content in the game.

## WHAT I CHANGED

Some failures now turn into the next job instead of ending in a red box.

- **The convoy you were escorting died** → a salvage contract goes live: its wreck is still out
  there, and the station that hired you pays for what you bring back.
- **Customs confiscated the sealed cargo** → a restitution job goes live: replace exactly what the
  manifest was carrying and finish the original delivery. The replacement is not handed to you —
  buying it back is the debt.
- **A rescue-priority clause broke** → a recovery job goes live for what the wreck left.

The failure is still a real failure. You still lose the reputation, you still take the settlement,
the contract still ends. What changes is that you are left holding a new objective instead of a dead
end, and the message you get says what the situation turned into rather than what you did wrong.

Failures that are a deliberate choice or leave nothing behind still fail plainly — quitting a job,
getting busted, running out of time, a fine-print breach with no wreck and no lost cargo. "Failure
should *usually* mutate the situation": usually, not always.

Nothing new was invented to do this. Both successor jobs are mission types the game already has, and
they are posted through the same authored-offer channel the story missions already use.

## WHAT YOU WILL FEEL

The escort you lost stops being a wasted twenty minutes. The wreck of the ship you failed to protect
is now the reason to fly back out there, and the station that hired you still has your name on a
line in its book.

## THE NUMBERS

| What | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Failed escort (`escortee_lost`) | ends in `Mission FAILED` toast, nothing follows | a `salvage_retrieval` contract is live and accepted, same tick |
| Sealed-manifest breach (`clause_broken:cargo_intact`) | ends in `Mission FAILED`, nothing follows | a `cargo_delivery` restitution contract is live and accepted |
| Rescue-priority breach | ends in `Mission FAILED`, nothing follows | a `salvage_retrieval` recovery contract is live and accepted |
| Deliberate quit (`abandonMission`) | plain failure | **unchanged** — plain failure |
| Successor survives save → reload | n/a | yes, with its provenance |
| Same failure twice, same seed | n/a | same successor, same offer id |

Bar B10 ("failure should usually mutate the situation"): from **never** to **3 of the game's failure
reasons**, with the other reasons deliberately left alone.

the new live contract, both asserted in the test.

### Engineering appendix

- **Hypothesis line.** `missions._failMission` ends every failure in `Mission FAILED` and
  `_removeActive`, which breaks the vision sentence "failure should usually mutate the situation";
  routing three physical-residue failure reasons through the existing
  `postAndAcceptAuthoredOffer` seam should replace the dead end with a live successor objective.
- **Files:** `src/systems/missions.js`, new `test/world-reacts-failure-mutates.test.mjs`.
- **Seam:** `_failMission` (:3909) already compiled and boarded a set-piece transition BEFORE
  emitting `mission:failed`. `_mutateInsteadOfFail` runs at that same point, while the failing
  mission is still `active` and before any public failure observer.
- **Mutation table** (`MISSION_MUTATIONS`): `escortee_lost → salvage_retrieval`,
  `clause_broken:cargo_intact → cargo_delivery`, `clause_broken:rescue_priority →
  salvage_retrieval`. Both successor types are existing `MISSION_TYPES` entries
  (`src/data/missions.js:122,155`). Three rows were deliberately dropped: `clause_broken:no_kills`
  and `no_scan` (no physical residue, and the shipped clause suite pins them to settle-and-remove),
  and `heist_failed` (already has its own authored failure→follow-up seam, `_boardHeistRecovery`).
- **Determinism:** the offer id, the salvage commodity and its quantity all come from
  `hash32(missionId, reason, salt)` — never a counter, a clock or an rng draw. Verified: no
  `Math.random` or `Date.now` is reachable from `postAndAcceptAuthoredOffer` / `_acceptPreflight`.
  Test: "the same failure twice mints the same successor offer id".
- **Single writers:** the successor posts with `collateral_cr: 0` and `factionId: null`, so
  `acceptMission` computes zero collateral and zero upfront cost and emits no `economy:chargeCredits`
  intent; `storyBranch` is null so `_maybeSetBranch` is a no-op; nothing preloads cargo. No credits,
  no reputation, no heat move.
- **Control-flow safety:** `postAndAcceptAuthoredOffer` pushes the successor at the END of
  `state.missions.active`, which cannot shift the failing mission's index; and `_removeActive`
  verifies `active[hintIndex].id === missionId` before splicing, falling back to a `findIndex`. The
  failing mission is always the one removed. Mutation cannot chain: successors carry no escortee and
  no clauses, so any later failure of theirs maps to `null` and the chain ends at depth 1.
- **Golden safety:** every added field is a conditional spread, so a failure with no descriptor
  keeps the exact legacy payload shape on the receipt (`missionReceiptFor`), the `mission:failed`
  event, the mission instance and the toast. Verified no 47-A replay tape drives a failure with any
  of the three mutating reasons — `escortee_lost` is emitted only from `missions.js:2903` (the escort
  mission's own escortee-death path); the story encounters fail with `b0_sample_lost`,
  `b1_cargo_lost`, `encounter_failed`, none of which mutate.
- **Save/load:** the mission serializer rest-spreads (`const { targetEntityIds, _escorteeId,
  _escorteeSectorId, _escorteeArrived, ...rest } = a;`) and `deserialize` spreads back, so
  `mutatedFromMissionId` / `mutationTag` round-trip without a whitelist entry. Asserted in the test.
- **Checks run (raw):** `node --test test/world-reacts-failure-mutates.test.mjs` and three
  neighbouring aftermath suites in one run — 28/28 pass. Cases include: the mutated failure still
  fires `mission:failed` exactly once; `abandonMission` does NOT mutate; a refused mutation falls
  back to the ordinary failure with no orphan; mutation fields round-trip the save and an unmutated
  mission never carries them.
- **Adversarial review:** Kimi K3 xhigh (`command-code`), which did not write the code — VERDICT
  ACCEPT, with the `_removeActive` index question answered from the guard itself. I independently
  re-verified the three load-bearing claims (`_removeActive` id check, the serializer rest-spread,
  `escortee_lost`'s only emitter).
- **Golden expected to move: none.**
- **Not my red:** `test/npc-jobs-runtime-wiring.test.mjs` pins the save envelope at v12 while the
  game is at v14; this lane touches no file under `src/save/`.
