## Player-visible result

Live gravity wells now publish bounded distortion records into the existing production
`DistortionField` path. A dedicated six-slot well pool can coexist with the existing weapon-haze
producer; the render graph clears its distortion target once and accumulates every live producer.
Collapsed wells release their slots and the pass sleeps when no producer is live. Reduced-motion
turns well refraction off while reduced-flash leaves it available.

At 1280 px, the authored 190 WU / strength-240 well calibrates to an 11.20 px peak. Radius and
strength are monotone: 95/190/285 WU at strength 240 produce 5.60/11.20/16.80 px, and strengths
120/240/360 at radius 190 produce the same bounded progression. GPU strength is capped at 0.4
(about 17.92 px), preventing a whole-screen wobble.

The effect is intentionally available only through `settings.video.renderGraph === true`; the
bloom/native route does not sample the distortion texture.
