## 2. Authority and collision work

- Physics emits the contact point, face normal, material, and incoming velocity. Reflection
  continues the same projectile body and spends the shared lineage bounce budget.
- Exact point/OBB and swept-circle/OBB contact replaced radius-only contacts for Foundry surfaces;
  rounded corners do not inherit an invisible square collision envelope.
- Rapier and the observer compose entity yaw with the same per-piece OBB extents used by rendering.
- Moving shutters use a fixed-but-scripted `physicsBody.kinematic` lane. They remain fixed Rapier
  bodies, reject impulses and Massline attachments, update spatial membership incrementally, and do
  not invalidate the complete static layer every tick.
- Foundry geometry persists across waves, releases on run/world teardown, and stays off radar while
  remaining collidable. Only the two loose plates advertise Massline interaction.
