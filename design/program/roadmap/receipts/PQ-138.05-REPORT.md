## Honesty gaps

- **The kill is injected.** `combat:damage` + `entity:killed` are emitted onto the bus rather than
  produced by flying and firing. The payload matches `src/systems/combat.js:585` in shape and in the
  `killerId` gate, so this is a faithful input, not a fabricated one — but no gun, physics, AI, or
  law system runs in this proof.
- **The witness is planted.** The harness gives the victim a second hauler co-targeting
  `station_ceres` so `_markRouteDisrupted` has someone to mark. The live 3-hull role mix does not
  guarantee a second hauler bound for that station.
- **The 8 s is decorative.** All five writes land synchronously inside the two emits. `stepWorld`
  only ticks `pirateRumor.update`, which decays heat. `traffic.update`, `economy.update`, and
  `aftermathWrecks.update` never run, so nothing that could relax a price, refill a lane, or expire a
  marker is exercised.
- **`routeDisrupted` has no consumer.** Written at `traffic.js:8589`/`:8592`; a repo-wide search finds
  no reader. Nothing about routing, spawning, or presentation changes because a route was disrupted.
- **The rumor has no consumer.** `state.ui.pirateRumor` is read only inside `pirateRumor.js`;
  `pirateRumor:card` / `pirateRumor:headline` have no listeners; `news:headline` has no production
  subscriber; `rumorReadoutForZone`, `routeDangerFeedbackForZone`, `routeAdjustedTrafficMix`,
  `routeAdjustedEncounterPlan`, and `pirateBaseCandidateForZone` are imported only by tests. The card
  title exists in the localization catalog and nowhere else — intended UI that was never wired.
- **The patch has no consumer.** `station.data.structurePatch` and the `patched` entry on
  `station.data.receipts` are read by nothing in `src/ui` or `src/render`. It is a save-durable
  receipt, not a docked visual scar and not a line on any screen.
- No shipping-camera strip. The campaign forbade headed capture.
- This leaf did not retune leftover 138.00–.04.

   a flag.
2. A surface for the rumor — either a `news:headline` subscriber, or a station/chart screen that
   reads `state.ui.pirateRumor` or `rumorReadoutForZone`.
3. A surface for the patch — a docked screen that reads the station's `patched` receipts, or the
   visual scar the leaf implies.
