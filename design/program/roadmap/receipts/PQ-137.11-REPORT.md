# Engineering appendix

## 1. The residue audit the last cycle asked for — what the 10/min in Helios rope actually was

Ordered before touching the rule, from one instrumented run of `helios_core / massline_rig / 4242`
(all 22 ambient events, `otherId`, thrust-into-other, closing speed, applied ΔV, and the gap to the
previous event's end):

**It was never the rope.** All 22 ambient events had `ropeInitiated: false`, and every one of them
was a contact with a hull **other than** the live tether target (id 310): 343, 345, 341, 373, 321,
330, 324, 372, 376, 340, 366. **The rope cell's residue was contacts with OTHER hulls while
reeling, never the rope.** Nine of the fifteen counted ones opened at 0.42–1.34× cruise closing
speed with the player named as the causal actor on the first receipt.

The gap histogram decided the second half of the fix. Of the 21 gaps between consecutive ambient
events: **none at all inside the old 6-tick bridge**, 6 in 7–30 ticks, 5 in 31–60, 2 in 61–120, 8
beyond. A bridge of 6 was not merging anything; it was splitting one grind into a dozen budgets.

## 2. The spin injector, found

A trap on every off-centre impulse that reaches the player body, over one run of each of the three
worst cells:

| cell | off-centre impulses on the player | source | before → after, in one tick |
|---|---|---|---|
| Helios Core · rope kit | **1** | `applyImpulse` `reason: 'weapon_hit'` | 0 → **−13.484 rad/s** (tick 3712) |
| Lagrange · starter | **1** | `applyImpulse` `reason: 'weapon_hit'` | +3.154 → **−6.186 rad/s** (tick 3984) |
| Cinder Sluice · rope kit | 0 | — | — |

That is the whole story: one weapon hit, applied at the point of impact rather than at the centre of
mass, is an **angular** impulse, and it accounted for 100 % of the player's spin in both cells that
had any. The cell with no such impulse had no spin (2.57 rad/s, all of it commanded). It was not the
tether spring and not the solver — `_applyPlayerStructuralGive` restores the player's rotation to the
no-contact prediction every tick, so the solver can never leave spin behind; what it cannot do is
un-do an impulse that arrived **before** the step.

Then it decays slowly rather than snapping back: ticks 3713–3720 of the Helios run read −13.20,
−12.89, −12.58, −12.28, −11.97, −11.67, −11.37 rad/s. One hit, and the hull is spinning for seconds.

**The fix, at the source.** `SG02DynamicBodyOwner.applyImpulse` and the attachment-spring path both
now refuse to apply an impulse off the centre of mass **to the player**, and only to the player:
`recordTakesOffCentreImpulse(rec)`. Every other hull still takes the torque, so a rock still tumbles
and a hostile still spins when it is hit off-centre. The **linear** half passes through in full —
`test/sg02-player-contact-give.test.mjs` measures the player and an NPC taking the identical impulse
and asserts their speeds agree to 1e-3 while only the NPC spins. This is not drag and not a clamp on
given momentum (both forbidden by the packet); only the torque arm is dropped.

**The safety net.** The player branch of `_applyStructuralGive` restores the prediction wholesale and
returns, so the player was the one hull in the game that skipped `SANE_MAX_YAW_RATE = 6 rad/s`. It no
longer does. The ceiling is applied in `_captureExpectedKinematics`, **before** the pose is
integrated from that rate, so the restored rotation and the restored spin are always the same
motion — a reader can never find a tick whose nose and whose spin disagree. The player's own
commanded yaw peaks near 3 rad/s, so the ceiling never touches steering.

Measured after, on the same three cells: max yaw rate **2.57 / 3.15 / 2.57** rad/s, **0** ticks above
6 anywhere, largest spin injected between steps 0.017–0.021 rad/s (numerical residue).

## 3. The rate: one contact episode gets one budget

`PLAYER_CONTACT_EVENT_BRIDGE_TICKS` **6 → 90**. A re-contact inside 1.5 s continues the SAME event
and draws on its **remaining** budget; past it, a new encounter has begun and gets a fresh one.
Nothing else about the rule moved: the response is still along heading only, still 25 % of the
solver's ask, still capped at 10 % of cruise, still never reverses, and no drag, clamp on given
momentum or transform write was added.

**The number is measured, not chosen to pass.** Across the three worst cells at seed 4242 there are
204 re-contacts with the *same other body*: p50 **14** ticks, p75 **27**, p90 **55**, p95 **88**, and
then a long tail (p99 582) that is plainly a separate encounter. 90 ticks is the p95 of one contact's
own flicker.

Both instruments now **import** that constant from the rule instead of typing it
(`test/fun-measurer.test.mjs` pins the identity). The flight scenario's ±6-tick *quiet-sample*
window is a different job and stayed 6 under its own name, `RECEIPT_QUIET_MARGIN_TICKS`; widening it
to the bridge would have thrown away most of the run and measured the noise floor on almost nothing.

## 4. The attribution hole, closed

Cycle 3's §11 named this first: 22 % of player-knock receipts carried no `causalActorId`, and one
such receipt anywhere in an event forced the whole event to ambient.

**Why the hole exists.** `directContactCausalActorId` names an author only while a contact is
**closing**. The resting ticks that follow a contact are not closing and carry no actor *by design*.
Requiring every tick to re-name the author therefore made every event longer than one tick
unattributable. Measured on the Helios rope cell: of its 22 ambient events, **12 had a named player
author on the first receipt and were vetoed only by later nulls**; 5 had no author on the first
receipt at all; 2 genuinely carried two different actors.

**The rule now.** An event's author is decided at **first contact** and never rewritten: the first
receipt must name it, and no later receipt may name a **different** one. A receipt that names nobody
neither confirms nor vetoes. A hole in the opening receipt, or two actors in one event, still fails
closed to ambient. The same correction was applied to the hostile-ram classifier.

**What each lever did, on its own** (seed 4242, counterfactual over the same receipts):

| Cinder Sluice · rope kit | 3.33 (8.00 as cycle 3 measured it) | 2.00 | **2.00** |
| Lagrange · starter | 3.33 | 2.67 | **2.00** |

Neither lever alone reaches the budget on all three. Nothing was weakened to get there: every case
`test/fun-bench.test.mjs` pins — a dead actor, a stale intent snapshot, mixed actors, a partially
