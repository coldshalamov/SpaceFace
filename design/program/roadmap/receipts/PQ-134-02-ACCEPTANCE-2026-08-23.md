# PQ-134.02 acceptance - the causal VFX grammar, reviewed at play size

The map's "done when" for this leaf is a capture reviewed at play size, not a passing test. A grammar
that reads correctly in a table and is invisible during a fight has not been delivered.

Captured with `scripts/capture-causal-vfx-families.mjs`: a normal New Game boot, flown, then each
family's cue fired through the live presentation path (`presentation:vfxCue`) and photographed at
1440x900. Sixteen frames in `.devshots/causal-vfx/`.

## 1. The eight families render

All eight produce a visible burst in the play frame.

## 2. They are distinguishable at a glance

`bank.png` is a cluster of amber four-point stars and chevrons. `chain.png` is pale ice-white PAIRED
ARCS strung out in a hop sequence. Different colour, different silhouette, different layout, and
different arrangement - they do not read as the same effect tinted differently, which is the failure
this leaf exists to prevent.

## 3. No two families rely on colour alone - verified over all 28 pairs

Computed directly from the grammar rather than taken from the implementer's table:

- **28 pairs checked. Minimum non-colour differences on any pair: 4.**
- The weakest pair is direct vs bank, and it still differs by silhouette (radial-star vs
  bounce-chevron), motion (fast-radial-short vs two-axis-bounce), layout (radial vs chevron) and
  primitive mix (6/2/4 vs 4/2/2).
- The three pairs that SHARE a hue, where colour cannot help at all, carry the most separation:
  direct/tether 5 differences, collision/reaction 5, terrain/field 6.

## 4. Reduced motion still communicates the family

`reduced-bank.png` keeps bank's amber and its chevron identity with fewer, simpler primitives - the
large four-point stars are gone, the chevrons remain. The family survives; the motion does not.

## Two false alarms this capture produced, recorded so they are not re-derived

**An empty sky is not a broken grammar.** The first pass photographed nothing and looked exactly like
the feature failing. Two causes, both in the capture:

1. A cue only reaches the structural pool if it carries `kind: 'vfx.arcade_structural'`. Without it
   the handler takes the generic particle path and no burst spawns.
2. Reduced motion is a SETTING the renderer reads (`state.settings.video.motionReduce`), not a CSS
   class. Toggling `html.sf-reduce-motion` changes nothing in the render owner.

Both would have been reported as defects by anyone trusting the first frame.

## Still owed, and it is not this leaf

Combat does not stamp causal tags on every kill or hit, so in ordinary play chain, field and reaction
only appear when the receipt already carries the flag. The grammar is complete; what feeds it is not.
That needs a seam in `src/combat`.
