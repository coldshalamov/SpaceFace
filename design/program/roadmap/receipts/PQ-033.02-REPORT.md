DONE  PQ-033.02 — The named minimum-spec GPU now has written floors, a two-hour public-route
soak on each host proves the game holds them, and the save/load round-trip is exercised end
to end at scale.

WHAT I FOUND     The release matrix named a target — 60 fps median, ≤1 gameplay hitch per
minute over 50 ms, bounded transition stalls, 8 GB footprint — but nothing measured it: no
spec named the GPU, no harness ran the loop, and no check recomputed the floors from raw
evidence. The soak then exposed real defects the floors exist to catch: cold-cache first-draw
stalls (multi-second `bloomScene` bricks while authored textures/programs uploaded inside
presented frames), a shadow-depth admission path that could never work (a standalone
`shadowMap.render` with no live render state), an autopilot limit cycle that orbited the dock
berth at ~42 WU/s forever, and a save/load path that grew a duplicate generation of persistent
actors across cycles.

WHAT I CHANGED   `build/min-spec.json` names the floor hardware (Intel Core Ultra 7 155U
integrated graphics, Iris Xe class or better) and the floors themselves; `minSpecFloors.mjs`
recomputes every floor from raw soak evidence and `check:pq033:min-spec` fails closed on
missing or forged evidence. The browser and Electron soaks drive the public route — new game,
authored flight, public input, map waypoint, undock, F5 save, F9 load, economy restore,
redock, market trade — for ≥200 cycles and ≥120 minutes each, hitch-classified by the game's
own attribution (external-scheduling noise is recorded but excluded from the gameplay floor).

The fixes the soak earned:
- The convoy job bag is bounded by the record ledger. `_captureSectorDurableRecords` refuses a
  first-time convoy record once 24 are alive in the sector and drops the hull's phantom
  `worldRecordId` — but the hull's npcJobs entry, keyed on that record id, stayed in
  `state.npcJobs.byId` forever. `npcJobsRuntime.deserialize()` restores every job VIRTUAL and
  `_tryRelink()` binds it by worldRecordId against a rematerialized hull, so a job whose record was
  never persisted can never re-link: the bag grew ~+4 jobs and ~+4 KB per save/load roundtrip until
  localStorage's 5 MB quota refused the quick save at soak cycle 42. The refusal now releases the job
  in the same breath as the phantom id; the hull itself stays live ambience under the ordinary
  traffic stepper (`traffic.js` keeps npcJobsRuntime as the movement writer only while
  `data.jobId` exists).
- GPU admission is sealed against first-draw work: shader compilation and texture/geometry
  residency are chained behind one refcounted pending latch per subject; bloom hides
  never-compiled drawables and routes them through admission instead of letting them link
  inside a presented frame; shadow-depth variants warm via a real render of a staged
  casters+key-light scene into a scratch target.
- The dock-berth autopilot carries a sustained envelope brake: inside the berth stage with
  speed above the gate, counter-thrust holds until the hull is under the legal docking speed.
- Berth-stage autopilot guidance holds the manifest corridor lane: generic obstacle avoidance
  inside it steered the hull across a spar/ring primitive and the compound proxy expelled the
  ship back out of the dock envelope at ~46 WU/s (`dock:range` flickered true then false).
- The lane lock now requires geometric lane membership — a hull knocked off the corridor wedge
  keeps the mouth aim and exits through the ring gap instead of grinding structure — and
  lane-locked runs pace behind traffic squarely ahead instead of ramming it.
- A contact-held ship parked just outside the dock gate no longer waits forever for the berth:
  the capture lane's heading gate exempted only "nearly stationary" hulls under 8 wu/s, and a
  hull wedged on the station proxy reads a residual ~8 wu/s of solver noise — so the assist
  refused to pull it in and the dock prompt never came (soak cycle 503). The exemption now
  covers anything at or below the authored berth dock speed; a ship that slow inside the
  capture lane is docking traffic by definition.
- Save/load restores one generation of persistent actors; repeated cycles no longer duplicate
  entities or grow quota.
- Convoy world-records are bounded per sector at capture (the rematerialize/top-up ratchet
  minted ~8 fresh convoy identities per round trip — ~1 MB of save growth by cycle 94), and a
  recovery-backup write that hits the storage quota now sacrifices the backup instead of
  aborting the save: a lost backup beats a lost save.
- The soak driver itself hardened against observed state-dependent traps: search rows that only
  name-match 'Helios Station' include courseDisabled rumor/goal targets that resolve to no
  course (the click emits nothing — the prior cycle-12/22 failure signature); a restore that
  lands inside the dock envelope makes the station's primary action 'RETURN TO SHIP', not
  'Set Waypoint' — and the re-approach loop now nudges the throttle for in-envelope stalls
  instead of re-plotting a course that cannot exist; and a berth expulsion or early arrival
  still gets the bounded re-approach loop like a player would fly.

WHAT YOU WILL FEEL   First flight after a cold boot no longer bricks mid-frame while shaders
link and textures upload. Autopilot actually parks at the dock instead of orbiting it
forever. Long sessions stay stable — saves don't fatten and the world doesn't accumulate
ghost actors.
