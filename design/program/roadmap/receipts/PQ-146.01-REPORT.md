## What changed (exact paths)

- `src/systems/stuntCombo.js` (new, pure, single writer of combo/score math): chain window
  300 ticks, bank-after-quiet 360 ticks, rarity 1x/1.5x/2x/3x, mass up to 2x, chain
  +0.25/step capped at 4x, gun kill 60 flat, Pulse kill 25 flat. Deterministic: finite-number
  coercion and clamps only, no RNG, no wall clock.
- `src/systems/stuntGrammar.js` (extended, not replaced): owns `state.stunts.combo`, feeds it
  from the same receipts it already observes, resets on `run:started`/`game:started`, banks on
  quiet per update. `stunt:trickDetected` emission unchanged, so `moment:holyShit` is unregressed.
  No registry change — no fight with `src/core/registry.js`.
- `src/ui/screens/crucible.js` (parallel read only): `stuntComboFor`/`comboLead`/`comboRows`/
  `comboTrickLines` are DOM-free and exported for tests; the results plate appends one
  `Stunt combo` band when the snapshot exists, reusing existing band CSS (no kit/hud edits).
- `test/stunt-combo.test.mjs` (new): pure-scoring units plus the seeded physics-vs-gun print.
