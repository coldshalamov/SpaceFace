## Why another quota or door pass will not close it

The kits that finish 15 kills in ~23 s need roughly twice as much work to reach 45 s. The kits that already take ~52 s cannot take twice as much work without crossing 90 s, dying, or stalling once the opening farm is over. Thinning and extra doors hit the slow kits first. Hit points were not, and must not be, the lever.
