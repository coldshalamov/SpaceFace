## RESULT: DONE — verdict: the capital is never killed. The "~12 s resolution" is the scripted PILOT's death, mislabeled by the duel-audit instrument. Authored gank, not a stat bug.

## The finding

The combat-variety vertical's audit printed
`dreadnought_boss:2v2 | ttk 12.25 s | fire/min 3669 | dmg taken 337` and read it as "the capital
dies to something other than pilot DPS in wing fights". Reproducing the cell on the real production
runtime (same boot law, same seeds, same scripted pilot as
`scripts/lib/bench/crucibleBench.mjs`) shows the opposite:

1. **The cell resolves by PILOT DEATH.** `simulateCrucibleDuel` pushes the pilot into
   `duel.deaths` (its `player:death` handler) and then computes
   `ttkSeconds = duel.deaths.length ? resolveTick/60 : null` — so a pilot death prints as a
   capital "ttk". In this cell the pilot always dies first and no capital ever emits
   `entity:killed`.
2. **Both capitals end the cell at FULL hull and armor, every seed.** They are never killed by
   anything: not the ally, not collisions, not friendly fire, not a subsystem chain. The audit's
   premise ("the capital dies to non-pilot DPS") does not survive contact with the attribution.
3. **The pilot death is authored gank arithmetic.** A tier-0 starter Kestrel (140 hull + 55 shield,
   +7/s regen, one 44 dps tier-1 pulse laser) against two level-8-scaled Iron Maws (levelRange
   [10,15] clamped to 8 by `duelLevelFor`; `scaleCombatant` ×1.84 → 11,040 hull / 4,048 armor /
   4,416 shield each; 30,928 combined EHP) mounting 14 turrets per hull (2 torpedo, 2 heavy beam,
   6 heavy autocannon, 4 flak/PD; ~2,080 paper dps for the pair). Only ~23–28 dps actually lands
   through range, arcs, and misses — the broadside is, if anything, under-delivering against paper.
   The armor gate is intact (armor never scratched: 4,048/4,048 at cell end) and no multiplier is
   doubled (applied tracks raw through shield→flat→armor→hull on every packet).

## Decision: authored (with one instrument defect named, outside this leaf's write set)

- **No combat-stat bug.** `src/combat/runtime.js` (combat catalog/state) and `src/data/missions.js`
  (CAPITAL_BOSS stat/mission blocks) were read end to end; both are net-unchanged. The damage
  router's layering (`src/combat/damage.js`) behaves exactly as authored on every attributed packet.
- **The pilot death is authored gank**: a starter hull has no business surviving two capitals, and
  the mission briefs themselves say "Guns are the slow way" — the capital is designed as a
  mass/throw gate, not a DPS check.
- **The instrument defect** (named, not fixed — `scripts/lib/bench/crucibleBench.mjs` is outside
  this leaf's write set): `ttkSeconds` counts ANY death because `player:death` pushes the pilot
  into `deaths`. Suggested follow-up for the bench lane: compute `ttkSeconds` from enemy-side
  deaths only and record `pilotDeathSeconds`/`resolver` separately; the vertical receipt's
  known-gap line then becomes "pilot dies in ~12 s to authored broadside" instead of a capital
  mystery.
