## Seed 15903 measurement

`node --test test/pq-159-03-photo-mode.test.mjs` prints:

```text
SEED=15903 pausePhoto=1 flags=hideHud,freeCamera,filtersOff exposure=1
SEED=15903 capture=spaceface-store-2026-09-11T10-56-36.png via=electron+download png=1
```

Pause mount lists the Photo action. `enterPhotoMode` sets `hideHud`, `freeCamera`, `filters: false`
(bloom forced off) and exposure 1, then `exitPhotoMode` restores bloom. `capturePhotoPng` returns
a `data:image/png` payload; `writePhotoCapture` uses Electron `savePhoto` when present and a
download anchor otherwise. Filenames are `spaceface-store-*.png`.
