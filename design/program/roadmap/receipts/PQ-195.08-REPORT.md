## Status: INTEGRATED

The Third Shift assembly no longer leaves the launcher as a free ballistic
body. A yard tug — a real `ship_hawser` flight hull with ordinary derived
stats and `data.intent` executed by flightV3 — hauls the caged SP-07 out of
the same launch mouth on the same off-line heading, held by the ordinary
attachment service through the new authored `attachment_transport_clamp`.
Release happens three honest ways and only ever removes the joint: the tug's
voluntary cut when it crosses the authored `routeReleaseWu` progress line,
the clamp bolt shot off (the hittable `subsystem_transport_clamp` on the
carrier's aft hull with `breakOwnedAttachments`), or the carrier lost
(`breakOrphans`). The released body is the same entity with the same schedule
custody and keeps whatever momentum it already had — no cinematic impulse,
ever.

## Implementation notes

- `heistFacilities._launchScheduledCapsule` branches on the variant: the
  historical free launch is bit-identical through the extracted
  `_payloadSpawnSpec`; the Third Shift calls `_spawnCarrierCagedLoad`, which
  spawns the tug ahead of the same mouth position and the load behind it at
  authored standoff, both already at cruise velocity.
- The joint binds ONE TICK after spawn: Rapier registers new physics bodies
  on the next step, so same-tick `attachments.create` was rejected
  (`physics_create_rejected`) and the launch retried forever. `_stepCarrier`
  binds the clamp once records exist (`carrierPendingClamp`/`carrierClampAttempts`
  bound the retry); a real refusal — missing socket, limit, dead endpoint —
  ends the cage honestly and the load continues free.
- Release and despawn are PROGRESS CROSSINGS, not arrivals: an early
  point-anchor design made the 140 WU/s tug overshoot and orbit, never
  settling. The tug now steers to a far point down the lane (`_driveCarrier`
  writes throttle = cruise/maxSpeed into `data.intent`; flightV3 owns all
  motion) and lets go when `dot(pos − launchPos, heading) ≥ routeReleaseWu`.
- The carrier is a bounded transient: after release it keeps the lane and is
  removed at `departureWu`. Sector exit removes it in `_dematerializeSector`
  (suspension had already snapshotted the load); `breakOrphans` covers any
  mid-run loss with no custom cleanup.
- If the attachment service is absent entirely (headless sims without
  combat), the launch degrades to the historical free arc — the load still
  physically exists; nothing fabricates a release.
- New authored data, all in `src/data/`: `BREAKAWAY_CARRIER` (hawser hull,
  55 WU/s cruise, 8 WU standoff, 1200/2400 WU route geometry),
  `attachment_transport_clamp`, `subsystem_transport_clamp`,
  `combat_profile_heist_carrier`, cue `combat.subsystem.clamp.disabled`.
- No mission-runtime changes were needed: the clamped body is the run's
  `capsuleEntityId` from launch, pursuit/capture/fence/recovery all read the
  same physical body, and `tether:attached`/`tether:broken` never route to
  the player latch/unlatch handlers.
