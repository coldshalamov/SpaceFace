## Better than claimed: it paints without a click

The receipt says "select Iron Ore". The player does not have to. `renderStage` falls back to
`rows[0]` when nothing is selected, Iron Ore is the FIRST entry in `COMMODITIES`, and
`input.js` sets `activeStationTab = 'market'` on dock. So the chain line and the this-dock line
are on screen the instant the player docks at Ceres, with no input at all. The done-when's
"after one inspector screen" bar is cleared with room to spare — at Ceres.

The role words also survive the trip to a place name: the ore-buying stations are literally
called Ceres Refinery, Expanse Refinery, Cut Refinery, Tethys Trade Hub, Dione Exchange. A
player told "Refinery and Trade Hub" can find those on the chart by name.

## The miss: the line is false at most docks

`presentSupplyChain` has four branches. The shipped test only ever feeds it two — `refinery`
(consume) and `mining` (produce), the two station types where the sentence happens to be true.
It never fed the law a `research`, `fab`, `military`, or `blackmarket` dock. Those four types
are 18 of the 32 stations in `sectors.js`.

At those docks the inspector prints **"This dock does not buy it."** The live economy disagrees,
on purpose, in a comment written directly above the code that does it:

```text
economy.js ensureMarket():
  "Every legal commodity trades at every station -- role only drives price/stock target,
   not availability. A 'none'-role commodity (e.g. iron ore at a military station) still
   gets an entry so the player can always sell what they mined/bought."
```

Booting the real economy and asking it for a sell quote of 5 Iron Ore:

```text
station              type        listed  role     sell quote   line the inspector prints
Research Veil        research    yes     none     ok, 136 cr   "This dock does not buy it."
Forge Foundry        fab         yes     none     ok, 135 cr   "This dock does not buy it."
Coalition HQ         military    yes     none     ok, 133 cr   "This dock does not buy it."
Ceres Refinery       refinery    yes     consume  ok, 175 cr   "This dock buys it."
```

There is no role gate on the sell path — `economy.js` execute() rejects only mission-locked
cargo, a missing listing, and a bad quote. The sale goes through. The screen that told the
player it would not is the same screen whose Sell tab shows "station pays".

The true information is sitting right there in those numbers: a refinery pays about 30% more
than a research post for the same ore. "This dock does not buy it." throws that away and
replaces it with a falsehood. A player who believes the line hauls ore past a dock that would
have paid.
