## Rack rules as implemented

- `state.bombs` = `{ selectedId, cooldownUntil, cooldowns, rack: { sockets, cells[] }, stock }`.
  A cell is `{ id, count }` or `null`; a cell keeps its `id` at `count: 0` — a dry socket
  remembers its fit so dock-side restock refills instead of re-deciding the loadout.
- Sockets: `BOMB_RACK.socketsBase = 2`, `socketsMax = 3`. The third socket is a one-time yard
  weld (`socketUpgradeCr = 4500`), arrives empty, fitted at an outfitting berth only.
- `cycleBomb` walks loaded cells only (catalogue order is the shop listing, never the cycle
  order); a dry rack answers the verb with one toast per press edge and drops nothing.
- `dropBomb` consumes one unit from the loaded cell; when a cell runs dry the selection
  auto-advances to the next loaded socket (`bombs:cycle` with `reason: 'depleted'`).
- `selectedId` is invariant-locked to a loaded cell (or `null` on a dry rack) — the HUD socket
  and the verb can never point at ordnance that is not on the rack.
- All rack writes are dock-side verbs: the `ui:*` intents are gated by
  `shipworksStationAccess(state).outfit` — the same berth authority as the module verbs.
  Direct methods stay open to internal callers (sandbox, rewards, tests), matching the
  `ships.fitModule` contract.

## Starter kit + rationale

`BOMB_STARTER_KIT = ['bomb_frag', 'bomb_concussion']` — the two cheapest, most legible verbs:
frag is the kill verb (teaches what the rack is for), concussion is the shove verb (teaches
that ordnance is a physics tool, not a damage tier). One full magazine each: a fresh save
leaves the yard armed but not solved. The same kit is the additive default for pre-rack saves.

Service prices (`BOMB_RACK`): restock fee **40 cr** flat per dock visit (a handling charge, not
per-unit — the units themselves were already paid for), socket weld **4500 cr** once,
sell-back at **0.5×** catalogue. Anchors: ammo service runs 12 cr/unit, hull wash 75 cr — a
bomb unit at 110–380 is a considered purchase, and the 40 cr rearm keeps a dry rack from
being a punishment loop.

## Resupply rule

Combat resupply is: dock at an outfitting berth → buy units into hangar stock →
`ui:restockBombRack` refills every fitted magazine for the 40 cr yard fee. There is no
mid-flight reload: a rack run dry in the field stays dry until the next berth.

## Economy-seam proof

The bombs system never writes `state.player.credits`. Every credit move is an emit on the
economy owner's existing seam: `economy:chargeCredits` (`ordnance:<id>` buys,
`service:ordnance_restock`, `service:bomb_rack_socket`) and `economy:grantCredits`
(`ordnance:resell:<id>`). The rack verbs pre-check affordability before emitting because
`chargeCredits` clamps at 0 rather than rejecting — `test/bomb-rack-economy.test.mjs` boots
the real `economy` system and asserts exact post-transaction balances (1000 − 2×goo.price;
broke buy → zero delta, `bombs:denied{reason:'credits'}`).

## Save round-trip evidence

`bombs` is a `saveSnapshotOwned` owner: `serialize()` writes `{v, selectedId, cooldownUntil,
cooldowns, rack:{sockets,cells}, stock}`; `deserialize()` restores it, clamps sockets to
`socketsMax` (overflow cell contents return to hangar stock — shrinking a rack never destroys
paid ordnance), drops bogus payload ids, and re-normalizes selection onto a loaded cell.
A missing/invalid `rack` key (every pre-rack save) applies the starter kit — additive default,
no migration bump (save version stays 14; `SAVE_SCHEMA.md` regenerated: `$.bombs` added).

`save:loaded` is no longer a reset — it only sweeps live bomb entities (`releaseAll`), because
deserialize already restored the bag. The choreography test's old `save:loaded → reset`
assertions were inverted to pin the new contract: selection and cooldowns now survive the
load boundary. `game:new`/`game:newGame` (both aliases fire on the live route) reseat the
starter kit and clear the run clocks.
