ducks under weight, and visual events have audio cues with captions. Seed 15805.

open void used the same dry bus. Some visual hits had no accessibility audio.

captions.

broadside ducks the score; a scout kiss barely does. Muzzle, explosion, shield, dock, boost, and
scan flashes also speak and caption.
