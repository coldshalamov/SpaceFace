# PQ-029.00 — Tractor head as a throw toy

## What the player can do now

A Hitch fitted with the tractor head can grab a cargo pod, a drone, a debris chunk, or a
light hull, winch the line tight to swing it up to speed, and cut on the swing to throw it.
A winched throw on a light pod leaves at about 123 wu/s against a 95 wu/s cruise — roughly
a 29% faster throw than the ship itself can fly — and every throw ends in a rated release
(clean or better on a loaded swing), never a silent cut.

## How to learn it

The drill `scenario.tractor-throw-drill` (data only, no Range screen change) runs three
beats inside 60 seconds: grab the pod, winch tight and swing, cut through the gate for a
rated release.
