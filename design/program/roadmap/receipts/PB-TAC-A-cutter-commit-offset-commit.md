## Gap, in player terms (before)

When the corsair blade (tether-control raider) finished its attach spool, your rope broke whenever
the blade was within 180 wu of **you** — no matter where the rope actually was, and nothing you did
with the line's angle could beat it (`specialistCounterplay.js` old cut branch: phase + player-range
+ cooldown were the only gates; the live rope was re-read every decision tick, so the cut tracked
perfectly until contact). Separately, nothing prevented a Quiet Ghost's field collapse from landing
in the same instant as the blade's cut, and no authored encounter ever put the two specialists in one
fight, so the "spend the rope or save it" decision was never taught.

## Mechanism (after)

**SF-046 — committed pass** (`src/ai/specialistCounterplay.js`, cut_line branch; plan tunables in
`src/ai/specialistPlans.js` tether_cutter row: `commitTicks: 45, sweepToleranceRad: 0.6,
missRecoveryTicks: 120`):

- **Commit at the telegraph** (`spool_cue`): the blade snapshots the rope's geometry once — anchor
  point, bearing at the anchor, attachment id, deadline = tick + 45 (spans the doctrine's own clock:
  spool 30t + attach window 15t, `combatDoctrine.js:32,55`). The pass never re-targets after this.
- **Freshness re-validated every decision tick** against the live rope: committed attachment gone →
  miss; anchor moved > 40 wu → miss; the line's angle at its anchor swept > 0.6 rad → **miss** —
  that is the player's counter (swing the rope; normal handling stays under tolerance, e.g. a
  300 wu rope crossed laterally at 130 wu/s accumulates ~0.16 rad across the whole window).
- **Miss → recovery**: 120 t (2 s) lockout stored on `data._cutterRecoverUntil`, also resolved when
  the window closes without a landing (doctrine egress) — the blade is exposed while it recovers.
- **Landing = real segment contact**: only inside `attach_window`, not on the commit tick itself,
  and only when the authored cut envelope (`cutRangeWu` 180) actually reaches the **live rope
  segment** (`pointSegmentDistance`), then through the existing `breakAttachment(..., 'specialist_cut')`
  authority path. No teleporting, no freezing the victim; the doctrine's own approach (APPROACH_SOCKET,
  preferredRange 90) still flies the hull.
- Success keeps the existing 90t cooldown. Dropping the rope mid-pass **cancels** without penalty
  (a cost already paid); re-anchoring elsewhere mid-pass is a miss.

**SF-052 — offset-commit composition**:

- Per-state commit ledger (`WeakMap`, tick-advanced — no rng, no wall time): while one specialist's
  pass is committed the other's verb defers (`commitLockHeld`), and each landed verb reserves a
  breather (cutter: until its pass deadline; disruptor: 60 t after the collapse). The pressures
  alternate and one counter is always available — never two unavoidable attacks in one instant.
- Authored composition: `src/data/encounters/359-tether-ghost-pincer.js` (order 359,
  `tether_ghost_pincer`): cutter anchor (`tether_control_raider`) + **guaranteed** `quiet_ghost` in
  the light pool, `minSeparation: 420` (different readable bearings), trigger
  `player_signature_massline_use` (springs while the rope is live), pressureCost 64 in the director
  budget (cutter alone costs 54–60), tier-3 gates like the rest of the 33x family.
