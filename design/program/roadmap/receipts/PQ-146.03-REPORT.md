# PQ-146.03 — The moment detector

When the player pulls off something genuinely spectacular — a wrecking-ball kill, a
clothesline, a rock discovery, a collateral chain, a well golf, a tow kill — the game now
feels it: a beat of slow motion, a camera punch, and a stinger cue, all fired from one
rated event on the bus that the clip recorder can also consume. Ordinary flying, bumps,
and clean passes never trigger it.

The event name is `moment:holyShit`. It carries the trick id, rarity, the rated score
with its rarity/momentum/collateral breakdown, the actors, and the sim tick. The rating
is rarity x momentum x collateral with a threshold of 6: legendary moments always fire,
rare and heavy uncommon chains fire, and lone common tricks (razor releases, near misses,
snap catches) never do, no matter how clean. Detection reads physics receipts only,
through the existing `stunt:trickDetected` stream — never button presses.

The slow-mo is a brief separate pulse that yields to every other slow-mo source, and it
never touches the player's held bullet-time meter: no drain, no engage cost. The feel
answer is a short hit-stop kiss, a score-scaled camera punch, and a small shake. No score
text ever pops in adventure flight; tricks still belong to the ledger and titles.

clothesline 12.53, rock discovery 7.47, collateral 10.44, well golf 65.63, tow kill 7.00),
