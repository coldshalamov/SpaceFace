## The three verbs

1. **Swing** (after tether): latch the loose rock, winch to <=60 wu, release, and put it through
   the derelict at >=15 wu/s. Payoff is physical: two salvage pickups shake loose at the wreck.
   A whip-impact receipt (`tether:whipImpact` rock-into-derelict) completes immediately.
2. **Shove** (after burst): put the scout (mass 16, inert, unarmed, invulnerable) into the
   asteroid at >=12 wu/s — the starter gun's 84-momentum hit walks it there at ~5 wu/s a hit,
   honest since the PQ-137.05 force table. Completion emits the real `combat:shove` the
   telemetry funnel already listens for. Scout drift (5 wu/s) can never fake it.
3. **Grab-and-run** (after disengage): latch the escape pod and carry it inside the beacon at
   running speed (>=10 wu/s). Latches held before the verb opens are adopted, never stranded.

Fails (rock/pod destroyed, scout escaped past 1500 wu, pilot down, body lost) record
`rescue:beat {result: fail}` with the beat named, restage that beat's body, re-speak the verb
once, and retry. A kill before its beat only restages the body — it never starts a beat early.
