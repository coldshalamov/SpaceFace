## Player-visible result

After a witnessed civilian kill creates a wreck or survivor pod, a response group with at least two
live patrols now makes a stable visible choice. The nearest patrol holds over the victim anchor in a
defensive loiter while the remaining responders keep chasing the attacker. Motion alone does not
swap roles, repeated wreck or pod signals do not restart the order, and a real membership change can
produce one deterministic reassignment.

## Honest bar result

The production real-path scenario met B10a on seeds **4242** and **8008**: the split appeared in
**1.2 s** against the **10 s** limit, with three responders, one holder, two chasers, and exactly one
`law:witnessChoice` event. The silent control opened no incident and made no choice. This establishes
the runtime law; it does not substitute for the remaining headed capture of the visible maneuver.

number, not a picture.

## The driver the 2026-09-07 note said was owed now exists

`scripts/capture-witness-choice.mjs` is the adventure-route driver the promotion review asked for. It
drives the **default route** — public New Game with **4242 typed into the New Game screen's own
"Universe seed" field**, into flight, shipping renderer, default quality, HUD not photographed —
lets the sector settle 40 s so the station's patrol is on its beat, spawns **one** civilian hauler

`clausePatrolChoice` does, and kills it with the production payloads (`combat:damage` with

allows and beyond, and every frame records each dispatched responder's role, activity kind, distance
to the wreck, distance to the player and speed — so the words below can be checked.

The frames were looked at and deleted.

## The law fires. Measured on the live route, seed 4242

- `law:incidentOpened` and `law:dispatchStarted` both fire.
- **Exactly one `law:witnessChoice`**, at **+1.63 s** after the kill, against the bar's 10 s.
- Three responders, split one/two: **308 → `witnessRole: 'hold'`, activity `loiter`**; **344 and
  346 → `witnessRole: 'chase'`, activity `attack_run`**.
- The holder converges on the anchor: 84 WU from the wreck at +1.6 s, 52 WU at +9.1 s, 27 WU at
  +22.7 s, decelerating to 3–8 WU/s as it arrives.
- The wreck keeps the victim's momentum and drifts (145 WU from the player at +0.3 s, 193 WU at
  +22.7 s), which is `.03` working.

That is B10a's *number* met on the adventure route, not only in the bench.

## And at the shipping camera you cannot see it

| What the bar asks to see | What the frames show |
|---|---|
| one responder **holds** at the wreck | the holder is on screen in every frame — but the **wreck it is holding at is 145–193 WU away from the player and drifting further**, so what a viewer sees is one small patrol pottering in empty space, not a ship standing over a body |
| one responder **pursues** the attacker | **never in frame, at any moment of a 23-second capture.** The two chasers are **2043 WU and 1955 WU** from the wreck when the choice is made and are still **947 WU and 894 WU** out at +22.7 s |

**THE EXACT REMAINING DEFECT.** The response group that the law splits is not a group that was
anywhere near the incident. Helios's reference pocket holds **one** lawful hull (measured: nearest
lawful hull 82 WU from the victim, and it is the only one in the sector at that moment); the other
two responders are recruited from ~2 km away and close at 41–65 WU/s, so they need roughly forty
seconds to arrive. One ship cannot both hold and leave, so no frame can ever contain the choice. The
split is therefore true as bookkeeping and invisible as motion.

The smallest honest fix is not in this leaf's own listener: the choice has to be made by hulls that
were **inside the incident's own `RESPONSE_CLEARANCE` (320 WU) when it opened**, and Helios's door
has to have two of them on the same beat for there to be a choice to watch. Constraining
`_respondersFor` to the co-located set without that second patrol would turn B10a from
"invisible" to "unmet" — an honest reading, but it converts a visible-motion problem into a
sector-population problem that belongs with traffic/`PQ-143`, and it would move the bench numbers
this receipt is built on. It was therefore **measured and reported, not quietly changed**:
`src/systems/lawSecurity.js` was not edited in this session (it also carries ~916 lines of another
lane's uncommitted WANTED-net work, which is preserved untouched).
