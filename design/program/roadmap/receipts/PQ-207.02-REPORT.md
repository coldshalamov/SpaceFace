## Status: DONE — the bark layer was never silent; the census name was wrong

The compass defect read "`bark:shown`: 0 events in 10 hours (every session), while comms popups run
~50/hour" and concluded the ambient layer never reached a live session. The caveat under it was
already honest — "silence here is 'no such event fired'" — and that is the whole answer: **no
emitter of `bark:shown` exists anywhere in the tree.** The zero measured an event name, not the
layer.

The live chain, verified end to end:

1. `barkDirector._speak` submits `{ channel: 'bark', … }` to `helpers.voice.say`
   (`src/systems/barkDirector.js:295`). On admission it emits **`barkDirector:voice`** — the receipt
   the audio bus listens to (`src/audio/audioSystem.js:1640` → `_onBarkVoice`: comms key click,
   faction/situation-resolved voice recipe, squelch tail, presentation caption).
2. The one-voice arbiter takes the entry onto the floor and emits **`voice:surface{channel:'bark'}`**
   + a `toast` (`src/ui/voiceArbiter.js:364,413`) — the actual "shown" seam. `comms:popup` is a
   parallel caption path shared with missions/AI/factionPresence, which is why it runs ~50/h while
   saying nothing about barks.
3. The Band is a separate opt-in layer: the HUD tuner chip (`src/ui/bandHud.js`) and `Shift+O`
   (`src/ui/input.js:298`) emit `band:cycle`; `bandRadio` then speaks `voice:surface{channel:'band'}`
   lines. Nothing automated ever presses it.

`surfaced` can exceed `admitted` because other systems also submit `channel:'bark'`
(aceMemory, encounterDirector, encounterScripts, bountyHunt, pirateDisengage) — surfaced counts
every voice the player heard, which is the question the census was asking. It counts floor
presentations, not distinct lines: a preempted-and-repromoted entry surfaces again.

What stands after correction:

- **Barks are live — 3 of 3 measurable sessions.** Every session whose harness registered
  `barkDirector`/`voiceArbiter` measured dozens of surfaced barks.
- **The three silent sessions cannot speak to the question.** `runMetadata.systems` in
  prospector-s4242, prospector-s8008, and improviser-s8008 lists no `barkDirector`/`voiceArbiter` —
  they ran a harness that predates the bark layer, so their zero is coverage, not silence.
- **The radio was never switched on.** `band:tune`/`band:cycle` = 0 in every session. Reachable on
  the live route (Shift+O, HUD chip); never invoked by any scripted pilot. Whether a scripted pilot
  *should* exercise the tuner is a coverage question for `.03`'s readback, not a defect this leaf
  owns.
- Named-captain content: not separately re-verified — history barks need aceMemory history that
  sparse sessions never build. The census correction stands regardless.
