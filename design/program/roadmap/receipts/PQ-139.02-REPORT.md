# PQ-139.02 — Death is sized to mass and cause

Lane IMPACT · fun-convergence campaign · 2026-09-04

## WHAT I FOUND

Everything died the same way.

The code that plays a death picked between exactly two explosions: "capital" if the thing looked
like a capital ship, and otherwise "ordinary". It never asked how big the ship was. A darting scout
and a loaded hauler produced the identical explosion, for the identical length of time, at the
identical size.

The parts to do better were already sitting there unused. The explosion system already knew three
different deaths — a short one (0.82 s, five stages), a normal one (1.42 s, six stages) and a
catastrophic one (3.4 s, eight stages, including the hull actually coming apart). Two of the three
were being asked for; the small one was never used at all.

## WHAT I CHANGED

**A death now reads the ship's mass and picks the death that fits it.**

| the ship | its death | how big |
|---|---|---|
| light — a scout, mass under 30 | the short one: 0.82 s, five stages, no internal blowout | 0.85x the hull |
| medium — mass 30 to 79 | the normal one: 1.42 s, six stages | 1.00x |
| heavy — a hauler, mass 80 to 199 | the normal one, but visibly bigger | 1.45x |
| capital — mass 200 up | the catastrophic one: 3.4 s, eight stages, the hull breaks apart | 1.90x |

Anything that already counted as a capital death stays a capital death, whatever its mass says — the
mass ladder can promote a death but it can never demote one.

And the death now carries **what killed it** down the same single path as its size, so a ship gutted
by gunfire and a ship that flew into a rock resolve their look from one place instead of two that
can drift apart.

## WHAT YOU WILL FEEL

Killing a scout is now a quick, sharp pop and you are already onto the next one. Killing something
big is an event you wait through, and it comes apart rather than just flashing. You will be able to
tell what you just killed with the sound off and the HUD hidden.

## THE NUMBERS

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| explosion kinds a death could pick | 2 (`capital`, `ordinary`) | **4 tiers over 3 pool classes** |
| death duration, scout | 1.42 s | **0.82 s** |
| death duration, capital | 3.4 s | 3.4 s (unchanged) |
| explosion size, scout vs hauler on the same hull radius | identical | **0.85x vs 1.45x — a 1.7x spread** |
| stages in a scout's death | 6 | **5** (no internal blowout — it has no interior to blow) |
| stages in a capital's death | 8 | 8 (unchanged) |
| does mass affect the death at all | **no** | yes |

## THE FRAMES

Not yet graded on camera. The unit evidence is in `test/vfx-death-sizing.test.mjs`: two hulls with
the *same radius* and different masses produce different pool classes, different radii and different
causes, asserted against the live entity rather than the event payload. The headed three-death
capture (light/gun, heavy/gun, light/terrain) is the next step and is what closes this leaf's
done-when.

## HONEST NOTE ON WHAT THIS LEAF DID AND DID NOT DO

The adversarial reviewer caught the worker's report overstating itself, and it was right. **The
"and cause" half was already in the engine** before this leaf: the explosion pool already ran
different stages for a terrain death, a collision shear and a kinetic tear, and the death event
already carried its cause. What this leaf actually added is the **mass ladder** — and then made the
cause resolve in the same one place as the size, so there is a single answer instead of two.

## Engineering appendix

**Hypothesis.** `vfx._onKilled` picks `capital` or `ordinary` without reading the victim's mass, so
every non-capital death runs the identical 1.42 s six-phase `ordinary` schedule at the victim's raw
radius; resolving the victim's live mass onto the three pool classes the effect system already
schedules should give a light death a shorter, fewer-phase, smaller explosion and a heavy one a
visibly larger explosion at the same hull radius.

**Files changed**

- `src/render/vfx.js` — pure exported `resolveDeathPresentationClass(victim)` and
  `scaleDeathExplosionRadius(radius, tier)`; frozen `DEATH_MASS_TIER_THRESHOLDS`,
  `DEATH_TIER_RADIUS_SCALE`, `DEATH_TIER_CLASS_ID`; `_onKilled` reads the LIVE entity's mass/radius
  with the kill payload as fallback; `_isCapitalKill` refactored to share the new
  `isCapitalDeathVictim` predicate instead of duplicating the regex; `_queueExplosion` gains an
  optional `radiusOverride` and `causeOverride` so the resolved record is the single decision point.
- `test/vfx-death-sizing.test.mjs` (new) — four tiers from the real `src/data/ships.js` masses, each
  a larger death than the one below; a capital never downgrades even with a scout's mass; cause and
  direction survive on the record AND reach the pool entry; every `classId` is one the pool already
  knows; `_onKilled` reads live mass and keeps the kill-juice cue; missing mass/radius still returns
  a frozen deterministic record. Assertion messages quote the vision sentences "A light dies small
  and fast, a heavy dies large and slow" and "The player should still understand: what caused it,
  what moved, and why."
