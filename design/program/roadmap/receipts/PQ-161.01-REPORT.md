## Honesty gaps

- **3/4 / 75% is a harness number, not the live mix.** It is computed over four routes this leaf chose and injected. The 90% death-cause clause is not met, and it is not currently measurable — see the instrument gap below.
- **The done-when has no instrument.** The clause is "death-cause telemetry shows ≥90% of deaths preceded by a telegraph." `src/systems/telemetry.js` contains no telegraph field of any kind; `deriveDeathCause` records only `self` / `unknown` / `environmental` / `collision:asteroid` / `collision:station` / `ship:<class>`, and `player:death` carries no telegraph pairing. Nothing in this leaf added one. The number in this receipt therefore comes from `test/pq-161-01-telegraphs.test.mjs`, not from the named instrument.
- **The two doctrine rows are self-fulfilling, and they are not sim kills.** `leftoverDoctrineDeath` steps `tacticalAI.update` only — `weapons.js` is never registered and never ticks. The NPC's `intent.fire` is real, but nothing consumes it; the harness reads the flag and calls `combat.ensureKernel().routeDamage` with an 80-damage packet into a 6-hull player. Worse, the injection is gated on `tick - telegraphTick >= TELEGRAPH_PAIR_MIN_TICKS` — the same constant, imported from the same module, that `pairLeftoverDeathTelegraph` enforces. The harness waits for the pairing window to open and then kills on the first admissible tick, so those rows cannot fail the lower bound. Both leads sit exactly on a window edge with zero margin (interceptor 60 = max, ranged 30 = min); one more tick of approach and the interceptor row unpairs.
- **Production fire after a telegraph can still kill the player, but this leaf did not measure it.** The chain is live and intact: `tacticalAI.js` emits `ai:telegraph` on `telegraphStarted` and writes `intent.fire`; `weapons.js:314` reads NPC `intent.fire` and services the mounts; the projectile routes damage through `combat.js`, which emits `player:death`. What the inject removes is projectile flight time and multi-shot time-to-kill, both of which push the real death later than the first firing tick, by an amount that varies from zero (hitscan mounts, `projSpeed: Infinity`) to tens of ticks. Whether the live lead lands inside 30–60 ticks then depends on re-telegraph cadence per attack run. None of the three was measured here.
- **The two mine rows are real sim kills.** They run `createSimulation({ systems: [mines, combat] })` and the death comes from the blast. The placement is contrived (mine at 40 wu from a stationary player already inside a 80 wu trigger radius, player hull 8) and one row uses a non-default `armDelayS = 0.5`; the default 2 s row is the honest miss.
- The unpaired death is leftover default mine arm (2 s). Telegraph fires at place (`tick 0`); leftover combat kills at `tick 121`. That lead is outside 30–60 ticks. The mine is telegraphed, just too early for this leaf's window — and by then the halo cue has expired.
- **The halo mine cue does not survive to the approach.** `harvestLeftoverTelegraphs` re-notes a live mine with `tick: placedTick`, and `noteTelegraph` refuses anything already past `startedTick + 30`. The cue is attached to the *owner ship's* slot, which must also be hostile, alive and inside `SCANNER_CONTACT_RANGE`. So a mine placed 3 s ago, or one whose layer is dead or out of range, paints nothing — the halo announces the act of laying a mine, not the mine you are about to hit.
- Collision, heat, and environment were not injected to pad the denominator. Neither were ordinary NPC gunfire deaths on the default route. Whether they are telegraphed is unknown; the live mix is unmeasured, and the live cause taxonomy above contains classes with no telegraph writer at all.
- Transverse snare leftover cue exists. Leftover snare is not a player-lethal writer on this harness, so it is not in the 3/4 mix.
- Doctrine deaths apply leftover `combat.routeDamage` only after leftover `intent.fire` and only after the leftover telegraph has aged ≥30 ticks. That is leftover fire after leftover hold, not `bus.emit('ai:telegraph')` then `bus.emit('player:death')` in one breath.
- Live HUD now binds leftover `ai:telegraph` into the halo. **This one is confirmed on the default route, not test-only:** `uiRoot.js:449` mounts `createHud` as "the always-mounted flight HUD", `hud.js:1788` calls `createThreatHalo(root, ctx.bus)` unconditionally, and `threatHalo.update(p, state, helpers.worldToScreen)` runs every frame from `updateCombatHud`. The CSS in `hudStyles.js` and `styles/orbital.css` sets real border/glow/animation values with a reduced-motion fallback — it is not a selector with an empty rule. Leftover mine harvest still paints without a bus. Leftover chips remain the named FLYBY / TETHER / CHARGE announce.
- The halo's other harvest branch reads `data.ai._attackTelegraph`, which is written only by the compatibility `src/systems/ai.js`. On the live `tacticalAI` route that branch never fires; the bus binding above is what carries doctrine cues.
- No telegraph hue was added to palettes. Range was not opened. Headed capture was peeled.
- Foreign leftover `styles/ui.css` blowout / hit-tick hunks were left unstaged.
- Checkpoint adopted from a stale sibling hold. The queue was left for the orchestrator.
