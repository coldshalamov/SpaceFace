# PQ-139.00 — Collisions answer: hitstop and camera trauma by exchanged momentum; the Massline release snaps

Lane IMPACT · fun-convergence campaign · seed 4242 · 2026-09-04

## WHAT I FOUND

When your ship hit something, **the game did nothing about it.** Not "not enough" — nothing.

The layer that owns how a hit feels (`src/render/feel.js`) was listening for gunfire, for damage,
for kills, for the Massline, for mining, for jumps. It was not listening for a collision. Not one
line. So a scrape at docking speed and a full-speed slam into a station produced exactly the same
response from the camera and the clock: none. No pause. No shudder. Nothing.

The physics had been publishing the event the whole time — with the exchanged momentum already in
it — and nobody was subscribed.

And the Massline release, the signature move of this game, had its "snap" hard-coded to zero. In
nine separate places. There was even a comment claiming the zero was necessary to keep the
deterministic replay tests stable. **That comment was false**, and I proved it rather than believing
it: the feel layer is a render-phase system, it is not loaded by the replay harness at all, and the
sim always steps on a constant fixed timestep. A render-side pause cannot touch those tests. The
comment is corrected in the file.

## WHAT I CHANGED

One thing: **the feel layer now listens for collisions**, and how loud it answers is set by how much
momentum the two bodies actually traded — mass times change in velocity, taken from the physics
event itself, not invented.

- Below 8 WU/s of change: silence. That is touching, not hitting.
- Above it: the pause and the camera shudder ramp up on a square-root curve, so a scrape is a *tick*
  and a slam is a *beat*, and there is a hard ceiling so nothing can ever freeze the game.
- Impacts that happen away from you are quieter with distance.
- A grind along a wall cannot machine-gun the effect: once a beat fires, the next one has to be
  *harder* to interrupt it.
- Reduced-motion settings still turn all of it off, on every path.

And the Massline release got its snap: a razor release now stops time for 55 milliseconds. It was 0.

## WHAT YOU WILL FEEL

Hitting something is now an event. The world catches for a beat, the camera takes the blow, and the
size of both tells you how hard you hit — you will feel the difference between clipping a rock and
burying yourself in a station without reading a single number. And when a Massline release lands
clean, something *snaps loose*.

**Two honest notes about this evidence.**

1. **A still frame cannot show a 50-millisecond pause.** The screenshots are 110 ms apart; the beat
   is shorter than the gap between them. What proves the beat is the per-frame *trace* — the game's
   own clock, sampled every rendered frame while the real game ran on the real route. Before this
   change it read 1.000 at every sample, at every speed. It now dips to 0.12. That is a reading off
   the running game, not an assertion in a test.
2. **The first three captures lied about trauma, and the instrument was mine.** It sampled the
   camera *controller*, which has no trauma field; trauma lives on `state.camera`. It read 0.0000
   forever, before and after, and I nearly reported that as a result. Two further bugs in the same
   script (a fixed standoff that meant the slow runs never actually reached the rock, and a
   screenshot call that waits for a live 3D canvas to hold still) are fixed too. The numbers above
   are from the repaired instrument.
   Related: the *shared* bench frame-strip capture still shows a mission card with HUD text off —
   that one is the INSTRUMENT lane's to fix, not this lane's.

The peak trauma per run is not ordered by closing speed (0.95 at 150 WU/s but 0.83 at 20 WU/s)
because the capture runs inside a live Crucible swarm where kills and gunfire add trauma of their
own. The clean, isolated ramp is the real-path measurement in THE NUMBERS above, where the only
event in the world is the collision being measured.

## ONE THING FOR YOU TO KNOW

At 400 WU/s closing — nearly three times the 150 case — the physics returns **exactly the same**
momentum and exactly the same 40 WU/s hull ΔV as at 150. The collision impulse a ship can receive is
capped at 40. It means **no collision in this game can ever reach the top of any impact ramp**,
however hard you hit. That is not this lane's file to change; it is filed to the physics lane.

## Engineering appendix

**Hypothesis.** When a ship collides, `src/render/feel.js` subscribes to no collision event, so the
render layer produces zero hitstop and zero camera trauma; subscribing it to the existing
`physics:impact` (which already carries `dp`, `playerDeltaV`, `pos`, `aId`, `bId`) and scaling
hitstop/FOV/trauma by exchanged momentum should move bar B9 from 0 ms / 0 trauma at every ΔV to a
monotone ramp with a floor at ΔV ≥ 8 WU/s and a bounded ceiling.

**Files changed**

- `src/render/feel.js` — new pure `resolveCollisionFeel(impact, context)`; `physics:impact`
  subscriber `_onPhysicsImpact` (best-of-frame selection, no allocation in the hot path);
  `_flushPendingCollision()` called once per `frame()`; a 0.18 s real-time cooldown with a
  louder-impact upgrade gate; cooldown fields cleared in `_resetHitStop`.
- `src/render/masslinePresentation.js` — `hsDur` 0 → 0.055 (razor), 0.038 (clean), 0.020 (good),
  0.028 (important tumble), 0.050 (whip crushing), 0.030 (whip solid). The four intentionally-quiet
  branches (messy release, ordinary tumble, whip glance, tumble end) stay 0. The false determinism
  comment at the top of the block is corrected.
- `test/feel-collision-impact.test.mjs` (new) — floor, monotone ramp, 1.35x spread per step,
  ceiling under absurd input, motionReduce null at every ΔV, distance falloff, purity. Every
  assertion message quotes "Impacts should answer instantly".
- `test/massline-presentation-uvp.test.mjs` — three assertions that pinned `hsDur === 0` rewritten to
  assert the vision sentence ("A Massline release should feel like something snapped loose"), per
  `design/FEEL_CONTRACT.md` (a test that pins behaviour the vision forbids is a defect) and the
  taste director's explicit approval. One NEW assertion added proving `motionReduce` still returns
  `null` for the razor release. Coverage is strictly stronger, not weaker.
- `scripts/lib/bench/scenarios/feel.impact_feedback.mjs` (new drop-in module) — boots
  `scripts/lib/bench/realPath.mjs` (real `createAuthoritativeRuntime`, `rapier-dynamic`), drives real
  contacts at 20/60/150/400 WU/s closing, runs the shipped `resolveCollisionFeel` /
  `resolveCollisionCue` / `resolveMasslineFeelPunch` over the real `physics:impact` payloads, and
  feeds five B9 clauses through `metrics.bars`. It emits nothing on the bus; it only drives contacts
  through the solver, so the harness's `withFeatures()` guard does not apply to it — confirmed by the
  harness itself once CONTACT landed the guard: the refreshed receipt JSON carries
  `featuresOutsideStep: { tumble: false, weaponImpulseConsequences: false }`.
