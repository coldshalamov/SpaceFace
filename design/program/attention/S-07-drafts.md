# S-07 drafts — Charon Expanse, three personas

## The Drifter (working-class regular)
The Expanse is a long shift in a cold belt — refinery intake, thin air, and a wall of names at
the Lung Marker. The Drifter hears it through the work channel: haulers on the Ballad Line
singing about canisters and snapped tethers, DMC hail voices that sound like the end of a
double shift. Draft: ballad lines about the pod that still calls it Tuesday, and refinery-voice
hails that price patience like ore.

## The Archivist (continuity and memory)
Nine POIs file empty discovery records — the largest gap in the assigned block. Each is already
authored fiction at the site level (`authoredPlaces.js` reasons: the warm freighter with no
crew, the garden of bridged hulls, the pylons nothing biological crosses). The chart should
file what the site already knows. Draft: authored `discoveryPlate` bodies on all nine —
consistency-locked to the zone reasons, eerie and spare. The Lung Marker already has rim lore;
the colony stays unplated so the writ pool keeps its POIs.

## The Skeptic (pokes at edges)
The skeptic flies to the coordinates that aren't on the chart. Charon rewards her with bodies
that shouldn't be warm and triangles that shouldn't hold — and then the game says nothing.
Draft: the radio ghosts should *notice her noticing* — numbers-station lines that read like the
sector answering back ("DO NOT ANSWER BACK"), plus the plates. She rejects more DMC station
chatter as duplication of walls the player already reads at dock.
