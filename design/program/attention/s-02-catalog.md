# S-02 catalog — Ceres Belt (first jump)

Row register: **company town, quiet dread.**
Sanctioned detail: wreck dressing, claim tags, miner chatter.

## What the player literally perceives now

- **Geography.** First jump destination. Ceres Refinery (-1100,620), Belt Outpost mining
  station (780,-940), three gates (Helios ~4.7k WU east, Tethys far east, Pallas southwest),
  three asteroid fields, one dense-asteroid hazard, enemy density 0.73 — the first sector
  that can hurt you.
- **POIs (10+).** Abandoned Driller ("Sold Twice"), Survey Cache, Refinery Flare Rack,
  Inbound Weigh Pin, Throughline Weigh Beacon, Wreck Cathedral (asteroid site), RUCKUS work
  order, Second Measure Shipbreak (asteroid site), the Kettle Line drift trail (3 pieces),
  the Closed Refinery, the Star Lantern.
- **Traffic.** trafficPerMin 10. Authored cast law: the pick pool is pinned to Rell of the
  Moisture Column (seam miner) with Jorah fixture-stamped on the refinery hauler — a second
  pickable face would displace her on some seeds (`fb-world-40-two-faces.test.mjs`). The
  Ceres activity machinery (tender, disabled-hauler recovery) is the deepest of any sector.
- **Voice.** DMC barks (Drift Shift Board register — rock crews, tallies, columns).
  ballad_line is the DMC-home channel (homeFactions). 8+ Ceres-tagged ad_pulse notices,
  11 local_wall tags (Shaft 7, Kessler/Tycho, moisture column lore already there).
  The Margin investigates Ceres (0.7t moisture loss, the driller sold twice).

## What is thin

- **Claim culture is named but unbuilt.** "Claim tags" is the sanctioned detail and no
  claim-tag prop exists: fields spawn rocks but nothing shows the pinned, staked,
  lapsed-and-restaked claim grammar of a company town. (The claim_mark body was admitted
  at Helios and is unused here.)
- **The wreck fields have no dead.** Wreck Cathedral + Second Measure are landmark bodies;
  the belt's ordinary wrecks have no named dead, no votive dressing, no salvage etiquette —
  "quiet dread" arrives through one driller.
- **Ration/canteen economy invisible.** marketEquilibriumFactors encode a real story
  (fuel-cell surplus at the refinery, food shortage at Belt Outpost — the cheap/dear ends
  of the ration run) but no physical trace of it exists on the map.
- **The Pallas leg is bare.** The southwest gate leg (~4k WU) has zero authored content.

## Tone register observed

DMC shift-floor stoicism under quiet horror: moisture is a column, the dead are a tally,
the company is a weather system. Never self-pitying. The dread is administrative (sold
twice, weighed wet, replacement pending year 17), not gothic.
