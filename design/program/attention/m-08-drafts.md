# M-08 drafts — black flag path

## Drifter

The wanted life is a conversation with your own poster. The hail lines already get it —
"Third hull this season. The pay must be good." More of that: marks who've memorized the
writ, who know which desk signed it, who offer you the poster framed. The outlaw voice is
a person who has read their own wanted notice more times than you have.

## Archivist

Fence culture already has its etiquette (the Den's drawer, the Scar's quiet). Hail lines
can lean on it — a mark referencing "the Scar's rate", a writ from "Marker Twelve-K's
desk" — so the wanted texture shares vocabulary with the fence bars and the patrol
procedural registers instead of floating generic.

## Skeptic

Every added hail must survive a re-read: no promises of backup that never spawns, no
named allies who don't exist. A mark can bluff, but the line should work whether the
player fights, runs, or pays. Keep them reactive — about the poster, the price, the lane.
