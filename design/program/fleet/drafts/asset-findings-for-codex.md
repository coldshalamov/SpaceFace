
## B — hulls (LOOK-05B, stopped unfinished at owner's scope change; nothing in src/ was changed)

**How hull marks work today.** `src/render/illustratedStencils.js` draws one 1024 canvas sheet per decal style (9 ids = `SHIP_DECALS` minus none), in code, in normalized hull plan space (u aft to nose, v across the beam; larger of x/z spans the sheet). `illustratedSurface.js` samples it once (`STENCIL_GLSL`) on top-facing surfaces and mixes it over paint. Payload is installed per material by `installShipStencils` in `partsLibrary.js` (only when `palette.decalId` is a real style, so hulls whose faction has `decal: null`, and 'none', get NO marks and NO scratches/soot/grime). Panel layout and seams come from `illustratedHullLayout.js` (`HULL_LAYOUT_GLSL`, only for asset ids matched in `hullLayoutForAsset`). Faction to decal default: `PAINT_PROFILES.decal` in `src/data/palettes.js`; `resolvePalette` in `visualFactory.js` applies it. Wear amount = `palette.wear`.

**Gaps found.** (1) No hull without a decal shows any wear at all (zealot, archivist, helix, default factions, explicit 'none'). (2) Code-drawn sheet has chips only inside marks; no scratches, edge wear, soot behind drives, grime. (3) No edge wear in the shader.

**Plan I had designed (not applied).** Add a `worn` sheet used when decalId is none/absent (keep `STENCIL_STYLE_IDS` and the test pin `stencilPayloadFor('none') === null`; map in partsLibrary and map `worn` back to 'none' in `hullDecalToken`). Composite two authored atlases into the same sheet (repaint on image load): faction marks per style (hull numbers, hazard patches, tags, roundels), soot plumes at u 0.05-0.25 behind the drives, grime/rust blotches, bare-metal scratches and chips. Add a cheap screen-space edge-wear term in `STENCIL_GLSL` (curvature from `fwidth(vNormal)` over `fwidth(vSfHullPosition)`, noise-broken, bare-metal tint, wear uniform on the payload), no new samplers, no new materials.

## A — rocks (LOOK-05A; stopped at the owner's scope change)

**What is actually on screen in Swarm.** Swarm rocks are NOT the procedural `ast_*` icospheres. `src/systems/swarmArena.js` `_spawnDebrisRock` stamps `authoredGeologySkin` + `placeId = place_rock_<substance>_<pebble|boulder|monolith>` (substances: silicate, slag, metallic_ore, reef_metal, crystal, cooked_rock, ice), so the player sees the 24 GLBs `assets/ships/parts/places/place_rock_*.glb` once admitted. Those GLBs have **no UVs and no textures** except metallic_ore/reef_metal/ice, which carry a tiny forge panel atlas on a few parts. Rock bodies are flat-colour `Material_Stone / _deep / _strata` (linear albedo ~0.05 to 0.11). Any texture set needs object-space triplanar sampling injected through the existing authored-material path (or a UV re-export from the Forge/Blender step); a set of tileable maps alone cannot reach them. The procedural path (`visualFactory.astMaterial`, `rockSurfaceLibrary`, `rockFamilyLibrary`) serves Helios' scattered field, the three starter boulders (`ast_metallic`, `material:'rock'`) and the arena fallback. The shipped common-rock albedo averages RGB 60/54/46 of 255, i.e. dark mud before vertex colours, which is part of "washed out then muddy".

**Not landed (not judged on screen):** new metal / crystal / exotic maps now sit in `rock-candidates/` (512 px, drop-in names for `asteroid-families/`; prompts in `rock-candidates/PROMPTS.md`). The live frames (`.devshots/fleet/looks/look05a/open3-*`) show Helios rocks as flat, faceted, untextured lumps (saturated orange or blue-and-gold triangles) even with the family set present, so a swap could not be judged; fix that material path first. Also candidates: `rock-candidates/silicate_*` and `ice_*`. Ice (`astMaterial` ice branch) takes no map today; wiring it means a `map`/`normalMap` there plus an `ice` entry in `rockFamilyLibrary.js` (a test pins that library). Common rock needs an ORM (R AO, G roughness, B ~0 metal) derived from the new albedo before its base colour can be swapped.

**Look script:** `scripts/fleet/looks/rocks.mjs <outPrefix> <round>` boots a Swarm round, reports each on-screen rock's placeId, admission state and material flags, shoots plain and zoomed frames. Before shots were queued behind the lock and never ran.
