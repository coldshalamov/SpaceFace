## MED findings

### R1 — Ordinary minerals qualify for the food delivery reward

**Commit:** `4c5233487f`. **Location:** `src/systems/faction_frontier.js:1563` (qualification), `:1572` (consumption/reward); supporting owner: `src/systems/lootShards.js:650`.

Jettison one unit of an ordinary non-food commodity such as iron, tether that original pod, and hold it in a homestead supply ring below 12 WU/s for two seconds. `_foodPodVerdict` accepts any positive `gardenFeedMass`. That helper's commodity map contains all `COMMODITIES`, because garden compost accepts ordinary material; it is not a food predicate. The stead consequently destroys the mineral pod, increments supplies, pays the supply fee, and grants reputation/deed progress as a food delivery. PRI-09 section E specifically requires a whole food load. Cheap single-unit mineral pods can repeatedly buy the food reward.

**Smallest fix:** Require the ordinary food commodity before caching the garden custody/whole-load qualification. Keep non-food pods untouched and unpaid.

### R2 — A cached food verdict survives a catch-net custody change

**Commit:** `4c5233487f`. **Location:** `src/systems/faction_frontier.js:1561`, `:1642`; supporting writer: `src/systems/lootShards.js:843`.

Tow a valid food pod long enough to cache `true`, then pass it through an outlaw catch net before delivering it to a stead. `_markCaughtByNet` sets `data.caughtByNet` on the same living occupant; it does not remove that body or cut the player's tether. `gardenFeedMass` explicitly refuses caught pods (`lootShards.js:637`), but Frontier never reads that qualification again and `_consumeFoodPod` checks only that the pod is alive. The caught original can therefore be consumed and paid as the player's offering despite its changed custody. An id plus occupant generation proves a life, not immutable cargo ownership.

**Smallest fix:** Retain the admission cache, but reject changed custody at the final handoff; at minimum a live `caughtByNet` flag must make the cached pod ineligible and clear its dwell timer. The remaining contents must still be the qualified whole food load.

### R3 — The new yard capstan's physical cable has no renderer

**Commit:** `4ee3f5fc1d`. **Location:** `src/missions/furnaceCapstan.js:323`; missing admission at `src/render/combat/npcCableFx.js:97`.

Hail the quarantine gantry and choose WINCH POD. This gantry-to-pod attachment meets none of those paths. The player sees a pod hauled without the promised visible cable or its held-length picture.

**Smallest fix:** Admit this exact capstan attachment/control mode to the existing NPC service-cable renderer, retaining its endpoint/life checks. The passing capstan tests use a physics port stub and do not exercise rendering.

### R4 — A finished or destroyed Ceres lift keeps searching the whole world

**Commit:** `9e3f95c4ae`. **Location:** `src/systems/ceresAnchorWinching.js:267`, `:294`, `:303`; `findAt` at `:81`.

Visit the winches once, then destroy the original Long Plate or a winch and fly elsewhere in Ceres. The durable swing row remains, so the proximity early return applies no longer (`!old` is false). Every half-second, each missing cached body is searched with `state.entityList.filter`, including permanently lost masts whose spawned/lost masks already forbid replacements. The plate search also continues after its job is terminal. Thus a lift with no possible live work keeps allocating arrays and performing whole-entity-list searches throughout the sector. No FPS magnitude is claimed, but this is an unbounded-in-world-size update cost expressly prohibited by the intake rules.

**Smallest fix:** Stop admission searches for terminal/lost originals and retain a negative lookup until an actual spawn/restore/sector transition invalidates it. Resolve replacement references on those transitions rather than retrying whole-world filters forever.

### R5 — Casing retry backoff does not back off its entity scan

**Commit:** `018cbe9714`. **Location:** `src/missions/furnaceCasingReturn.js:57–73`; caller: `src/systems/furnaceIntake.js` update tail.

Finish all three quarantine deliveries while the casing spawn pockets are obstructed (or the spawn helper refuses). The pending casing has no live body. Every subsequent intake update executes the full `entityList.find` at line 61 before reaching the two-second `nextAttemptAt` check at line 73. The expensive adoption search therefore runs at 60 Hz throughout the blocked interval; only the pocket/spawn attempt is backed off. This adds a persistent full-world search while the owed reward cannot spawn.

**Smallest fix:** Gate the missing-body search with the retry deadline too, while retaining a one-time reconciliation on restore/spawn transitions. A pending blocked return should perform no full-world search between retries.

## LOW findings

### R6 — A stead with multiple obligations gets multiple overlapping receiving tags

**Commit:** `12a4f863a7`. **Location:** `src/missions/frontierReceivingTags.js:55`, `:101`, `:108`, `:118`.

A stranded stead that is also an open aid debtor gets both `stead:<id>` and `aid:<debt>` beacons at the same position; a salvage claim adds another. `add` deduplicates only the different string keys, not the receiving body. PRI-09 section C requires one tag per stead, with the first applicable reason in supply/aid/claim order. The ordinary stranded-debtor case instead puts conflicting labels and concentric receiving rings on the same destination.

**Smallest fix:** During wanted-set construction, remember body id plus generation and keep only its first reason. Let reconciliation remove the lower-priority tag and reveal it when the higher-priority obligation closes.

### R7 — Disposed plate-theft helpers retain a spawn subscription

**Commit:** `05879ec1b4`. **Location:** `src/systems/foundryPlateTheft.js:94`; owner teardown: `src/systems/survivalArena.js:1363`.

Reinitialize the arena on the same bus (`init` calls `destroy`), or dispose and recreate it with that bus. Each `createPlateTheft` installs an anonymous `entity:spawned` listener and discards the unsubscribe function. `clear` retires the event but never removes the listener, and `survivalArena.destroy` has no helper disposal hook. Each retired instance remains reachable through the bus, retains its captured state, and adds another callback to every later entity spawn. This is a lifecycle leak, not a claimed duplicate reward.

**Smallest fix:** Keep the unsubscribe returned by `bus.on`, expose a helper `destroy` that clears its event and unsubscribes, and call it from the arena's existing destroy path.
