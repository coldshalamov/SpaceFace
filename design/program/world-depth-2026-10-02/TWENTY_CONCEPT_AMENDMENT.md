## Enforceable visual-quality acceptance, independent of budgets

This is a stronger use of the existing visual evidence column, not a revived stack of retired
asset paperwork. Follow the current graphics front door: look at the real picture, fix the defect,
keep one system. No mandatory number of review cycles and no human approval ceremony.

Before multiplying a new family, the maker and an independent image-review agent must inspect
matched current-game reference images and the new candidate at real gameplay framing. Reference
selection is a production task: this plan identifies concrete sources, not aesthetically approved
screenshots. The independent audit now identifies Kestrel/Hitch, Salvage Cutter, Trade Hub and Helios Arclight
as strong form benchmarks; use its exact diagnostic qualifications and later verify the real-game
picture. Existing procedural fauna provide species context, not an acceptance ceiling. Start
with live Morrow/R-07, Vesper/SV-3 and BRACKET/BX-9 for authored character
construction/motion; the current Forge work fleet and the actual collector/service craft/Ceres
breaker/receiver for manufactured work bodies; the current `veil_ray`/Cinder Nursery for ecology;
and current live ORRERY pre-run and shipworks screens for service integration. Resolve their actual
runtime IDs through current consumers/manifests. Pick the strongest relevant *observed* shipped
examples and explain what is being matched or exceeded; weak old art is not a ceiling.

Required visual comparisons are compact and tied to the changed claim:

- Same renderer, lighting/post, camera and apparent scale for reference and candidate. Include
  default gameplay view and one useful close/inspection view. For a screen, inspect the real
  screen at 1280×720 and 1920×1080; for moving anatomy, inspect its defining phases and peak pose
- Readable designed silhouette and negative space, distinctive from its nearest existing analogue;
  role-specific supported machinery or anatomy instead of pasted primitives, floating greebles,
  generic recolors or a blockout with more triangles
- Clear large/mid/small form hierarchy; deliberate three-value separation; coherent manufactured
  or biological material language; bevels/recesses that survive gameplay scale, no muddy noise,
  blown glow, z-fighting, detached details or underside-only essential cues
- Motion explains actual state and physical work. Threat/clearance/custody remains readable with
  muted audio and reduced flash; loaded/empty, damaged/repaired and denial/recovery differ honestly
- Composed scene retains the player, exit, objective and negative spaces. No scene becomes visually
  impressive only in a studio thumbnail, extreme zoom, empty background or altered global grade

**Pass condition:** the independent reviewer actually opens the images, identifies the source
revision/runtime identities/view conditions and gives a concise pass or concrete fix list against
all applicable criteria. The reviewer must not accept an asset they authored solely from their own
receipt, a triangle count, or the source pack's concept plate. Obvious blockout quality, unreadable
role, false collision space or an explicit rejected candidate fails. The maker fixes identified
visible defects and obtains a fresh independent look at the changed evidence. Review missing or
failed means `visual` stays unpassed; source/integration progress can still land honestly as a
candidate. No whole-slice completion, approved-art claim or production-ready claim until it passes.
Working stills need not become permanent repository artifacts; retain concise revision-bound
review findings and useful current evidence rather than stale screenshot archives.

Technical acceptance remains separate: proper source/export/release/package identity; sockets,
axes, hull/collision agreement, LODs, loader/fallback, lifetime, draw/material/texture/memory costs,
and matched declared-device performance. Proposed pack timings, dimensions, masses, triangle/draw
ceilings, per-feature milliseconds and nearby caps are starting proposals, not certified limits.
Measure and reconcile against current owners. More detail does not mean more draw calls; optimize
batching, shared surfaces, residency and allocation without stripping authored quality. A technical
pass cannot compensate for weak visuals, and good pixels cannot establish target-GPU p95/p99.
