3 October 2026. This is the continuation after PR #209, which was merged before
the newer implementation packets were published. The existing TARGET,

## Publication contract

This draft begins with the plan. Candidate code, authored assets and verification
are added in small, coherent commits after reconciling each packet with the pinned
upstream source. Candidate results below are not CI results for an unported PR.
No merge or deployment is part of this work. Never overwrite newer upstream fixes
with an older whole-tree snapshot.

A completed packet includes its actual consumer, finite ownership/lifetime rules,
source assets when required, automated acceptance and an honest account of missing
visual/performance evidence. A model sitting unused in a registry is not completion.

## Concrete packets

1. **Reliable asset publication.** Preserve authored source identity, native/visual
   geometry agreement, scoped source/release/compiled generation and per-instance
   lifetime. Run the final census before the final dependency refresh/freshness
   check. Keep resource-bounded census measurements reproducible. Preserve old
   geometry when unchanged; disclose real sibling reseal differences.
2. **Physics and continuation.** Port exact native body/site/life/spec/attachment
   reconciliation and the stable off-centre transport clamp without removing newer
   upstream save-envelope, entry-epoch or rematerialization protections. Duplicate
   semantic remaps or changed geometry must select honest fallback when exact native
   continuation cannot be proved. Retained New Game must reset private owners and
   cannot let stale numeric IDs steer a new occupant.
3. **Physical working districts.** Integrate the authored collector, cooling rack,
   service craft, finite storage/repair/theft loops, structural salvage and Ceres
   breaker/head/receiver machinery. The receiver needs visible positive-depth
   keeper rails matching its actual contact geometry. Preserve occupied legacy
   cradles coherently; do not install new collision through a saved payload.
4. **Four distinct Brood encounters.** Integrate original mite, charger, Spitter and
   Leecher bodies, motion and ordinary wave ownership. The Leecher is a real attached
   mass with bounded biological counterstroke; exposed-body scraping, acceleration,
   rotation and weapon damage provide physical escapes. It does not change player
   movement stats or replace control with a debuff overlay. Preserve the existing
   cap of 40 until larger populations have their own measured acceptance.
5. **Continue the agreed breadth.** After these packets, continue the remaining five
   Brood roles and colony terrain, the other declared enemy/arena/boss families,
   world connections, progression and modes through the existing P00-P21 plan.
   Consolidate overlapping verification rather than generating duplicate task lists.

acceleration, with a modest steady-speed effect, not a universal speed reduction.
