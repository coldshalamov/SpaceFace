# SpaceFace: a mature visual and physical asset program

Planning proposal, 2026-10-02. Source baseline: `c488ecc10c23ad613dc35c61c04f124a3b77723a`. This document specifies a game-wide target and the production families it needs. It does not certify the game’s current appearance or mark any proposed asset implemented. New equipment names and exact silhouettes below are design proposals. Existing names retain their existing canon.

## 1. Design the finished experience before choosing the cheapest mesh

The governing fantasy is a busy physical universe the player can interfere with: momentum, working people, real cargo, dangerous tools, and consequences that outlive a fight. The asset program should make this readable before the player opens a menu. A loaded ship looks loaded; a launch bay launches something; a broken machine changes both the route and its outline; a living wreck is an organism using recognisable human anatomy. Beauty and physical legibility reinforce each other. [VISION](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/c488ecc10c23ad613dc35c61c04f124a3b77723a/design/VISION.md)

The mature collection must support six complete experiences: expressive flight through moving landscapes; physical fights with distinct opponents; a personally recognisable ship/build; lived-in trade, institutions and industry; alien exploration with biological and procedural-machine rules; and dense Swarm spectacle with authored biomes and bosses. Anvil’s collector/rack/cradle is one excellent first district, not the inventory ceiling. New hero geometry is ordinary production work when an experience needs it.

Reuse should happen at the level of finish, manufacturing language, joints, materials and technical contracts. Do not turn every occupation into a Mule variant, every place into a hub plus containers, every creature into a sphere with spikes, or every world event into loose cargo. A model brief needs an outline, an observable action and a changeable state. If its only distinction is colour or flavour text, it is not yet an asset-family brief.

## 2. What is represented now, and what remains unproven

This is a source-level inventory, not a visual-quality verdict. The current [Forge registry](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/c488ecc10c23ad613dc35c61c04f124a3b77723a/tools/blender/forge/fleet.json) has 116 entries: 70 place-layout, 31 NPC-layout and 15 player-layout entries. The last group includes the special Massline liner, so it is not a claim of 15 ordinary purchasable hulls. The [parts manifest](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/c488ecc10c23ad613dc35c61c04f124a3b77723a/assets/ships/parts/parts_manifest.json) has 183 rows, including legacy modular parts and LOD members. Neither number measures distinct player experiences or proves default-route reachability.

- **Player and human fleet: authored representation exists.** Fourteen ordinary player hulls, the liner, Ashline fighters, Helios civil craft, occupational hulls and faction derivatives are registered. Hornet’s cranked delta and Kestrel’s pressure-hull/drive-drum design have explicit anatomy. Wasp and Span faction variants demonstrate shared-body families, which should not be counted as entirely different vessel architectures. Existing damage hooks, hull posters, silhouettes and paint must survive new work.
- **Occupations: more than placeholders.** Ore barge, repair tender, salvage cutter, survey pin, yard tug, apron shuttle, rescue lifter, prospector skiff, scrap sweeper, volatiles tanker and inspection cutter are distinct registered bodies. The occupational table wires roles to jobs. Its old note that towing awaits admission is contradicted by the later runtime audit: do not commission towing again from that comment. Missing target vocabulary includes the proposed collector skimmer and equipment specifically shaped for its harvesting/service cycle; operational adequacy of each existing craft is a separate question. [Occupational bindings](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/c488ecc10c23ad613dc35c61c04f124a3b77723a/src/data/occupationalTrafficCraft.js)
- **Stations and works: substantial authored base.** Refinery, mining, fabrication, military, research, black market, trade hub and faction overlays exist, as do cranes, maintenance gantry, radiators, tanks, freight platforms, drill/conveyor hardware, claim modules and navigation structures. These are not all one generic station. What is unproven is the full institutional frontage and stateful public life proposed below. Asteroid Works pieces and Wreck Cathedral are identified as older-pipeline bodies, not missing ones. [Graphics program](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/c488ecc10c23ad613dc35c61c04f124a3b77723a/design/program/GRAPHICS_PROGRAM.md)
- **Landmarks and physical terrain: authored exemplars exist.** Candle Fleet, Resonant Cathedral, Skerris Throne, Quiessence freighters, rock families, Wreck Cathedral, SP-07 and its capture fork already supply real forms. Cinder Sluice has authored warning/surge/calm phases and durable operations. Therefore “make the first physical machine” would be false. More landscape families and specific moving/cuttable geometry remain legitimate expansion needs. [Machinery](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/c488ecc10c23ad613dc35c61c04f124a3b77723a/src/data/environmentalMachinery.js)
- **Alien biology: represented procedurally, not absent.** `faunaVisuals.js` constructs differentiated rays, cysts, leeches, worms, beasts, kites, eels and other species from cached Three.js geometry; it also builds growth morphologies. `visualFactory.js` dispatches fauna to that renderer. Ecology data supplies site casts and morphology mixes. This is a functioning representation path, not evidence of finished authored anatomy or a verified beautiful encounter. No dedicated fauna family appears in the inspected Forge/parts registries. [Fauna renderer](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/c488ecc10c23ad613dc35c61c04f124a3b77723a/src/render/faunaVisuals.js)
- **Ancient machines: also represented procedurally.** `machineVisuals.js` has kind-specific bodies and structure props; data defines machine/site behaviours. The Prism, Custodian and Auditor are not blank IDs. The current data is present despite older program-map text saying those paths exist only on PR #170. Evaluate current consumers rather than treating the old warning as an absence finding. [Machine renderer](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/c488ecc10c23ad613dc35c61c04f124a3b77723a/src/render/machineVisuals.js), [machine data](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/c488ecc10c23ad613dc35c61c04f124a3b77723a/src/data/precursorMachines.js)
- **Wrecks/cargo/damage: partial authored vocabulary.** Standard cargo, tow container, evidence spindle, rescue capsule, dead hulk, debris, several major wreck derivatives and Wasp bow/aft rupture fragments exist. A universal intact-to-fractured-to-salvaged anatomy is not established by those examples. Equally, old modular weapons/hulls in the manifest are not proof they are currently drawn: the Forge rule explicitly removes runtime bolt-ons from Forge bodies.
- **Swarm expansion: target, not certified current roster.** Its document calls for designed Brood and rogue-Foundry Machine families, ten bosses and physically different biomes. This audit has not traced every current Swarm consumer; no blanket “unimplemented” claim follows. Brood is explicitly a separate creature family from the Adventure infection; Foundry Machine is not the ancient procedural-machine civilisation. [Swarm expansion](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/c488ecc10c23ad613dc35c61c04f124a3b77723a/design/swarm/SWARM_EXPANSION.md)

## 3. The production families

### A. Working ships that show what they do

**Anvil thermal fleet:** a collector with a broad low body, two open forward intake cheeks and aft folding radiator fans; a mobile asymmetric cooling rack with off-centre handling yoke; a cold-service cradle whose receiving void matches the rack. Add pressure-canister saddles, visibly occupied/empty banks and a service head. The collector’s working and transit outlines differ. A failed fan stays bent or absent; damaged canisters expose a meaningful risk state. Consumer: finite NPC harvest, transfer, recovery, interception and district repair. Do not turn the current passive Anvil Mule witness into a “working skimmer” solely by changing its model.

**Ceres heavy handling:** one recognisable long retired hull, an open-rib breaker with lateral bridle arms, a sectional cutting cradle, hull-section transport shoes, a detachable cutter head and a compact survey-instrument set. Prefer a suitable established capital/liner identity; commission a new long hull if none supplies the required traversable/separable anatomy. Preserve existing tug and cutter identities; author the breaker only for the larger handling role. Intact/cut/supported/released joints must be visible. Consumer: choose a cut, brace a mass, release it, redirect the aftermath, sell or reuse the recovered part.

**Public-service extension:** expand existing rescue/tender/shuttle bodies with purpose-authored service configurations: pressure-cradle lifter, broad evacuation boarding sleeve, open repair rig and quarantined load carrier. Distinguish them by large negative spaces and load placement, not tiny roof accessories. Civilian windows and entry collars give scale. These assets serve rescue, interrupted maintenance, passenger transfer and custody handover; no new anonymous ship is commissioned merely to increase traffic density.

Each family needs towing/loading sockets, drive and RCS anchors, pickup/receiver alignment, stable motion groups, readable work lights and collision proxies. Pin names and transforms in source: Blender +X nose/+Y port/+Z up must survive GLTF conversion and the game’s XZ plane. Joint pivots, maximum travel, stowed bounds and visible attachment locations are part of the brief, not export cleanup. Share couplings and panel finishes. Author genuinely different loaded silhouettes rather than random container permutations.

### B. Combat bodies with physical counterplay

Develop a **line-cutter** with a narrow hook-like nose and exposed lateral cable-cutting sweep; a **formation anchor/support ship** with a heavy horseshoe hull surrounding a field core; and a **launcher/carrier** with asymmetric launch trenches and service access behind its bays. Their geometry communicates where to approach, what can be disabled, and what changes after damage. These are proposed form/state commissions for role families, not claims that the roles are absent. Check the existing role lattice and opponents before adding a duplicate behavior.

Faction differentiation belongs in structure: disciplined paired armour and enclosed machinery for institutional forces; offset salvage-derived thrust and exposed service access for raiders; broad cargo-safe clearance for escorts. Paint-only variants remain useful for ownership, but cannot substitute for a missing tactical silhouette. Keep smart human pilots as human ships; neither Brood nor ancient machines should erase that layer.

Author bay closed/open/disabled states, severable emitter housings, removable armour and a small number of meaningful fracture boundaries. Gameplay owns hit zones, disablement, launch counts and attack timing. Animation shows those facts rather than inventing fake vulnerability. The carrier is complete only when something actually emerges through its visible bay and the remaining silhouette explains its reduced capability.

### C. Player build identity and earned special hulls

Preserve the existing hull ladder and their strong outlines. First provide an **authored configuration grammar** for the builds that materially change play: field-control emitter shoulders, recoil-braced heavy-gun deck, heat-rejection fins, salvage fairlead/handling frame, and high-capacity cargo saddle. Each is designed with the base hull, not a generic loose component slapped over its skin.

The older fitting spec requests visible large modules, while current Forge forbids runtime bolt-on clutter. Resolve this explicitly through approved authored configuration variants or integrated semantic attachment geometry with a single presentation owner. Do not silently revive the old parts assembly. Coverage follows actual supported builds, with clear power/heat/cargo state and preserved muzzle/socket transforms. Regenerate HUD traces, posters and refit-jig images from the same model. [Fitting design](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/c488ecc10c23ad613dc35c61c04f124a3b77723a/design/spec3/SPEC3-F5-ships-outfitting-progression.md)

New flyable architectures need a movement/build reason: a wide recovery platform that handles offset loads, or a specialised field-control hull whose exposed core creates a risk/reward shape. Do not replace a satisfying existing hull simply for novelty. Saucer already has a registered body; preserve its Swarm-earned unlock and later Adventure purchase boundary rather than inventing cross-mode currency rewards.

### D. Institutions and planetary workplaces as places

Build **institutional frontages**, not another set of generic station cores: a customs pen with separate approach, inspection pocket and exit; a salvage court with tagged retained hulls and open transfer arms; a relief berth with pressure sleeves and emergency staging; a repair quay with open cradles; and a black-market intake with crooked screened approaches. These attach to or sit alongside appropriate existing station archetypes. Open bays, traffic lanes and crew-scale lighting communicate function at the flight camera.

For regional identity, complete Anvil’s hot/cold district, Ceres’s shipbreaking/survey works, and selected planetary work approaches from established planet identities. Each selected playable planetary workplace gets a characteristic local work silhouette and hazard-readable equipment: collector jaws near an atmospheric band are unlike ice-gripping service scaffolds or a mineral transfer terrace. Do not declare all named planets physical Anvil clones. No spherical landing, free vertical flight or planetary weather simulation is implied by this asset plan.

Landmarks require a recognisable macroform and deliberate empty space. Upgrade Wreck Cathedral’s authored shell without erasing its cavity and flythrough sockets; give selected machine/colonised landmarks their own spatial anatomy rather than a circle of repeated props. Each place needs a normal working read and authored repaired, shut-down, damaged or occupied states that the world actually uses. Consumer: station operations, traffic, law, claims, named local stories and return-visit consequences.

### E. Physical landscape and mechanism library

Produce compatible but visually distinct families for **cut geology**, **moving cover**, **pressure/current machinery**, and **capture/release hardware**.

Cut geology includes seam-bearing parent rock, believable fresh fracture faces, a few matched removable masses and anchored survey tools. Moving cover includes paired orbital fragments, conveyor-ring chunks and wreck sections that remain identifiable after displacement. Mechanisms include sliding bulkhead leaves, hinged vent vanes, piston jaws, a deployable launch cradle, catcher forks with honest openings and weighted shutters. Use SP-07/fork and Sluice as existing precedents, not cloned skins.

The artist authors planar clearance and meaningful contact surfaces, not merely decorative depth. Every opening needs matching collision gaps; every moving solid needs sweep limits and a simulation-owned transform. Keep most fine motion visual. Separate rigid bodies only where independent movement changes a decision; hundreds of physically independent decorative fins would add cost without agency. Consumer: slingshots, timed passes, cover disruption, release chains, theft/recovery and authored Swarm arenas. Persistence stores changed bodies/operations, not just a completion flag over reset scenery.

### F. Adventure ecology with real anatomy

Replace or enrich the current procedural representations by behavioural guild. **Veil Rays/Mourning Kites** need large asymmetric membranes, folded resting poses and readable sensory edges. **Hull Leeches/Casket Worms** need gripping structures that visibly attach to host surfaces. **Lantern Cysts/Spindle Mothers** need supported sacs, protective ribs and transport/rupture anatomy. **Anchor Beasts/Furnace Maws** need body-scale contact roots, jaw/throat mechanics and a clear relation to nearby terrain. Needle-like small organisms require one authored instanced shape per important role, not a bespoke rig per individual.

Growth is a second family: nerve lace, calcified collars, vent lungs, chimneys, bridge/tendril spans and dead crowns. Build host-specific placements around actual ducts, ribs, cargo mounts and wounds. A growth atlas is reusable; a universal green overlay is not. Author dormant, active, severed, burned and regrowing states only where the ecology owner distinguishes them. Healthy, infected and recovered fauna should share recognisable anatomy, with infection expressed in altered structure and motion as well as colour.

Consumer: heat/power intervention, moving a specimen, severing coherence, preserving a useful organism, quarantine and later route changes. Non-hostile ecology must be visually compelling in its own right. Creatures do not all need to become enemies or drop loot. Preserve the canon’s off-frame creators and avoid explaining the whole mystery with a visible alien king. [Taxonomy](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/c488ecc10c23ad613dc35c61c04f124a3b77723a/design/alien-ecology-program/02_FAUNA_AND_HOST_TAXONOMY.md)

### G. Ancient machines and their procedures

Evolve the existing kinds into a precise family: **Surveyor** as an offset optical prism with calibrated moving apertures; **Custodian/Mason** as tool-bearing frames that fit their repair surfaces; **Shepherd** as a long field-spine with unfolding boundary vanes; **Auditor/Witness** as threshold/observation geometry whose apertures visibly acknowledge a completed procedure. Keep their existing behavioural distinctions rather than reskinning all into fighting drones.

Structures include quarantine pylons, articulated thresholds, nested repair faces, sealed vault joints, dormant racks and a broken Shepherd corridor. Common joint tolerances and a restrained pale/dark surface language unite them; large proportions distinguish them from human welded industrial hardware. An ancient machine can remain motionless and still look intentional. Consumer: hold, witness, restore, clear, reroute and boundary-state procedures. Required states are inactive, measuring, working, refusing passage and mechanically damaged where supported. These do not invent faction reputation, a humanoid creator reveal or a mandatory fungus-versus-robot war. [Machine design](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/c488ecc10c23ad613dc35c61c04f124a3b77723a/design/alien-ecology-program/07_PRECURSOR_MACHINE_LAYER.md)

### H. Swarm bodies, bosses and arena anatomy

The complete target needs the Brood attack roster’s distinct bodies: mite, spitter, charger, leecher, burrower, splitter, mother, hive node and segmented tendril. Reuse a coherent carapace/membrane anatomy but make attack posture readable in outline. The Foundry Machine family needs formation drones, sweeping beam hardware, shield linkers, repair/assembly tools, saws and magnet sentries. Its industrial manufacturing language must remain distinct from ancient machines.

Boss coverage follows the authored roster: Queen sacs, Tendril breakable segments, Crusher pistons/walls, Mothership launch bays/core, Rival’s actual build, Binary Twins’ physical link, Storm Leviathan and relays, Gravity Engine, Iron Maw phases and the Producer’s final form. These are not ten scaled normal ships. Some are multi-piece environments; the Rival should reuse the player’s truthful configuration.

Arena assets must support each promised physical rule: Mill fracture rocks, Derelict Spine doors/vents, ring-cover conveyors, Hive growth gates, Corona shadow-making masses and relay machinery. Space backgrounds, warning effects and audio remain companion work, not substitutes for physical geometry. Modelled instanced populations serve the 100–400-body target; this is a measured rendering/simulation requirement, not permission to promise that quantity is free.

### I. Aftermath, cargo and persistent history

Make payloads readable by handling and consequence: pressure canisters, ore bulk, awkward machinery, fragile habitat sections, evidence custody and biological containment. Shared couplings standardise handling while shells remain unmistakably different. A coolant rack cannot look like a crate with a blue label.

Extend the existing wreck kit into matched intact/damaged/fractured families for important new ships and mechanisms. Author torn cross-sections, load-bearing survivors, disabled-but-recoverable states, repaired patches and scavenged remnants. Bound fracture to a few authored pieces, preserve source identity, and pool cosmetic shards. Consumer: salvage, rescue, repair, loss, claims and persistent places. Never sell an elaborate destruction animation as durable world change if the entity reloads intact.

## 4. Map asset work to the experience stages

[TARGET](TARGET.md) owns staging; this is its asset crosswalk, not an independent priority list.

- **Stage A, new human movement ecology:** Anvil collector, rack, cradle, actual loads and working/damaged states, integrated with a complete normal shift and physical opportunity. The selected first slice, not the entire family inventory, is the production unit.
- **Stage B, broader flight/combat/build expression:** Ceres cut/handling set and changing passage, interception-carrier hardware, complementary opponent silhouettes and truthful build configurations. Add the terrain, custody frontage and aftermath each selected experience actually uses.
- **Stage C, frontier:** a complete ecological ensemble, its host-specific growth and terrain, plus one ancient-machine service route. Creature anatomy, motion and site geometry ship with behavior; the first attractive ray is not completion of the whole family.
- **Stage D, dramatic reach:** campaign-specific operation bodies, deep landmarks, consequence states and late aspirational fittings. Fill missing anatomy for ordinary operation, interruption, recovery and return; retire superseded placeholders only after live replacement is verified.
- **Parallel Swarm track:** dense Brood and Foundry Machine bodies, contrasting arena anatomy and authored boss mechanisms. It does not wait for Adventure's Stage D; shared technique and tested assets can move between the two tracks while their canon and progression remain distinct.

These are production tranches, not permission gates for every ordinary asset. A brief is judged on
coherence, canon and gameplay need. Escalate material scope/canon/system decisions; make routine
modeling decisions within the selected brief. Do not postpone all large authored bodies behind an
endless supply of small props.

## 5. Cost and preservation plan

Use Forge’s shape/finish/export discipline; organic anatomy may require new authoring helpers and semantic materials, not metallic plating painted green. Any kit extension must be tested against existing bodies. Forge describes review framings of approximately 170 pixels per ship at chase and 450 at close zoom; these are not measured guarantees for every gameplay situation. At those scales, silhouette, layered depth and meaningful motion matter more than invisible micro-detail. [Forge](https://github.com/coldshalamov/SpaceFace/blob/c488ecc10c23ad613dc35c61c04f124a3b77723a/tools/blender/forge/FORGE.md)

Measure per candidate and composed scene: triangles/vertices by LOD; mesh/submesh/material counts; actual draw submissions and shader variants; texture source, compressed and resident bytes; GLB download/decode/upload time; animation/update CPU; collision/contact cost; load admission and longest frame; first-visible delay; steady p95/p99 CPU/GPU time; and residency after leaving/returning. Compare identical seed/camera/device/quality and report initial load separately from warm flight. Library-wide source size and simultaneous on-screen cost are different budgets.

Preserve quality through clean topology, meaningful bevels, hidden-face removal, shared trim/panel atlases, consolidated static material groups, repeated-detail instancing and conservative distance LOD. Do not merge across independently animated or destructible joints. Do not atlas unrelated assets into one enormous residency obligation. Use coarse collision compounds/planar proxies with genuine holes and separate query semantics; visual LOD never changes collision, sockets or gameplay dimensions. Transparent membranes, glow, shadows and particles can cost more than triangles, so test overdraw and layer composition explicitly.

Maintain source/release hashes, semantic materials, sockets, motion names, bounds, collision scale, damage hooks and default-route loader selection. Verify fallback counters: a procedurally rendered substitute can hide a broken GLB. New modules must preserve old ships’ weapons, thrust, docking and previews. Existing shared-image deduplication, batching, admission, package and LOD systems are assets to preserve, not bypass.

Cloud Blender 4.3.2 CPU model/export/render is proven by the separate feasibility probe. Subsequent setup also rebuilt the real Forge `place_transfer_arm.py` using its canonical source and six shared textures: 883,104-byte preview, 5,856 LOD0 triangles, 27 meshes, 9 materials, with two canonical sockets and asset identity preserved. glTF Transform parsed source/release/preview. Production publisher/release regeneration, full-game visual review and real-renderer performance are still unproven. Current shell Chromium launch is blocked by a sandbox socket restriction and official CUA localhost reports `ERR_BLOCKED_BY_CLIENT`; these are specific review-route blockers, not evidence that export failed. Each production tranche needs the existing Forge review/publish/package/census process plus focused gameplay fixtures; one integration owner manages global generated package outputs. No new screenshot bureaucracy replaces useful runtime evidence, but a purely visual claim still requires looking at the actual renderer.

## 6. Bounded packets, individual models and real consumers

[IMPLEMENTATION](IMPLEMENTATION.md) is the finite delivery crosswalk for this revision. Its packet IDs identify experience-sized handoffs inside existing owners, not a new dispatcher, priority queue or catalog of automatically missing assets. Families A–I are a shared design vocabulary; a packet combines their pieces into one playable outcome. Complete coverage means closing the roster, not stopping when the first Anvil packet looks good.

Before a packet's first implementation commit, name each required model or reusable model-state, its authoring path, its intended runtime ID, consumer, motion/socket/collider contract and state transitions. Resolve descriptive proposal names against current canon. A working state may share one mesh and use a simulation-owned transform; separate files are not required for every state. Conversely, a single file does not excuse omitting a necessary open bay, fracture face or occupied load. The packet lists are finite briefs; additions to their delivery scope require a reason tied to the target, not automatic expansion into every possible species/configuration permutation.

Classify every piece as **reuse suitable**, **adapt**, **new**, **built/unconsumed** or **unverified** after live inspection. Suitable existing models count only after their actual intended consumer and state are demonstrated. Keep an authored contact sheet of the packet's named pieces and relevant states alongside its existing graphics receipt. This is a review aid, not a second manifest. Concept work and placeholders can unblock behavior development, but must remain explicitly temporary. A generic crate, recolored ship, stretched primitive, invisible hit zone or inert machine cannot close a brief requiring specific anatomy and motion.

### The per-model iteration loop

1. **Baseline:** capture the current body, or explicitly record its absence, at fixed chase/close views in the real renderer. Record source/release hash, camera, light, quality, transforms, active state and resource counts. For a new body, compare against the approved silhouette/interaction brief and an existing scene composition, not an unrelated pretty render.
2. **Hypothesis:** name one visual or physical defect and the expected improvement: for example, intake cheeks disappear at chase distance, the launch mouth is occluded, a membrane produces excessive layered overdraw, or loaded mass is unreadable. State what must remain unchanged.
3. **Change:** make one causally interpretable model/state/material/LOD change. Keep sockets, motion groups, collision gaps and dependent diagrams in sync. Do not rebuild the fleet for a bevel edit.
4. **Same-scene comparison:** inspect paired captures and relevant measurements at identical settings. Include the worst relevant angle/state and real nearby actors, not only a flattering isolated turntable. A loader or fallback change invalidates a visual comparison until diagnosed.
5. **Keep or reject:** retain changes that improve the declared target without losing silhouette, readable motion, physical truth or measured budget. Revert unsuccessful experiments; record the reason in the existing receipt/commit rather than accumulating candidate variants in runtime.
6. **Commit:** make a small reviewable source/consumer change with the relevant evidence. Global release outputs land at the coherent packet boundary through the integration owner. Repeat only on a named remaining defect; stop that loop when its acceptance evidence passes. Close a rejected approach by choosing another concrete implementation, never by silently downgrading the feature.

Each commissioned model gets this loop, including the new creatures, large landmarks and player variants. A convincing collector is not evidence that the carrier or ray is finished. A three-round diagnostic checkpoint is useful when an approach stalls: after three failed attempts, reconsider anatomy, representation or algorithm with the owner. It is not permission to abandon the feature or spend indefinitely polishing one body while other named pieces remain placeholders.

### The per-system and composed-experience iteration loop

Use the same baseline → hypothesis → one change → same-scene comparison → keep/reject → commit sequence for **every changed system**, including motion/physics, traffic/work, tactical behavior, fitting/progression, ecology/machines, persistence and Swarm population/arena control. A system baseline includes observable player behavior and CPU/update/contact/allocation measures, not screenshots alone. A rendering baseline includes live draw submissions, shader/material/texture residency, decode/upload/admission spikes and CPU/GPU timing. State the expected scaling with bodies, contacts, jobs, effects or loaded resources; then vary that axis while keeping the scenario fixed.

Prefer measured structural improvements: spatial queries over all-pairs searches, cached immutable geometry, state-delta updates, bounded wake sets, pooled transient effects, batched instance uploads, shared materials, stable broadphase proxies, deferred admission and bounded background cadence. Preserve foreground physical authority and observable outcomes. Do not conceal regressions by removing new silhouettes, shortening the meaningful route, thinning promised encounters without disclosure, freezing important work, lowering the comparison resolution, or substituting cheap stubs. Distant work can be coarser only if custody, conservation, elapsed work and return-state reconciliation remain truthful.

Run component loops first, then compose them. A fast carrier alone may still stall when it launches into a busy thermal district; an individually cheap creature can produce expensive all-pairs avoidance in a flock. At packet acceptance compare idle/ordinary work, interruption/peak action, aftermath and leave/return. At stage acceptance compare the supported combined scenario. Fix the algorithm/resource responsible before trading away the target experience; any unavoidable target tradeoff is an explicit owner decision, not a hidden optimization.

## 7. Exact questions to close before commissioning each family

- For every candidate ID, trace `fleet.json → parts_manifest → PLACE_FILES/ship resolver → render-package pilot → release → spawning gameplay row`; record loaded authored body versus intentional procedural body versus fallback, and unreachable entries separately.
- Search current occupational consumers for `yard_tug`, tow attachment and save restoration; for Anvil, trace NPC harvest/loading rather than trusting occupational comments. Check the thermal nearest-ship limit before populating the district.
- In current fauna/machine consumers, inventory kind-to-builder and morphology-to-builder mappings; compare each against a gameplay-camera view and saved state. Which species need replacement anatomy, which already read well, and which catalog names have no reachable consumer?
- For every proposed opponent, compare `shipRoleLattice`, enemy behaviours and Swarm catalogs before adding a duplicate. Which role needs a new body, and which existing body needs a truthful disable/attack state?
- For player configurations, resolve the older visible-module promise against current no-bolt-on Forge ownership. Which actual fit deltas are presently visible, and who owns sanctioned authored variants?
- For terrain and wrecks, trace collision proxies, movable-body admission, fracture membership and persistence. Does the fresh opening exist in collision, remain after reload and preserve AI navigation?
- For each planet/landmark, separate sky representation, local physical body, site manifest and traffic/job consumers. Which proposed workplace can use existing physics, and which needs an explicit new system?
- Run baseline and candidate composed scenes through `probe-frame-solid` and applicable renderer diagnostics. What resource actually regresses? Optimise that measured cost without deleting the family’s defining silhouette or mechanism.

The result is a broader, authored vocabulary with honest physical consumers. It is neither a model-count target nor permission to keep reusing generic bodies forever.
