## Required construction

### Causal physical grammar

Small contact, heavy slam, shield response, structural break, successful latch, refused latch, line release and actual kill need recognizable differences. Cue choice follows committed cause/energy and material where supported. Preserve proper pauses and lifetime cleanup; no per-frame retrigger.

### Inhabited places have ordinary sound

Give each sector a small acoustic palette tied to its real work: shuttle approach, receiver clamp, quarry bell, coolant cycle, held listening interval. Use distance and occlusion consistently. An ambience bed may support a place but cannot pretend absent NPCs are working.

### Music follows readable state

Use the existing adaptive/stem owner. Safe harbor, industrial work, exposed travel, actual conflict and aftermath have appropriate transitions. Sector identity can come from instrumentation/texture without making twenty-five unrelated music engines. Avoid constant dramatic escalation in noncombat work.

### One voice, not a committee

Respect the transient attention owner, dedupe and priorities. Urgent hazard/combat information wins; local acknowledgments wait or use persistent readable context. Wire authored character lines only where they fit actual conditions. Keep Swarm free of unrelated Adventure news and lore.

### Spectacle retains information

Synchronize material impact, directional effects, structural response and sound without maximizing all of them simultaneously. Explosions occur in the world, not as white screens. Reduced motion/flash retains hit direction, important timing and success/failure information; volume/mute options remain functional.

### Content and runtime budget

Reuse approved recordings and synthesis appropriately, document licenses/provenance for new sources, and honor decoded/encoded payload and voice limits. Stop/cancel/pause correctly; no leak of loops or stale music after mode switch, death or Continue.
