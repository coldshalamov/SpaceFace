## Required construction

### One bounded causal chain

Start with a specific shipment/site: actual exposure or admission → native local contamination/operation change → visible attached growth and service response → bounded downstream stock/traffic effect. Document exactly which event commits each transition. Avoid cosmetic timers disconnected from state.

### Stages with decisions, not just colors

Author clean/normal, suspicious, visibly compromised, contained and recovered presentations using supported native bands; do not change canonical biology merely to fit a five-state UI. Early cues give a reasonable chance to act. Each relevant state changes an affordance: isolation berth, service access, route choice, supply need or useful harvest.

### Contained scope and understandable spread

Use current ecological connections and geographic scope. A small contaminated lot cannot silently destroy the galaxy. Do not advance crises on an offline wall clock or during a paused game. Important escalation requires legible pressure, not a surprise on returning from unrelated play. Performance uses coarse existing ecology updates and bounded presentation.

### People react materially

Workers move or stop a specific operation, a receiver refuses a risky lot, traffic reroutes, and a contact offers a relevant containment or replacement job. These are projections of the same native condition, not independent stories that can contradict it.

### Recovery is gameplay

Offer safe isolation, removal/pruning, replacement supply or supported productive cultivation depending on place. Consume finite resources honestly. Reopen services and routes when the canonical work completes. Keep a restrained remembered scar and accurate acknowledgment, but do not leave a “repaired” facility permanently useless.

### Generalize only proven seams

Apply the same consequence projection to a non-biological case such as a delivered repair part or a restored freight route. Shared technology should handle truthful states; it must not turn every consequence into the same glowing damage overlay. Charon owns its authored story and the first deep infection consumer.

## Direct acceptance and finite stopping point

- No duplicated contamination, quantity, economic or save authority.
- Clean, compromised, contained and recovered visits are visibly and operationally distinguishable at normal camera scale.
- The player can identify the causal choice and a currently available response without reading a developer ledger.
- Save/Continue, pause, interrupted work and repeated receipt preserve the exact state and finite resources.
- No new autonomous enemy construction is smuggled in as ecology.

## Reject these substitutes

Green tint; population loss inferred from a texture; global outbreak from one trivial mistake; fake market-price multipliers beside the native economy; irreversible surprise sabotage.
