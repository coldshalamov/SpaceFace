The story gives direction to an expanding life, not a reason to throw that life away at the credits. The player learns the world, gains useful machines and relationships, operates property, contests power and continues living with the results. Existing endings remain reachable and meaningful; the economic/physical universe remains playable afterward.

## Required construction

### Four experience movements, not four replacement quest engines

Arrange the existing story into local belonging, useful regional reach, contested institutions and an open-ended resolution. Each movement has a visible practical goal, a capability/access gain, a consequential set piece and a reason to continue. These are editorial organization over the existing spine, not new save IDs for the same beats.

### Optional breadth around a legible path

Side work supports the player’s chosen activity without burying the next main step. A mission board recommendation, physical contact and map point should agree. Give alternative preparation/recovery when a desired fit or route is unavailable. Do not require every sector commission to be completed before any story act can work.

### Character arcs through action

Every supported recurring contact receives an introduction, a practical transaction/intervention, a fact-based response and a later callback where it fits. Reuse voice and existing memory. The finale should acknowledge concrete relevant deeds, not list every contact artificially. Do not manufacture guilt for harms the player prevented.

### One pilot, several scopes

Reconcile persistent pilot knowledge/blueprints/skills with owned ship inventory and run-scoped Swarm offers. Respect P3’s current approval/migration decisions. New progression must expand usable options and avoid compulsory permanent specialization. Display what persists and what is run-only before a player commits.

### Endings do not erase the world

Preserve ending identities and established story facts while implementing the latest direction for continued free play, operation ownership and recoverable relationships. Repairs remain repaired, rescued people remain rescued and owned machines remain owned unless a clearly authored actual event changes them. A campaign ending is not automatic prestige.

### Strategic rise is playable

Connect local ownership, logistics and faction influence to later ambitions through existing operations. The player can become powerful without every problem becoming a top-down RTS menu. A challenge should still present something their personally piloted ship can affect.
