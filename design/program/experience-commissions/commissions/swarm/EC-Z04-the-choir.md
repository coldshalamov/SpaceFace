**Construction:** Support/pressure relationships invite breaking the group.

**What must be perceptible:** Interrupting the right body changes what the others can do.

**Construction:** Reward a large clear with the native breathing mechanism.

**What must be perceptible:** Reinforcements do not instantly erase a player-earned opening.

**Construction:** Use an existing admitted event/variant to change the question without rewriting controls.

**What must be perceptible:** A concise cue explains the difference and its end condition.

### Round 35 — Choir champion

**Construction:** Run the native boss with a curated accompaniment, not every specialist type together.

**What must be perceptible:** Boss threat, player action and collateral remain separable at peak intensity.

### Round 36 — A new combination

**Construction:** Let high-level fit interactions create a new method against familiar targets.

**What must be perceptible:** The reward is usable agency, not a new compulsory rarity tier.

### Round 37 — Pressure from history

**Construction:** Use surviving terrain and current native arena phases as the tactical context.

**What must be perceptible:** No forced reset or impossible route from accumulated debris.

### Round 38 — Counterplay at speed

**Construction:** Test an established tell at faster composition pressure while preserving the actual response window.

**What must be perceptible:** Difficulty does not come from suppressing the telegraph.

### Round 39 — Controlled extravagance

**Construction:** Stage a high-density but budgeted culmination with familiar causes.

**What must be perceptible:** A reviewer can explain why the player succeeded or died.

### Round 40 — An open horizon

**Construction:** Resolve current zone/records clearly and enter the hardened repeating sequence.

**What must be perceptible:** No forced replay/reset; custom/ladder distinctions and persistent rewards remain correct.
