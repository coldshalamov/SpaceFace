## The experience to produce

The player has learned individual threats. Now opponents arrive as a situation: a front, a flank, support and a weakness. The pleasure is breaking a formation with a build that has begun to feel personal. Difficulty grows through relationships, not through turning every ship into a health bar.

## Round-by-round authored questions

These are experience targets over the existing planner, not ten new mission systems. Inspect the actual roster and behavior before binding a role; preserve supported identities and meaningful unlock timing. Do not invent an unadmitted specialist solely because a row uses a role word.

### Round 11 — A second bearing

**Construction:** Use familiar pressure from complementary readable approaches.

**What must be perceptible:** One reposition changes which threat can reach the player.

### Round 12 — Protect the opening

**Construction:** A supported ranged/support role sustains pressure until interrupted or flanked.

**What must be perceptible:** The source of support is visible and counterplay changes the fight.

### Round 13 — Break the group

**Construction:** A compact approach invites pull/shove/line or a well-placed gun angle.

**What must be perceptible:** Mass behavior and score follow real events, not formation labels.

### Round 14 — Recover an error

**Construction:** A denser but readable composition makes escape and a surviving supply opportunity valuable.

**What must be perceptible:** A supported mobile kit can disengage without hidden speed clamps.

### Round 15 — Wing champion

**Construction:** Use the native boss cadence with familiar flank pressure at a lower readable support load.

**What must be perceptible:** Boss and support tells do not combine into unavoidable untelegraphed damage.

### Round 16 — Upgrade under pressure

**Construction:** Let the next meaningful choice solve a visible weakness from the prior fights.

**What must be perceptible:** The current hull can use every offered selection or receives an honest alternative.

### Round 17 — Pick the target

**Construction:** Two recognizable roles create a genuine priority decision.

**What must be perceptible:** Either clever ordering or a physical combination creates breathing room.

### Round 18 — An altered route

**Construction:** Existing terrain history changes a previously easy approach.

**What must be perceptible:** The room remains navigable without resetting all props.

### Round 19 — Combine your tools

**Construction:** A mixed but familiar group supports several earned combinations.

**What must be perceptible:** No single compulsory rare card gates progress.

### Round 20 — Keep the run legible

**Construction:** Resolve the block and current refit/checkpoint/result semantics clearly.

**What must be perceptible:** The player sees what persists, what can be changed and where continuation begins.
