## Diagnosis and decision

The current planner already has three authored acts, real role substitutions, arena-dependent
final bosses, finite wave ownership, refit transitions and final results. Replacing that machinery
would duplicate working owners. Two disconnected promises remained: `foundry_plate_theft` was
announced without a physical theft consumer, and a generic `victory` label could satisfy the
authored-course unlock/lineage readers for an incompatible run.

A scripted disappearance would make the bank's apparent loss independent of physics. A new
escort AI/towing engine would duplicate existing motion and attachment authority. The selected
connection releases one native bank, lets at most two actual wave-20 screen escorts attach through
the combat attachment service, reels the real line, and reads the resulting poses. The bank remains
usable geometry at its new location. No pose, force, kill, reward or wave-clear verdict is invented.

- The existing arena owner admits one contested native bank on authored wave 20. It releases the
  existing arrival-facing plate where present; other supported arenas receive one existing bank
  shape within the twelve-toy room bound. No new artwork or parallel room lifetime was added.
- An actual `pd_screen_escort` within 240 units starts a two-second warning. At most two escorts
  can attempt a real `attachment_snarl` tow; a successfully severed line cannot instantly re-latch.
  The native constraint reels at 18 units/second. Moving the same bank at least 100 units and
  bringing it alongside a captor settles the theft; the relocated bank remains in the fight.
- Killing a towing actor or snapping its line is supported intervention. A valid surviving bank
  at wave clear is secured. A missing/recycled bank is lost, rather than a fabricated success.
  The existing announcer describes the threat, counterplay and actual clear outcome within its
  current line budget. The existing attachment renderer owns the visible tow.
- Room teardown releases its attachments. Replayed planning receipts cannot recreate a spent or
  moved bank in the same run; a genuinely new run may install one. Run identity and occupant
  generation guards cover synchronous exit during warning, attachment creation and line cutting.
- A shared read-only course identity gate now serves unlock and lineage settlement. Authored
  victory requires an admitted course ruleset, a real thirty-wave clear, compatible mode/run
  stamps and no later-wave start. Swarm, practice, circuits, endless runs, partial clears and
  contradictory stamps cannot award the authored completion merely by saying `victory`.
- Existing thirty-wave planning, Forge Regent arena substitution, champion/combat ownership,
  profile rewards, refit agency and campaign isolation are retained. This PR does not include
  EC-Z01's first-ten Swarm composition change.
