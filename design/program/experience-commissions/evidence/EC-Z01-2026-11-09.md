## Diagnosis and decision

Current master already supplies the title's direct Swarm entry, native opening, first champion,
finite cohort/refill/earned-breath logic, persistent opening terrain, refit choices, last-enemy
discovery, native records and retry. Active PR #942 independently develops Brood pressure. This
change does not rebuild those systems or replace their population/score accounting.

The generic opening planner did not express several first-block questions as repeatable spatial
compositions. An announced specialist could be seen first as a random reinforcement, and forecast
text omitted the already-authored Iron Kraken arrival. Increasing health or body caps would add
duration without teaching a new response. Seven arena-specific scripts would fork the roster and
determinism contract. The chosen change is one small pure composition table over admitted native
ships, consumed by the existing planner and presentation readers.
