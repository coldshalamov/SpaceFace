# 19 — What players expect: the basics that decide whether they stay

**From the lead designer, 2026-11-10.** None of this is the hook. All of it is what a player notices in the first evening and judges the game by. A great throw inside a game that takes forty seconds to restart is a game people stop playing. Each row names the plan that owns it.

## Getting in

| Expectation | What it means here | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| **Playing in seconds** | title to flying in under ten seconds; a new Swarm run in under five. Nothing to choose unless you ask for it | EC-G10, EC-G14 |
| **Taught by doing** | four prompts, each shown once, at the moment it matters: *move, fire, the Line, boost*. Never a wall of keys. Powers arrive one at a time, in a situation that needs them | EC-G10, EC-G18 |
| **One more go** | on death: what killed you, your best throw in a sentence (*"You put a Bruiser through the furnace door"*), the round you reached, one dry line from Kiln — and **Again** under your thumb. Back in the arena inside five seconds | EC-C01, EC-Z01 |

## Staying in control

| Expectation | What it means here | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| **A controller works fully** | everything reachable; the Line latches to the nearest sensible body inside a small cone, so aiming a rope with a stick is fair; rumble on tension and on impact | EC-G18 |
| **Keys can be changed** and stay changed | existing custom bindings survive the move to six inputs | EC-G18 |
| **Pause anywhere; quit and come back** | a Swarm run is saved at each round's end and can be resumed tomorrow. Adventure saves at every dock and whenever you are wrecked | EC-G12 |
| **Never stuck** | no state the player cannot get out of by their own hands. If one is found, it is a defect of the highest rank, and there is always a "return to berth" as a last resort | EC-G12 |

## Being able to read it

| Expectation | What it means here | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| **I can always find my ship** | a constant outline on the player's ship that nothing draws over | EC-G16, EC-G03 |
| **I can tell what will hurt me** | enemy fire is one family of colour, yours another. Every danger shows itself at least six tenths of a second before it lands. The screen never goes white | EC-G08, EC-G03 |
| **Shape, not only colour** | every enemy kind differs by silhouette, so colour-blind players sort them too | EC-G08 |
| **Text I can read on a small laptop** | nothing smaller than a set minimum; a size option; plain words | EC-G16 |
| **Motion I can turn down** | shake, flash and camera punch each have a reduce setting. The game is fully playable with all three low | EC-G16, EC-G18 |

## Being treated fairly

| Expectation | What it means here | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| **Difficulty I choose** | in Swarm it is **Kiln's mood** — *Generous, Fair, Cruel*. It changes how many come and how hard they press. It never changes the player's own physics, so skill carries between moods. Adventure has one tuning and separate assists | EC-Z01 |
| **Death that teaches** | whatever killed you is named and, where it can be, shown. Dying to something you could not see is a defect | EC-G08, EC-C01 |
| **My time respected** | nothing unskippable twice. No timer that only makes you wait. No travel with nothing on the route | EC-G09, EC-G15 |
| **My progress safe** | a save never breaks. An old save loads into a new version and keeps what it earned | EC-G12 |

## Running well

| Expectation | What it means here | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| **Smooth first** | frame rate is protected ahead of prettiness. Quality settings change how it looks, never how it plays | EC-G11 |
| **Loads in seconds** | under ten to the title, under five to a run | EC-G11 |
| **Does not crash** | an evening's play without one. A crash loses at most the current round | EC-G11, EC-G12 |

## How to check

One sitting, start to finish, by someone who did not build it: install, start, play Swarm to a death, press Again, quit mid-run, come back, resume, switch to a controller, turn the motion settings down, start Adventure, dock, quit, continue. **Write down every moment of waiting, squinting or not knowing what to press.** Each one is a defect. This is the sitting that the owner's ten-minute card (18 §3) repeats every week.
