**D202 is not closed.** I fixed every below-floor text size I could reach inside `src/ui/**` and `styles/**`. The biggest remaining problem is the flight HUD shrinking on phones, and that needs a design call, not a font bump.

`★ Insight ─────────────────────────────────────`
- The repo's checker only reads literal `font-size: Npx` in a few stylesheets, so it misses sizes hidden in `var(--x, 11.5px)` fallbacks, `calc()`, `em`, and SVG text inside scaled drawings.
- A `max(12px, …)` wrapper fixes the first group in CSS alone. SVG text needs the size divided by the drawing's scale, so I pinned that to each dial's fixed width.
- At 12px, ring labels got longer than their arcs. Tightening letter-spacing fixed the overprint, which a font-size bump alone would not have.
`─────────────────────────────────────────────────`
