# 04 · A working world with people and recoverable missions

**Parents:** 398, 424–426, 441, 460, 467, 477, 478. Use existing traffic, NPC jobs, missions,
Chronicler, law, cargo and faction owners. Do not build a parallel life simulator.

## Minimum useful person

A recurring captain needs a stable identity, current job, ordinary route, knowledge boundary,
one remembered interaction and a later behavioral consequence. A portrait and 200 random lines
are not a substitute. Start with an existing working actor, not a new spawn family.

CLOUD-03 improves the existing WORK/Hail surface. The next larger slice should connect one rescue
or robbery to one return interaction through existing durable identity and memory. A rescued
captain might offer a job; a robbed captain might refuse or route around the player. Death remains
death. Do not respawn the same named captain to preserve content, and do not let a recycled numeric
entity ID inherit the captain's memory.

## Causal chain contract

Trace **physical fact → authorized state change → information available to a witness → response →
return consequence**. Each arrow has an existing owner and a testable condition. A public event is
not evidence that every NPC knows it. An actor absent from a streamed sector is not automatically
dead; a failed callback is not proof of an economic loss.

Select at most one immediate transient line for the event. Longer history belongs on requested
surfaces. A story callback should recognize a specific fact, not merely increase a generic respect
meter. Flavor can remain quiet, but mechanical consequences must be traceable to actual facts.

## Mission dossier, not mission wallpaper

Every authored job declares: who needs what; how the player learns of it; visible target and physical
verb; at least one alternative; clean/rough/failure outcomes; reward owner; successor eligibility;
interruption and Continue behavior. Extend the existing board, log and condition language.
No new mission engine, global event bus or competing objective tracker.

### Worked slice: the interrupted escort

A working freighter must reach its buyer. The clean outcome is an intact arrival. A rough outcome
can be a damaged but completed delivery. If the freighter is actually destroyed, existing loss
and cargo custody determine whether a recovery opportunity exists. If there is no recoverable
body or cargo, the game says so; it does not mint a convenient replacement crate.

CLOUD-09 implements exactly one successor for one existing escort template. The original mission
settles its failure once; the successor is a separate explicit offer bound to that loss. Recovering
a surviving manifest/payload pays only the successor reward and uses the existing cargo/economy
writers. A player-caused loss remains attributable and cannot become a profitable infinite loop.

A bigger multi-solution encounter can later add theft, negotiation through existing Hail, or a
lawful claim dispute. Do not claim dialogue choices that lack a real command path.

## Production workflow

1. Trace the chosen template from normal offer to acceptance, actor binding, outcome and settlement.
2. Sketch the outcome table before changing code. Include abandon, target lost, target merely
   unresident, accepted successor, recovery already consumed and Continue between settlement steps.
3. Implement the narrow catalog/owner extension. Preserve seeded generation and existing skip rules
   for story/onboarding/authored offers; do not attach random clauses to protected jobs.
4. Exercise each outcome through production handlers. Prove one visible discovery route and one
   settlement, then repeat/reload to prove no duplicate.
5. Review the actual copy against receipts. Present-tense labels must not claim arrival, delivery or
   repair before the physical condition holds.

## Cutscene and content policy

Use a brief existing camera/voice beat when the player needs orientation to a new relationship,
not to make an unsimulated event look real. Critical dangers remain perceivable; skip/cancel leaves
the same authoritative outcome. Reuse existing assets. Commission new art only after the mechanic
makes the missing silhouette, motion or role precise.

## Completion bar

A stranger can say who needed help, what they did, why it worked or failed, and what can happen next.
The same identities, claims and rewards survive normal Continue. One mission slice does not certify
the whole mission system, and 30 authored variations do not automatically equal 30 distinct jobs.
