# Tethys: the receiving copy and the open ring

## Diagnosis and direction

The published opening reaches the real six-unit sealed-alloy contract. Its destination
consumes those reserved units through the delivery owner and recodes the manifest as
HOUSEHOLD MINERALS. Scale 4 already exists, with an open measuring ring, authored
freight apron, inspection platform, tally and rack. It already reads an actual resting
pod and preserves its short plate reading beside its body stamp. The missing work was
the local connection between those facts, not another scale, crate, reward or quest.

Mira is already Tethys's Freight Seal Clerk. Her new conversation compares the delivery
copy with the old seal, distinguishes a changed description from a measurement, and
asks for a number the seal did not choose. The ordinary Bar now puts this choice on
her existing contact. She does not become another cargo, scale or mission owner.

## Player route

1. Carry the accepted reserved alloys to Tethys and dock through the receiving desk.
   A short or wholly missing load remains open. Mira reports the actual reserved units
   available and never calls a partial load delivered or repairs it with free stock.
2. After the genuine handoff, talk to Mira. She distinguishes the manifest recode from
   a weight reading and names current free cargo. An empty hold gets honest mining or
   Market advice; existing loose freight gets recovery advice, not a replacement pod.
3. Choose **Mark Scale 4. I keep the freight.** This plots the real global ring position
   through the world course intent, with autopilot off. It does not launch or move cargo.
4. Launch, choose an unreserved lot in Cargo, and Jettison. One unit can make a real pod.
   Catch it with the Line, tow slowly through the station-side approach, and brake the
   body inside the ring at 8 WU/s or below. The scale reads physical body mass, not units.
5. Release the Line and recover the pod through the ordinary proximity collector.
   A full hold may collect only part. The remaining live stock is still a real pod.
6. Return to Mira. She reads only retained paired measurements. Live weighed pods are
   reported using remaining salvage-pool units. No live pod is not proof of recovery:
   destruction, collection and departure are not collapsed into a made-up success.
7. Furnace Detail is the actual next contract. If already accepted, Mira says so and
   can track that exact mission in Mission Log. The weighing never releases 47-A's
   withheld payment. A second different-load comparison is optional, not a new gate.

## Taste and ownership

The scene uses the existing clerk, Bar controls, course marker, cargo panel, Line,
scale furniture and physical collection. Mira's voice is short and commercial; her
interest is the difference between a seal and evidence. Never invent an alloy crate
after delivery, forecast a weight from cargo units, grant money for a conversation,
call absent freight recovered, or present a signed mission as a new offer.

The new presenter reads canonical state and emits only existing course/tracking/screen
intents after an explicit choice. Choice identity binds seed, current assignment,
free stock, pod identity/generation, retained measurements and Furnace acceptance.
The Bar rejects hidden, undocked and stale choices, shows the full accounting without
typewriter delay, and clears old answers at save lifecycle/reopen boundaries.

its choice handler stored an answer, restoring stale text on reopening. The Mira-only
dispatch now checks the current visit epoch, visibility, state, station, contact and
choice before and after synchronous intents. Added regressions cover actual Furnace

UI labels, accessibility, 55 screen imports and product-file syntax checks passed.
These results do not resolve the physical continuation limit described below.

and one first-comparison invitation in the existing scale contact. No new registry,
