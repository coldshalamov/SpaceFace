# The Registry civilian holds for an actual choice

## Diagnosis and chosen road

The earned Tethys checkpoint contains the active Registry Writ, 158 credits, 10 debt,
and four fuel. On the exact parent `653cf18d50b957956fe959b8f498c67875f009f1`, the
ordinary scan succeeds, but the civilian's inherited swarmer attack routine drives
him into a rock before the player can reach Line range. At simulation time
2604.883333, Elroy 228 dies in a terrain collision, 252.965 WU from the player.
The kill producer names Elroy himself as killer and `playerCaused=false`. The player
has not fired or latched him. This is neither force nor custody.

This is an authoring/ownership error, not a reason to replace the AI or physics
engine. The writ says the marked civilian holds station for inspection; generic
bounty stamping instead supplies an attack run and a player target. Reusing the
existing passive, hold-fire and hail-hold channels for the authenticated Registry
slot is the smallest coherent fix. Ordinary accepted bounties retain their pursuit.
There is no immunity, health grant, collision removal, teleport, resize, weakened
custody predicate or new asset. Native physics can still pull and damage the hull.

## Bounded implementation

`missionSpawningOps` checks the active Registry contract, Charon destination, Elroy
role, slot zero, ship life, and every present mission, stable-record, identity-key
and sector field before generic stamping can repair them. The mission owner may
stamp an unclaimed fresh civilian with exact Elroy person/role fields, preserving
the existing authoring contract. Explicit foreign provenance is rejected untouched,
so repeated stamping cannot manufacture eligibility. A restored world record may
regain either or both missing story fields only after its pre-existing exact durable
identity proof; present person/role fields must match. Conflicting, foreign and
non-ship candidates do not receive the hold.
The existing AI authority suppresses autonomous offense/propulsion; the hull remains
an ordinary dynamic body. Existing mission/world owners still perform custody,
force settlement, payment, cleanup and persistence. No other production file changes.

This is a three-path isolated candidate: one production file, one new test and this
report. The dirty shared checkout is not an execution source. All other repository
imports and source reads resolve from the verified parent overlay plus immutable
base objects. Nothing is published, merged or deployed by this work.

## Open external-loss defect

The failed parent branch is preserved separately and is not repaired by the successful
replay. Independent inspection confirms that after Elroy's terrain death, the parent
adopts/replaces the mission target despite its destroyed durable record. Its final
save already contains a live replacement, and a separate title Continue keeps that
replacement alive for twenty simulated seconds while the durable record remains
destroyed. No reward or legitimate player outcome accompanies this resurrection.

That external-loss lifecycle is still an open blocker. This change prevents the
observed wrong offensive stance, but does not make Elroy invulnerable or claim to
solve every possible collision/third-party death. Correct future work needs an
explicit authored loss/recovery decision and terminal-record admission guards. It
must not loosen the player-force predicate, pay custody for a dead hull, silently
repost a new Elroy or fabricate campaign advancement. The held civilian's successful
custody and two-Continue evidence must not be used to claim terminal-loss coverage.
