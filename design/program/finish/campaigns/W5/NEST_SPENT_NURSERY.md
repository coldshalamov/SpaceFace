couplings retain their two-second exposure windows. After the sixth actual admission, both
empty nursery coupling assemblies remain exposed. A Class I pilot who spent the opening
watching, catching a Wasp or managing flight has not permanently missed the taught return
option. The existing bounded bay indicators and scan label read the real state.

combat death owner remain necessary. No proximity credit, distant marked death, elapsed time,
new reward, cargo, progression, health or weakened mass/rating rule is introduced. Guns and
living withdrawal remain alternatives. Killing all available Wasps does not manufacture three
replacement returns. Standard/random and legacy broods retain their existing exposure cadence.

opponent about its own destruction. Location-specific narration is restricted to the compact
authored visit. A failed launch says that remaining brood is still aboard and that no new
exposure window happened; any already-torn flank remains exposed. The final-launch read says
that the surviving Wasps remain dangerous. Journal guidance distinguishes a spent nursery from
cycling bays. Ordinary carrier destruction records DESTROYED, not the distinct GUTTED result.

Native play also exposed an inherited Continue defect. Generic persistence can restore moving
Wasps before the encounter director's next scheduled tick. The old route rejected those
already-advanced poses, leaving the carrier absent. The fix adopts only an already-validated
Wasp with matching encounter/shape/sector/fingerprint/model provenance, a matching dynamic
physical recipe, and finite motion. It never rewinds its position, rotation, velocity or damage.
A moved Wasp remains a real obstacle for every missing actor's clearance check. Missing/fake
provenance, malformed motion, duplicate identity and incompatible mass/radius/variant still fail
closed. Moved carrier/baffle assemblies remain rejected. No second cast or pacing charge is added.
