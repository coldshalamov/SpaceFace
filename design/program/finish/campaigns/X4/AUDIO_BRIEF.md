# X4 audio brief — what "good" sounds like

**The bar every sound and every stem is judged against.** One page, written before the pass, used by
the independent reviewer.

## The game's sound in one sentence

**A busy industrial machine you are allowed to hit.** Weight where mass is, bright where energy is,
and silence that means something.

## References — study the quality, never copy

| Game | The quality |
|---|---|
| **Homeworld / Sins of a Solar Empire** | space as a quiet room; a score that arrives, breathes, and gets out of the fight |
| **Freespace 2** | weapon and impact layers that tell you what hit what without a HUD |
| **Hades** | a combat loop that changes gear on the phrase, not on a timer |
| **Burnout / Wreckfest** | impact mass: a heavy hit is low, short and physical, not a bright tick |
| **Alien: Isolation** | tension built from room tone and distance, not a stinger every 4 s |

## Always

- **One score, five states.** Exploration (A), tension (B), combat (C), station (D), boss (E). Each
  state has its own authored body; the transition lands on a bar or phrase boundary.
- **The boss is a different piece.** Not the combat loop louder: lower, slower harmony, more space
  between hits, a choir/brass weight the fight does not have.
- **Mass is low and short; energy is bright and directional.** A hull hit has a low thump; a shield
  hit is a bright snap; a rock hit is grit; a station hit is metal.
- **Three variants at the top.** The 30 most frequent events rotate at least three bodies (or a body
  plus two authored pitch/form variants), so a 60 s fight does not machine-gun one sample.
- **Distance and occlusion are audible.** A fight across the sector is quieter and duller than one
  at the nose.
- **Silence is a tool.** After a big kill, leave a hole before the next layer.

## Never

- A 4-bar loop over a 90 s fight with no development.
- The station theme under a boss (the current defect).
- A screen-wide "orchestral hit" on every event; stingers are for story beats.
- A weapon that sounds the same as another weapon's body; each weapon family keeps its own voice.
- A sound that fires on an event the player did not cause and reads as reward (S3's fair-credit rule
  applies to audio too).
- Music that ducks the voice line into inaudibility; the voice wins.

## Budgets

- Music bodies: 16 s loops per state, 5 states, mono 32 kHz WAV, regenerated byte-identically.
- Stems: ≤ 6 layers per state, crossfade 0.25–3 s by phase urgency.
- SFX: the top 30 events, 3 variants each, ≤ 2.6 s per body, tier 0/1 residency.
- No per-frame audio work at rest; the sample runtime is event/promise driven.

## How a sound is accepted

1. The event is on the top-30 table or a named story beat.
2. It has ≥ 3 variants (or a written exception).
3. It is heard on the ordinary route at the gameplay camera, in a 10-minute recording, against this
   brief.
4. One independent reviewer names what each of the 30 events is, blind, from the recording.
