# A dropped claim must not leave the hulk permanently pinned

## Continuity outcome
The prior increment retained an actual player-attached salvage haul through a fresh native-world restore, but review identified an orphan case: a malformed or evicted dispute row could disappear without calling its normal close path, leaving the scene's persistent flag on the hulk forever. This follow-through keeps the physical wreck but releases demonstrably abandoned scene-owned retention. It neither deletes nor teleports the hulk and never pays a reward.

The ordinary salvage owner may subsequently file a new claim if its usual conditions apply; this helper does not create one.
