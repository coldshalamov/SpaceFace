WHAT ALREADY EXISTS (do not rebuild; extend)
1. Predictive camera. src/render/cameraGlide.js + camera.js + renderer.js `cameraRoofAt`.
   The camera sweeps ~3 s of the path ahead, and dollies UP AND BACK along its own view ray, easing
   in before contact, holding while over the object, and settling back to the set zoom afterward.
   Its last-resort clamp guarantees it is never inside a solid. Tests: test/camera-glide*.test.mjs.
   In-game witness: `node scripts/probe-camera-clearance.mjs --headless` (records every frame;
   insideFrames must be 0, hardClampFrames ~0).
2. Measured truth. src/data/modelTruthCensus.json (196 rows, written ONLY by
   scripts/model-truth-census.mjs) holds each model's real footprint, height, and a proposed
   collision skin. Fixed bodies (stations, places, rocks) already use the skins; dynamic bodies
   (ships, drones, wrecks, pods, payloads) still use one capsule or ball.
3. The scoreboard. `node scripts/check-solid-truth.mjs` compares every live collider to its
   model. At handoff: 129/196 clean; FAT 33, SHORT 39, THIN 5, LONG 2, COARSE 10. Your goal is
   `node scripts/check-solid-truth.mjs --strict` exiting 0. Report writes to .devshots/solid-truth/.
   The thresholds are in the script; do not loosen them to pass.
