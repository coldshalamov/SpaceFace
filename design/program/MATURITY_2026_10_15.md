# SpaceFace maturity campaign — 15 October 2026

## Goal

Make ordinary play dependable, smooth, readable and complete: bodies load on time, ship and
camera motion remain coherent, controls and transactions recover correctly, and an interrupted
operation leaves a useful way forward. Preserve authored visuals and default quality. Continue
the owner's performance target: three times fewer hitches, lower worst-frame delays, and FPS
toward the display limit, measured on actual game motion rather than the HUD spinner.

This implements [build_map §1.8](../../build_map.md#18-production-maturity-debugging-and-polish)
through the existing finish lanes. It is an execution plan, not a second defect ledger or queue.
Unfixed discovered defects belong in [the existing demo ledger](DEMO_READINESS_2026-09-20.md#6-the-demo-defect-ledger--everything-a-demo-player-will-hit-live-opened-2026-09-22).

## Analysis and decisions

Choose cause-driven repair across player workflows, followed by representative route checks
and the full existing matrix. A feature-first expansion would preserve the broken experience;
an unbounded review campaign would delay the repairs. Build additional machinery only for a
demonstrated ownership, cancellation, scheduling, loading or testability gap.

Review existing evidence when its owner has not changed. Do not repeat a completed inquiry
for confidence. A new failure, conflicting result or changed production owner reopens only the
affected check. Prior three successive clean scoped polish reviews remain bounded coverage;
the owner's newer whole-game research request expands the workflow coverage below.
