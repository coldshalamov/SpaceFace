## Implementation sequence

1. Choose an existing industrial input and two existing consumers in one reachable region.
2. Generate bounded offers from their actual unmet needs and available freight; accepting a physical delivery reduces the corresponding need and may invalidate or alter the competing offer.
3. Reuse present workers and the existing request budget; the region responds through activity/service changes, not a newly spawned crowd or fabricated permanent emergency.
