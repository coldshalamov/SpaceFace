## Implementation sequence

1. At each extraction commit, distinguish material freed from material actually accepted by cargo.
2. Preserve the unaccepted material in the existing rock/chunk/remaining-yield representation appropriate to that verb; do not subtract it from the rock and then silently discard it.
3. Let ordinary collection, jettison and return recover the remainder, while keeping field depletion and cargo mass caches consistent.

## Acceptance and counterexamples

1. Freed material equals accepted cargo plus recoverable remainder plus explicitly caused destruction.
2. Full-hold retries cannot duplicate yield or reset depletion.
3. Partial acceptance updates mass/volume once and does not block on mass as a second capacity.
