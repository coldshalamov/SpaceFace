## Implementation sequence

1. Choose an existing accepted damage-mark family with a visible positional purpose. Store its presentation anchor in the body's proper local frame while keeping damage authoritative in combat.
2. On supported LOD/material rebinding, project the same accepted mark to the legitimate corresponding hull surface or retain its current allowed approximation, never a world-floating card.
3. Dispose/transfer mark resources under the existing hull lifecycle without discarding persistent gameplay scars or contaminating another ship that reuses a pooled mesh.
