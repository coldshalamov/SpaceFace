## Implementation sequence

1. Select existing timed contracts and derive the displayed time basis from their canonical deadline and state.simTime.
2. At offer/acceptance, distinguish a known physically plausible route from an unknown estimate, accounting for the chosen ship's ordinary travel capability and required service stops.
3. Preserve current pause/Continue semantics and let deadlines expire through missions, never through wall-clock UI timers; refusal/expiry must leave a coherent cargo disposition.
