## Implementation sequence

1. Choose a retreat corridor from currently perceived navigable space and keep it for a bounded commitment unless physically invalidated.
2. Assign one eligible covering member through the existing squad decision; it pressures the pursuer while the wounded member obeys normal inertia, clearance and escape rules.
3. End cover when the retreat succeeds, the coverer is disabled or the corridor becomes invalid; reacquire a current plan rather than preserving a stale target.
