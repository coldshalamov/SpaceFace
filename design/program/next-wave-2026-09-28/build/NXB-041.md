## Implementation sequence

1. Separate direct observation, reported observation and inference using the existing witness/legal record rather than a new reputation engine.
2. Retain the stable subject/cause and permitted observation time/location; evidence may become stale or contradicted without deleting a settled historical fact.
3. A lawful response consults the current admissible evidence in its jurisdiction and uses proportionate existing behavior, not knowledge of every world event.
