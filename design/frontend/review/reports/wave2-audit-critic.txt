WAVE 2 AUDIT CRITIC — five ORRERY surfaces vs the 8.0 bar
Critic: wave2 audit critic. Stills opened with own eyes (1920 + 1280 each).
Scoring law: design/frontend/review/CRITIC_BRIEF.md. PASS = overall >= 8.0 AND no axis < 7.0
AND owner (a) band-weight/generic-element test AND owner (b) signature interaction.
Axes: Composition / Hierarchy / Legibility / ORRERY fidelity / Craft-detail / Interaction clarity.
Palette law checked on every surface: one amber Hand + one amber Lamp Key; bone rgb(236 230 216)
rest light; ice rgb(223 238 255) motion-only; red threat-only; 4.5:1; >=12px; reduced-motion keeps
info; no key-cap chrome (dotted words current law); bands >=2:1 vs glass or they are wires.

Sources: src/ui/station/screens/shipworks.js (+ styles/station.css), src/ui/screens/range.js,
src/ui/screens/automationPanel.js, src/ui/screens/replay.js, src/ui/screens/clips.js.
Px below are in the named shot's own pixels. 1280 shots are 1280x720, 1920 shots are 1920x1080.

1. SHIP (my ship / Hitch fit screen) — 6.9 — FAIL (fixes owed, no rebuild)
================================================================================
Axes: C 7.5 / H 7.5 / L 6.5 / O 6.0 / D 7.0 / I 7.0.
1920 is the closest thing in Wave 2 to an A-list screen: display title, handling prose, hull with
leader callouts, stat band, hero readings, right systems rail. It fails on 1280 breakage plus
ORRERY-fidelity gaps, not on ambition.

Verified with own eyes (1280): CONFIRMED slot-label/prose overlap — the handling prose
(x~300-460, y~95-270) collides with the Weapon/Cargo/Mining/Utility chips (x~425-510, y~95-275);
glyphs touch. CONFIRMED bottom stat rows pushed out of view — Agility y~580 and Inertia y~628
visible, "Top speed 439" half-clipped at y~672-690, Brake + BUILDS + SAVE FIT below the 720 fold.
Right-rail helper ("Choose a system...") also clipped mid-line at y~625-640.

Owner (a) FAIL: (1) the seven slot chips (1920 x~780-1090, y~335-520) are black filled boxes with
white text — generic chips, not leader callouts; (2) the systems wheel (1920 x~1610-1770,
y~130-235) is a near-black disc with ~9px labels — invisible instrument, hairline-only; (3) the
handling bars (1920 x~355-580, y~728-880) are thin rules whose lit portion must be measured at
>=2:1 vs glass or thickened — eyeball says the unlit track passes as scale, the fill is fine, but
the wheel fails outright. Owner (b) HALF: signature is named — "pick a slot on the hull, preview
compatible hardware" (SELECT A SLOT strip) — and it IS distinct per-screen, but the still shows no
selection state, no bead/arm on the chosen slot, no preview delta; not yet memorable.

Palette: amber is scattered, not law-abiding — ACTIVE (top-left), TOP SPEED label, S1/S2 region,
wheel labels? No single Hand arm and no single amber Lamp Key identifiable. SAVE FIT (1920
x~315-455, y~915-950) and DOCK TO EXTEND (right rail foot) are dark chips; one of them must become
the Lamp Key. No red seen (good — nothing here is threat). Bone/ice discipline OK on readings.

RANKED FIXES (shipworks.js / station.css):
S1. 1280 overlap (P0, layout): prose column vs slot chips, 1280 x~300-510 y~95-275. Give the prose
    max-width ~200px ending by x~300 AND clamp the callout stack to x>=560 at <=1366 widths (or move
    left-stack chips right of the hull centerline). Re-shoot 1280: zero glyph intersections.
S2. 1280 fold (P0): stat block 1280 y~560-720+. Compress hero readings + handling bars into a
    two-column band (Agility/Inertia | Top speed/Brake side by side) so Brake + SAVE FIT sit above

S3. Slot chips -> Leader Callouts (ORRERY #7): unbox the seven chips — bone label + OPEN/size line

S4. Systems wheel: relight or remove. Either give it a >=2:1 bone band + tick ring + amber Hand to

S5. Lamp Key: make SAVE FIT (or DOCK TO EXTEND if that is the commit verb) the ONE amber Lamp Key

S6. Right rail rows (1920 x~1545-1835, y~330-690): add the ladder spine + ticks; chosen row gets
    bead + arm. Kill the hairline row dividers if they read as box edges.
S7. Contrast/type sweep: wheel labels and "0/1 fitted" sub-lines are ~9-11px — raise to >=12px and
    run ui-contrast; every reading >=4.5:1 on composited pixels.
Memorable if: selecting a slot swung an amber arm from the wheel to the hull point and the stat
ghosts showed the delta live — nothing here attempts that yet.

--- BUILD BRIEF: REPLAY — the instrument is the TAPE ---
Hero instrument: a 30-second Tracing-Beam timeline — the input tape drawn as a horizontal beam
(scale with tick marks every 5s, minor ticks every 1s) across the lower third, floating over the
held world (photo-mode presentation, HUD hidden, world DOF). Event ticks on the scale mark what
the tape holds: stunt tricks (named glyph tick), kills (red tick — threat channel's only other
use, earned), damage taken, boost windows. The playhead IS the Hand: an amber arm riding the scale
with spring overshoot when scrubbed or when playback crosses an event.
Readings: elapsed/total as thin Archivo Counter numerals (96px class, bone) top-right; seed +
tick count as small codes (Spline Sans Mono, the one mono allowance) beneath.
Transport: PLAY/PAUSE is the Lamp Key (amber, 45° cut) centered under the tape; step-frame and
speed (1x/0.25x) are dotted words flanking it; Exit is a dotted word top-left, never bare amber
text. Scrub = drag the playhead along the scale (pointer) or arrow keys; gamepad shoulder steps
between event ticks — THAT is the signature: "ride the tape," no other screen scrubs time.
Empty state (the shot we have): the SAME instrument, dormant — unlit bone scale with ghost tick
marks, playhead parked at 0, one line of copy where events will be, and the Lamp Key reading
FLY TO RECORD (commits to closing + unpausing into flight). An empty Replay must still look like
an instrument asleep, not a title card. Never an empty black screen again.
Motion: arrival per ORRERY §3.5 (rings/scales tick in, numerals roll, Hand swings to playhead);
reduced-motion: full timeline rendered instantly, pulses stopped, seeks instant, all info kept.
Palette: bone scale + ticks, ONE amber playhead-Hand, ONE amber Lamp Key, ice ONLY in the playback
pulse travelling the beam, red ONLY on kill/damage ticks. Bands >=2:1. Type >=12px except the
mono codes (>=11px codes tolerated, keep 12). Contrast >=4.5:1 on composited pixels.
Builder files: src/ui/screens/replay.js view + its stylesheet, composing src/ui/orrery/
(Scale, Beam, Counter, Lamp Key, layout solver for event labels). Screenshot gate: populated tape
+ dormant tape, 1920 and 1280, no overlap, nothing below the fold.

  badges, borders, glyphs, labels) goes bone or ice-by-rule.

  not eyeballed; hairline-only = wire = owner-(a) fail.
- Reduced-motion: arrival choreography resolves instantly with all info kept; verify per screen.

Memorable-line summary: ship needs its slot-pick alive; range needs the run made visible; automation
needs the machine's pulse; replay needs the tape; clips needs the reel. None may ship as a title
card or a web form.
