4. BLOCKERS, IN ORDER (each with its smallest fix)
   1. (b) The signature is not wired. Code facts:
      - The tether pulse is an ambient 3.6 s loop from the key to THIS STATION. It rests at the key for 30% of the
        cycle.
      - The beam pulse is a separate 3.4 s loop from THIS STATION to the berth.
      - The two periods differ, so the pulses drift apart and never read as one run.
      - The hold exists only when collateral is at risk. The default contract (collateral None) is a plain tap.
      - Holding does not drive either pulse.
      Fix:
      a. Put ONE path in one mpath: the tether path, then the beam path, key to station to berth. Run one pulse
         along it.
      b. On press of ACCEPT, give every contract a hold: 450 ms with no collateral, 720 ms with collateral. The
         hold's progress drives a lit fill along that combined path: stroke-dashoffset from key to berth equals
         the hold fraction. About 45% of the run is the tether and 55% is the beam.
      c. Release early and the light retracts.
      d. On fire, the berth ring flashes to hot amber (#ffd98c, 180 ms), and the tether retracts into the YOURS
         row's TRACKED bead.
      e. Shoot a still at 50% hold with the fill head at THIS STATION.
   2. (a) The RISK scale is a wire and a stock slider.
      - Track: 1 px at 67 with a 20 shoulder (1920 row 547; 720 row 417, 68), a knob, and five hairline ticks.
      - Fix: draw the track as a band, 6 px at bone .27 (about 65 on 8) under a 1.5 px core.
      - Light the stretch from ROUTINE to the cursor at bone .6, and make the cursor a 3 x 14 px lit blade.
      - Better, and ORRERY-native: a 3-zone Arc Gauge.
   3. (a) The STANDING bar is a wire and a stock range slider.
      - Track: 1 px at 69 (row 588), with a 3 px white fill (244) from -3 to +4.
      - Fix: section 3.2 says standing is an arc. Use the library Arc Gauge with a ghost delta: failure -3 drawn
        dim, success +4 lit, bipolar about a centre tick. At minimum, the same band as RISK.
   4. Hierarchy: the Hand must be the heaviest line on the dial. Either change works:
      - Raise the beam's glow from 47-48 to about 110-120 (bloom stroke 12 px at amber about .45).
      - Drop the rest-ring cores from 133-174 to 95-105, keeping the 72-79 band (the weight the owner asked for).
   5. 1920 tether origin regression.
      - Code: kx = key right + 20*z, and z evaluates wrong at 1920. At 2560 the bead is 24 px off the key and at 720
        it is 19 px, but at 1920 it is 0-1 px.
      - Fix: clamp the 20 px offset to at least 16 css px at every scale. Then re-shoot the hold at 1920 to confirm
        the ring clears the bead.

   Also (not blocking):

   - Put the 1920 YOURS major at 726 and the 720 tracked tick at 559, off the series.
