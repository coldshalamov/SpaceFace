7. OWNER CRITERIA
   (a) Weight, not wire:
     TITLE: PASSES. The dial is a six-tier luminous instrument, not a hairline drawing: rim
       scale with Y140-173 ticks and a Y155 edge; bold 2.6px dots at Y115; three filled
       worlds with Y150 lit rims; sun core + halo; 8px blade with hot core and bloom; hub
       with amber disc. The bands alone measure below the 2:1 bar (Y28-47, ~1.2-1.5:1),
       but per the ng-r7 precedent (bands Y32-38 passed under bright edges + beads) the
       assembly carries weight everywhere. No generic elements: no cards, boxes, pills,
       stock sliders or buttons. T-C1/C2 lift the two faintest tiers as stretch.
     PAUSE: PASSES. Same dial, same finding; plus rows with halo light (not boxes), legends,
       and the brief as pure type over the world. Nothing generic; the 0% gauge is the one
       thin element (P-C1).
   (b) Signature interaction (reduced-motion safe, keyboard/gamepad reachable):
     TITLE: FAILS. The §4 signature — "the Hand follows the pointer round the dial; the rings
       answer with parallax" — is not implemented: the Hand moves only between verb stops on
       hover/focus (verified live both ways), and no parallax wiring exists. The stop-to-stop
       swing is the ORRERY-wide mechanism shared with Pause, not this screen's own; the idle
       attract (live replay behind the menu after 12 s) is Title-only but passive — not what
       the player DOES. T-S1 is the ask. What exists is reduced-motion safe (spring snaps,
       drift/arrival/trail off) and keyboard reachable (roving focus); gamepad rides the same
       focus path (assumed, not probed in the bench).
     PAUSE: PASSES. "Rows as stops: the Hand steps row to row, left/right along a row" is
       exactly what is built and was verified live (Down/Down/Right → MY SHIP, Hand on SHIP).
       It is this screen's alone (the only grouped dial), spring-driven with glint + legend
       light, reduced-motion safe (instant step, state preserved), keyboard reachable, hover
       unified with focus. Strong; §6 names what would make it memorable.
