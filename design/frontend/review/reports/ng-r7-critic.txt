4. RANKED FIXES
   1. The launch beat renders nothing (screenLayouts.js:877 against 905-909).
      Cause: the base bead rule sets `background:rgb(10 12 16) !important; box-shadow:... !important`.
      A CSS animation cannot override !important declarations, so `@keyframes ng-bead-lit`'s `to
      {background, box-shadow}` never shows.
      Measured: is-launching is set and the word reads "Launching...", but beads 2-4 keep
      background rgb(10,12,16) at 100, 200, 300, 400 and 500 ms after the click. In fd7-launch they are
      still hollow rings at 954, 1253 and 1552 x 930.
      Fix: drop !important from background and box-shadow in the rule at line 877, raising specificity
      instead if needed. Or animate a separate ::after overlay's opacity, which no !important rule
      touches.
      Check: at 500 ms after the click, bead 2's computed ::before background is rgb(252,249,240), and
      beads 3 and 4 are lit by 800 ms.
   2. The yard moves 12 px on the first touch and stays off the ring's axis.
      Measured at y 780, the rim runs 896-1625 at rest (centre 1260.5, the same as the stat ring's centre,
      about 1259). Mid-drag and after the Pelican pick it runs 908-1637 (centre 1272.5, 13 px right of
      the ring), and the bead moves with it.
      Fix: anchor the yard's centre to the ring's centre, not to a box that changes after the first
      interaction.
      Check: the rim's centre stays within 1 px of 1259.5 at rest, mid-drag and after every pick.
   3. The form still has a 270 px empty band at 1920 (y 600-870, below the seed note) and 338 px at 2560.
      Fix: move the dial and seed group down so the column's air is split above and below it, or give
      the column a fixed rhythm (dial centred in 330-760).
      Check: the largest empty band in the form is at most 160 px @1920 and @2560.
   4. @1280, "way." is alone on the blurb's third line (461-486 x 155-167).
      Fix: text-wrap:pretty on the hull blurb (.orr-ng-caption sentence).
      Check: the last line holds at least two words.
   5. The Pelican poster to hero grade. This is an asset job, recorded. Detail density is .092 against
      the Hitch's .376; the target is at least .28.
