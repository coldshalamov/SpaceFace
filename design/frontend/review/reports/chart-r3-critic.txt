8. THE ONE STRUCTURAL CHANGE TO LAND FIRST
   Give SYSTEM a non-linear radial scale. Plot marks at r' = R_gate * sqrt(r / r_gate), or a log scale,
   and mark it on the ring's ticks so the dial stays honest.
   - The inner zone, where the ship, goal, station and fields live, opens to roughly 55-60% of the dial
     radius instead of ~25%.
   - The gates stay on the gate ring.
   The knot then has room, so the placer seats every name against its own mark at 1280-2560 without
   crossed leaders (fix 1). The drag reading can dock inside the dial instead of breaking the gate ring
   (fix 2). The empty outer band of the dial gets used. A label rule alone cannot do this, because the
   marks really are within 24 px of each other at the dial's linear scale.
   Cost: the radius no longer reads as linear distance. The reading's WU figure and the gate-ring label
   become the honest distance cues, so say "radial scale: sqrt" on the ring beside "GATE RING 2870 WU".
   Blockers this round: fixes 1 (the knot) and 2 (the SYSTEM reading dock). Fixes 3-9 are polish.
