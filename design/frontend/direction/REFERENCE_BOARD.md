## Controller judgment

**Primary reference: Hardspace: Shipbreaker.** Work, debt and dangerous machinery belong in the interface, but the ship and the decision must stay larger than the terminal chrome. **Composition reference: Control.** Borrow the confidence of a large title and an image with breathing room; retain a dense, readable register for prices and equipment.

Hades supplies warmth and consequence-rich choices. Into the Breach supplies the test for every permanent mark: does it tell the player something actionable? Destiny supplies object scale for THE SHIP. Death Stranding supplies a brief, satisfying delivery receipt. Persona’s constant motion and HighFleet’s cockpit density would compete with our flight field; use their confidence, not their full visual language. NieR and Alan Wake inform restrained hierarchy and evidence presentation, rather than the game’s overall mood.
