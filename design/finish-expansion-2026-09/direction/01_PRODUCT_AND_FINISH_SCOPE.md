# Product direction and a finite finish scope

## Preserve the game already hiding in the code

The owner-defined fantasy is understanding how a busy local universe moves, then using that understanding to do surprising things. Massline, physics-earned speed, impulse, cargo, terrain and witnesses belong to the same situation. A meaningful miss can create a better story. The game is personally piloted, fast and materially colorful; its industrial economy supplies choices and toys rather than replacing flight with administration. [S01, S12, S15]

The player should be able to say: “I shoved the pirate into the cargo, rescued the pilot, stole the wrong crate, and used the wreck to escape.” That sentence crosses combat, physics, law, economy and story without requiring five separate minigames. This is the product's advantage. It does not need the largest galaxy, the most realistic fluid simulation, or the highest equipment count.

Do not normalize everything into a conventional shooter. Guns remain a viable straightforward solution, but light hulls can become ammunition, heavy hulls can become moving terrain, and operators can alter the room. Equally, do not make every interaction require expert tether work: a gun-first, work-first or exploration-first pilot must have a coherent route to competence.

## Three layers, one game

**Human work and conflict:** readable mass, manufactured machines, traffic, trade, industrial work, witnesses, imperfect decisions. This layer carries the first session and remains valuable late.

**Alien biology:** one Vethari-linked fungus exploiting many hosts and structures, lossy coordination and behavioral residues, not universal intelligence or magical nanotechnology. It introduces contact, adhesion, pressure, biological stimuli and ecological consequences. Geographic contamination and what the player knows are separate facts. [S23]

**Ancient machinery:** surviving Verge-Layer procedures, exact sparse construction, limited authority and extraordinary competence. The first improvement is a machine doing a real job with a real object. Ancient presence does not require another enemy faction. The creators and Vethari apex remain indirect. [S17, S23, S24]

This manifest is not satisfied by filling tables. The selected content has to work together. The actual number of retained mission variants should follow distinct decisions and current campaign needs, not a planner's round number.

## Four scope dispositions

**Core finish:** a supported present feature or essential product promise is broken, thin or disconnected. Repair, deepen or connect it.

**Selected expansion:** an additional capability materially improves a retained loop and can be completed with acquisition, art, sound, counterplay and save support. Admit a small representative slice first.

**Optional reservoir:** attractive concepts whose dependencies or value are not yet proved—large membranes, nonlocal momentum routing, galaxy-scale succession, huge new fauna populations, complicated robotic jurisdictions. Keep the idea; do not make the finish depend on it.

**Pre-release preparation:** store assets, distribution-specific compliance, public telemetry services, trailer and marketing. Plan these, but current root policy parks them until a release is being prepared. Reliability and packaging parity remain development work. [S02]

## Refusals that protect quality

No engine rewrite to pursue prestige. No separate alien simulator. No camera-facing glow discs impersonating designed matter. No new UI aesthetic. No full walking interiors, multiplayer economy, live-service calendar, procedural galaxy expansion or universal machine-language simulator on the core path. No compulsory Vethari boss. No higher-HP difficulty treadmill. No loss of long bright trails or authored assets to disguise a performance bug.

These are scope decisions, not claims those ideas could never work. A later prototype may earn an exception by demonstrating specific value without destroying the current game.

## Cut rules

Merge a proposed mission with an existing one when the physical question, preparation and consequence are the same. Cut an item when it is another delivery mechanism for an already-complete verb with no new decision. Defer a foundation when fewer than three admitted outcomes need it and a local extension is cheaper. Cut a visual detail if it increases noise but not recognizable form; do not cut the form itself. Drop a proposed metric when agents can satisfy it while making the game worse.

Finishing means the retained whole is excellent and complete. It does not mean the universe has run out of ideas.
