# SFQ-P16 — Precursor geometry as a small coherent force vocabulary

Ancient engineering creates comprehensible spatial rules that can be combined with human tools and biological matter.

**Disposition:** supporting source program, not an independently active queue. **Milestone band:** 5. **Existing owners to search:** Existing fields and machine-site owners; proposals, not established physics.

## Baseline and uncertainty

Radial/moving fields and machine suppression are already present in different owners. Nonradial/selective effects are proposed extensions; their visual geometry must not claim a force region or conservation law the simulation does not implement.
