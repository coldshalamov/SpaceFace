# Whole-game programs

| Program | Area | Promise |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [SFQ-P01](01-integration.md) | Truth, integration, and the first playable reference | Agents improve the game that actually runs, not a second implementation or a historical description. |
| [SFQ-P02](02-flight.md) | The hand: nimble control without stealing momentum | Every deliberate input produces a trustworthy physical response; earned speed and readable control survive combat. |
| [SFQ-P03](03-massline.md) | Massline, contact, and terrain as the signature toy | One simple relationship between two bodies produces readable swings, theft, rescue and violent collisions. |
| [SFQ-P04](04-combat.md) | Weapons, states, and combinatorial combat | Every weapon changes a physical situation; shooting remains fun and physical setups expand possibilities rather than replace it. |
| [SFQ-P05](05-ai.md) | Readable adversaries and a living encounter director | Enemies reveal intentions the player can disrupt; civilians and specialists share the same physical world. |
| [SFQ-P06](06-crucible.md) | Crucible: the repeatable physics showcase | A run escalates from readable play to spectacular earned chaos, with fast retries and genuinely different builds. |
| [SFQ-P07](07-adventure.md) | Adventure: first-hour trust and a complete campaign spine | Personal flight, an owned ship and persistent consequences carry the player from ordinary work into deeper strangeness. |
| [SFQ-P08](08-missions.md) | Mission craft and authored world-site interactions | Contracts are physical situations with multiple solutions, not interchangeable errands with different nouns. |
| [SFQ-P09](09-economy.md) | An economy the pilot can understand and physically influence | A contract, trade or theft changes an intelligible local chain of goods, money and opportunity. |
| [SFQ-P10](10-industry.md) | Mining, salvage and construction as tactile work | Work in the belt is an interesting physical activity, not a progress bar financing the interesting game. |
| [SFQ-P11](11-regions.md) | Regions, routes and places with a physical identity | Players remember places by how they fly, work and change—not just their skybox color. |
| [SFQ-P12](12-people-law.md) | People, law and memorable consequences | The world understands the difference between a rescue, a mistake and a crime, and gives the pilot something to do about it. |
| [SFQ-P13](13-ecology.md) | Alien ecology that shares the physical world | Animals have motives, anatomy and consequences without becoming a second human combat faction. |
| [SFQ-P14](14-alien-matter.md) | Xenotechnology: useful matter with strange behavior | Goo, pressure organs and elastic tissue change the fight through honest contact and force—not neon status icons. |
| [SFQ-P15](15-machines.md) | Ancient machines that perform consequential work | Verge-Layer machines are surviving institutions whose procedures visibly move, preserve and transform the world. |
| [SFQ-P16](16-precursor-fields.md) | Precursor geometry as a small coherent force vocabulary | Ancient engineering creates comprehensible spatial rules that can be combined with human tools and biological matter. |
| [SFQ-P17](17-research-regional-change.md) | Discovery, behavioral archaeology and bounded regional change | Learning changes the player’s options; physical events change the world; the two must never be conflated. |
| [SFQ-P18](18-art.md) | Authored models, spaces and animation at the gameplay camera | Every important object has a strong silhouette, coherent material and visible function in the real game. |
| [SFQ-P19](19-vfx.md) | Force-readable VFX with materially distinct lifecycles | The player sees where force began, what it acted on, and what continued after the source stopped. |
| [SFQ-P20](20-audio.md) | Sound, music and silence as mechanical information | Without looking at a meter, the pilot can hear mass, load, danger and release. |
| [SFQ-P21](21-interface.md) | ORRERY as a complete usable instrument | The interface makes the game’s choices legible while looking like the same authored instrument everywhere. |
| [SFQ-P22](22-performance.md) | Smoothness, residency and predictable work | Full-quality play remains responsive through cold use, dense action, travel and long sessions. |
| [SFQ-P23](23-reliability.md) | Save integrity, input access and product completeness | The game preserves a player’s progress, explains failures, and stays usable across ordinary devices and sessions. |
| [SFQ-P24](24-convergence.md) | A finite finish line and the final convergence campaign | One coherent game is finished to a defensible bar; optional ambition remains available without holding it hostage. |
