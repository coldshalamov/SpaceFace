# SFQ-P17 — Discovery, behavioral archaeology and bounded regional change

Learning changes the player’s options; physical events change the world; the two must never be conflated.

**Disposition:** supporting source program, not an independently active queue. **Milestone band:** 5. **Existing owners to search:** AE contamination/revelation / evidence / current sector simulation.

## Baseline and uncertainty

The original brief separates geographic contamination from revelation and asks for lossy shared-memory, progressive strangeness and machine contrast. PR 170 provides initial state/evidence; a full evolving galaxy is neither verified nor necessary.
