# F31 — Crucible rhythm and outcome

**Disposition:** CORE. This is a VFX/audio-family brief, not delivered media or proof the current family is deficient. Read the current VFX technique and lifecycle standards and reuse the selected production owner. [S01, S21]

## Authoritative signal

Current round, earned breather, draft and final run result.

This is a semantic data requirement, not a fabricated event/API name. The implementation agent must find the live receipt/state owner; add a narrowly required receipt there only if genuinely absent. Renderer and audio never decide gameplay success.

## Onset

A round-change cue follows the actual threat state; no early victory while enemies remain.

## Sustained form and motion

Shop/results use current ORRERY and actual build/seed with controlled motion; retained world policy explicit.

## Source-off and aftermath

Return to action neutralizes held inputs and clears interface-only effects.

## Sound design

Clear combat/recovery contrast without restarting music for trivial state changes.

Use current mix and voice-priority owners. Audition in context and at controlled loudness; release every loop with its emitter. Captions/visual alternatives preserve critical information without inventing source precision.

## Target review cases

Fast retry, pause at round end, attached wreck, final kill offscreen and controller focus.

## Scene consumers

[M19](../missions/M19.md), [M20](../missions/M20.md), [M36](../missions/M36.md)

## Non-negotiable production tests

Normal gameplay camera, moving and stationary sources, full-density combination, bloom off/on, pause/resume, origin rebase, target/source death, pool reuse and reduced-motion/flash modes. Appearance may be rich, but cosmetic detail cannot extend damage/force or consume sim RNG.

A designed effect has distinct preparation, active change and cooling, not the same grow-spin-shrink loop. Primary form is substantial material/geometry appropriate to the class; a soft card or particle glitter is not the object. Preserve the solid world and the user's bright physical identity. Profile marginal cost before multiplying actors. See [production workflow](../workflows/06_ART_AND_EFFECT_PRODUCTION.md).
