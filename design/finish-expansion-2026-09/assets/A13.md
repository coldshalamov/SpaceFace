# A13 — Infestation filament and seam modules

**Family:** fungus modular dressing. **Disposition:** CORE alien art grammar.

This is a model-family production brief, not a missing-asset assertion or an actual mesh. Resolve existing IDs/source assets and reuse what already works. Consult current Forge/graphics instructions, asset manifests and nearest nested policy. [S01, S22, S25]

## Silhouette at the real camera

Sheets bridge actual seams with thicker trunks and thinner secondary branches.

## Visible function

Show connected occupation and likely energy path rather than random tentacle decoration.

## Motion and articulation

Propagation travels along a seeded topology; quiet microdeformation does not imply force.

## Collision, sockets and authority

Cosmetic by default; promote only a named cuttable/attachable trunk with a coarse proxy.

Declare source axes, scale, pivot, attachment/work sockets, collision policy and state identity. Use current exporter/runtime conventions; art must not create extra solver bodies by accident.

## Materials and light

Pale-red/white tissue, dark wet channels, sparse hot active edges; solid material readable without bloom.

## Required states

Trace, active, severed but living, dead/calcified.

## Scene consumers

[M21](../missions/M21.md), [M26](../missions/M26.md)

If no selected scene needs a family yet, it stays in the reservoir. Three concrete uses can justify a shared primitive; a speculative list is not enough.

## Production sequence

Inspect the current source and production render. Block the major forms and interaction geometry; test silhouette/value at chase scale beside existing assets. Add functional articulation and state hooks. Refine materials and selected details only after the form holds. Publish with the existing pipeline, required LOD/proxies and bounded resource ownership.

## Acceptance

Inspect chase/close and relevant oblique/reverse views, a moving ordinary scene, cold production loading and LOD transition. No floating parts, false open passage, displaced nozzle, hollow back, noisy surface or debug-only asset. Demonstrate the relevant action and state change. Check disposal/shared resources with repeated instances. Follow [asset/presentation contract](../engineering/03_PRESENTATION_AND_ASSET_CONTRACT.md).
