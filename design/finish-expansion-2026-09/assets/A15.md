# A15 — Adhesive deposit and calcite variants

**Family:** reactive biological material. **Disposition:** SELECTED after material primitive.

This is a model-family production brief, not a missing-asset assertion or an actual mesh. Resolve existing IDs/source assets and reuse what already works. Consult current Forge/graphics instructions, asset manifests and nearest nested policy. [S01, S22, S25]

## Silhouette at the real camera

Lenticular thick deposits, stretched connecting sheets and irregular hard crusts.

## Visible function

Coating coverage, attachment, load and curing must look distinct on a small moving hull.

## Motion and articulation

Vertex/surface motion follows authoritative attachment and material state; preserve target-local location.

## Collision, sockets and authority

Sparse coverage/anchor representation; do not regenerate complex mesh colliders every frame.

Declare source axes, scale, pivot, attachment/work sockets, collision policy and state identity. Use current exporter/runtime conventions; art must not create extra solver bodies by accident.

## Materials and light

Dark wet body, luminous reaction margins; cured material opaque and brittle, never a neon ball.

## Required states

Wet impact, adhered, stretching, curing, fractured, inert residue.
