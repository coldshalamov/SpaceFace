## Discover and commit

Ancient machines begin a containment sequence for a physical reason the player can partly observe.

Expose actual equipment, cargo, permission and capacity prerequisites before charging money or moving cargo. Required tools need a legitimate acquisition/service route. Preserve a decline/abort and a recovery path.

## Physical layout

A finite sequence of local closures threatens a convoy route. Every state retains a reachable refuge or remedy, and map updates reflect observed—not omniscient—knowledge.

Measure actual reference hull/workpiece sizes, mass, cruise/earned speed, turn radius and camera depth before choosing final coordinates. Use [play-space guidance](../engineering/04_PLAYSPACE_AND_BALANCE.md); no coordinates in this brief silently override the current level.

## Playable phases

Identify first closure; compare signals and route records; choose a refuge or a specific appeal/repair; move the mission object; witness the consequence without an apex reveal.

## At least two approaches

Repair or satisfy one limited transit condition; take a known longer null corridor at a resource cost.

## Failure and aftermath

Missed timing changes destination or rescue requirements, never permanently traps a low-resource save.

## Reward and progression

Campaign progression through an established arc plus bounded route knowledge; Vethari remain indirect.
