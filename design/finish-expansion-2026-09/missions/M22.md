Measure actual reference hull/workpiece sizes, mass, cruise/earned speed, turn radius and camera depth before choosing final coordinates. Use [play-space guidance](../engineering/04_PLAYSPACE_AND_BALANCE.md); no coordinates in this brief silently override the current level.

## Playable phases

Identify specimen; lower stress or distract neighbors; capture; choose safe transport route; deliver or release after containment failure.

## At least two approaches

Gentle cradle capture; redirect the specimen into a prepared receiving fixture using an admitted lure.

## Failure and aftermath

Dead or damaged samples have limited research value but do not satisfy live delivery; safe release is permitted.

## Reward and progression

One research finding plus condition-sensitive payment; unlock only a capability demonstrated by the result.
