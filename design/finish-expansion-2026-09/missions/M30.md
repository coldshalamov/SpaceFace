Measure actual reference hull/workpiece sizes, mass, cruise/earned speed, turn radius and camera depth before choosing final coordinates. Use [play-space guidance](../engineering/04_PLAYSPACE_AND_BALANCE.md); no coordinates in this brief silently override the current level.

## Playable phases

Join the moving pocket; escort traffic; respond to one machine fault or drifting obstruction; restore movement or divert to refuge; complete passage.

## At least two approaches

Keep the Shepherd functional and match pace; take a prepared longer boundary route using lures or quiet travel.

## Failure and aftermath

A failing pocket gives a telegraphed retreat window. No invisible mass death the moment the field ends.

## Reward and progression

Convoy payment and stronger local route knowledge; no complete immunity technology.
