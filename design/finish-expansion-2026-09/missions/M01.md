**Disposition:** CORE / enrich existing, do not duplicate. **Setting:** Existing Helios opening hauler raid. **Player verbs:** shove, protect, recover, choose.

This is a proposed authored scene or refinement of an existing one, not a claim the mission is absent. Search current content for the same physical question before adding a new row. ## Existing runtime integration

Candidate route: **current opening encounter + current recovery/consequence owners**. The current mission catalog and world-site owners decide exact type/params and real event/API names. Description strings in older mission data are not executable contracts. Do not create a new mission engine for this brief. [S16, S17]

## Discover and commit

The fight is already happening during ordinary undock. A short local plea names freight and destination, not a quest menu.

Expose actual equipment, cargo, permission and capacity prerequisites before charging money or moving cargo. Required tools need a legitimate acquisition/service route. Preserve a decline/abort and a recovery path.

## Physical layout

Keep the existing hauler lane. Place one useful heavy anchor beside—not across—the escape route, one loose object reachable before the first threat commits, and a clear exit. Size interception distances using actual speeds and time-to-contact, not arbitrary world coordinates.

Measure actual reference hull/workpiece sizes, mass, cruise/earned speed, turn radius and camera depth before choosing final coordinates. Use [play-space guidance](../engineering/04_PLAYSPACE_AND_BALANCE.md); no coordinates in this brief silently override the current level.

## Playable phases

Observe the chase and choose involvement; disrupt one attacker; react to any freight spill or survivor; decide whether to pursue, rescue or leave; return to an existing service for the consequence.

## At least two approaches

Protect with ordinary gunfire and cover; use a shove or Massline throw to break pursuit. Do not require both. Missing the pirates may damage freight and change the job.

## Failure and aftermath

Convoy damage becomes contested salvage or pilot recovery; the escape route remains open. Failure cannot despawn the on-screen actors just to clear encounter state.

## Reward and progression

Use current opening rewards. The payoff is one affordable, immediately usable capability or recognition—not a free endgame weapon.
