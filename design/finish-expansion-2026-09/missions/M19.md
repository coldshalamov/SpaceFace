## Discover and commit

The first wave teaches the room by letting the player make its next piece of ammunition.

Expose actual equipment, cargo, permission and capacity prerequisites before charging money or moving cargo. Required tools need a legitimate acquisition/service route. Preserve a decline/abort and a recovery path.

## Physical layout

One large anchor, two offset cover masses and a generous escape corridor. Early kills leave useful wrecks whose mass and remaining lifetime are readable.

Measure actual reference hull/workpiece sizes, mass, cruise/earned speed, turn radius and camera depth before choosing final coordinates. Use [play-space guidance](../engineering/04_PLAYSPACE_AND_BALANCE.md); no coordinates in this brief silently override the current level.

## Playable phases

Fight first light threats; use a fresh wreck; react to one counter-tether specialist; earn a breather; draft a physically distinct upgrade.

## At least two approaches

Gun-first strategy with terrain defense; rig-first strategy reusing wrecks. Both earn viable progression.

## Failure and aftermath

Death explains cause and offers a fast retry with the same seed. The shop never deletes an attached valuable object without defined handoff.
