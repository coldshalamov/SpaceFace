## Discover and commit

A tug requests help with a broken coupler; the cargo is already moving and its manifest is visible.

Expose actual equipment, cargo, permission and capacity prerequisites before charging money or moving cargo. Required tools need a legitimate acquisition/service route. Preserve a decline/abort and a recovery path.

## Physical layout

A loose cargo train drifts toward a yard obstruction. Provide a broad safe receiving bay and an alternate sling approach. Use a heavy anchor to teach that the pilot moves when the target is too heavy.

Measure actual reference hull/workpiece sizes, mass, cruise/earned speed, turn radius and camera depth before choosing final coordinates. Use [play-space guidance](../engineering/04_PLAYSPACE_AND_BALANCE.md); no coordinates in this brief silently override the current level.

## Playable phases

Identify the valuable segment; match relative velocity; capture it; either tow into the bay or release through the receiving corridor; settle accepted intact quantity.

## At least two approaches

Gentle tow using a wide turn; controlled throw with a receiving net. Both use the same physical object and acceptance volume.

## Failure and aftermath

Damaged freight pays less and creates a cleanup job; a lost segment is recorded, not magically recreated.

## Reward and progression

Existing freight reward plus demonstration access to a better coupling tip. No payment for repeatedly crossing the boundary.
