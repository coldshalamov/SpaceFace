# M15 — Shortage at the gate

**Disposition:** SELECTED. **Setting:** Existing trade gate and nearby industrial station. **Player verbs:** diagnose, deliver, tow, restore.

This is a proposed authored scene or refinement of an existing one, not a claim the mission is absent. Search current content for the same physical question before adding a new row. ## Existing runtime integration

Candidate route: **cargo_delivery + repair/recovery offer**. The current mission catalog and world-site owners decide exact type/params and real event/API names. Description strings in older mission data are not executable contracts. Do not create a new mission engine for this brief. [S16, S17]

## Discover and commit

Traffic queues and a specific parts shortage explain the lost service.

Expose actual equipment, cargo, permission and capacity prerequisites before charging money or moving cargo. Required tools need a legitimate acquisition/service route. Preserve a decline/abort and a recovery path.

## Physical layout

A closed service lane has one failed mechanical dependency, not a mystery menu lock. An alternate public route prevents trapping the player.

Measure actual reference hull/workpiece sizes, mass, cruise/earned speed, turn radius and camera depth before choosing final coordinates. Use [play-space guidance](../engineering/04_PLAYSPACE_AND_BALANCE.md); no coordinates in this brief silently override the current level.

## Playable phases

Inspect the failed part; source a replacement or salvage it; transport it through a physical obstacle; restore the lane; observe traffic and price response.

## At least two approaches

Buy and deliver the correct component; salvage an equivalent workpiece and accept more handling risk.

## Failure and aftermath

Bad delivery leaves a recoverable job with partial costs, not a permanently closed mandatory gate.

## Reward and progression

Payment plus actual route/service restoration and a finite price correction.
