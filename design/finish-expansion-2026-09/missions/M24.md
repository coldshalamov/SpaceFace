## Discover and commit

Workers want the lane reopened; researchers want the organisms alive. Both can be satisfied with competent handling, not a forced moral binary.

Expose actual equipment, cargo, permission and capacity prerequisites before charging money or moving cargo. Required tools need a legitimate acquisition/service route. Preserve a decline/abort and a recovery path.

## Physical layout

A migrating group is drawn to a heat source crossing commercial traffic. Two lure positions create distinct safe detours; civilian traffic remains physical.

Measure actual reference hull/workpiece sizes, mass, cruise/earned speed, turn radius and camera depth before choosing final coordinates. Use [play-space guidance](../engineering/04_PLAYSPACE_AND_BALANCE.md); no coordinates in this brief silently override the current level.

## Playable phases

Read migration and resource attractor; place/transport lure; shepherd across a safe route; manage a predator or impatient hauler; restore normal traffic.

## At least two approaches

Move the heat source gradually; use a temporary admitted suppression corridor to guide the group around the hazard.

## Failure and aftermath

Injury triggers a bounded panic that changes the route; recovery or partial success remains possible.

## Reward and progression

Lane-service payment and ecological observation; killing everything is an inferior but explicitly accounted fallback where allowed.
