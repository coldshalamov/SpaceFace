## Discover and commit

An owner asks for legal release, while a broker offers a faster theft.

Expose actual equipment, cargo, permission and capacity prerequisites before charging money or moving cargo. Required tools need a legitimate acquisition/service route. Preserve a decline/abort and a recovery path.

## Physical layout

A seized workpiece sits in a visible restricted bay with a permitted approach and a physically possible theft route. Security boundaries match the map and signage.

Measure actual reference hull/workpiece sizes, mass, cruise/earned speed, turn radius and camera depth before choosing final coordinates. Use [play-space guidance](../engineering/04_PLAYSPACE_AND_BALANCE.md); no coordinates in this brief silently override the current level.

## Playable phases

Inspect manifest and condition; obtain or recover evidence; choose declaration or covert extraction; handle the resulting custody record.

## At least two approaches

Prove rightful ownership via an existing evidence operation; physically steal it and accept the incident and limited buyer options.

## Failure and aftermath

Bad evidence is rejected with a reason; theft does not silently clear the original owner. A failed attempt can be abandoned safely.

## Reward and progression

Returned property produces payment; stolen property produces resale with actual legal costs.
