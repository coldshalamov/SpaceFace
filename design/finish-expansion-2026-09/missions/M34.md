## Discover and commit

A lawful salvage claim meets ancient maintenance rules.

Expose actual equipment, cargo, permission and capacity prerequisites before charging money or moving cargo. Required tools need a legitimate acquisition/service route. Preserve a decline/abort and a recovery path.

## Physical layout

A Sorter visibly approaches a finite set of unprotected debris after a battle. The valuable workpiece has enough warning time for a normal pilot to act.

Measure actual reference hull/workpiece sizes, mass, cruise/earned speed, turn radius and camera depth before choosing final coordinates. Use [play-space guidance](../engineering/04_PLAYSPACE_AND_BALANCE.md); no coordinates in this brief silently override the current level.

## Playable phases

Identify the desired component; decide quick extraction or permitted preservation; intercept its transport if needed; deliver or present classification evidence.

## At least two approaches

Recover before processing; change its classification through a real custody/preservation action that the machine recognizes.

## Failure and aftermath

Processed material becomes a lesser recoverable resource, not a silently deleted prize. Essential story objects are excluded.

## Reward and progression

Original salvage value or lower processed value; machine permission is local and specific.
