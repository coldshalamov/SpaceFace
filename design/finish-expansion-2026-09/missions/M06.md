## Discover and commit

A broker identifies a lot, route and legal risk. The player can decline or investigate ownership.

Expose actual equipment, cargo, permission and capacity prerequisites before charging money or moving cargo. Required tools need a legitimate acquisition/service route. Preserve a decline/abort and a recovery path.

## Physical layout

A hauler carries a separately readable freight rack, escorted along a route with cover and witnesses. No mandatory pixel-perfect part sniping; an exposed coupler becomes interactable at a readable scale.

Measure actual reference hull/workpiece sizes, mass, cruise/earned speed, turn radius and camera depth before choosing final coordinates. Use [play-space guidance](../engineering/04_PLAYSPACE_AND_BALANCE.md); no coordinates in this brief silently override the current level.

## Playable phases

Find the real hauler; observe escort rhythm; separate or recover the flagged lot; break pursuit; choose a lawful return or illicit buyer.

## At least two approaches

Disable a coupler and tow the lot without destroying the hauler; go loud and salvage through a more dangerous lawful response.

## Failure and aftermath

Destroyed goods cannot satisfy intact theft. A failed theft becomes escape or restitution rather than resetting the lane.

## Reward and progression

Payout follows actual custody and condition; the illicit route has a distinct market destination.
