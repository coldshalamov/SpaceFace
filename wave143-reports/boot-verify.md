1. **`ruckusMeshData.js` — ~3.4 MB of packed strings still inside the post-gate eval task.** `src/render/characters/ruckusMeshData.js` (3,402,006 B: four entries, each `{json, binary}` — body0 alone ≈ 1.77 MB base64) is statically imported by `ruckusModel.js` ← `visualFactory.js:30` ← the render graph. The wave sliced the *decode*, but the module's entire text still tokenizes and interns at eval (~est. 10-25 ms — now the largest single literal payload left in that task). Fix shape: move the packed data off the static graph — per-key lazy `import()` or a fetched asset the warm pre-loads — with the warm driver awaiting it. The hard part is contractual, not mechanical: `template(key)` is synchronous, so either the warm must provably always complete first (RU-7 spawns are post-title, plausibly true but unproven) or the cold-consumer path needs an async seam. Impact: ~10-25 ms out of eval. Effort: M. Risk: M (sync-contract edge).

Same lazy-import/fetch family as finding 1, same sync-materialize caveat (a `deferredObject` cannot await). Lower value than (1): the intern is the small remainder of an already-won deferral.
