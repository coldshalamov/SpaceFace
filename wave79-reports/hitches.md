## Ranked findings

### F1 — `_nearbyLedgerRowsContext` memo-miss refill is an unbounded atomic step inside the sliced pumps — every far/field version bump or cell crossing lands the whole corridor query in one presented frame

- **Evidence.** `warmNearbyLedgerRows` → `_nearbyLedgerRowsContext` (`src/world/presentationSources.js:559-561`, ctx at `:345-470`): on a memo miss the refill runs `queryFarActors(state, _meshWalkOrigin, walkRadius + collectOverlap, _meshFarScratch)` at `:419` and `queryAsteroidField(...)` at `:450` **synchronously** — the walk disc spans the authored decode runway (multi-thousand WU, `farLedgerScanRadius` at `:365`) quantized to cells + `+500` buckets. Misses are not rare: they fire on quantized-cell flips AND on every `far.version`/`field.version` bump (`:401-405`, `:437-441`) — mid-fight spawns/promotions/despawns bump those versions, so every spawn event forces the next collect to re-pay the full corridor query. Reachable inside presented frames by every consumer of `collectMeshPresentationEntitiesChunked` / the beat drivers: the ~1 s residency poll (`renderer.js:19247` inside `_reconcileMeshResidencySteps`), a dirty reconcile (`:19101` inside `_reconcileMeshesSteps`), the ~100 ms hold-exempt beat during first-flight and jump-arrival holds (`:2531`, `tolerateMiss:'covered'` — strict form still refills on any non-covered miss), the seam collect (`:13583`), the opening widen (`:11464`, loading). The drivers deliberately hoist it as "the driver's own step" (`:19099-19100`, `:2521-2523`) — that places it inside ONE `iterator.next()` of the reconcile/poll generator or inside the hold beat's mint: the slice consults at `:2134`, `:2181`, `:2558` can only measure *between* steps, never inside one. Comment at `:2493-2496` prices the class: "~5-12 ms atomic".
- **Mechanism.** During travel or a fight, a quantized-cell flip or any far-actor membership change turns the next poll/reconcile/hold-beat step into a synchronous grid query over the corridor — a multi-ms brick inside a presented flight frame, recurring (not amortized — each version bump re-arms it). This is the largest unbounded atomic unit left in flight-reachable sliced machinery; every other step is per-row or count-capped.
- **Fix sketch.** Generator-ize the refill itself: `queryAsteroidField`/`queryFarActors` per cell-batch or row-bucket behind the same keyed memo (commit each segment's partial stamp so a resume continues rather than restarts), driven by the same slice clock as the collect that consumes it. `tolerateMiss:true` paths already tolerate an empty row set for one beat (`:408-415`) — a chunked refill can serve that shape mid-walk. Alternative S: drive `warmNearbyLedgerRows` as its own yield-separated stage BEFORE the pump mints the iterator so at least the step's size is measured and the pump can defer the *rest* of the beat — isolates but does not bound.
- **Effort** M (segment-committed memo refill) / S (isolate only) · **Magic-frame impact** H on miss frames (a corridor-wide grid query inside the picture), L on hit frames · **Risk** M — the row-snapshot contract (`:565-567`, `:600-601`) must keep the frozen row set consistent with the refilled scratches across suspensions.

### F2 — `_pruneMotionTrackerRecords` runs "one atomic multi-ms sweep" inside the final `next()` of the reconcile/poll generators — ~1/s, inside a presented frame, after the sweep's heaviest segment

- **Evidence.** `renderer.js:19146-19149` (reconcile) and `:19253-19256` (poll) call it inside the generator — no `yield` between it and the preceding bind-loop iteration, so the last `next()` carries: last bind + `queueOrRequestAuthoredUpgrade` + 7 global registry prunes (`globalShipMicroMotion/AsteroidMotion/PickupMotion/OrdnanceMotion/InfrastructureMotion/ForgeCrown/LawArenaDressing.prune`, `:18785-18791`) + `_releaseDetachedBoundaryOwners` (`:18792` → `:18802-18858`: claimed-set census = O(meshes × ancestor-depth + entities + meshRefs) build at `:18812-18830`, then per-owner membership + release). The amortize gate `residencyPruneSweepDue` (`:2476-2484`, `RESIDENCY_PRUNE_SWEEP_MS = 1000`) is shared across both passes, so ~once per second ONE presented frame eats the whole sweep inside a single pump step the 4 ms consult can't preempt. The code's own comment calls it "one atomic multi-ms sweep (7 global registry prunes plus a claimed-set census over meshes/entities/refs)" (`:2471-2474`).
- **Mechanism.** A periodic multi-ms brick inside the frame that follows a poll/reconcile sweep — the rhythm the smooth-flight probe reads as a recurring hitch, independent of what's on screen.
- **Fix sketch.** Yield-isolate + chunk: `yield` before the sweep so the pump measures it alone, then drive the claimed-set census and the 7 prunes as their own yields (the census build is a flat loop over `_meshes`/`entities`/`refs` — trivially chunked; `releaseDetachedBoundaryOwners` takes a bounded batch per step). Alternatively skip it inside the reconcile/poll generators entirely and run it off the despawn-dispose drain's own cadence — it only reclaims a slow-leak class.
- **Effort** S-M · **Magic-frame impact** M-H (one multi-ms spike ~1/s, every session, every sector) · **Risk** L — reclaim-later is the designed safe state; ordering within the sweep is already order-neutral by construction (`:18811`).

### F3 — `_drainMeshBuildQueue` tail + the urgent re-sort hide inside the reconcile/poll generators' last `next()` — the pump's 4 ms consult can't observe the sweep's heaviest segment

- **Evidence.** `_reconcileMeshesSteps`: `yield* enqueueMissingMeshBuildsSteps(...)` at `:19116-19125` — the *same* `next()` that returns the last enqueue step continues synchronously into `const built = this._drainMeshBuildQueue(buildBudget)` at `:19126`. `_reconcileMeshResidencySteps` is worse: the urgent/rest O(pending) partition+sort at `:19390-19405` and the drain at `:19406` all ride inside one `next()`. Inside the drain, `shouldContinueAdmissionSlice` (`:19566-19574`, `usePacedLedger:true`) self-bounds per item — but a saturated ledger still guarantees ≥1 examined row plus a `hoistDeadlineGlassMeshBuilds` O(queue-tail) verdict scan (`:19579-19582`), and an on-glass build deliberately overflows the count cap (`:19546-19565`). The pump debits the whole `next()` at `:2139`/`:2185` — debited, but unbounded: one step can carry tail-scan + up to `RUNTIME_MESH_BUILD_BUDGET` (8) composite `vf.build`s + hoists.
- **Mechanism.** A dirty reconcile or the ~1 s poll that finds a cohort to enqueue ends with a single `next()` containing the entire build drain — composite ship/asteroid builds (the multi-ms unit every other lane exists to amortize) stacked inside one presented frame the slice consult only sees once it returns.
- **Fix sketch.** `yield` immediately before the drain call in both generators so the pump's consult fires between enqueue-tail and drain; then split the drain into per-step items (`while (...) { this._drainMeshBuildQueue(1); yield; }` — its internal consults stay intact, the pump gains a consultable boundary per build). The urgent re-sort is one flat pass — either yield before it or accept it (O(pending) small ints, sub-ms typical).
- **Effort** S · **Magic-frame impact** M (bounded-in-code but lumpy: the drain is the sweep's heaviest tail and it currently hides inside a step) · **Risk** L — identical item order, the drain's own consults unchanged; deadline-glass hoisting contract preserved.

### F4 — Minted scan-context/latch snapshots span a suspended sweep's whole life — a stale-false `shellLatched` re-opens the unsliced dispose storm the latch exists to prevent

- **Evidence.** `_reconcileMeshesSteps` mints `reconcileSpeed`, `reconcileCam`, `reconcileEvictBase/ShipWreck`, `reconcileScanOpts`, and `shellLatched` (`liveSectorGpuAdmission === true || sectorShellAdmission === true`) once at generator start (`:19047-19062`); `_reconcileMeshResidencySteps` does the same at `:19189-19198`. Staleness is bounded only by the enterSerial epoch-abandon (`:2092-2097`, `:2151-2153`) — same-world latches and camera/speed drift are NOT re-read. Direction that matters: minted `shellLatched=false`, then a live-sector cook arms `liveSectorGpuAdmission`/`sectorShellAdmission` mid-sweep → the remaining rows run `residencyEvict` + `queueOrRequestAuthoredUpgrade` on departed-home rows — the unsliced dispose/upgrade storm inside presented frames is exactly what the latch defers (`:19054-19059`, `:19133-19139` own the same guard per-row and it keys on the minted value). - **Effort** S · **Magic-frame impact** M (only during cook/transition windows — exactly the frames the shell latch protects) · **Risk** L — re-reading live verdicts is strictly more correct than minted ones; the scan-context re-mint is memo-shaped so per-resume cost is bounded.
