## Verdict

**`saturated: false`** — W108 machinery verified clean, but two lane-native mechanisms remain: forced-pass stacking is bounded per-lane yet unbounded in sum (no shared forced wallet), and the hold-exempt fault park dead-codes the *only* in-hold enqueue path for the epoch — the brief's assumption that "normal build-queue lanes keep builds flowing" is wrong under both hold regimes, where reconcile/poll are suppressed and only the drain (not the enqueue) keeps running. Six findings; F1–F3 are the tier that matters.

## Ranked findings

### F1 — Forced-pass stacking: per-lane caps are independent and unconditional floors are ungated — a storm frame can carry ~25-60 ms of "forced" work
**Evidence:** skip-cap gates (own counters, all ≥2): live dispose `renderer.js:2150-2154`, warm dispose `2187-2191`, envRebind `29932-29938`, meshRelease `22636-22646`, depth-stage arm `28117-28123` (after-present task, delays next present), policy mint starvation `≤1/class/seq` for classes `f`/`c` (27797-27832) + coverage class `v` (27703-27724); min-1 floors: `_drainMeshBuildQueueSteps` `itemsDone < minItems` (24161-24169) at 3 call sites per pump (2578, 3045, 3049), `drawnCull` FIFO `rows === 0 ||` floor with **no ledger consult at all** (`visualCullRadius.js:113-127`, reset once per pass at `renderer.js:24675`); unconditional presented-frame drains: `_presentationRebindIter` ≤4 ms (26063-26090), `_fencePackPending` ≤4 ms (26139-26169), `_shadowCensusForFrame` ≤4 ms **per call site** × ~15 sites (27983-28020 — see F3), `driveProtectedFirstFlightDrain` ≤4 ms + ledger (2293-2311, called at 2621 and 2755), hold-exempt beat ≤4 ms per 100 ms (2598/2748), seam FIFO slice ≤4 ms (2617), plus deadline-glass hoist tails `O(queue)` per drain call (24039/24174 — F6).
**Mechanism:** `paceSpentEpoch` is shared for *gating* but each lane's skip cap fires on its own counter — under a sustained storm every cap trips on the same presented frame: live drain (escalated ≤4 ms with headroom) + warm leftover + envRebind ≥1-check+≤4 ms + meshRelease O(pending) no-wall + rebind 4 + pack 4 + census 4×sites + ≤2 policy slices + 1 cov slice + protected drain 4 + ~3 build-drain rows + ≥1 drawnCull walk. Each unit is "one bounded forced pass"; the **set** is unbounded — the presented frame carries the whole stack, which is exactly the hitch class the ledger exists to prevent, and every over-run ms delays the next admission beat (popin coupling).
**Fix sketch:** shared **forced-spend wallet** on the pace epoch (`forcedPacedSpentMs`, cap ~4 ms): a lane whose skip cap fires consults it — over-cap lanes defer one frame (counter stays at cap → forces next frame in arrival order). Explicitly NOT a strict one-unit `lastForcedLaneSerial` serialization: forced units carry real debt (corpses, releases, builds) and a hard serial re-creates the starvation the caps fix. drawnCull's floor joins the wallet too — its unit is a subtree `Box3.setFromObject`, not µs.
Effort **M** · impact **H** · risk **M** (wallet sizing decides whether under-pressure lanes wait one frame or starve — keep the wallet ≥ the heaviest single forced unit).

### F2 — `_holdExemptFaultEpoch` parks the only in-hold enqueue path for the whole epoch; the "normal lanes cover" assumption is false under holds
**Evidence:** head gate `renderer.js:3377` (`=== serviceFaultEpoch → return 0`); single shared stamp at all 4 fault sites (3433, 3468, 3542, 3561); under `holdFirstFlightStreaming` the pump `return 'held-first-flight'` at 2756 — reconcile (2925-2999) and poll (3000-3047) **never run**; the seam-hold window likewise ends `return 'deferred'` (2695). The only enqueue inside either hold is `enqueueHoldExemptMeshBuildsSliced` on the 100 ms beat (2598, 2748); the comment at 3430-3433 claims "the normal build-queue lanes keep builds flowing" — they only *drain* (`driveProtectedFirstFlightDrain`, 2755, doesn't consult the fault epoch; `_holdExemptRepartition` still stamps at 2751).
**Mechanism:** one transient skeleton fault in warm-refill/collect/commit/kick parks **all four phases** for the epoch (`enterSerial|contextRestoreSeq`). Rows needing exempt scheduling *minted mid-hold* — the rescue set piece spawning at flight start, an explicit-focus flip, a row crossing onto glass inside a continuous seam — are never collected: the parked function is the only lane that enumerates them, so they wait the whole hold (sector-scale, not a frame). Already-queued rows are unaffected (drain + repartition keep running), so the failure shape is precisely "pop-in at hold release": the exempt tail mounts after the blend instead of inside it.
**Fix sketch:** degrade explicitly instead of implicitly — on the park path, run one bounded `enqueueMissingMeshBuildsSteps(state.entityList, …, shouldQueue=isHoldExemptMeshBuild)` pass under the same ~4 ms slice clock once per epoch (the predicate family is already imported at this site); alternatively split the stamp per phase (`_holdExemptFaultEpoch` per warm/collect/commit/kick) so a kick fault doesn't kill the collect. Effort **S-M** · impact **H** (gated on a fault, but when it trips the entire exempt lane is dead for the hold) · risk **M** (the non-chunked evaluator must stay under the beat's slice clock — do not call it synchronously unbounded).

### F3 — Shadow-census drain wallet is per call site, not per presented pass
**Evidence:** `renderer.js:27983-28020` — `deadline = memo.sig == null ? Infinity : startedAt + 4` with `startedAt` minted per invocation; ~15 call sites consult `_shadowCensusForFrame`/`lightSigFor` per pass (9950, 10217, 11262, 12232, 14071, 14468, 15859, 16958, 27392, 27434, 27547, 27750, 28167, 28251, 28332, 28384). The drain loop checks only its own `deadline` — unlike every sibling lane it never consults `pacedFrameSpend()`.
**Mechanism:** while `memo.pendingIter` is mid-walk (any light mutation re-mints — dynamic lights in combat are the sustained-storm case), *each* call site pays up to 4 ms in the same presented pass → up to ~60 ms per pass until the walk completes or the ≥4-superseded storm pin stops re-mints. Distinct from lod-lane F7 (that was the `sig == null` unbounded sync fallback; this is the stepped walk while a sig exists — the common case). Also note `sig == null` first-mint drains **synchronously to completion** (deadline `Infinity`, 27990) — documented once-per-scene, but a scene swap re-pays it; low frequency, leave as-is.
**Fix sketch:** stamp `memo.lastDrainSeq = this._viewSyncSeq` (already incremented per pass at 24674) — only the first call site per pass pays the drain, later sites serve `memo.sig` immediately; or add the standard `pacedFrameSpend() >= PACED_FRAME_BUDGET_MS` break to the drain loop. Effort **S** · impact **M-H** · risk **L**.

### F4 — `_rebindFaultEpoch` suppresses the cheap spawned-slot chain along with the faulted whole-map walk
*(Cross-reported: identical mechanism to lod-lane F3 — confirmed independently for this lane.)*
**Evidence:** `renderer.js:26030-26061` — `rebindFaulted` gates both the remap/rebuilt mint (26031) and the `spawnedCount > 0` chain (26048); the parked iter was nulled at 26077.
**Mechanism (popin angle):** the spawned leg (`_bindPublishedPresentationMeshesSteps` over `publication.spawnedSlots`) is per-row idempotent and cheap — but while parked, spawned slots whose only bind path is this leg defer `world.bindMesh` for the epoch → those meshes draw stale/unbound (no pose writes) until the next `enterSerial`. Fresh mounts still bind at 24271, so the blast radius is journal-respawn rows only — narrower than lod's framing but real in the remap window.
**Fix sketch:** scope the epoch gate to the whole-map mint; mint/chain the spawned leg normally (or a separate `_spawnedBindFaultEpoch`). Effort **S** · impact **M** · risk **L**.

### F5 — Newcomer census pays O(entityList) per drain call while shell latches arm
**Evidence:** `renderer.js:24060-24101` — `newcomerSnapshot = newcomerList.slice()` + `admitNewcomer` per row, gated on `liveSectorGpuAdmission || sectorShellAdmission`; runs inside *every* `_drainMeshBuildQueueSteps` invocation (×~3 presented calls per frame at 2578/3045/3049). Under the sync drain (23956-23962) the per-1024 yields are exhausted inline — the census is atomic inside the call. `makeHoldExemptScanContext` (24053) also re-mints per call (cheap, but uncached).
**Mechanism:** during the sector-shell admission window, each drain call re-pays an O(entityList) slice+scan; 3-4 calls/frame → ~3-4 full censuses per presented frame while the latches hold, though membership changes only on spawn ticks.
**Fix sketch:** key the census on `_residencySweepBeatStamp` (already minted per sweep beat at 3016) or the pace epoch — run once, reuse within the frame. Effort **S** · impact **M** (shell window only) · risk **L**.

### F6 — Deadline-glass hoists pay O(queue-tail) per drain call, un-gated by the ledger
**Evidence:** `driveHoist` mints a fresh `hoistDeadlineGlassMeshBuildsSteps` tail scan on refused-start (24039), on the budget-cap branch (24157), and on every saturated-ledger break (24174-24177); `glassVerdictMemo` (24002) dedupes within a drain but not across drains.
**Mechanism:** on a spent ledger every drain call still walks `O(_meshBuildQueue tail)` for the hoist before breaking — ~3-4 calls/frame → multiple tail scans inside one presented frame during a sustained-saturated window. The hoist is debited (24023-24029) but never consults the ledger before paying — it is *forced* by construction on the exact paths where the ledger said no.
**Fix sketch:** skip the mid-drain hoist when `pacedFrameSpend() >= PACED_FRAME_BUDGET_MS` (it's ordering-only — rows still drain in queue order), or carry `glassVerdictMemo` on the sweep beat across the frame's drain calls. Effort **S** · impact **L-M** · risk **L**.

## Lane residuals summary (brief's a–d)

- **(a) Forced-pass stacking — enumerated:** live+warm already share one window (warm inherits `max(0, 2 − liveWall)`, 2408-2409) — that pair cannot stack ~8 ms; the remaining stack is real and listed in F1. A shared `lastForcedLaneSerial` (strict one-forced-unit-per-frame) is **not** recommended — it re-creates starvation; a shared forced-*spend* wallet (~4 ms/epoch) is the right shape.
- **(b) FIFO outgoing-key stash correctness — verified, no finding:** every rewrite stashes the outgoing key before overwrite at *both* sites (consult 196-205, drain 141-159). A→B→C churn preserves each intermediate (consult at B stashes A, consult at C stashes B, drain stashes the final outgoing). The `drawnCullRadiusMax` merge is unnecessary: the over-budget miss serves `max(cached.radius, max over lru.values())` at 237-239 — the merge happens at serve time on the per-mesh LRU window. Stash of a `0` radius is honest (the key's `childCount` term re-keys on subtree growth, and `known > 0` gates every stale serve). Eviction past 8 keys is the only loss — designed.
- **(c) Hold-exempt park blast radius — real (F2):** an in-hold entity needing exempt scheduling **can** out-wait the hold; the park should degrade to the normal queue explicitly (bounded pass once per epoch) rather than implicitly, since no normal enqueue lane runs inside either hold regime.
- **(d) Remaining un-budgeted walks, ranked:** ① `_flushMeshReleasePending` — 5×O(pending) atomic once forced; `_meshReleasePending` is an unbounded `Set` fed by every unbind (22715) — **lod-lane F8 already filed; cross-ref only**. ② `_shadowCensusForFrame` per-site drain wallet (F3). ③ Newcomer census O(entityList) per drain call under shell latches (F5). ④ Deadline-glass hoist tail scans O(queue) per drain (F6). ⑤ Depth-arm preamble `_sweepDetachedDepthStageRoots` + nearest-first sort O(pending log) (28094-28144) — after-present task, not presented frame; honorable mention. Excluded as deliberate/bounded: `flushDisposeQueueLane` sync (teardown seam only), `settleRebuildBridges` (≤32 visits), `drainSceneEnvRebindQueue` (≥1 check + ≤4 ms), `enqueueMissingMeshBuildsSteps` under the poll (4 ms + ledger, 3039-3040), hidden-freeze drain (node/visit caps + ledger gate at 22307), `cameraClearanceBoxForMesh` `setFromObject` on miss (lod-lane ranked).

## Consistency note (not a finding)

The `_holdExemptFaultEpoch` park suppresses **all four phases** on any phase's fault (collect vs. kick share the stamp), while the sibling drains keep their own epoch stamps. The keepGpu window proves the per-phase degrade vocabulary exists (`runCookSyncTwin` once per epoch) — the hold lanes are the one place it wasn't applied. Also: the drained-while-`irrelevant` queue row is consumed without re-queue (24187) — safe only because the 250 ms residency poll re-enumerates (`RENDER_RESIDENCY_POLL_SECONDS`, 872) and deadline-glass rows never get skipped as irrelevant.
