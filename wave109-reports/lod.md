## Ranked findings

### F1 — Fault-parked resident-reattach silently defeats the keepGpu restore it precedes
**Evidence:** `renderer.js:2458-2486` (mint gate + fault stamp), `renderer.js:2506-2510` (reconcile mint requires `!owner._residentReattachIter`), `renderer.js:2525-2540` (keepGpu degrade covers *reconcile only*).
**Mechanism:** The reattach sweep re-keys kept GPU meshes onto restored entity ids before reconcile runs — reconcile's mint gate waits on a *live* sweep, but a parked fault leaves `_residentReattachIter === null`, so the gate passes instantly. Consequence chain: transient fault in the sweep → reconcile proceeds → releases every dead-id mesh the sweep would have re-keyed → the entire kept GPU set is torn down and rebuilt inside the same restore. keepGpu becomes release-all + a rebuild storm — longer loading shell and a burst of late mounts on first flight. One transient fault converts the whole optimization into its opposite for that restore.
**Fix sketch:** Give the sweep the same degrade shape the W108 reconcile got: on `_residentReattachFaultEpoch === serviceFaultEpoch(st)` with the remap still pending, run `reattachResidentGpuMeshes` (sync twin exists at 1920) once via `runCookSyncTwin`, epoch-latched — or re-mint the stepped iter once after a short dirtySince window before letting reconcile mint. Effort **S-M**; magic-frame impact **H** when it trips (restore shows missing/rebuilt visuals); risk **L** (the sweep is idempotent by design comment; remap-guarded).

### F2 — `_fencePackFaults` 3-try cap freezes every interpolated pose world-wide for the rest of the sector
**Evidence:** `renderer.js:26102-26161`; `_advancePosePackEpoch` at 24583-24597 (epoch bumps only on `publication.remapped` or sector-id change).
**Mechanism:** A persistent pack fault parks `packFaulted` for the pose epoch — `latestSnapshot()`/`previousSnapshot()` freeze, so `_applyPresentationPose` (24599+) writes the last good snapshot forever. The whole presented world visibly locks in place while the sim keeps running — worst possible "real but stale" frame, worse than a hitch. Re-arm exists on remap/sector flip (new `packEpoch` re-keys the fault counters), but nothing inside an epoch retries, and this is exactly the class keepGpu already proves is degradable.
**Fix sketch:** Mirror R9: after ~1 s of parked `snapshotNeedsPack`, run one `packPresentationWorldToFence` sync twin via a `_fencePackDegraded`-style epoch latch. The sync twin already exists as the non-steps fallback at 26170-26177. Effort **S**; impact **H** when it trips; risk **M** (one atomic O(activeCount) pack inside a present — bounded, and strictly better than a frozen world).

### F3 — `_rebindFaultEpoch` parks the cheap spawned-slot bind leg along with the faulted whole-map walk
**Evidence:** `renderer.js:26030-26059` — `rebindFaulted` suppresses the heavy mint AND the `publication.spawnedCount > 0` chain at 26050.
**Mechanism:** The faulted work is the O(_meshes) rebind walk; the spawned leg (`_bindPublishedPresentationMeshesSteps` on `publication.spawnedSlots`) is independent, per-row idempotent, and cheap. Rows whose only live bind path is this leg — spawned slots announced while their sweep bind hasn't run yet — defer presentation binding until the next residency sweep reaches them (23476), or indefinitely if the sweep lanes are *also* parked (F4). Presented as: spawned entities invisible for a beat (or the epoch). 
**Fix sketch:** Scope the epoch gate to the whole-map mint only; let the spawned leg mint/chain normally — or track a separate `_spawnedBindFaultEpoch`. Effort **S**; impact **M-H**; risk **L**.

### F4 — Flight-mode residency lanes (reconcile + poll) have no bounded in-epoch retry
**Evidence:** mint/fault pairs at `renderer.js:2936-2999` (reconcile) and `3007-3028` (poll); the keepGpu degrade at 2525-2540 exists only inside `mode === 'loading'` (2415).
**Mechanism:** In flight, a transient fault parks each lane for the whole sector: banked `_meshReconcileDirty` never re-mints → dead-id meshes never released (GPU creep + stale corpses drawn) and stray rebinds never happen; a parked poll stops residency re-evaluations → missing visuals persist and evictions stall. The poll is a *subset* of reconcile, so it cannot heal a parked reconcile — it only keeps residency breathing. A sync degrade in flight is the wrong fix (it would pay the atomic census inside a presented frame — the hitch the steps exist to remove).
**Fix sketch:** Bounded in-epoch *re-mint*, not sync twin: re-arm the mint gate once per epoch after a cooldown (e.g. `_reconcileRetryAt = now() + 1000`, one retry, or a fault counter cap like the fence pack's). A transient fault heals in ~1 s instead of never; a persistent fault re-parks. Effort **M**; impact **M-H**; risk **M** (re-minted walk must tolerate its own applied prefix — the abandons already handle partial state).

### F5 — `_protectedFirstFlightFaultEpoch` parks the exempt repartition + budgeted drain for the whole epoch
**Evidence:** `renderer.js:2283-2310` (mint/drain), call sites 2621 and 2755 (post-keepGpu seam and `holdFirstFlightStreaming` window).
**Mechanism:** A faulted repartition parks the hoisted-exempt priority + the budgeted drain for the enter — during first-flight/streaming holds the exempt cohort loses priority and lands late (pop-in-adjacent: mounts arrive after the hold lifts instead of inside it). The ordinary queue drain keeps builds flowing, so this is a priority loss, not a stall — but it's exactly the window where late mounts are most visible.
**Fix sketch:** Same bounded-retry shape as F4 (one in-epoch re-mint after a beat); the repartition is idempotent and re-anchored on the live tail already (23890+). Effort **S**; impact **M**; risk **L**.

### F6 — `_arrivalReserveIter` never abandons and never re-arms
**Evidence:** mint/drive at `renderer.js:2886-2916`; restore paths clear `_arrivalRosterIter` at 18878 and 18901 but **never** `_arrivalReserveIter`; second fault parks with no epoch stamp at all.
**Mechanism:** (i) A reserve drive suspended across a sector flip or restore commits the departed sector's `asteroidField`/`entityList` counts into `reserveAsteroidInstanceCapacity` — the new sector arrives under-reserved. (ii) A reserve that faults once parks for the rest of the enter: nothing re-arms `_arrivalReservePending` (only a *fresh roster fault* re-arms it, and a faulted roster never re-mints either). Net: first asteroid-heavy fight of the sector pays un-amortized pool growth — a hitch burst, and (b)'s "sector where the roster never re-mints" leaves this as the only net.
**Fix sketch:** Epoch-key the reserve iter (`_arrivalReserveEpoch = enterSerial`) + abandon on enter/restore like the roster; give the parked reserve one bounded re-arm (pending re-flag on the next pump after a cooldown). Effort **S**; impact **L-M**; risk **L**.

### F7 — `_shadowCensusForFrame`'s null-sig sync fallback is un-parked: O(whole scene) per call, per presented pass, under a persistent fault
**Evidence:** `renderer.js:28021-28030` — `if (memo.sig == null) { try { memo.sig = lightCensusSignature(scene) } catch { warn } }`.
**Mechanism:** The storm cap (superseded ≥4 → `stormEpoch` pin) bounds the *stepped* mint, but a persistent signature fault with no sig yet falls to the sync fallback on **every call** — and `lightSigFor`/`_shadowCensusForFrame` is consulted ~8+ times per presented pass (9950, 10217, 11262, 12232, 14071, 14468, 15859, 16958, 27392, 27434, 27547, 27750…). Worst-case cost = whole-scene traverse × call frequency, unbounded, un-parked — the opposite failure of the storm the cap exists for. Null is already the served conservative answer; the retry buys nothing past a small attempt budget.
**Fix sketch:** Fault-park the fallback the same way: stamp `memo.sigFaults`/epoch, serve null after K tries within the epoch (null is the conservative direction callers already handle), re-arm on epoch bump. Effort **S**; impact **M** (only under persistent fault — but that is the regime this whole layer exists for); risk **L**.

### F8 — `_flushMeshReleasePending` has no inner deadline: admitted flush runs 5×O(pending) atomically
**Evidence:** `renderer.js:22627-22690` — entry gate + measured debit + whole-set re-pend, but no wall-clock check inside the registry loop; each `releaseMeshSet` is a full `O(pending)` Map-driven walk (e.g. `shipMicroMotion.js:2307-2314`).
**Mechanism:** A mass-despawn storm (`entity:destroyed` bursts mint thousands of pending rows) defers while the wallet is spent — good — but the first admitted pass pays 5 registry × N-row walks atomically inside the pump. Rows are cheap map ops, so typical N is fine; a 10k-row storm is ~milliseconds of unbudgeted atomic work once it fires.
**Fix sketch:** Per-registry time check against a 4 ms deadline with remainder re-pend (`pending` minus already-released registries' rows is already the unit of re-pend — resume mid-list by re-pending the tail). Effort **S-M**; impact **L-M**; risk **L**.

### F9 — Never-measured `drawnCullRadiusForMesh` consults still pay an unbudgeted `Box3.setFromObject` inline
**Evidence:** `visualCullRadius.js:230-300` — `keyMoved && _passRemeasureCount >= PER_PASS` defers only when `known > 0`; never-measured rows (`cached == null` → `known === 0`) fall through to the inline walk by design.
**Mechanism:** `primeDrawnCullStamps` (320+) covers authored commit tails, so the residual is non-authored/fallback meshes and any authored path that skipped priming: a burst of fresh binds pays one subtree walk each inside the presented consult. Mitigated: binds ride the paced build-queue legs, so the walks are usually ledger-adjacent already.
**Fix sketch:** For `known === 0` over-budget consults, serve a conservative over-serve — e.g. `drawnCullRadiusMax`-style max-ever envelope or the authored census radius if stamped — and queue the real measure on the FIFO. Must over-serve only (under-culling = pop-out, the illegal direction). Effort **M**; impact **L**; risk **M**.

## Lane residuals summary (brief's a–d)

- **(a) Fault-epoch granularity:** the composite key is correct, but parking is *all-or-nothing per epoch* — enumerated parked lanes and who waits on them inside one sector: residentReattach (feeds reconcile's keep decision → F1), rebind (suppresses spawned-slot binds → F3), reconcile+poll (residency recovery itself → F4), protectedFirstFlight (exempt priority under holds → F5), holdExempt (degrade documented — normal lanes cover, no retry needed), arrivalReserve (→ F6). **Bounded in-epoch retry is missing everywhere except the keepGpu loading window** — F1/F4/F5 are that gap enumerated.
- **(b) Reserve vs roster divergence:** none — the pending drive mints `_reserveArrivalAsteroidCapacitySteps(state.entityList)` (2891), the same `entityList` the roster tail passes (22114, 22158-22161) with the same `batchRows` default. The reserve is genuinely standalone. Residual is availability, not divergence → F6.
- **(c) `_fencePackFaults` cap:** the cap re-arms on pose-epoch bump = world.clear/rebuild or sector flip; inside a pose epoch a persistent fault freezes every interpolated pose sector-wide — the intended degrade is missing → F2.
- **(d) Remaining presented-frame whole-subtree walks, ranked by worst-case node count:** ① `_shadowCensusForFrame` null-sig sync fallback — whole scene, *unbounded frequency* (F7). ② `cameraClearanceBoxForMesh` `setFromObject` on memo miss (`renderer.js:4208`) — largest structural subtree (station ~10⁴ nodes), per miss; misses now rare via posY translate. ③ `disposeObject` sync flush via `flushDisposeQueueLane` (2097-2118) — fat corpse subtree (~1-3k nodes) × queue; teardown-path only. ④ `_flushMeshReleasePending` — 5×O(pending) registry walks (F8). ⑤ `drawnCullRadiusForMesh` never-measured inline `setFromObject` (F9). Excluded as deliberate/documented: unified `scene.updateMatrixWorld()` at 29155 (clean-skip contract), `_sampleDrawHistogram` (opt-in `?drawhist` instrument only), stepped drain legs (all bounded 4 ms + paced debit), first-boot census mint (documented one-time atomic cost).

## Consistency note (not a finding)

`driveShadowPolicySteps` has the *opposite* park semantics to the fence pack: a drive fault deletes the parked slot and returns null, so it re-mints+warns on **every** eval under a persistent fault (self-healing but unbounded warn/alloc churn), while the fence pack caps at 3. Both regimes are defensible; worth standardizing the retry vocabulary next time the layer is touched.
