## Findings (ranked)

### F1 — Sector-scoped warm latches are session-scoped and never re-arm after demote — return visits skip re-warm entirely

**Evidence (MECHANISM_VERIFIED, code-path; materiality conditional):**
- `src/render/renderer.js` ~5964-5975 (wanted-tier speculative warm): `const warmed = owner._wantedTierWarmSectors || (owner._wantedTierWarmSectors = new Set()); const sectorKey = sectorId || ''; if (warmed.has(sectorKey)) return; warmed.add(sectorKey);` — the latch key is bare `sectorId`, session-scoped, and is never deleted/cleared anywhere in the file (grep of `_wantedTierWarmSectors.delete/.clear` returns nothing).
- Same shape for `_modularPoolWarmSectors` (~6020s, key `sectorId\0role\0armKey`) and `_gateWingWarmKeys` (~6050s, key includes `sectorId|to|day|ctx`). All three are session Sets keyed on sector identity with no demotion/epoch component.
- Sector demotion destroys the warm cohort's coverage: `src/systems/world.js` ~1700 `_demoteSectorToRecordOnly` → `_despawnEntitiesForSector` + tier → RECORD_ONLY, and the renderer's residency prepare (`prepareSectorExit`, renderer.js ~18752) runs on exit paths. Whether the decoded blueprints themselves are evicted between visits depends on downstream cache retention, but the latch never consults residency — it suppresses the warm unconditionally on every later visit.
- **Mechanism (what the player sees):** re-enter a sector (gate loop, mission bounce, backtrack) and the speculative warm passes — wanted-tier SCN roster, seeded modular pool, gate-wing sets — no-op because the latch says "already warmed". Any spawn arm firing on the second visit (wanted-tier post, intervention guard, gate approach) whose decoded assets churned or were never covered pays cold decode/mint inside presented frames → the late pop-in this lane hunts. First cook enjoys pre-armed warm; every subsequent visit is silently unwarmed.
- **Fix sketch:** tie the latch to the residency epoch — e.g. delete the sector's warm keys on demote (the `world:residency` listener at renderer.js:18498 already re-dirties `_meshReconcileDirty` on the same event), or store `{sectorId, enterSerial}` so a new enter serial re-arms the warm. Worst case is one redundant bounded warm per re-entry.
- **Effort:** S · **Magic-frame impact:** M (conditional on eviction churn; grows with session length/memory pressure — long sessions are where re-entry happens) · **Risk:** low — warm callers are deduped/idempotent within a visit; over-warming only re-spends a bounded speculative decode that exists to prevent this defect class.

### F2 — Second entry into a cooked sector runs the keep-GPU shortcut: coverage censuses skipped, cohort coverage falls to the paced reconcile, and the lift is not gated on it

**Evidence (MECHANISM_VERIFIED for the skip path; spill severity timing-dependent → flagged):**
- `_sessionLiveSectorCookedId` is stamped at cook end (renderer.js ~15408) and cleared only on `game:new` (~18697) and context loss (~10445) — **not on sector demotion**. `world.js` ~1700 despawns the sector's entities on demote, so the "cooked" stamp outlives the world state it describes.
- On a subsequent loading-mode `sector:enter` the listener recomputes `_sessionRecookKeepGpu` from that stamp (renderer.js ~19570-74) → keep-GPU early return at ~19608-19624: `reattachResidentGpuMeshes` (rebinds only meshes still in `_meshes` — demote-despawned entities' rows are gone or stale-mapped) + `_meshReconcileDirty = true` (comment at ~19615: "serviceRenderMeshResidency builds the gaps during loading") + release `sectorShellAdmission`; the cook with its `collectFirstFlightCookEntities` censuses does **not** run (both censuses are `!recook`-gated at ~13892 and ~14010; `requestDecodeRunwayPromote` is likewise skipped at ~13577).
- Gap coverage falls to `serviceRenderMeshResidency`'s paced reconcile: `_reconcileMeshesSteps` sliced at ~4 ms/frame + shared `pacedFrameSpend` ledger (renderer.js ~3390-3470), enqueues into `_meshBuildQueue` drained under the same ledger. Re-materialized entities dirty the flag via `entity:spawned` (~18497) — discovery works — but keep-path `prepareLiveSectorBeforeFlight` resolves after a bounded ~4 s queue drain + one rehearsal (~13282-13314); nothing waits on `_reconcileIter` completion or queue-empty before the veil lifts.
- **Mechanism:** on re-entry into a previously cooked (then demoted, then re-materialized) sector, the cohort the first cook's censuses would have forced ahead of lift is instead admitted at paced-service rate across the loading window and — if the cohort is dense enough that reconcile+drain outlasts the window — into early presented flight. Player-visible as a sparser set of late-minted meshes over the first seconds back (deferred pop-in), plus the skipped `requestDecodeRunwayPromote` leaves the decode runway un-promoted for the session.
- **Fix sketch:** in the keep-GPU early return, either (a) gate prepare resolution on `_meshReconcileDirty === false && _meshBuildQueueHead === _meshBuildQueue.length` under the existing ~4 s deadline (report `partial` on timeout), or (b) clear `_sessionLiveSectorCookedId` when the sector demotes so a re-materialized sector re-runs the real cook with censuses. (b) is simpler and restores first-entry coverage parity; (a) keeps the decode savings.
- **Effort:** M · **Magic-frame impact:** M-H (re-entry is a routine path; cohort density decides whether the tail spills past lift) · **Risk:** (a) can extend the veil on dense re-entry — the mandate's intended trade (bounded loading beats presented pop-in); (b) re-pays the cook cost on re-entry — same trade, blunter.

### F3 — `pickNextWhere` re-grades the whole pending queue per pick (hunt-class a, observed — likely non-material)

**Evidence (MECHANISM_VERIFIED, minor):** `src/render/liveGeometryAdmission.js` ~95-113: every ambient pick walks `pending` O(Q) re-computing `entryPriority` for all entries, then `splice`s. The ambient lane drains ~1 root per present and `pending` stays small post-throttling, so per-pick cost is bounded tens of entries — it matches the hunt shape but not material scale. Observation only: if the urgent-lane batch path ever grows `pending` into the hundreds, convert the ambient pick to a maintained min-heap or sticky front-of-queue cursor rather than re-grading. **Effort:** S · **Impact:** L · **Risk:** trivial; probably not worth touching alone.
