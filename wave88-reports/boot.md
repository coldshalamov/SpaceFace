### F1 — four sync `reattachResidentGpuMeshes` call sites still atomically exhaust the W87-stepped sweep inside shell-visible tasks
- **Evidence:** `src/render/renderer.js:11745` (`prepareLiveSectorBeforeFlight` keep-GPU branch), `:16813` (`sector:enter` listener, keepGpu), `:17185` (`mode:changed` recook branch), `:17258` (`save:loaded` listener, keepGpu). Each calls the sync wrapper at `1782–1784`, which sync-exhausts `reattachResidentGpuMeshesSteps` — the stepped twin minted at `2076` exists but is only reachable through the paced pump's mint gate (`feedMoved || _residentReattachFrame === 0 || pacedFrameSpend() < 2`, `:2073`). All four sites run inside bus listeners or an async continuation task under `state.mode === 'loading'`; the `save:loaded` site lands inside a `drainEmitSlice` beat (`saveSystem.js:5055` budgets 8 listeners/beat, and this listener's atomic sweep rides inside one beat).
- **Mechanism:** the sweep is the renderer's largest shell-era walk — a full `_meshes` scan plus a lazy `collectMeshPresentationEntitiesChunked` census plus the rekey apply under `_meshReleaseBatchBegin/End`. Under a keep-GPU restore it runs at least once synchronously at the event, *and* the paced pump re-runs the whole sweep on the next tick once feeds move — the atomic cost is pure overhead on top of the stepped machinery W87 already built. Worse, the ordering guarantee these calls provide is implicit: `_reconcileIter` mints only while `!owner._residentReattachIter` (`:2110–2116`), so the calls can't simply be deleted — remove them and a dirty-flagged reconcile mints before any post-restore reattach, and `keepResidentSet` releases dead-id meshes the sweep could have re-keyed (kept-GPU set discarded and rebuilt — a quality regression, not just a perf one).
- **Fix sketch:** at each site, replace the sync call with `owner._residentReattachIter = reattachResidentGpuMeshesSteps(owner)` (mint only — the pump drives it under the 4 ms/paced clock). Ordering is preserved or strengthened: the reconcile mint already waits on `!_residentReattachIter`, and the minted iter beats the pump's own gate. For `:17185` (pre-restore task) the iter may drain over the pre-restore world — harmless: `feedMoved` re-mints post-restore regardless.
- **Effort:** S. **Impact:** M–H. **Risk:** low — the pump driver is unchanged; only the mint site moves.
