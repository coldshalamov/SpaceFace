## Findings (ranked)

### 1. partsLibrary authored-swap invalidates bypass the fault-rec un-latch — M impact / S effort / low risk
- `src/render/partsLibrary.js:3450, 4484, 9626, 9658, 9927` — five DIRECT `invalidateShadowCasterPolicy(boundary)` calls; none clear `owner._policyRootFaults`.
- The renderer-side wrapper `_invalidateShadowPolicy` (renderer.js:28868-28872) deletes the fault rec with the landed rationale: "the dirtySeq bump IS explicit evidence the tree changed." The partsLibrary sites are the strongest version of that evidence — fallback→authored grafts (3450, 4484 via commitAuthoredPlaceBoundary tail... actually the commit-internal call at 4484, plus 9927 ship path), retained-root LOD `swapTo` (9626), stale-retained detach (9658).
- Mechanism: a boundary that parked after ≥3 transient step-walk faults (e.g. mid-swap rebind) then receives its authored commit keeps serving the parked no-change verdict until `deferUntil` expires. First park ~30-90 `_viewSyncSeq`s (~0.5-1.5 s), doubling per heal→refault to cap 2048 (~34 s @60 fps, far longer under load since the unit is syncs, not seconds) — an authored hull that should cast shadows sits shadowless in presented frames.
- Fix sketch: stamp the fault rec's invalidation on the ROOT (e.g. `root.userData.sfPolicyFaultEpoch`) or clear it inside `invalidateShadowCasterPolicy` itself — every invalidate driver, renderer or library, already funnels through that export; then no call site can ever bypass the un-latch and the six manual sweep seams become belt, not load-bearing.

### 2. Un-latch discards the deferral escalation — broken-root + churny-boundary retry churn — M impact / S effort / low risk
- `notePolicyRootFault` (renderer.js:32148-32170) escalates `deferUntil` by `deferrals` doubling — the comment's own goal is decaying a persistently-bad root to ~one retry per 30 s. `_invalidateShadowPolicy` deletes the whole rec, so every invalidate on that boundary (any descendant change — LOD swap, packaged re-graft, rebuild) resets `deferrals` to 0.
- Mechanism: a deterministically faulting subtree on a boundary under active churn re-faults at the fresh ~30-90-sync cadence forever, and each retry mints a parked iterator + pays the stepped whole-scene receiver recount on the presented frame — exactly the hitchy frames this lane exists to shrink.
- Fix sketch: un-latch by clearing only `deferUntil`, preserving `faults`/`deferrals` (heal semantics unchanged: the next eval retries immediately, a re-fault continues the decay ladder); or stamp `rec.dirtySeq` at fault time and un-latch only when the invalidate's bump covers that subtree's generation.

### 3. `tryPresentedCall` parks SERVICE-class tails for the whole epoch — M impact / S effort / low risk
