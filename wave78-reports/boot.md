## Ranked findings

### F1 — `uniqueAdmissionUnits` never dedupes materials that *pass* `skipReady` — shared ready materials pay one `properties.get` probe per owning subject

- **Evidence**: `src/render/openingGpuAdmission.js:278-287`. The W77 reorder put `seenMaterials.has(material)` *before* `skipReady(material)` at `:280-281`, but a material that passes the probe (`skipReady` returns true → `continue` at `:281`) is never `seenMaterials.add`-ed. Every later subject sharing that material re-runs `skipReady` → `materialHasCompiledProgram` → `renderer.properties.get` + `currentProgram` read (`openingGpuAdmission.js:255-263`).
- **Mechanism**: the launch call site exists precisely for the recook case — the comment at `renderer.js:13060` says "a same-sector recook re-collects subjects whose programs already linked". On a recook the *dominant* material class is ready (already-linked) — i.e. exactly the class that cannot join the dedupe set. Probes scale as `sum over subjects of that subject's ready-material count` (~subjects × 1–3 materials each), not `unique materials`. Thousands of subjects × shared ready materials → low-single-digit-ms to ~10ms+ of atomic Map/property probes inside one unsliced block of the cook (`renderer.js:13067-13069`), behind the loading shell. The W77 measurement (18.2 ms pre-fix on a synthetic 21k-node scene) was dominated by this same probe profile on the not-ready side; the ready side is the half the reorder could not reach.
- **Fix sketch**: output-identical two-set (or earlier-add) dedupe — add every *probed* material to a `probedMaterials` set before the `skipReady` call, keep `seenMaterials` for kept materials only (or simply add to `seenMaterials` before `skipReady`: a ready material is skipped either way, so the outcome for later subjects is unchanged). Each unique material then pays exactly one probe.
- **Effort** S · **Magic-frame impact** M (bounded atomic stall inside the cook on recooks; zero flight effect) · **Risk** L — `materialHasCompiledProgram` is a pure read; dedupe-by-probed-identity is order- and output-identical.

### F2 — `cookCompileSubjects` re-traverses every compile-root subtree the fused census already walked

- **Evidence**: `src/render/renderer.js:13039` `collectInstancePoolCompileRootsAndSubjects(scene)` (one whole-scene walk, `latePipelineAdmission.js:189-211` — the code's own comment at `:13038` sizes it at ~21k nodes), then `:13055` `cookCompileRoots.flatMap((root) => collectCompileSubjects(root))` (`compilePresentSlice.js:6-18`) traverses each compile root's subtree again. Every member of `cookCompileRoots` is scene-mounted at collection time: pool roots are discovered *inside* the scene walk; `asteroidLeafWarmRoot` is `scene.add`-ed at `12801`; `crucibleWarmRoot` at `12852`; `stampedCookRoots` are cook-stamped content roots of the same scene.
- **Mechanism**: the W77 fusion merged the roots census with the *scene-wide* subjects list but not the per-root subjects list — nodes under compile roots are visited twice (once by the fused walk, once by `collectCompileSubjects`). On a sector where pool/stamped/warm roots hold a few thousand nodes this is ~2–15 ms of atomic DFS plus a second callback pass over each node, sitting in the same unsliced block as F1 (the only bound is the outer `cookDeadlineMs`, checked between steps — `cookOverBudget()` at `13057` gates `sceneCompileSubjects` but not this walk). This is the "optionally wrap/fuse the unit-construction block" follow-up W77-F2(c) contemplated but did not take.
- **Fix sketch**: bucket subjects by root during the fused walk — carry a compile-root membership context through `scene.traverse` (push on entering a pool/stamped/warm root — DFS guarantees a discovered pool root's descendants are visited under it — pop on exit; stamped/leaf/warm roots are known before the walk so they pre-mark their context). Emit `cookCompileSubjects` as the per-root buckets concatenated in `cookCompileRoots` order. Nested compile roots need mark-once semantics (a node's first enclosing root wins) to keep flatMap-equivalent membership.
- **Effort** M · **Magic-frame impact** M-L (bounded serial JS inside the cook; grows with compile-root subtree size on dense sectors — frozen-shell time, never flight hitching) · **Risk** M — `uniqueAdmissionUnits` dedupes first-wins and the admit batch consumes order; bucketing preserves per-root grouping only if concatenated in `cookCompileRoots` order, and nested-root edge cases (a stamped root containing a pool root) change grouping unless handled. Verify subject multiset identity on a seeded scene before landing.
