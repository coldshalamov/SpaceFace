- **Effort** S-M · **Magic-frame impact** H — this is the last flight-mode slicer family; every hard jump on hardware GL runs it, and its spend stacks on top of every paced lane it shares frames with · **Risk** L — pacing-only; ordering, yield points, and per-item work are unchanged.

### 3. Emit-queue overflow — `pendingSlicedEmits.length >= MAX_QUEUED_SLICED_EMITS` (8) delivers the oldest queued emit whole via `emitAll`, the one remaining unbounded emit path in flight

The private-clock seams are the mirror image: their first check fires only after `sliceMs` of their own work — a spent wallet never defers them — so they can't stall, and that is exactly why they double-spend (findings 1-2). The only wholesale-skip ledger sites remain the two aged entry gates: `drainDespawnDisposeQueue` (`:1809-1849`) and `_armDepthStage` (`:22252-22253`, `SHADOW_DEPTH_LEDGER_MAX_SKIPS = 2`) — bounded, queues intact.
