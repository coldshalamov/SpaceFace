# Breakwater Spur — integrated candidate

An optional Ceres industrial worksite: survey and recover two original18T
cartridges, seat them in real open receivers, commission with4scrap+1electronics,
and choose cooperative services or bonded freight. The operating and destination
cards, explicit chart course, scene lighting/audio and bounded worker views are
connected to the ordinary owners.

Native seating requires full-body mouth entry, actual support/dwell, accepted
line release and same-original fixed-body acknowledgement. The receiver markers
have no collision bodies. Damage and repairs retain the original condition.

Cargo owns atomic commissioning and real accepted finite lots. A generic unload
intent cannot create stock or wages. The native Mule must physically reach its
clear outboard loading apron before its job advances. Bonded receiving retains
an accepted original and deposited bond until return settlement. Economy owns
keyed one-time payments/refunds; refusal stays retryable.

The focused feature/cargo/worker batch passed67 cases. It covers native seating,
force-driven Mule arrival, real raider damage/defense, full save-owner restoration
of damaged fixed/dynamic originals and active actors, accepted-but-unsettled and
paid bonds, stale callbacks and lifecycle cleanup. The actual production Node
worker test covers offer/action/refusal/reset and restoring into a new host.
Some isolated custody cases initialize poses directly; they are not manual flight.

Ordinary player flight/towing, rendered receiving and service usability, the
complete defended encounter, and cold browser-worker Continue remain open.
This is a source candidate, not a published or whole-game release approval.

The controller retains creation and integration patches in dated run artifacts.
Do not apply historical patches over the integrated owners. Current Finish
outcomes are recorded in design/program/finish/INTEGRATION_COVERAGE.md.
