# The Cinderglass Job — integrated implementation

The live assembly includes Breakwater, Counterweight and Cinderglass. The original author proposal
and its patch generators are historical creation artifacts; production and tests import live owners.

## Player operation

After the actual first Tethys `alloy_run` delivery, request Tala Rook's disputed consignment through
Contracts. Find the original convoy, scan its markings, then negotiate during the inspection hold,
service the external latch unseen, or use ordinary combat against its finite external target. Tow
the same original pod to the chosen receiver and explicitly accept its current physical receive
quote. Revisit the parties and journal for the outcome. Possession alone never pays.

The cohort is one Mule, two Wasps and one original 18-tonne pod. Its four-leg route is driven by
canonical NPC jobs. The 60-HP external latch uses a released salvage-clamp part and a real physical
attachment. Scanner identity, line of sight, uninterrupted service, original identity, generation,
native attachment and receiver custody are validated by their owners.

Negotiation reserves the disclosed 150-credit deposit through the wallet owner. Tala's accepted
return pays 450 credits intact or 300 damaged, plus a legitimate deposit refund. The broker's
accepted intact original pays 300 credits and preserves the unlawful-sale consequences. Cargo owns
physical transfer; economy owns credits and exactly-once settlement. No caller-supplied amount,
proximity-only sale, reminted original or intent-only delivery is accepted.

## Integration and direct checks

Production factories, init/update/reset, browser prompt, command surface, bounded worker mirrors,
save validation, combat/scanner seams, chart, journal and Contracts are connected. Each occupation
borrows one prompt owner. The real cargo/economy methods are in ownerSeams.js.

Focused live-owner suites cover physical release, service and force, refusal/debit, custody,
settlement, native convoy progress and Continue. Combined production-host checks exercise all three
commissions, stale commands, malformed optional-save refusal before destructive restore, paid-record
preservation and reset. A browser-mount check verifies single prompt ownership in main and worker
lanes. These are implementation and owner/transport checks.

Ordinary earned campaign play, gameplay-camera latch readability and aimed combat, full rendered
tow/receive/aftermath, and cold browser-worker Continue remain unverified. This document does not
claim publication, player-route acceptance or whole-game release readiness.
