# Counterplay live adapter

`tacticalAI` calls the ten custom policies for tagged hulls at the existing fixed step. These hulls and their explicitly owned companions are excluded from the stock planner, squad choreography and cohort driver. Untagged actors retain the stock behavior and cadence. Capital score ownership remains authoritative.

Only the existing `aiSensors.frameFor` supplies external positions. Policies apply additional range, rock occlusion and twelve-tick reaction delay. Actor role and generation metadata cannot reveal a hidden pose. Committed points retain their snapshot; recycled entity slots clear delayed sightings and bind durable case roles again.

Maneuvers pass through the existing heavy/capital/Nemesis shaping port. Fire passes canonical engagement authority and live capital/Nemesis denial, with actual ROE and response windows. Latch, reel and cut use the existing combat attachment service; repairs use the subsystem writer; companion orders require a sensed allied actor stamped with this controller. No native owner, teleport or second integrator is stored on GameState.

Adventure approach within 700 WU of a charted situation activates an authored cast in Helios, Ceres, Vesta or Tethys. Every policy has a public situation; labels, receipt toast and `ai:telegraph` announce its physical goal and counter. Casts carry `flags.persistent`, durable case keys and home-sector ids. Existing world.discovery[sector].pois journals prevent replay after the cast is gone. Ordinary entity data serialization preserves custom doctrine, objective and memory; loaded casts rebind numeric role references. No registry entry, debug flag or new save domain is required.

The arena remains an explicit policy fixture, while test/counterplay-production.test.mjs exercises the real tactical owner, native rope, cut, allied repair, companion delivery, live sensors, Adventure activation and real projectile damage. Arena attachment verbs now use the same custody service. A stationary pilot and charted peek expose actual shot outcomes; damage scoring initializes from actual player vitals.
