# src/testing/

Lab harnesses and scenario fixtures. Not a registered gameplay system.

- Start at `lab/AGENTS.md` and `docs/VALIDATION_WORKFLOW.md`.
- `metrics/`, `perf/`, `policies/`, and `scenarios/` support those proofs. They do not own player outcomes.
- A scenario that needs a live system goes through the registry or the authoritative runtime. Do not copy a second update order into a fixture.
