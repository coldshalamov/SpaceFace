## Findings (ranked)

### 1. The deferred warm's exemplar-build and palette-mint legs pace on private 4 ms clocks with macrotask yields — they never consult `pacedFrameSpend()`, so their slices stack on top of a frame's already-spent paced wallet, and the once-at-end debit posts the whole leg's wall time (yields included) into whatever epoch hosts the end

Evidence: `src/render/renderer.js:17478-17489` (build leg: `buildNow() - sliceStartedAt > 4 → await yieldToBrowser()` per ship exemplar), `17529-17532` (identical hulk leg), `17570` (`notePacedFrameSpend(buildNow() - buildStartedAt)` — one debit of the whole leg's wall span, in `finally`), `17736-17747` (mint leg: same private-clock shape; comment claims "debit the paced ledger so sibling slicers see the spend" but no `pacedFrameSpend()` consult exists in the loop), `17788` (`notePacedFrameSpend(scanNow() - scanStartedAt)` — same end-only debit), `src/render/startupGpuResidency.js:684-689` (`yieldToBrowser` = `scheduler.yield()` or `setTimeout(0)` — a same-priority macrotask, not a present boundary), `src/render/decodeTaskBudget.js:84-107` (spend buckets keyed per presented epoch / stale-epoch wall window). Contrast pattern: every other in-flight slicer consults the ledger mid-walk — despawn entry `1817`, reconcile `2134`, poll `2181`, hold-exempt `2557+`, deferred-enter `sectorEnterDefer.js:155`, emit/tail drains `eventBus.js:349/416`, park sweep `17845`, compose driver `partsLibrary.js:10317`, opening publication resume `partsLibrary.js:6338`, mesh-build drain via `usePacedLedger` `19194-19201`, depth arm `21981`.

Mechanism: both legs run during the armory dwell while the draft screen presents frames. Two stacked gaps: (i) the slice check reads only the leg's own clock — on a frame whose 4 ms paced wallet the sibling drains already spent, each leg still runs its own ~4 ms slice, and the macrotask yield can host several such slices inside one presented frame (nothing ties the yield to a present); (ii) the debit posts once at leg end and posts the leg's entire wall duration — including every ms spent inside `yieldToBrowser` — into the epoch that happens to host the end. Sibling slicers therefore see ~0 leg spend for the leg's whole run and then one enormous debit (potentially hundreds of ms) that stands every paced drain down on a frame the leg didn't even touch. Burst-then-starve pacing, exactly in the dwell this wave exists to keep smooth. What the player sees: armory/draft frame-time spikes on warm waves under load — a hitch class the magic-frame bar names directly.

Fix sketch: mirror the park sweep's own contract (`17830-17845`): debit per-slice (`notePacedFrameSpend(now - lastDebit)` inside the loop) and extend the slice check to `ownClock > 4 || pacedFrameSpend() >= PACED_FRAME_BUDGET_MS`; optionally swap `yieldToBrowser()` for `yieldToNextPresent`/`admissionPaceYield` while `state.mode === 'flight'` so slices land post-present like every other flight slicer. Effort: **S**. Magic-frame impact: **M** (bounded per slice, but it fires inside the armory dwell on every warm wave and stacks with whatever else that frame paid). Risk: **L** — ordering/consult changes only; the ledger contract is identical at nine other sites.

### 2. `createSlicedYield` — the slicer behind the flight-time residency lane (`gpuResidencyAdmissions.prepare` → `prepareStartupGpuResidency`) and the in-flight sector-admission yields — consults only its private `sliceMs` clock and never consults or debits the paced ledger, so its work is invisible to sibling slicers on both sides

Evidence: `src/render/pipelineReadiness.js:133-147` (private `sliceMs` clock; yields only when its own elapsed time exceeds the target — no ledger read or write), `src/render/renderer.js:9936-9950` (`gpuResidencyAdmissions` yield: `sliceMs: ADMISSION_SLICE_TARGET_MS` = 3, `admissionSliceBudget.js:9`; flight yield = `admissionPaceYield()` = `yieldToNextPresent({boundMs:24})` — present-bound, `9767`), `10118-10123` (every non-skipped subject admission ends in `preparePipelineSubjectResidency` on this lane), `11052`/`11620` (`createSlicedYield(yieldLiveSectorGpu…)` — sector-admission lanes reachable during flight-mode handoffs; the `sliceMs:16` lanes at `11966/12005/13054/14034` are loading-only).

Mechanism: the flight yield IS present-bound, so the lane pays at most ~one 3 ms slice + item per present — but the slice condition never asks whether the frame's paced wallet is already spent, and the work is never debited, so sibling slicers keep spending as though it did not run. Additive, bounded per present — smaller than finding 1's shape (which can stack N slices inside one frame) but the same contract hole, in the utility several flight lanes share.

Fix sketch: plumb a ledger check through `createSlicedYield`'s options (e.g. `options.shouldYield` consulted alongside the private clock, or wrap `yieldToMain` at the `gpuResidencyAdmissions` call site with `pacedFrameSpend() >= PACED_FRAME_BUDGET_MS → yield` and a `notePacedFrameSpend` debit for the elapsed slice). Effort: **S–M**. Magic-frame impact: **L–M**. Risk: **L** — consult/debit additions only; per-present pacing semantics unchanged.
