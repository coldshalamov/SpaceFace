- Evidence: `src/render/assetResidency.js:654-664` and `:691-703` (`enforceSoftResidencyBudgets` + Steps twin call `hasActiveRequestForEntry(entry)` per asset — an O(P) linear scan of `pendingRequests` at `:513-519`); same per-entry consult at `:942` (`releaseUnreferencedCacheOwnersPassSteps`), `:812`/`:837` (`evictOldestSoftEntries`+Steps per candidate), `:1028-1030` (`evictIfUnowned` per release).

- Effort: S-M · Impact: M-H · Risk: L (pure read-path speedup; version counter touches 3 functions).

**P2 — `afterEntries` second census is provably replaceable by a filter of the first**
- Evidence: `src/render/assetResidency.js:1456-1458`.
- Mechanism: when `plan.evict` is non-empty the code re-runs the full `governorEntry` census inside the sector:exit emit. So `afterEntries = entries.filter(e => assets.has(e.key))` is exact, not approximate.
- Fix sketch: replace the re-census with the filter; pin a test asserting receipt parity (blockedBytesByRole/byReason) vs. a fresh census.
- Effort: S · Impact: M · Risk: L.

- Effort: S · Impact: M · Risk: M — needs one pinned test (armed flag still drains across a pass; no re-arm after a draining enforce).
