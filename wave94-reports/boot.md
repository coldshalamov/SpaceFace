## Findings (ranked)

### F1 — drift-exhaustion shape mints `{skipped:true}` WITHOUT `aborted` — the arm's denied-park gate cannot tell "evaluated clean" from "never ran": false undrawable parks on census churn — effort S, magic-frame M, risk L

Evidence: `driveCompileShadowDepthPipelines` (`src/render/renderer.js:530-556`) retries only `step.value.stale === true`; after 4 attempts it returns `{skipped:true, reason:'light-census-drifted-repeatedly', subjects:0}` at `:556` — no `aborted` field. The W93 post-opening twin mints the identical shape at `:15596-15598` when the last retry wasn't session-closed. The single behavioral consumer of any drive result is the arm leg's verdict gate: `legAborted = !!(legResult && legResult.aborted === true)` at `:25459`. A drifted-exhaustion `legResult` has `aborted === undefined` → the denied-park block (`:25460-25503`) runs its `allUnmarked` evaluation on a cohort whose marks could never have landed — every `legSet` mesh fails `casterDepthMarkCurrent` because the drive never staged — so each root parks `denied:true` into `_parkedDepthStageRoots` (`:25473-25497`) under the `sfDepthUndrawableCycles` geometric recheck backoff (~96-128 arm passes × up to 8× ≈ up to ~16 s of withheld `castShadow=false`). The sibling skip shapes DO carry the marker — `session-closed-mid-drive` (`:885`,`:1017` propagated at `:748`) and `shadow-map-disabled` (`:895`) — because they mint at the callee; the driver-minted exhaustion shape was never brought under the same contract. The wave log documents this exact family once already ("closed-skip→undrawable-park window", PR194-WAVE-LOG:1604) and W93 boot's own enumeration asserted "no consumer parks undrawable on a skip-without-abort read" — true for callee-minted shapes; the driver-level exhaustion shape is the missed case.

Mechanism (what the player sees): census churn is a boot-window condition — sector/station/pool lights mount in bursts behind the shell and through first flight, each bumping `shadowCensusEpoch`. A drive suspended on `postPace`/`yieldToBrowser` under that churn burns its 4 stale retries, and the arm then parks the deferred cohort as undrawable-forever: parked casters draw no shadows for up to ~16 s into flight, and the park's own recheck cadence re-pays collect→offer→drive cycles on a root that was never undrawable. Self-healing via the denied-park `allowCast`-flip release and the census/ortho-keyed recheck, but long-lived.

Fix sketch: add `aborted: true` to both `{skipped:true, reason:'light-census-drifted-repeatedly'}` returns (`:556`, `:15598`) — the established W93 contract — or, more robustly, widen the gate at `:25459` to `legResult && legResult.skipped === true`: every skipped drive means zero marks could have landed, which is the invariant the comment at `:25451-25458` already states. The wider gate also future-proofs the other non-aborted skip shapes (e.g. `'shadow depth compiler unavailable'` at `:683`).

### F2 — detached-root compile-subject fallback is unpaced: `collectCompileSubjects` runs a whole `root.traverse` per root inline inside a "paced" leg — effort S, magic-frame M, risk L

Evidence: `subjectsForCompileRoots` (`renderer.js:15363-15398`) paces its per-subject bucket walk correctly (`await postPace()` per subject at `:15384`), but the terminal `out` loop (`:15391-15397`) calls `collectCompileSubjects(root)` — a full `root.traverse` (compilePresentSlice.js:6-18) — for every `bucket === undefined` root with NO `postPace` between roots. Unbucketed roots are exactly the detached ones the scene census cannot reach: `_rosterPrewarmRoots` (spread into `rescanRoots` at `:15639`; pushed at `:14188`,`:14239`,`:17701`,`:17791`,`:17814`,`:18673`,`:18705`,`:19482`,…) and `_rosterPartCatalogRoot` (`:15641`) are prepared/warm roots parked OFF the scene graph, so `censusSubjects` (a scene walk) provably never contains their members and `enclosingCompileRoot` can never map them — every one takes the inline-traverse branch. K detached warm roots × their subtree sizes land inside one paced leg. Twin site: the `cookCompileRoots.flatMap` fallback at `:14486-14492` inside `cookLiveSceneGpu` (same shape under `paceCookStretch`'s jurisdiction — the bucket loop paces at `:14479`, the flatMap does not).

Mechanism: the predictive-warm surface is largest exactly in the opening window (roster prewarms mint per archetype plus leaf/catalog roots), so a rescan pass carrying dozens of detached roots pays dozens of subtree traverses back-to-back inside one leg — a hidden collect that dodges every wallet and slice clock introduced this wave. On a packed-warm rescan this is the largest un-budgeted atomic stretch left on the boot path.

Fix sketch: `await postPace()` inside the `bucket === undefined` branch (per root — the traverse IS the slice's cost; at minimum every N roots and on elapsed), or mint a `collectCompileSubjectsSteps` chunked twin driven under `postPace`. Same edit at `:14487` under `paceCookStretch`. Order and verdicts unchanged — pure pacing.

### F3 — `UNSTAGED_COLLECT_OVER_COVER` consumer census is clean; the residual is a caller-side trap for the next edit — effort S, magic-frame L, risk none (hygiene)

Evidence: producers `collectUnstagedShadowCastersFlag` / `collectUnstagedShadowCasters` (`shadowDepthAdmission.js:375`,`:504`); consumers `renderer.js:10679` (batch flag → whole-subtree withhold), `:15609` (post-opening break), `:15685` (rescan break), `:24949` (pass over-cover → withhold), `:25343` (arm escalate→park at max scale), `:25694` (toggle-task `queueOverCover`) — every one guarded by `=== UNSTAGED_COLLECT_OVER_COVER`, and the pin test (`test/shadow-depth-admission.test.mjs:654-666`) locks the producer contract (`{remaining:0}` → string sentinel, `{remaining:64}` → `Array.isArray`). No live defect — enumerated. The residual is structural: the sentinel is the 24-char string `'sfUnstagedCollectOverCover'`, so a future caller that forgets `===` and treats the return as an array reads `.length` = 24 (truthy — the wrong direction) and `for..of`/`push(...x)` iterates characters → `mesh.castShadow = false` on a char throws TypeError under module strict mode inside a presented frame. Swapping to a Symbol/object sentinel flips the failure mode to silent-empty (`undefined.length > 0` → false → the caller concludes "nothing unstaged" → skips the withhold → cold depth links) — no better. Fix sketch: export `isUnstagedCollectOverCover(x)` beside the constant so callers never hand-roll the check, and extend the pin to assert non-array on both producers. Cheap insurance on a contract that has already been misremembered once (W93 boot F1's fix note).
