### 1. `_drainMeshBuildQueueSteps` epilogue debits the drain's whole suspended lifetime into the completion frame — over-debit starves every paced sibling (H)

- **Evidence:** `src/render/renderer.js:20175` mints `const startedAtMs = performance.now()` inside the *generator body*; `:20465-20467` fires `if (built > 0 || now() - startedAtMs > 0) notePacedFrameSpend(now() - startedAtMs)` at generator end. Epilogue is verbatim-carried from the pre-W79 sync drain (`git show 5810add6e~1` — same `now() - startedAtMs` epilogue, then correct because the call was synchronous).
- **Mechanism:** under the sweep drivers (`yield*` at `:19738`/`:20058`) and the cook driver (`drainIter.next()` across `await buildYield` at `:11295-11309`), the generator parks across presented frames. The epilogue debits `now() - startedAtMs` = the drain's entire multi-frame wall life — suspension gaps **plus** CPU already honestly debited by the enclosing drivers (`reconcileSliceStart`/`pollSliceStart` spans at `:2186`/`:2233`, `createSlicedYield` debit at `:11275-11278`) — as a single lump into the *completion* frame's epoch bucket (`decodeTaskBudget.js:84-99`). A drain spanning ~10 slow frames posts hundreds of ms into one frame. `pacedFrameSpend() >= PACED_FRAME_BUDGET_MS` then reads permanently true that frame: every ledger-gated lane — eventBus sliced-emit and presentation drains (`src/core/eventBus.js:349`/`:416`), `sectorEnterDefer` (`:155`), `shouldContinueAdmissionSlice` (`src/render/admissionSliceBudget.js:38`), the drain's own continue check (`:20255`), deferred-warm build/census loops — parks for that frame. One full paced-lane stall per drain completion, worst exactly in heavy scenes where drains span the most frames.
- **Fix sketch:** segment-accurate debiting inside the generator: re-mint `segStartedAt` after each `yield` resume and `notePacedFrameSpend(now() - segStartedAt)` at each yield/return (or accumulate `spentMs` per segment and debit once). Identical total under the inline-drained sync path (segments sum to the whole span); drops suspend windows and the double-count under suspended drivers. Do **not** just delete the epilogue: `_drainPendingMeshBuilds` (`:20064`) / `_drainProtectedFirstFlightBuilds` (`:20120`) / the jump-cook sync call (`:14097`) rely on it to charge the drain's span.
- **Effort:** S. **Impact:** H — this corrupts the fairness signal the whole W78/W79 pacing scheme steers by. **Risk:** L — accounting only; identical under sync drain.
