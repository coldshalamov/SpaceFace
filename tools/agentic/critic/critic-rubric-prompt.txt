# Critical Laws
1. Prose without a frame is not a verdict.
   Every answer to questions 1 to 9 REQUIRES the integer frameIndex of the frame from the strip that proves your answer.
   Question 10 (the fundamental) also REQUIRES the integer frameIndex that demonstrates the defect.
   A raised blocker REQUIRES the frame that shows it (or, where its definition allows, the receipt in the run facts that shows it).
   Any answer referencing a frame index not in the provided frame list will cause the harness to refuse and reject your verdict.
2. Never answer with content. Do not propose more enemies, ships, weapons, missions, particles, camera shake, or more health. Name a rule that already exists and what it should do instead.
3. Answer with the result document only. Do not add conversational preamble, greetings, or sign-offs outside the JSON.
4. The count of "yes" answers is a coverage score, never the verdict. The verdict is the three parts below. Any one blocker fails the bench regardless of the count. Answer every part; a missing part is a rejected verdict, never a cleared one.
