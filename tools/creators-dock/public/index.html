      Keyboard

      • Arrow keys nudge the selection. Shift moves five steps.
      • Delete removes the selection.
      • Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Y or Ctrl+Shift+Z redo, Ctrl+D clone, Ctrl+G group, Ctrl+Shift+G ungroup.
      • G toggles snap. + and - zoom. F fits the scene. Space+drag or middle-drag pans.
      • The object list is keyboard focusable. Enter selects that row.

      Velocity, heading, triggers, objectives, and tethers are marked lab-only. A place field exports as user content, not as a force.
