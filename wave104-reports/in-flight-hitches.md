## Findings (ranked)

### H1 — Restore side of the hidden-freeze pair has no dwell: period-≥4 flaps still pay the mint+restore pair per cycle

**Evidence:** freeze queue entries defer until `viewSeq - max(lastShow, queuedSeq) >= 3` (:21300-21304); the unfreeze drain has no corresponding show-age gate — it deletes the flag and mints `unfreezeHiddenSubtreeMatrices` at first visit once the entry passes the drop checks (:21358-21365). Re-show stamps `sfHiddenFreezeLastShowSeq` and queues the restore in the same pass (:23572-23585).

**Mechanism — the enumerated oscillation patterns:** (i) *slow flapper*: hidden ≥3 passes mints a freeze (~2 subtree walks: `traverse` + `markStaticMatrixSubtrees`, `staticChildMatrices.js:155-169`), shows 1 pass → restore mints (~3 walks: traverse + ancestor clear + re-mark, :177-201), re-hides ≥3 → re-freeze. A period-5 hide/show oscillator pays ~7 subtree walks per 5 passes to save ~3 compose-frames — net negative until the hidden streak exceeds ~7 passes. (ii) *dwell expires between queue and drain*: an entry queued at seq S but unvisited for ≥3 passes (backlog or a same-pass re-show+re-hide where `lastShow` moved S→S+2 → dwell re-derives to S+5 correctly, so this seam is closed only when `lastShow` advances — a root re-shown while its entry sits unvisited past the original dwell still defers via the `max(lastShow, queuedSeq)` term: covered; the remaining loss is pattern (i), where mint already spent). (iii) *same-cycle re-hide*: re-show clears `sfHiddenFrozen` and queues restore; re-hide re-queues freeze (`sfHiddenFreezeQueued` was deleted at mint :21305); the pending restore drops via `sfHiddenFreezeQueued === true` (:21362) — no restore walk, but the next dwell+mint re-pays the freeze. Asymmetry is the defect class: hysteresis exists only on the hide side.

**Fix sketch:** mirror the dwell on the restore drain — defer `unfreezeHiddenSubtreeMatrices` until `viewSeq - ud.sfHiddenFreezeLastShowSeq >= HIDDEN_FREEZE_FLAP_DWELL` (keep `pending[w++]`, don't drop; the re-cull drop already handles re-hide). Stronger variant worth the extra care: keep `ud.sfHiddenFrozen === true` until the restore mint actually runs, so a sub-dwell re-hide early-outs `_queueHiddenSubtreeFreeze` at :21260 and costs zero re-walks — interior `sfCullFrozen` marks persist and force-descend keeps the pose correct through the window (the deferral contract already documented at :21322-21329). Both are contract-preserving: no output changes, only when work lands.

**Effort S (gate) / M (flag-semantics variant) · magic-frame impact M · risk low** — flap-boundary sectors and zoom-at-cull-edge cameras are exactly where a 6-mint backlog already exists; the gate costs a comment's worth of hysteresis.

### H2 — `notedWalk` ancestry probes pay O(depth) per pop while `detached` is non-empty, and O(depth) per remove-note

**Evidence:** pop-time ghost probe `for (let p = object; p; p = p.parent) { if (detached.has(p)) break; if (p === scene) live = true; }` at `shadowReceiverTally.js:58-64` — runs for every pop whenever `detached.size > 0` for the walk's remainder; note-time probe `notedWalkRootFinished` walks `lastPopped`→scene ancestry (:136-141) up to once per remove-note.

**Mechanism — worst case inside a paced beat:** a combat despawn burst mid-recount seeds `detached`; every subsequent pop of the remaining ~N-node walk pays an ancestry walk to `scene` (depth ~10-30 on authored hierarchies) — N×d parent hops folded into the 4ms/512-node slices: the slices complete on time but carry ~10-30% probe overhead, stretching the dirty window (and the armed-walk `visits` write span) by the same factor. The note-time probe is the same shape at ~1 call per removal — M notes × depth ≪ N×d, so the pop side dominates.

**Cheaper alternatives — both legal:** (i) carry `dead` on a parallel int array alongside `stack`: `dead = parentDead || detached.has(node)` at push time makes each pop O(1); nodes stacked *before* their ancestor detached are the only stale entries — a remove-note can mark them by one pass over the live frontier (`stack.length` ≤ fan-out × depth, typically ≤ a few hundred) once per note — total O(notes × frontier) replaces O(pops × depth). (ii) `notedWalkRootFinished` becomes O(1) if the stack carries a child-index per node (parallel arrays, no per-node alloc): subtree finished ⇔ `lastPopped`'s frame reached its end — or equivalently maintain an open-ancestor chain cursor walked back on each pop; a depth stamp alone is insufficient (siblings at equal depth aren't comparable — needs ancestry or frame-end bookkeeping). The sketch must keep the exact semantics: skipping the -delta under a mid-descent removal is what keeps the prefix conservative-on.

**Effort M · impact L-M · risk low** — storm-slice CPU bound, not a spike; primarily stretches the recount window on despawn-heavy frames.

### H3 — Remounted mid-teardown corpses re-arm only castShadow; ~7 torn-state classes have no flag (lod lane L4 — confirmed, two extra edges)

**Evidence:** flag mint :2059-2062, consume :25957-25960 → `invalidateShadowCasterPolicy` (cast flags only). **Mechanism:** `m.parent != null` at drain head (:2052) means a live owner re-mounted the boundary while its parked iterator held a torn prefix. The re-arm covers the caster registry; the remaining torn classes surface as: released residency rows → the still-mounted subtree's records become evictable → authored pop-out plus a decode churn inside presented frames (the hitch class, not just a visual gap); disposed geometry/materials → first draw re-uploads/re-links inside `projectObject` (same brick class as an unmasked link); released owner instance slots → missing instanced draws until re-adopted; unregistered prepared-authored roots → parked payload never commits → pop-in later; detached motion pivots → dead motion channels; torn retained-LOD levels → demote-swap pop-in. `heldLight` epoch bump (:28627/:28650) is likewise skipped for a parked teardown.

**Two edges beyond lod L4's enumeration:** (i) the step-throw path (:2104-2109) sets `iter._measure = null`, `iterDone = true`, consumes the corpse — if the same mesh is later re-mounted, it carries a torn prefix *with no flag at all* (the mint only fires at the `m.parent != null` head-check while `abandonedIter._corpse === m`; a thrown iterator is already gone). Rare — requires a dispose-hook throw AND remount — but silently unflagged. Cheap hardening: stamp the flag (or `sfDespawnNoted`-style tombstone) on the throw path when `m` is identifiable. (ii) `heldLight` is unknowable mid-park — the conservative cover is a one-line `noteShadowCensusLightMutation()` whenever the flag is consumed at :25957-25960.

**Fix sketch (same direction as lod L4):** widen `sfDespawnPartialTeardown` into a torn-state record consumed on the remount path — re-run bind-time acquisitions over the subtree (residency re-claim, owner-slot re-adopt, motion re-attach, prepared-root re-register) with per-corpse generation dedup so only the torn prefix changes state; bump the census epoch unconditionally on consume. Effort L · impact M · risk medium — trigger is rare but lands on churn frames; per-hook idempotence must be audited first.

### H4 — `visits` Map persists for the whole armed walk (lod lane L5 — confirmed; `detached` persists too)

**Evidence:** `visits.set` arm-gated per live pop (:81-83); cleared only at walk end (`clearNotedWalk` :123-130) or re-mint (`resetNotedWalk` :109-122). A noted storm mid-recount pins one Map entry per live-popped node — full-scene worst case (tens of thousands of entries, a few MB + per-pop Map hash on the storm frames the tally exists to protect).

**Enumeration:** pruning `visits` entries below the oldest pending note's `seq` is provably safe — every consult treats a miss as unvisited: stamp compare `visitSeq === undefined || visitSeq <= note.seq` (:261-264) applies the delta (conservative-on for adds), the -delta creation gate at :190 already wrote the note if the root was visited *at note time*, and the pair-delete at :176-179 fires identically on a pruned miss. `notedWalk.detached` is likewise never pruned mid-walk — bounded by removal count (≤ number of removed roots, ≤ subtree count) so it's the smaller term; the same FIFO window could cover it only if removal roots were re-added (the `detached.delete` at :161 already handles that edge — pruning would need it preserved, so a seq-window prune on `visits` is the clean half).

**Fix sketch:** at each slice yield, drop `visits` entries with `seq < min(pending note.seq)` — Map preserves insertion order so one early-exit scan suffices — or a hard FIFO cap (~4k). Mirror the conservative-on argument in the comment (lod lane's wording applies verbatim). Effort S · impact L · risk low — memory/churn bound on storm frames.

### H6 — `_releaseSurvivalRosterPrewarm` queues never-mounted roots into the despawn drain

**Evidence:** the roots loop pushes every entry to `_despawnDisposeQueue` unconditionally at :20837-20838 (no `wasMounted`-equivalent gate — the capture at :20821 only steers the note).

**Mechanism:** a roster root released before it ever mounted still pays a parked-iterator drain slot (up to `DESPAWN_DISPOSE_DRAIN_MAX` per beat shared with real corpses). Accounting is correct — mount-time `+K` notes are answered by the drain's measured `-K` walk on the detached-but-untorn subtree, netting zero — but a release batch of ~hundreds of warm roots delays real corpse drains behind no-op subtrees and pays O(roots × subtree) paced walk time that buys nothing. Harmless to the count; mildly wasteful of paced capacity at run end.

**Fix sketch:** gate the push on the captured mounted flag — unmounted roots go straight to `disposePreparedAuthoredBoundary`/disposer with an inline measured note (same shape as `teardownWarm`'s unmounted branch :18975-18984). Effort S · impact L · risk low.
