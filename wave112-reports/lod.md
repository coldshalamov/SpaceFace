## Findings (ranked)

### F1 — Unsigned-window arm legs denied-park every root they successfully staged
**Evidence:** `renderer.js:29479` (`lightSig = this._depthCollectLightSig(scene)` — serves null inside presented frames whenever the memo is unsigned: fresh scene swap, post-mutation re-mint, storm pin), `:29673` (`casterDepthMarkCurrent(mesh, lightSig, camera)` inside the leg's `.finally` undrawable verdict), `:29680-29716` (`pending.delete(deferRoot)` + `parkedMap.set(...denied:true, deniedAllowCast:true...)`) plus `_withheldDepthCasters.set(deferRoot, …)` at `:29719-21`, and the same stale-null compare feeding `leftoverByRoot` at `:29746`.
**Mechanism:** `casterDepthMarkCurrent` requires `mark.l === lightSig` (shadowDepthAdmission.js:334). Marks are always keyed by a real string — the ceremony derives `markLightSig` from its own fused census when the override is null (`:717-725`), or takes the refreshed `lightSigFor()` override on drift retries (`renderer.js:642-645`). So a leg armed under `lightSig = null` that completes lands real marks — and the verdict comparing them against `null` can never hit: `allUnmarked` stays true for every deferRoot whose meshes all landed in `legSet`, and the .finally parks them all as "denied/undrawable." Withheld casters keep `castShadow=false` until the recheck cadence releases them — `Math.ceil((96 + stamp%32) * min(8, 2^(cycles-1)) * glassAdj)` at `:29696` ≈ 1.6–2.7 s on the first park, escalating to ~16 s/cycle, and `sfDepthUndrawableCycles` survives release so each re-park costs more. Every sector enter mints a fresh unsigned window (memo replaced at `:29155`), so the misfire recurs on the exact fresh-census path — newly-staged station/ship casters go shadowless for seconds after jumping. The same null compare at `:29517` (unstagedCache re-prove) and `:29746` (leftover) degrades to "everything unstaged" — bounded re-collect churn feeding more roots into the misfire.
**Fix sketch:** a null arm sig can't prove unmarkedness — gate the undrawable verdict and the leftover eval on `lightSig != null` (defer the verdict to the next arm; roots stay queued), or compare against the sig the ceremony actually marked under (the session exposes it via `get lightSig()` at shadowDepthAdmission.js:1049 — plumb it onto the leg result). Cheap alternative: re-consult `this._shadowCensusForFrame()` inside the .finally — the memo serves the same real sig the marks took once landed.
**Effort:** S. **Magic-frame impact:** H (real models rendered shadowless for seconds on the routine sector-enter path — the visual class this lane exists to kill; recurrence is per unsigned window, not a rare fault). **Risk:** low — deferring the verdict keeps roots queued = the pre-park steady state.

### F2 — `collectUnstagedShadowCasters` still pays an atomic `lightCensusSignature` per call under a null override
**Evidence:** `shadowDepthAdmission.js:553` (`typeof lightSigOverride === 'string' ? … : lightCensusSignature(lightingScene)` — synchronous whole-scene traverse). Presented-frame consults now route `_depthCollectLightSig`, which only mints when `paceEpochNow() < 0` (`renderer.js:29316` — headless/hidden windows); in live play it serves the memo's sig-or-null. So every call reached during a presented unsigned window still mints its own traverse: the arm's per-root loop at `renderer.js:29534` (up to 32 calls under one `SHADOW_DEPTH_ARM_COLLECT_MS` deadline that cannot interrupt an atomic traverse), the post-opening 512-subject chunk loop at `:17573`, and the rescan delta leg downstream of `:17600`.
**Mechanism:** the W111 fix deduped the *consult* side; the callee fallback is the same unmetered O(lightingScene) walk W111's F1 reported — relocated, not closed. Worst case: an arm firing early in a sector entry pays 32 whole-scene traverses inside one presented frame (~2–4 ms each on a dressed scene), and two roots can observe different light sets mid-arm since each mint re-reads the live scene.
**Fix sketch:** serve the memo's in-flight/last-good sig (the memo already deliberately serve-lasts), or have `collectUnstagedShadowCasters` accept the shared per-`_viewSyncSeq` consult instead of minting (extend `_depthCollectLightSig`'s cache consult into the fallback — must not mint atomically inside presented frames; serve-last or defer the root to the next arm, matching the flag-consult's withhold direction).
**Effort:** M. **Magic-frame impact:** M-H (atomic O(scene) traverses multiplied per call inside presented frames — bounded to unsigned windows, but those recur at every sector enter / census churn). **Risk:** M — the fix must not reintroduce an unmetered mint; deferring roots is safe, serving a stale sig needs the epoch check.

### F3 — Row-fault heal can't heal `updateDamageState`: latches commit before the fallible call
**Evidence:** `renderer.js:26063-68` — `userData._dmgHull/_dmgShield/_dmgAlive` are written to the entity's current values *before* `userData.updateDamageState(entity, simNow)` runs, all inside the row `try` whose catch lands at `26181` (`notePresentationRowFault`).
**Mechanism:** hunt (d)'s stamp-before-throw class: a fault inside the closure leaves the latches claiming the new hull/shield/alive state was applied. The bounded retry (≤3 faults, ≥1 s) re-runs the row — but the latch compare `entity.hull !== userData._dmgHull || …` now reads false, so `updateDamageState` is skipped forever: the damage-flash/shield-drop visual for that transition is permanently lost until the next state change. The W111 heal verdict ("all row writes are set-to-current idempotent") missed this latch pair. Sibling ordering is correct (`_appliedLodLevel` stamps after `updateLod` at `25905-06`); also noted: `world.clearDirty(slot)` precedes `_queueHiddenSubtreeFreeze` at `25713-14` — a freeze-queue throw drops the freeze mint on a now-clean row (bounded, benefit-only).
**Fix sketch:** snapshot `_dmg*` to locals, call `updateDamageState`, commit latches after the call returns.
**Effort:** S. **Impact:** L-M (one mesh's damage visual stale for the fault window and beyond; requires a throw, rare). **Risk:** trivial.

### F4 — Storm pin transfers to a fresh epoch without any mint attempt
**Evidence:** `renderer.js:29171-29200` — `memo.superseded` is never reset on census-epoch flip (only on scene swap `:29159`, successful mint `:29247`, or storm re-arm `:29187`). A consult arriving under a new epoch while `superseded >= 4` hits `memo.stormEpoch !== epoch` → the `superseded >= 4` branch re-pins `stormEpoch = epoch` + `stormRetryAt = now+1000` immediately — the new epoch is born pinned.
**Mechanism:** hunt (b)'s pin-survival path: a sustained storm pinning epoch N, then quieting with one final bump to N+1, still serves the stale sig for ~1 s — the fresh epoch never gets a mint attempt at flip. Under a *sustained* storm the pin↔re-arm cycle costs one ≤4 ms stepped-mint window per ~1 s — bounded and self-limiting (the intended direction: it stops the per-call mint+discard livelock), so the residual is only the epoch-transfer case. `stormRetryAt` correctly does not survive memo replacement (scene swap mints a fresh memo — the fresh mint path runs).
**Fix sketch:** reset `superseded` when the consult observes `memo.epoch !== epoch` (pin is per-epoch state), or carry the pin only while the epoch that earned it is still current.
**Effort:** S. **Impact:** L (≥1 s unsigned window after every storm-ending mutation). **Risk:** low.

### F5 — Park mints/re-key bypass the shared mint and the in-scope arm sig
**Evidence:** `renderer.js:29563` and `:29698` stamp `lightSig: this._shadowCensusForFrame()` while the arm's `lightSig` (the `_depthCollectLightSig` mint, possibly fresher than the memo) sits in scope; `:28638` re-keys the same way on out-of-ortho drift-keep.
**Mechanism:** hunt (a)'s bypass enumeration — three parked-entry stamps consult the memo directly. When the forced mint succeeded but the memo is still pending they stamp `null` (healed by the 28584 back-fill one eval later), and they can stamp a memo sig that differs from the sig the ceremony actually marked under — the release compare then fires on non-drift or misses the drift the park was keyed against. Bounded: nulls heal next eval and the epoch compare gates the sig compare.
**Fix sketch:** stamp the arm's `lightSig` when non-null, else the memo — the park's key should be the sig the leg's marks took.
**Effort:** S. **Impact:** L (one-eval stamp delay; rare release-timing skew). **Risk:** low.
