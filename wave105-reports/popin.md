## Findings (ranked)

### F1 — Queued roster emits pay a full serial re-drain; a coalescing spec map beats FIFO

- **Evidence**: `src/render/renderer.js:18441-18462` (drive loop `for (let emit = ...; emit;) { ... emit = emitQueue.shift() }`), `:18435-18437` (queue pushes `{specs, compilePipelines}` only — no `exemplarSpecs`), `:18445-18449` (`emit.exemplarSpecs || emit.specs.concat(wreckVisualExemplarSpecs(), combatSpawnableExemplarSpecs(), asteroidVisualExemplarSpecs())` re-minted per queued emit), `:18491-18665` (`_drainRosterPrewarmCohort` full-universe walk per emit).
- **Mechanism**: a `survivalArena:rosterPrewarm`/`onboarding:rosterPrewarm`/`run:wavePlanned`-family emit landing while `_rosterPrewarmCohortDrain` is live queues FIFO and then runs a *complete second* `_drainRosterPrewarmCohort` after the in-flight one settles. The drain yields at ~8 ms slices, so a ~40-60-boundary cohort stretches over seconds of presented frames; the queued emit's genuinely-new hulls therefore don't start building until the entire wave-N tail finishes, then re-walk the whole exemplar universe (per-spec skip is cheap but O(table)) and re-concat the three exemplar families. A wave N+1 spawn materializing inside that serial window finds its hull un-warmed → `vf.build` + `requestAuthoredUpgrade` + `compileObjectPipelines` inside the round — the exact pop-in/hitch the prewarm exists to prevent. Same-payload re-emits during a drain also queue a guaranteed extra full walk because `_rosterPrewarmIds.add` lands at drain time, not emit time (needsWork sees unmarked specs → push → later re-drain skips them one by one).
- **Fix sketch**: coalesce the tail into a spec-level map instead of FIFO-of-emits: fold each queued emit's specs into a pending `Map<spec.id, {spec, compilePipelines}>` (dedupe across emits *and* against `_rosterPrewarmIds`/`_meshes` at push time — push only the delta, nothing when fully covered); have the running drain pick up appended specs at its next yield rather than starting a fresh pass, or merge all queued emits into one re-drain. Carry the minted `exemplarSpecs` on the queued emit so the 3-family concat isn't re-run per emit; stamp per-emit `needsWork` re-verified at consume so a now-covered emit costs one map lookup instead of a full pass.
- **Effort** M · **magic-frame impact** M (un-warmed spawn inside the round) · **Risk** L — dedupe reorders mount order; the drain's own ledger already documents coverage-priority-only ordering, and `_rosterPrewarmIds` dedupe makes ordering correctness-neutral.

### F2 — Stale cohort drive survives release: no epoch guard on the async drive

- **Evidence**: drive mint at `src/render/renderer.js:18443-18464`; release at `:20906-20916` clears `_rosterPrewarmEmitQueue`, `_rosterPrewarmCohortDrain`, `_rosterPrewarmRoots`, `_rosterPrewarmPending`, and `_rosterPrewarmIds` — but the in-flight `drive` closure keeps iterating its captured `emitQueue` array and calling `_drainRosterPrewarmCohort`, which has no epoch/run check (`:18491-18665` gates only on `_rosterPrewarmIds`/`_meshes` membership).
- **Mechanism**: `_releaseSurvivalRosterPrewarm` (run:ended/teardown) mid-drive: the cleared id set makes every subsequent spec look uncovered → the stale drain re-`vf.build`s hidden exemplars and pushes them into the *new* run's `_rosterPrewarmRoots` (leaked mounts carried forward), `_rosterPrewarmIds.add(spec.id)` pollutes the new run's ledger so its own `needsWork` scan treats those specs as covered, and its `requestAuthoredUpgrade` calls run under `isResidencyOwnerActive: () => this._rosterPrewarmIds.has(spec.id)` → false → dead-owner drops (partial self-heal, but the build+scene.add+push already landed). Net effect at run boundaries: double-mount churn, hidden exemplars surviving under the wrong epoch, and a new-run ledger claiming coverage it never minted.
- **Fix sketch**: stamp a run epoch (`this._rosterPrewarmRunSeq`) at drive mint; `_releaseSurvivalRosterPrewarm` bumps it; check it once per spec/yield inside `_drainRosterPrewarmCohort` and bail (mirroring `cookStale()` discipline). Also clears the ids-pollution path since the stale drive can't reach `_rosterPrewarmIds.add`.
- **Effort** S · **magic-frame impact** M/L · **Risk** L — additive guard, identical to existing generation-guard patterns.

### F3 — `drawnCullRadiusMax` over-serve: grown hulls under-cull (missing ×1.15), shrunk hulls over-draw under sustained key-move storms

- **Evidence**: `src/render/visualCullRadius.js:144-147` (over-budget key-moved miss returns `known` **without** `DRAWN_CULL_STALE_SERVE_BIAS` — the bias exists only on the cadence-expiry branch `:150-153`), `:132`/`:176` (`drawnCullRadiusMax` monotonic max-ever, never cleared anywhere), `:65-116` (cadence + LRU).
- **Mechanism A (under-cull → pop-in)**: the over-budget key-moved serve assumes demotion only. A hull whose *new* key's envelope exceeds every prior measure (fresh bigger composition: packaged graft/attachPackagedBody adding parts, grown authored state) gets short-culled — radius smaller than the true envelope → the overhang culls a hull that is on-screen → missing pixels until a later pass pays the real `Box3.setFromObject` (retries are per-pass, unbounded while the pass stays over-budget). W104's own stated contract was `max(drawnCullRadiusMax, cached.radius) * 1.15`; the code serves `known` raw — the bias keeps the deferred serve strictly on the legal over-draw side.
- **Mechanism B (over-serve)**: same-key/interior shrink is cadence-covered, but cross-key shrink (whole-ship LOD descent — `wholeShipLodActiveLevel` is in the key; visual swap — `authoredCompositionId`; child-drop) leaves `drawnCullRadiusMax` at the old large radius. Under a sustained key-move storm (>4 moves/syncEntityViews pass, every pass — e.g. a fleet-wide regrade wave) the serve never reaches a re-measure and hulls stay classified at the lod0-era envelope: chronic over-draw and retained on-glass admission for hulls that should have culled.
- **Fix sketch**: multiply the serve by `DRAWN_CULL_STALE_SERVE_BIAS` on the key-moved branch (closes A by widening the margin the right direction); optionally cap `drawnCullRadiusMax` per lruKey family or re-derive it as max-over-LRU rather than max-ever so B decays with the LRU window.
- **Effort** S · **magic-frame impact** M (A) / L (B) · **Risk** L — over-serve is already the chosen legal direction.

### F4 — Warm-lane starvation is bounded (verified) but forced drains stack deadlines on spent frames

- **Evidence**: `src/render/renderer.js:2036-2039` (warm lane = same `drainDisposeQueueLane` machinery), `:2076-2084` (shared `pacedFrameSpend() >= PACED_FRAME_BUDGET_MS` skip + per-lane `_warmDisposeLedgerSkips` aging cap 2 → forced drain every ≤3 presented frames), `:2070-2071` (backlog>32 doubles the wall budget to ~4 ms), `:2296-2297` (live first, warm second in `serviceRenderMeshResidency`).
- **Mechanism**: enumerated the sustained live-corpse stream (desertion waves / mass despawn): the warm lane *cannot* park indefinitely — its own aging counter forces a slice every ≤3 frames regardless of the live lane, and `limit = max(8, ceil(backlog/4))` bounds retention stretch. The residual cost is the forced drain stacking its own freshly-minted 2→4 ms deadline on a frame whose paced budget the live lane already consumed — under a deep two-lane backlog that's ~8 ms of corpse teardown inside one presented beat. Whether a per-class floor beats budget-share: a floor wouldn't help (starvation isn't the failure); a *combined* wall deadline covering both lanes in one service pass would bound the stacked case.
- **Fix sketch**: let the second lane inherit the first's remaining wall budget when it force-runs on a spent ledger (pass the remaining ms rather than a fresh `DESPAWN_DISPOSE_BUDGET_MS`), keeping the aging guarantee but capping the two-lane frame total.
- **Effort** S · **magic-frame impact** L · **Risk** L.
