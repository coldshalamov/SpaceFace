## Fresh findings — ranked

### F1 — Deny/park release deletes the park before the collect gate can open; promised re-verify never runs on the release eval, and escalation terms are wiped

**Evidence:** renderer.js:25259-25263 (unconditional `parkedMap.delete` + `_withheldDepthCasters.delete` + `delete sfDepthUndrawableCycles` on `parkedRelease`) vs. collectGate at :25281-25284 (`parkedRecheck || parkedReleaseOnDrift || (band !== 1 ? !queued : dirtySeq > stampedSeq && dirtySeq > 0)`), `queued` captured from pre-delete `parked` at :25264, `withheldCoverageKnown` gate at :25421-25430.

**Mechanism:** the denied-allowCast clause (:25202) and the castBand clause (:25215) mint a *plain* `parkedRelease`, which is not itself a collectGate opener. For every denied-park provenance the gate is provably closed on the release eval: band<1 roots read `queued=true` (stale `parked`), and band-1 roots satisfy `dirtySeq === stampedSeq` by construction — a genuine re-dirty would have fired the earlier dirtySeq clause (:25186) first, and both the mint (:25941) and the drift re-key (:25230) stamp `seq` at the current generation. The release's own comment promises "the collect re-verifies drawability under the live census and simply re-parks with cycles+1" — that path is unreachable except when `parkedRecheck` coincidentally expires the same eval. What actually happens: park + withheld cache + `sfDepthUndrawableCycles` are deleted now; the collect defers to the **next** eval via the band-0 `!queued` opener; that eval's re-queue call reads a fresh `parkedEntry` (undefined) so the earned `depthNodeScale` is **not** carried (:25560/:25569 read the local that no longer exists) — a permanently-over-cap root re-pays the 1→2→4→8 escalation ladder the park comment says it exists to avoid (:25820-25823); and `sfDepthUndrawableCycles` restarts at 1 so the ~96–127-frame base recheck never escalates toward its 8× backoff for chronic verdicts.

**Fix sketch:** let the release do what it says — add `parkedRelease` to the collectGate disjunct (the pass cap + node budget still bound it), or re-derive `queued` post-delete (`pending.has(root) || (parked && !parkedRelease)`) so band<1 released roots collect same-eval; and preserve `sfDepthUndrawableCycles` across denied releases — it's the denial memory, delete it only on a collect that proves the root drawable/empty. Also decide whether the stale `parkedEntry` should still feed `depthNodeScale` into the next-eval re-queue (carry the terms onto a root-scoped side record rather than the deleted map entry).

**Effort:** M (release ordering has many seams — scopedSync, pending∧parked coexistence documented at :26240ish, recheck). **Magic-frame impact:** M (presented-pass churn on the belt; escalation loss makes chronic parks re-pay fast-cadence collect+offer cycles indefinitely). **Risk:** M — the release chain is the densest part of the sync; pin a test that a denied park released under `allowCast:true` collects same-eval and keeps its cycle count.

### F2 — Retro-denial: the drift re-key upgrades non-denied (over-cap) parks into armed deny releases

**Evidence:** renderer.js:25223-25257 — on `parkedRelease && parkedReleaseOnDrift && opts.allowCast === false` the keep-verdict unconditionally stamps `parkedEntry.denied = true; parkedEntry.deniedAllowCast = false` (:25255-25256), with the comment "The keep verdict is the same denial the mint stamped." But the only mints are :25938 (denied — `denied` set) and :25809 (over-cap aborted collect — no `denied`/`deniedAllowCast` fields at all). An over-cap park therefore becomes `denied:true, deniedAllowCast:false` — armed for the :25202 release clause — without ever being deny-verdicted. `opts.allowCast` is positional (derived from `_shadowPolicyOptions` → `allowRealtimeShadowCast`, :25100-25110), so this retro-denial mints whenever an out-of-radius parked root rides a lightSig bump or an `oqX/oqZ` ortho-cell move — the ortho target follows the player, so cells flip every ~150 WU of travel.

**Mechanism:** an over-cap park exists precisely because the subtree is too big to collect — its designed lifecycle is "wait out recheck/dirty/drift." The retro-stamp hands it a release trigger it never earned: on the next in-radius eval (`allowCast===true`, no earlier clause firing) the release deletes the park; F1's closed gate defers the collect one eval; the re-collect over-covers at scale 1 (earned scale lost, F1) → whole-subtree withhold → re-queue → arm re-collect over-covers → re-park at cycles=1. Every radius/ortho oscillation cycle re-pays an aborting max-scale collect plus an atomic withhold on a root the system already proved uncollectible — self-manufactured churn, and the recheck backoff can never accumulate because F1 wipes the counter each loop.

**Fix sketch:** mint provenance. Only keep `deniedAllowCast=false` re-keys for entries that were `denied===true` at mint (stamp e.g. `parkKind: 'denied' | 'overcap'` at :25809/:25938 and gate the re-key's denied upgrade on it), so over-cap parks keep waiting on their real terms (dirtySeq/drift/recheck) instead of acquiring a positional release trigger. Combine with F1's `parkedRelease`-in-gate fix so even genuine deny releases verify immediately.

**Effort:** S-M. **Impact:** M (bounded but repeating presented-pass burn per boundary oscillation on the fattest roots). **Risk:** M — low code churn but needs the F1-side test coverage first.

- **Deny-mint reachability (hunt a):** the only `denied:true` mint is the arm `.finally` (:25938-25961), and it is offer-gated — `allUnmarked` requires every `deferMeshes` member ∈ `legSet` (the drive's offered leg) and not mark-current, so a root denied without a staging offer cannot exist through that path. Cross-cook adoption is likewise guarded: `unstagedByRoot` is drive-local, `unstagedCache` re-validates `{seq, epoch}` (:25764-25766), `deferredRoots` prevents double-offer (:25755), and `legAborted`/`legDriveFailed` block mints on unevaluated roots. The one retro-denial is F2's re-key. Side observation: a root with >128 unstaged casters (`SHADOW_DEPTH_ARM_MESH_CAP`) can never satisfy `allUnmarked` — it queues forever paying arm collects instead of parking; bounded by the arm's own collect deadline, arguably intended, noted for completeness.
- **Release ordering (hunt b):** the allowCast release is fifth in the else-if chain (after scopedSync keep, dirtySeq, lightSig drift, oqX drift; before castBand). No same-pass re-park exists inside the sync — mints live in the async arm `.finally` — but that `.finally` can consume a pending entry minted by an intervening withhold (`pending.delete(deferRoot)` at :25926) — marginal, ordering-safe since the fresh park mints at current seq. The material hole is F1.
- **Uncovered-withhold (hunt c):** covered above in F3 — an opts-known-coverage record (stepped collect parked per root, seq+epoch keyed) can serve it without re-opening the cold-link window, because the suspended collect is read-only and the flags it would write stay in their last-known (suppressed or previously-linked) state.
